## Consequences for weak gravity and a wave experiment

- At fixed measured mass and charge the ordinary clock metric retains the Newtonian 1/R coefficient. In operational areal radius its first direct charged-clock correction falls as 1/R⁴; the source metric's first explicit correction falls as 1/r⁶. The trace contact leaves pure electrovacuum alone but can change the mass supplied by the interior. See DERIVATION.md for normalized coefficients and independent static clock trajectories.
- A radial short-wavelength scalar front and a photon separate locally only through **γ₁+2γ₂**. The ordinary empty-scalar control gives exactly zero; radial electric terms cancel from their relative characteristic. Summing along the two measured rays reproduces the separately saved path kernels and passes fine-grid checks for both frequency and delay (8/8). This is not the group speed of a finite-frequency massive particle.
- Re-solving at the prior illustrative 0.1 GeV/cm³, the signed relative delay coefficients are +7.249e−16 outgoing and −3.660e−15 echo, in code times per unit γ₁+2γ₂. The relative frequency coefficients are −1.940e−15 and −1.915e−14. All four refined comparisons pass; rescaling the dense curve alone would have missed the new calculation by up to 1.55e−5 relatively. No sky residual, density, occupation or Wilson coefficient was inferred here.
- Under the *previous* example pole and strict-EFT caps, the first-order dense combined fractional-frequency envelopes for these two terms are 1.06e−52 (outgoing) and 1.61e−52 (echo); the gravitational parts alone are ≤1.72e−58 and ≤1.65e−58. That is an algebraic distinction, not astronomical sensitivity.

## What the failed large steps taught us

In the **exactly cold, coarse-grained dust fixture**, the rest energy is n−(γ₁+γ₂)n²/4. The slow mode therefore has c²=−(γ₁+γ₂)n/2 at first order. Positive sum gives a gradient instability; negative sum supplies positive stiffness at this order. This is not a sign bound on all microscopic MTS matter. The independently sampled mixed scalar/material principal matrix and homogeneous Fourier controls are in mixed-characteristics.json and dust-stability.json.

Both +4 runs lose their real numerical domain near the centre at code time 2.547 (tensor) and 2.579 (trace), for **both** the event and quiet. All four −4 cases reach 3.24. The failed runs had already developed strongly unphysical densities; this is not a signal caused by the compact disturbance at radius five. The local symbol explains why the positive sign amplifies short waves. It does not by itself establish which discretization error seeded the collapse, or the exact nonlinear failure time. In the local homogeneous principal-part estimate at the example caps, the largest 160-mesh mode accumulates <1e−21 e-folds over this window. The continuum sign question nevertheless survives; finite-duration numerical agreement is not hyperbolicity.
