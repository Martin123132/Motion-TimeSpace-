# Predeclared nonlinear occupation continuation
The small-fraction tangent helps the inner polar gravity but weakens inward radial gravity. It is not an exclusion of finite occupations. Test the *entire interval of allowed fractions at nine prescribed knots* (0, 1/8, ..., 1) rather than extrapolating the tangent to a fraction greater than one. The 1/8 spacing is not a proof about every unsampled point. Keep the original ground state as the zero control; at fraction one the ground component has no occupation. Equal counter-rotating populations and positive common self-gravity throughout, no matter input changes. Follow both a fixed-number sequence and the same one-outer-speed calibrated sequence. Never select a fraction from a Gaia, quasar, or rotation residual.
Solve the two projected stationary equations together, normalized in the inherited basis. Save full substate amplitudes after each completed knot. Check number fractions, projected field-equation errors, full scalar virial, and continued fixed anchor. Test both sectors' fixed-potential eigen ordering at the final occupied mode separately; stationarity is not a dynamical-stability guarantee. Repeat comparisons at other resolutions; do not promote sub-tolerance claims for quantities that miss the frozen 2e-5 check. Report if the outer-speed constraint becomes singular or a branch can't be continued. Additionally test the formation energy cost at fixed total mass: the total energy derivative per unit mass equals the difference of the two chemical potentials. These are state-preparation diagnostics, not a cosmological occupation prescription.
