# Opposite rotation helps the inner polar force, but loses the inward force

**The retained field can generate the missing directional pull on this stellar map. The tested state does not generate it in the right places at the same time.** We changed the occupation of the *same* gravitationally coupled field, not the force law. A mirror-even ground population was transferred in equal parts to the lowest opposite-rotation modes. They have zero mean spin but nonzero angular stress. The fractions were prescribed before the finite tests, not fitted to any rotation or force data.

## What the sky comparison says

Foreground: the preceding four Lian populations with global component masses and a spherical central excess, the same gas, and no change to the light-to-mass map. Locations are cylindrical R=6 and 8.3 kpc, each at z=±1.5 kpc. Polar means the spherical angular force directed toward the plane, **not** the Cartesian vertical acceleration. Values below reflect the two hemispheres into the same sign convention. The reference for the bands is [Lim et al., Gaia DR3 force field, Fig. 9](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.13358v3). The quasar acceleration readout uses the [Gaia reference-frame dipole](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.02036). These are exposed measurements; individual north/south bands are not independent likelihoods.

Accelerations in units of 10⁻¹⁰ m/s². The full-transfer state falls inside both inner polar bands, but outside both near-Sun polar bands; the 87.5% knot lies outside all four. On this branch the 100% state needs 2.125 times the original wave mass to keep the outer speed unchanged. With total wave mass held fixed instead, even the largest sampled inner polar force is only 0.438 (at 50%); at 100% the outer speed falls to 105.41 km/s. None of the nine sampled fractions in either comparison reaches even one of the four radial bands. The standard halo control misses those bands too, but its radial, quasar and rotation predictions are closer here. No survey was refit to favour one side. There is no global exclusion of other stellar distributions, odd modes or histories, or a certified enclosure of all intermediate fractions.

## What was actually derived

At small transfer the computed excited tracer has a vertical-to-equatorial second-moment axis ratio of 0.702. At fixed total number the inner polar derivative is +0.06112 per unit fraction: +0.05218 directly replacing ground mass and +0.00894 from relaxation of the remaining ground population. Keeping the original outer speed adds a mass response and raises this derivative to +0.07164. Its inward radial **magnitude decreases** by 0.4446 per unit fraction. The nonlinear 100% result, unlike a linear extrapolation, can genuinely clear the polar band. A one-direction success alone would be physically misleading.

The number-conserving first variation costs about **25,101 (km/s)² per unit mass transferred** off the ground state; it is not a free angular rearrangement. The energy derivative calculated directly from the retained weak-field kinetic and potential energy terms, including self-gravity with its factor of 1/2, agrees with the chemical-potential calculation on all interior knots (the matched ensemble also includes work from changing total number). The pure matched pair stores 39.6% of its kinetic energy in the azimuthal phase gradient, even though the signed angular momenta cancel. Its integrated azimuthal phase-stress contribution divided by its rest energy is only 2.30 × 10⁻⁷; this is not an extra Newtonian gravity source.

There is also a formation constraint that is independent of fitting. A perfectly axisymmetric preparation preserves m=0, and even a purely twofold bar preserves even m: neither can take an initially symmetric pure ground wave into this pair. After removing uniform free fall, the first possible *distant* gravitational multipole coupling these two mirror-even states is an octupole. The actual matrix element has magnitude 18.22 kpc³ for the equatorial point-source convention in PREPARATION.md; it vanishes on the spherical pure s-to-p angular control. Multiplying this by a physically known tidal amplitude and time history is still necessary. The roughly 490 Myr frozen-potential phase period is not an inferred collective resonance. We have not shown that a coherent drive prepares this incoherent mixture.

## Numerical checks and decision

Precomputed the first derivatives before six independent finite-fraction relaxations at each of two resolutions; their positive-side extrapolations agree to 2.32 × 10⁻⁶ or better on the retained scaled metric. Independently solved the coupled normalized equations at 32 nonzero knots counting both ensembles and ground grids (16 distinct ensemble/fraction combinations). Direct one-dimensional monopole/quadrupole integrals checked the force of pure basis densities. The pure mode was independently rediagonalized on its own final potential. Half- and full-transfer states were re-solved in a second physical domain (248 versus 388 kpc). All 216 saved resolution comparisons pass the unchanged 2 × 10⁻⁵ scaled tolerance (largest 7.49 × 10⁻⁶). This is numerical convergence, not an observational error estimate or a proof of dynamical stability.

**Decision:** retain the directional-stress and preparation derivations, but do not promote this counter-rotating mixture as a Milky Way fit, or search for a best transfer fraction on these data. The next empirical move is to replace the transplanted inner-disk height with a separately measured tracer shape, keeping stellar column, ordinary gas, wave law and spherical control fixed. [Wu et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.24456v1#S5.SS2) measure the relevant red-clump heights and a 5–7 kpc angular density feature; tracer counts are not a measurement of remnant mass. See NEXT.md.
