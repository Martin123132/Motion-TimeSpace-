# Why the ordinary component barely deforms in this center measurement

A quadratic external potential separates the center of a self-gravitating collisionless system from its internal motions: writing \(x=X+\xi\) makes its external force \(-H X-H\xi\), with \(\langle \xi\rangle=0\). No assumption of a rigid cloud or a pressure law is needed. This is a property of the ordinary tracer embedded in the wave; it does **not** make the perturbed wave a quadratic potential everywhere.

There is a useful bound even without a perfectly quadratic potential. In the core \(H_w\) has radial and transverse eigenvalues \(\rho_w-2\bar\rho_w/3,\ \bar\rho_w/3\), with \(\bar\rho_w=3m_w/r^3\). The nodeless equilibrium decreases across the core: its total potential increases outwards, is still below its eigenvalue at the edge (checked on both backgrounds), and \((r^2u')'=2r^2(\Phi-\epsilon)u<0\). Therefore \(\rho_w(r)\le\bar\rho_w(r)\le\rho_w(0)\). Setting \(h_0=\rho_w(0)/3\) gives, throughout the core,
\[
 \|H_w-h_0 I\|_{\rm op}\le \rho_w(0)-\rho_w(R_b),\qquad
 |f_{\rm shape}|\le [\rho_w(0)-\rho_w(R_b)]\sqrt{\langle|\xi|^2\rangle}.
\]
The equations use wave units; the density on the right includes \(4\pi G\) if using physical mass density and physical time. This bounds the first-order force due to *internal* displacement, not the response of the center to a displaced or distorted wave. The coefficient bound is 30.4 and 8.66 parts per million of the central harmonic curvature on the compact and diffuse preparations. The force inequality holds at all 65 saved epochs for both, with the measured force never exceeding 3.01% of its own nonzero bound. This does not extrapolate time-sampled shape amplitudes to an arbitrarily long history.

There is a separate analytic check on the removal of spurious fast growth. For the enclosed-shell system in an exactly harmonic wave background \(h\ge0\), with the wave perturbation switched off, write \(b=m_b/r^3,\ d=\rho_b,\ \Omega^2=b+h\). In the rotating planar dyads the characteristic determinant factors as
\[
 (z^2+\Omega^2)^2[z^4+(4b+d+10h)z^2+3h(3h+d)].
\]
The discriminant of the last quadratic in \(z^2\) is \((4b+d)^2+80bh+8dh+64h^2\). Thus these local eigenvalues have no exponentially growing branch for nonnegative density. The normal-to-plane dyad has frequency \(\Omega\). Zero or repeated roots need not exclude neutral secular terms. The symbolic determinant is checked independently of the time integrator. It is not a global stability proof for the self-consistently perturbed, nonharmonic wave/cloud system.
