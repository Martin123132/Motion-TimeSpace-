# Reuse and verification

Offline, private Python 3.13 / NumPy / SciPy / SymPy. Use the retained interpreter with `-B` in this directory. The numerical modules fix their math-library thread counts to one. No network access, package installation, fitting or source acquisition is needed. No long-lived process is used.

- `live_dipole.py balanced-core central_cap` and `live_dipole.py balanced-core illustrative`: reproduce the primary two preparations. Packet generation is non-overwriting: an existing packet requires equality of its numerical arrays. Other accepted grid names are in `CONFIGS`. The old `trial-illustrative.npz` is deliberately rejected; `orbit_controls.py` reconstructs its original unstable collocation operator and verifies its leading eigenvalue. It is not part of a physical average.
- `independent_readout.py` and `characterize.py independent` reproduce the first and refined inertial comparisons; `orbit_controls.py` also runs the finite Cartesian orbit benchmark. The inertial comparison resolves the wave, with a translating nondeforming cloud; it is not an independent solution for the very small material shape. The exact enclosed-shell identity and direct orbit checks test that part separately.
- `characterize.py channels` integrates the three physical driving terms using the same causal response kernel. `characterize.py summary` rebuilds the endpoint and refinement tables. `restart_bank.py` retains the complete first-order fields and verifies the second half from the saved middle state; `cloud_limit.py` verifies the harmonic determinant and source bound.
- `verify_stage.py` checks the local archive against its existing seal, immediate parent file hashes, independent signed vectors, analytical controls and numerical refinements. Archive initialization uses the explicit `seal` option only once. The earlier parent result and its orbit remain unchanged.

**Large-box primary first-order restart layout.** Each `restart-*.npz` has 65 equally spaced samples of a (924,3) state in wave units. Rows are: 176 real and 176 imaginary internal wave dipole basis coefficients; 5×56 enclosed-center displacement and 5×56 velocity coefficients (radial-radial, radial-tangent, tangent-radial, tangent-tangent, orbit-normal); relative position and velocity (ordinary minus wave); total center and velocity; externally driven free-wave center and velocity; rigid, polarization, and cloud-shape oscillator positions and velocities. Each vector column is in the Cartesian frame of the archived trajectory. The cloud-centered physical dyads are recovered by `Live.reconstruct`. The potential is solved from the densities; it is not a second independent field state. Also saved: logarithmic satellite-distance input bank, radial roots and geometry, stationary background samples, units and times. The orbit direction comes from the existing local `orphan_orbit.txt`.

**Limits of error statements.** Endpoint vector errors are relative to the norm of the endpoint, not to each component near a zero. History errors use the maximum vector error over the 65 saved epochs divided by the maximum comparison norm. Split-source closure, special harmonic limits and exact restarts do not substitute for frequency, spatial or domain refinement. The inherited numerical budget is 2e-5; rows that miss are kept. There is no empirical uncertainty or confidence interval inferred from grid differences.
