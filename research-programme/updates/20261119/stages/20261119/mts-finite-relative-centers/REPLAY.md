# Private replay and comparison map

Use the existing retained Python 3.13, NumPy and SciPy; launch with `-B` inside this directory. Load only this stage and the hash-pinned ancestor chain. No network, Git, data download, fitted optimizer or GPU is needed for the replay. Do not run all jobs in parallel. Each numerical worker sets BLAS runtime limits to one thread before importing NumPy. No output belongs in the old stage. The seal excludes itself. The verifier does not bless arbitrary material distributions or make all convergence differences disappear.

- `python -B verify_stage.py` checks the stored seal, the immediately preceding stage's complete manifest, finite endpoints against their histories, source chains and restarted ordinary initial states, independent integral reconstructions, controls and the known precision misses. Only the initial producer uses the explicit `seal` argument once.
- `packet-index.csv` gives the exact retained parameters and replay invocation for every packet: full-strength, counterfactual controls, tangents, contours and independent conservation histories. Presence of a packet is not alone a pass. The scripts compare an existing packet on replay rather than replacing it. To reconstruct a numerical packet from scratch in a separate copy, remove the command's replay flag if present and restore its listed dependencies first, including the original ancestors. Do not remove archived packets here.
- `budget.py weak`, `budget.py impact`, `response_controls.py roots`, `response_controls.py summaries` and `spatial_budget.py` rebuild signed finite/weak, second-order, numerical convergence and causal operator controls. Prefix each by `python -B`. Weak and strong series at the same angular order are different comparisons from varying the angular cutoff.
- `budget.py dense-momentum-cap-spline.csv ward-cap ward16-cap dense-ward-cap` and `budget.py momentum-diff-spline.csv ward-diff ward10-diff ward22-diff ward28-diff` reconstruct the relative endpoints from independently sampled external impulse. The older lower-cadence and Simpson tables remain, including the misses. These are tests of the force and time readout; by themselves they are not estimates of field domain error.
- `field_checks.py direct-force.csv angular16-cap angular16-diff`, `compact_tube.py angular16`, `continuum_cloud.py poles` and the `shell_replay.py` rows in the index check different approximations. The positive-growth control of the causal solver is synthetic. The cloud's quadrupole calculation is not a replacement for the nonlinear wave.
- `sky_dipole.py sky-dipole.csv angular24-cap angular28-diff`, `sky_components.py sky-components.csv angular24-cap angular28-diff still-cap still-diff` and `rigid_control.py` rebuild the line-of-sight experiment, its null and signed channel decomposition. `sky-matched16.csv` and `sky-outer16.csv` use the matched angular cutoffs on the two domains. No empirical timing measurements enter these files.
- Sources: initial material and occupations from `../mts-live-relative-dipole-20261119/`; separated angular integrator and orbital reader from the retained ancestors referenced there. Orbit provenance is in the [Erkal release](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.2661108). Terminal forces are evaluated on the supplied five-Gyr track, not refitted positions. A finite external-source response should not be confused with an isolated force-free measurement.
