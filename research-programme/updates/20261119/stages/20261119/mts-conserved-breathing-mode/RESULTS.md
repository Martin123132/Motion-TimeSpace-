# What a conserved breathing wave changes

The retained positive quadratic wave does not need an externally imposed pressure envelope to oscillate. Its own Newtonian gravity supplies a spherical mode that moves mass between inner and outer radii while preserving its total number. **The mode is real in the equations; its population is not determined by its existence.** All calculations here are private, on the retained pole, with the inherited 2×10⁻⁵ relative numerical tolerance. There was no fit and no new observational sample.

**Independent dynamical test.** The derived dimensionless frequency is 0.290260709 (isolated) versus an escape threshold of 0.692230118. A second direct gravitational Green-function eigensolver, extrapolated in spatial resolution, differs by 2.61×10⁻⁸ relatively. The frequency is consistent with 0.290 for the first radial excitation in [Chan, Sibiryakov & Xue, Table 1](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP08(2023)045). This isolated mode is established Schrödinger–Poisson physics, not an MTS-specific discovery. Its period is 1.774 billion years at our *illustrative* halo scale, and changes with the square of the scale. It is distinct from the preceding fast scalar pressure cycle.

Three full nonlinear isolated evolutions cover 1⅛ periods, each with an unexcited wave and ±0.001, ±0.0005 central field preparations at equal integrated number. The frequency is read from density zero crossings, not fitted to a sinusoid; the free equations contain no prescribed oscillatory force. The finest paired measurements give 0.290260218 and 0.290260464, respectively 1.69 and 0.84 parts per million from the linear prediction. The maximum paired density/current profile errors on that grid are 5.19×10⁻⁶ and 4.88×10⁻⁶ at 0.001. Including time- and radial-grid changes in the empirical comparison gives 9.16×10⁻⁶ and 8.55×10⁻⁶ respectively. All five wave numbers remain conserved to 3.59×10⁻¹² relatively. A one-sided finite perturbation is **not** equal to its linearization at that accuracy: the density remainder relative to the first-order signal peaks at about 0.15% at 0.001 and halves with the amplitude. The paired comparison isolates the derivative; it does not erase those remainders.

**Ordinary matter is allowed to move.** In the retained weak, spherical, angular-momentum-supported ordinary core the rigid and live frequencies are both 0.290260421 to the precision warranted here. The live-minus-rigid frequency shift is unresolved; the material response itself is not. At 20.01 pc, for +0.001 at the central-density maximum, the shell moves inward by 1.396×10⁻⁵ pc. Its +0.127 solar-mass contribution inside that radius accompanies +0.177 from the wave. The linear shell/momentum check from independently integrated density has fine error 4.19×10⁻⁷, and the material work closes to 6.24×10⁻¹¹ on its own scale. The positive perturbation energy at the density and velocity maxima agrees to 8.41×10⁻¹¹. Numerical errors are not physical relativistic uncertainty. Finite nonlinear trajectories were tested on the isolated branch; the live material result is linear.

**What this does to a real-data comparison.** Keep the [17.58-kpc Cepheid anchor](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.21780v1) unchanged and reserve its entire centripetal force for the wave, exactly as in the previous upper envelope. For field amplitude 0.001 (roughly 0.2% peak central-density change at fixed normalization), the first-order change in the 100-kpc ceiling is ±1.067×10⁸ solar masses, or **±0.0384%**. At the more permissive +3 quoted-error speed, it is ±1.073×10⁸; the largest sampled finite increase is 1.074×10⁸. Opposite phases change the sign. The absolute scale comes mostly from the corresponding change in allowed normalization, not matter flowing across 100 kpc. At each of five phases we independently integrated the evolved densities and solved the anchor equation anew; the signed derivative meets the original tolerance in all 240 comparisons (including the anchor controls). These counts span shared simulations, not 240 independent datasets.

This small breathing excitation covers about **0.081%** of the previous 1.330×10¹¹ solar-mass difference to the central [Sagittarius/LMC inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.10726) under the generous ordinary-matter inventory and +3 quoted-error anchor. The lower historical stream estimate required no deficit in that inventory. This is neither a fit to the stream nor a bound on arbitrary occupations, other modes, larger excitations, or nonspherical halos. It says *which proposed repair does not appreciably change the earlier test*.

**What can determine an amplitude.** The overlap with a specified ordinary gravitational potential determines the leading excitation without fitting an oscillator amplitude. For the same small amount of ordinary matter moved in and back, the frozen linear predictions are 2.75054×10⁻⁴ after a half-period pulse, 3.24040×10⁻⁴ after a one-period pulse, and exactly zero in this mode after a two-period pulse. The latter is destructive temporal interference, not absence of gravity.
