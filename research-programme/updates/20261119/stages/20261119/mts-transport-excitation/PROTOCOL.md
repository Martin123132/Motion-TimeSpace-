# Fixed ordinary transport, unforced wave after release

Use the previously sealed pulse, pole, boundary, normalization, signed projections and tolerance (2e-5). Test T=0.5,1,2 fundamental periods. Retain a static positive donor in each paired control; add an identical stationary carrier density to the *specification of the total density* in both arms. This last detail is necessary: a current between two disjoint shells cannot pass through a vacuum gap. The carrier is transported by the combined fluid; it is not a separately static species required to deliver a current in empty space. Total shell mass is constant. Its imposed density/current obey continuity; external support and acceleration are specified, not inferred for real galaxies.

Solve each loaded initial equilibrium at the same wave number, then evolve the complex wave and its self-potential, not a reduced oscillator. Use three positive moved masses at ratios 1,1/2,1/4 to the old 1e-4, three resolutions and no-amplitude fits. For zeroth-mass tangent comparisons use 2*response(m/2)/(1/2)-response(m) on responses expressed per nominal moved fraction; repeat with half and quarter to check scaling. Record both quadratures at switch-off and one quarter fundamental period later. Require error below 2e-5 against magnitude of nonzero predicted signal; for a predicted null use the nonzero single-period signal at the same mass as reference. Test the next radial mode independently, freezing its eigenfunctions, projections and expected sign before evolving. Compare total work to the change in wave energy and account separately for energy outside the first two modes. No new data fit. Original sources stay read-only. If a particular scale fails, retain the residual.
