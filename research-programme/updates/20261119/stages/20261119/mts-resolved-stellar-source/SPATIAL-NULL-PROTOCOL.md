# Three-point test, before evaluating the projected wave

Motivation: the single-point angular force of both star reconstructions exceeds the estimated wave distortion. Test whether the *shape* of a coherent excess can be separated without choosing either stellar normalization. This proposal follows the single-position result; it is not an independently held-out observational test.

Use (R,z)=(6,1.5), (8.3,1.5), (12,1.5) kpc, all at the same height, inside/to either side of the retained near-Sun point; do not optimize these positions, the signs or any weights on a wave or velocity outcome. For both stellar templates independently calculate the three outward-upward gravity combinations T=g_z+(z/R)g_in. Use the unique unit vector w perpendicular to both template vectors, with first nonzero component positive. Freeze it first. Test w·T on the already computed full-strength calibrated and fixed-occupation wave states and spherical comparators. Spherical potentials drop out pointwise; any single globally weighted linear combination of these two stellar templates drops out across the three points. New shapes, gas, measurement uncertainties, non-axisymmetry and non-spherical comparison halos are not covered. Retain a small or unresolved result; do not search for better positions after seeing it. A rotation curve alone cannot measure this statistic.
