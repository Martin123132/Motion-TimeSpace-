# Replace resting dust by a supported source without adding a force

This is a separate preparation, not a relabeling of a dust clock. Adopt a spherical ensemble of ordinary collisionless particles on circular geodesics with isotropically distributed orbital planes and both senses of motion. This is the GR Einstein-cluster construction; see [Böhmer & Harko, MNRAS 379, 393 (2007)](https://academic.oup.com/mnras/article/379/1/393/1140594). The calculation here fixes the coordinate baryon count to the same retained b(r)r²dr, not a rotation speed, and adds no scalar-matter force. The orbital distribution is a chosen physical state; one radial density does not prove that a real system has these orbits.

In geometric units 4πG is absorbed in the density. With F=1−2m/r and lapse exp(Φ), the exact ordinary equations are
```
Φ'=m/(r²F),   v²=rΦ'=m/(r−2m),   γ=1/√(1−v²),
ℓ²=r²m/(r−3m),   m'=b r² √F γ,
p_r=0,    p_t=ρ v²/2,     ρ=m'/r².
```
Require r>3m wherever occupied. Exterior is Schwarzschild with time normalized at infinity. Both the radial force equation for particles and the divergence of the directional stresses give the same stationary balance. Keep binding separate when integrating: (m'−N')/N'=(1−2u)/√(1−3u)−1, u=m/r, N'=br². Its weak-field leading term is −u/2. Hence W=−∫ N dN/r, kinetic energy T=−W/2, M−N=W/2; unlike the resting dust, **half** the Newtonian potential energy is offset by orbital motion. These are ordinary GR/Newton results, not MTS inventions. They are checks on importing the same material profile and correctly counting the energy of supporting it.

## Add the inherited scalar, keeping particle actions fixed

Fix each specific angular momentum ℓ **from the unoccupied calculation**, do not choose a new circular speed after switching on the scalar. Use polar-areal slicing locally. On the initial slice, each particle has zero radial velocity. Its proper-time areal acceleration is
```
d²r/dτ_particle² = −(1+ℓ²/r²)(m/r²+r p_r) + F ℓ²/r³.
```
It vanishes in the ordinary equilibrium. For the perturbed slice the difference is exactly −(1+3ℓ²/r²)Δm/r²−(1+ℓ²/r²)r p_scalar. This is a scalar-on minus scalar-off comparison for the **same particle action**, not the dust four-corner acceleration. In polar-areal slicing a nonzero initial scalar momentum flux requires a radial extrinsic curvature from the momentum constraint (K^r_r=r S_r with this density convention); it need not be set to zero and does not enter this instantaneous circular-particle acceleration. No time evolution of the occupied supported cluster is asserted here.

Let d=Δm/z, F_* the occupied radial factor, F_b the unoccupied one and θ the specified initial oscillator phase: H_phys=√z H cosθ, Π_phys=−√z H sinθ (unit scalar mass in this clock scaling). The mass equation at fixed ℓ is
```
d' = r²(H²+F_* H'² cos²θ)/2 − 2rbγ d/(√F_*+√F_b).
p_scalar/z = [(1−2cos²θ)H²+F_* H'²cos²θ]/2.
```
It yields d≥0. At kinetic phase the added pressure and enclosed mass both pull inward in this acceleration. At the field turning phase the negative radial pressure can dominate, and here it does. For an equal-phase population of **alternative preparations**, to first stress the mass-potential pressure cancels on averaging and the gradient pressure is positive. The finite phase-panel calculation checks this cancellation including metric loading. It is not evidence of chosen phase coherence or a measured time average for a single evolving body.
