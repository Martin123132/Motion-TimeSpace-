# An open channel is not a fitted bound population

The first low-resolution mean-bar solutions put the absorption energy above escape. We therefore extend the original frozen protocol before reading any driven-wave force. Use physical outgoing Coulomb propagation for the positive-frequency sideband, vacuum decay for its negative-frequency partner and a decaying gravitational potential. No reflecting-wall resonance, fitted damping, forcing duration or occupation. The input is only the complex m=2 coefficient of the same rotating stellar bar. Compute an outgoing steady *linear* response on the relaxed mean field. Compare external forcing alone to the coupled, linearized Schrödinger–Poisson equations; distinguish local coherent force, outgoing flux and any secular change in the mean.

In the parent dimensionless units write v± for the radial (r times) perturbations per normalized angular harmonic, taking the minus variable as the conjugate counter-rotating amplitude, and w=r δΦ for the m=2 gravitational coefficient. With the normalized mirror-even associated Legendre basis, L_l=l(l+1), B_ij=<Y_i (u0/r) Y_j>, F_i=<Y_i u0 V2>, and V_ij=<Y_i Φ_total Y_j>, the driven radial equations are
v±'' = [L/r² + 2(V−ε0∓ω)] v± + 2 B w + 2 F,
w'' = Lw/r² + B(v+ + v−).
Here ω=2κΩ/v_unit². There is no factor of two in the complex Fourier coefficient of the linearized density; the real field is reconstructed as twice its real part. Ground normalization fixes the Poisson coupling. The first-order perturbation changes no net number after azimuthal integration. A first-order coherent force is not a phase-averaged excess rotation curve.

Compare coupled results on independent radial and angular meshes and move the outgoing boundary, retaining the 2e−5 acceptance threshold. Check the potential independently from the induced density with the interior/exterior Poisson Green function; check integrated source work against escaping wave number current. Quantify the fractional perturbation in the occupied interior; do not extrapolate a steady mass flux to a Galaxy age without a history. The full bar also has m≥4; their direct gravity is included in the direct readout, not falsely attributed to the induced m=2 response.
