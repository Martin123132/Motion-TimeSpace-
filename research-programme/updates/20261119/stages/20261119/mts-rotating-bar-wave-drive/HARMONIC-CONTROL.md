# Which part of the bar drives the response?
The m=2 and m=4 calculations at 33.29 km/s/kpc are already available. Before evaluating m≥6, use the *same* 44th-degree source with all its stored even m≤32, not a new source model. Use the reference and positive added-mass endpoint (the most helpful sampled ordinary-material change), with the high and far radial discretizations at this speed. No search over phase or amplitude. Treat a failed high-m current or grid check as a resolution limit, not evidence for zero flux. Report aggregate direct and induced polar/radial forces separately from per-mode relative convergence; report m>32 as outside the source bank. Do not equate a zero first-order azimuthal mean with absence of second-order redistribution. The external speed scan remains an m=2 scan.
