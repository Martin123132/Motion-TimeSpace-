## What reached the sky

At R=6 kpc, z=+1.5 kpc, on the more favorable stellar background, the northward-probe polar component starts at 0.45638 in units of 1e-10 m/s². Across the six histories the satellite's direct differential gravity contributes -0.00297 to -0.00209, and the wave it induces in the lowest live m=2 mode contributes -0.000586 to -0.000442. The subtotal is 0.45282–0.45385: the existing observational band starts at 0.57340. At R=8.3 kpc the polar subtotal remains inside its band. The matched static NFW controls also miss the inner polar band and both north radial bands. The control contains the same direct satellite gravity, not a simulated collisionless halo wake. Separate south, radial and azimuthal results are in present-sky.csv; forces are not reflected copies of the actual LMC field.

The problem is not just the magnitude of the forcing. At the same observing azimuth the polar and radial forces of this one even mode are both fixed multiples of the same real mode displacement. For **all twelve orbit/background combinations** the required intervals do not intersect. For example, on the favorable L2M10 background the inner north polar band requires a coordinate of at least +2.57, while the inner south radial band requires at most -3.95. The predicted coordinate is -0.0101. Even the inner polar requirement alone would extrapolate the first-order sideband norm beyond the ground norm. This is an obstruction for this mode on these foregrounds, not a bound on all live modes or arbitrary baryonic changes.

There is also a source-independent shape test. Let E=(a_theta,north-a_theta,south)/2 at common height magnitude h. For any *additional constant tidal tensor*, r E/(R h)=Tzz-Txx at both radii. The necessary intervals are disjoint for both MTS and static NFW even allowing arbitrary tensor amplitude and orientation. On the favorable MTS subtotal, any differentiable additional even polar field must change this scaled quantity between 6 and 8.3 kpc by at least about 31.6 (km/s)²/kpc², or attain a radial derivative magnitude of at least 13.7 (km/s)²/kpc³ somewhere between them. Applied to the specified local bands, not a joint statistical confidence statement. A pure bending field cannot supply the required even part.

## Calculation and uncertainty

The field uses all 49 real harmonic coefficients through satellite degree six (bound material plus stripped material), cubic Hermite reconstruction using published values and radial derivatives, linear mixing in the satellite frame between snapshots, and Hermite interpolation of the relative orbit. A constant and uniform acceleration have zero m=2 projection; direct solar-probe forces explicitly subtract the force at the Galactic centre. Kernel convolution uses spherical Hankel transforms and Gaunt products; an independent rotated position-space quadrature checks it. The complex drive already carries the azimuthal phase. Evolve c''+sigma² c=-2d in parent time; read the present forced position and velocity without switching off the encounter. Source-native time is 0.97779222 Gyr per unit. The projected density of the normalized mode and the saved ground reproduce the induced gravity through the Poisson Green function. Full lookback zero-added-state results are conditional; alternative start times, instantaneous-equilibrium initial data and monopole-only histories are retained separately.

Numerical controls: final phase-space changes below 9.4e-8 across all 136 matched cases under grid/time/harmonic refinement; independently integrated high-resolution full-history ODEs agree within 1.55e-8. Four refined independent spatial samples agree with the high-resolution full drive within 6.1e-8 relative. Earlier underresolved angular checks are preserved rather than counted as passes. Analytic and finite-difference direct accelerations agree within 4.6e-11. These are checks of the specified calculation, not the uncertainty of the physical source. Full-history final states change 0.48–0.88% with alternative radial Hermite interpolation and 3.02–4.49% if alternate shape snapshots are omitted. The present local tidal-tensor shortcut misestimates the mode drive by 20–29%. No AGAMA binary equivalence, complete live response, formation occupation or new fit is claimed.
