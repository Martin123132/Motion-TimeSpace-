# Inputs, exposure and meaning of the measurements

- **Classical Cepheids:** Feng, Huang, Zhang & Liu, [Table 1, arXiv:2512.21780v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.21780v1#S3.T1). The 12 data triples in `cepheid-table1.csv` are radius (kpc), inferred circular speed and its bootstrap width (km/s). Source checked against the displayed Table 1; errors from 100 bootstrap samples, not total uncertainties. Use 17.58 kpc only for the calibration; the other 11 bins were previously exposed, never used in this solver or to choose its particle pole. Young stellar kinematics, assumed Galactic frame, disk disequilibrium and correlated systematics matter. Equal-bin RMSE is on reported central values, not on raw stars.
- **Vertical red clump:** Hagen & Helmi, [paper and abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.09291), [journal PDF](https://pure.rug.nl/ws/portalfiles/portal/63445140/aa32903_18.pdf), §3.2, Eq. (5), Fig. 7. Thin/thick TGAS×RAVE estimates at local R=8.3 kpc, |z|=1.5 kpc: −2454±619 / −2141±774 (km/s)²/kpc; adopted stellar tracer heights 0.28 / 1.12 kpc. Comparison uses the actual downward force, not a claimed observed density. These are two inferences from one study. Different tracers from the calibration point; frozen prediction before consulting this result in this stage, not proof of programme-wide non-exposure. Source frame and tracer histories are not identical to those of the Cepheids; common Galactic foreground was itself informed by older data.
- **Secondary older-star test:** Eilers, Hogg, Rix & Ness, [original Table 1 and systematics](https://arxiv.org/html/1810.09466). `eilers-table1.csv` contains all 38 four-number rows of Table 1, 5.27–24.82 kpc, in published order: radius, speed, lower and upper errors. Used exact model evaluation at the tabulated radii, not interpolation of Cepheid residuals. These positions are off the original forecast mesh; both states stayed fixed. APOGEE/Gaia red giant branch, different tracer and release but shared Galaxy, astrometry and reference-frame issues. They quote 2–5% systematics out to ~20 kpc and larger uncertainty beyond. No fit; no joint probability with the other data. Secondary addition after the primary forecast; prior project exposure not audited.
- **Local kinematic interpretation:** Bovy, [Galactic rotation in Gaia DR1, §§4–5](https://arxiv.org/html/1610.07610). Main-sequence TGAS field gives -(A+B)=−3.4±0.6 km/s/kpc but also nonzero C and K. Its author explicitly cautions against equating local stellar streaming with the axisymmetric circular-speed derivative. This contextual check was not a primary discriminator and is not scored; overlaps Gaia systematics with the other data.
- **Model inputs:** `../mts-solar-differential-acceleration-20261119/{disk_state.py,disk_tangent.py,potential.py,disk_readout.py,seal.json}`; its saved disk-loaded states and their upstream physical-unit packets are reused. This is the ordinary bulge and disk of the [MWPotential2014 reference](https://arxiv.org/abs/1412.3451) plus the separately retained compact population; the model includes inferred astrophysical inputs. Older galaxy-lab empirical kernels and binary timing outcomes are not inputs. No claim of a blind catalogue competition.
