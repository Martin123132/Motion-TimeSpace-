# The same field phase, two different answers to the drift experiment

**Prediction tested.** At scalar phase π/4, compare a material shell starting from radial rest against one starting at the velocity derived from the periodic solution. Same positive quadratic parent pole, scalar amplitude, orbital angular momenta, particle counts, radial grid, time grid and matching tolerance. The 0.1 GeV/cm³ normalization at 40 pc is the old illustrative one, not a Galactic fit. At launch the scalar density and pressure on its mean metric equal their phase averages, but the scalar flux is maximal rather than zero. Both starts leave that flux intact. The finite calculation includes the tiny change in the initial mass constraint sourced by radial kinetic energy. That energy is physical; the two states do not have exactly the same total mass.

| At 20.01 pc | Radial rest | Derived periodic velocity |
|---|---:|---:|
| Initial radial coordinate speed in m/s | 0 | +3.1701235e−5 |
| Frozen one-cycle displacement | −68,385.672 m | 0 m to first order |
| Finite displacement at frozen period | −68,385.672 m | −1.28e−8 m |
| Change in the latter calculation on mesh / time sampling combined | 1.33e−8 m | 1.32e−8 m |
| Displacement at the actual pressure return | −68,385.672 m | −1.11e−8 m |

The matched shell makes a roughly **10.88 km excursion** and comes back; the signed nanometre remnant is comparable to its numerical uncertainty. All 12 frozen endpoint checks and 36 trajectory/flux comparisons pass at the unchanged 2e−5 oscillation-scale tolerance. The actual pressure-return marker shifts by only ~55 microseconds of central coordinate time and gives an orbital proper period of 68.35731 years. Neither outcome is a long-term simulation. The sixth radius is an unoccupied tracer, not another baryon detection.

**Dynamics, not a preferred initial condition by decree.** The response has a calculable particular solution and an undamped homogeneous epicycle. At 20.01 pc the linear epicycle has a 16.65 million-year period. During the simulated cycle the free energy of the rest start varies by only 3.1e−12 of its initial value; the matched start excites essentially none on that scale. A finite-time retarded-source calculation explains when a previously resting shell would actually approach the matched velocity: the *rate of assembly* must have little Fourier weight near the two driving sidebands. A smooth 64-cycle force build gives 1.9 mm predicted next-cycle drift. A monotone build of identical duration with 50% modulation of its rate gives 17.10 km. These are independently integrated examples, not reconstructed halo formation histories. See DERIVATION.md.

**Which clock sees the distinction?** All 72 original single-preparation/frequency/delay checks pass. In the naive subtraction between the two simulations, 12/36 contrast checks fail on their own tiny scale. We retained those rows. A new calculation instead evolves the contrast directly in the fully finite field equations and tests clocks in two different time coordinates (lapse contrast anchored at radii 6 and 6.5), with a separate half-time-step evolution. The change to the held inner echo delay is −34.49 femtoseconds; the middle change is −0.603 femtoseconds. At the exterior receiver the leading change is **exactly zero**: at first order the exterior mass has not moved and the spatially constant lapse shift is only a change of time coordinate. A separate null-ray integral proves this cancellation. The finite calculation gives an exterior echo-delay contrast of ~1.00e−27 s, many orders below the total signal, numerically stable to these grid, gauge, ray and time checks. This last number is conditional on the classical equations/occupations and is not a measurable clock prediction. The combined finite own-signal checks pass 10/12; the tiny inner and middle launch-referenced echo **frequency** differences remain outside budget. Neither the leading zero nor an accurately computed total certifies those two coefficients.

**Outer-Galaxy data.** The predecessor's frozen single-mode mass ceiling was compared to ordinary matter at matched apertures. The heavier 100-kpc tracer estimates leave a substantial deficit even when we credit generous stars, cold gas, and the *entire* 250-kpc X-ray hot-gas allowance. The low historical stream mass need not leave a deficit. Radio dispersion gives a separate profile-dependent shell test, not another reservoir to add to the X-ray gas. No raw dynamical refit or formal model exclusion. See ORDINARY-MATTER.md.

**Next physics:** replace the illustrative net-force turn-on functions with a conserved perturbation of the parent wave. A radial eigenmode of the coupled Schrödinger–Poisson ground state has a density perturbation, potential perturbation and a definite forcing frequency; check self-gravity and the response of the material cluster, not merely a second test wave in a prescribed potential. This can derive a physical source spectrum. Its occupation and history would still require formation information.
