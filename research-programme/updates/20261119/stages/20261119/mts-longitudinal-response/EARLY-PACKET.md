# Independent early-time coefficient
For W[j₀(9r)−j₀(15r)] both the central scalar shift and its velocity vanish at launch, so the central material density has no quadratic response from this seed. Nevertheless ∂seed ḧ(0)=144√n from the radial curvature. With F=6h₂−h₀ the full minimal, constrained equations predict
    ∂seed ln ν(t) = 12√n(4F−h₀)t⁴+O(t⁶).
The leading term is independent of η; it is not a light travel time or an empirical fit. Retain the exact coefficient from the launch regardless of the outcome of numerical extraction. Compare with fixed even-in-time polynomial extrapolation of the ratio X(t)/t⁴ from dyadic times; different time ranges check sensitivity to early roundoff and finite higher terms.
