# What wavelengths does this calculation actually cover?

**Inherited control, not a new UV completion.** The earlier 4878 strict derivative expansion is at spatial angular wavenumber q=k/L for a slow longitudinal mode (|ω|≪k). Its two insertion sizes are |12a_R (m_h/M_R)² k²| and |4a_C (m_h/M_R)² k²|. Do not check the coefficients at a fifth-force experiment's length and then use that pass for arbitrarily short waves. In particular, the large-code-coefficient ±4 trials are useful for exposing characteristics of the truncated equations, not evidence of a physical instability inside this corridor. Interpretation of coefficients as extra resummed particles is a different branch.

At |d₀|,|d₂|≤0.01 and retained η=.8, the first-order material-gravity gradient can change the inward Newton term by ≤1/60. The leading baseline ω²/n=−0.36, **not zero**. Including the charged intercept and allowing the massive field to respond, the non-k² correction is bounded by 6.79e−52 in code ω² at the earlier coefficient caps. The baseline value is −2.53378e−4, and the k-dependent part can move it by at most 1.174e−5. Thus neither a reversal nor a rapid extra collapse follows at this order. This is an initial central statement; the short clock run does not measure an infinite-time exponential growth rate. The force-balanced η=1 control is a real exception to the dominance argument, not a fitted change to η.

**Compute the proper correlated cap.** The largest |γ₁+γ₂| allowed by the *same two* rectangular a_R,a_C control caps is 8(|a_R|+4|a_C|/3)(m_h/M_R)² = 2.1270e−45. Separately maximizing |γ₁| and |γ₂| gives 3.8286e−45, a safe but unattainable simultaneous extreme. At the jointly saturated a_R,a_C the one-percent condition requires wavelength ≥0.3267 mm for the example L=6.6713 pc. Smaller coefficients can move this boundary; 0.3267 mm is not a universal cutoff. The dimensionless 1/60 transfer bound applies without fixing the magnitudes separately.

At that same joint extreme, extrapolating the slow frozen mode to one growth time in Δτ=3.24 would require λ≈3.63 μm and |d₀|,|d₂|≈81, not 0.01. Actual **e-fold growth of the amplitude from rest** requires cosh(sΔτ)=e, hence ≈2.19 μm. Neither extrapolation is licensed by the reduced equations. In contrast, within the corridor the largest frozen-rate product is 0.0528 versus the baseline 0.0516. This argument does not exclude unrelated instabilities, nonlocal operators or later growth in an evolving background.

**Independent material check.** In these units the central equivalent mass density associated with the ordinary rest-number component alone is ρₘ=c²n/(4πGL²)=1.78e−12 kg/m³. A coarse-grained continuum needs many constituents in a wavelength volume; its count is N_λ=ρₘλ³/m_constituent. At 3.63 μm the mass of the entire wavelength cube is 8.52e−29 kg. If the rest mass is borne by ordinary baryonic constituents of at least one proton mass, that is ≤0.051 constituents per cube; the one-per-cube spacing is already 9.80 μm. The reference mass is from [NIST/CODATA, 2022 recommended constants](https://www.physics.nist.gov/cuu/pdf/wall_2022.pdf). This is a conditional count, not an identification of the charge carriers or a measured collision length. The geometric charge-to-mass ratio of this .8 fixture converts to 6.89e−11 C/kg; it is not a free proton's ratio. No stellar gas temperature, species or microscopic occupancy is specified by these launch files.

**What a velocity measurement could supply.** In a nonrelativistic local collisionless waterbag, the two sharp velocity edges obey ∂t v±+v±∂x v±=F. Density is proportional to v+−v−. Linearizing at v±=±u gives a k² contribution +u²k² exactly for this family, with variance u²/3. To leading order in width and contact the total short-wave coefficient is u²−(γ₁+γ₂)n/2. No width is required or fitted for the cold calculation. At the example caps the largest compensating half-width would be 2.60e−16 m/s. This conditional calculation is not a hydrodynamic equation of state for an arbitrary galaxy or for Standard Model matter. The cold fixture sets width to zero; applications to actual materials require an independent state or stress correlation, rather than adding this width as a convenient stabilizer.
