# Calendar transfer, not a sky fit

Use the existing pinned EPTA DR2full J1713+0747 calendar only: integer observation day, instrument group, and counts, stored in mts-two-radius-pressure-clock-20261119. Read no measured residuals or timing uncertainties. Apply it to the new simulated ONE-WAY delay excess; an ideal reflected echo is not a pulsar timing measurement. Map the dates to quiet ordinary arrival times t_emit+0.5+D_quiet(t_emit). Attach the finite delay of the same emitted pulse to that reference arrival, as in an injection. Do not treat data on unrelated real lines of sight as if they were the three simulated radial links.

Use three predetermined placements (start at 0%, 50% and 100% of the slack between the experiment and calendar durations). Do not select the best alignment. Label them early, middle and late. Carry both material signs on the retained full-mass case, and the positive event on the two lower ordinary masses. With equal weight per tabulated arrival and separately equal weight per observing day, remove offset/linear/quadratic spin terms; then add annual and semiannual sin/cos and instrument jumps as in the archived example. Also project a freely normalized copy of the ordinary material delay (a source-amplitude nuisance, not a physical refit). Run the same operation on an ordinary injection and a combined injection. Report pre/post RMS in SI, attenuation, ordinary-source leakage, and sensitivity to using half the saved emission samples. Keep fits to this synthetic projected template entirely separate from empirical fits to the sky. No noise, observing threshold or evidence for an actual source is inferred.
