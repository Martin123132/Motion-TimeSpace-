## Where the idealization meets observations

- **Galaxy scale:** the original neutral baryonic source *by itself* gives a Newtonian circular speed of **482.4 km/s** at 5.15 kpc. The existing exposed NGC3198 SPARC row is **132.0 ± 4.34 km/s**. It overshoots the central value plus the tabulated error in 43/43 radial bins, without adding an unknown halo. This rules out treating the original isolated source calculation as a standalone description of this galaxy. Exterior disk material can contribute outward force; this is not a confidence limit in a fit allowing other signed terms. The low-mass source lying below the data is not a successful rotation curve either. The independently maintained galaxy lab was not changed. The input and its hash are recorded in observational-input.json; the data release is described by [Lelli, McGaugh & Schombert](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.09251).
- **Actual calendar, synthetic signals:** at the full source mass the positive inner *one-way* excess sampled on the saved [EPTA DR2full J1713+0747 observing calendar](https://gitlab.in2p3.fr/epta/epta-dr2/-/tree/2911d0e52e0c8a4e528c4e3aa46b868ced1910e8/EPTA-DR2/DR2full/J1713%2B0747) yields raw equal-arrival RMS of 9.00–119.74 ps across three predetermined calendar placements. Removing quadratic, annual, semiannual and instrument-offset terms leaves 0.220–0.496 ps. Also allowing a free ordinary-source template amplitude leaves **0.0156–0.2124 ps**. For the millionth-mass cloud the corresponding range is 1.56e−20–2.12e−19 s. These are projections on actual observing dates, not a fit of pulsar data, noise, instrument sensitivity or the actual line of sight.
- **Energy and preparation:** at the smallest mass, moving between the two *resting* ordinary neutral configurations of the positive event would require a change of asymptotic energy of **+3.023e38 J**. At η=.8 it is +1.088e38 J. The change is calculable from the gravitational binding and electric self-energy; the η=1 spherical dust limit has exactly zero exterior mass change. Number/charge conservation alone does not pay this bill. Even ignoring the energy difference, some mass shells would have to move outwards while others move inwards. Isolated positive-mass neutral dust released from rest contracts instead. At this small mass it takes **about 115,541 years** for even the central density to rise 1% by that mechanism, not the approximately 74 years covered by the clock emissions. These do not invalidate the comparison of possible initial conditions; they invalidate interpreting it as an unforced transition between them.

Do not assume an unexplained 1% rearrangement occurred during an observing campaign.
