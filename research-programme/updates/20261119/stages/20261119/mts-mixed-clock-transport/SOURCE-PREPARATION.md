# Can the prescribed event be a later state of the original cloud?

The clock comparison allows two different initial configurations. That is not the same assertion as an isolated body passing between those configurations. In the ordinary control both slices are time-symmetric: same total number and charge, zero material velocity, all ordinary material inside r=5. Outside that radius the difference in asymptotic mass equals the enclosed mass difference, since the exterior electric term Q²/(2r) is identical. In spherical electrovacuum with no external energy flux that total mass cannot change. A nonzero ΔADM is therefore an energy-accounting requirement for a real preparation, irrespective of the success of the clock calculation. The signed value is a difference of total energies, **not** a dissipated power, scalar-wave emission or an unconditional lower bound on the work an arbitrary astrophysical environment must do.

Let N(r)=∫b r²dr and δN(r) be the analytic compact redistribution; Q=ηN and δQ=ηδN for this fixture. To first binding order in total source mass at fixed size, m′=r² b[1−m/r+…]+Q²/(2r²). After cancelling the zero exterior δN and integrating by parts,

    ΔM_Newton+electric = −(1−η²) ∫ (N+δN/2) δN dr/r².

This is gravitational binding **and** electric self-energy. It is quadratic in overall baryon normalization and does not need a fitted compensator. Compare it to the exact nonlinear constraints by solving for Δm−δN so the leading zero is not manufactured by cancellation. For η=1 the exact solution m=N−N²/(2r) on each slice gives zero exterior difference at every mass, providing a stringent null distinct from the finite η=.8 fixture. For η=0 this reduces to Newtonian binding; the retained scalar has not replaced the metric coupling.

There is an independent obstacle to interpreting the prepared input as unforced neutral dust collapse. Before shell crossing an initially resting positive-mass shell has R̈=−m/R² and subsequently moves inward. Matching the radial baryon-count quantiles on the event and quiet slices instead requires both inward and outward changes of areal radius for this k=4 event. Also the new slice prescribes zero velocities. A time-symmetric density event is a legitimate alternative initial condition, **not** the spontaneous evolution of that same isolated resting dust. To test the timescale rather than guessing: at the homogeneous centre R/r=cos²θ, τ=(θ+sinθ cosθ)/√(2b₀/3). Central compression by 1% fixes θ=acos(1.01^(−1/6)). No observed preparation time was used. These statements refer to ordinary neutral dust; pressure, injected work, scalar occupation, anisotropy or a different matter state need their own dynamics.

For a future sky-facing prediction, require independent evidence for a source history, not merely a measured radius and a plausible scalar density. A zero-input next control is the actual geodesic contraction itself with its conserved mass and velocity, without the handcrafted ±1% event. In an approximately stationary astronomical cluster, pressure/velocity dispersion may be essential instead of dust collapse.
