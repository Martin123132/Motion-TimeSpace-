# Private numerical replay

Use the existing Python 3.13 runtime with NumPy and SciPy, one numerical thread per process. All input is offline. The original fitted orbit is Erkal et al. 2019, [paper](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stz1371) and [trajectory](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.2661108); see the parent LMC.md for source hashes and conventions. The two wave scales, astrometric covariance, intended linear targets and tolerances were not selected using these finite runs.

**Generation order in a fresh copy.** The initial code and targets are recorded in frozen.json. The first radial-mesh preflight refused to run: peak Gram error about 3.77e-6 at basis size 28 versus a 1e-8 orthogonality gate. Its source is archived byte-for-byte in trial-v1/grid_wave.py. No physical packet was produced by the rejected setup. The corrected quadrature uses a global Gauss–Legendre grid with 4n+32 points. qualified.json binds it and the batch driver to the original frozen targets; do not overwrite either receipt. Rerun batch_wave.py freeze only in a clean reconstruction that already has a copy of frozen.json.

- For each of illustrative and central_cap, batch_wave.py LABEL weak finite angular controls radbox runs the original full histories. propagate.py pilot central_cap jet is the corrected low-resolution control. high_band.py LABEL small-amplitudes adds positive fractions 1/16 and 1/8 to the original quarter, half, full sequence. Zeros in filenames label control forcing; scalar strength inside the archive is authoritative (0062 denotes 0.0625).
- The real/imaginary linearized wave plus live Poisson use a matrix exponential for each spherical degree, including monopole and translational modes, without inverting a zero mode. The source and nonlinear terms use exponential Adams predictor/corrector (four time nodes, fresh force at the corrected state); DOP853 starts the first three steps. The finite equation and second variation are separate evolutions, not coefficients inferred from the amplitude scan.
- The radial wave and Poisson bases satisfy respectively Dirichlet and exterior vacuum matching. The real spherical harmonics are normalized under dΩ/(4π). For an angular wave cutoff L, wave densities and forces use degrees through 2L with angular quadrature sufficient to project fourth-degree products of the wave harmonics. The actual orbit forcing is expanded about its moving direction by a radial Legendre integral split at the exact separation limits; no preferred m=0 frame is imposed. The source potential's coefficient in the adopted harmonic basis is its averaged Legendre projection times the corresponding source-direction harmonic. No redshift, CMB or galaxy fitting.
- High angular runs use the mathematically equivalent Fourier/Legendre transform, checked separately against direct three-dimensional angular sums. high_band.py LABEL initial generates the saved first 3.75 and 4.375 Gyr histories and compares extending the angular representation at those two epochs. Additional configurations use high_band.py LABEL START:DEGREE [core44|outer72]. Raw state, parent hash, initial and final time index, number, center, energy and signed tensor are in each level*.npz; only the angular representation changes at the handover, adding zeros to the newly available channels. The orbit clock and existing components are never reset.
- centre_theorem.py, turning_uniform.py, torque_wave.py, error_budget.py, endpoint.py and state_population.py are the separate diagnostics. Their create option refuses to overwrite tables; without it their checks are read-only. Sizes in basis functions and box radii are dimensionless; the inherited orbit gives kpc and Gyr. Covariance units are an idealized sensitivity to just the quoted change in the Gaia EDR3 solar acceleration, not a measured residual.
- verify_rotating.py checks the receipts and scientific acceptance results. Preserve misses as well as passes. All work stays local; no service, scheduler, GPU or separate agent is needed.
