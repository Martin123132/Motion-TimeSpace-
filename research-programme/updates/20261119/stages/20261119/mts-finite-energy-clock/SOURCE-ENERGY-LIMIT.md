# What normalization actually permits

The earlier 0.1 GeV/cm³ scalar density at code radius 6 is a conditional comparison, not a detection or a universal maximum. Retain the baryons, charge and scalar shape; solve for the quiet scalar amplitude that gives that density on the solved quiet geometry. No galaxy rotation or pulsar data set the amplitude.

**Leading mass surface.** For a coefficient vector p=(ε,c), let L_i and K_ij be the h-profile cross terms and the profile Gram matrix computed with measure r² exp[−∫ᵣ³ x b/√F_ordinary dx]dr and gradient weight F_ordinary. They include binding to the unperturbed baryons. Then the leading scalar-stress change in enclosed mass is L·p+pᵀKp/2. Equal enclosed mass is an ellipse, not an arbitrary injected wave:

    (p+K⁻¹L)ᵀ K (p+K⁻¹L) = LᵀK⁻¹L.
    E ≤ 2 λ_max(G,K) LᵀK⁻¹L   on this quadratic mass surface.

The first equation fixes the allowable region in pulse/shell coefficients; the second bounds the quiet-slice energy norm there. Scale the quiet field by s and L scales by s while K stays fixed; capacity in this limit scales as s². We evaluate the extremum on the one-dimensional ellipse and test individual directions against the *full nonlinear constraint*. A sampled nonlinear maximum is not a certified global maximum. Nor is the quadratic ellipse an exact nonlinear non-existence proof.

**Exact exclusion at the originally requested energy.** At regular, untrapped finite launch with F_e>0, the exact difference in enclosed mass at r=3 is

    ∫₀³ W r² [(F_q v'²+v²)/2 + F_q h'v'+hv] dr,
    W=exp{−∫ᵣ³ x[(h'+v')²+2b/(√F_q+√F_e)]dx}.

If F_q≥F_min and W≥exp(−A), the first term is at least exp(−A)√F_min E. By Cauchy–Schwarz the magnitude of the second term is at most 2√(E E_q,raw), where E_q,raw=∫r²(F_q h'²+h²)dr/2 on the support. This is a necessary constraint, not a fit.

For E=1.70e−11 we bound the exponent **uniformly over both signs and all shell amplitudes at that energy**. On 0.18≤r≤0.22 the shell vanishes and |j₀(9r)−j₀(15r)|>0.30; the value part of the positive energy bounds |ε|. On 2≤r≤2.2 the shell exceeds √n (0.96)⁸ while |u|≤√n(1/18+1/30); the same energy then bounds |c|. The integral representation of j₀ gives |j₀|, |j₀'|≤1. With the explicit smooth cutoff and shell derivatives these coefficient bounds give |v'|≤0.01556. Upper bounds on |h| and |h'| use sums of absolute coefficients on each retained spline piece, not a search for a favorable radial sample. Since b≤n, |Q/r²|≤0.8nr/3, and m_q≤r³ sup(ρ_q)/3, we have F_min>0.9957 on this support. Hence A≤(9/2)[(sup|h'|+sup|v'|)²+2n/√F_min]<0.00744. The positive term is ≥1.6838e−11 while the cross term is ≤6.6240e−12. **Δm(3)>1.02e−11** for the dilute preparation at this input energy. No regular equal-mass solution of this two-profile launch family exists there. This does not exclude other scalar profiles, ambient populations, incoming flux, a different mass pole or external energy deposition.

**A feasible prospective test.** Instead of extrapolating an impossible fixed-mass event, specify E=2.50e−16 in the same code units on the independently normalized dilute quiet solution. Choose the branch continuously connected to c=0 as ε→0 and solve separately for each sign. Compare the entire predicted time, not just its peaks. With the gravitational response expanded about the same ordinary baryon background the earlier five unit stress shapes can be reused exactly at leading stress order: multiply cross terms involving h by s, but keep the positive pulse self-terms at their actual finite amplitudes. No amplitude is rescaled after reading a clock. Freeze both grids and both signs of both preparations before any dilute target. A real, paired nonlinear evolution retains the finite differences of two nearly equal metrics without mistaking numerical subtraction for an absent pulse.

**The exact empty-background limit.** If h=h'=0 initially, the cross term vanishes and W>0: any nonzero regular time-symmetric compact scalar disturbance at unchanged material number and charge has Δm(3)>0. It cannot also be an equal-mass rearrangement. At fixed material number alone a positive packet is allowed, and the scalar sign symmetry makes its gravitational and clock signal entirely sign-even. With no prepared scalar at all the material and electromagnetic equations reduce to their ordinary Einstein–Maxwell sector. This is a result about initial preparation in this retained minimal subfamily; it neither determines a galaxy's occupation nor forbids an external source from supplying energy.
