# The first material-to-scalar coefficient with no inserted scalar pulse

Changing the ordinary mass profile is different from inserting our u,w wave. On a time-symmetric quiet background perturb only the conserved baryon distribution by its saved compensated seed, hold h and its initial normal momentum fixed, and solve the mass and Gauss constraints. In synchronous variables A=a²,C=(R/r)², Θ=K_r/A+2K_t/C, the subsequent scalar variation obeys the inhomogeneous linearized Klein–Gordon equation with zero scalar initial data. Its forcing is

    −(δA/A) Δ_sp h + h'/A [∂r(δC/C)−∂r(δA/A)/2] + δΘ ∂t h.

These are metric sources already in the action, not a new coupling. If h vanishes identically, there is no classical scalar excitation from them. For nonzero occupation this predicts a response without selecting the outgoing scalar shape or its amplitude.

**Local derivation.** Write h=h₀+h₂r²+O(r⁴), A=1+a₂r²+O(r⁴), C=1 at launch. Then ḧ₀=6h₂−h₀ is unchanged by the matter seed. At order r² in ḧ, δḧ₂=−8h₂ δa₂ and the mass constraint gives δa₂=2δn₀/3. Regularity and the two spatial Einstein equations give δÄ₀=−2δn₀/3 and δΘ̇₀=δn₀. Differentiating the central KG equation twice gives δh⁽⁴⁾₀=6δḧ₂−6h₂δÄ₀+2δΘ̇₀(6h₂−h₀)=−2δn₀(h₀+8h₂). Thus

    δh₀(t)/√n = −δn₀(h₀+8h₂)t⁴/(12√n) + O(t⁶).

For the stored material launch δn₀=n per seed unit. This leading coefficient depends neither on the seed wavenumber nor on charge per unit mass; both can matter later. Compare it to the previously retained *matter-only* massive trajectories at k=1,4,24 and η=0,.8, not the scalar-driven clock trials. Use the 160 and 320 radial grids, the same 2e−5 criterion, and two fixed four-point extrapolations at t=(2,3,4,5)×0.01 and t=(2,3,4,5)×0.02 of [δh(t)−δh(0)]/t⁴ versus t² to t=0. Preserve any radius-resolution/cancellation miss. This is a retrospective independent coefficient check, not a new observational holdout or a derivation of the body's original baryonic motion.
