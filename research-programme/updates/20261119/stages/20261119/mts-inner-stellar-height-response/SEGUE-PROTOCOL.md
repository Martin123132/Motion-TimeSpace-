# Cross-check the frozen states on different tracers
Use the published Bovy–Rix ApJ 779:115 SEGUE G-dwarf abundance-population measurements (Table 3 journal, arXiv:1309.0809v3 source file surf.txt). The quantity to score is |Kz(R,1.1 kpc)|/(2πG), *not* actual surface density and *not* the Gaia spherical polar component at 1.5 kpc. The table's distance convention holds R0−R fixed; use R0=8.122 kpc, and retain the native R0=8 as a sensitivity check. Keep all published force rows and their printed errors. No new fit, radius choice, ground-state adjustment, halo parameter tuning, rotation-slope correction to the measurements or selection on performance. Compare old height versus new pooled height for the pre-existing ground, spherical NFW with the same one outer speed, and opposite-rotation endpoint. Also compare fixed-number/fixed-amplitude controls for the altered height. Compute signed bias, RMS and count inside individual reported errors, overall and split at the predetermined physical R=6.5 kpc (on the primary distance convention). Diagonal standardized errors are descriptions, not a covariance-aware likelihood. Do not double count the table's correlated surface densities, or count 43 radius bins as 43 galaxies. Separate tracer survey, pre-Gaia, but historical exposure is not exhaustively established and the published forces themselves depend on equilibrium/DF assumptions. Models and source scenarios already fixed. A conditional model may fail both surveys; a win at a different radius is also a valid result.
