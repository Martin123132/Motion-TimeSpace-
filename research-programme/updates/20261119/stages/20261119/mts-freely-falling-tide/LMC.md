# External inputs, comparison, and exposure

- [Erkal et al. 2019, Section 4 and Appendix A](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stz1371); [authors' best-fit release](https://zenodo.org/records/2661108). No stream particle file was needed. This is a **fitted dynamical reconstruction**, not 5,001 independent measurements. Source metadata identifies the prolate, responding host model. Its Appendix A1 best-fit total LMC mass is 12.657 × 10¹⁰ M☉, not the posterior median of 1.38 × 10¹¹. The paper uses a Hernquist profile with 1.7 × 10¹⁰ M☉ enclosed within 8.7 kpc, giving scale 15.03888 kpc for this total. Do not interpret all of this gravitating mass as measured baryons.
- Use time column 0→5 Gyr for increasing physical time; the archive rows themselves run backwards, with the present first. Use LMC **minus moving MW** position and velocity. Differentiating all interior positions verifies the velocities. At the current endpoint the relative distance is 51.5734 kpc, outward radial speed 71.11 km/s and transverse speed 346.60 km/s. These are this fitted track, not HST central measurements. For orientation, [Kallivayalil et al., Table 5](https://doi.org/10.1088/0004-637X/764/2/161) report 64 ± 7 and 314 ± 24 km/s. Similar data constrain the published fit; no independent validation is inferred. The 5-Gyr history uses its own mass model at all times, not an independently measured satellite mass-loss history.
- Carry the two existing wave normalizations separately without fitting occupation to the orbit. In each, compute retarded and instantaneous tensors on the same source. Start at archive times 0, 2.5, and 4 Gyr with either vanishing perturbation or static initial deformation at rest. These are sensitivity cases, not a probability distribution on formation states. The tabulated phase and amplitude assume no additional freely prepared present-day mode.
- [Gaia Collaboration, EDR3 solar-system acceleration, Table 2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.02036): Galactic components (+5.04, −0.10, −0.29) microarcsec/yr with quoted bootstrap standard errors (0.35, 0.36, 0.41) and correlations (XY +0.036, XZ −0.014, YZ −0.079). Use the covariance only for detectability. Compute the full induced acceleration from the angular gradient and radial Green integral; independent derivatives at displaced Cartesian positions agree. At the Sun (negative Galactocentric x in the archived convention), +x points to the Galactic center, +y in the rotation direction and +z north. The astrometric dipole vector in radians/s is acceleration/c. For the central-cap state at 8.1 kpc the induced components are (+0.007734, +0.001923, +0.000947) microarcsec/yr. No adjustment to the total Milky Way acceleration or fit to Gaia data. Source orbit, Cepheid calibration, and Gaia acceleration precision are now explicitly **exposed** here.
- The proper-clock contrast in the tables is the instantaneous extrema difference for hypothetical stationary equal-radius clocks in the freely falling host frame. Directions need not coincide with the satellite. It is not an actual clock observation or light-path time series. The solar glide is a separate vector observable, not obtained by dividing that contrast by an arbitrary distance.
