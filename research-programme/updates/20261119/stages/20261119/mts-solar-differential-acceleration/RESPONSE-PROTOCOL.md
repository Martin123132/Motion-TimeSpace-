# Disk and occupation sensitivity, not recalibration

Before varying the external field or particle number, differentiate the stationary Euler equation at each fixed solution. Test the constrained tangent separately for a common fractional change of disk+bulge normalization and a fractional change of the wave mass at fixed boson pole. Include the gravitational response of the wave; do not multiply an already-solved force curve by a mass factor. Compare the analytic derivatives of the equatorial wave and total circular speed squared, ground-state energy and chemical potential against symmetric nearby *control solves*. Use ±1/128 fractional steps and halve the step for discrepancies where needed. These are not fits or replacements for either supplied occupation. Report absolute and relative errors, physical signs and near-zero failures. No changes to the timing protocol or acceleration-data access.
