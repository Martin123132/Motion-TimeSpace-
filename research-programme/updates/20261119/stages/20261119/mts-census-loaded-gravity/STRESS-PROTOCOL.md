# Wave stress test, before evaluating the census states
Use the saved nominal, fixed and outer-matched states of both stellar sources. Do not fit a pressure or boundary term. Compare the metric stress on the plane to the wave's independent integrated weight in the upper half-space and its finite spherical wall traction. Compare each Cartesian tensor-virial projection (vertical and transverse trace) and integrated Hilbert trace to the spectral kinetic moment. Repeat numerical quadrature; report residuals and test against the inherited relative accuracy target 2e-5 separately. A failed continuum balance in a stationary projected solution is useful evidence, not grounds for loosening the target. This is a leading nonrelativistic, period-averaged stress, not a pressure term to add to the Newtonian force. Both original source geometries, calibrations and finite volume are retained.

Additional independent-observable check: predict the secular-aberration amplitude and Galactic components at R=8.122, z=0.0208 kpc for both states and the same spherical control. Use the published Gaia EDR3 quasar dipole amplitude 5.05 +/-0.35 microarcsec/yr as a distinct measurement method (not claimed blind, no rotation tuning here). Use the original stellar/gas ring integration and independent quadrature refinement. Axisymmetric force only, negligible assumed extra-Galactic/differential acceleration; compare amplitudes, not significance of a joint Gaia score. The true full barycentre acceleration includes any external field. Quantify the smooth Galactic model only.
