# A finite forcing, without an assumed Galactic bar age
Start from the 294-file sealed rotating-bar stage, and use the same baryons, wave mass/kinetic coefficient, fitted outer anchor, pattern speed (33.29 km/s/kpc), angular/radial solar and inner comparison sites. Do not change the source bank, fit the pulse, or tune a halo occupation. Keep the azimuthal mean of the stellar bar in the background throughout. For positive elapsed time, multiply only its rotating harmonics by sin⁴(πt/T) for 0<t<T and zero otherwise, with T one full stellar-pattern turn (not one m=2 oscillation). This is a specified response history, not a measured bar lifetime. Start first and second variations at zero. Test the leading m=2 harmonic first; distinct |m| contributions to second order in the azimuthal mean add, but uncomputed harmonics must not be silently included. Use the reference and positive added-mass endpoint with the same pulse. Save during the pulse, at switch-off, and for another full turn unforced. A steady outgoing field truncated by hand cannot replace a retarded solution.

Calculate the m=0 density from sideband intensity AND ground deformation, including mean self-gravity. Keep separate the spherical and nonspherical multipoles. Compare shell and total number, external torque, work during amplitude change, time and space discretization, and outer boundary sensitivity. An identically zero nonaxisymmetric drive must reproduce zero perturbation. Keep the previous 2e-5 numerical tolerance as a relative *scale*, with units and denominator explicit for cancellations and zeros. Inspect the observed residual only after forming the response; explicitly label failures and do not normalize a wave to fill a force deficit.
