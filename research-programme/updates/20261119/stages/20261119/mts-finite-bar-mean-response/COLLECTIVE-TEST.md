# Independent live prediction before opening the mode result

The frozen one-body projection changes after switch-off in the live histories. Test whether it is mixing distinct modes rather than evidence for continued production: derive the *coupled* normal mode and predict its amplitude from the same finite pulse, without reading the evolved amplitudes into the prediction. Use the lowest mirror-even m=2 frequency first and report its localization, stability and box sensitivity. Check its exact complex amplitudes against the saved fields at both switch-off and one turn later. Freeze one-, two-, three-, five- and ten-turn predictions for that same mode, not a fit to a selected force. Distinguish collective action from particle mass. Positive definiteness is only to be certified on the actual discrete m-sector or separately proved in the continuum; an isolated bound-mode calculation is not a proof of a fundamental mass gap.
