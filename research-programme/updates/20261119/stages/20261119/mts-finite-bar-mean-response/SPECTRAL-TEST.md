# A bound-state test distinct from finding mass at finite radius
Do not identify the sideband intensity inside an aperture with bound occupation. At switch-off and one pattern turn later project the actual live amplitudes onto the stationary one-body Hamiltonian of the matched mean. For the low modes keep the first eight mirror-even eigenstates in m=2 by energy before looking at the overlaps; record every state. Flag localization independently: negative specific energy more than 8% of the ground binding AND probability outside 12 parent length units below 10⁻⁵. Check the surviving subspace by extending the outer radius with the inner mesh fixed. This is a diagnostic basis for the live self-gravitating calculation, not a proof that those modes are eternal or complete. The entire finite-pulse sideband mass is an unconditional upper bound on the norm of its projection onto *any* orthogonal bound subspace at that epoch.

With dynamic self-gravity switched off, the same projections at switch-off have an analytic amplitude: the Fourier transform of the prescribed sin⁴ envelope at the relative detuning, times the bar matrix element, including the phase. Compare a separately integrated frozen-field control to this formula, not just a flux identity. A one-turn pulse has finite spectral width (sin⁴ itself contains a second pattern harmonic); any low-frequency loading is conditional on this history. No pulse parameters are chosen using these results.
