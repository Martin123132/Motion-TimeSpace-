# Ordinary matter to clocks: a sourced excess, with the material cost made explicit

**Result.** Start the charged or neutral material at rest with the same compensated density redistribution, without changing the initial scalar field. On the retained occupied background its subsequent clock response differs from the corresponding ordinary Einstein–Maxwell material response. The prediction uses the actual driven field, not a pulse template. At the independently normalized dilute occupation, a finite positive material event gives an inner round-trip arrival **0.39854 ns earlier** than the event-minus-quiet change expected from ordinary gravity alone; reversing the material change gives **0.39854 ns later**. These are largest sampled excess delays, at the last emission in the window, not measured absolute arrival times or universal maxima.

**The physical cost matters.** The retained mapping puts the inner clock labels at radii 5.00 and 8.34 pc. This particular 1% *central density* change redistributes **4.3230e38 kg** of ordinary matter (about 220 million solar masses) inside 20.01 pc. The total baryonic rest mass inside the source body's 33.36 pc outer radius is 5.5440e41 kg. Although the redistributed mass is only 0.0780% of that total, this is not an everyday stellar event. The two preparations have zero initial velocity; the calculation does not supply an astrophysical agent that arranged them. The dilute scalar normalization of 0.1 GeV/cm³ at radius label 6 is also an input, not a model-derived galaxy population. Neither the timing nor a bound on a real galaxy follows from knowing the scalar density alone.

## What is tested against what?

Take the clock change between the material event and its own quiet history in the occupied model, then subtract the same change in the empty-scalar model. Thus neither direct ordinary gravity nor a quiet scalar offset is counted as the material-driven excess. A quadratic scalar with zero initial field and momentum remains zero under classical propagation; the no-occupation control is the ordinary Einstein–Maxwell material experiment, not a new law fit separately. This is a subfamily and a calibrated thought experiment, not a GR violation or evidence for a new direct material force.

## What actually made the clock change?

On the original charged background, at the peak of each round-trip delay excess, the driven-field diagnostic accounts for **58.9% on the inner link** and **91.4% on the middle link**. On the far link it contributes **−1.37%** at the peak: the dominant term there is how the quiet scalar changes the gravitational response to the material and the path of the light. Different pieces can peak at different emission times or cancel. These are signed waveform terms in one specified diagnostic split, not fractions of emitted energy. The physically compared quantity is their sum. A scalar field supplied to GR with identical stress and initial data has the same common-metric signal.
