In the original coordinates chi=phi/sqrt(6), y=dchi/dN, integrate y_N=-(3+dln E/dN)y-(m/H_phys)^2 chi. Its exactly equivalent momentum equation is free of the oscillatory derivative. Reproduce the documented original DOP853 method, absolute/relative tolerances and maximum step in these coordinates; this is a fixed-coefficient replay, not the original joint code. It has a different coefficient sampling path, so numerical identity is not presumed.

Compare three predeclared changes: tightening only the absolute tolerance on y, imposing phase resolution as omega_h rises, and doing both. Use two successive phase caps, measured on independent halves of an e-fold with the maximal local frequency plus 10% margin, and compare full versus half-density cubic expansion input. For the baseline use a constant cap 0.04 instead of introducing a fictitious heavy frequency. Evaluate at the same two knots and matter event as before. Compare with the integral of the current and with the saved velocities. Accurate replay of the original bias would identify a numerical mechanism in this controlled problem; similar bias or its disappearance under refinement alone does not uniquely diagnose the original solver. The primordial or observational interpretation is not changed by a replay result.
