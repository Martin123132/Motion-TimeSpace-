# Which initial scalar difference actually sources the radiation change?

The slow MTS velocity discrepancy is large on its own scale, but its contribution to the homogeneous expansion-rate difference is tiny. That does not by itself bound its coupling to perturbed matter. Test this directly rather than assuming that the homogeneous energy fractions equal the radiation fractions. Beginning at the same primordial reference and original material state, change only the slow pair (phi_s,v_s) or only the heavy pair to the archived smooth values at the matter event, then evolve both fields and the complete material hierarchy under the common metric. The physical full case is already saved. For a null all fields, scale and matter match; check zero incremental derivatives and source over the saved material trajectory. Baseline has just one scalar and its full response is reused.

Use the same two resolutions and half-size operator probe. Report signed terms and interaction E_both-E_slow-E_heavy+E_none. Judge a small remainder against summed absolute componentwise numerical budgets before interpreting it. These controlled changes of initial data are not alternative fitted theories or a simplification of the physical equations.
