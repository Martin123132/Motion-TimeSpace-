# Primordial source convergence, not phase fitting

On the initial 5/6-order-pair study (levels giving orders 10 and 12), the baseline event expansion difference is resolved, but the MTS difference between two large H values is not: the sum of the last resolution and independent e-fold differences is 0.00208 of the small correction. The large initial-data shot also reports a 24.7% slow-velocity discrepancy against the MTS archive. Do not assume either number is an observable. Retain the first trial.

For both cases, test two finer phase caps (0.12, 0.06 radians) in coordinate time, and separately solve the oscillator equations in log scale factor using binary128 arithmetic, direct phi and U=v/(am), from the same primordial words. Use the one-sided thermal polynomial on each side of its specified switch in the scale-factor solver. Independent-variable branches, absolute elapsed times, oscillator amplitudes, archived velocity and the small event Friedmann difference must all be reported. Evaluate the two final refinements and cross-method discrepancies relative to the small source, with the same 2e-5 gate; neither choose a solver by smaller radiation nor move a target scale. The original looser solver is a documented convergence diagnostic. For a material sensitivity propagation, use the directly integrated log-scale state at the exact event, with the coordinate-time finest state as an independent control.
