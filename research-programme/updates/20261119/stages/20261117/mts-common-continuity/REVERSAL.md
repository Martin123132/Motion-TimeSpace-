# New timing test of the local sign reversal

On the fully smooth matched state the zero at preparation has a known cause and is excluded. The first nonconstant terms predict a second zero of the *instantaneous excess rate* at T_rate=−2u'/u'' and of *accumulated excess energy* at T_net=−u'/(2C), where C is the derived coefficient of T² in EΔ/E0. Only use roots inside the first original background/observation subinterval; do not extrapolate this Taylor law across a knot. The predictions extrapolate local curvature, not an exact crossing theorem. Labels are for a difference of energy transfer for the prepared mode, not negative total radiation.

This is one retained mode and two background models, measured in conformal Mpc, not an observation or temperature scan. Freeze both models' times and valid ranges before evaluating the new times. For every eligible clock use the interval (0,1.4 T_net] on both models. Check 64 and 128 sample brackets and two independent DOP853 step caps (32 and 64 steps per short event window), retaining native tolerances and original preparation. Compare timing on relative-to-crossing scale with 2e-5; report a sign/order success separately from a timing failure. A baseline with no eligible predicted root is a same-elapsed-time control, not proof of no turnover outside the scanned range.
