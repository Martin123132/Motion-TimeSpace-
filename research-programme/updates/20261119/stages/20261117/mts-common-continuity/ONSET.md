# Consequence at the actual matched preparation (frozen small-time test)

Let P0=(4/3)χs² be the original positive bulk radiation rate and E0 its accumulating time integral. Write α=a1/a0−1 and d=Δs. The relative rate is u=[(1+d/s)/(1+α)]²−1. At the original matter preparation d=0, so

    u* = −α*(2+α*)/(1+α*)²
    u'* = 2/(1+α*)² [ d'*/s* − (H1*−a0'*/a0*) ]
    EΔ(T)/E0(T) = u* + T u'*/2 + O(T²).

No derivative of the opacity is needed: the common derivative of the positive weight cancels in the ratio of integrals. For a fully matched smooth background all primitive displacements vanish, therefore d'*=0, α*=0 and the signed changed energy **starts quadratically**, EΔ(T)=−P0*(H1*−a0'*/a0*) T² + O(T³). A scale mismatch instead creates a term proportional to T. This is a short-time numerical/theoretical prediction for this prepared mode, not a sky observable. In particular T is conformal elapsed time. A change in either the retained spline versus evolution, or the reference preparation, changes the prediction; it is not a new physical interaction.
