## Conventions and exact identity

Use a local orthonormal tetrad, metric (-,+,+,+), c=1. Let G_b=(E_b,F_b) be collision energy-momentum delivered to baryons, G_gamma=-G_b. For component four-velocity u_i=gamma_i(1,v_i), define Q_i=-u_i.G_i: its rest-frame internal-energy source. Define h_i=Q_i/gamma_i. These are collision sources, not total time derivatives of the lab-frame energy.

    h_b = E_b-v_b.F_b
    h_gamma = -E_b+v_gamma.F_b
    h_b+h_gamma = (v_gamma-v_b).F_b

With retained photon momentum gain J=-F_b=A(v_b-v_gamma), this becomes

    E_b = h_b-v_b.J,  E_gamma = -h_b+v_b.J
    h_gamma = D-h_b,  D=J.(v_b-v_gamma)=A|v_b-v_gamma|^2.

This identity is exact for a supplied common four-force. The linear constitutive J and the saved velocities fix its application only through quadratic perturbative order. It introduces NO adjustable gas/photon share. Nonzero h_b must be computed from the microscopic energy moment, not inferred from J alone. h_b can have either sign with thermal exchange.

For a fluid, contraction of divergence T with u gives dot(rho)+(rho+p)theta plus the appropriate heat-flux/anisotropic-stress terms = Q. Thus this is the energy source in that equation, not an identification of T00 minus an ad hoc kinetic term. Radiation boosts change pressure and volume as well as energy. Photon u_gamma is the energy frame to the retained linear order, v_gamma=3q_gamma/4. The identity does not discard shear work or replace the full second-order Einstein/fluid system.

## Elastic limit: a definite allocation

In the strictly elastic Thomson limit in the cold baryon rest frame, Q_b=0. Therefore h_gamma=D and h_b=0. Baryon lab energy loss -v_b.J is bulk work, not gas heat. The relative-flow dissipation enters the photon rest-energy equation in this limit. Thermally moving electrons require ensemble projection onto the BULK gas velocity; zero recoil in an individual electron frame alone does not imply zero bulk gas heating.

The old quadratic bulk bookkeeping K'= -D is consistent with this result but did not by itself establish it. Rest-frame projection supplies the additional physical assumption and derivation. Non-elastic Compton, double-Compton and emission/absorption require their independent energy moment. Do not add D to a full microscopic collision kernel that already contains the same bulk terms: a matched correction replaces/subtracts the overlapping slip contribution.
