# Finite Thomson transport as a controlled intervention

The perfect-fluid comparison first loses local accuracy in a nearly cancelling scalar energy and later in the photon/baryon densities, flux, and metric. Test the following intervention on the same 327 times, not by adjusting field data or fitting the radiation amplitude. The previously unseen photon anisotropic stress is a direct discriminator; score it separately, as well as photon/baryon velocity difference, not only its effect on the fields.

With x=kτ, h=H/k, R=3ρ_b/(4ρ_γ), V=(v_γ+R v_b)/(1+R), S=v_b-v_γ and D=δ_b−3δ_γ/4, exact bulk momentum cancellation gives

    (1+R)V_x = δ_γ/4 − π_γ/2 + R δp_b/ρ_b − h R V,     D_x=−S.

Here π_γ is the native photon quadrupole (twice the MB shear convention); velocities are θ/k, photon heat flux is 4v_γ/3. Subtract the two Euler equations. At first order in L=k/(a n_e σ_T), the algebraic slip is S=L R/(1+R)[δp_b/ρ_b−h V−δ_γ/4+π_γ/2]. Restore v_γ=V−R S/(1+R) and v_b=V+S/(1+R); weight both in the gravitational sources. The net momentum is unchanged at fixed V; the baryonic density contribution changes by ρ_b D. There is no new field force. Use adiabatic fully ionized gas at T=T0/a, taking δp_b/ρ_b=c_b² δ_b, c_b²=(4/3) k_B T [1−3Y/4+(1−Y)x_e]/(m_p c²). At the earliest epochs this is an extrapolation of the retained nonrelativistic matter model, not a full treatment of the primordial plasma.

At leading order π_γ=(32/45)L U, with U=σ_metric+v_γ and σ_metric=−δ_c,x+3η_x. The coefficient includes polarization; it is standard transport, not an MTS derivation. Also test the next shear relaxation term −(32/45)(11/6)L d(L U)/dx using the leading Einstein shear and bulk Euler derivatives with L_x=2hL. Score the leading and corrected shear separately without selecting a coefficient from the target. This is not a full second-order treatment of the slip. The baryon density and helium/electron abundance come from the saved physical densities, original PRIMAT table and CAMB composition/Thomson constants, not from the saved radiation outputs. Related independent treatment: [Blas, Lesgourgues & Tram (2011)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1104.2933).

Predict both branches before scoring. Keep the original 2e−5 limit. Separately report inter-resolution disagreement, peak-scaled errors, and failures on the signed small difference; coincident agreement on large densities is not resolution of the slip. Compare the algebraic slip to the retained difference of the two saved velocity words; do not use those words to evolve anything. The finite-slip origin uses D=0, a common initial bulk velocity and the first-order attracting slip, not an independently prepared isocurvature mode. Initial transient and archival precision in a difference of nearly equal words are possible limitations to be tested.
