# Which physics controls the elastic photon-energy correction?

**Result.** The retained baryon and photon Euler equations give an independently evolvable equation for their relative velocity (slip). It reproduces the directly integrated MTS radiation correction in two tested cells when driven by the actual interior matter and metric histories. The most consequential input in both cells is the change in the expansion rate multiplying the original baryon velocity. No new coupling or calibration was needed. The independent variable is conformal time; the output is a stored-mode collision energy, not an observable spectrum.

The direct integrals are negative, so a negative shift makes the correction more negative. The same term ranks first at both physical step sizes, also in the retained approximation. The other photon density and quadrupole terms are not zero. These tests replace one input at a time by the line between its endpoints. They are conditional sensitivities, not extra physical forces or fractions of cosmic energy. Using real slip samples (rather than integrating the slip equation) and independent Simpson quadrature at 257 times reproduces each parent reference integral to <1e-13. These interior samples come from the same coupled solution, not independent cosmological initial conditions.

**Baseline control.** Both interior probes fail the unchanged relative tolerance 2e-5, with combined integral working fractions 0.00882 and 0.02270. The leading sensitivity in the baseline is not robustly the expansion term. Direct integration of the baseline's *sampled* slip agrees with its own same-step dense integral within 1.6e-7 in these cells. Thus adding integration nodes alone does not remove uncertainty in the tiny changed state. At the final baseline epoch the finite Euler rearrangement improves the 64-step slope mismatch from 0.659 to 0.000956 in units of the sum of the native derivative components, but this is still above tolerance (and is not accuracy relative to the much smaller resulting slip slope). Do not set the baseline response to zero.

**Coverage.** All 64 full-state restart endpoints in the initial four-block-per-case experiment were identical to their saved native states. On MTS the direct source meets its local working precision in all four blocks (16/7854 distinct original intervals; worst 4.21e-7); on baseline none of the four blocks qualifies (0/3614 intervals). There are 7855 MTS time samples and 3615 baseline samples. The two interior probes above are subsets, not additional coverage. The worst decile of MTS blocks accounts for 30.39% of unsigned saved-stride spread, not all of it. The mixed parent integral is -1.221416330319569e-14 with an unsigned diagnostic working allowance of 7.79845e-18 (relative 6.38e-4); it does not meet 2e-5. The prior result's smaller signed allowance also failed. Neither is a certified continuum enclosure.

**Shortcuts tested.** Interpolation of the saved fields/slopes (Hermite) qualified on only one of four MTS blocks. Independently evolving the slip with linear or four-knot cubic *forcing* and no endpoint correction qualified on none of the four, for either MTS or baseline. At fine temporal sampling of the true interior forcing, both MTS probe cells recover. This distinguishes an unresolved time-dependent input from a failure of the slip equation in these cells. The first manufactured RK4 check at 32 subdivisions missed the algebra threshold by a small amount (1.146e-10 versus 1e-10). The preserved second run explicitly verifies convergence from 8 to 128, reaching 4.45e-13; the physical tolerances and both predeclared endpoint-forcing tests were not relaxed.
