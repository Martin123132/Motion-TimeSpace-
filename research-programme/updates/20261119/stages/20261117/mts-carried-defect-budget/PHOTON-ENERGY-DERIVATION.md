Retained variables: baryon v_b=Q_b/B_b and photon q_gamma=Q_gamma/B_gamma. B_i=kappa_grav a^2 rho_i are the existing common-metric weights. At fixed instantaneous background, internal collision terms are Q'_b|coll=-J, Q'_gamma|coll=+J, with J=A*s, s=v_b-3q_gamma/4 and A=(4/3)B_gamma*chi>=0.

For a real linear perturbation amplitude define the quadratic bulk kinetic bookkeeping quantity
K_bulk=(1/2)B_b*v_b^2+(3/8)B_gamma*q_gamma^2.
Its COLLISION contribution is
K'_bulk|coll=v_b*(-J)+(3/4)q_gamma*(+J)=-J*s=-A*s^2<=0.
The missing bulk kinetic energy must be accounted for by the appropriate internal/radiation sectors in a complete collision-energy calculation. This identity alone DOES NOT determine that allocation or replace the Compton energy kernel. Expansion and background derivatives are excluded from the collision-only derivative, not dropped from a full conservation equation.
