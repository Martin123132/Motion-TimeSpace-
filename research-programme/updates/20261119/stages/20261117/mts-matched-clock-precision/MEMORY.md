# Conditional drag memory and an energy check

What does it mean that the difference enters with the left state? In the retained Euler equations s0'=F0-L0 s0 and s1'=F1-L1 s1, L0=chi0(1+w0), L1=chi0 g²(1+w0 g) and g=1/(1+alpha). At prescribed forces and background the difference of two solutions on either branch is exactly the initial difference times exp(-integral L). Let u be the shift of s0 and v the shift of the change d=s1-s0. Then u=u_left E0 and v=v_left E1+u_left E0 expm1(-integral(Delta L)); equivalently v=v_left E1+u_left(E1-E0). Delta L=chi0(g-1)[1+g+w0(1+g+g²)]. This isolates direct memory through drag; in the coupled problem velocity, density, anisotropic stress and metric also change the driving forces. It is not a general damping theorem for cosmological perturbations.

For prescribed forces integrate the finite energy shift without subtracting two large total energy integrals:

    shift R = (4 chi0/3) [(g²-1)(2s0 u+u²) + g²(2s0 v+2u d+2u v+2d v+v²)].

Check this against the actual left-state swaps in the previous calculation on the very same cells, on the scale of the small shift. Then propagate only the earliest archived 32/64 slip displacement with the measured opacities, inertias and matched scale. Contrast it with the actual displacement entering each cell. Keep the full inhomogeneous solution and both initial states untouched. A small inherited-only contribution points to forcing generated over the intervening history; it does not identify a particular species without another test.
