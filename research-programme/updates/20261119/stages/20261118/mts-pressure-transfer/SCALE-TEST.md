# Cross-scale discriminator: freeze before reading the four original channels

The retained Stage56 calculation already reported a late linear matter-power turnover. Do not claim to discover that turnover. Today's self-consistent calculation found that the heavy scalar replaces the missing homogeneous cold density at k=5 without replacing its clumping. Is this restricted to k=5, or does the original archive show a scale dependence?

Freeze a short-window diagnostic using only the physical background. For y=aφ and v=aδφ the frozen natural frequencies in conformal time are √A and √(A+k²), A=(am)²−a''/a. Compare their beat rate to the isolated Newtonian self-gravitational frequency √g, g=4πGa²ρ_h. The time-scale equality gives k_* = [2√(Ag)+g]^(1/2), with no amplitude, mass or radius fit. It does not predict a cosmological half-power wavenumber. The medium is expanding, there is external gravity, and the initial population matters.

Use **all four** modes with early baseline and MTS states already available together: 0.03, 0.1, 1 and 5 Mpc⁻¹. Compare (δρ_h+3ℋ δq_h/k) to the density source that the removed cold matter would have supplied, using the original cold-mode contrast at the same scale; also compare the two stored Weyl potentials. Exclude no inconvenient mode. The qualitative check is whether the fraction supplied at k=5 is below that at both modes below k_*/2, not whether any absolute amplitude is predicted by this criterion. Do not apply a Newtonian Jeans interpretation at horizon scales. Use original files only as an **archive diagnostic**, compare to the independently evolved physical k=5 result, and explicitly identify the modes still requiring a physical continuation. This is not new survey or held-out evidence.
