# From field pressure to the potential felt by light

This is the retained flat scalar sector in synchronous CDM coordinates. Use x=kτ, h=ℋ/k and F=δ_c,x. D, P, Q and Π are the total density, pressure, current and anisotropic stress multiplied by 8πGa²/k². Fields use β=φ_x, μ=am/k, J=μ²φ. For a canonical perturbation (u,w)=(δφ,δφ_x), its D=βw+Ju, P=βw−Ju and Q=βu. It has no scalar anisotropic stress at this order. Numerical stress outputs named slow_pressure and heavy_pressure are k²P. Photon stresses in the readouts are normalized Pγ and Πγ. Do not compare the differently normalized columns as energies.

In the metric convention of the retained native Fortran, W=k²(Φ+Ψ)/2=−(k²/2)(D+3hQ+Π). Thus k²Φ=−(k²/2)(D+3hQ), k²Ψ=k²Φ−k²Π. A useful density-only Newtonian estimate has fractional error bounded by (k²/2)(3|hQ|+|Π|)/|W| wherever W is nonzero. This is the *linear cosmological* density-to-potential limit. It is not an assumption about a stationary galaxy. potential.py reconstructs both potentials independently and checks the bound without discarding sign changes in the radiation terms.

**Which pressure?** The oscillator and pressure-product identities in METHOD.md separate the inherited (u,w), fresh metric/velocity forcing, friction, mass and instantaneous readout. To see gravity's response, form δD_s and δQ_s from the two actual trajectories. Solve the rest of the linear Hamiltonian system from zero environmental response on the physical medium driven only by these differences. Add the direct scalar contribution to W to this induced environmental W. Also solve with the original physical environmental initial state and scaled source S_P+A(S_P−S_A) for both density and current. The difference from the unscaled trajectory divided by A checks the tiny response without assigning gravity to an arbitrary pressure pulse. This causal decomposition concerns forcing **after** the chosen starting state. Stored initial differences elsewhere are not attributed to it. The two constituent sources need not separately satisfy a conservation law in this decomposition; the unmodified complete trajectories provide that control.

**Why removing cold density is not the same as adding a wave.** For a comparison at identical scale, use t=ln(a/a_L)/0.0025, so dx/dt=0.0025/h in each branch. Let E include every perturbation except the heavy scalar. With its realized density and flux as an external input to this block, E_H'=L_H E_H+I_H S_h, E_C'=L_C E_C. The prime here means d/dt. Introduce L_* equal to L_H but with the *cold branch's CDM density coefficient*; this is a linear decomposition, not an admissible third cosmology. Then

    ΔE = U_H ΔE_initial + ∫ U_H [ I_H S_h + (L_H−L_*) E_C + (L_*−L_C) E_C ] dt.

For each observed quantity add its direct heavy stress and the corresponding changes of its readout coefficient. This gives four signed, independently integrated terms: inherited environmental differences; heavy stress including its already-present state and subsequent feedback; changed CDM coefficient; all other medium and clock changes. The two artificial operator contributions cancel when recombined. This attribution convention fixes what is meant by the CDM share; it does not identify a measured probability or all causes before 3038. Terms can be large and opposite, especially for photon shear. The simultaneous solution matches the unsplit two physical trajectories.

**Derived wave response.** On the self-consistent conformal background define y=aφ_h and v=aδφ_h. Exactly on the retained quadratic subfamily,

    y_xx + ω₀² y = 0,                       ω₀² = μ² − a_xx/a,
    v_xx + ω₁² v = a β_h F,                 ω₁² = 1+ω₀².

The driving velocity is recovered as β_h=y_x/a−hy/a. In each oscillator the unfitted leading kernel is G(x,r)=sin(θ(x)−θ(r))/√[ω(x)ω(r)], θ_x=ω. For the homogeneous basis, applying ∂²+ω² leaves e times the basis, e=3(ω_x/ω)²/4−ω_xx/(2ω). Hence the exact retarded equation has effective force f−e v (or −e y), not f+e v. Iterate from the original value and derivative. Use the inferred homogeneous velocity in the perturbation source, not later target heavy stresses. envelope.py compares zero, one and two kernel corrections, deletes the metric forcing as a control, and tests the zero source. The numerical integral norm estimates are not certified floating-point enclosures. Anisotropic stress from photons/neutrinos remains in F through the reference trajectory: this is a **conditional** field response, not an end-to-end sky prediction.

The two modes permit a slow product phase with frequency ω₁−ω₀=1/(ω₁+ω₀) as well as the fast sum phase. In dimensional conformal time, the beat rate is √(k²+A)−√A with A=(am_h)²−a''/a. Comparing its square to g=4πGa²ρ_h, the acceleration scale from the pressureless Newtonian Poisson term, yields k_*²=2√(A g)+g. It is a local comparison of propagation and **isolated heavy self-gravity** time scales. Other matter gravitation and state preparation still enter the density response; k_* is not a prediction of a half-power point, universal cutoff, or galaxy occupation.
