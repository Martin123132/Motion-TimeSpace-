# Regular tensor centre on the same proper-time foliation
Let T=diag(−1/2,−1/2,1) and s=xᵢxⁱ. Smooth even-parity quadrupole perturbations of either spatial metric or covariant extrinsic curvature have the local Cartesian form
    α(s) Tᵢⱼ + β(s)[xᵢ(Tx)ⱼ+(Tx)ᵢxⱼ] + γ(s)(xTx)δᵢⱼ + δ(s)(xTx)xᵢxⱼ .
A spherical monopole has the separate form a(s)δᵢⱼ+b(s)xᵢxⱼ. No central shear amplitude is prescribed. Write H P₂ for the radial metric perturbation, x J ∂θP₂ for the mixed component, and K P₂ ± (3/2)G sin²θ for the angular components normalized by x² and x²sin²θ. Then 2G=α, 2J−2G=sβ, K+G=sγ, H−4J−K+G=s²δ. The last condition, order x⁴ rather than merely x², is essential for a regular Cartesian spin-two tensor. Spherical H−C is order x². A regular covector is c(s)(Tx)ᵢ+d(s)(xTx)xᵢ: pθ is order x², and the combination p_r,2−2pθ,2/x is order x³. Densitized contravariant polar electric fields carry x² times a regular Cartesian vector. The polar magnetic two-form coefficient is order x³. Scalar quadrupoles and Aφ/sin²θ are order x²; the enclosed spherical number is odd and order x³. These powers regularize the same equations, not a new material model.

A staggered signed meridian has mirror points on either side of the origin; no excision boundary or frozen inner cell. Density Jacobians are even: J/sinθ=|x|√(1+cφ)√det h_meridian. Continue the enclosed number oddly, the material specific angular-momentum law evenly, and its enclosed primitive oddly. Positive-radius continuum equations are unchanged. The central cap projects *rates* onto the smooth Cartesian powers on a shrinking set of rings (default 12). Its constant tensor, charge, density and wave coefficients remain dynamical. Degree, width, closure, boundaries, resolution and the exact through-centre wave are numerical checks; none is a parameter calibrated to an astrophysical target.

**Why the naïve evolution failed.** The first implementation imposed those correct powers but used the original geodesic ADM spatial curvature directly. Even the finite flat discretization exhibits growing modes. The recorded spectral probe is local, tapered and based on the spatial acceleration block, not an all-grid stability theorem. The raw .05-grid coupled solution contaminates the metric volume; adding eighth-order numerical dissipation by itself still gives a nonfinite .025-grid state by τ=4.08. No physically singular behaviour is inferred. A trace comparison or just a successful coarse time integration would not have exposed the problem. For context on discrete second-order Einstein evolutions see [Calabrese, Hinder & Husa](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0503056); the particular implementation and tests here are our own.

**Replacing the troublesome derivative, not the gravity theory.** Let sᵢⱼ now denote the flat reference spatial metric and D its connection. Define Vᵢ=Dʲγⱼᵢ−Dᵢ tr_sγ, with index raising by s for this definition only. Define Mᵢ=∇ʲKⱼᵢ−∇ᵢK+Rₙᵢ using the *physical* untwisted metric and sources (scalar, moving charged material, Maxwell flux and twist as in the parent). On the constraint surface M=0. Initially evaluate V from the actual constrained metric; evolve V̇=V[−2K]+2M. Thus the new connection equals its metric definition on an exact solution. This choice cancels the flat principal derivatives of K in its rate. It is not an arbitrary force, gravitational-wave source or physical damping. Do not confuse this auxiliary Vᵢ with cloud volume.

The spatial Ricci tensor used for evolution is its full nonlinear remainder (full meridional Ricci minus the same operator linearized about flat space) plus
    −½ Δ_s (γ−s)ᵢⱼ + D₍ᵢ Vⱼ₎ + ½DᵢDⱼ tr_s(γ−s).
The diagnostic Hamiltonian always uses the **original** unreduced curvature. The trace in this replacement is calculated from the spatial metric, not from the independent focusing integral. This preserves the latter as a separate readout. The flat component Laplacian is taken in Cartesian tensor harmonics. For normalized angular coefficients a=α, b=sβ, c=sγ, d=s²δ and L=∂ₓ²+2∂ₓ/x the Laplacian maps them to La+4b/x², Lb+(4d−6b)/x², Lc+(2d−6c)/x², Ld−20d/x². These follow by applying the Cartesian Laplacian to the homogeneous polynomials xᵢ(Tx)ⱼ+(Tx)ᵢxⱼ, (xTx)δᵢⱼ and (xTx)xᵢxⱼ before normalizing by powers of radius. They retain the nonvanishing tensor at the origin. A vanishing-with-resolution eighth-order grid filter is allowed as a numerical control; the independent analytic controls distinguish its effects from real attenuation. No claim of unconditional long-time stability follows from these finite-window tests.
