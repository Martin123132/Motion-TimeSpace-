# A mass and stress equation, not Newton added as a second force
This concerns the unspun background of the retained minimal branch. The even perturbation still propagates on that background; it is not treated as a spherical dust shell. Write ds²=−dτ²+a²dx²+R²dΩ², with n=∂τ and s=a⁻¹∂x. Define geometrical enclosed mass by
    2m/R = 1 + Ṙ² − (R_x/a)².
Let e=T_nn, p=T_ss and j=−T_ns in the parent's 4π-rescaled units (G=c=1). The two-dimensional warped-product Einstein tensor is G_ab=−(2/R)∇ₐ∇ᵦR+g_ab[(2/R)□R−2m/R³]. Its ss projection on a freely falling normal contains no radial second derivative: G_ss=−2R̈/R−2m/R³=2p. Its other projections, or differentiation of the defining norm relation, give
    R̈ = −m/R² − R p,         m_x = R²(e R_x + a j Ṙ),
    ṁ = −R²[p Ṙ + j R_x/a].
The last two are independent spatial and temporal mass equations. Vacuum does not imply ṁ=0 in a Maxwell-filled region. E_rr=R_nn+2R̈/R fixes ä/a=−E_rr. These allow a second evolution of the **same** unspun geometry via m/x³ and the two time-dependent metric scales, without second spatial derivatives of the spherical metric. It does not use the prior radial solver's clock or a fitted mass history. The full scalar, material and electromagnetic stresses and fluxes are computed at each step. The connection/Einstein evolution for the quadrupole and the separately integrated directional volume are retained. Reconstruct m from the metric, check m_x against matter, and compare the actual ruler to the original evolution.

For neutral cold comoving dust, p=j=0, so m is conserved on each trajectory and inverse-square acceleration follows immediately. In the uncharged exterior this reads R̈_SI=−G M_SI/R_SI². For an electrovac exterior e=−p=Q²/(2R⁴), j=0 and m=M−Q²/(2R), giving R̈=−M/R²+Q²/R³, with the same metric and same definition of mass. For the neutral scalar the pressure is (π²+h_x²/a²−h²)/2 and its flux is −π h_x/a; those terms can change both the pressure force and the mass carried between spheres. Simply multiplying a Newton force by a new fitted factor would miss both. At the regular centre isotropy gives ä_c/a_c=−R_nn/3: for dust at rest and the scalar this is −(ρ₀+2π²−h²)/3. Positive scalar potential and kinetic terms therefore have opposite effects on freely released neighbours; this sign is a result of the common-metric equation, not a change to clock definitions.

This derivation supplies a concrete reason why these prepared spherical controls follow Newton and why the scalar/charged cases need not. It uses the retained Einstein kinetic term and calibrated G. It does not derive the numerical value of G, occupation of galactic wave states, or the ultraviolet matter action.
