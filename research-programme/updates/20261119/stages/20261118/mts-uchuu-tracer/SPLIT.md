# Tracer transfer experiment — locked after inspecting South 0, before scoring lightcone 1

All primary weight products are those of the published Uchuu COMB catalogues, positive weights at 0.2 ≤ z_obs < 0.5. Central halo velocities remain nonzero. Hold cone 1 unopened by the analysis until cone 0 forecasts have been saved for both caps; report repeats of training halo IDs, and also score a stricter subset excluding any training id/pid in either cap (snapshot IDs, not merger-tree ancestries). There is no independent simulation here.

At each satellite find a central with the exact parent ID, within 3 comoving h⁻¹ Mpc in BOTH observer-frame box coordinates and sky coordinates. Demand exactly one such candidate in that cap, including unweighted members among potential hosts. Report absent, nonlocal and ambiguous separately. Define u = χ(z_obs) − χ(z_if_host); the counterfactual replaces the satellite vector velocity by the actual central's at the satellite direction and z_cos. The HDF5 convention z_obs = z_cos + (1+z_cos)vlos/300000 agrees to roundoff in South 0. Use this same conversion for counterfactuals; χ uses physical c=299792.458 km/s and flat Ωm=0.309 as printed in Table 2 of https://arxiv.org/html/2311.14456v2. Use real Cartesian radii from the release for pair separations, oriented by the catalogue RA/Dec. χ differences use direct quadrature, not subtraction of two rounded absolute positions. Distinguish the positional radial observable from a Fourier multipole.

Freeze one complex weighted characteristic φ_s(k) per cap from measured satellite u in cone 0, φ_c=1. Report the frozen all-measured-tracer mixture, a single centred Gaussian with its training second moment, and a population envelope allowing any missing-host phase of modulus ≤1. Test paired internal motions, full radial shifts with/without internal motion, and their coupling to real radial separation. At each shell measure cos(k Δu); predict Re(φ_i φ_j*) conditional on measured pair types. For the exact radial pair response with host plus real-separation phase B=k(Δr+Δu_host), predict Re(exp(iB)φ_i φ_j*) versus measured cos(B+kΔu). The conditioning phases, positions, and types of the evaluation mock are supplied, not predicted by MTS. Save an unconditional pair forecast from the training shells as well. Separate same and distinct hosts. These are weighted pair observables, not the survey power spectrum. Report all-object pair phases without internal decomposition when hosts are absent.

Use full unordered pairs to 10 h⁻¹ Mpc, above 10 use directed uniform anchor sampling (4096 anchors per cap, seed recorded) to 80 h⁻¹ Mpc, with product weights. Exclude identical object IDs in pair estimands. Shell edges 0,2,5,10,20,40,80; eight training-defined RA strips per cap, deleting BOTH endpoints for a validation-only jackknife on model discrepancy. Sampling/volume uncertainty and shared simulation are limitations; not an independent model-comparison significance. Report the discrepancy at all frozen modes; 0.01 absolute in the cosine moment is a diagnostic portability screen at k_parallel ≤ 0.1, not a changed parent numerical tolerance or formal posterior interval. Never rescore BOSS here. Require velocity projection <1e−6 km/s and pair identity/replay <2e−5. No fit to BOSS.
