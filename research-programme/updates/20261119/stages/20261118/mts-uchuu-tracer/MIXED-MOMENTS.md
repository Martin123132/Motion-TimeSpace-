Let B be the actual radial host displacement difference and D the internal displacement difference of a pair. Also test B+Δr in place of B, keeping the actual geometry. D' draws independent internal displacements from the weighted training satellite law or the central point mass according to each endpoint's type. The original forecast was Re〈exp(ikB) E[exp(ikD')|types]〉. Its error has exact even Taylor coefficients. With μ_j=E[(D')^j|types],

    K_m = (-1)^(m/2)/m! × Σ_{j=1}^m binom(m,j) 〈 B^(m−j) (D^j−μ_j) 〉,  m=2,4.

In particular K₂ = −〈B(D−μ₁)〉 − ½〈D²−μ₂〉. The first term is the mixed relative motion; its sign is not specified by a one-galaxy dispersion. Both terms are invariant to a common additive scalar radial shift of both endpoints. Expanding real cosines through fifth order bounds the absolute remainder by k⁶〈(B+D)⁶+E[(B+D')⁶|B,types]〉/720 for the *same weighted sample*. This bound does not control transfer between samples.
