# Satellite motions in a BOSS-matched tracer population

**Result.** An independent satellite displacement reproduces the one-galaxy distribution in a second mock, but overdamps close, distinct-host pairs. A term coupling the *difference* in host motions to the *difference* in satellite motions accounts for most of the leading error. We derived its contribution to the observable, measured its coefficients only in low-redshift mock 0, and transferred it without adjustment to the next redshift interval. It reduced the mean absolute error in the designated long-wavelength pair observable by **77–90%** after excluding low-redshift object/family IDs. It is a tracer result from a ΛCDM simulation, not a change to MTS gravity or a new survey fit.

## Data and what was actually tested

- [Authors' methods, HFD Uchuu, Table 2 and COMB selection](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.14456v2); [catalogue directory](https://skun.iaa.es/SUsimulations/UchuuDR2/Uchuu_GLAM_BOSS-eBOSS/Uchuu/). Used COMB North/South 0/1, not GLAM. Five original files including column documentation, each with URL/length/SHA receipt. The measured BOSS scores remain in the preceding stage. No MTS occupation, galaxy force parameters or tracer dispersion fitted here.
- 0.2 ≤ z_obs < 0.5, measured displacements in h⁻¹ Mpc, the published product of three weights. All branches move the *same* selected galaxies, without reselecting or reweighting them after a hypothetical move. Cartesian projection reproduces vlos, and the catalogue encodes z_obs−z_cos=(1+z_cos)vlos/300000, not /299792.458. Verified across the files to <10⁻⁶ km/s. We use physical c and published flat Ωm=0.309 for the distance integral. At low z in cone 1 the difference between catalogued radii and that rounded background is below 0.017 h⁻¹ Mpc.
- A satellite's counterfactual velocity is its exact parent central's **vector**, projected along the satellite's own direction, not a central radial velocity copied from a different line of sight. Match by integer ID and require a single local central in both coordinate systems. A central has zero *internal* motion by convention, not zero absolute velocity.
- Low-z cone 1: South 11,483/20,387 and North 23,491/46,083 satellite rows matched. They carry respectively 60.7% and 57.0% of satellite weight. Unmatched samples have lower median subhalo mass. Excluded missing, nonlocal or ambiguous hosts are not zero. The subset with no object or family ID in the entire training files contains 58,025 South and 231,384 North selected galaxies before the match requirement.

## Why one dispersion is insufficient

At k_parallel=0.10 h/Mpc the matched-mixture one-object characteristic modulus squared is 0.97374 → 0.97473 (South) and 0.97631 → 0.97621 (North) from train to evaluation. The corresponding higher-level population statement is much weaker: allowing an arbitrary phase for each missing satellite gives South [0.80015,0.97590], North [0.78327,0.97744] in evaluation. Even the narrower matched-cohort numbers are **not power-spectrum damping factors**.

For pairs of different matched hosts with true separation ≤2 h⁻¹ Mpc in evaluation cone 1, the full relative-displacement cosine is **0.88839 measured vs 0.87017 predicted** (South) and **0.88183 vs 0.86762** (North) at that same k. Measured minus predicted = +0.01821/+0.01422; delete-region descriptive SE 0.00143/0.00150. The corresponding errors on the nonreused-ID subset are +0.01807/+0.01391. Both exceed the prespecified 0.01 screen. In contrast, maximum distinct-host *internal-only* errors for all shells and k≤0.10 are 0.00366/0.00464. In close pairs only ~77% of the full pair weight is available for the decomposition. Same-host pairs have different responses and are tabulated separately.

Writing B=kΔu_host and I=kΔu_internal, the exact difference is 〈cos(B)[cos(I)−Re F]〉 − 〈sin(B)[sin(I)−Im F]〉, with F the frozen conditional type-factor. The second term accounts for +0.01498 South / +0.01224 North at ≤2 h⁻¹ Mpc. Thus getting the isolated satellite speeds nearly right does not recover their joint response. This does not identify central wobble, bulk infall, sample selection or other dynamics as the unique cause. Adding the radial separation phase still gives errors +0.01784/+0.01432. Full unseparated pair moments for all galaxies are available even when the host decomposition is not.

## Derived correction, new redshift test

The quadratic correction coefficient to measured-minus-independent is **−〈B₀(D−μ₁)〉 − ½〈D²−μ₂〉**, where B₀ and D are host and internal displacement differences, μ₁,μ₂ are the pair-type moments under the frozen independent distribution. Translation cancels. In the lowest separation bin the two pieces are +2.356 and −0.160 (South), +2.177 and −0.074 (North), in (h⁻¹ Mpc)², for the displacement-only observable. The **mixed** term dominates; changing just the width does not supply it. The quartic term and an absolute sixth-order Taylor remainder on a fixed sample follow without fitting, in MIXED-MOMENTS.md. The moment numbers themselves are simulation measurements, not fundamental constants.

The registered third COMB cone is **unavailable**: both official URLs return 404 and the index lists only 0/1. No third-cone test is claimed. Separately, after saving WINDOW-TRANSFER.md and locking code and coefficients, we tested the window 0.5 ≤ z_obs < 0.6 in every available cap/cone. Primary cells: two frozen k (0.025, 0.05), two radial observables and three shells to 10 h⁻¹ Mpc. Below are mean absolute cosine-moment errors in the 12 cells per row after excluding IDs/families in either lower-redshift selection. All samples include their independently measured host phases as conditioning inputs, not predictions of those phases.

| Region / cone | Selected after ID cut | Independent | + quadratic | + quadratic & quartic |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| South 0 | 108,003 | 0.002477 | 0.000327 | 0.000255 |
| North 0 | 255,900 | 0.002059 | 0.000362 | 0.000218 |
| South 1 | 107,599 | 0.002141 | 0.000645 | 0.000493 |
| North 1 | 255,879 | 0.002059 | 0.000362 | 0.000218 |

Both corrections improve on independent motions in every primary cell, and the quartic correction gives lower mean error than quadratic in every reported row. The largest remaining primary error for the quartic prescription is 0.001151. The same direction of change holds when retaining all IDs (see window-transfer.json). **These are not four independent replications**: in this higher-z interval 99.73% of North-1 rows reuse North-0 families; only 0.78% of South-1 rows reuse South-0 families, though they too share simulation physics. The overlap explains why the North rows are nearly identical. IDs cannot exclude common merger ancestry or shared large-scale modes.

The long-wavelength expansion is not an unrestricted multiplier: at k=0.15 the disjoint North-1 closest-bin displacement error changes from +0.02149 (independent) to −0.02582 (quadratic only) and +0.01037 (quadratic plus quartic). Do not apply the low-k improvement at arbitrary scale. Do not pool these cells as independent data or compare their error directly to a cosmological χ². Testing the finite mock response is not evidence for MTS over cold matter.
