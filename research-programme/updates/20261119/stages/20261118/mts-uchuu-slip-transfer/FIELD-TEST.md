# Link to the retained fields without another galaxy-bias fit

For an isotropic real-space tracer with constant linear bias b and common large-scale flow, Pˢ(k,μ)=b²Pδδ+2bμ²Pδθ+μ⁴Pθθ. With Δ the redshift-minus-real power, ΔP₂−2ΔP₀=6Pθθ/35 (equivalently 3P₄/4). This is an ideal linear identity, not a licence to subtract a measured survey monopole and call the result a velocity field. Apertures mix multipoles, and real-space positions are not independently measured by BOSS.

Instead apply a **covariance-only accessibility test** to the previously sealed, native heavy, same-background cold and matched ΛCDM predictions with the published DR12 z≈0.38 response already applied. No observed band powers, no χ² fit, no mock-to-observation correction. For vector t(b,N)=b²D+bX+V+NS and published mock covariance C=LLᵀ, collect L⁻¹D, L⁻¹X and L⁻¹S from all three branches into a common nuisance span. Its orthogonal complement Π preserves the residual velocity-only contrast. For d=L⁻¹(V₁−V₂), a unit-variance weight is L⁻ᵀΠd/||Πd||; expected separation in units of the assumed covariance is ||Πd||. Orthogonality is required for *both* models at arbitrary constant b and additive noise, separately in each cap. Test actual injections, covariance normalization, all branch templates and near-collinearity. A single-reference null and a before-projection contrast are sensitivity checks, not substitutes. No claim of invariance to arbitrary scale-dependent bias or tracer stress.

Use the original 0.02≤k<0.10 h/Mpc bins: ℓ=0,2 primary; additionally ℓ=0,2,4. Include both published cap covariances as independent blocks for the joint sensitivity and label that assumption. Report fixed linear templates and the saved coherent number-conserving mapping through order P² as a sensitivity (its b-independent part is not just linear velocity power). Only this data channel and the retained branch parameters are assessed; do not interpret a weak separation as a failure of the full field theory.
