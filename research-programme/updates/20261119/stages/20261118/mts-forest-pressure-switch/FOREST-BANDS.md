# What the radial bins can and cannot test at this stage

Use only the released redshifts, velocity wavenumbers and instrument labels, not the measured flux values, to grid the diagnostic. Solve new MTS field modes at each radial minimum k_parallel=k_v H/(1+z). Compare to identical-preparation pressure-off and the two consistently sampled cold controls. Apply five width/time/order variants of the earlier phase projection; 2e-6 threshold in its original normalization and common qualified k≤5/Mpc ceiling. Do not infer a trusted transverse tail from a qualifying radial minimum. For standard ΛCDM use its own H(z).

In addition, compute a **truncated linear baryon density and velocity-gradient power matrix**, not an absorption curve. For common positive linear optical-depth responses A and B, susceptibility is proportional to A D_b+B μ² U_b, U_b=−θ_b/H_conformal, μ=k_parallel/k. Integrate only on [k_parallel,5/Mpc]. No cosmology-dependent reassignment of A/B, no fitted gas history, no extension above 5, no nonlinear gas, thermal broadening, UV fluctuations or comparison to measured flux powers. More generally gas density and velocity-gradient responses are distinct and thermal/bias effects matter; see Seljak (2012), https://arxiv.org/abs/1201.0594, and Cieplak & Slosar, https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.07875. A positive common response is an explicit assumption for this diagnostic, not a calibration of either branch.

Report variation across all nonnegative A,B using extrema of the two quadratic forms' ratio, including pure-density and pure-velocity endpoints. Test independent integration and residual sensitivity to mode sampling (union of directly sourced nodes versus initial mode panel). Report unresolved integration errors at 2e-5, not spurious data exclusions. If k_parallel≥5 the full mode and all transverse modes are outside current support; return unavailable, not zero.
