# Errors and scope of the measurements
The two collocation latitudes implement exact quadrupole parity at the order studied. The absolute clock comparison is stored separately for each charge, scalar strength and initial convention. Independently propagated quantities include the axial potential, induced polar magnetic and electric fields, meridional mechanical momentum and scalar stress. The constraint CSVs include an explicit causal-margin column for good reason: an artificial holding zone is *not* constraint-preserving. Large residuals at late inner monitor points are real errors in the truncated computation there. The published numerical test in this private folder is the initially round detector at x=6 plus interior checks only while separated from the holding zones. None of the late inner monitor rows is used as a formation model. The truncated domain cannot be carried arbitrarily far forward by just taking more time steps.

For the vacuum reference the parent gave an interpolated constrained launch. As the readout is differentiated, short-wavelength interpolation residuals can be amplified. The raw finer-grid results are retained, including their failure on the small dynamic tide. The second run reconstructs the known decaying vacuum multipole from the exterior Schwarzschild constraint, scaling it to the saved u(6), **not** to the wave amplitude or time change. It makes no interior adjustment and reuses the original Schwarzschild mass. Only the neutral pre-front comparison uses that representation. For the same reason the independent direct-wave bank and a second radial-differentiation operator are kept: agreement of two volume values alone would not detect an incorrect emitted field on its rapidly growing front.

The relative-error convention is a whole-window maximum absolute difference divided by that signal's own whole-window peak, unless the heading specifies a single proper time. Thus passing on the total volume does not validate a small difference, and matching a finite-time sign does not imply matching a zero time. An exact electromagnetic null is tested as such without dividing by zero. No spherical-radius independence is claimed in the neutral rows that lack a separate trajectory file. Constraints printed as absolute values without a comparison scale are diagnostics, not relative accuracy claims. The stage outputs contain no likelihood fit, inferred real object or sensitivity claim.
