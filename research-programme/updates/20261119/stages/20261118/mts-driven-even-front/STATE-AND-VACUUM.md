# Two independent checks that do not put the desired signal into the solver
**Scalar preparation difference.** Let Y₀ be the unspun solution, U the first axial perturbation, and Z the even quadrupole. Expansion of the field equations gives Ż=L[Y₀]Z+S[Y₀,U,ℓ]. Preparing the neutral scalar on round shells instead of label spheres changes neither Y₀ nor U. Thus ΔŻ=L[Y₀]ΔZ: **all explicit quadratic radiation and rotational driving cancels exactly in this difference, even after the wave arrives**. The induced electric quadrupole, the charged material and the common metric do not drop out of L. This is not a prescription to drop the emitting source from either absolute run. It explains why even a perfect measurement of this emitted dipole cannot choose between the two initial scalar preparations at linear spin order.

Obtain Δu directly, instead of subtracting two nearly equal constraint solutions. In log radius write the label system u'=a b, b'=−b+c u+d. The round-shell system has coefficients a/μ, μ(c−ar²e_s), μd with μ=exp∫r h_r²dr, e_s=f h_r²+r f h_r h_rr+r h h_r (f is the initial inverse squared radial scale). On the shell operator the forcing of the difference is δa b_label and δc u_label+δd. The outer boundary has the inhomogeneous term β(μ_out−1)u_label. Use expm1 on the integral; do not subtract two order-one coefficients to produce the weak source. The initial difference of scalar fields is 2r(u_label+Δu)h_r P₂; the metric difference uses Δu, not u_shell. The same physical-event correction uses −2rΔu. This experiment uses no axial source in the *difference* solver, and compares with subtraction of complete charged, emitting runs.

**Independent vacuum wave readout.** On a material-free neutral Schwarzschild domain the even gauge-invariant [Zerilli–Moncrief equation (Martel & Poisson)](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0502028) supplies a check that is independent of our Gaussian normal evolution. Use the parent's stored wave at fixed areal radius 6 and Schwarzschild time .65, not the volume on a falling worldline. Find the meeting shell and proper time using the unspun geodesic (R̈=−M/R², ṫ=√f_launch/f(R)). In vacuum E_rr=−R_θφθφ in an orthonormal frame; a radial boost leaves the normal radial sectional curvature unchanged. For a constant-label shell this gives E_rr=k_θ k_φ−K_intr−K^θ_θ K^φ_φ, from the two Gauss equations. The k's are spatial principal curvatures of that shell, K the time-extrinsic curvature. To linear even order, if its fractional angular metric perturbations are K_s P₂ ± (3/2)G sin²θ, an angular relabeling by −G∂θP₂/2 removes the tracefree part. The radial areal shift needed to make the surface intrinsically round is −R(K_s+3G)P₂/2. Evaluate the Schwarzschild tide derivative 6M/R⁴ on this shift. A mere comparison of the large static tide is insufficient: report the error on the much smaller *time change* separately. This master does not supply the charged interior forcing.

**Constraints.** The numerical momentum residuals use D_j K^j_i−∂i K+R_ni in the untwisted geometry; R_ni includes scalar flux, moving dust, radial Poynting flux and the monopole crossed with induced polar field. Per-component cancellation errors are divided by the sum of absolute values of the actual quadrupolar terms at that event, not by the spherical rest mass. The Hamiltonian has an additional −2o²sin²θ on its constraint side; the two extra twist spatial Ricci traces cancel. The code retains even the residuals of the artificially frozen boundary region. Those rows must not be interpreted as a physical interior continuation; report the spatial and temporal domain of validated measurements. At sufficiently late times a regular centre, or a larger causally valid domain, is needed.
