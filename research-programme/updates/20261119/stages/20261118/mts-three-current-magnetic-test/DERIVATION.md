# Eliminate a three-component material current using electromagnetic measurements

Set x=N/M and P=2x−1 on 0≤x≤1. The saved family has J=M(32/3)x³(1−x)³(v₁+v₂P+v₃P²), with specific angular momentum ℓ=∂J/∂N. J and its endpoint flux vanish at both ends, so ∫ℓ dN=0 for every combination. Varying v₃ changes the radial distribution of the current, not its total angular momentum, the scalar charge, Newton's constant, or its electromagnetic coupling. The same ℓ,J enter the Maxwell forcing, matter stress, twist and constrained initial metric. The spin expansion is a weak-rotation expansion: magnetic fields are per spin, and directional gravitational log-volume per spin squared, in the retained solver units.

Fix the scalar initial data (ordinary A=0 or full A=1). Spherical evolution is independent of v to the retained order. The axial Maxwell equation with those currents is linear on that background. The even forcing and initial conformal constraint are quadratic in v; their linear propagation and measurement at this spin order preserve that dependence. Thus B(t)=f(t)v and L(t)=vᵀK(t)v, with the same quadratic law for determinant and each integrated focusing contribution. Compute the diagonal coefficients from actual single profiles. For i=1,2 the cross with component 3 is half of L(eᵢ+e₃)−L(eᵢ)−L(e₃). Use the saved (1,−c,0) combination to recover the 1,2 cross entry with its own sign. This is algebraic polarization of *solutions*, not a linear superposition of squared source densities.

Let D contain the rows f(3),f(4),f(5). Rank is tested on dimensionless rows as well as in original units. The length of the third normalized row projected onto ker(D[:2]) measures angular independence, not experimental resolving power. The inverse v=D⁻¹d uses only three fields; later clock and gravity predictions are then fixed. Equivalently L=dᵀD⁻ᵀKD⁻¹d and the late field residual f(t)D⁻¹d−B(t) must vanish on that model. It can be indefinite even if representative examples have the same sign. Use T−I=D_scalar⁻¹(D_ordinary−D_scalar) and expand C to avoid unnecessarily subtracting two rotated large responses.

**Eliminating the unmeasured source direction.** Under two field constraints the allowable sources form an affine line v=v₀+t n, n∈ker D[:2]. Their ruler traces form an actual quadratic in t. Solving at the final clock for a matching value exhibits a deliberate impostor, not an independent prediction of that value; the third field is an independent check. In three dimensions a prescribed positive ordinary source cost vᵀEv=W gives sharp limits Wλ_min, Wλ_max for matched L_scalar−L_ordinary. Cross-grid operator disagreement is assessed after energy whitening, on the same unit sphere as the bound. Eigenvalue sign on a given grid is algebraic; comparison of two grids is evidence about discretization error, not a validated continuum enclosure.

**Finite measurement errors.** On the nominal source compare two independently movable calibration boxes of early-field half-widths εsᵢ. For each arm set P=D⁻¹diag(s); the shift in its ruler is 2vᵀKP e+eᵀPᵀKP e, with |eᵢ|≤ε. A sufficient absolute error bound is ε times the sum of absolute linear coefficients plus ε² times the sum of absolute quadratic entries. For the actual nonoverlap limit also enumerate every face, edge, vertex and interior stationary point of each quadratic on its box; at a singular stationary face a flat direction continues to a lower-dimensional face with the same value. Increase ε until the two extrema touch. Compare 2 sources/2 readings and 3 sources/3 readings using identical absolute error scales for the first two clocks. These conditional limits do not presume any particular receiver noise or precision on the gravitational ruler. Add independent ruler error half-widths against the predicted gap.

**Additional later observables.** For a fixed ordinary cost the singular values of the late magnetic residual rows H=f_scalar T−f_ordinary after normalizing channels and whitening by E give exact extrema of the vector norm over all matched sources. A positive, numerically resolved smallest value means all nonzero states in this family leave *some* EM discrepancy across those later samples; it says nothing about measurement feasibility. A signed linear combination of the two withheld ruler clocks has its own symmetric C and extremal eigenvalues. Choosing its weights only on the coarse grids and keeping their absolute sum one produces a testable detector design without inflating a uniform ruler-noise budget. Thresholds for all possible sources of the same specified ordinary cost use the worst-case box-to-vector and energy-to-linear norms, not the better sensitivity of a selected source. No energy minimum or restriction on other possible current shapes is a parent field equation.
