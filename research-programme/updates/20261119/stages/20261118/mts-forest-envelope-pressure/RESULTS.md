# From a missed pressure term to a forest-scale force prediction — 18 November 2026
**The short-wave discrepancy has a physical source on the retained branch.** The averaged pressure is not just a function of density. At the earliest matching surface the extra current contribution is +(6.02 to 7.22)×10⁻⁶ of the mode norm. The gravitationally driven offset opposes it, −(3.54 to 4.93)×10⁻⁶; the change multiplying density is below 8×10⁻⁹. Their signed sum predicts why the old instantaneous formula missed the tolerance on three of four modes. This decomposition uses the original gauge and phase convention. Its terms are not new free parameters. See pressure-origin.csv.

**What was predicted rather than fitted.** We used only the saved a200 averaged density and momentum as the two field initial data. The other species came from the existing averaged cosmology and the homogeneous field from its own saved solution. Two initially frozen forecasts at a=0.0002125 and 0.0002375 recovered pressure, but updating only the density constraint missed the density/current transfer target by up to 7.05×10⁻⁵. Prescribing the entire averaged metric did better, but was a control, not a returned metric. The first forecasts and misses are preserved in pressure.csv. We then evolved the metric expansion itself, sourcing it with the field’s density **and pressure** in the Einstein trace, and froze a new forecast at a=0.0002600, beyond that first run. Against the unaveraged field solution, the largest normalized differences there were 2.36×10⁻⁸ in density, 9.10×10⁻⁸ in current and 2.10×10⁻¹⁰ in pressure, versus 2×10⁻⁵, 2×10⁻⁵ and 2×10⁻⁶ respectively. These are observed differences, not guaranteed error bounds: step refinements can be larger. Removing only the field pressure from the gravitational trace raises the current difference to 1.06×10⁻⁵ there; it degrades accuracy but does not itself breach 2×10⁻⁵. The other species are supplied in this brief overlap, not evolved afresh in response to every fast oscillation. The cold controls independently pass the integrated metric trace check (largest difference 1.33×10⁻⁷, relative to each peak expansion perturbation).

**Where the simpler fluid can take over.** The derived response predicts that just 1/4 modes meets the original simple-pressure threshold at a200, 2/4 at the first new epoch, and all four at the a225 and a250 continuous-density interfaces. We kept the failed a200 simple-pressure rows, used the sourced overlap, and independently propagated from both accepted interfaces with the full matter/radiation/metric solver. Through z=99 to 4.2, the largest relative interface change was 2.83×10⁻⁶ in force and 2.72×10⁻⁶ in either gas transfer. Replacing the forecast moments with directly projected ones changes the late forces by at most 1.05×10⁻¹⁰ at the same interface; gas readouts are identical at their native output precision. A tighter native integration emitted a Romberg warning (estimated error 7.04×10⁻¹⁴ vs requested 8.76×10⁻¹⁵); its largest force difference was 1.91×10⁻¹⁰ and both independent interface runs were retained. Earlier k=5/Mpc control reproduced to 1.36×10⁻⁷ or better.

**Physical payoff.** The four new comoving radial wavenumbers (7.0523, 7.3052, 7.5513, 7.7909 Mpc⁻¹) cover seven HIRES/MIKE entries; overlapping instruments are not seven independent field predictions. Restart all species identically at z=99, then remove only the heavy field pressure. At the observed radial minima the resulting gravitational force is **2.14–2.22 times** the pressure-on force; the baryon density transfer rises by **29.8–35.4%**. Keeping just the leading mass-derived wave term k²/(4a²m²) instead reproduces pressure-on force, baryon density and velocity to within 6.75×10⁻⁸ there. The two cold pressure interventions agree bit-for-bit. At fixed k, pressure-on gas transfer power is only 0.00394–0.00438 of the rolling-scalar all-cold branch’s for these rows. These are large, potentially discriminating differences in **linear matter and gas**, not measurements or a Lyα flux score. The seven entries no longer lack radial predictions; combining with the prior stage gives radial linear coverage for all 49 entries (28 distinct minima). Neither the continuous interval between every knot nor the transverse tail is thereby qualified.

Keep pressure, thermal history and flux conversion distinct. Do not assign a likelihood until nonlinear and observational response coverage are demonstrated. Nothing was published.
