# What is predicted

This uses the same positive quadratic heavy field on the retained flat common metric, not a new interaction. The cold control has the retained slow scalar but no heavy component. Background densities, masses and regular growing-mode normalization come from the retained preparation. Only linear perturbations are considered. No fitted galaxy parameter or observed galaxy velocity enters.

In synchronous CDM coordinates let a prime mean conformal time in Mpc, and let C be the fractional CDM density perturbation. The background satisfies φ″+2ℋφ′+a²m²φ=0. The perturbation U=δφ satisfies

    U″ + 2ℋU′ + (k²+a²m²)U = φ′ C′ .

The comoving heavy density (including the retained 8πGa² factor) is D=φ′U′+a²m²φU+3ℋφ′U. Pressure is φ′U′−a²m²φU, and momentum q=kφ′U. Signs follow the retained synchronous variables; D is not just the density in that coordinate system. Φ_W is the average of the two scalar metric potentials, with k²Φ_W=−(δρ_total+3ℋq_total/k+Π_total)/2 in the same stress units. The last term includes photon and neutrino shear. D=0 does not imply k²Φ_W=0.

**Independent preparation.** Write x=kτ, s=ak/α, where α is the native radiation scale. The mass coefficient multiplying s² in the dimensionless oscillator is (mα/k²)², the matter load is ω/k, Thomson rate is opacity/k, free-streaming speed is unchanged. Choosing the retained code's reference unit 5 means rescaling its input α, ω and masses by 5/k and its thermal clock by k/5; the physical scale factor and dimensionless sound speed do not change. Convert its outputs back with k/5 for conformal rates and (k/5)² for stresses. The thermal tables supply only background microphysics. Early field displacements and velocities, including the regular perturbations, are initialized from the adiabatic series; photon slip, polarization and additional angular moments vanish in the leading start used here. Halving that initial time, halving the integration step, increasing the moments, switching metric form and changing the integrator are separate controls. No post-start metric history is supplied. **Causal budget.** Linearity conditional on the self-consistent metric permits a sharper question than which frequencies appear in a fit. Integrating the tags is the retarded Green-function integral over each band, including propagation to the final time. Their signed stresses add at every sampled time. These are signed source contributions, not probabilities or a decomposition into independent universes.
