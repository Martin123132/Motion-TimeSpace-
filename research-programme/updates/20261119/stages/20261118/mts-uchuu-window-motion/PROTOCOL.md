# Survey-window test of bulk and internal tracer motions

Before scoring: use all four sealed Uchuu COMB galaxy catalogues, published North/South random windows, same selected galaxies, 0.2≤z_obs<0.5, original weights and previous real radii and displacements. Do not use observed BOSS values, alter the parent or tune a parameter to this test. Calibrate separate North/South velocity distributions and pair moments on cone 0. Freeze before evaluating cone 1 multipoles. These catalogues share simulation objects and were previously exposed to other statistics; this is not external cosmology validation.

Use Fourier modes in a 2816 h⁻¹ Mpc box, bands k=0.025,0.05 and stress bands 0.075,0.10 h/Mpc, each ±0.0025. Local endpoint Legendre orders 0/2. Subtract the weighted official random selection in each split with its actual weight ratio. Primary is a paired real-to-redshift power *difference* in units of Σgal w². It is not an infinite-volume power or an absolute BOSS amplitude. Fix a random two-way partition once per catalogue. Four times its symmetrized cross-power permits deterministic convolution of independent residuals with **no self-pairs or shot-noise approximation**. Also measure the conventional full-catalogue auto difference; self-terms cancel because weights and sight lines do not change.

Use all shell modes. Primary angular weights impose the isotropic order-2/4/6 polynomial identities on the Fourier lattice at the relevant k powers; also report standard equal-mode-weight results. Use one numerical thread, accurate nonuniform Fourier sums, exact direct-mode checks. Keep earlier tolerance 2e−5 for numerical identities; it is not a statistical pass threshold for a measured field. Save the effective window, counts, depth, uncertainty checks and normalizations. The official Uchuu paper and Bianchi et al. estimator are sources, not derivations by MTS.

Reference host-motion field: for centrally identified tracers use their observed bulk displacement; for previously matched satellites retain the sealed parent-central displacement. For satellites without a traceable host, use an explicitly labelled local-flow proxy: inverse-square average of eight nearest different-family central radial displacements in real sky coordinates, no adjustable smoothing scale. Test this subset against its own recorded total shift; its true motion is never set to zero or passed into its evaluation forecast. Predictions are conditional on actual mock central velocities, not generated by the MTS cosmological transfer function.

Forecasts fixed in advance: host/proxy only; cap-specific independent satellite residual (matched and missing separately); leave the same independent one-object distributions but add the derived angular mixed pair term through quartic order for true separations ≤10; and a distinct one-body control allowing residual mean linear in host/proxy displacement from cone-0 moments, no power fit. Represent each weighted velocity distribution by positive moment quadrature; verify its phase on the entire k range. Compare both multipoles, both caps, all bands and split vs full results. Use absolute errors in the shared normalization. Keep failures and cut contributions; no postselection among caps or modes. A failed small-pair transfer is reason to localize the remaining contributions, not to fit a new damping length.
