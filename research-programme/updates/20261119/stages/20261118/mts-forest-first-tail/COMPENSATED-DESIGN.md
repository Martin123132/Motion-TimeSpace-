# A finite-support observable for the retained linear model

A tail one cannot bound individually need not prevent every comparison. At one redshift let x_i=(q_i/q_max)^2, q_i the comoving radial wavenumber of sightline bin i. At fixed temperature the unknown contribution of *all* total wavenumbers k>K≥max(q_i) is C exp(−u x_i)(M0+2x_i M1+x_i²M2). Here u=2d at the highest bin, C is independent of i and M is a positive Gram matrix. This follows by squaring the parent's linear response p(k)+x_i v(k); no shape assumption about unmeasured density and velocity is needed. For observed velocity units, u is proportional to the square of the velocity wavenumber, so the exponential factor does not require a common cosmological H(z).

Freeze weights before acquiring the new lower-wavenumber sources or reading any measured flux values. At z=4.2 use the seven previously recorded HIRES radial centers. A weighted linear combination of *powers* with weights orthogonal to exp(−u x_i)x_i^m for m=0,1,2 has zero contribution from every k≥max q_i. Its prediction is an integral between the selected minimum and maximum radial wavenumber, including the moving lower limit of each bin. The individual powers still have unknown tails. A contrast may be signed; do not apply a positive-power lower bound to the contrast itself.

Prespecify (A) the highest four bins, exact null for the common 10,000 K; (B) all seven bins, one and the same weight vector nulling both distinct frozen equal-root temperatures; (C) all seven bins with weights at 10,000 K orthogonal through polynomial degree five. C cancels the first three derivatives of the damping factor as well for any quadratic Gram combination. Gram coefficients may depend on the true temperature; the identity is applied at the *same* true temperature, so their derivatives need not be bounded. For a uniform T in [9,000,11,000] the relative exponential Taylor remainder yields
     
    |unseen contribution to C| ≤ exp(smax) smax⁴ / 24 × Σ |w_i| P_i(total),
    smax = 0.1 u(10,000 K).

This last inequality uses positivity of the individual powers and is a bound relative to measurable total powers, not a guarantee relative to a near-zero contrast. Hold the weights fixed for all arms. Compare roundoff/high-k suppression on actual independently acquired fields as well as exact arithmetic. Report cancellation noise, mesh errors, and any loss of pressure sensitivity rather than choosing the statistic that scored best. Source 49 modes from the six log intervals between the seven q values (8 subdivisions each); re-use controls and independent matching surfaces. Do not compare with measured flux or infer signal-to-noise without bin response functions, covariance, and noise treatment. Finite bin windows must replace point-bin columns by their window integrals; appreciable redshift mixing, thermal inhomogeneity, non-Gaussian broadening or additional angular structure need a new test.
