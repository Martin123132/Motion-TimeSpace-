# The conserved scalar current survives a full-interval, live-gravity test

The same initial parent scalar was evolved through both matching surfaces with cold matter, baryons, photons, neutrinos and metric perturbations evolving together. The other-species trace was **not** held fixed. We changed the solver's inverse Friedmann-time grid, not the field action, pressure law or matter coupling. All results are private calculations on the retained cosmology and thermal model, not fits.

**Direct current balance.** Four frozen modes, k=8.6793, 14.2705, 29.7993 and 37 Mpc⁻¹, 150,561 diagnostic times spanning both interfaces, about 11,700 carrier radians between the two centers. At every interior time, compare the actual change in aQ with an independent integral of akP from the initial surface. The worst discrepancies were 4.83×10⁻⁶ on the 0.24-radian inverse mesh and **2.84×10⁻⁷** at 0.12 radians, normalized to the initial mode-current scale. Changing only the pressure quadrature moved the finer result by at most 1.03×10⁻⁹. These are maxima on a sampled interval, not continuum error bounds. The previous 1.26×10⁻⁸ number was for a much shorter supplied-trace control; it is not the comparator for a full-interval error. See impulse-fine.csv.

**Prediction across the match.** Start the averaged native hierarchy only from the earlier phase-projected density/current near a=0.0012; compare near a=0.0015 to a separately evolved microscopic field. On the four fine-mesh modes the worst density/current/pressure errors are 1.28×10⁻⁵ / 8.71×10⁻⁶ / 4.97×10⁻⁸, in their original mode normalizations. There is no normalization fit or copied later seed. A second matched run starts instead from the directly projected later state to test downstream sensitivity.

**The late gravity test.** Across seven readout redshifts, z=99 to 4.2, the two native initial surfaces agree to **4.73×10⁻⁷** relative or better in unheated force, gas density and gas velocity on the four modes, including pressure-on and switched-off interventions. The standalone unheated gas calculation using the pressure-on external radiation agrees to 1.37×10⁻⁷ in peak normalization with the autonomous hierarchy. The full initial vectors are identical before switching pressure within each pair. At z=99 the raw heavy current differs by at most 1.48×10⁻⁶ *relative* across the matching surfaces; the raw heavy density can differ by 1.76×10⁻⁴ relative, so do not translate the force agreement into identical fractional precision for every field coordinate. Disabling the private clock reproduces the saved cold control bit-for-bit. Enabling it moves the four warm all-cold gas controls by at most 5.26×10⁻¹² relative, and switching heavy pressure with no heavy occupation gives bit-identical native histories.

At z=4.2 removing heavy pressure INCREASES the native force by factors **2.314–2.408** at these four modes. A pressureless limit is not a disappearing gravitational source. The distinction matters when connecting MTS to local Newtonian measurements: the action fixes this branch's metric coupling, but the cosmic state does not by itself fix a galaxy's local density or environment.
