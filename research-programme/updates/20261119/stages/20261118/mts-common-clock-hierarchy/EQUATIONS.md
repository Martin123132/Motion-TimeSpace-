# One clock for the microscopic field and gravity

The retained action, quadratic mass, perturbation equations, metric couplings, background abundance and microscopic phase trajectory are unchanged. The change is to the inverse time coordinate used by the numerical solver.

**Clock.** If N=ln a and x=mt, then dx/dN=am/ℋ and dτ/da=1/(aℋ). We provide only additional abscissae a, NOT a supplied H(a), perturbation or τ(a). The private native solver evaluates its own dτ/da there with the actual species, slow field, and exact or matched heavy density of that run. This matters: using the exact heavy background time map even after a dust switch would be inconsistent. A mesh uniform in x/δx+N/δN_max resolves the carrier while retaining slow-background resolution. In the heavy interval the full hierarchy uses the resulting inverse a(τ) rather than resampling it from a second, coarser thermal spline. When conformal a′ is requested it is the derivative of the *same* inverse. Opacity, sound speed and other thermal quantities are still tabulated by the original recombination model on the revised background. Exact→averaged switching already locates the target a with that inverse. The integration is neither a per-mode clock calibration nor a free parameter in the physics. Coarse, fine and most-resolved diagnostic carrier spacings are 0.24, 0.12 and 0.06 radians, not physical coupling strengths.

**Action current and work.** In synchronous gauge let f,v=f′ be the homogeneous field and F,V=F′ its perturbation. Primes denote conformal time. The heavy outputs are conformal stresses; with conserved dust normalization ρ⋆ set

    D = a(vV+a²m²fF)/ρ⋆,   P = a(vV−a²m²fF)/ρ⋆,   Q = akvF/ρ⋆ .
    v′ = −2ℋv−a²m²f,   V′ = −2ℋV−(k²+a²m²)F+vJ,   J = −kZ.

The same metric expansion J appears in the synchronous ordinary cold-matter continuity equation δ_c′=J. Return the heavy density AND pressure in the native Einstein evolution, with the other species live. Direct differentiation gives the microscopic identities

    (aQ)′ = akP,
    D′ = −3ℋP−kQ+βJ,       β = av²/ρ⋆ .

The metric work multiplies the instantaneous heavy enthalpy; β is **not** identically one for an oscillating real scalar. It becomes one for background dust in the averaged equations. No dissipative source is introduced. The second identity needs a metric driver independent of the heavy momentum diagnostic. With baryon fraction f_b among the ordinary baryons and cold matter, native combined growth g obeys J=gℋ[(1−f_b)δ_c+f_bδ_b]+k f_b v_b in the retained transfer convention. A separate δ_c continuity comparison checks that reconstruction. The earlier cold branch has no heavy occupation and requires no division by its zero density.

**Connection to the gravity limit.** For this minimally coupled canonical subfamily, ∇_μT^μν=(□f−m²f)∇^νf; the two linear identities test its energy and longitudinal momentum projections on the specified cosmological solution. The coefficient β is a³(ρ_h+p_h)/ρ⋆ and is fixed by the kinetic part of the parent stress. Taking small averaged pressure makes the Euler equation geodesic; it does **not** remove the field's comoving density D+3ℋQ/k from the scalar metric constraint. Thus one must distinguish recovering pressureless motion on the common metric from recovering the ordinary-source-only Newtonian potential in a locally unoccupied environment. The first has an intervention test here. The second still needs an independently prepared local population/boundary and a bound on its gravitating comoving density. The cosmological calculation cannot choose that boundary for a galaxy.

**Transport between averaged endpoints.** A microstate at a single instantaneous phase is not the phase-averaged initial state of a fluid. Let Π_i be the retained carrier-harmonic removal functional at each endpoint. With A(τ)=∫akP dτ, set C=(Q₁−Π₁[A/a])/Π₁[1/a], and Q_prediction=(C+A)/a. Construct I=∫[−3ℋP−kQ_prediction+βJ]dτ, and D_prediction=D₁+I−Π₁I. Interior and final projected densities are read only after forming the transport. These are conservation tests using dynamically computed pressure and metric work; they do not predict a pressure history from two endpoint numbers. Replacing only β by 1 is a distinct counterfactual. **Late test.** The native averaged comparison uses its preexisting P=c_s²(D+3ℋQ/k), c_s=k/(am+√(a²m²+k²)). At z=99 we restart paired native runs from identical saved full states and change only subsequent heavy pressure in the off arm. An agreement of these late observables need not imply high *fractional* precision of a heavy density or a spectral kernel near a zero; both raw and physically normalized errors are kept.

**A sufficient missing-band bound, without assuming a cutoff.** Put Δ²_X=k³P_R(k)|X(k)|²/(2π²), and σ_X²=∫_K^∞Δ²_X d ln k for the baryon density D_b and its dimensionless velocity-divergence transfer U_b. For fixed k_x the linear power tail is πℋc A² exp(−2d) times the integral of the primordial-normalized squared combination [B D_b+(k_x/k)² U_b] divided by k over d ln k. Positivity, k≥K and the triangle inequality for this weighted squared norm give

    P_tail ≤ [πℋc A² exp(−2d)/K] ( |B|σ_D + (k_x/K)² σ_U )² .

Thus a specified fraction of the acquired partial power translates to a concrete sufficient upper limit on the still-unseen density and velocity variances. The last measured octave's variance is **not** that upper limit. The bound requires finite variances and the same conditional linear transfer model; it is not an assertion that the universe follows the prescribed heating law or that the missing tail is small. missing-tail-budget.csv evaluates the required ceilings, not an invented infinite integral.

There is a converse **necessary** condition for erasing the finite-band contrast. Write the exact, positive partial powers as W and C for wave and rolling cold. For the total wave power to reach a fraction f of total cold, its tail must supply at least max(0,f C−W), *regardless* of the cold tail. Combining with the bound above gives a lower limit on the weighted tail RMS needed for that outcome, not a prediction of that RMS. The CSV substitutes numerical partial powers, so their frequency-quadrature uncertainty carries into those thresholds.

**Disentangling pressure from the prescribed gas temperature.** At fixed radial wavenumber and expansion history the unsquared response is R=B D_b+μ² U_b, where μ=k_x/k, B=2−(γ−1)(0.7+d), and d is proportional to the temperature normalization T. A positive overall flux or broadening prefactor cannot move a simple zero. Differentiating at λ=ln(T/T₀)=0 gives

    R_λ = B Z_D + μ² Z_U − (γ−1)d D_b,
    dk_null/dλ = −R_λ / (B ∂_k D_b + μ² ∂_k U_b − 2μ² U_b/k).

Here Z_D and Z_U are *dynamical* derivatives, not refits. For the saved affine gas system Y_N=L Y+s the tangent satisfies Z_N=L Z+L_λ Y+s_λ, Z(initial)=0 when the preparation precedes heating. The fixed external radiation history and background have s_λ=0. In the existing implementation only the baryon velocity-potential row of L depends on the added heat; its driving term is −(a/ℋ)c_hot² times the baryonic coordinate Y_b. Project Z through the same metric constraint as Y; in particular Z_D=Z_b+3 δΦ[Z], not just Z_b. The prescribed hot term acts on the evolved coordinate Y_b rather than on an arbitrarily substituted gauge density. This is a sensitivity of the retained prescription, not a derivation of a reionization or gauge-covariant heating law.

For the limited band the integrand derivative follows directly as prefactor × (2R R_λ−2d R²), including the thermal broadening of the power. It is finite at R=0; never divide by R there. This supplies a temperature prediction independent of evaluating the score at the test temperatures. Freeze signed amplitudes and root shifts before independently evolving altered temperatures.
