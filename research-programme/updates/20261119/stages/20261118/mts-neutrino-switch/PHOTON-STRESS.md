# Bridge to polarized photon stress in the actual saved plasma

The same metric coupling can now be tested with electromagnetic radiation, not just a neutrino approximation. Use an independent unit photon intensity quadrupole, and separately a unit linear-polarization quadrupole with zero initial intensity. These are normalized linear probes on the two saved plasmas; neither unit pulse is the sky or a new fitted source.

For finite Thomson rate κ_x=opacity/k and fixed matching of all initial densities and fluxes, Fγ=4vγ/3 obeys Fγ,x=−2πγ/3+…; drag is initially zero and generates baryon motion only later. Baryon density does not enter the leading radiation density term. Thus replace f_r by fγ in the short-time shear kernel. At fixed normalized initial intensity quadrupole, the leading scalar-pressure response relative to the same massless-neutrino quadrupole must be fγ/f_r. This limit is at fixed finite collision rate; taking an exactly tight-coupled fluid first is not the same limit.

An initial polarization E2=1 with πγ=0 produces πγ,x(0)=0.6 κ_x from the retained Thomson quadrupole block. Consequently its scalar-pressure response begins at order five, with coefficient (φ′*)² fγ (0.6 κ_x)/(60 g*²), whereas the initial intensity-shear response is fourth order. As a surgical null remove only the E2→intensity term in the collision matrix (including its implicit inverse); polarization may decay and stream but cannot create intensity or metric forcing from these initial data. Use the actual radiation, baryon and scalar coefficients on each branch. Set local probe times from the faster baryon–photon relaxation time R/[κ_x(1+R)], check trace and constraint evolution and finer steps, and measure where the leading short-time term starts to depart. This is a prediction about the retained EM radiation stress coupling, not a derivation of electric charge or of the microscopic Maxwell action.

The polarization-only null is about a *linear polarization perturbation with zero initial intensity perturbation* on an existing photon background. It does not say polarized light carries no energy or exerts no gravity.
