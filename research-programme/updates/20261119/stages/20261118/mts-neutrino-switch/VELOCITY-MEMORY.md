Compare both preparations without another native run. Start once at 1999 using the independently saved native and switched scalar states. Stop at the *first* native sampling gap exceeding 1/40 in x (one fortieth of the unit gradient oscillation time). This restriction is for reconstructing a continuous right-hand side from the saved clocks; it does not remove any failures from the full 2000–4252 count.

With y=(δφ,δφ_x), write y'=A y + (0,C_x U/k) where A acts as (v,−[1+(am/k)²]u−2Hv/k), U is the archived homogeneous conformal field velocity, and C_x is the common-metric CDM drive. For S=switched geometry and N=native reference the exact linear difference obeys

    d(y_S−y_N)/dx = A_S (y_S−y_N) + (A_S−A_N)y_N
                     + (0,(C_S−C_N)U_N/k + C_S(U_S−U_N)/k).

Propagate the separate inherited field displacement and velocity with the homogeneous operator A_S. Integrate the three remaining sources from zero. The readout P=L y changes separately through (L_S−L_N)y_N. Record any native replay defect and switched replay defect explicitly rather than forcing them into a physical source.
