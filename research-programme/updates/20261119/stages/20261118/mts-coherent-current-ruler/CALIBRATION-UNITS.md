# How to use the magnetic calibration, and what its units are
For the specified prepared source family, read a signed radial magnetic field twice at the pole on a neutral freely falling clock. Do not substitute flux density squared, luminosity, an arbitrary sky projection, or the matter's rest clock. Positive and negative signs use the same orientation at both times. The output is the perturbation of pole-minus-equator log volume of initially round freely released neighbour clouds, not a redshift, gravitational-wave strain, or rotation curve.

If the known parent length unit is L* metres, physical clock times are 3 L*/c and 4 L*/c (late test 6.4 L*/c). Geometrized magnetic amplitude b = L* sqrt(4π G/μ₀) B_SI / c². This follows by matching the parent's magnetic energy b²/2 to (4π G L*²/c⁴) B_SI²/(2μ₀). For a given later clock the conditional prediction in SI readouts is therefore
    δln(Vpole/Veq) = [4π G L*²/(μ₀ c⁴)] (B₃,B₄) H (B₃,B₄)ᵀ,
where B are in tesla and H is the corresponding numerical matrix in field-to-volume-matrices.csv. The dimensionless common spin scale is absorbed in the measured fields: scaling the currents by ε scales B by ε and the leading δlnV by ε². The two clocks do not themselves fix the independent matter scale or establish that an emitter has the stipulated current family. If scalar physics fixes L* through its Compton length, do not tune L* to the experiment as an extra free parameter.

For errors with known covariance C in the two dimensionless field readouts, a zero-mean additive measurement error introduces the quadratic noise bias tr(H C); the first-order variance is 4 BᵀH C H B. Non-Gaussian errors need not have vanishing third moments. The bound reported instead assumes only a maximum absolute Euclidean error norm s, not a covariance, independent channels or a specific instrument. It pertains only to the calibrated family. A source-shape ambiguity in the absence of measurement noise is a separate issue; smaller error bars cannot remove it.
