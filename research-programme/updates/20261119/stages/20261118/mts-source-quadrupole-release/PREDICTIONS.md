# Frozen launch comparison

Reuse the sealed charged/ordinary, full-scalar and 1/160-scalar preparations, both saved radial meshes, balanced source spin law, no fitted coefficients and tolerance 2e-5. Compare at the poles and equator of an intrinsically round equal-area launch sphere, not a common gauge coordinate in an arbitrarily deformed geometry. Release neutral radial separations initially at relative rest. Include a charge-zero control. Classify results for the conformal preparation as conditional: the conserved source does not determine arbitrary free tensor waves.

Predictions to try before looking at the numbers:
1. In the preparation with no added even conformal-shape or transverse momentum perturbation, the dust spin and its interior frame dragging have a definite-sign Hamiltonian quadrupole. They do not cancel just because the total angular momentum is zero. The exterior conformal coefficient should be negative. Derive the source normalization and direction of radial relative acceleration, compare *independent* constraint inversions and both original meshes. Relative discrepancies must also be scored on the scalar-minus-ordinary difference, not just on the absolute field.
2. Outside ordinary matter, on the round sphere the initial electric monopole strength is unchanged; a change to the pole–equator volume acceleration can arise only through the scalar sampled on the displaced shell, not a new charge force. A nonzero radial deformation need not imply a nonzero volume trace. Test this cancellation without subtracting rounded large nearly equal numbers as a sole check.
3. Compare an exact rotating vacuum and electrovac geometry to a separately derived analytic polar/equatorial tide **on a round sphere**. The Kerr controls have their own tensor geometry and net angular momentum; do not use them to choose a material quadrupole. Recover the far-field inverse-fifth-power radial-tide dependence.
4. For the actual emitted dipole propagate its known direct Maxwell stress projection on the saved histories. Keep it separate from induced even Maxwell, scalar, matter and metric perturbations. Determine explicitly at least one piece of unconstrained initial tensor data so that a conditioned launch prediction cannot be mistaken for a unique formation history.
