# The missing evolution at wave arrival: which source terms to carry forward

Prior to the causal front the angular field of the initially balanced current is absent from the measured exterior. Beyond it, switching on only the dipole's local energy is not a complete Einstein or Maxwell calculation. A convenient starting action uses the same axial Killing orbit, without imposing spherical symmetry:
    ds²=g_ab dxᵃdxᵇ+λ²(dφ+w)²,         A₄=a+χ(dφ+w),
    W=dw,   f=da,   F₄=(f+χW)+dχ∧(dφ+w).
Here a,b run over time and two meridional coordinates. λ is a circumference radius, not a photon wavelength. In the retained geometric units, integrating over φ gives, up to the Einstein boundary divergence and the overall 2π/(16π) factor,
    ∫√−g λ [ R₃−λ²W²/4−(f+χW)²−2(∇χ)²/λ²−2(∇h)²−2h² ].
This is a rewriting of the classical minimal sector; omitted higher parent operators have not been set to zero in all applications. A material parcel at fixed specific canonical axial angular momentum j has Routhian per rest mass
    −√(1+(j−ηχ)²/λ²) dτ₃ + η a_a dxᵃ + j w_a dxᵃ.
Fix j when taking a meridional derivative, *then* evaluate j=εℓ(N)sin²θ; otherwise one differentiates the initial condition as if it were a new force. At background χ=ε A sin²θ, λ=r sinθ the prescribed dipole's direct angular momentum forcing per unit ε² contains (α/W_dust)(ℓ²−η²A²)sinθcosθ/r². Polar geometry and the induced even electric field still have to be added. The trace/stress and λ variations should be performed **before** eliminating the twist by its constraint; substituting an on-shell stress directly into an unfixed action can reverse a sign.

The conserved horizontal Maxwell displacement is √−g λ(f+χW)^ab, not √−g λ f^ab. In parent conventions w=−ωdt−βdr, W_tr=C=ω_r−β_t, f₀,tr=−FQ/r², F=αa_rad. The known coefficient in √−g λ χ W^rt/sinθ is r² A C sin²θ/F, with l=2 coefficient −2r²AC/(3F). In the balanced exterior C/F=4QA/r⁴. The checkpoint evaluates this term on the saved emitted histories; it is a gauge-decomposition contribution to the flux, not charge production or a full electric quadrupole. Ordinary and scalar use precisely the same coupling.

The direct physical Maxwell stress on the radial observer's frame can be kept separately. Set eφ=T sinθ, bθ=S sinθ, b_r=Z cosθ (wave parts only). The l=2 coefficients of energy, radial flux, radial stress and mean tangential stress in 4π units are (Z²−T²−S²)/3, 2TS/3, −(Z²+T²+S²)/3, Z²/3 respectively. The angular flux and mixed radial stress along DθP₂ have coefficients −TZ/3 and SZ/3; the tangential tracefree coefficient along DθDθP₂+3P₂ is (T²−S²)/3. Independent sky integration and comparison to the parent's monopole test these normalizations. The direct pole-minus-equator Ricci source is Z²−T²−S², not the full observed tide. In the local ordinary reference its sign changes over the saved transient. Before attributing any such feature to the neutral detector, propagate metric, polar electric displacement, scalar and material response with the interior constraints, no extra fitted tensor waveform. Keep both scalar preparations as controls, and separately identify signals common to them.
