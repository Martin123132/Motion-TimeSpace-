# Companion test before extending the nonlinear window

Use actual opacity, baryon loading and native metric histories to distinguish two competing continuations before the same heavy-field switch: (i) ideal photon–baryon acoustics, (ii) polarized Thomson angular transport plus a separate dragged baryon velocity. No fit, phase adjustment or damping amplitude. Compare density, momentum, baryon velocity and photon quadrupole to the original native tracks and refine the angular cutoff. A <1% of peak agreement for each full-domain quantity permits reuse of the *linear* operator. This test alone never promotes a quadratic result past kτ=20. Include a polarization-disabled control to test if the term matters numerically and retain all assumptions about cold electrons and baryon pressure.
