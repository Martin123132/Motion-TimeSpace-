# Diagnosed a time-table error and repaired the actual short evolution

**The numerical problem and the physical test are distinguishable.** For a canonical scalar the exact conformal momentum obeys (aQ)′=akP, from its own action, before any equation of state is imposed. Integrating directly sampled pressure in windows surrounding the two matching times missed the raw current change by up to 5.28×10⁻⁵ in the prior current normalization. Dropping every second integration sample barely changed this. The native perturbation clock obtains a(τ) from a thermal lookup. Its derivative need not equal the Friedmann clock of the supplied homogeneous scalar. The calculable residual from that mismatch, with no coefficient fitted, explains the short-window defect: the largest unexplained residual across both outer panels and the separate nine notch modes is 6.45×10⁻⁸ after the small homogeneous-table term is included. This diagnostic uses the actual microscopic field F and background; it is not a new force. See DERIVATION.md and clock-defect-*.csv.

**We then repaired the evolution, not just the diagnostic.** Four modes, two separate carrier windows, two integration steps, identical initial fields, and the same external density/pressure. Re-evolving the heavy perturbation with its density AND pressure returned to the metric trace on the old lookup reproduces the native field to 2.44×10⁻⁸ on its component normalization. From each window's starting state the worst normalized current-balance error is 8.69×10⁻⁵. On the same Friedmann clock for the field, returning metric and pressure integral, it is **1.26×10⁻⁸** (2.18×10⁻⁹ on the finer step). The two reported unrepaired maxima use different integration origins: center in clock-defect, start in common-clock-transport. This is a real short-time dynamics test with supplied external species, not an a posteriori correction to the full flux integral.

**A separate end-to-end control improved the interface comparison.** At k=8.6793, 14.2705, 29.7993 and 37 Mpc⁻¹ we repeated the full field acquisition, matching, native radiation hierarchy and resolved gas response with a four-times-denser native background lookup. Both a≈0.0012 and 0.0015 were independently used. The worst normalized current difference at the second interface fell from **1.434×10⁻³ to 9.963×10⁻⁶**. Across these four modes the original late wave/off interface checks went from **4/8 to 8/8** at the fixed 2×10⁻⁵ criterion. The selected modes were numerical diagnostics, not a random sample of the full band. Doubling then quadrupling the clock lookup does *not* monotonically eliminate the microscopic short-window identity error. The explicit shared-clock re-evolution and the refined native full-history test are not the same correction. No trial replaces the original 37-point estimates.
