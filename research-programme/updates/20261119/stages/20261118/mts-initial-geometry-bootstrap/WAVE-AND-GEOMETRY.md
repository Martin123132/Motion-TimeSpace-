# Closing the low-order first-exit estimate

This is a finite-time classical calculation, not an empirical density bound. Use the signed parent's exact spherical Einstein–massive-Klein–Gordon–dust equations, normalizations and initially resting, phase-zero prepared cloud. Initial scalar momentum and current vanish. Distinguish the density D specified on the initial polar slice from the proper rest density ρ and total Eulerian energy e. Set c=m=1; a,α are the radial metric factor and lapse, F=αa, B=α/a, M is Misner–Sharp mass, s and j the total radial stress and outward flux. See DENSITY-SLOPE.md for the nontrivial source derivative and LIFESPAN.md for continuation. All suprema in the first-exit argument are simultaneous upper bounds, *not* measured history inputs.

**Scalar regular through the origin.** Put u=r h, p=rΠ, w±=p±u_r, z±=∂r w±, V=F(1+K), K=2M/r³−ρ−h². The simplification uses e−s=ρ+h², true even for moving dust. The constraints give F_r/F=r a²(e+s) and B_r=FrK. With D±=∂t∓B∂r the exact equations are
    u_t=Bp;  D± w±=±B_r w±−V u;
    D± z±=±2B_r z± ± B_rr w±−V u_r−V_r u.
Use regular odd extension of u,p rather than a singular inner boundary. Let N be the maximum of |u|, |w±|, |z±| in the chosen dimensionless units. Oddness gives |Π|≤N, |h|≤N, |X=h_r|≤N/2 since X=r⁻²∫₀ʳ s u_rr ds. Also |rΠ_r|, |rX_r|≤2N and |rX|≤2N. Hence e_h≤9N²/8 including the centre. This positivity estimate costs more regularity than the integrated H from the parent; see CONCENTRATION.md for why.

On the first-exit box, take N≤0.001, |rρ_r|≤0.003. In particular ε=1.125e-6 bounds e_h. The initially isolated scalar stays inside r≤26+t≤33 because B=F(1−2M/r)≤1. The dust remains inside 5.1 from the shell estimate below. Throughout the scalar support, |rK_r|≤4E+0.003+4N², E=0.0008, using M_r=r²e. Consequently |B_rr|≤(1+sup|rF_r|)sup|K|+sup|rK_r|. The product |V_r u|, **not V_r in isolation**, is ≤[sup|F_r|(1+sup|K|)+sup|rK_r|]N since u=rh. This eliminates the need for an unweighted density derivative at the centre. Inside the dust use |B_r|≤5.1 sup|K|. Outside use 2M∞/5.1²+33N². Bound F_r and rF_r similarly using e≤ε outside the dust. The maximum of the five characteristic right-hand sides is ≤kN in the integral sup-norm estimate; bootstrap.py evaluates k<1.03665. Therefore N(t)≤N(0)exp(kt). This conservative estimate loses the free carrier's oscillatory cancellation; it is not a prediction of exponential instability.

**No trapping, shell crossing or redshift assumption as an input.** Label an initially normal dust geodesic by χ and its proper time by τ. Use A=R/χ, J its normalized proper radial Jacobi separation, U=∂τ. Equal initial proper clocks give A=J=1 and UA=UJ=0 here. Number conservation gives ρ=ρ₀/(JA²), ρ₀=D/a₀. Trial bounds on A,J,v immediately give e≤Dmax W²/(Jmin Amin²)+ε<E. Initial ADM≤M_b+ε26³/3<0.010; it remains conserved for the isolated solution. If the trial compactness is c, then M≤min(E r³/3,M∞), so
    2M/r ≤ 2(E/3)^(1/3) M∞^(2/3).
Also ∫re dr≤E(3M∞/E)^(2/3)/2: at fixed ∫r²e the largest integral packs e=E from the centre outward (or integrate the enclosed-mass bound by parts). With F(∞)=1, |s|≤e, this gives F≥exp[−E(3M∞/E)^(2/3)/(1−c)]. These bounds apply to material and vacuum, without assuming α is a fitted external function.

Write Q=(Π²−X²)/a² and s'_h for the scalar radial pressure in the comoving frame. Then U²R=−M/R²−R s'_h and U²J=(2M/R³−Q)J−ρ₀/A². Since |Q|≤2ε and |s'_h|≤(1+vmax)ε/(1−vmax), these give both upper and lower margins for A,J by two integrations, even at χ→0. The maximum of M/R² is ≤(E/3)^(2/3)M∞^(1/3). For all radii the scalar force term vanishes outside r=33. Since |v|≤a|UR| and τ≤t, all margins improve before t=6.5: |A−1|<0.00578; 0.98446<J<1.01189; |v|<0.00609; 2M/r<0.00598. B>0.98506, V>0.99022 and every proper clock reaches τ=6.4. With no scalar, the exact initially resting ordinary Lemaître–Tolman shells obey the same estimate, independently of fitting. For the scalar, the one outstanding coupled quantity is |rρ_r|; the next note **derives** its improvement rather than assigning this derivative a prior.

**What is initially small?** For the locked prepared profile Π=0 and h=0.0008 λ T(r)(1+χ_p(r)); χ_p is obtained from Δχ_p=2Φ_b before its smooth cutoff and normalization at r=12. Include both derivatives of the actual cutoffs; the unit-width normalized beta(6,6) taper has |T'|≤2772/1024, |T''|≤2772×5/256. The two tapers' derivative supports do not overlap. Bounding |rh|, |(rh)'| and |(rh)''| gives N(0)≤0.02205483 |λ| from the initial prescription, with no evolved numbers. The printed arithmetic uses unrounded constants. For |λ|<5.37e-5 the N first exit is excluded through t=6.5. Inserting ε(λ)=9[N(0)exp(6.5k)]²/8 into the parent's comoving comparison, initial μ≤εχ³/3, initial radial speed mismatch zero, yields a simultaneous 2e-5 ruler guarantee at 2.5 and 6 for |λ|≤1.44e-5. This sufficient condition does not decide the parent's 1/160 or full-amplitude trajectory. Its merit is deriving the margins from initial data, not improving the fitted precision or selecting a physical galactic λ.
