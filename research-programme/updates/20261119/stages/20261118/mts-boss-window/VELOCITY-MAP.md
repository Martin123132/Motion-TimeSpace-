# Follow-up after the frozen BOSS comparison: velocity mapping, not a damping fit

The ordering changed under a fixed phenomenological velocity dispersion. Instead of choosing its best value, use the retained coherent baryon+CDM velocity, and carry the actual redshift coordinate change one order beyond the linear screen. This diagnostic was motivated by an exposed result: it is not an independent cosmology test.

Assumptions: Gaussian primordial curvature; deterministic real-space tracer δ_g=bδ_cb; no velocity bias or internal virial motion; existing irrotational, linear, equal-time inputs. These are not a derived galaxy population. Number conservation in s=x+n u, u=v_parallel/(aH), gives the Fourier transform of (1+bδ) exp(−i k_parallel u) minus the zero mode. This is the usual streaming relation (Scoccimarro 2004, https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0407214); the application and checks below use the retained MTS and control transfers. A uniform boost multiplies the mode by a phase and cannot change its power. In particular the one-point velocity variance alone is not a physical bulk damping kernel.

Absorb sqrt(P_R) into the density and velocity-divergence transfers D and U; they now have units Mpc^(3/2). Put K=k_parallel, L_p=p_parallel U(p)/p², q=k−p. The linear power is (b D(k)+μ² U(k))². The symmetric quadratic kernel from the Taylor series is Z2=[bK(D(p)L_q+D(q)L_p)+K² L_p L_q]/2. Gaussian pair contractions give P22=2∫ d³p/(2π)³ Z2². The cubic×linear contractions give P13=−K² σ_u² P_linear with σ_u²=∫ dp U(p)²/(6π²); density–velocity same-point contraction vanishes by parity. P22 regenerates the long-translation part subtracted by P13. Both are required. This keeps only the coordinate mapping through order P_R², not all nonlinear gravitational, formation or tracer terms at that order. Do not interpret it as a complete one-loop theory or a virial dispersion. The saved density is in the inherited synchronous prescription, not a new relativistic light-cone tracer model.

Before evaluating this extension: use both caps, original primary bins, per-cap nuisance freedom and all three branches. Use physical p,q from 1e-5 through 5 Mpc^-1 and repeat with 0.35 and 1 Mpc^-1 as internal cutoffs. All external modes needed by the windows lie inside the smallest cutoff. Check transverse invariance, positive P22, soft cancellation, independent azimuth/radial quadrature, spectral/angular interpolation and that perturbative contributions are small in the region of interpretation. Include every fixed comparison (worse or better); do not fit a dispersion or pick a cutoff to the data. High-k window leakage is a separate test from changing the loop cutoff.
