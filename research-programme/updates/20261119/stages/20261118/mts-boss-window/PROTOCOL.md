# BOSS DR12 low-redshift shape test — prior to reading measured amplitudes

Use Beutler & McDonald (2021), arXiv:2106.06324 public pre-reconstruction low-redshift NGC/SGC products at z=0.38. For each cap use its own 2048-mock covariance, full five-pole window W, wide-angle matrix M and ten-mode-bin-weighted published measurements. Apply W M to the predicted three even multipoles before selecting observations. Do not sum these scores with the existing four-point compressed growth χ²; this catalogue is exposed.

Primary sample: monopole and quadrupole for nominal bin centres 0.02 ≤ k < 0.10 h/Mpc. Fit one constant positive tracer bias (optimizer range 0.5–4) and one additive, free residual monopole noise per cap and branch, inside the window. Do not fit background, field fraction, primordial normalization, tilt, AP ratios or velocity amplitude. Separately report the two caps; a sum assumes zero cross-cap covariance. Finite-mock precision correction (2048−p−2)/2047 on the selected covariance. No partial inverse selected from a larger precision matrix.

Use the saved cb-density and cb-velocity transfers for a linear comoving tracer (a tracer assumption, not a galaxy occupation law). The observed angular power follows from the radial redshift-space displacement: δ_g^s = b D_cb + μ² U_cb in curvature-seed units. Use the saved model D_M and H to remap k and μ and the Fourier volume element from the survey's flat Ωm=0.31 fiducial coordinates. No acoustic-ruler rescaling in this uncompressed k-space mapping. Velocity dispersion zero in the primary linear model. Full source table to 0.4 h/Mpc is needed inside W even when observed bins are cut. Use the same procedure for fixed heavy, same-slow-field all-cold, and matched ΛCDM. Use radiation-active spectra for the two MTS-family controls as a numerical cross-check.

Frozen sensitivities: primary cuts tightened to 0.08 and extended to 0.12; add the hexadecapole on the primary interval; a total-density/CDM-velocity tracer assignment; Gaussian **power** damping exp[−(k_true μ_true σ)²] at fixed σ=3 h⁻¹ Mpc; turn off AP as a diagnostic only. Measure leakage by changing input clustering above 0.15 h/Mpc by 10%, without refitting physics. Report how much of the corrected-heavy vs slow/cold distance remains after bias and noise are profiled, not just which χ² is smallest. Check same-model, cross-model and numerical cancellation controls. Use a synthetic blind-to-data injected signal, identities at unit AP, angular quadrature convergence, mock-whitening and alternate residual minimization. No new nonlinear or tracer physics claimed; data do not supply a parent preparation. Do not promote the ranking if a control responds comparably to nuisance changes.
