# The retained field faces the BOSS low-redshift power-spectrum shape

**Result:** MTS remains competitive in this two-cap screen, but the heavy field is almost indistinguishable from the all-cold version of the same background at these wave numbers. The choice of tracer velocity physics matters more than that difference. We also derived a coherent velocity correction from the retained density and velocity spectra without fitting a new dispersion.

## What actually met the sky

The release is Beutler & McDonald's remeasurement of BOSS DR12 North/South at effective z=0.38, before reconstruction (https://fbeutler.github.io/hub/deconv_paper.html; https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.06324; wide-angle construction https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.05051). Catalogue and mock credit: Alam et al. 2015, Reid et al. 2016, Kitaura et al. 2016; original links on the release page. Eight source products are saved unchanged, with URLs and SHA256. This is the same low-redshift galaxy population contributing to earlier compressed growth tests, not new independent sky. We never add their χ² values.

For each cap we formed N-mode-weighted 0.01 h/Mpc bins. The theory used 400 fine k points per multipole spanning 0–0.4 h/Mpc, applied the published 2000×1200 wide-angle operator and 200×2000 window, then selected observed bands. Primary: ℓ=0 and 2, centres 0.025…0.095 h/Mpc, 16 numbers per cap. Model distances and expansion rates remap radial/transverse coordinates, using the catalogue header's Ωm=0.31, flat fiducial model. Fiducial coordinates have h scaled out: q_perp=h D_M/D_M(fid,H0=100), q_parallel=100 h E_fid/H. True k is h k_fid times the angular stretch, and true power in Mpc³ is multiplied by h³/(q_perp² q_parallel). The two MTS variants have effectively identical geometry at this z. Frozen bases predate scoring.

The leading prediction is (b D_cb + μ² U_cb)² times the primordial spectrum. Observed galaxies are assumed to follow the coherent cb velocity. This is a tracer prescription, not a derived stellar occupation; the predeclared total-density/CDM-velocity alternative is retained. Per branch we profiled the same two tracer parameters in each cap: constant bias and residual monopole noise. No parameters in the parent physics, background, normalization or matter preparation were fitted. We used each selected covariance submatrix before inversion, with its finite-mock precision normalization. Sums assume no North/South cross covariance, which is not in this product.

The leading MTS cap contributions are 19.39 North and 21.87 South. After fitting bias and noise, a synthetic primary MTS signal at b=1.83 is only 0.038 covariance units from the cold control in the North and 0.023 in the South (combined distance 0.044). These are expected shape separations, not significances of the real measurements. Thus a near-tie here is both interesting and *expected*, not evidence that BOSS requires a wave population. The old incorrect radiation shortcut would score 38.48 in the primary test. The better score is no reason to reinstate an error.

When the monopoles alone set bias and noise, MTS predicts the withheld-from-fit quadrupoles with a covariance-weighted quadratic of 5.65 for eight North bins and 19.42 for eight South bins. ΛCDM gives 8.28 and 21.81. This uses the measured mono/quad cross-covariance plus a linearized allowance for parameter estimation; it is neither an independent catalogue nor an exact p-value. The South mismatch deserves a tracer and survey-systematics check in all branches, not tuning MTS alone.

## Which motion the parent fields already predict

Starting from number conservation under s=x+n v_parallel/(aH), we evaluated the quadratic×quadratic and cubic×linear corrections to the redshift-space power of a biased, linear Gaussian density and velocity field. VELOCITY-MAP.md gives the kernels, units and assumptions. The coordinate transformation is standard; the use of the retained scale-dependent, corrected MTS fields and the numerical tests are this project's contribution. It includes neither nonlinear gravitational evolution nor random motion of galaxies within a halo. It was developed after the first scores and is not blind data.

The one-dimensional coherent velocity displacement inferred in MTS is 3.33 h⁻¹ Mpc (cold 3.39, ΛCDM 3.52). **It cannot be substituted for an independent Gaussian damping length.** At b=1.83, in the primary k band the negative one-point term alone reaches 11.3% of linear directional power. With the required pair term included the largest net correction is 1.56%. Long-wavelength motions translate pairs together. Eighteen direct soft-mode checks reproduce their leading cancellation to 1.33e-5 relative. The additional velocity treatment improves the MTS score by about two, but improves both controls as well. The illustrative fixed damping improves all three even more. It has not been measured or derived as a virial velocity by this experiment.

Using loop cutoffs 0.35, 1 and 5 Mpc⁻¹ gives MTS χ² 39.50, 39.22 and 39.21, respectively; the corresponding cold values are 39.46, 38.96 and 38.82. Thus numerical quadrature precision is not the same as physical closure. At the largest k needed by the window the mapping correction can reach 57% of directional power: do not trust the expansion there as a precision theory. Omitting the full new correction above 0.15 h/Mpc moves the *selected bands* by at most 0.0023 covariance-distance units in any tested branch or cap. This checks leakage for this term, not all possible nonlinear signals.

## Controls and next test

- Independent angular orders, no-AP analytic multipoles, window multiplication order, synthetic bias/noise recovery and independent numerical vs global polynomial profiling pass. Independent rebinning and covariance-eigenbasis scoring are checked in verify.py. Corrected and radiation-active frozen band predictions agree to within 3.6e-6 of peak in both branches and caps. The two cap scores and every predeclared sensitivity are retained.
- A separate finite periodic Gaussian box compares the Fourier transform of the exact pair-displacement characteristic function with the perturbative kernels. All 12 linear and order-power-squared coefficients agree. The first finite-amplitude halving was not asymptotic for every harmonic; at smaller amplitudes the maximum remainder ratio was 0.133, consistent with a cubic remainder in the variance amplitude. No survey tolerance was changed.
- The strongest next discriminator is the observed redshift-space velocity distribution of the selected tracer. Separate **pair-correlated coherent infall** from **residual small-scale dispersion**, using the same phase-space/halo selection for mock and survey, and apply it to both controls. A low-redshift mock with known positions and velocities can test the projection and missing terms without using the South residual to select a dispersion. An independent tracer or redshift bin can then test the frozen result. The empirical fit is not a determination of the parent heavy fraction or a galaxy state-preparation law.
