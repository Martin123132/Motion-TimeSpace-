# Spin energy backreacts: the averaged constraints and neutral curvature

**Domain and measurement.** Use the retained classical common Einstein metric, minimal Maxwell dipole, charged ordinary dust and neutral spherical scalar. Expand to second order in the amplitude of the *existing* counterrotation. Write s=a_spin², n=2/3 from the dipole angular integral, and δ=∂/∂s at zero. Hold fixed initial conserved baryons, charge/mass ratio, scalar h and its canonical momentum Π, radial canonical momentum p, and spin law l(N). This is not an assumption of equal total initial ADM. No independent first-order even mode or incoming gravitational wave. At each event the ruler measures R_{U i U j}; the instantaneous relative acceleration is minus this times the separation. U is instantaneously at rest normal to fixed-area spheres in polar slicing, with zero axial angular momentum. Quote the angular average of the radial section and the mean of the two tangential sections. A local clock held at that radius synchronizes repeated release experiments. These are not individual-direction full metric reconstructions or an integrated displacement along a single fall.

**Why the spherical projection works at this order.** At first order only the axial l=1 metric connection and Maxwell field are present. At second order keep their products before doing the spherical angular integral. Linear terms in second-order l=2 quantities have zero mean. Choose the monopole area gauge and horizontal metric −α²dt²+a²dr²; f=1/a²=1−2m/r, F=αa, B=Ff. The area element is unchanged by the axial shift; the trace of its tangential shift on a sphere is zero. The product of the null expansions entering the Hawking energy thus has the spherical value plus linear even multipoles, whose mean vanishes; m is the area-sphere Hawking mass through this order, NOT the whole spacetime's ADM and NOT an invariant local gravitational stress. The spherical mass definition/flux relation are recalled by [Hayward](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9408002). The exact Kerr–Newman checks below test this identification without assuming spherical symmetry of the control metric.

**Reduced matter and rotational stress.** Let N'=r²D, Q=ηN, I=∫ l dN, m_l=l−ηA, Y=QA−I. X=A_r, P=A_t/B are coefficients, not order-one radiation added to the background. The regular angular constraint fixes C=4F Y/r⁴ per first-order amplitude. At fixed electric flux and canonical angular momenta the exterior potential in the 2D action per n is A²/r²+2Y²/r⁴. The reduced angular-wave kinetic term is −(∇A)²/2. Use these terms inside the source as well, retaining the dust Routhian at fixed l. In 4π-rescaled units the axial corrections to the effective spherical sources (coefficients of s) are
    r² e_ax=n[f(P²+X²)/2+A²/r²+2Y²/r⁴],
    r² p_ax=n[f(P²+X²)/2−A²/r²−2Y²/r⁴],
    r² j_ax=−n f P X,    t_ax=n[A²/r⁴+4Y²/r⁶].
Here t denotes pressure in either transverse direction of the averaged action, not time. Its expression follows by varying the radius at fixed Q,I,A and horizontal metric. These sources are for varying the reduced action, not the physical four-dimensional electromagnetic stress alone.

The matter Hamiltonian has W²=1+fp²+n s m_l²/r² through first order in s. The dust contributes e=D√f W, p_rad=e fp²/W², j=D f p and t=n s D√f m_l²/(2W r²). The ordinary electric terms are e_E=−p_E=t_E=Q²/(2r⁴); the scalar has its canonical energy, pressure and flux. Terms linear in an induced radial matter or scalar response must not be dropped. Moving a baryon label at fixed exterior field varies electrostatic energy and the constrained rotation energy; their forces per label are +ηFQ/r² and −n s m_l C. Differentiating the local particle Hamiltonian at fixed l gives the centrifugal plus magnetic terms. In Eulerian variables,
    V=B√f p/W,  N_t=−V N',
    p_t=−V p_r−αW[(log α)_r−(√f p/W)²(log a)_r]+ηFQ/r²
          +n s [α(m_l²/r³+ηm_l X/r²)/W−m_l C].
The baseline scalar still obeys h_t=BΠ and Π_t=(r² B h_r)_r/r²−Fh; varying this equation evolves the induced scalar. Its angular derivative is zero, not its gravitational response.

**Constraints and source loss.** The reduced Einstein equations are m_r=r²e, (log F)_r=r(e+p_rad)/f, m_t=−r² B j. Central m=0, F(∞)=1 with the same clock convention as before. They are independent ways to read a finite sphere's changing mass. On the static-source electrovac exterior, F₀=1,
    δm=δADM−∫_r^∞ n[f(P²+X²)/2 + A²/r²+2Q²A²/r⁴]dr,
    δlogF=−∫_r^∞ n(P²+X²) dr/r,     δm_t=n f² P X.
In particular the outgoing flux *reduces* what is left inside. The initial cost δADM=n∫[D α l²/2 + 2F I²/r⁴]dr already accounts for the source; adding radiation to that unchanged mass would double count. In the dynamical exterior include the induced scalar stress and its flux, not just the photon. With no perturbation reaching the outer grid edge during the run and fixed total charge, the change from the initial mass also follows by integrating the changed energy density from the outside inward. At infinity through null slices this tends to the previous Bondi loss, with the finite-R induction term tested in NEAR-FAR-PREDICTION.md.

**A better density variable, not an adjusted tolerance.** The two large rest-mass terms in a tangent density update can spoil the tiny transient. Evolve z=δN/r³, so δD=3z+r z_r, r³ z_t=−δ(V N'), and outer δQ is identically zero. For varying the inner mass constraint at fixed r, δf=−2δm/r. Its integrating factor is F because 2∂_f(e_d+e_h)=(e_d+p_d+e_h+p_h)/f at fixed D,p,h,Π. Integrate the term αW δN' by parts. Using the zeroth-order Euler equation at fixed r, ηFQ/r²−(αW)_r=p_t, obtain
    F δm=αW δN+∫₀ʳ [p_t δN+V N' δp+F r² {f(ΠδΠ+h_r δh_r)+hδh}+F S_ax] dr,
    S_ax=n[D√f m_l²/(2W)+f(P²+X²)/2+A²/r²+2Y²/r⁴].
An interior surface term cannot be dropped before fixing the area sphere. This form never subtracts separated large changes in baryon rest mass to recover a much smaller electromagnetic number. It is exact in the tangent system. The code also retains the original density-based discretization and the error of integrating through the entire moving source.

**The physical 4D curvature.** The effective spherical base alone gives E_r^base=e−p_rad+2t−2m/r³ and E_ang^base=m/r³+p_rad at normal rest. It is not what the physical four-dimensional ruler sees. The horizontal radial sectional curvature has the additional −3 r²sin²θ(C/F)²s/4. The azimuthal section gains +r²sin²θ(C/F)²s/4 and the meridional section has no such term. An exact coordinate-Riemann calculation in a horizontal frame checks all three coefficients; angular averaging gives −3 n s r²(C/F)²/4 and +n s r²(C/F)²/8 for the radial and the mean tangential directions. This cancels the explicit Y² terms in those physical tidal sections; its contribution to m does not disappear. Outside ordinary matter the resulting expressions through order s are
    E_r=−2m/r³+2Q²/r⁴+f(Π²−h_r²)+4 n s A²/r⁴,
    E_ang=m/r³−Q²/(2r⁴)+[f(Π²+h_r²)−h²]/2
          +n s [f(P²+X²)/(2r²)−A²/r⁴].
Their sum E_r+2E_ang is the physical Ricci contraction, including 2(Uh)²−h² and electromagnetic energy; no independent rotational-energy density is added to that trace. In the ordinary nonradiating exterior these give the RN tides and, at leading weak field, the Newtonian −2m/r³ and +m/r³. At a spinning source's initial exterior event the added spin mass therefore gives −2δADM/r³ and +δADM/r³. Subtract these when asking for the *change during emission* at matched initial exterior mass; keep them for the identical-baryon comparison.

**Proper clock and signed scalar response.** For normal clocks dτ=αdt. On the background, δτ=∫δαdt and δY|τ=δY|t−(∂tY₀/α)δτ. Subtract the spin coefficient at the initial event before comparing the emitted transient. One especially strong check needs no local gravitational mass: ξ=δh|τ=δh−√f Π δτ, so δ[2(Uh)²−h²]|τ=4(Uh) dξ/dτ−2hξ. A derivative of a scalar history on the actual clock tests the result obtained by combining the two tidal curvatures and independently removing the photons. This distinction matters because the induced matter and the clock correction can each exceed their sum by more than twenty-fold.
