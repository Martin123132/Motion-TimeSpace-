# Magnetic calibration, scalar focusing, and the time the ruler changes sign

For the specified charged burst the scalar correction to a falling detector is **not a monotone offset**. Derived at zero amplitude, with the two early signed magnetic readings held fixed, it first contracts the pole-minus-equator log-volume relative to ordinary charged gravity, becomes positive between proper clocks 1.74 and 1.76, and becomes negative again between 4.24 and 4.26. These are sign brackets on the saved grid, not precision measurements of a zero. The final leading difference is −4.2071323×10⁻¹² A² per squared spin. The known full scalar run gives −4.2073009×10⁻¹², or −0.37218% of the ordinary signal. No late gravity reading was used to choose the two source currents.

**How it was derived.** Propagate the scalar wave at order A on the ordinary dynamical metric, and the sourced material, Maxwell, metric, field-energy and ruler equations together at order A². Vary the initial constraint, exterior boundary, and initial displacement of the matched detector as well. From the two ordinary magnetic basis fields solve for the current adjustment: (2.15891785, −2.20567668)×10⁻⁵ per A². The fixed-source final coefficient is −4.2545371×10⁻¹². The calibrated result is slightly smaller in magnitude, not erased. At clock 6.4 its integrated direct scalar focusing contribution is −4.3123382×10⁻¹², induced shear offsets +9.5474321×10⁻¹⁴, and the other terms sum to +9.7316205×10⁻¹⁵. At clocks 3 and 4 the direct scalar contribution and induced shear instead both have positive integrated signs. See EVOLVED-COEFFICIENT.md and focusing-contributions.csv.

**Where the leading prediction failed and what explains it.** On the full matched response the discrepancy is 4.01×10⁻⁵ of the peak scalar signal, outside the unchanged 2×10⁻⁵ target. The full fixed-source leading prediction passes. Polarization of independent fixed-current solutions splits the full matched discrepancy exactly into four finite contributions (nonlinear-remainder.csv). At the final clock the measured excess beyond leading is −1.68651×10⁻¹⁶. The largest term is −1.77835×10⁻¹⁶: adjusting the current to keep the early field fixed **also changes the response to the scalar**. The small fixed-source nonlinearity offsets some of it. The independent decomposition closes across the history to 1.69×10⁻⁶ on the *excess* scale, not just on the much larger total ruler scale.

We then calculated the mixed part of the A⁴ coefficient using only the zero-amplitude equations for the two individual currents and the opposing mixture, including the derivative of the early magnetic map. No finite-amplitude gravity discrepancy sets this coefficient. Its final value is −1.76520×10⁻¹⁶. Applied without retuning, whole-history error on the fine direct matched run drops from 4.01×10⁻⁵ to **3.85×10⁻⁶** at A=1, from 1.00×10⁻⁵ to **9.60×10⁻⁷** at A=1/2, and from 6.53×10⁻⁷ to **4.73×10⁻⁸** at A=1/8. All five full-arm reporting clocks individually pass, worst 1.60×10⁻⁵. This is a derived mixed coefficient, **not** a calculation of every intrinsic fourth-order term. The previously known full-arm miss motivated this diagnostic; this is not a blind dataset. Details: MIXED-COUPLING.md and mixed-coefficient-tests.csv.

**Subtraction and numerical resolution.** The main fixed/matched tangent histories were frozen before new finite runs (forecast SHA-256 e14819e289d0829ce1b586f55c1a97d53fde9ca231c554bab4d48d69de0db58d). On the matched susceptibility scale the two radial grids differ by 5.73×10⁻⁹, the fine direct determinant by 4.90×10⁻⁸, and independent Simpson convolution of the focusing terms agrees to 5.22×10⁻⁸. At A=1/160 the independently evolved, simply subtracted fine ray is within 5.46×10⁻⁶ of leading, but its raw determinant differs from that ray by **2.29%**: do not use this subtraction to resolve the next coefficient. Exact finite divided evolution on the original coarse grid avoids the near cancellation: matched ray within 1.57×10⁻⁹ of its tangent, determinant agrees to 3.66×10⁻⁷. The first short complex-log determinant subtraction did not work; its packet and the repaired comparison both remain visible. Across the original leading full-history tests 12/14 pass (both full matched grids are the misses); the separate 37 numerical controls and 16 algebra checks pass. Individual clock results remain in per-clock-tests.csv.

**What precision would a test need?** In this two-current family the measured field vector d fixes v=D⁻¹d; L=dᵀD⁻ᵀKD⁻¹d. Assign the two early absolute field errors magnitudes ≤ ε times the corresponding unit-current row norms of the ordinary D, not percentages of a nearly cancelled reading. For each model let E=D⁻¹ diag(scales), then its ruler error is bounded by 2ε‖EᵀKv‖₁+ε²∑|EᵀKE|. The sum of the ordinary and scalar bounds being smaller than their gap suffices for nonoverlap, even allowing each source to move independently within the measurement box. At 6.4, **ε ≤ 0.0395%** (strict cutoff about 0.0395964%; see calibration-precision.csv) suffices before ruler noise or source-model uncertainty: the two absolute field half-widths at the cutoff are 1.79×10⁻¹² and 1.95×10⁻¹⁰ in solver units. In contrast, merely preserving the *sign of the difference* under a common shift of the same two supplied fields is much easier; it is not a nonoverlap or detectability test. Independent ruler half-widths must also be charged against the gap. This is a conditional measurement specification, not a real instrument performance claim.

**Next physics test.** The remaining practical ambiguity is how many source shapes the early fields constrain. Extend the known third balanced current to the same charge-.8 preparation; freeze a third signed magnetic clock independently of the late gravitational signal. Check rank and noise amplification on both grids, then test a mixture not used to construct the three-current response. This asks whether the scalar difference remains distinguishable once an extra physically allowed source can imitate it, rather than tightening already adequate numerical precision. Keep energy or formation assumptions explicit for currents outside that subspace. No observational fits, galaxy-repository changes, or publication here.
