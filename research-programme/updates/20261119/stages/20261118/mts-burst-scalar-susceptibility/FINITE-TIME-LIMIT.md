# Fixed magnetic readings do not make the scalar invisible

Let z=A² and v denote the two balanced current weights at fixed baryon preparation. Each row of D(z) is a signed early polar-field reading from a unit current. Spherical geometry is independent of v at the retained spin order: the odd Maxwell/current equation is linear in v, the spin-squared ruler is quadratic in v, and changing relative scalar amplitude is a different operation from changing spin. In particular the two early readings d give an actual implicit family v(z)=D(z)^{-1}d, not permission to choose a late ruler value. It exists near zero whenever the early matrix is nonsingular. A third unrestricted current would invalidate this identification.

**Finite-time approach to the ordinary charged solution.** Consider either saved spatial grid over its finite number of time steps, with positive metric denominators, the regular parity cap and nonsingular matching BVP. At A=0 the ordinary material fields and both readouts are even in A. The ordered scalar pair is odd; write it as A times its transported wave. After factoring A out, scalar stress is z times a smooth quadratic form of the transported wave, with coefficients depending on the evolving even fields. The resulting step map in the even variables and transported wave is differentiable in z at zero. The apparent ends of the clipped number law pose no first-derivative jump: J vanishes cubically and its slope quadratically at either endpoint. The finite cap and radial differentiation act linearly. Finite composition, then inversion of the nonsingular early matrix, preserve differentiability. Thus on these finite sets of clocks

This is a limit of the actual retained step map for these regular data, not a continuum stability estimate or a vacuum statement. The computed S is obtained by solving the variational equations, not by asserting these constants are small.

**Direct finite check without throwing away significant digits.** At finite z use a formal coordinate a with a³=z a. The three coefficients store the value at a=0, the centered odd quotient at ±A and the even divided difference relative to zero. This is evaluation at three distinct nodes expressed in a basis that stays useful as they coalesce. Operations exactly recompose to the three ordinary operations in exact arithmetic, provided the same regular branches of the square root and sign are followed. For even denominators and roots, use the rationalized differences. For the matched case solve D(z) [(v(z)−v(0))/z]=−[(D(z)−D(0))/z]v(0).
