# Retained metric → two-species or three-species triangle

N=ln a, c=1, k in Mpc⁻¹, H in Mpc⁻¹, ℋ=aH. The cold controls contain distinct pressureless CDM and baryon velocities. The retained example also contains a coherent positive quadratic heavy scalar of mass 149896.229/Mpc. The light sector, neutrinos and radiation are in the parent background and native linear metric; no independent second-order stress for them was assumed. The second-order Poisson response here comes only from the explicitly evolved clustering species. This is a subhorizon, weak-field, single-stream calculation, not a relativistic second-order Boltzmann solver.

**Parent-to-Newtonian map.** Use the gauge conventions of the retained CAMB symbolic.py and equations.f90 (cf. [Ma & Bertschinger](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/9506072)). U=−div v/ℋ. For the CDM-frame shear σ=−ℋ U_c/k, D_N=D_CDMframe−3ℋσ/k for vanishing background pressure. Φ=etak/k−ℋσ/k and Ψ=2Weyl/k²−Φ: no equal-potential assumption. For the matched heavy mode, the native density and momentum outputs are (matching_density/a) times its CDM-frame density contrast and velocity. Divide first, shift frames second. Massive neutrinos are not treated as a pressureless second-order species. The negligible background baryon-pressure correction to the gauge shift and neglected baryon/radiation forces belong to the approximation, not the numerical tolerance. All first-order modes were replayed at existing sourced k knots with late radiation truncation off, matched to retained Weyl anchors. Close-spaced differences of native interpolants can magnify rounding; integrated continuity and Euler tests are also saved.

**Convention for amplitudes.** Start with two distinct real plane cosines at p,q. The target is the cosine at K=p+q, not a single ordered Fourier coefficient. Each external mode is divided by its all-cold total matter transfer today, identically for every model. Reinsert the normalization when forming the dimensional power. Species weights f_s are their fractions of the clustered background mass. Let g_s=4πGρ_s/H², W_s=1/(4 m_h² a⁴H²) for the heavy mode, zero for dust; F=2+d ln H/dN. Use α_p=K·p/p², α_q=K·q/q², β=K² p·q/(2p²q²). With D,U denoting sum-mode density and bulk-velocity divergence amplitudes, primes d/dN, each species obeys

    D' = U + A,   A=(α_p U_p D_q + α_q U_q D_p)/2
    U' = −F U + Σ g_t D_t − W_s K⁴D
         + β U_p U_q + (W_s/2)K²(p²+q²+p·q)D_pD_q .

Equivalently evolve V=D' (a mass-current variable) with the same linear gravity and pressure and the following three quadratic sources:

    S_gravity=(α_p G_p D_q + α_q G_q D_p)/2,
    S_flux=α_p α_q U_p U_q,
    S_wave=W_s(K·p)(K·q)D_pD_q.

G_p=−p²Ψ_p/ℋ² when using the native first-order acceleration. For autonomous evolution use the first-order common Poisson sum instead. If U initially vanishes at second order, V initially equals A, **not zero**. For general supplied histories define e_D=D'−U and e_U=U'+F U−G+W_s k⁴D at first order. Subtracting the two second-order formulations leaves a forced solution with source −[α_p(e_Up D_q+U_p e_Dq)+α_q(e_Uq D_p+U_q e_Dp)]/2. Solving this residual independently tests the projected equations rather than requiring the native relativistic inputs to be exactly pressureless.

**Initial data and bispectrum.** For a linear forced system X'=M X+S, the adjoint row with final condition (f_s,0) gives the exact sum-mode response to specified initial density and velocity plus the retarded source integral. The optional Einstein–de Sitter particular initialization uses F₂=(5α_sym+2β)/7, G₂=(3α_sym+4β)/7 with α_sym=(α_p+α_q)/2; apply F₂ D_pD_q and G₂(U_pD_q+U_qD_p)/2 species by species. That is exact in the matched growing ideal dust fixture, a comparison assumption elsewhere. It is not obtained from the native linear initial-condition table. For Gaussian adiabatic first-order modes, the conditional tree B is the sum of 2F₂ P_pP_q over all three cyclic legs. Q=B/(P₁P₂+P₂P₃+P₃P₁), using each model's own powers. The full growth solutions, not an EdS kernel inserted at the measurement time, enter these expressions.

**Independent evolution.** field_replay.py evolves collisionless single-stream dust via continuity and its Hamilton–Jacobi velocity potential and the fast mode via a complex Schrödinger equation with the same evolving Poisson source. The two periodic coordinates are p·x and q·x: the reciprocal metric retains the actual oblique dot product. There is no need to interpolate a new phase-table entry for the generated mode. The solver extracts its cosine from the full evolved fields and checks the absent sine. The comoving phase is m a²H times the dimensionful e-fold velocity potential. It is never replaced by the measured density. The coherent wave limit is inherited, not an identification of a mixed galactic population.
