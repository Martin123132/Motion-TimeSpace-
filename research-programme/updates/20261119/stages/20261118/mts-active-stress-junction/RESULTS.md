**The relativity result.** At z=3999 the heavy-minus-CDM density coefficient is −954.74 on CDM-comoving synchronous slices but **−341.80 in Newtonian coordinates as measured by their normal observers**. The spatial-coordinate contribution alone is +612.98. The same Newtonian observer measures a relative normalized energy current of +1.32839 but a relative energy-frame velocity of only +0.038785 (scaled −kΔv/H=−6.48662). Transporting the actual mode fields and Lorentz-boosting their stress tensors reproduces the result; adding an arbitrary tested second-order shift leaves the relative answer unchanged to extraction accuracy. The analytic cancellation uses equal dust-like homogeneous dilution of the two components on this branch; it does not determine the common second-order slicing needed for their individual densities. The small relative velocity is a cancellation: the 0.000516 forward momentum discrepancy alone corresponds to about 1.3% of that final relative velocity at fixed linear map. Do not interpret the 0.0435% momentum check as velocity accuracy.
