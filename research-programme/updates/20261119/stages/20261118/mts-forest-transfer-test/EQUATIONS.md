# Definitions and reproducibility

k is comoving Mpc⁻¹; k_h=k/h is in h Mpc⁻¹. A radial mode has k_v=k(1+z)/H(z), in s/km. This conversion by itself does not project density into a line-of-sight absorption observable.

**Matter.** In native synchronous variables, D_m=(Σ_i ρ_i D_i)/Σ_i ρ_i, including CDM, baryons, massive ν and the heavy oscillating field; the slow dark-energy component is not counted as matter. Native background densities are 8πG a⁴ρ_i and the extra heavy numerator and denominator in that convention are a² δρ_h(native) and a ρ_match. The dimensionful linear power is 2π² A_s (k/0.05)^(n_s−1) D_m²/k³. Separately compare the Newtonian-gauge clustered (no ν) power to the previous release; do not relabel it as the total matter used for the slope test.

For C_i=ρ_iD_i/Σρ, n_eff=n_s−4+2 Σ_i ∂C_i/∂ln k /ΣC_i. Differentiate signed contributions, not logarithms of a possibly vanishing species. Direct log-power and the independently differentiated species sum are stored separately. The difference of slopes is the log-derivative of a **power ratio**, not of a transfer amplitude. In the heavy rest-pressure branch c_s²=[u/(sqrt(1+u²)+1)]², u=k/(am). A subhorizon coefficient diagnostic, not a full relativistic stability test, is c_s²k²/(4πGa²ρ_m)≈(k/k_J)⁴, k_J⁴=16πGa⁴ρ_m m². No sound speed is assigned to the comparison CDM, and the matter-only denominator does not include radiation gravity at early times. The ratio includes background matter ν in the diagnostic, not a claim that ν clusters as CDM.

**Coverage proof.** R=P_model/P_control, T_i are released amplitude templates, w_i≥0 and Σw=1. For a proposed maximum squared-transfer error e, set l=sqrt(max(0,R−e)), u=sqrt(R+e). The inequalities l≤T w≤u are linear. If A=(T;−T), b=(u;−l), any y≥0 with min_j (yᵀA)_j−yᵀb>0 certifies infeasibility. Upper bounds are independently evaluated using actual convex weights; coefficients in mixtures.json refer to zero-based released block order, witness rows to the corresponding subset of hull.csv. The two-knot witness is available without re-running an optimizer. We test a common weight vector across k, as in the release; this does not exclude a new hydrodynamic simulation, unconstrained signed extrapolation is not validation, and overlap-only upper bounds do not imply coverage up to k_h=200. The 0.10 comparison budget was frozen locally; the release separately checks changes on smoothing, and its cosmology gate on n_eff is hard.

**Flux.** Template power correction is a ratio of simulated **flux** powers; at the cold identity, add the astrophysical ratio minus one, clip at zero as in the release and multiply by its reference flux bandpowers. Use the seven-dimensional inverse-distance weights (distance+1e−8)^−6 independently at each measured redshift on the released 1513 rows. These gas nuisance defaults are not MTS-derived. Treat HIRES and MIKE with their released inverse covariances, no imputed cross-instrument covariance. Ordering is four HIRES redshifts then first three MIKE redshifts, seven k_v bins each; both instruments observe the same model but not the same measured vector. Compute rᵀC⁻¹r, not a pointwise error-bar sum. Neither these controls nor transfer-grid representability constitutes a marginalized ΛCDM/retained likelihood ratio.
