The retained mixed heavy branch, matched all-cold slow-background control, and flat ΛCDM comparison keep their sealed parameters. No tuning to forest data. Use the Hooper et al. MIKE/HIRES Lyman-ABGD release (arXiv:2206.08188, MontePython 5b365b093982099b926a0c0c169697c9dc6cbbc4). Do not interpret a failure of the emulator as rejection of the theory by observations.

1. Recover radiation-active total matter, including massive neutrinos, on saved projected modes. Independently check today's total matter against prior radiation-active or conventional-control output. Compare clustered-field power at shared forest epochs to the preceding prediction. Calculate the actual z=3 log power slope at 0.009 s/km for each branch, using that branch's expansion, without extending any table. Compare local log-polynomial, finer derivative around the pivot if necessary, and spline. Compare against the published [-2.6,-2.0] slope and [0.5,1.5] sigma8 domains. No clipping or setting a branch's slope to its control's.
2. Independently bound representability of z=0 power using the pointwise envelope of all 201 released hydro templates, separately relative to the matched cold and conventional reference. On positive normalized mixtures the squared minimum template is a rigorous lower bound; one impossible knot suffices regardless of missing ultraviolet coverage. On trusted overlap report maximum absolute squared-transfer mismatch lower bound; flag >0.10 (our conservative diagnostic, not a likelihood cut). Exact cold template is a positive control. No extrapolation from k≤5/Mpc to 200 h/Mpc, no signed/hybrid negative weights. Admit no heavy flux χ² if there is a domain failure.
3. Exercise the actual 28 HIRES and 21 MIKE flux and precision entries and hydro template response, with the release's redshift/bin ordering, jointly and individually. Use the release's fiducial gas history for a fixed reference pipeline check; also use released nuisance defaults for fixed conventional and (only conditionally) same-cold-transfer control. No profiling or cosmic-background likelihood; these scores are controls, not a model ranking. Verify whitening, units, positive covariance and identity-template recovery; keep measured and predicted flux separate from matter power. If the equivalent LCDM mapping is not defined, label that branch's control an approximation, not a calibrated likelihood.
4. From the gate(s) derive a quantitative necessary change of transfer, slope or growth for future physical branches or hydro simulations. Preserve the prior spectrum and thresholds. Do not rescale the field/fraction to hit the forest. No new simulation, external project edit or publication.
