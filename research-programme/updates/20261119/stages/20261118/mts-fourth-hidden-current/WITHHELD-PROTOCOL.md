# Do not fit away a discrepancy with a new clock

Keep the actual calibration at three magnetic readings. In addition to the original three withheld fields, test whether a pre-specified late ruler record can contain a source-independent discriminator at the nominal measured early fields. Take the fourteen proper clocks 5.1, 5.2, …, 6.4; these follow the calibration clocks and are chosen before constructing either hidden ruler line. At fixed early fields each arm's allowed ruler is L₀ + a L₁ + a² L₂. A weighted sum orthogonal to both arms' linear and quadratic hidden-source changes annihilates even arbitrary independent hidden amplitudes in the exact retained four-profile model. Determine weights by projecting the nominal difference onto the complement of those four directions, not by fitting detector data. Normalize weights to unit sum of absolute values. Do not interpret the fourteen measurements as independent noise samples. For the three withheld magnetic fields at 5.5, 6, 6.4 the corresponding nuisance space has two columns because the field is linear. Scale each field by the ordinary three-unit channel norm at its own clock, using the same scales on both grids.

First freeze both sets of weights on the coarse equations alone, then test their leakage and separation on the fine grid. Also construct a conservative joint-grid ruler statistic nulling the union of the coarse and fine source directions (at most eight). Check the nominal gap and independent direct trajectories; keep noise, rank and grid budgets explicit. The observables are a *detector design* using synthetic models. Even a perfect algebraic cancellation cannot guarantee a uniform laboratory error for unbounded amplitudes with uncertain profiles, independent of resolution. For each statistic calculate an explicit leakage bound as a function of a possible independently supplied energy cap (illustrations at the nominal cost and twice that cost); never assert that the cap is supplied by the theory.

As a separate degeneracy audit, solve the two-arm quadratic compatibility equations for both withheld ruler clocks 6 and 6.4 as well as for common launch cost and the final ruler. Report all real intersections, all costs and non-fitted readouts. If there is no directly tested common-cost candidate within the predeclared computational budget, one affordable two-ruler intersection may be directly tested in its place (least larger-of-two costs). Otherwise do not launch an extra pair merely to improve the count.
