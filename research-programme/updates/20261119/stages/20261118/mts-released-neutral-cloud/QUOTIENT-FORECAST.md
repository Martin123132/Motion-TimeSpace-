# Killing-field simplification and a curvature-free check
For the unspun spherical fall from rest, J_t=r/r0 exactly: an orbit-rotation Killing field furnishes the angular Jacobi field with the right initial conditions. Therefore the radial–azimuthal shear coefficient divided by sinθ is s=o−o(0)/(J_r J_t), where o=2(QA−I)/r³. This follows from the covariant deformation equation; it is not a fitted relaxation law. On our source exterior o(0)=0, so s=o. In particular J_{rφ}^{(1)}/sinθ=J_t∫o dτ in the parallel frame. In Kerr with o(0) nonzero the initial offset must be retained.

For a zero-angular-momentum *normal congruence* on the initially untwisted, time-symmetric emitting exterior, the azimuthal shifts of the test bodies do not change the Jacobian determinant. Equivalently the axial shear squares in the physical optical equations exactly restore the curvature of the horizontal metric. Predict the fractional volume directly from neighbouring radii: V/V_i=(r/r0)² [a/(a_initial W)] (∂r/∂r0)_τ. Use the variation of geodesic flow in initial radius and source spin to test this without importing either tidal readout into that independent calculation. Avoid promoting volume differences below the accuracy of differentiated metric data. This determinant prescription does not set the physical shear to zero or apply to the initially twisted exact Kerr test without its distinct initial-velocity term.
