# Predeclare an independent, genuinely four-dimensional cloud experiment
Use exact Kerr (vacuum) and Kerr–Newman (Q/M=0.6), M=1 and fixed initial area radius 4.5, released initially normal to the stationary slices. Fix the *area*, not the radial coordinate, for every spin. The proper-time horizon is 2.4, initial separation matrix identity and zero covariant derivative. Predict the spin-squared sky-averaged log-volume from the monopole metric already tested, plus the off-diagonal first-order axial coupling from the Killing vector. No independent even quadrupole enters the mean at this order. Separately compute the actual volume in full Boyer–Lindquist spacetime via neighbouring geodesics (Hamiltonian variational flow); no curvature, shear scalar, prior cloud data or fitted transfer coefficient may enter that independent calculation. Compare the angularly weighted results at proper times 0.6,1.2,1.8,2.4, turn the spin down to isolate the quadratic coefficient and retain failures. In exact vacuum the instantaneous Ricci term vanishes: any volume response must be due to tidal deformation. Do not treat the charged material experiment as a Kerr source.
