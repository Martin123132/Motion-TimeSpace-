# A magnetic readout of local neutral shear
The freely falling, vorticity-free cloud on the initially untwisted exterior has a particularly simple observable. For the zero-axial-momentum centre let S be its outward radial unit vector and eφ the unit azimuthal Killing direction. The local, signed off-diagonal deformation rate is σ_{Sφ}=sinθ·2QA/r³ at first order in spin; the azimuthally diagonal sphere expansion and radial drift are not part of this cross component. This is physical shear, not just rotation of a chosen coordinate frame. Write B_pole=2A/r² for the signed radial magnetic dipole harmonic amplitude in the same radial rest frame (a radial boost preserves B_radial). Then
    σ_{Sφ} = (Q/r) B_pole sinθ      [geometrical units],
    σ_{Sφ} = G Q_SI B_pole,SI sinθ /(c² R_SI)     [per second].
Orientations are those of Aφ=A sin²θ and positive azimuth; the actual B and shear both contain the source spin amplitude. No unknown spin amplitude is needed once the charge and magnetic field are measured. This is the canonical common-metric Einstein–Maxwell prediction under the stated release and no-exterior-angular-momentum assumptions, not an extra MTS-only coupling or an observation.

In the radial frame the azimuthal Killing column stays tangential. The first-order components per sinθ satisfy D_Rφ=0, D_φR=2 J_T ∫o J_R/J_T dτ, o=2QA/r³, J_T=r/r0. Test both entries against the separate parallel-frame matrix after rotating it back. The trace of this deformation vanishes to first order; the volume effect enters at second order. Reversing source rotation reverses shear but leaves the volume change even. Reversing the *source charge* at fixed rotation flips both Q and its generated dipole, so does not reverse this shear. If there is a nonzero initially enclosed external spin the rate instead contains the angular constraint offset and a launch-dependent homogeneous term. The pure charged formula does not apply in that case.
