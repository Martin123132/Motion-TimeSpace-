# Work and a sufficient stress-error criterion on the solved metric

For the actively evolved slow perturbation minus the passive archive-velocity perturbation, the linear force is F=(φ′_phys−φ′_archive) C,x/5. The two histories start equal. Their difference obeys u′=v, v′=−2hv−ω²u+F on exactly the same switched metric. Define Q=(v²+ω²u²)/2. Then Q′=vF−2hv²+h(am/5)²u². Test supplied scalar-mode work, expansion damping, and changing restoring energy separately against the **independently saved states**. An exactly unforced identical pair would keep Q identically zero. This is energy accounting for the *difference mode*; Q is not the difference of two stress energies and not the energy of the entire universe.

Record how conservative it is on the saved cases. The direct change of stress-readout weights when changing physical background is separate; this criterion does not bound that term or a general nonlinear/local-GR limit.
