# Freeze before acquiring scalar/metric trace readouts

Use the same canonically normalized Maxwell and scalar stress. On a charge-source-free sphere a neutral radially falling observer reads a mass-independent combination of radial and angular geodesic deviation and the electric field:
    I = E_radial + 2 E_angular − Q²/R⁴ = 2(dh/dτ)²−h² .
Here E is the curvature sign in J''=−EJ and h is the retained sqrt(4πG)-scaled real field (pole mass one). The Maxwell radial boost leaves its energy unchanged, and trace reversal gives its normal-normal Ricci equal to Q²/R⁴. This is a pointwise contraction of the Einstein equation, not an inferred halo profile. It cancels any same-event Coulomb-background mass ambiguity. Do not subtract electric curvature at a different areal radius. Inside ordinary matter add ρ[2(−u_d·U)²−1] before comparing; for the charge-free gravitational field with no scalar the exterior combination is zero. No statement about transverse radiation follows from the radial electric configuration.

Without evolving the scalar, compute h₂=DⁱDᵢh₀−h₀ using the initial three-metric; geodesic proper-time I''(0)=4h₂²−2h₀h₂. A spatially constant flat free carrier additionally predicts I/h₀²=1/2−(3/2)cos(2τ), first zero arctan(1/√2). Treat that waveform and zero as an approximation to test, NOT a forced cutoff, universal frequency shift or an exact phase prediction for an inhomogeneous packet. Do not tune the template to different radii. Full and 1/160 states, dust-only with and without charge, radii 6,14,18,36; keep the zero at 36 an absolute null, not a relative pass. Read the trajectory's h and its proper-time derivative independently of the two rulers.
