# Initial-data estimate outside the charged core

This is a finite-time estimate for the specified classical spherical data on the domain r≥s(t)=6+t, 0≤t≤6.5. It does not establish charged-dust regularity inside the cut. The ordinary matter starts at r≤5 and is timelike. In an untrapped, asymptotically flat exterior F=αa≤1 and B=α/a≤1, so neither ordinary matter nor an interior signal enters across this moving boundary. Q is constant there. Use continuity from the regular initial data to improve the metric margins below, not a measured maximum from an evolved pulse.

**Exterior constraint.** Let E_out=ADM−μ(s(t))=∫ₛ^∞ r²e_h dr. Then dE_out/dt=s²(Bj_h−e_h)≤0. Its initial value is at most E₀=λ²∫[(rh₁)'²+(rh₁)²]dr/2 over the whole initial slice: integrate by parts at the regular origin and outside the compact packet, and use a≥1. The initial ADM is at most M_b+E₀+|ηQ|25 D_max/14, including electric energy outside the source. The initial enclosed ordinary mass bounds ADM below by M_b sqrt(1−c₀), where c₀=2(ADM_upper² e_max/3)^(1/3) from M≤min(e_max r³/3,ADM_upper). Here e_max includes both the prepared scalar bound and the electric-field maximum from |Q(r)|≤|η|min(r³D_max/3,M_b). These bounds give μ−Q²/(2r)>0 even after subtracting E₀ throughout the cut. Thus 1≤a≤a_*=(1−2ADM_upper/6)^(-1/2) and F≥exp(−2a_*²E₀/6). Cauchy–Schwarz on h=−∫ᵣ^∞ h_s ds gives h²≤2a_*²E₀/r.

**Positive energies on the moving half-line.** Set u=rh, p=rΠ, K=2μ/r³−2Q²/r⁴−h², V=F(1+K). Then B_r=FrK, (log F)_r=r a²(e_h+s_h), and rK_r=2e_h−6μ/r³+8Q²/r⁴−2rhh_r. In particular, a radial electric field cancels from e+s. Start with the trial e_h≤0.00012 λ². The exact scalar support lies below 26+t<33; use this only on terms containing the scalar. exterior_bound.py evaluates explicit upper bounds b,b₂,d,g on |B_r|,|B_rr|,|B−V|,|V_r| with these inequalities. In bounding the term −2rhh_r use |hh_r|≤a e_h, not an assumed wavelength.

Define Y₀²=∫ₛ^∞(p²+u_r²+u²)dr and Y₁²=∫ₛ^∞(p_r²+u_rr²+u_r²)dr. Integrating the parent massive wave identities by parts now leaves lower-limit terms −B p u_r−(p²+u_r²+u²)/2 and −B p_r u_rr−(p_r²+u_rr²+u_r²)/2. Both are nonpositive. Hence Y₀'≤(b+d)Y₀/2, Y₁'≤(3b+d+b₂)Y₁/2+(b+b₂+g)Y₀. Use the parent's exact initial polynomial integrals over the whole half-line as upper bounds. Denote their integrated norm ceilings by X₀,X₁. Integrating from infinity gives |u|≤X₀, |u_r|≤X₁, |p|²≤2X₀X₁. At r≥R the resulting scalar energy ceiling is
    e_h ≤ [2X₀X₁+2X₁²+(1+2/R²)X₀²]/(2R²).
At R=6 this improves the trial for both preparations. No measured stress or central dust slope is an input.

**Neutral rulers, same ADM and Q.** Bound |δμ|≤E₀ and use the above local stress in the two comparison equations in EQUATIONS.md.
