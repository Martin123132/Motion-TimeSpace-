    R''=−μ/R²+Q²/R³−R s'_h;
    J''=(2μ/R³−3Q²/R⁴−K_h)J,     J_angular=R/R₀.
Use M_r=r²e and M_t=−r²Bj: along the neutral probe μ'=R²(W/a)(v e_h−j_h). With zero scalar these give neutral geodesic tides E_r=−2M∞/R³+3Q²/R⁴ and E_t=M∞/R³−Q²/R⁴, equivalently f''/2 and f'/(2R) for f=1−2M∞/R+Q²/R². These are standard GR predictions [Crispino et al., 2016](https://doi.org/10.1140/epjc/s10052-016-3972-5). The inverse-square Newton force is the leading term for neutral probes; Q²/R³ is gravity of Maxwell stress, not a direct Lorentz force on a neutral body. For nonzero probe charge even the weak-field leading force changes and this experiment is not its trajectory.
