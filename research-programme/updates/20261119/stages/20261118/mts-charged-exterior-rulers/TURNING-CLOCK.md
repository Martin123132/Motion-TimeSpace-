# Why the clock belongs in the prediction

The field is initially at a turning point and the neutral observer starts at rest. Set f=1/a², α=F sqrt(f), B=Ff. The scalar equation gives h_tt=(B/r²)∂r(r² B h_r)−F² f h at this instant. The observer has t'=1/α and R''=−f(ln α)_r. Combining these gives the local proper-time acceleration
    A = d²h/dτ² = f h_rr + (2f/r + f_r/2) h_r − h.
All lapse dependence cancels. Thus the local acceleration of a uniform, initially stationary profile is −h, irrespective of an exterior clock redshift. This does not make a spatially structured waveform a uniform oscillator. A scalar wave evolved in coordinate time without also evolving the observer's clock does not obey this identity.

Let h=λh₁ initially and f=f_b+λ²d+…, d=−2μ₂/r, d'=−2r e₁+2μ₂/r² in the exterior. The coefficient of λ³ in the initial acceleration is C=d h₁,rr+(2d/r+d'/2)h₁,r. Since I=2(dh/dτ)²−h² and dh/dτ=0 initially, I₄''(0)=(8A₁−2h₁)C. This uses only the specified initial profile and weighted mass constraint. No waveform was used to set the amplitude, initial derivative or lapse.

Three numerical readings are deliberately kept separate. (1) Differentiating the sampled fourth-order waveform twice at its endpoint only passes its own-scale gate at 19.25 and 20. (2) Extrapolating the three independent nonlinear runs' initial Taylor coefficients passes at 18, 19.25 and 20 on the 1920 grid, but subtraction loses accuracy for the two smallest coefficients. (3) Varying the actual discretized mass constraint before subtracting nearly equal large curvatures gives a stable check at all five radii. At 3840 cells its worst discrepancy against the continuum integral is 1.20e−8 of the coefficient. This is an independent calculation of the initial derivative, not a repair to or a rerun of the frozen time series.

In the time-dependent test, at R₀=19.25 the individual fourth-order terms peak at 3.10e−10 (field), 1.02e−11 (observer position) and 2.98e−10 (clock); the actual sum peaks at 3.48e−11. The components add point by point, not by adding peak magnitudes. The measured nonlinear coefficient was extracted for scoring only by taking secants of I(λ)/λ² at λ=1/2,3/4,1 then extrapolating to cancel the λ⁶ contribution. This comparison is not a fit used by the predictor and does not erase the individual full-strength absolute waveform tests.
