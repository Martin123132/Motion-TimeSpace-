This is a private continuation of the retained two-field example (F=0.5), with the same gravitational coupling and same slow-field/all-cold control. No parameters were fitted. This is a numerical comparison to prior simulations, not a new sky measurement.

**Why the negative region ends.** In x=kτ write h=ℋ/k, S=δ_h+3h u_h and E=8πGa²(ρ+p)/k². The conservation equations give exactly S_x=C_x−(1+3E/2)u_h, where C is the synchronous CDM perturbation. The pressure cancels from this equation but drives u_h. Integrating the two velocity contributions recovers the full change in S−C, within 5.45e−14 of the trajectory's peak S. In a separate linear split, at k=1 the environment present at the handoff generates 112.96% of the final matter amplitude; initial heavy density supplies +0.63% and initial heavy momentum −13.59%. This signed split includes subsequent metric feedback, and is not a decomposition into probabilities.
