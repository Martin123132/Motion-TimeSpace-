The common Einstein action and its coupling are inherited; the calculation concerns decoupling on that branch.

**What makes the comparison work.** Match initial baryon rest mass shell by shell, not the gravitating mass after adding the field. The scalar flux and pressure then control its acceleration. Dust number conservation similarly eliminates the unknown later dust density from radial geodesic deviation.
