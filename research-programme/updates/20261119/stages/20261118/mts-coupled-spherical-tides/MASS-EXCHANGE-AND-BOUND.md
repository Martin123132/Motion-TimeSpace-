# A sector-resolved mass law and conditional GR error bound

Use 4πG-rescaled Eulerian energy e, radial and transverse pressures s,t and outward energy flux j. The Misner–Sharp mass splits on each polar-areal slice as M=M_d+M_h, M_i(r)=∫₀ʳ R²e_i dR. This is **not** the split into two independently conserved rest masses. Dust conserves baryon number. Each retained sector is covariantly conserved on shell, without a new direct coupling.

The time component of ∇·T_i=0 gives
    ∂t e_i=−(α a r²)⁻¹ ∂r(α²r²j_i) − (∂t a/a)(e_i+s_i).
Integrate over r²dr; apply (lnα+ln a)'=r a²(e+s) and a_t/a=−r α a j from Einstein's equations. The actual sector balance is
    ∂t M_i + r²(α/a) j_i = ∫₀ʳ R³ αa [(e_i+s_i)j − (e+s)j_i] dR.
The two work terms cancel exactly. The total obeys ∂t M=−r²(α/a)j. The work is gravitational bookkeeping of quasi-local energy on these specified slices, **not** a nonconservation of the Hilbert stress tensors. A common rest frame for two perfect fluids makes their antisymmetric current combination vanish; it is not generally zero for counterflow. This identity includes the exterior volume, not just a homogeneous patch. Check time-integrated flux and the two work terms separately at fixed areal radii. If cancellation overwhelms a tiny integrated work, report the signed magnitude and give its own relative resolution rather than dividing by total ADM mass.

Instantaneously consider an observer moving radially at physical speed v, starting from the common geodesic experiment. For positive canonical scalar h and its orthonormal time and radial derivatives P̂,X̂, the **direct** scalar terms in the measured radial and transverse electric Riemann components are
    E_parallel^h = P̂²−X̂²−2M_h/r³,
    E_perp^h = M_h/r³ + (s_h−2v j_h+v²e_h)/(1−v²).
These follow from the Einstein equation and the exact angular sectional curvature 2M/r³. In particular the radial component is invariant under radial boosts; scalar mass-potential energy contributes to it via the enclosed mass, not via a local mass-potential term. Similarly C_parallel^h=−2M_h/r³ + (2/3)(e_h−X̂²). Dominant-energy positivity implies the rigorous direct bounds
    |E_parallel^h| ≤ 2e_h + 2M_h/r³,
    |E_perp^h| ≤ [(1+|v|)/(1−|v|)]e_h + M_h/r³,
    |C_parallel^h| ≤ (2/3)e_h + 2M_h/r³.
They apply to an arbitrary radial free/forced state, not only the locally uniform particular solution. These are local curvature bounds, not equality statements or measured densities; they deliberately retain enclosed energy and cannot bound a real galaxy with unspecified initial occupation. Exact radial traces and freely falling relative acceleration are different: vacuum Weyl tides can persist with zero Ricci tensor.

For the full, matched comparison, add the *difference* of the ordinary-material contributions along the two identified geodesics. For the radial curvature this is exactly Δ[ρ_d−2M_d/r³] (ρ_d in rescaled proper rest units). For the transverse curvature it is Δ[M_d/r³+ρ_d (u_d·e'_r)²]. Thus the three displayed inequalities become sufficient bounds on total changes only after adding the respective absolute ordinary differences, including event/radius and flow changes. A pass for just one source derivative is not a bound on unspecified free stress or ordinary rearrangement. The unoccupied scalar, identical baryon mass/velocity and matched boundary/clock recover the ordinary GR solution without introducing a new screening coupling.
