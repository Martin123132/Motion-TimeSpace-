# Equations, conventions and independent controls

This is the local spherical sector of the same positive real pole and common Einstein metric. Ordinary material is freely moving cold dust, not a stationary density enforced by hand. Set c=ℏ=m=1, rescale the field by √(4πG) and densities/pressures by 4πG. A unit of length is one **reduced** Compton length (6.671 pc for the retained example); a unit of proper time is that length divided by c. A mass in the solver is the geometrical mass GM_phys/c² expressed in those lengths. A tidal coefficient is in inverse length squared. These synthetic clouds are not proposed mass models for an observed object. The inherited local Einstein/common metric and G are inputs here.

In ds²=−α²dt²+a²dr²+r²dΩ² let M=r(1−a⁻²)/2, B=α/a. The radial equations use M_r=r² e, (lnα)_r=a²(M/r²+r s), (ln a)_r=a²(re−M/r²), and M_t=−r²Bj. At the regular centre M=0 and odd fields vanish. At the outer endpoint, beyond material and the causal signal for the duration sampled, α=1/a; thus the time coordinate is asymptotically normalized. The momentum equation is an independent evolution check, not imposed on M in the primary integrator. Polar-areal Einstein evolution is established methodology; see [Choptuik, Physical Review D 44 (1991)](https://bh0.phas.ubc.ca/Group/Papers/PRD-44-3124-1991.pdf).

Its energy, radial pressure, transverse pressure and flux are ρW², ρW²v², 0, ρW²v. Transport r²D by the coordinate velocity Bv, and p by the Hamiltonian H=α√(1+p²/a²). The two sectors only interact through the metric. No local scalar abundance is implied by the mass parameter.

A persistent field-only contribution is not called a response to the ordinary mass.

With proper time on initially normal radial geodesics define E_i=R_hat0,hati,hat0,hati (positive E compresses). For a normal observer, M/r³+ s is tangential and e−s+2t−2M/r³ is radial. A radial boost leaves the latter unchanged; its tangential stress contribution becomes (s−2vj+v²e)/(1−v²). Radial electric Weyl is −2[M/r³−(e−s+t)/3] and angular Weyl is minus one half the radial. The timelike Ricci component is the trace E_r+2E_t. These are curvature components, not coordinate accelerations. Integrate J_i″=−E_i J_i with J_i(0)=1; J_r'(0)=−r a j, J_t'(0)=0 for this initially normal congruence. Separate geodesic and Jacobi initial data must not be silently identified in a generic slicing.
