previous stage. Relating that result all the way back to the original
covariant scalar requires the earlier rescaling boundary too:

    a^2[y'^2-(k^2+mu^2)y^2]/2
      =[q'^2-Omega_k^2 q^2]/2-(H q^2/2)'.

Thus the normalized carrier-averaged boundary from the original field action
is boundary in DERIVATION.md MINUS H(R^2+I^2)/(2W0). This does not change
the bulk matter trajectory with fixed endpoint data. It must accompany the
other endpoint terms in a composed canonical transformation or a metric
variation with nontrivial boundaries. The verified canonical pullback is
unchanged; this is the explicit bridge to the unrescaled parent, not a new
coupling or an additional physical force.

claiming that the entire gravitational variation has been completed.

## Off-shell carrier bookkeeping

FREE-RESPONSE-BOUND.md states its response equation for an on-shell carrier.
For the actual W2 carrier inserted into the canonical action, eliminating
the shifted momentum gives S0+C R0 instead of S0. Its exact plane-invariance
condition is therefore epsilon=C R0-R_k Z. In the response-error formulas
replace epsilon_n by epsilon_n-C R0; the norm integrals acquire the extra
|C R0| term. This is the already-accounted background residual, not a new
force or an assumption that the WKB carrier is an exact field solution.
The action flow includes this term explicitly. Comparing to an exact
background also needs the previous stage's carrier transport error budget.
