## What was derived

The retained minimally coupled positive quadratic mode fixes the two metric
source combinations C=Psi+3Phi and D=3Phi-Psi. Rescaling the action gives
the derivative-free canonical system and its momentum shift, not just a
force appended to an unforced oscillator. The physical lapse contribution
is retained in the density and pressure. Exact matter energy and momentum
identities hold for arbitrary supplied Phi/Psi on an on-shell background.

The previous W2 residual-to-solution bound now covers the forced triangular
background/perturbation system, its counter-rotating terms and supplied-metric
uncertainty. It does not replace the full gravity constraints. A separate
physical-coordinate derivation shows how an off-shell background residual
enters; it is bounded rather than absorbed into a fitted coupling.

In the constant-metric flat limit Phi cancels at fixed physical initial
field/derivative, and S'=-mu Psi. This verifies the expected unit Newtonian
acceleration coefficient within the retained subfamily. The underlying
minimal coupling and numerical G were already inherited; the new result
is the consistent forced higher-order state/source bridge and its controls,
not a claim that minimal coupling itself was newly discovered.
