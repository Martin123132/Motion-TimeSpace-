# A simpler physical form: the carrier correction is pressure-sourced

The retained flat Einstein/background equations imply

    2H'+H^2=-P,    P=a^2 p_total/M_Pl^2.

Consequently the homogeneous second-adiabatic frequency becomes

    W20=mu+3P/(8mu).

This is the same derived W2, not a new equation of state or fitted pressure.
It explains the dust cancellation directly: dust has P=0. Radiation has
positive P; a rolling field's negative pressure can oppose it. All retained
species must be included. Constant conformal H would require P=-H^2 in
these flat background equations, so its earlier test correction was not
the generic matter-era correction.

For finite k the same substitution gives

    W2k=omega+[P(2k^2+3mu^2)omega^2-H^2 k^2(2k^2+7mu^2)]/(8omega^5).

This also yields a stable beat frequency. With y=k^2/mu^2 and r=sqrt(1+y),

    W2k-W20 = mu*y/(r+1) *
      [1-P/mu^2*(3r^2+r+1)/(8r^3)
        -H^2/mu^2*(r+1)*(2r^2+5)/(8r^5)].

This avoids subtraction of nearly identical large carrier frequencies at
small k. Setting P=-H^2 recovers the previous constant-H beat expression.
It is an algebraic simplification under the retained flat background
equations, not an independent recovery of those Einstein equations from
an unspecified deeper action. The full residual still needs pressure
derivatives; small pressure alone is not a complete error certificate.

In the saved z=10 interval, the heavy and cold backgrounds have
H'/H^2 about -0.49833. The homogeneous frequency correction is therefore
about0.333%-0.335% of the frozen-H value. This refers only to that specific
small correction, not to a300-fold improvement in the model or a data fit.
