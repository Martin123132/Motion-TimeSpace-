## Carrier connection is not new physics

The encoded heavy amplitude is A=exp(i chi) a(sqrt(omega) delta_phi+i delta_pi/sqrt(omega)). Its native generator contains i chi_N A. The original carrier's derivative comes from its saved integral; the instantaneous fibre's default uses the local mu. At fixed chi these representations differ by

This term must be handled before subtracting background generators. A CommonCarrierFibre supplies the same saved chi and chi_N to the retained and reference native equations. The explicit connection identity and its absence from instantaneous readouts are checked; it is not introduced as a fitted interaction. Earlier carrier-independent retained equations are preserved.
