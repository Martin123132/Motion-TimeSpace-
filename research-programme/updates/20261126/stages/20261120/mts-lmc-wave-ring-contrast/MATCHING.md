# Comparison policy

The primary estimate uses the two earlier fixed occupations, central/extended bar response, same weights and six satellite forcings. No adjustment of any branch to the outcome. A zero initial even m=2 perturbation is primary; approximate initially equilibrated displacement and velocity is an explicit sensitivity test. Archive time-zero is the present epoch, not the starting point.

For a separate controlled comparison only, take the fixed ordinary disk and bulge of each collisionless simulation, with no inserted Galactic bar. Require a stationary MTS wave whose azimuthal-mean dark radial force at R=17.58 kpc, z=0 equals the simulated **initial** dark force. Determine one occupation from this scalar before propagating anything or using an inner force or ring contrast. Do this separately for both representations of each archive. Freeze it during propagation. This matches the initial circular normalization, ordinary model and external satellite history, not the full initial halo profile, figure or velocity distribution. Compare the three-ring plane-even m=2 cosine change **final minus initial** on both sides; avoid calling the archive's total evolution a pure satellite wake without an isolated no-satellite simulation. No empirical preference from agreement with one simulation. The matched tests do not replace the two primary occupation predictions.
