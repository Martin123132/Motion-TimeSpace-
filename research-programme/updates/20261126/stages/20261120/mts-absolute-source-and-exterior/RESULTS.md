This separate local formation test starts from the four existing cold or wave preparations at scale factors 0.65 and 0.925159. The old metric was replayed unchanged before making the radiation and thermal neutrinos gravitate. All rows below are worst absolute-site trace errors across the same seven radii, in parts per million on the inherited expansion-drive scale. They are short-interval numerical controls, not observations or a fit.

The sign and magnitude of the missing drive were forecast from the lapse and the native photon and neutrino enthalpies before evolving the added sources. The massive neutrino supplied most of the thermal enthalpy, and the exterior contribution was too small to account for the old residual. These new columns use actual feedback in the common metric, not subtraction of the forecast. The last column sets the background expansion derivative from physical enthalpy, rather than differentiating a log-linear interpolation of H. Source equations, assumptions and raw scores are in [the derivation](DERIVATION.md), [finite evolution](FINITE-EVOLUTION.md) and [all 19 live trajectories](evidence-001.zip) (archive member: `comparison-table.csv`).

**Independent prediction:** the four frozen additional radii on each of the four preparations were not used to determine the forecast. The coarsest transport misses 20 ppm at all 16. Separating the analytic initial-potential response from the smaller evolving residual matches all 16 signed changes to within 2.18 ppm **of the thermal change itself**. See predictions (not bundled here) and [tests](evidence-001.zip) (archive member: `unseen-site-tests.json`). This is a numerical holdout, not sky data.

**Microscopic check:** the independent characteristic integral for shell energy and the separately accumulated flux/work budget need their own scale. For the fine early wave case, a 38.21 ppm light-species discrepancy falls below 0.47 ppm in the extra-resolution direct check. On the early wave and cold level-6 live histories, each shell energy is within 18 ppm of its independent characteristic prediction. In the live balance, the worst early wave light/massive residuals are 6.75/4.07 ppm, but the early cold massive residual reaches 22.90 ppm. Some lower-resolution and late component checks miss by more. This does not erase their common-metric result, nor does that result certify every particle component.

**Diagnostic correction:** the original shell-work readout used q divided by m/q where the mass is q multiplied by m/q. This affected the one recorded massive metric-work term, not the force/metric integration. The raw ledgers, exploratory forensics and two provisional cold-late work reconstructions are retained. Only the `*-native-work` pairs are authoritative for that term: the recorded volume integral is checked against the original weight, recovered from the massless channel and combined with independently native-checked physical enthalpy. Flux, pressure, shell energy and geometry were not adjusted. A coarse quadrature failing on the enthalpy's own scale remains a failed component control.
