# Conditional formation population on the retained branch

This is separate from the driven fixed-occupation LMC experiment. It asks whether the existing formation preparation, passed through a specified stationary mapping, supplies the missing inner vertical force. All three census foregrounds, the total wave number on each and the measured intervals are inherited. No force band enters the population construction.

**Formation patch.** At a=0.01 use the radius (3M/4πρ_com)^(1/3) of an equal-mass patch at the saved mean heavy density. Remove its bulk translation with W(kR)=3j₁(kR)/(kR). The coordinate number transfer D and phase transfer S come from the saved positive-frequency approximation (S=−amθ/k² in the adopted convention). A Gaussian primordial field drives both. Conditioning on a +1σ spherical mean of the physical overdensity changes means and subtracts one covariance direction. This is not conditioning on a real Milky Way formation history. The patch is not a demonstrated closed boundary. Use only the common scale-screened nodes in the primary result; neither those screens nor the full finite bank imply a uniformly accurate pressure or high-frequency limit.

**Population.** Take the candidate wave (1+D/2) exp(iS) and average ψ*(x)ψ(y) over those Gaussian draws. This choice respects the linear number response but is a specified completion, not a parent-derived nonlinear amplitude law. For X=S(y)−S(x), the Gaussian identity for a linear insertion is E[D exp(iX)]/E[exp(iX)] = E[D]+i cov(D,X). A second insertion contributes the D–D covariance as well as the product of shifted means. Keep the phase exponential intact. Remove a common phase and the mean velocity: in the spherical expansion of S the radial Bessel factors become j₀−1, j₁−krW/3 and j_l for l≥2. Project the unequal-radius covariance onto Legendre polynomials and the positive radial volume measure 3r²dr/R³. After division by its integrated trace, diagonalize each radial kernel. Equal-radius integration independently checks every angular trace; unit trace and the angular second moment check the sum. Rotational invariance gives equal weight to each m for a given l; reflection across the plane has parity (−1)^(l+m). The resulting numbers are ratios of ensemble mean masses, not a measured single-galaxy state. Unconditioned, phase-only, log-amplitude and finite-band variants are separately tabulated where calculated.

**Transport and closure.** Evolution in a common closed unitary preserving azimuth and reflection preserves the eigenvalues in each symmetry block. A patch losing material, or an ensemble whose members see different nonlinear potentials, need not preserve this covariance spectrum. As a conditional stationary benchmark, place the largest occupations on the lowest energies in the same block and solve all levels in their common self-consistent Newtonian potential. This ordering minimizes the occupied energy at fixed external single-particle Hamiltonian; it does not prove actual relaxation or global minimization of the nonlinear self-gravitating energy. Compare at identical total occupation without repairing the outer speed. The near-plane totals use the same ordinary plus direct satellite force for MTS and static NFW. Do not add the lopsided or axisymmetric wave from a different experiment.

**How much shape change is needed?** In the mirrored inner vertical component, maximize the observationally allowed wave contribution over the original marginal intervals after subtracting the known ordinary and direct accelerations; this relaxes correlations in favour of the model. Write the force as the mean of a discrete multiplication operator A over occupied states. Allow every secondary state its most favourable mesh value. With largest population λ this leaves a necessary upper bound b on the principal state's mean of A. For any normalized candidate u with ⟨A⟩≤b, if yI+α(A−bI)−|g⟩⟨g| is positive semidefinite and α≥0, then |⟨g,u⟩|²≤y. Here g is the original normalized compact ground, and A in the principal block is the projected force operator. Positivity is checked by diagonalizing its radial blocks and testing the positive diagonal minus rank-one criterion. Report 1−y as a necessary squared-overlap loss; it is neither lost mass nor an energy. The stationary shape change is measured directly as the squared norm perpendicular to g. This certificate applies on this mesh, for the retained symmetry, λ and background, not to every continuum halo.
