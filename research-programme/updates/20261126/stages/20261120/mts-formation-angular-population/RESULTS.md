# Formation support for an axisymmetric force change

Local comparison, 20 November 2026. For the specifically requested satellite-driven fixed-occupation calculation see the six-history force table and retarded derivation (not bundled here) and density-shell attribution (not bundled here). Those are the original two preparations; the following is a separate test on three census-supported preparations.

| Quantity | Screened, one-sigma preparation across the three patches |
|---|---|
| Equal-mass comoving radius at z=99 | 1.306–1.353 Mpc |
| Fraction in the principal natural state | 99.9957–99.9962% |
| Heavy vs cold matched mass-weighted rms specific j_z | 15.52–16.63 vs 37.99–40.33 kpc km/s |
| Inner radial change on the stationary mapping | +0.0000685 to +0.0000870 in 10⁻¹⁰ m/s² (less inward) |
| Inner north polar change | about −0.0000005 in the same units |
| Change in squared ground overlap on this mapping | 3.88–6.68 × 10⁻¹⁰ loss |
| Necessary loss for the inner mirrored vertical band | at least 0.0392% to 0.3075%, depending on foreground and history |

The formation calculation did not use the measured forces. The stationary population ordering barely changes the ground. No signed decision changes in either environment on the screened, finite-band, or occupation-threshold alternatives. With identical direct LMC fields across all six histories MTS passes 2/8 on each census ground and static NFW passes 2/8, 4/8 and 4/8 respectively (isolated NFW: 2, 3 and 4). These are marginal force checks, not independent likelihoods. The stream-strength changes are small; neither stream selects an occupation here. See the signed and stream tables for actual totals and original intervals.

This identifies an alternative to transferring matter into new angular sectors: the dominant state could change its spatial shape within the even m=0 sector. A squared overlap loss of 0.0392% is not an enormous mass requirement; it is a necessary, not sufficient, change on the most permissive tested ground. A self-consistent stationary ordering of the supplied initial eigenvalues does not deliver it. The open physical question is what actual baryonic or tidal assembly does to that coherent state at fixed number, in particular whether it strengthens the inner vertical force without making the off-disk pull worse.

A separate check uses 48 hash-verified historical UGC09133 paired snapshots. The orientation-averaged rms specific angular momentum at the same resolved 36.44-kpc aperture ranges over roughly 1048–2584 (historical MTS) and 1106–2565 (cold) kpc km/s. This is a collisionless historical control with a **different** wave-mass transfer (10⁻²⁰ eV), not the present 9.59×10⁻²⁵ eV Milky Way case or a normalization for it.
