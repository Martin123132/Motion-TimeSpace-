# Scope and discrete checks

The retained Newtonian sector supplies the metric source coupling; this test changes its ordinary source in space and time rather than adding a response coefficient. The independent external simulation is not an MTS prediction of galaxy formation. In the local weak-field limit, ΔΦ_b is sourced with the same G by Δρ_g + Δρ_*; a strictly co-spatial mass transfer between the two cancels, even when both components individually change. For a time-dependent wave with fixed norm, dE/dt=∫ρ_w ∂tΦ_b. With the isolated Newton kernel symmetric between source and test densities, this equals ∫Φ_w ∂tρ_b in common mass units. Applying baryonic continuity trades the density derivative for mass flux dotted into ∇Φ_w, with the surface flux retained for an open domain. Neither a star-formation rate nor a potential depth alone is injected work. The reciprocity check exchanges the two grids and their integration order; it tests the common discrete kernel, not a covariant conservation theorem.

Freeze the z=0 spin axis when testing the m=0, reflection-even response. From centered positions in the periodic zoom box construct shell masses from every gas and star particle within 500 physical kpc. The final aperture is not the whole homogeneous cosmological box. For shell radius s, field point radius r and direction u=cos(theta), a shell mass at cosine v contributes

    dΦ(r,u) = -G dm / max(r,s) * Σ_l [min(r,s)/max(r,s)]^l P_l(v) P_l(u).

This follows by averaging the Newton kernel over source azimuth and both sides of the disk. Use even degrees through 64, cell-averaged P_l over |v|, midpoint shell radii, 256 angular bins and 1,800 log intervals plus the central bin. Preserve stellar and gas mass separately as well as their sum. Snapshot coordinates are comoving kpc/h, distributed galaxy centers are comoving kpc (no h); subtract in a common unit, wrap periodic displacements, then multiply by a/h. Snapshot masses are in 10^10 solar masses/h. The GIZMO release convention is documented by [Wetzel et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2202.06969). Gas is represented by mass at the cell position, not its finite hydrodynamic softening kernel; do not interpret central-bin structure as resolved. Binned quadrupoles, a separate ring potential, a lower-degree expansion, an alternate aperture and frame, and an independent transposed energy calculation test different approximations. At zero angular order, each shell contributes exactly the isolated shell potential. The rotation diagnostic uses normalized angle via atan2(cross,dot); the per-piece metadata's unnormalized arccos of a nearly unit float32 normal has a small rounding floor even at the identical endpoint.

The separate positive-density transfer to the observed foreground is described in TRANSFER.md. In its linear cell ratio, the sum of the gas and stellar differences is divided by the same *total* terminal mass; separate component denominators would incorrectly respond to relabeling. Outside the variable support the ordinary source stays as in the parent. Use the complete nonlinear, self-consistent finite-mesh wave in the simultaneous radial and angular potential. Initialize with a fixed-number instantaneous equilibrium at the earliest snapshot using continuation from the retained endpoint ground, not a force-fitted state. Use the arithmetic mean of endpoint densities for Poisson and of endpoint external potentials in the implicit midpoint Cayley update. Its discrete energy identity is ΔE=⟨(ρ_old+ρ_new)/2, V_new−V_old⟩. Separate the monopole and angular work in the same convention, not by adding separately evolved wave densities. These work amounts depend on the pinned potential gauge; the excitation above the actual final equilibrium and signed accelerations are independent of a spatial constant. The matched static NFW endpoint includes the same ordinary and direct LMC forces; no separate live collisionless response is computed. No history is picked by its band score.
