# Joint ordinary gravity at fixed occupation: source protocol

Use the public FIRE-2 core m12i_res7100 simulation, not as a Milky Way observation, and not as evidence for an MTS halo fit. Keep FIRE's cosmological initial conditions / CDM background distinct from its baryonic mass distribution used as a source. First use all four files of each requested snapshot, all gas (type 0 including hot gas) and all stellar particles (type 4) without a temperature or star-formation cut. Exclude dark particles and audit the remaining particle types; report omissions explicitly. Do not insert the FIRE dark halo into MTS. Public cylindrical fit is a stars+cold gas comparison, not the total. Convert source header units explicitly and use distributed center and orientation; do not select by resulting force. Define the even, axisymmetric projection relative to a stated disk frame. Resolve any mismatch in frame before interpreting an angular drive. Do not combine direct gravity at a simulated site with the real MW bands as an observational test without independent endpoint normalization.

Select 572, 586, 600 as the first three coarse epochs spanning the last roughly half Gyr, before scoring, with additional intervening epochs as an independent interpolation check. Preserve radial and angular variations together, and diagnose stellar-only, gas-only, and joint forcing (component conversion cannot gravitate twice). Continue to use the same parent wave operator and fixed N. Work offline from compact verified products after ingestion. No snapshot-sized file on disk, no unbounded downloads and no unrelated jobs. Label finite resolution and unaudited time interpolation honestly.
