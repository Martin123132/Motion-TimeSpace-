# Joint gas and star formation history: right force directions, insufficient wave energy

20 November 2026 · Local. We used all gas and stellar particles from five outputs of the independent [FIRE-2 m12i simulation](https://users.flatironinstitute.org/~mgrudic/fire2_public_release/core/m12i_res7100/) across **425.18 Myr**, not the simulation's dark particles or the Milky Way force residuals. We evolved the retained MTS wave with its original particle number while the radial and angular ordinary gravity changed together. Two experiments must be distinguished: **(i)** unmodified simulated ordinary gravity, with its own initial and final equilibria (an analogue, no Milky Way score); **(ii)** a nonnegative, endpoint-preserving transfer of the simulated *changes* onto each retained local foreground (conditional, scored against the same eight signed bands and six direct LMC alternatives). The earliest wave is assumed to be on the instantaneous compact equilibrium, not derived from cosmological formation. See [source result](SOURCE_RESULT.md), [transfer rule](TRANSFER.md) and [replay and credits](REPLAY.md).

## What the sky test says

On all three locally matched foregrounds the northern **radial and polar wave changes now both have the sign required at 6 kpc**, with the corresponding mirrored southern response. This is a different wave shape, not a rescaling of the old LMC-only result. Across the histories and foregrounds the wave closes **0.268–0.591%** of the inner radial deficit and **0.076–0.152%** of the inner polar deficit. Only the two outer polar bands are met by MTS: **2/8 across all six histories**. Matched static NFW: 2/8, 4/8, 4/8 respectively, not six independent tests. No live-halo response was assumed. On the measured foreground, Pal 5 and GD-1 force **directions** are within a quoted width and their **strengths** remain outside it. ## Why the gas and the clock matter

- Within 8.3 kpc of this simulated galaxy the stars gain 1.10 billion solar masses, but the gas loses 0.50 billion: net +0.60 billion (rounding independently). In-place conversion cancels to numerical precision. The source/wave reciprocity check agrees within 1.2×10⁻¹² (km/s)² in both integration orders. This is a Newton-sector test of *one* common coupling, not an independent measurement of G. [Weak-field derivation](WEAK_FIELD.md).
- The three-knot source predicted two held-out all-phase source fields before their comparison. The measured-foreground, wave-weighted, spatial-mean-removed RMS discrepancies were **118.4 and 105.2 (km/s)²**. The gas contribution to each discrepancy was larger than the stellar one. Those RMS values are potential-shape discrepancies, **not** available wave excitation energies. With five real knots, the measured-foreground terminal excitation rises from **0.132 to 0.621 (km/s)²**. Coarse cadence got the force-band count right but understated excitation by a factor of 4.7. No time ordering was fitted. Frozen-source comparison (not bundled here).
- In the measured-foreground five-knot run the actual external work was −1321.8 (km/s)²: −692.7 from gas and −629.1 from stars (common potential gauge). Most changes the binding energy, rather than surviving as free motion. Direct evolution in the simulated baryons, without any Milky Way transfer, also gives an inward radial and positive northern polar change at the inner coordinate (−0.000175, +0.0000108 in force units); its final excitation is 0.423 (km/s)². This is a comparison of two different hosts, not a second observational success.
- All **144** primary MTS signed totals pass the unchanged 2×10⁻⁵ relative time-step check, but all 144 tiny induced components on their own miss that relative threshold. No band membership changes. Source interpolation, spatial transport partition and inclination are separate systematic choices: all 144 totals do change by more than that threshold when switching from three to five knots. Co-rotating the source reverses the small inner radial correction on anchorA. The result is not frame- or source-history precision at 2×10⁻⁵. In the independent, untransferred analogue run the small induced components are also visibly time-step sensitive. **Next discriminating observation within the simulation:** the 29 published stars+cold-gas fields identify snapshot **581** as the largest missed phase-changing drive on the five-knot history (84.64 (km/s)² in wave-weighted tangent norm). Two competing predictions of its full all-temperature ordinary potential are already frozen (not bundled here): five-knot interpolation versus interpolation plus the independently archived cold-source detail. The latter assumes the missing remainder is locally smooth, rather than asserting hot gas is cold or dark. Test both without using any sky score in the selection. If the total source stays too weak, the physics question is what earlier formation independently supplied to the initial wave or through its boundary, not which phase to fit.
