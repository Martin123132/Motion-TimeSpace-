# Fixed number is not fixed density

**Direct satellite calculation.** Use the LMC histories as time-dependent *drives*, not six measurements. In the rotating frame let the normalized real stationary wave be g, write the displacement x+i y, and project with P=1−gg†/N. To first order, δρ=2 Re(g*δψ) in physical wave units. In the saved mass-weighted coordinates the equations are x′=Hy, y′=−P(Hx+Bw+F), Lw=−2B†x, where Hg=0, L is the positive Poisson operator and F is the saved vertically even azimuthal average of the satellite potential times g. Hence (g†x)′=0 and the integrated first-order number change vanishes without fixing the mass inside each radius. Solve the projected oscillators with their causal sine kernels, from zero added wave at the first archive epoch. Compute radial and polar forces from the induced Poisson field. ## Necessary inherited excitation

Fix a local background. Let g be its terminal ground, g_i its matching initial equilibrium at 572, and U(t) the additional ordinary potential relative to the terminal potential. For a normalized pure state ψ write e=E_final(ψ)−E_final(g), I=E_initial(ψ_i)−E_initial(g_i), c=E_initial(g_i)−E_initial(g), and d_A(ψ)=〈A〉ψ−〈A〉g, with *unnormalized* matrix expectations. Since Hamiltonian evolution gives dE_total/dt=〈∂U/∂t〉ψ, subtract the reference expectation and use U(T)=0 to obtain

    e(t) = I+c + ∫₀ᵗ d_{U′}(ψ(s)) ds + ∫ₜᵀ d_{U′}(ψ(t)) ds.

The future integral evaluates the *fixed present state*, not an unknown future state. Along each straight segment between source knots, the weights of past and future pieces sum to one if the scalar progress is monotone; it can have any speed. The upper bound therefore uses one upper support bound per increment ΔU_j, not two absolute variation allowances. After the last knot e is conserved. Excursions off these five specified segments are **not** covered.

Use loss L=1−|g†ψ/N|². On L≤S and e≤Ecap the inherited energy/force pencil (not bundled here) bounds the inward force below its necessary value on all six foreground-history comparisons. The positive shape pencil bars a first crossing of L=S while e≤Ecap. For each A=ΔU_j or A=−U(0), remove its ground expectation as a spatial constant. The projected tangent is A′g. For ψ=a g+x, g†x=0, |a|²=1−L, the rough multiplication-operator bound on d_A is N[2√(S(1−S)) σ_A+S max(0,max A−〈A〉g/N)] for S≤1/2, σ_A=||A′g||/√N. Alternatively subtract t A′ from the wave block of the energy pencil. A bordered inertia test checks positivity and bounds the constrained inverse on the source column by C_t; completing the square gives d_A≤(e+ε)/t+t C_t. ε includes the numerical ground residual. Use the lesser verified bound. These are padded floating-point matrix tests on the retained finite space, not interval-arithmetic or continuum spectral proofs.

Entry requires a separate check: e(0)=I+c+d_{−U(0)}(ψ_i); use either the energy-independent rough bound or t>1 so that moving e(0)/t to the left gives an upper bound on e(0). ## Removing the small-initial-loss condition for one observable

Let H_i be the one-particle Hamiltonian in the frozen *initial* self-potential, not the nonlinear Hamiltonian at an arbitrary evolved density. Let δρ=ρψ−ρ_i. With the same Green operator and boundary convention,

    I+Bρ = 〈H_i〉ψ−〈H_i〉g_i,    Bρ=−½〈δρ,Φ[δρ]〉 ≥ 0.

This follows by expanding the quadratic gravitational energy. For large initial loss L_i a separate one-border inertia check gives H_i−μ_i ≥ δ on g_i-perp (quadratic forms), μ_i its ground Rayleigh quotient. Decomposition along g_i gives the specific lower bound (δ L_i−3||r_i||/√N) v_unit², r_i=(H_i−μ_i)g_i; the conservative residual pad covers the cross term. For small L_i use I≥F. Taking the lesser covers all fixed-number states in this discretized sector and bounds **I+Bρ**, not I alone for arbitrary changes of density. A phase-only state has Bρ=0. In the smooth corresponding continuum normalization, ψ=g_i exp(iS) has specific flow energy ½〈|κ∇S|²〉, hence the speed equivalents in RESULTS.md. These are thresholds, not predictions for the Milky Way or sufficient conditions. A constant overall phase contributes nothing. The identity is checked on the constant-phase ground, restart and terminal waves, and an independent complex perturbation.
