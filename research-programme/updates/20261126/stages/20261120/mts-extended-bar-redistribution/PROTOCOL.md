# Transfer of the independently inferred long bar to the retained stellar rings
Test the ordinary-matter source first, not a new wave coupling. Preserve the 7.354e9 solar mass central E3, gas, brown dwarfs, total four-star columns in each ring, original signed force intervals and observer geometry. Compare the same changes on the frozen E3 MTS and NFW fields. Do not call such totals re-prepared dark states. The purpose is to test the new radial leverage implicated by the previous calculation, not to refit the Galaxy.

1. Transcribe Eqs 9–10 and Tables 1–2 of [Wegg, Gerhard & Portail](https://arxiv.org/html/1504.01401) separately. Distinguish the reported integrated masses from the prefactors. Test literal t>1 and a continuous t>0 cutoff sensitivity. A positive symmetric shape using |x0| for the negative superthin entry is a diagnostic, not an authenticated implementation. Do not add the N-body part. Also test the two executable, published short/long additions of the [Sormani et al. analytical bar implementation](https://github.com/GalacticDynamics-Oxford/Agama/blob/master/py/example_mw_bar_potential.py) as a separate transfer, not an independent star catalogue. Do not import their central X bar, disk, or dark halo.
2. A literal redistribution needs a nonnegative residual in every annulus. Use the area-averaged angular surface density, not its peak. Compare nominal WGP Table 2 masses as well as the raw Eq 9 integrals. For each source report a maximum ratio and, separately, integrated masses in the physically relevant 4.5–6 kpc annulus. Report failures rather than concealing them with a fitted normalization.
3. Isolate the newly available 4.5–6 kpc annulus. Two tests: transfer the nominal profiles there only if locally admissible; and a deliberately generous stress test allowing arbitrary nonnegative transfer fractions up to all counted stars there, into the source angular and height shapes. Remove the same mass in the original vertical distributions of its actual species. At each test point sum the signed Newton kernel; a bound allowing independent radial and population choices is not one physical state. Apply no change of orbit or of observational thresholds. A separately propagated optical-depth response must use the *same* redistribution (not a bound assembled independently per sightline); distinguish conditional source distances from a recovered survey selection.
4. Check angular, radial and vertical quadrature, symmetry, Newtonian normalization, mass equality and exact zero transfer. Unchanged relative target 2e-5, disclose any failures and absolute changes or band sensitivity. No publication. This stage need not replace the parent response if no source can provide the required force directly.
