# Can the extended Galactic bar supply the missing gravity?
20 November 2026 · local parent MTS calculation · inputs not fitted to the test

**Not by directly rearranging the counted stars in the tested bar profiles.** At 6 kpc the saved wave backgrounds require at least **+0.15164** more polar acceleration to satisfy the north and south bands with an even potential; the static NFW controls require **+0.16050**. The largest direct contribution permitted by the annular mass constraints, across the tested fixed-shape reconstructions, is **+0.02465**. These are accelerations in 10⁻¹⁰ m/s². The experiment neither assumes that the dark field remains fixed when the ordinary potential changes in nature nor measures a halo occupation. It isolates how much the *ordinary source itself* can do. [Equations and scope](DERIVATION.md).

## Count the stars first

We retain the sealed 7.354 billion solar mass central Cao E3 excess, the saved [Lian stellar census](https://github.com/lianjianhui/Source-data-for-MW-mass-paper/tree/78ba3c4e8e92a2d3b6e03842e437d6e95df6e9fd) and its adopted normalizations; gas and brown dwarfs are not transferred. Four populations can supply mass: living stars and white-dwarf, neutron-star and black-hole remnants. An excess in a particular ring cannot be paid for with stars from another ring without changing the observed-source model.

The whole disk has 19.21 billion M☉ available. For the executable two-component source **one ring near 0.838 kpc calls for 325.5 against 140.6 M☉/pc²**. In 4.5–6 kpc it calls for only 0.358 billion of 3.707 billion M☉. The issue is distribution, not total mass. It is an incompatibility between this disk/central split and these transferred models, not evidence that either catalogue miscounted stars. Changing the central decomposition would be a different source. Annular budgets (not bundled here).

**Why keep two sources?** [Wegg, Gerhard & Portail](https://arxiv.org/html/1504.01401) constrain red-clump structure, not mass uniquely. Their Eq. 10 has a jump at cutoff argument 1. In their super-thin row the negative major scale cannot be inserted unchanged into the fractional power and positive normalization. Our symmetric magnitude-scale reconstruction, normalized to their Table 2 masses, is labelled conditional. In particular, the raw positive super-thin Eq. 9 gives only 0.022–0.028 billion M☉, not 3.3. We do **not** present that reconstruction as the authors' original code. Even halving the one-component mass for a different stellar population leaves a witness ratio of 1.24–1.36; increasing it by the paper's IMF factor 1.43 worsens the mismatch. The super-thin population already used continuous star formation. These are population sensitivities, not confidence limits.

[Sormani et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2204.13114) provide a fitted approximation to an observationally constrained dynamical model, not a second independent survey. We checked the [authors’ executable parameter array](https://github.com/GalacticDynamics-Oxford/Agama/blob/4e62db66e2934284013caf8b7dc97ce6156c1eae/py/example_mw_bar_potential.py) and recover both published component masses at their printed precision. Their X-shaped centre, disk and dark halo are **not** imported. The 28° viewing orientation for this transfer is a stated choice, not fitted to the forces.

## Confront both radii and both hemispheres

- **Physical, nonnegative transfers.** For each family we computed the largest safe tenth-step multiplier from the stellar columns alone. The executable example permits 0.4 over the whole disk or 1.0 in the outer annulus alone. Whole-disk 0.4 shifts 2.19 billion M☉ with zero net ring mass change. Its northern inner polar increment is **+0.005235**, its radial increment **−0.011844**. The outer-annulus-only increments are **+0.003132** and **−0.002446**. All ten admissible source scenarios, three background preparations, six satellite histories and both frame conventions leave **MTS and static NFW at the same 2/8**: both outer polar bands. The radial bands and inner polar bands stay outside. These are correlated checks, not 720 independent experiments. All 5,760 signed comparisons (not bundled here).
- **Remove the assumed mass calibration.** Allow each added component an independent, nonnegative multiplier of *any* size. Keep the measured radial/angular shapes and adopted heights. Allow even the most favorable donating stellar species at each radius. A nonnegative weighted sum of witness-ring inequalities caps the north inner polar increment at **0.01228–0.01822** for the Wegg reconstructions and **0.02465** for the executable model. The optimizing multipliers are not claimed as measured states. Even if another sector can independently repair arbitrary north–south asymmetry, the minimum remaining *even* polar force is **0.12633 for MTS, 0.13519 for NFW** across these cases. For MTS this translates to at least **2,411 (km/s)² per radian** in the residual even potential at the probe. Dual certificates (not bundled here) · [required weak-metric derivatives](evidence-001.zip) (archive member: `metric-residual-contract.csv`).
- **Remove the radial bar profile too.** As a deliberately generous numerical stress test, donate anywhere from none to *all* of each stellar population independently in every ring from 0–30 kpc, mixing the five angular/height shapes, with orientations anywhere in 25–33°. In **4.5–6 kpc alone** the upper polar increment is about **0.069**, less than half the minimum required on either background. Over the **whole disk**, the relaxed polar ceiling is **0.192**: this broader test does **not** exclude the inner polar band by itself. But the most additional inward radial force is about **0.183 at 6 kpc and 0.131 at 8.3 kpc**. Even after granting free opposite-parity corrections the MTS backgrounds need at least 0.554 and 0.392 respectively; NFW needs at least 0.227 at 8.3. Joint agreement still does not follow. Each reported ceiling is favorable to one component separately, and the azimuthal bounds are not one attainable Galactic model. Unlike the prior compact-bar interval proof, these wide limits are numerical enclosures of the angle dependence with ordinary spatial quadrature. Detailed convergence is weaker than for the physical transfers; the largest pooled coarse/fine change is 0.00262, much smaller than the decisive radial gaps. Numerical envelopes (not bundled here).

## What the same stars do to light

We propagated the identical admissible transfers into the ideal point-lens optical depth. The [OGLE survey geometry](https://www.astrouw.edu.pl/ogle/ogle4/microlensing_maps/) supplies 697 monitored subfield centres; 291 have unique usable [red-clump distance moments](https://ftp.astrouw.edu.pl/ogle/ogle3/ext/blg/). For the executable 0.4 transfer, their count-weighted Gaussian-in-modulus result moves from **0.9730 to 0.9863** (units of 10⁻⁶). In contrast, the Wegg transfers decrease that depth by **0.037–0.080** in those units. Transferring the executable model *only* in the outer annulus changes the Gaussian result by **−0.00211**, opposite to the whole-disk sign. Lensing therefore probes which material moves, not just how much. Both halo comparisons use the same compact stellar lenses.

For **EROS-2**, all 66 field centres use the retained, explicitly approximate 6.623-billion-source-day weighting; 44 have usable distance moments at their centre. The 0.4 transfer gives **0.906** for these 44 with the Gaussian prescription. Putting every source at 8 or 10 kpc instead gives **0.710 or 2.219** over all 66, bracketing the [published all-field 1.68 ± 0.23](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0601510). This is **not** a scored agreement: the true source distances, within-field weights, detection-duration window and actual acceptance mask have not been reconstructed. No distance or bar parameter was chosen to reproduce the measurement. Same-source optical predictions (not bundled here).

## Decision

There is also a derived starting condition for the wave calculation. Ring-mass conservation cancels the added monopole exactly. The change of stellar *height* drives the axisymmetric ordinary field: for the executable 0.4 source its leading moment is **−5.621×10⁸ M☉ kpc²**. Together with the derived fourth moment it predicts the independently integrated polar-axis potential at 200 kpc to **3.8×10⁻⁷ relative**. This supplies a signed input and a numerical check, not an amplitude chosen from the force gap. [Derivation and far-field test](MEAN-FIELD.md).

The fixed-profile direct stellar route now has a quantitative limit; tuning the long-bar mass is not the next experiment. Use the *same physical source* (executable family, whole-disk multiplier 0.4) and both saved occupations to solve the induced change of the wave and metric. Zero-start LMC and rotating-bar responses on another ground cannot simply be added to a new ground. The target is the remaining even potential gradient, alongside the radial and optical controls; it must come from the equation, not a force-band-adjusted occupation.
