## A different question: what sources the signed forming clouds?

At the same final time, on each archived nonlinear spherical seed with the same incident quadrupole, we projected the *density-weighted* material flow and evaluated the canonical matter contribution to the difference between the trace-free free-fall tide and the electric Weyl tide. We did not evolve the clouds with a nonlinear Einstein solver.

- At 1.353 Mpc, using bare excess phase would give −9.09×10⁻¹⁴ Mpc⁻¹ for the overdense heavy current; the longitudinal projection of the **actual excess mass flux** is −1.1913×10⁻¹². For the reversed seed it is +1.1736×10⁻¹² versus bare +9.06×10⁻¹⁴. Advecting the quadrupole with the spherical flow and moving the spherical density with the quadrupole cannot be dropped. In the **on-minus-off** current the direction of this approximation error flips: the bare phase overstates its magnitude by factors 6.35 and 5.73. The cold on/off current is zero but its ordinary current is not. Independent integral and variational determinations differ relatively by at most 7.35×10⁻¹⁰ on all nonzero measured currents.

These are about 155 and 157 ppm in magnitude **of the small host-only wave change**, not additions of that size to the total gravity. Streaming, gradient and cold controls are separate.
