# When does the independently evolved environment overcome the initial current?

**The frozen forecast got the order and sign of all three crossings right.** Exchange only the target dark-sector velocity quadrupole between the heavy-wave and cold preparations at z=99, keeping their respective densities, other species, geometry and imposed cosmological tides. Initially this reverses which material patch contracts more along the compressive tidal axis. The reversal does not last. On the reference 1.353-comoving-Mpc aperture the exchanged shapes become equal at **z=30.12754**, and then the cold preparation again deforms more. The two **native** preparations do not cross there. These are numerical formation experiments, not measurements of a Galactic halo.

## Why the order changes

- At z=32.166 on the reference aperture the original axial-minus-equatorial strains are −1.44157% wave and −2.17700% cold. Giving both targets the same starting velocity removes 54.41% of their gap for the wave velocity and 48.56% for the cold velocity; it had removed over 90% in the short initial window. This is about one target velocity, not equality of the environments.
- When the initial velocities are shared, integrating the dark density quadrupole **outside the material aperture but inside the numerical box** after window 8 supplies signed shape differences of +0.002579 and +0.002988 against total remaining differences of +0.003353 and +0.003783. The applied exterior field contributes only +0.00000189 in either comparison. These are signed terms, not fractions of independently measured matter. All radii and sources (not bundled here).
- The two-by-two symmetric attribution of the native gap to the initial target velocity versus all other preparation differences goes from 51.48%/48.52% at window 64 to 48.37%/51.63% at 72 (reference). At the exchanged crossing those shares are exactly equal **by algebra**, not a second independent success. Four actual states (not bundled here).
- Subtracting the field equations yields an independent response to the initial velocity with no exterior tidal drive. On a separately evolved finite sphere it recovers all 18 tested native-pair shape changes to 0.00136 ppm or better; increasing the radial cutoff shifts the result by at most 0.633 ppm of that signal. Using a *homogeneous* background instead meets 20 ppm on only two of the 18; at the reference aperture at window 64 its misses are 647 ppm (wave) and 5,533 ppm (cold). The finite foreground matters. ## A derivable source of common gravity, not an extra fitted coupling

Prepare opposite internal velocities with **zero total density and total mass current initially** on the homogeneous background. Pure pressureless components remain gravitationally invisible at linear order for this mode. With the retained wave pressure, the common Newtonian potential and its first two time derivatives vanish initially, but the third derivative of each mode is C_h k⁴ V_hi/(8 a_i³ m²). A relative phase velocity can therefore produce common gravity after a calculable delay, without an extra force coefficient. This is distinct from choosing the initial state of a real galaxy.

Before testing, the signed cubic coefficient and next expansion term were recorded at five radii for potential and comoving radial acceleration, with the retained mass and two diagnostic mass scalings. On the first interval doubling, measured powers are 2.999549–2.999553. All 90 tests in the first three elapsed log-a increments (through 0.0005) pass 20 ppm for the quartic approximation; at 0.008 none does. The complete equations still evolve normally there. Independent canonical-momentum integrations for the separate species agree with the summed evolution at 150 later readouts to better than 1.1e−11 on the specified scales (absolute component sum for a null). [Frozen predictions](evidence-001.zip) (archive member: `balanced-frozen-source.csv`), [all errors including misses](evidence-001.zip) (archive member: `balanced-source-tests.csv`), [independent species](evidence-001.zip) (archive member: `balanced-independent-species.csv`).

At the reference aperture at window 64, balancing the initial current on the homogeneous radial grid gives potential −2.33514e−11; the infinite-domain Fourier transfer of the same 99-mode drive gives −2.33533e−11. Their roughly 81 ppm difference exceeds 20 ppm; taper, admitted basis and finite outer boundary differ. In the **stratified** heavy preparation the balanced potential is −2.74850e−11 (−2.05% of the uncompensated +1.34019e−9); removing only the varied gradient response leaves −6.24e−13. The stratified cold preparation gives −3.31e−12 despite having no wave pressure. This is why the homogeneous cubic law is not a universal screening law. ## Numerical and physical boundaries

The true finite-tide cap changes match their integrated lateral current within 0.409 ppm; the infinitesimal changes within 0.056 ppm for the tested epochs. For an isotropic flow the exact lateral current vanishes: the worst full-material drift is 3.55e−10 of initial mass, and a relative error on that tiny cap drift can reach 100%. This null is reported, not scored as precision on an angular signal. Force-to-shape reconstruction errs by at most 0.149 ppm on individual shapes and 16.92 ppm on the signed pair differences. Six earlier numerical sensitivity failures remain in the pinned upstream release. Error estimates quantify this numerical branch, not the correctness of the dark matter content, box boundary, baryon approximation or observed Galactic mass.
