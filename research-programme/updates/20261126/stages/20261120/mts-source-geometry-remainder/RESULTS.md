**What makes the prediction work.** The conserved rest-density equation reduces to compression minus the divergence of the phase current; the rapid normal-momentum terms cancel *algebraically*. The highest measured relative discrepancy with the unreduced derivative on the central prepared grid was 5.52 × 10⁻¹⁶ or less. Holding the launch rates constant leaves 50.87–52.61 ppm missing. Propagating the launch slopes once, analytically retaining the normal wave rotation, supplies 51.00–52.73 ppm, giving net errors only 0.112–0.132 ppm. Metric, wave and dust must respond together. On the positive, narrow case the isolated metric, dust and wave channel increments are about −13,482, −27,586 and +41,265 ppm; the extrinsic-curvature increment is −145 ppm. These are fixed-coordinate bookkeeping splits, not separate measured forces. Their small net cannot be recovered by keeping just the largest channel.
