Keeping its original 46.6455 (km/s)² target and tightening just the overlap trap still gives a work envelope above it. These unsuccessful bounds are preserved. The following calculation closes the remaining case by joining the *early and late* implications, not by assuming that kinetic energy or phase is unchanged at 572.

4. At any hypothetical first energy/shape crossing the bounded source work precludes the energy exit, and positivity precludes the shape exit. Since the start is strictly inside both, the history stays inside. Free evolution after the terminal knot preserves the terminal energy. The inference covers arbitrary monotone progress along each specified segment, including arbitrary dwell times or a sudden limit with unchanged wave at the jump. The sequence need not be globally monotone as a physical potential. Deviations off the prescribed segments, an initially excited or mixed population, changing occupation, a changing orientation prescription, a live boundary and other spatial sectors are outside the result.
