# Fixed-occupation planar response: energy test

Use the saved central outer-speed preparations only, six histories each, and the same eight signed force bands. Take the vertically antisymmetric vertical acceleration at each radius, (a_z(+h)−a_z(−h))/2. Each a_z is obtained from the *signed* radial and polar components at that site. This eliminates any spatially uniform reference acceleration, including the recoil of the ordinary matter. Use the widest interval implied by the original rectangular component bands; do not appeal to unknown covariance to tighten it.

Compare the minimum potential excitation energy over both vertical intervals with the actual kinetic-plus-potential energy of the zero-start coupled m=1 wave. Eliminate the kinetic energy of the unobservable common translation, not the relative displacement. A factor above unity is an obstruction for the driven energy in this fixed linear model. Do not interpret it as a nonlinear bound, an observation of mode energy, or a bound on an independent formation perturbation.
