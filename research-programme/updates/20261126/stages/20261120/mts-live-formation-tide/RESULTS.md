# Exchanging the current reverses the ordering of the two early shapes

The halo's prepared motion makes a causal, testable difference. On the original paired formation patches the wave is compressed less along the tide than cold matter. **Exchanging only their initial quadrupolar velocities reverses that ordering.** No gravitational constant, initial density, external tide or Milky Way force datum was changed to produce the reversal.

The readout is (polar radial displacement minus equatorial radial displacement) / initial comoving radius, per one rms of the initially specified tide. Negative means relatively more contraction along the compression axis; these are not axis ratios. The reference radius is 1.35308 comoving Mpc. The primary window runs from a=0.010000 to 0.010715 approximately; the independent local-matter extension ends at **a=0.01147924**. Unlike the previous z=10 linear forecast, the spherical foreground and perturbation now both evolve. The following are the longer-window infinitesimal responses on the finite spherical ground:

| Prepared target currents | MTS wave | Cold CDM | Wave minus cold (percentage points of initial radius) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Each keeps its own | −0.10550% | −0.15956% | +0.05405 |
| Both start with the wave quadrupolar velocity | −0.10550% | −0.11053% | +0.00502 |
| Both start with the cold quadrupolar velocity | −0.15442% | −0.15956% | +0.00513 |
| Currents exchanged | −0.15442% | −0.11053% | −0.04389 |

**What explains it?** Giving both targets the same quadrupolar velocity removes **90.5–90.7%** of the original difference over this interval. These are two interventions, not an inference from comparing the original curves. An independent integration of acceleration along the material trajectories separates initial velocity, gravity and wave-gradient pressure. Reintegrating the actual live density of each species in shells reproduces gravity on the moving tracers. With shared wave velocities the remaining positive shape difference is 5.024×10⁻⁵; the dark-density quadrupole *outside the probe but inside the box* contributes +4.880×10⁻⁵, partly offset by −5.141×10⁻⁶ from the interior dark quadrupole. Outer baryons add +6.458×10⁻⁶; ongoing wave-gradient pressure adds +7.877×10⁻⁷. The exterior beyond the 24-Mpc box adds +2.54×10⁻⁸ to this **paired difference**, not zero to either individual history. Pressure and gravity can also have helped set the original current; this experiment does not assign its primordial origin to a new force.

**The particles moved; they were not added.** The flow changes both the surface radius and the angle at which matter sits inside it. On the positive finite unit tide, polar caps lose 0.0422% (wave) and 0.0683% (cold) of their respective *initial total material number* at the reference aperture. Independent sideways currents account for that loss. Across the runs the whole tagged volumes conserve occupation to better than 1.3×10⁻¹¹ of initial number. This is a check of pre-crossing transport, not a measurement of the Milky Way's bound wave occupation.

**Checks that constrain the interpretation:**
- The initial short-window integration checks both unit tide signs and halves the amplitude without changing the tolerance. The difference of the two signs agrees with the independently advanced tangent to 12.97 ppm or better across the six readouts; halving gives 3.25 ppm. The 12-to-24 Mpc comparison still misses 20 ppm on five local-density/metric cases (worst 30.45 ppm); 24 to 36 Mpc stays below 0.78 ppm. One comparison on the much smaller nonlinear *difference* misses at 33.32 ppm. These failures are retained.
- The density quadrupole can change by more than the material radius suggests. In the short window the finite monopole increases the **change in the enclosed D₂ projection** at the reference radius by 11.17% (wave) and 16.69% (cold), relative to the homogeneous-state evolution. The corresponding short-window material-shape changes are only about 31 and 21 ppm of their respective homogeneous-state shapes.
- The matter-only solver has no native interior lapse remainder. A second implementation recomputes gravity directly from live densities throughout the finite volume. Even when the interior reference functions are held at their initial values, local shape, density and potential agree with the primary matter-only implementation to within 11.46 ppm on the tested grid/options. The nonzero exterior and the assumed early initial conditions remain inputs.
- Before the swap, the initial-velocity-plus-constant-jerk forecast was frozen. At the reference radius in the longer window it overpredicts the **small deflection beyond ballistic drift** by 7.05% for wave and 10.77% for cold. The ballistic part alone accounts for 99.83% and 99.60% of each individual *swap-induced change*; this is a different denominator from the 90.5–90.7% closing of the original model gap. A separate homogeneous linear transfer check reduces the total swap error to 1.87 ppm and 52.99 ppm there; the cold case still misses 20 ppm. Neither approximation replaces the measured trajectory.

**Next physical test:** follow these same prepared states and current-only swaps through the existing dilute z≈32 checkpoint, without selecting a new patch. The signed mode-transport forecasts for windows 16, 32, 64 and 128 are already on disk. Compare the measured late ordering and shell gravity to those forecasts, not just the number of numerical checks. Do not extrapolate an early percentage to today's Galactic forces.
