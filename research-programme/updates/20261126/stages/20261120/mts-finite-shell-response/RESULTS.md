## A finite response that is not the old instantaneous artifact

We followed the same formed cold and heavy states for **40.875 Myr**, starting at a=0.65. Perturb the 100–200 comoving kpc shell and examine how its own potential contrast, an overlapping contrast and a separate 250–350 kpc contrast respond. Eight unforced tangent forecasts were saved before their signed pulse tests. Across 840 model/grid/time/strength/species/component comparisons, the maximum discrepancy is **1.27×10⁻⁵** on the predeclared nonzero drive scale (limit 2×10⁻⁵). The maximum discrepancy between the paired metric response and independently accumulated source transport is 3.99×10⁻⁹ on that scale. No assertion here is 10⁻⁵ *relative accuracy on a null*. Both branches use the same coupling and mass-label rules.

## When actual cold material reaches the distant probe

At the end of the window the nearest initially driven cold dark-matter label is only about 95 pc (comoving) from the outer probe. Before following it, we froze the time of first contact predicted by its instantaneous momentum, taking the earliest outward trajectory over the original kicked material. After allowing the common gravity to keep changing, the unforced native calculation gives 0.03754066 comoving Mpc of conformal time past the window, versus 0.03753879 predicted — **about 79.9 thousand years of local background cosmic time**. Halving the contact-tracking step changes the measured conformal delay by 3.05×10⁻⁸ Mpc; halving the spatial spacing changes it by 7.19×10⁻⁵ Mpc, about 0.19%. These spreads exceed the precision of some printed digits. Baryons do not arrive during the 0.084-Mpc follow-up, nor does heavy-branch tagged dust. Longer ballistic extrapolations for nonarrivals are not measured arrival times.

This is not just an unforced mass crossing: on the native grid the two opposite impulses move the contact to 0.036717 and 0.038365 Mpc. At the end of follow-up the half-difference in the *additional* separated metric change is +6.6550×10⁻¹⁸, of which +6.6550×10⁻¹⁸ (99.9988%) is accounted for directly by the transported tagged CDM. Here the first incoming particles really do carry the later cold differential force. This last fractional comparison uses opposite pulses on the native grid; the refined contact-time control is not an independent fine-mesh pulse pair. Tagged arrival (not bundled here).

## A photon can read that metric, conditionally

For two equal-arm light paths arriving at the same comoving receiver, the sourced fractional frequency contrast changes by **−6.49, −5.11, −4.19 and −3.95 ×10⁻¹⁶** for the separated heavy shell as the spatial mesh is successively refined at fixed physical drive coefficient. The two finest differ by ~6% of the fine value. The comparable cold output changes from +7.17×10⁻²⁰ to +6.52×10⁻²² on the first two grids: it is not resolved at the accuracy of the heavy result. Both are synthetic *pulse increments*, not observed shifts. The unforced frequency contrasts are much larger. A temporally varying spatially uniform field cancels in this experiment.

There are three readouts: time evolution along the light path, independent integration of spatial forces, and the change in separation of two successive emissions. On the two initial meshes the first and second tangent readouts agree to 7.76×10⁻¹⁶ on the drive scale. On the native grid the larger cold pulse **narrowly misses** the tangent test for the directly driven shell (2.012×10⁻⁵ vs 2×10⁻⁵). All six comparisons at a quarter of the pulse strength pass (maximum 7.47×10⁻⁶ after normalizing back). We retain the original miss. The leading common-potential approximation is explicit; passing these checks cannot validate omitted relativistic terms. ## Beyond equal potentials: a signed prediction from stress, not a free slip

On the retained Einstein-metric branch, a trace-free spatial equation gives a curvature of the difference between the logarithmic spatial potential V and lapse U. The three-radius combination at 100, 200 and 350 comoving kpc removes *both* homogeneous radial solutions at leading order; we do not choose an exterior slip to obtain it. It includes the actual oriented collisionless streams, the scalar Hilbert gradient stress, and the square of the Newtonian gravitational field. The geometric term contributes 41–53% here; dropping it would be quantitatively wrong. The coherent wave adds 23.5% and 28.2% of the heavy result at the two saved epochs. No term from the separate LMC experiment is reused as a source.

The finest two meshes on the same saved material states differ by at most 1.58×10⁻⁵ **relative to this three-radius prediction**. This tests the readout, not the formation history's continuum error. The underlying exact trace-free identity also reproduces Schwarzschild vacuum and Reissner–Nordström electrovac. The radial Maxwell tension has the *opposite* sign to radial massive stress; with the retained Einstein–Maxwell relative coefficient it cancels the geometric term exactly at extremality. These are known-solution controls; the local result is the new stress-based numerical target on the saved states, not an independent discovery of those solutions.
