**Why the old face failed.** Reflecting a potential at the radius where its gradient is measured forces the material normal flux to zero there. In the short run this does not make the detector at 1.35 Mpc into a wall measurement. We retained that failure, moved the diagnostic sphere into the bulk, and reproduced the original interior control rather than retuning the new answer. All 22 fields on the old 16 Mpc launch overlap reproduce exactly, element by element, in the 18 newly exported full-domain packets. Outer-shell propagation uses the same incident stress and proper clock.

**What is actually bounded.** Independent finite changes in densitized normal energy are compared with transport through both faces, radial/angle redistribution, stress work and dilution. The bound sums absolute errors *before* allowing individual GR members' and Newton branches' numerical errors to cancel. It is an accounting bound on this finite experiment, not a continuum supremum or a constraint on freely supplied gravity outside 120 Mpc. In the preferred final paired exterior, the sum of absolute shell pressure-work contributions is at most 0.071 ppm; the work was calculated, not omitted because it was expected to be small. The Newtonian reference here is the matched evolving cold/wave number calculation, not a fit to galaxy data.

For the full 6–120 Mpc shell the independently integrated effective source matches this metric calculation within **0.256 ppm of the static paired launch moment**; the worst split-shell, one-sided weak-trace discrepancy is 8.16 ppm. Raw point derivatives alone miss by up to 3,037 ppm on the smaller outer signal.
