# Local replay

Use the retained Python 3.13, NumPy and SciPy, from this folder with `-B`. No network or fits. Calculations are single-threaded and tables are write-once/compare-on-repeat. The supplied owner and upstream stop gates remain in force. Run workers serially, or at most two at a time.

1. Fast receipt: `python -B verify.py`. Raw inputs include the previous sealed Newton current stage and the 18 saved regular relativistic histories. The original central histories are not overwritten.
2. `python -B balance.py` reads the saved histories. `python -B controls.py` checks the analytic KG mode, FLRW radiation, boundary stencil and forced exponential integrator. `python -B newton_rings.py`, `python -B newton_rings.py --extended`, and `python -B newton_flux.py` use the separate 120 Mpc Newton solver. The time-dependent incident sources, shell crossing, dilution and sign are explicit; the last one is the unscaled current required by the endpoint expansion formula.
3. `python -B outer_launch.py` recreates the original constrained initial solve, exports all 600 elements rather than the original first 80 and checks all saved fields over the identical overlap. `python -B launch_constraint.py` compares metric traces at the original knots against the physical stress-energy and checks the growing/decaying harmonic modes.
4. `python -B collar.py GRID LEVEL RESOLUTION PAD FLAG` propagates three host values at a time, with flag 0=wave and 1=cold. The complete list of integer quadruples is CONFIGS in verify.py; run each with both flags. Resolution is steps per original mass period (the 2μ normal frequency oscillates twice as fast). The finest time test uses nine, not four, to separate sampling phases. PAD 1,2 vary artificial ends; 3 covers the near and intermediate surrounding material; 5 covers the rest on the original reflected far wall; 6 independently samples the last Mpc with a one-sided far trace for sensitivity. Radiation time samples are in geometric proper time, with q multiplying normalized curvature units. PAD 4 is an available geometry, not a reported run.
5. `python -B score.py --save`, `python -B exterior_score.py`, `python -B gates.py`, then `python -B verify.py`. Keep the two failed old-wall tests. The signed contrasts always remove each model's own no-host control. Do not use the magnitude of the static launch tide as the denominator for the time-dependent comparison.
