# What the exterior metric fixes, and what it leaves free

Use the parent normal proper time, signature −+++, unit lapse, zero shift and geometric stress units (8πG=1). Indices below are spatial, and D is the derivative of the physical three-metric. The identities apply to solutions of the retained common-metric Einstein equations; the numerical evaluation keeps the original first angular perturbation about each spherical host. They do not determine a new coupling or the exterior state.

## The evolved Hamiltonian constraint

Write the three-metric as ψ⁴ times a unit-relative-determinant conformal metric, with ψ=exp[(ℓ+2β)/6] and shape h=ℓ−β. The radial and transverse conformal logs are 2h/3 and −h/3. The normal evolution gives (log ψ)′=−K/6 and h′=−3Aᵣ/2, where the prime here means proper time. Integrate both as passive fields: they do not change the source, bank, metric evolution or clock. In particular, this avoids forming the small determinant increment by subtracting two evolved metric components.

Substituting R=ψ⁻⁴Ṙ−8ψ⁻⁵Δ̃ψ into R+2K²/3−AᵢⱼAⁱʲ=2E gives

    −4Δ₀ψ = ψ⁵(E − K²/3 + 3Aᵣ²/4) − ψṘ/2 + 4(Δ̃−Δ₀)ψ.

Here AᵢⱼAⁱʲ=3Aᵣ²/2 at the retained angular order; Ṙ includes the off-diagonal metric and angular tensor. For the quadrupole u=ψ₂, let B=uᵣ/r+3u/r². Then dB/dr=Δ₂u/r. Thus the exterior source moment between **two** faces is −4[B(high)−B(low)]/5. The regular harmonic u=b r² adds exactly −4b to each normalized face and zero to their difference. The decaying harmonic r⁻³ gives zero to both. Neither a shell integral nor its time derivative determines an independently changing incident harmonic. Keep each face in the output. This trace of the determinant in the retained slicing is not by itself a geodesic tide.

At homogeneous expansion A=a/a_launch, ψ scales as A¹ᐟ². The matter weight in this trace is ψ⁵; the corresponding normalization for the matched Newton source units is q A⁻⁵ᐟ² rather than q A⁻³ for the densitized normal-energy moment. Each increment includes the known action of this factor on the static datum. Apply wave-minus-cold and signed-host-minus-own-host-free differences **after** evolving each member. All ppm use the full changing Newton tide for that sign, aperture and clock sample, not the magnitude of the static initial value.

The independent launch-only forecast of the determinant uses the trace evolution K′=K²/3+3Aᵣ²/2+(E+3p)/2. Hence ψ″=−ψ[K²/36+Aᵣ²/4+(E+3p)/12]. Evaluate the face functional on ψ, ψ′ and ψ″ on the native Gauss polynomial, with one-sided differentiation at 120 Mpc. Propagate its quadratic time Taylor polynomial and the known clock dilution. The forecast is a finite-time test, not an assumption that the third derivative vanishes.

## The physical tidal projection

Let X=(³Ric)ᵀᶠ, A=Kᵢⱼ−γᵢⱼK/3 and T=(−⁴Rⁱ₀ⱼ₀)ᵀᶠ in the normal orthonormal frame. Spatial Gauss projection of Einstein's equation gives

    Y = T − Sᵀᶠ − (A²)ᵀᶠ = −X − KA/3.

Take its divergence. Contracted spatial Bianchi gives DᵢXⁱⱼ=DⱼR/6; the momentum constraint gives DᵢAⁱⱼ=2DⱼK/3+jⱼ. Use E=R/2+K²/3−AᵢⱼAⁱʲ/2. The explicit K DⱼK terms cancel:

    DⱼE = −3 DᵢYⁱⱼ − K jⱼ − Aⁱⱼ DᵢK − Dⱼ(Aₐᵦ Aᵃᵇ)/2.

In the static flat stressless limit the scalar particular solution is Tᵢⱼ=−(∂ᵢ∂ⱼ−δᵢⱼΔ/3)Φ, ΔΦ=E/2. This fixes a density-sourced longitudinal projection, not independent homogeneous tidal or transverse tensor data. Stress was separated in Y, not assumed absent in the parent.

For the angular readout write the orthonormal STF components as B (radial), C (mixed) and D₂ (spin two). The quadrupolar angular divergence, as a coefficient of ∂θP₂, is

    e⁻ˡ⁰[C′+3(1/r+β₀′)C] + (e⁻ᵝ⁰/r)[−B₂/2−2D₂−3B₀ℓ₂/2],

where primes in this expression are radial. Multiply by V₀ exp(β₀), integrate by parts, and the β₀′ contributions cancel in the mixed component, leaving explicit endpoint terms. Add K j, the two shear-gradient terms and V₂E₀ to recover the densitized quadrupolar source moment. The reconstruction uses the matter **monopole** in the volume correction, angular currents and the metric, not the angular density it is tested against. The energy side is integrated separately. The report includes the mismatch of adjacent fluxes; they are not silently counted as physical sources.
