# Evolved exterior trace and density-sourced tidal projection

Local, 20 November 2026. Same prepared wave/cold histories, signed and no-host fields, 273.43-year proper interval, both apertures, four quarter-epochs, preceding Newton reference and 20-ppm gate. This is a parent mechanical test, not new Milky Way data.

**Positive result:** On the continued boundary the independent metric/current projection of the exterior energy at q=16 agrees with the matched changing Newton source to a maximum of **12.80 ppm** at 320 cells/Mpc on the outer patch. The metric/current versus integrated relativistic energy difference is at most **11.56 ppm**. The derivation identifies the cancellation of the pure expansion gradient; current, shear, stress and independent boundary conditions still matter.

**Where the stronger comparison stops:** At q=32, the physical projection's disagreement with Newton *increases* from 13.43 to 20.94, 27.26, and **31.30 ppm** at outer resolutions 80, 160, 320 and 640. Against relativistic energy alone the finest discrepancy is 29.91 ppm. Refining the time step at 320 gives 27.26 ppm again. The reflected 320 comparison reaches 38.34 ppm. These are maxima across signs, times and widths, not statistical errors. The smaller values on coarse grids do not establish the 20-ppm result at q=32.

**Why a second diagnostic was needed:** the recovered scalar constraint trace matches its source to less than 0.93 ppm on every refined case, while the one-sided, direct scalar derivative misses it by up to 31.34 ppm on the finest continued boundary. The weak face uses the local source in its last 0.2 Mpc; it is an internal consistency test, not an independent boundary measurement. For the positive narrow case, q=32, final epoch, the original launch predicts a −48,127.31 ppm change of the far face; evolving it at 320 gives −48,129.92 and at 640 gives −48,120.90 ppm. The latter is within 6.58 ppm across both signs, widths and times of its own launch forecast. The cold-control dust channel dominates this signed outer signal in the source decomposition; the forecast is not uniformly accurate at the inner face.

**Upstream preparation matters more than rounding.** An independently saved 0.25-Mpc formation history reproduces all 90 overlapping field checks exactly before forecasting; its far-face forecast for the same positive sample shifts by **+17,319.62 ppm** relative to the 0.20-Mpc preparation (negative sample −16,813.20 ppm). For the positive sample the shift at 119 Mpc is only +0.0018 ppm. This comparison is of **launch-based forecasts**, not a claimed complete repeat of the 0.25-Mpc relativistic evolution. A stable forecast for one specified preparation does not supply a universal exterior tide. A homogeneous quadrupolar tide is separately free even for exact bulk density data.
