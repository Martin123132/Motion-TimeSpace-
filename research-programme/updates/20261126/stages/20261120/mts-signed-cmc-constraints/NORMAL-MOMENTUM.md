# What the real parent field leaves to fix

The retained positive quadratic heavy mode is **real**. On a slice its field and normal derivative are separate Cauchy data. A saved complex slow envelope specifies number and phase to nonrelativistic order; it cannot be used as an exact instantaneous real-field momentum without a lift. There is nevertheless a useful analytic restriction on that freedom.

At a point, write h=A cos θ and ∇ₙh=mA(ν sin θ+ζ cos θ), with ν>0. The oriented symplectic area of this phase ellipse divided by 2π is I=mνA²/2. Fix mI=ρ̄ n/u⁶ rather than holding the peak real amplitude fixed as ν changes. ζ represents the normal amplitude drift divided by m; this interpretation is conditional on a slowly varying carrier. If pᵢ=∂ᵢθ/(am) and g=∇ln n−6∇ln u−∇ln ν, phase averaging the real canonical stress gives

    E/(Rn/u⁶) = 1 + [(ν−1)²+ζ²]/(2ν) + (p²+εg²)/(2u⁴ν),
    jᶜ/(Rn)   = p − ζg/(2amν),
    Ŝ^TF      = Rn (pp+εgg)^TF/(u¹⁰ν),      ε=1/[4(am)²].

In particular the normal kinetic plus mass energy has its minimum at ν=1, ζ=0 for fixed area. **An infinitesimal change in carrier frequency does not change that term linearly.** This explains why choosing that leading quadrature is less arbitrary than simply fitting an energy density. It does not fix the spatial stress, or remove the part of the mass current linear in ζ. Minimizing rest energy alone is not a time-evolution law. With no amplitude gradient and no drift, minimizing the full local energy at fixed p instead gives the expected ν²=1+p²/u⁴.

In a direct average over 16 real phases (not bundled here), retained frequency, locally constant plane-wave frequency, homogeneous dilution drift, and a deliberately large algebra-only perturbation reproduce all three tensor expressions on both archived preparations. The locally constant frequency test does not set ∇ν=0 for an actual spatially varying global solution. In the sampled physical scale checks the largest plane-frequency rest excess is 3.60×10⁻¹⁹ of the rest density; a homogeneous ζ=−3H/(2am) gives 5.59×10⁻¹³. The latter also changes the conformally densitized current divided by its species coefficient by up to 2.46×10⁻¹². These are point checks, not bounds on an arbitrary inhomogeneous KG solution or a rerun of the whole slice with this altered current.

Feed the corrected energy **and** current back into the same constraints: jᶜ then depends on the metric gradient, so the convenient triangular leading solve cannot simply be reused as a fully coupled answer.
