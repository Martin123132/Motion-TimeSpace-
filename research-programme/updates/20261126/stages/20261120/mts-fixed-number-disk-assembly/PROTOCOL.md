# Fixed-number disk assembly control (local)

Freeze the three saved foregrounds (anchorA, cut1, cut2), occupation, final ordinary field, force bands, six direct-LMC readouts, and past static NFW controls. Test the dominant coherent ground approximation, not transport of the whole cosmological covariance. No fit, inferred Galactic age, extra wave mass, tuned phase, or change to the earlier perturbation banks.

Start each control in the stationary wave for the spherical average of the ordinary potential at every radius; interpolate only its angular part with coefficient s in [0,1]. This leaves the ordinary monopole and total wave number fixed. Follow stationary solutions at 0, 1/4, 1/2, 3/4, 1 for the slow-assembly benchmark. Use the original nonspherical *final* ordinary acceleration at every final-state observational comparison. Instantaneous assembly leaves the wave frozen at the spherical solution at t=0+, but not in the subsequent evolution. Use the same final Hamiltonian after the switch. Derive the initial force acceleration from the discrete equations and test real-time response; zero initial density velocity is not a zero response. Define time by the saved length/velocity, not by the force residual. Freeze output times at t=0, 1/4, 1/2, 1, 2, 3 and 4 in those units. Check time resolution and invariants before interpreting weak force corrections. Keep all snapshots, not the most favourable phase. An assumed spherical precursor and these elapsed times are controlled prospects, not observed Milky Way formation initial data. No claim of slow tracking without a gap/formation-time test.

Aim: compare the signs and magnitudes of the inner mirrored vertical force and both stream strengths, as well as all eight original signed components. A sudden–slow comparison is not a bound on arbitrary histories. Separate the direct LMC correction from the wave's response to the assembling foreground.
