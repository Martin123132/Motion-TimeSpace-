# Does the LMC move the wave, or does the ordinary-matter model set the result?

**Local result, 20 November 2026.** The LMC moves wave mass inward without changing the total occupation. Which radial and polar force bands are reached depends much more on the assumed Galactic disk and the initial occupation than on that small forced response. Nothing was fitted to the eight inner force bands. We used the same four sites, their original north/south intervals and the same six LMC histories throughout.

## The original prediction and the controlled change

On the original two preparations, the [sealed axisymmetric calculation](../../20261119/mts-lmc-fixed-occupation-axisymmetric/RESULTS.md) added 1.67–3.48 × 10⁸ solar masses inside the sphere through the inner probes. The resulting forces still missed all four radial and both inner polar bands. The complete saved foreground and updated direct-field/frame controls are retained here in signed-forces.csv. These are the original MTS test results, not replaced by the following counterfactual.

We then solved the stationary Schrödinger–Poisson equation in **the exact disk and bulge used by the collisionless simulations**, without any of their dark matter. We evolved the even axisymmetric perturbations of these new states through all six unchanged satellite histories. No old wave response from a different background is added. The rows below use the freely falling ordinary-matter frame. K is the north–south vertical force difference per kpc, in 10⁻¹⁰ m/s²/kpc. Ranges span histories (and, for the native simulated halo, the two representations), not confidence intervals. The same eight signed radial/polar components are scored separately.

**Fixed-number dynamics.** For the two unchanged occupations, the LMC adds 0.82–1.77 × 10⁸ solar masses inside 6.185 kpc and 1.39–3.03 × 10⁸ inside 8.434 kpc, at the expense of the exterior. Integrated net number changes by less than 1.9 × 10⁻¹⁷ of the ground across all three preparations. At the 6-kpc north site the radial increment is −0.00617 to −0.00273 and the polar increment is **positive**, +0.00030 to +0.00051 (acceleration in 10⁻¹⁰ m/s²). On the original foreground the polar increment was negative. The sign is not a universal MTS prediction; it depends on the state. On the heavier ordinary background all four polar forces pass for the two old occupations; **all four radial forces are too strong**. The outer speed overshoot independently indicates that those occupations do not transfer unchanged to this foreground.

**The outer-calibrated case.** Restoring 224.03 km/s gives 1.7000 × 10¹¹ solar masses in the wave, rather than 2.142–2.158 × 10¹¹: about 21% less occupation, not a newly derived particle mass. In every history, the wave passes both northern radial bands and all four polar bands. The southern radial force at 6 kpc is too strong by 0.00185–0.00937, and at 8.3 kpc too weak by 0.00506–0.01370. These signs are for the material frame; a single arbitrary acceleration cannot repair both sets of components. Minimal common band enlargement after such a shift is 0.00616–0.00663 for the wave, versus 0.10755–0.10814 for the equivalently calibrated static NFW. NFW also passes six bands but misses **both inner radial bands**. This is a force-space diagnostic, not a likelihood, preference statistic, or independent validation. The outer-matched wave *before* the induced response already passes six or seven; do not credit the LMC for those matches. There is no complete nonaxisymmetric wave calculation on these new backgrounds in this stage.

## What the live-halo control isolates

We read the two endpoint halo fields in both representations of each [Vasiliev simulation](https://zenodo.org/records/8015660). Retain the final direct LMC and frame in both cases; either freeze each halo at its own initial field or use its final live field. Both compression bands were already reached with the frozen host. Evolution *increases* K at both radii for every history and representation: +0.00177 to +0.00909 at 6 kpc and +0.00128 to +0.00441 at 8.3 kpc. Without an isolated no-LMC counterpart this is a simulated host change, not a measurement of the LMC wake alone. The live simulation and the outer-matched wave do **not** share the same initial circular speed.

The reason a foreground comparison matters is visible before evolving anything. The archive disk+bulge contributes K=0.67731 and 0.35445 by itself. Its ordinary column within ±1.5 kpc is about 114.14 and 53.02 solar masses/pc²; our saved ordinary inputs give 78.43–86.68 and 41.06 at the same radii. Conversely, using the native halos on our retained ordinary sources, calibrated once at the starting outer dark acceleration, gives only 0.61261–0.65112 and 0.37035–0.38486 after halo evolution. That swap is a diagnostic, **not** a newly equilibrated collisionless simulation. The simulated baryons are a prescribed model, not an independent measurement selecting it over the retained observed-source construction.

## Numerical resolution and next physical test

The six physical source banks were checked against their saved hashes. The native field controls check potential gradients, two halo representations, the combined published endpoint and the change of the extended-body frame under quadrature refinement. Wave checks cover the two initial perturbation prescriptions; 8 versus 16 time substeps; angular and radial trial-basis refinement; and an independent 30-to-40-length box test. Largest change in an individual total force in these comparisons is 1.33 × 10⁻⁶; **none** changes a signed component's in/out decision. This is finite refinement evidence, not an infinite-domain error bound. The box test changes a near-zero induced component by up to 7.87% relatively. We do **not** mark it converged to the inherited 2 × 10⁻⁵ relative target. Inputs, residuals and per-component precision flags are saved rather than relaxing it.

**Next decision:** before importing the simulation's disk as the physical answer, compare its extra ordinary column and radial force against independent Milky Way stellar/gas constraints, at the same radii and heights with compatible mass conventions. Keep the parameters fixed against the inner force bands. If an independently supported foreground survives, propagate the remaining nonaxisymmetric wave sectors on that *same prepared ground* and test the two opposing southern radial residuals. No new universal coupling or formation-derived Galactic occupation follows from this calculation.
