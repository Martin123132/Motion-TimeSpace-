# Six simulated hosts: frozen comparison
Keep the retained 6/8.3 kpc, ±1.5 kpc sites, force components, original signed bands and 17.58-kpc outer readout. Read only the first and last halo potentials in each saved MW/LMC simulation, both Multipole and BasisSet representations. Compare each simulated host to its own first halo snapshot; the change need not be caused exclusively by the LMC. These are six simulated histories, not measurements or independent realizations of the Milky Way.

Separate (a) the halo change, (b) fixed simulation stars/bulge, (c) the present direct satellite, (d) the accelerating reference frame. Include a frame-independent vertical compression and a full signed comparison wherever the frame and potential can be verified. Treat a hybrid that adds the halo *difference* to our saved static comparator as a diagnostic, not a self-consistent new run. Never add the simulated halo to MTS, tune a halo to the inner bands, or call the archived host our fitted static NFW. A noisy or undefined central force is not a valid substitute for a shared frame. Compare alternative expansions before giving the band counts a physical interpretation.
