# A source-derived short-time tide; the largest-speed endpoint fails

20 November 2026. Local continuation of the finite-ruler Newton comparison, **not** the separate LMC force-band calculation. No observations or couplings fitted. G, physical phase, particle mass, occupation profiles, elapsed time and detector are held fixed; the mechanical prescription for the surroundings is explicit. Different q values are artificial weak-field tests, not estimates of a variable physical speed of light.

**Result:** A leading transverse-traceless *material stress*, predicted from the launch number and phase without using the observed tide as input, gives **57.98–59.84 ppm** of the signed Newtonian secular change on the two detector widths and two signed clouds. This explains why the excess need not simply halve when q doubles on this short measurement. After removing the independent launch slopes and extrapolating adjacent speeds in 1/q, predictions at the saved time fractions through q=16 differ by at most **2.39 ppm** of the full-duration Newton change. At the exploratory q=32 endpoint this same test **fails by 87.31–100.65 ppm**. Do not infer a limiting coefficient from the last pair, a nonzero asymptotic intercept, or a tighter limit merely because one q=32 amplitude approaches Newton.

## Scaling and controls

- Current: across both signed hosts, all seven levels and three components, the largest relative error against the independently constructed leading weak source is at most 3.089×10⁻⁵/q after removing η⁵. The zero-host radial monopole does not have a meaningful leading-relative-error denominator; the cold branch has no additional wave current.
- Narrow positive-host total Gauss excess for q=1,2,25/8,4,8,16,32: **260.88, 159.02, 121.89, 107.70, 83.26, 70.26, 14.44 ppm**. The corresponding launch curvature alone falls from −361.11 to −11.33 ppm. The forecast for the whole tide based only on 1/q with zero constant does not pass the original gate. The momentum-only result is not a proof about the whole tide.
- Through the original five speeds the maximum difference between direct Runge–Kutta and oscillatory collocation is 0.013 ppm (Gauss), 0.029 ppm (Ricci); the two spatial readouts at 120 vs 160 cells/Mpc differ by at most 0.087 and 0.680 ppm respectively. Archive reproduction at q=1: within 0.011 ppm. Source accounting closure on the full family: below 0.00055 ppm. These are numerical consistency checks, not sky errors.
- q=16,32 extend the original forecast, on one spatial grid only. For the narrow aperture the q16/32 curvature-minus-mechanical comparison at half duration is −0.12/−1.02 ppm (positive/negative hosts respectively). By seven eighths the residual is −5.85/+1.93 ppm, then −99.22/+87.31 at the end. Signed disagreements and rapid late growth make a small unverified endpoint neither a pass nor a disproof of the limit. No claim yet that this is exclusively numerical or exclusively physical.
- Two TT projector fixtures: a longitudinal strain and independently divergence-free tensor pass at all three exterior radii. The worst spurious longitudinal readout is below 3.5×10⁻⁸ of its unprojected value. The exact expanding-dust control, on two grids and three speeds, recovers the nonzero monopole change to 0.044 ppm and limits the pure-coordinate quadrupole leakage to 0.54 ppm of that change. This is an independent control of the ruler and both curvature formulas, not a wave test.
- The family is shorter than a signal crossing of the source. Quasistatic initial tensor data, the local mechanical surroundings and the order of limits remain physical choices; the comparison does not derive them. Flat-order stress transfer is predictive in the tested intermediate regime, not an adjustable constant to subtract from all data.
