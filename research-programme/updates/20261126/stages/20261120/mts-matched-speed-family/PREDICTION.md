# Matched-speed family: before opening the family scores

Test the total finite-ruler secular tide, not a single current switch. Set q=(c/c★)² and η=q⁻¹ᐟ². Values q=1,2,4,8 and 25/8; the noninteger case tests whether ending at an integer number of carrier cycles masks a transient. Same physical elapsed 273.43 yr, two proper widths and signed hosts, with both pressure preparations and the unhosted subtraction. Same physical G, particle mass, phase, coordinate number and mass-density profiles. Compare to the independent saved proper-time Newtonian solution, not a fitted asymptote.

Hold the surrounding *mass-equivalent density*, mass flux and prescribed mechanical spatial stress fixed in SI at corresponding physical times. Initial normalized ρ scales as η², J as η³, P and anisotropic stress as η⁴, H as η and μ as 1/η. Evolve E and J locally. Supply the same spatial-stress history at rescaled proper time, not fresh cosmologies at different c. Preserve the regular incident quadrupole by scaling the scalar Robin data by η² and the two longitudinal vector wall values by η³, as for density- and mass-current-sourced potentials, with no new free tensor shear. Re-solve both constraints and the normal graph. These are explicit external data for a local nonrelativistic contraction. The fixed-mechanical-stress family is not a claim that photons keep both their original mass-equivalent density and p=E/3 for all c.

At corresponding physical times the geometric duration is T★/η, the number of carrier periods is 2q, and the common Newtonian curvature scales as η². Forecast the actual wave-normal current to be η⁵ times its derived leading weak source plus a relative O(η²) correction. If the total family tends to the saved Newtonian tide in a regular expansion, its normalized fractional excess should be O(1/q), not a finite offset. Use the q=1 calculation as an unadjusted coefficient diagnostic; use q=2,4 to predict q=8 under an affine law in 1/q, not to calibrate the dynamics. Report the extrapolated constant 2E(8)−E(4) **even if nonzero**. Test Gauss and independent Ricci readouts, time steps and spatial resolution, same 20 ppm criterion on the Newtonian change; numerical agreement is not a proof of the continuum limit. Retain the original point/cold caveats. Never relabel a significant constant as a new Newtonian parameter.
