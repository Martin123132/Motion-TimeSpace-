# Fixed occupation, radial work and the signed force

**The original LMC experiment.** In the rotating frame of the real stationary wave g, write δψ = x + i y. The observable linear density is 2gx in the physical mass inner product; x′ = Hy, Hg = 0 give (gᵀx)′ = 0. Thus the wave can move mass without adding particles. The retarded mode coefficient is −∫F(t)sin[σ(T−t)]dt/σ, at the final time T, using the six original histories and the saved self-gravity. Only the induced, even axisymmetric wave is mirrored: radial even, polar odd. The direct LMC force must be evaluated separately at both signed locations. See original retarded calculation (not bundled here) and independent shell integral (not bundled here).

**An amplitude-independent obstruction.** Express each prediction as b+λd with b the frozen subtotal including the direct satellite term and d the signed induced wave. This is a diagnostic in a linear response family, not a fit or an assumption that the equations remain linear for large amplitudes. For d≠0 the allowed λ is the closed interval with endpoints (L−b)/d and (U−b)/d. Disjoint intervals rule out a scalar rescaling in that family; they do not exclude another shape. The far domain is checked separately, not pooled as extra measurements.

**Spherical preparation.** Keep the measured terminal ordinary potential, wave number N and original computational boundary. For each earlier stellar distribution the varying external potential is U(t)=Φstar(t)−Φstar(end), multiplied by the independently assigned measured-foreground fraction, not added as a second final disk. Recalculate the starting spherical equilibrium for the earliest potential. Apply the same nonlinear self-field as in the original parent Hamiltonian. In normalized radial variables E = ψ†Kψ+ρ·V+ρ·Φ[ρ]/2, ρ=|ψ|². For unitary dynamics dE/dt=ρ·∂tV. The midpoint Cayley step uses the mean of the two endpoint densities in the self-field and the two endpoint external potentials in the Hamiltonian; the discrete chain rule gives ΔE=(ρ1+ρ0)·ΔV/2 up to the nonlinear solve residual. The source work on the final-reference Hamiltonian is separately −Σ U_mid·Δρ. Summation by parts with cumulative enclosed density provides an independent shell-flux calculation. Divide native energies by N and multiply by the squared velocity unit to obtain (km/s)². Comparing negative source work alone with a positive excitation threshold would erroneously spend the change in equilibrium binding energy.

**Rebasing the angular test.** Let D=Vdisk−angular_average(Vdisk), g the full final equilibrium, and u *any* normalized spherical incoming wave, including its radial phase. Its angular integral gives ⟨D⟩u=0 just as for the old spherical ground u0. Let εr=Esph(u)−Esph(u0). Then e_i=Efinal(u)−Efinal(g)=e_i,old+εr exactly; the constant c=e_i−(⟨D⟩u−⟨D⟩g) gains precisely the same εr. The actual initial squared loss 1−|g†u/N|² is computed as the norm of the orthogonal residual. A previous finite-mesh certificate can be reused only when initial energy is below its cap, initial squared loss is below its radius, εr is strictly below its source-work margin, and it has positive restricted shape, drive and force tests with sufficient signed margin as derived previously (not bundled here). A missing certificate is not a positive prediction.

**Angular response after radial evolution.** Preserve the full complex radial field at the transition; do not zero its current or choose a phase. Let Q project off the spherical harmonic and H0 include the instantaneous self-potential. Since Qu=0 and QH0u=QDu, ||Qψ(t)||²/N=t²||QDu||²/N+O(t³). The time derivative of the angular density due to a real multiplicative disk potential vanishes initially; a pre-existing radial current can change the monopole at first order. The acceleration of the wave is −i(H0 ψ̇+Φ̇self u); the density second derivative is 2(|ψ̇|²+Re(u*ψ̈)). Both are evaluated before propagation. The switch to the terminal angular disk is a conditional controlled preparation, not evidence of a sudden Galactic disk formation; no LMC-induced wave from a different preparation is superposed. The six saved direct LMC forces are then used in the same final signed bands against the same static NFW control. This does not calculate a live collisionless-halo response or a joint LMC+assembling-disk trajectory.
