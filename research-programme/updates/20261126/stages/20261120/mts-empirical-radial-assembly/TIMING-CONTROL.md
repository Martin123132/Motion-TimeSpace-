# What six snapshots cannot resolve

Keep the same six source profiles, independent of the Milky Way force bands. Compare linear-in-proper-time interpolation with cubic smoothstep on every interval. For a separate stress test, place the entire measured change of **one** interval at its midpoint over the retained wave time L/v, with a cubic onset and endpoint and no density discontinuity. In each case leave the other four intervals linear. Test all five possible intervals, not just a promising one. These five bursts are counterfactual histories compatible with those same endpoint masses, not observed events, probability draws or an MTS timing prediction. Keep final and initial states labeled separately, and do not convert a failed exclusion into a force pass.

The measured part evolves as concentric spherical shells during the radial test; it is the spherical average of a positive local stellar source with donor mass removed only once and bar/central stars counted once. All gas, the unclassified native component, and the wave norm stay fixed. The published shapes are transported conditionally using local radial ratios, tapered over the last common measured region of successive epochs (one kpc by default, two as a sensitivity); add a common-aperture alternative. Recompute early spherical stationary states for every source variant. At the late spherical endpoint, compare its *residual excitation* to the saved mathematical bounds for subsequent angular assembly; compare both with and without a rapidly changing interval. Neither the absolute potential depth nor work spent following a changing equilibrium is freely reusable excitation.
