# Separate Milky Way archaeological control

Use Table A.1 of Ratcliffe et al., arXiv:2509.02691v1 (published as A&A IV, DOI 10.1051/0004-6361/202557057). Use its own lookback ages, surviving stellar mass and BOTH effective-radius tracks; no choice based on our force data. One radius assigns old stars their current radii, the other their inferred birth radii. Neither is an observed past trajectory. Total mass and one radius do not fix a profile: transport the retained local stellar distribution homologously in three dimensions, with mass and all lengths rescaled relative to the authors' most recent table entry, as a separately labeled closure. Its last epoch is 0.7 Gyr lookback, not today. Transfer its ratios to the same endpoint; do not call the original masses equal. Observe the same fixed-gas and fixed-occupation conditions as the stacked-progenitor calculation. Test this separate history; do not replace the original one after its results are known.
