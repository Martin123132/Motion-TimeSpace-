# External radial history: source and comparison frozen before force testing

Use van Dokkum et al. (2013), arXiv:1304.2391v2, DOI 10.1088/2041-8205/771/2/L35. Use the 3D-HST plus SDSS stacked stellar profiles of progenitors, not the force data under test. These are different galaxies across epochs, not a measured Milky Way movie. Check the angular/radial definitions and recover six independent input profiles or, if vector profiles are not recoverable, use the authors' structural measurements with an explicit analytic profile assumption. Do not assign a stellar history to all ordinary matter. Keep gas, bulge conventions and the final local census explicit. Do not add the present stellar mass twice.

Primary question: on the existing fixed-number weak-field branch, how much work is reversible contraction, and how much survives in the wave above its final stationary energy? The earlier free-work thresholds refer to specific initial conditions and do not simply transfer to a different early equilibrium. Compute the new offset. Compare final orbits/forces only on the same endpoint, with unchanged bands and matched static halo. Treat interpolation between averaged epochs and the choice of shape/geometry as assumptions. Keep systematic differences, refinement errors and failed tests. No fits, no publication.
