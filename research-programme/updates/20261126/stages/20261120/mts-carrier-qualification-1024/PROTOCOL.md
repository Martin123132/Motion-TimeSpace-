# q=1024: first carrier clock qualification
Use the sealed q=128 formation and the same Hamiltonian/source preparation, not a fit. Reuse 144 Mpc, h5, the 160 cells/Mpc, 3 Mpc near field, signed and zero hosts, matched wave and cold controls, both Einstein evolution laws. The LMC check stays separate.

For a low-cost prospective stress test, stop the first window after 8 fast rotations and a quarter rotation (8.25/4096 of the full clock). Compare a single long step (intentionally skips 8.25 rotations) and four shorter steps to independently resolved runs with 4, 8 and 16 steps per fast rotation; to land exactly on this time their counts are respectively 33, 66 and 132. All integrate from the same launch. This noninteger endpoint tests within-step forcing and does not use a resolved restart for the shortcut. If a long step fails, do not qualify it or extrapolate to higher q. Compare the two proper-width tidal detectors and record the momentum, energy and constraint fields separately. The threshold for tides is the retained 20 ppm of the matched Newton increment; for this early diagnostic the archived quarter-clock Newton increment scaled linearly to this early time is an explicitly approximate yardstick, not independently measured early Newton evolution. Record absolute errors too. Errors in other quantities must not be called tide errors. This short launch test does not validate secular evolution or spatial convergence.
