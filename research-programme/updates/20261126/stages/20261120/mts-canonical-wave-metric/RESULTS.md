At the late epoch the corresponding backwards change increases by about 0.002121. Independently integrated face currents account for these changes. This bilinear is not interpreted as Maxwell charge. The matched cold material already has its own metric/geodesic test; its finite-interval rate is recomputed beside each wave case in [the shell table](evidence-001.zip) (archive member: `shell-score.csv`).

A few sampled times give a misleading budget because kinetic transport and pressure contain rapid positive/negative frequency interference. For the alternative early preparation the apparent error even reaches 86% on one shell. Integrating the fast phase rather than discarding it gives a worst residual of **2.20 ppm** over both epochs, both preparations and both spans. Removing either signed work term breaks the check. - The small optical difference relative to the archived envelope is **not** a stable large clock signal. Across the eight primary histories on the two finer grids, maximum normalized errors were 2.13 ppm for the initial metric rate, 2.38 ppm for its **integral across the entire interval**, and 0.513 ppm for the independently integrated shell charge. ## A stronger Einstein test exposed a different error

Moving V correctly is not sufficient: does the independently measured normal expansion h change as its radial pressure and geometry predict? Using the Hamiltonian constraint and shear-preserving lapse, the trace equation reduces to [a radial-pressure law](TRACE-LAW.md). No value of h was advanced using that law; h was reconstructed from momentum. Static vacuum and charged controls, including extremal Maxwell tension, reproduce the law to floating-point precision. In the actual archived preparations the **unsmoothed** 100–200 and 200–350 kpc expansion contrasts do not meet 20 ppm on the original dust label representation. At the late epoch the outer span misses by **2.61% on the wave branch and 4.34% on the cold control**, normalized to the largest relevant term. The matched control is a byte-identical replay of the previous geodesic movie. These misses are distinct from the successfully resolved scalar energy budget. [Trace test including omitted pressure](evidence-001.zip) (archive member: `trace-score.csv`).

The original source has multiple streams crossing one radius. An equation using true pointwise radial pressure transports their momentum differently from an acceleration first projected to the nodes of a long folded material element. We computed the difference from the source, including both shell faces, not from a fitted adjustment. On the late heavy outer span, the estimated residual after that contribution is separated drops from +2.61% to −0.0116%. The projected force term alone gives +5.960e−14 compared with the observed +5.934e−14 absolute discrepancy; both drift/face terms are below 1e−18 here. On the late cold outer span the initial-generator estimate leaves −6.57e−7 on its control scale. This locates an error in **material acceleration within folded elements** shared by cold and wave preparations, not a newly fitted force or a repaired orbit. See [the derivation](DUST-TANGENT.md) and actual late decomposition (not bundled here).

**An independent prediction of the numerical error.** Before moving the doubled-label late wave state, the material integrals predicted changes of +2.553786e−13 and −2.614068e−14 in the two trace discrepancies.
