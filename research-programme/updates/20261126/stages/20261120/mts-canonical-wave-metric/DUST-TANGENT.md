# Separating a material discretization error from an Einstein trace residual

The integrated radial momentum constraint gives h(H)−h(L)=4πG ∫_L^H j_r dr for two interior radii L,H (here H denotes the upper radius, not the Hubble rate). For signed stream coordinate X, y=|X|, σ=sign X, covariant canonical momentum p, and shell mass dM, the dust integrand with constants factored out is σ p W(y) times the fixed-shell indicator, with W=b⁻⁶/y². Differentiating it respects the motion of *the edge of the integration region in label space*. For the difference between the finite-element generator and the continuum geodesic evaluated on the same current matter, put δf=pdot_projected−pdot_local and δs=Xdot_projected−Xdot_local. The instantaneous change in the predicted span rate, up to the common 4πGρ̄ per species, is

    ∫_shell [σ W δf + p W(3V'−2/y) δs] dM
      + [Σ_streams (dM/dy) W p δs]_lower − [same]_upper .

There are **three** contributions: force projection in the volume, transport in the volume and material moving at the two faces. No observed discrepancy appears in their calculation. The sign of the second part uses σ²=1. Intrinsic background and spatial-metric time terms cancel in the *instantaneous difference of material generators* at the same initial state. They do not cancel in the full Einstein evolution law, which is checked separately.

We evaluate both fields with the mass and velocities of each actual crossing. Split the integral at every intervening metric/stream knot; compare projected nodal Hamilton equations with their continuum geodesic values inside each element. The heavy estimator multiplies the average of independently computed initial and final generator differences by the signed window duration. The cold diagnostic, where the prior trajectory archive contains metric movies but not final material labels, uses the initial instantaneous generator only. Subtraction in the diagnostic is **not** a repaired spacetime. Finite time, projection of the gravitational readout, the conditional core, and the non-dynamical smooth cosmological remainder are not automatically explained by this estimate. A genuine repair requires a new evolution that predicts the change without adding a correction to its output.
