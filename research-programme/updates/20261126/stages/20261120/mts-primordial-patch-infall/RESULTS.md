# A primordial wave patch can turn around; state preparation sets the clock

The original LMC force tests are untouched. This calculation instead starts from the archived perturbations at z=99 and carries both density and momentum forward. It asks a prior question: can the same amount of initially unvirialized wave matter stop expanding without borrowing a galaxy's rotation curve, an infall velocity, or a late-time stationary halo? Yes for the stronger of the two tested perturbations, not by the comparison epoch for the weaker one.

| Frozen primordial top-hat | Retained MTS heavy wave | Paired classical cold cosmology |
|---|---|---|
| +1σ | Still expanding at a=0.92516: proper radius 0.6167 Mpc, peculiar speed **−36.31** but total radial speed **+8.91 km/s** | First turnaround at a=0.49972 (z≈1.00), radius 0.3604 Mpc |
| +2σ | First turnaround at **a=0.34638 (z≈1.89)**, radius **0.2590 Mpc** | First turnaround at a=0.23074 (z≈3.33), radius 0.1749 Mpc |

The table uses the same primordial fluctuation in both branches, 99 common modes, the original 1.35308-comoving-Mpc mass scale, explicit late radiation evolution and no force-band calibration. The comparison epoch is the scale factor of the older snapshot 544, not a claim to have reconstructed that simulation. Increasing the conditional perturbation changes density *and* velocity coherently, not the scalar mass, total cosmic abundance, or gravitational coupling. In the weaker wave case the comoving aperture contains 5.56 times its homogeneous reference mass at the end; material is already infalling relative to Hubble flow even though the proper radius of the original enclosed mass has not turned.

**What causes the delay?** Retain the MTS cosmology and its original +1σ heavy preparation but replace the subsequent wave evolution by pressureless flow: the patch still expands at **+8.48 km/s** at the comparison epoch. Conversely, give the *unchanged MTS wave equation* the cold initial density and velocity: it turns around at **a=0.50648**. Cold initial data with pressureless flow turn at **a=0.49972** in the same background. These counterfactuals separate pre-z=99 suppression of the prepared state from later wave support. They do not say wave pressure is negligible at every scale or after shell crossing.

**A metric-source error caught and isolated.** The older radiation approximation used only the native matter list when estimating photon and massless-neutrino streaming, before the custom heavy perturbation was added. This caused the cold particles in the MTS run to infer an acceleration different from the independently calculated gravitational lapse. Deriving the signed conservation residual, and then switching the approximation off in the same binary, independently accounted for the discrepancy. At z=0 the independent Newtonian vs cosmological heavy linear contrast error falls from **1.2446% to 0.00434%**; the corresponding cold error stays near 0.00376%. The original heavy preparation gave +10.52 km/s in the weak case and a=0.35090 in the stronger one. Repairing the input matters quantitatively but changes neither turnaround decision. For further growth work do not assume the old heavy transfer can simply be renormalized. [Metric derivation, source paths and comparison limits](METRIC-CONSERVATION.md).

**Numerical checks and limits.** The original three mass scales and both original selectors were tested on the finite-volume solver. The revised, more precise radiation/turnaround table above is for anchorA and the shared heavy-defined selector. Two spatial solvers with different wave boundary implementations give the same qualitative ordering. Uncompensated fine-box runs stop at a spurious *outer* dust crossing; they were not silently counted through to the end. Compensating the initial enclosed excess only outside two thirds of the box removes that failure without touching the inner perturbation. In the corrected +1σ spectral evolution, halving the spatial step at fixed time step changes final speed by 1.61×10⁻⁵ relative; halving the time step at fixed fine spacing changes it by 1.11×10⁻⁵. An independent spectral derivative of the saved wave reproduces the peculiar velocity to 2.45×10⁻⁷ relative. Not all quantities pass 2×10⁻⁵: the accumulated open-boundary balance defect reaches 1.76×10⁻⁴ of the material patch mass, and the stripped-down linear Newtonian growth is not identical to the relativistic hierarchy. The closed-volume method preserves its total wave number to roundoff. None of these diagnostics is an observational goodness of fit.

**Where the calculation stops.** In the corrected +2σ case the first central *dust* crossing is at a≈0.535 (cold: ≈0.335). A turnaround was measured before it. A post-crossing wave core, virial mass, spin, or 6-kpc force was not inferred. The next physical step is to continue from an uncrossed restart with collisionless phase-space transport that allows multiple streams at one radius while keeping the wave and common metric live. Compare to the cold branch under the same change; do not fit the initial overdensity to the LMC bands.
