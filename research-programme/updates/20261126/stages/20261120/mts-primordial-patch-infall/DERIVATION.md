## State preparation before using a Galactic force

For the retained quadratic nonrelativistic scalar, use comoving radius χ, proper time t, c=1, dimensionless comoving wave amplitude ψ, with |ψ|²=1 in the homogeneous state. The archived late chart gives the heavy physical density, velocity divergence θ and metric. In Newtonian gauge the *coordinate-number* contrast is D=δρ_N/ρ−3Φ at linear order, not simply the physical density contrast. In the synchronous export this is δ_sync−3η for a species with dust background. The irrotational phase is S_k=−a m θ_k/k², since v=∇S/(ma). This is not the momentum of a cold particle or a gas ring. ## Common source and a material radius

    i ∂t ψ = −∇χ²ψ/(2 m a²) + m Φ ψ,
    ∇χ²Φ = Σ_s C_s D_s/(2a),       C_s = 8πG a³ ρ̄_s,

The slow component and radiation affect H(a); they are not separately nonlinear gravitating fluids in this spherical evolution. In the linear limit the equations give Ḋ=−θ/a and d(aθ)/dt = k²Φ + k⁴D/(4m²a²) for the wave; omit the last term for dust.
