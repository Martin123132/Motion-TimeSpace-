# Fixed-occupation LMC test: which force changes, and by enough to matter?

Two original wave backgrounds, six alternative satellite histories, identical north/south force bands and direct tide for MTS and the static NFW control. No refitting or change in occupation. The common ground g and its first-order displacement x+i y give δρ=2 Re(g*x) in wave-density units. Projecting x orthogonally to g conserves the total wave occupation to first order, not the mass inside a chosen sphere. The projected, self-gravitating modes respond to the *history* of the incident field, with a retarded sine kernel. The original equations (not bundled here) and independent time integration (not bundled here) give the dynamics. The north/south radial perturbations are equal and the polar perturbations opposite. No mirror symmetry was imposed on the pre-existing direct satellite force.

At R=6 kpc the wave moves 1.67–3.48 × 10⁸ solar masses into the sphere through the probe (spherical radius 6.185 kpc); the exterior loses the same amount. This closes only 1.08–2.44% of the radial shortfall. The northern polar correction here points away from the required band. At R=8.3 kpc the two polar forces are already inside; the radial forces are outside. MTS and the matched static NFW comparison each pass **2/8** in every ground/history pairing. This compares signed spherical force components at ±1.5 kpc, not eight independent probabilities or a fit to a new galaxy. Static NFW has no live wake.

**How close was a band call to changing with the box?** For each signed force F, take the signed margin min(F−low,high−F); it is positive inside and negative outside. Compare its absolute value with the measured change in F on extending the wave domain from 30 to 40 ground lengths, using the actual sign in each hemisphere. Across all 96 MTS component tests the smallest ratio is **22,349**: the northern polar force at 8.3 kpc on the reference ground with the L3M10rad history. Its margin is +0.0129083 while its change is −0.000000577565, in 10⁻¹⁰ m/s². No induced wave term is assigned to the 96 static NFW controls. This is separation from the **observed box change**, not from all possible numerical or physical error; it is not a sigma or odds ratio.

The original verifier rechecked 227 local and 1,070 upstream files. Independent density-to-potential-to-force integration reproduced all 192 signed decisions, with maximum relative difference 3.01×10⁻¹² on the nonzero induced forces. The small original polar response itself varied by as much as 0.2264% in the larger domain, so it is the band decision, not every digit of that response, that is stable.
