# A changed boundary fixes the compact force sign; native density drives an earlier response

**Formation test only.** For the pressure-on minus pressure-off force of the compact modes at 36 comoving Mpc, the old wall gave a null/visible ratio of −1.235% or −3.151% depending on mesh. On the latter mesh, changing propagation alone gives +0.3554%; changing the initial compensator consistently gives +0.1060%. With live matter, enlarging the actual domain from 36 to 72 Mpc gives +0.10598%. The final extra radial forces on the fine 48-Mpc domain are +1.63862×10⁻²⁴ and +1.73665×10⁻²⁷ Mpc⁻¹ respectively. This is a previously cubic-null phase acquiring a small finite-time response, not zero total gravity. At that radius the null-phase wave moment is −9.89211×10⁻²³ and is independently reproduced by the surface-plus-volume flux. The uniform control has exterior forces at numerical floor for these compact phases; their ratio has no physical meaning. Both matched all-cold perturbations give exactly zero difference.

**A different test with saved cosmological density and velocity.** Using the native 99 modes and the fixed tidal selector, initial density produces a quadratic force onset, unlike the cubic velocity-only perturbation. At r=1.35308 Mpc the frozen radial launch curvature is +1.62461×10⁻¹⁶ Mpc⁻³, at r=36 Mpc it is −1.40454×10⁻¹⁸. All 27 frozen potential/radial/polar checks at nine radii agree with extrapolated evolution on the fine 48-Mpc run; largest relative discrepancy 0.025 ppm (target 20 ppm). Doubling the first short time gives 3.9997 times the inner radial response. This tests the launch derivative, not an entire orbit.

At the first saved epoch (z≈97.29) the inner radial force difference is +1.39862×10⁻¹⁴ Mpc⁻¹ against total −1.63375×10⁻⁸: slightly less inward. The local spherical environment contributes −3.04561×10⁻¹⁶ of that absolute positive difference; the rest is the homogeneous Fourier response. At 36 Mpc the total difference is −1.20689×10⁻¹⁶ with an environmental contribution of −9.82623×10⁻²⁰. The 36-Mpc host-only term changes by about 0.14% when the boundary moves from 36 to 72 Mpc on matched fine meshes; it is not certified to 20 ppm there. Varying the spherical preparation outside the old core changes this small host-only signal further. Small uncertainty in the **total** does not certify the environmental subtraction. The conditional Gaussian projection explains 4.31% of the launch radial variance at the inner probe and only 0.0513% at 36 Mpc. These are model-prior projections on a specified host, not measured sky variance or error bars on the final force.

**Independent boundary control.** A compact analytic dust density with known exterior r⁻³ velocity distinguishes a correct exterior from a wall, even when the gravity boundary is matched in both. The discrete Poisson-consistent control recovers the analytic growing mode to numerical precision. Sampling the bare continuum initial potential instead gives up to 0.0345% peak-normalized density error on the coarsest matched mesh despite excellent exterior forces; mass/phase and force checks are therefore reported separately. The nonzero matter flux is part of the solution. A buffer ending before the initial collar and a general outgoing dispersive boundary are not covered by the exact dust argument.
