# Offline replay and research handoff

This stage is private and requires no service, fitter or new simulation archive. Use the existing 64-bit Python with -B, one computational thread, in this directory adjacent to mts-measured-baryon-gravity-20261120. The source and original signed bands are not rewritten. The single-stage verifier checks its own files and upstream hashes and independently tests key physical claims. It is not a proof that the acquired astronomical inputs are complete.

1. Run bar_inputs.py to confirm the frozen protocol and photometric table. Run central_bound.py and exact_ceiling.py for the analytic kernel, monopole-normalized positive ring masses and one-sided rational upper certificates. Equations and symmetry hypotheses: DERIVATION.md.
2. bar_forces.py computes 9 direct geometries and the ring-preserving E3. requirements.py compares their ordinary forces with the old dark sources *frozen*, for diagnostic purposes only. stellar_leverage.py and kernel_enclosure.py remove the old inner stars before allowing their heights and azimuths to vary; negative remaining differences are not detections. Look at the outer aperture separately.
3. Run primary_wave.py with `coarse 0`, then `fine 1` to recompute the stationary primary E3 means. Run with `coarse 0 40` and `fine 1 64` for the two six-history propagations (three occupations each; 8 and 16 time substeps). Six stationary packets and 36 LMC density/displacement packets are retained.
4. rotating_response.py takes one or several arguments such as `fine:2:2` (setting, occupation, harmonic). Both broad and fine settings cover all three populations and every even m through 32. The edge setting repeats m=2 for all three. The old bar bank supplies only the measured external potential, never a saved wave disturbance. Each CSV retains the measured-angle force, independent density Green integral, outgoing flux and source work. bar_comparison.py combines the first-order forces in the two legacy satellite frames and separately lists the instantaneous outer radial force proxy.
5. Run verify_bar.py to verify the final local seal. Use a separate directory when comparing another runtime, not a reseal over the original.

**Which result to use:** exact-rounded-upper.csv applies to the compact central *excess* at fixed columns. radial-support-threshold.csv instead concerns all mapped stars inside successive cylinders relative to the original disk, with the old dark state held. geometry-only-paired-bands.csv isolates direct gravity with dark held. full-paired-forces.csv is the forward calculation with the primary mean, a new LMC m=0 response, and the rotating bar's first-order m=2,…,32 response. The halo comparator is spherical and static; the satellite histories are reused alternatives. No artificially shifted star count field, missing galaxy population, or arbitrary best-phase response should be added to these predictions.

**Independent next source:** [Wegg, Gerhard & Portail, The Structure of the Milky Way's Bar Outside the Bulge](https://arxiv.org/abs/1504.01401) infer a two-component half-length of 5.0±0.2 kpc (thin component alone 4.6±0.3 kpc) from red-clump photometry. This is the relevant range for the extension test; it is not evidence that the force requirement is satisfied. Use their geometry and magnitude-count separation of bar and disk, account for tracer-to-mass calibration and overlap, and preserve each measured annular total. Do not add their whole bar mass on top of an inner E3 plus an unchanged stellar disk. Compare to the existing compact-lens optical-depth work and to both outer and inner forces. The current compact-support certificate does not exclude a genuinely extended bar.
