# Equal-mass central geometry test

Do not change the disk, gas, particle mass, universal metric coupling, two original populations or signed bands. First subtract the *entire* spherical central excess from the preceding 84-file stage; its polar force vanishes outside its 4-kpc edge, but its radial force does not. Redistribute the separately retained living, white-dwarf, neutron-star and black-hole central masses in the same fractional shape, not in addition to the disk. This is a test of tracer-to-mass geometry, not a new fit or a dark occupation prescription.

Use the centred untilted G1, E2 and E3 solutions from Cao et al. Tables 1 and 3 (three full-sample and six subset solutions). The primary is the full-sample E3; the rest probe photometric model dependence and are not confidence bounds. Keep physical probe locations at R=6 and 8.3 kpc, z=±1.5 kpc, the old reference 17.58 kpc and each fitted angle. Do not rescale these Galactocentric tests to each fitted Sun distance. Report the total and the change in angular and radial forces, with a static spherical NFW matched to the same reference speed. A change of ordinary gravity on a frozen wave is a component test only. Re-prepare the primary bar's axisymmetric mean on the retained wave and separate any rotating response from direct gravity; never add a perturbed wave from a different ground.

Separately derive a conservative upper bound over *every* nonnegative vertical/azimuthal redistribution of the retained central annular masses inside R'=4 kpc. Make clear which bounds need bisymmetry and which do not; allow odd structure for free when projecting the two signed measurements to an even requirement. The smooth published models have exterior tails and need a direct force test distinct from this compact-support theorem. Retain original 2e-5 relative numerical target and report exceptions, not relaxed acceptance. No phases, tracer profiles, masses or forces selected by scoring the bands. Local only.
