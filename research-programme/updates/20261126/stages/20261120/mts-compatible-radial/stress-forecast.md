# Newtonian source prediction fixed before testing

For each signed host, at both previously fixed finite widths, use only the retained Newtonian density, velocity potential, wave mass, gravity and surrounding modes to compute the secular change of the explicit kinetic-plus-anisotropic-stress contribution. In the nonrelativistic expansion this contribution is +ρv_r²/2 for cold matter and +ρ[v_r²+(∂r ln n)²/(4a²m²)]/2 for the wave. Subtract the all-dust heavy off branch and the unhosted signal. Make no sign or amplitude recalibration from the relativistic terms. Report bulk and amplitude-gradient terms separately. Compare velocities in the native normal-initialized flat flow and with a Newtonian normal congruence initially at rest in those coordinates, freely falling under the same potential. In the second case propagate its speed through the short interval from the incident plus excess gravity. These are conditional frame identifications, not a completed nonlinear gauge map. Test the sum against the separate relativistic stress/energy budget, not against the total tidal discrepancy. We have already seen that explicit kinetic/gradient energy and stress have a combined contribution of about 180 ppm; the test is a derivation check, not a blind observation.
