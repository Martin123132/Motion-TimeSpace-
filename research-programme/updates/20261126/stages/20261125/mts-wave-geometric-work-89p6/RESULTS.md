# The unaveraged wave has its own work discrepancy; matching does not simply inherit it

25 November 2026 · saved photon launch and 89.148503803–89.6 conformal Mpc window. **The native wave already requires a different time history of U from the one it is supplied.** Matching increases the RMS discrepancy and changes its phase. With the energy and momentum equations evaluated independently on both branches, their photon difference is **0.57797% coarse / 0.57799% fine**, above the unchanged 0.5% precision requirement. The much larger coarse-to-fine swing in the previously saved comparison is separately accounted for; we have not promoted an extrapolated result or changed a tolerance.

## Replay and independent test

From the respective 83.2 Mpc restarts the native wave reproduced all saved potential, lapse, momentum-rate, observable and multipole histories and both seeds of the final 91-variable state exactly (16,384 coarse and 32,768 fine steps). The physical calculation was conditional on the wave's own saved converged 77-bin stress. No matched matter state, averaged pressure law or predicted spatial U correction was used to propagate the wave. On independent samples all 12 matter RHS components and the potential rate agree with the native wave operator. We then withheld U in both half- and quarter-cell integrations, froze the results at both gravity steps, and scored against native U on the same 1,156 and 2,312 intervals as the matched run. The first fractional interval is retained; excluding it leaves the source comparison essentially unchanged. ## Why an exact replay and an independent wave prediction can disagree

The saved native wave's own projected discrepancy has magnitude 0.297453% coarse / 0.084913% fine, versus 0.082724% / 0.082732% from the blind wave work. In the complex energy/current vector the independent wave result **does not** reproduce the uncorrected saved one at 0.5%: the relative joint differences are 100.88% / 44.20%. The similar fine-step magnitudes do not imply similar phases.

The endpoint identity in the method note accounts for that difference using the change in matter stress, momentum integral and interpolation/entry terms. The heavy scalar displacement and velocity account for **99.995% / 99.993% of the signed projection of saved-minus-blind wave readout**. That is a fraction of the numerical readout difference, not of the physical energy or of the full matched gap. Both components of the original native readout are recovered within the unchanged tolerance when those measured terms are included. Blind wave photon time-subdivision (two versus four parts per cell) differs by 0.1311% coarse / 0.00840% fine in joint norm. This supports a step-size contribution from the heavy scalar; two gravity steps do not prove a continuum result or erase the archived failure.
