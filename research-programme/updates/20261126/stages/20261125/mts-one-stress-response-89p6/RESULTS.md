# One response of all 77 massive bins leaves the photon gap above 0.5%

On the retained switch-to-89.6 conformal-Mpc window, allowing the massive stress to respond to the changed gravity does not close the gap. The small change points along the previous photon discrepancy on **both** gravity clocks, not against it. The signed complex correction was projected and frozen on each clock before comparison.

The feedback vector has a norm of 3.24×10⁻¹⁰ / 2.30×10⁻¹⁰ percentage points on the common scale. Even reversing it and aligning it perfectly with the gap would not supply the needed 0.07635 / 0.07636 percentage points. That conclusion is for this one anchored response with the saved sources and amplitudes, not for arbitrary feedback at later times. Both component gates still miss; the independent 0.163%-of-original-launch size gate still passes. Baseline is the original retained control, not a new fit or independent observation.

**Numerical findings.** All 77 original microscopic source files per branch were hash-checked, not replaced. Both continuous angular trajectories replay the previous final states and quadrupoles to better than 3×10⁻¹⁶ peak-relative error. After varying the kinetic driver only, three widely separated bins were independently reproduced with the original single-bin solver (worst peak-relative difference 2.33×10⁻⁹); both stress integration formulas agree to better than 6×10⁻¹⁶, normalized to peak stress. The direct forcing supplied to matter was checked against the difference of the original matter operators. No second feedback iteration was taken.

45 of 46 photon precision controls pass the original 0.5% criterion. The exception is the **feedback vector itself**: coarse versus fine differs by 49.1% of the fine feedback norm. Do not quote its size as a converged physical coefficient. By contrast, the signed corrected total vectors differ by only 0.00214% of the fine gap. On the fine clock, denser stress sampling changes the feedback vector by 0.00055% of itself. The gate decision is the same under the time and angle controls, without changing either tolerance.
