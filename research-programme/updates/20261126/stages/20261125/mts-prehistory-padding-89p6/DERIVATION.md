# The part of a trailing average that was never measured by the padded initial state

Write θ=0 at the switch; one period of the stress is π in this clock. Let j be the *native* current, j₀ its shared value at the switch, and J the matched trailing-phase current. For 0≤θ≤π, the two filters have identical post-switch samples. Their difference is exactly

    δJ(θ) = (1/π) ∫[θ−π,0] (j(u)−j₀) du.
    δJ′(η) = (θ′/π)[j₀−j(θ−π)].

Both vanish after θ=π. This is not a new damping force or an adjustable phase. The primary implementation integrates the native momentum equation (drag, pressure, lapse and exchange), divided by phase speed, over the missing arc to obtain its rate contribution. Differentiating the endpoint formula above supplies an independent calculation. Shapes come from the retained native reconstruction restricted to negative phase plus the common switch sample; no later photon residual is used.

**Which quantity is matched?** The retained companion prescribes the mean *current*, not the mean of every stress. On the common reference metric its conservation equations therefore require δp_ref=(δJ′+HδJ)/k² and δe_ref′=−δJ−3Hδp_ref with δe_ref(0)=0. Only the residual coefficient δp_res=δp_ref−c_s²(δe_ref+3HδJ/k²) is supplied to the evolved matched branch. Its true pressure responds to its own evolving states. The reference density offset can remain after the first cycle even though the prescribed current difference is exactly zero. Sources of energy and momentum, shear, and sound speed are not retuned.

**Switch constraint.** Initially the change in matched physical heavy energy is δe=δh_f+3δΦ=0, while δh_w=δJ₀. Keeping the other state variables and supplied geometry fixed gives (D−3ρ/2)δΦ₀=−3HρδJ₀/(2k²). Thus δh_f=−3δΦ₀. δΦ′₀=ρδJ₀/(2k²)−HδΦ₀. This compares two preparations with distinct constrained potentials; it does not establish that either can arise from a physically smooth junction.

**Propagation and scoring.** The source difference in the heavy evolution rates is (−3Hδp_res,k²δp_res), in addition to the original coupled homogeneous operator for all 90 variables. The two initial-value problems have the same linear operator. Their difference is exactly the sum of a trajectory driven from zero and one evolving freely from the changed switch data. Native-wave and cold-baseline contributions, and the massive-bank response already computed at the previous checkpoint, are shared controls. The signed additional cell impulse is −Δ(W·δx)/D_right, with W the retained weights on the 13 lower states, including Φ. The integration is split at the exact first-cycle crossing, without resetting any variable. For the control-only time split, a saved piecewise-constant cell source is split in proportion to time if it straddles that crossing. The decomposition refers to *when a source drives the photons*, not independent positive powers.
