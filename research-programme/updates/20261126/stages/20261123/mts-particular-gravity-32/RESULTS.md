# What the 25.6–32 Mpc matter-sourced interval contributes
23 November 2026 · Private. Longest frozen output wave (4,2,1), k=0.3368801156243423/Mpc. The curvature-zero solution was evolved from the genuine 77-bin restart, not inferred from a fitted potential or from the neighboring regular adiabatic mode.

**This interval increases the predicted MTS-minus-cold photon contrast across last scattering, from 3.930% to 4.432% of the original launch contrast.** It also increases the contrast at the visibility peak. This is the signed, coherent partial sum through the present source coverage, not a forecast that every later interval will add with the same sign.

Ratios use the same original joint energy/current launch-difference norm, 1.89236e-12, fixed early amplitudes, equal-scale comparison and normalized cold visibility over 750≤z≤1500 as the preceding stage. The complex response is not in phase with the previous signal: their window alignment is +0.312 (peak +0.262). This is why the increase is +0.503 percentage points rather than +1.163. In fact each branch's own photon amplitude becomes slightly smaller, while their difference grows. These are numerical predictions for one Fourier mode, not measurements of a sky spectrum.

## What was actually continued
- Reused both finite-momentum restart states, their physical incident fields and slopes for all 782 Fourier legs, the original 77 q values, 81 incident moments and 69 output moments. Computed the first-order leg histories through the new interval and applied the same Fourier pair contractions. The fast implementation reproduces the earlier flight and direct projection on slow, intermediate and fast bins in both backgrounds (worst relative difference below 1.2e-15).
- Recomputed matter, radiation and polarized collision sources without altering the bank or the early coefficients. Both curvature bases were evolved as a control; only curvature zero is the particular source in the reported increment. The 77 finite lags and spectral shape return all four stresses to the gravitational equations. Independent stress quadratures agree below 1.0e-15; successive stress and gravitational increments converge on pass two. Material stresses join the saved restart; the largest metric seam across the two branches is below 5.2e-9 of its reference.
- The restricted forces are 4 Phi' for photon energy and 4 k Psi/3 for photon and baryon acceleration, applied only inside the measured interval. We explicitly split the time integration at each edge; turning this contribution off does not differentiate a masked potential or introduce an impulsive force. The photon and baryon state, intensity and polarization continue freely after the block. The archived regular-drive and launch histories add linearly, without changing their phases.

## What failed before it passed
The first MTS doubled-step comparison moved the potentials by 4.80%, although the cold control passed. Simply taking smaller steps on the same cubic coefficient curves was insufficient: the independent curvature derivative then missed by 3.05% near an edge. The original failures and their checks remain on disk. We retained the 77-bin bank, directly evaluated the native material sources at the interleaved times, and supplied eight supporting half-steps outside each edge for interpolation only. The matter solution still starts at 25.6 and ends at 32; no out-of-window force was applied. Both branches got the same quintic material interpolation and inner time refinement.

On the final native material histories the two curvature-derivative methods (momentum constraint vs derivative of Phi) differ by at most 0.0039% of the cold derivative peak and 0.0141% for MTS. On the matched-scale signed contrast grid the discrepancy is 0.0819% of the contrast's own peak. Recomputing the metric with twice the inner step at fixed converged stress shifts the signed Phi, Psi and Phi' histories by at most 0.239%, 0.231% and 0.0242% of their respective peaks. The independent switched-energy readout, time, angular, sampling and freely carried photon controls pass for each branch and their contrast. See the separate frozen-stress-step propagation check as well. These are numerical sensitivity checks against the unchanged 0.5% gate, not physical or observational error bars.
