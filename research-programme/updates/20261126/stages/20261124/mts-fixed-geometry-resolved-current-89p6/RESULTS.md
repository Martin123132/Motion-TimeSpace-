**What conservation required.** For the change from averaged to resolved heavy momentum on the same source metric, Δp=(Δj′+HΔj)/k² and Δe′=−Δj−3HΔp with Δe(entry)=0. We reversed the saved shifts, reconstructed the pressure residual for the retained sound speed, and cross-checked it against the separate wave projection. We kept both external work terms, both normal entry values and the shear rather than importing a different work history. In the actual coupled evolution the potentials respond: the integrated variables are e−3Φ and j, with derivatives −j−3Hp+Sₑ and −Hj+k²(p+Ψ)+Sⱼ. An independent complex-valued cell integral splits at both source corners. On the restored evolutions the largest local or cumulative discrepancy across both laws and seeds is below 0.000010% (p16) and 0.0000012% (p32). Both control integrations pass as well. These checks measure agreement with the evolved equations, not the accuracy of the saved wave derivative.

**A real failure kept visible.** Differentiating the original resolved density on its native phase clock and comparing with the fixed external work misses the 0.5% gate on the coarse preparation (0.57282% / 0.57394% by seed); the fine step passes (0.15507% / 0.15504%). This coarse failure was present in the prior resolved wave. Its photon run is diagnostic only; the integral check does not supersede it. All saved per-seed metric checks and fixed-gravity photon time/angle checks pass. The restored gap vector differs across gravity steps by 43.28% of its fine-step norm: the small residual is not gravity-step converged. The explicit-wave reference alone contributes a magnitude of 0.29745% / 0.08491% on the common scale; it is distinct from the stable matched magnitude near 0.539%. Branch magnitudes do not add as scalars.
