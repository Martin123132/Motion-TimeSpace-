# Q and Fq do not close the remaining photon gap at fixed U

**Local saved-window experiment · 89.148503803–89.6 conformal Mpc.** The resolved-wave slip source Q has a negligible effect on the photon discrepancy. The resolved-wave momentum-rate source Fq changes it measurably, but in the wrong direction. Supplying both reproduces the Fq-only result to the precision relevant here. The complete pre-switch U forecast, the matched external inputs other than the named swaps, the cold baseline and the tolerances stayed fixed.

| Source replacement | Coarse: joint rate gap | Fine: joint rate gap |
|---|---:|---:|
| Neither (frozen U control) | 0.826863% | 0.606615% |
| Q only | 0.826863% | 0.606615% |
| Fq only | 0.835772% | 0.614593% |
| Both | 0.835772% | 0.614593% |

These are the differences between the two complex photon rate routes as a percentage of each cell's own small momentum-route correction. Neither energy nor current reaches the unchanged 0.5% rate gate (fine, both: 0.61483% energy and 0.61377% current). All cells remain within the separate 0.163%-of-original-launch size limit: the largest primary joint correction is under 0.00366% of launch. Both metric seeds pass the 0.5% metric gate in both windows and at both steps. On the fine photon launch the peak metric error even **falls** from 0.39076% to 0.37728% when both are supplied; improving that local maximum does not ensure better agreement after signed photon transport.

**How much of the gap is explained?** Using a *fixed denominator* and the full visibility-weighted vectors, Q alone changes the vector by under 0.00028% of the baseline gap on both steps. Fq makes the gap norm **larger** by 1.069% (coarse) and 1.307% (fine); its signed projection removes −1.068% / −1.303% of the initial discrepancy. The joint interaction is under \(2\times10^{-12}\) of the initial gap on both steps, consistent with zero; its numerical remainder is not individually resolved. On the fine step the matched-side residual, excluding the independent explicit-wave reference, also grows: 0.53900% to 0.54737% of the current frozen-U small correction. The last stage's 0.53932% used the older **flux-only** denominator; this is not a change to the baseline state.

**Why Fq behaves this way.** For this constraint-evolved branch, Fq enters the momentum-rate readout but does not drive the evolved matter or metric states. For both choices of Q, all 68 saved comparisons of state histories, differentiated curvature, kicks, and non-Fq ledger entries were bit-identical before and after changing Fq. The two routes therefore separate by the direct transport of the added Fq, with no hidden change of occupation or preparation. Q can drive states through the lapse, but its effect here is small. Independent transport of the signed direct and induced terms recovers each measured complex change; the worst joint RMS closure error is below \(2.1\times10^{-11}\) of the original gap. Photon time/angle controls pass for both single-source changes. The gravity-step change of the Fq response is below \(10^{-8}\) of that response, even though the full baseline gap remains gravity-step-sensitive. This is a result about these substitutions on the two saved clocks, not a continuum error bound.

**No fractional Fq blend can rescue this test.** For either *fixed tested Q* and any fraction \(0\le t\le1\) of the measured donor-minus-host Fq, let \(b=M_1-M_0\) be its full transported change to the momentum-route vector. Linearity and the state equality imply \(M(t)=M_0+tb,\ g(t)=g_0-tb\). Thus \(\|g(t)\|/\|M(t)\|\ge(\|g_0\|-\|b\|)_+/(\|M_0\|+\|b\|)\). The primary joint lower bound is at least **0.81719% coarse and 0.59697% fine**, above 0.5%, without fitting a fraction. This does not exclude a different physical source or arbitrary alternative Q histories.

Keep external work, shear, and initial normal energy/current fixed. Test whether that moves the signed photon gap at both steps. No extension or new authoritative restart was made here.
