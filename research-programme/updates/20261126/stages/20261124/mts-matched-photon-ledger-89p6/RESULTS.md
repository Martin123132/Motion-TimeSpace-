# What adds up to the 0.645% matched photon discrepancy?

**24 November 2026 — sealed-history experiment at the 89.148503803 Mpc switch.** All 25 signed matter, radiation and geometric terms add back to the archived matched photon **energy and current** at both gravity steps, in the previously fixed interior and the full window. Across primary, time and angle settings the worst relative error was below \(6.3\times10^{-11}\) in either visibility-weighted component, and below \(9\times10^{-11}\) on the full saved readout grid. The cold baseline, independently resolved reference and physical thresholds are unchanged.

## The cancellation, not a single faulty species

On the fine step the following are **signed projections in units of the net matched vector**, not standalone errors or fractions of observed energy:

| Source group | Post-first-cycle interior | Full interval |
|---|---:|---:|
| Geometry | +8.0721 | +5.2784 |
| Radiation and collisionless hierarchy | +4.3437 | +6.8663 |
| Remaining matter (cold, baryon and both fields) | −11.4158 | −11.1448 |
| **Sum** | **1.0000** | **1.0000** |

The corresponding net magnitudes are 0.590496% and 0.645009% of the frozen small photon correction. Interior here means **89.241796875 to 89.5984375 Mpc**, not the whole interval after the switch. The 25 individual vector magnitudes sum to approximately 975 and 1,128 times the corresponding net magnitude. The cold-state and explicit cold-current terms, for example, point strongly in opposite directions along the net. Ranking their individual magnitudes would not identify a broken species. The geometry, radiation and matter vectors have parts perpendicular to the net, so projection and norm are distinct.

## What actually moves the answer?

These are *saved, controlled alternatives*, with their states allowed to respond. The interior and whole-window columns use the same small momentum-route correction as denominator at each step. The last column gives the actual **full two-route gap**, including the independent wave reference, divided by each run's own correction.

| Geometric source in the matched calculation | Interior p16 / p32 | Whole p16 / p32 | Full route gap p16 / p32 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| No paired flux correction | 0.72532% / 0.72533% | 0.80007% / 0.80007% | 1.08392% / 0.86768% |
| Frozen entry-only flux correction | 0.59049% / 0.59050% | **0.64501% / 0.64501%** | 0.93481% / 0.71034% |
| Supply the entire geometric energy source \(U\) from the resolved wave | 0.47071% / 0.47072% | **0.53942% / 0.53942%** | 0.82696% / 0.60673% |

Giving the solver the full resolved \(U\) is a diagnostic, not a prediction. It improves the whole-window result but does not clear the 0.5% route gate. The broader same-companion control, using resolved-wave surrounding sources rather than only replacing \(U\), gives a whole matched-side magnitude of 0.58992% at both steps. It is not the original independent reference, and changing more inputs does not monotonically improve the answer.

**A specific next physical target.** When changing only \(U\) from the entry forecast to the resolved value, essentially all of the signed *change* in the photon vector is direct geometry (99.99999% on the fine step). At fixed matched background we predicted that direct effect from \(D_\eta(\Delta U/D)+{\cal H}\Delta U/D\), independently of the downstream matter response. Its signed composition over the full interval is:

| Piece of the direct geometric change | Signed share |
|---|---:|
| Potential–Laplacian term | **69.846%** |
| Squared potential | 16.931% |
| Potential-gradient term | 13.229% |
| Remaining squared-flux error after the frozen forecast | −0.0057% |

These percentages apply to the **difference between two controlled calculations**, not to the entire 0.645% residual. The interior Laplacian share is 69.872%; the two gravity steps give the same ordering. Four reconstructed geometric sources sum back to the directly transported metric change at both steps. The signed reference was kept separate, not counted as a matter term.
