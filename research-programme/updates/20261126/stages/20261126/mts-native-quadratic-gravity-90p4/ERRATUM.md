# Correction to the 26 November auxiliary memory-load normalization

Keep this with any release of the total-phase-stress memory calculation. In mts-total-phase-stress-90p4-20261126/memory_history.py the optional qe_qj samples are taken from the late canonical source polynomial on a time axis beginning at 89.275. That table begins at 89.55. The samples before 89.55 are extrapolations and must **not** be used as physical source values. memory_load.py used its first Qe for the additive normalization of one changed-source diagnostic. Its agreement at launch was agreement with this erroneous normalization. Do not quote the old percentages for the change of the complete constraint load.

Use the archived in-domain early source overlay through 89.6, the matched host source through 89.65, and the late source only after that. A fresh read-only replay is in memory-erratum.json. At p32 /1280 the instantaneous, first-order and second-order rolling-current approximations now have maximum load-change errors of 0.191055%, 0.057965% and 0.066653% across the two gravity routes, instead of roughly 0.000335%, 0.000102% and 0.000117%. The worst corrected comparison across both grids and samplings is 0.19115%, inside the original 0.5%. The original constant offset should not be used as an absolute energy prediction. A different offset to Y produces a physical energy difference proportional to rho; it does not cancel from the fractional change in the complete unnormalized load.

The correction does not change the exact Y equation, the direct historical Y-drift replay, or the old stored gravity and photon evolutions. In the later source split the optional table is only used after 89.65, inside its support. The original separate photon comparison and today's 24-mode repair address different quantities. Both the original source artifact and this correction are preserved. See harmonic_audit.py and the paired original/corrected rows in memory-erratum.json.
