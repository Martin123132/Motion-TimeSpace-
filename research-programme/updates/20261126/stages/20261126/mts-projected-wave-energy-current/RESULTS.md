## Derivation and controls

1. **Balance of the actual projection.** Apply the retained ±6π slow-intercept operator to the appropriately scale-normalized field densities, current, pressure and the work done by the metric. Differentiation of the moving projector and projection of the microscopic equations do not commute; samples entering or leaving the fit give discrete changes. On the primary grid there are 121 such changes across 31 supported centers, 89.70625–89.89375 Mpc (1.015 microscopic cycles). The derived budget predicts the changes in the independently saved continuous-inverse means. Across both heavy geometries the worst mode error is 0.000687% for energy and 0.02193% for current. On the resolved wave, omitting metric/work correlations makes 34 energy modes and five current modes fail. On the matched geometry the uncorrected mean itself already passes this particular integrated-change test. This is an equation and numerical-operator check, not independent observational validation. The resolved heavy wave and the matched fluid are not competing baseline cosmologies; a cold-only control has zero heavy stress.
2. **Is the independently conserved mean current different?** Preserve projected wave energy on each specified geometry and use energy and momentum conservation to derive a retarded equation for the required current. On the resolved wave its correction reaches 0.01012% of the peak mean current, but 5.17% of the peak change during the window; it clears ten times the sampling variation for every mode. Metric/work correlations dominate. On the matched geometry the putative correction is only about 0.0000002% of peak mean current; denser sampling changes it by 51% of itself and it disagrees with direct energy-only reconstruction. **Its nonzero amplitude is unresolved**, and it does not explain the saved matched fluid–wave gap. The native-density-only positive control separately reconstructs native current drift within 0.00040% on every mode, without using later current to drive it.
3. **Why the difference grows.** Subtract the projected wave equations from the native isotropic pressure/energy/current system. This gives distinct signed inherited, pressure, background enthalpy, metric-work, moving-fit and support-edge terms (CONTRAST.md). The known-metric reconstruction and two numerical controls led to an early-input pressure test. On the **retained preparation's** eleven-center test, the inherited term has a signed projection of **95.43%** onto the total current contrast and pressure **4.568%**. When the inherited solution is removed on the scoring side, the predicted pressure has about 100.0001% signed projection onto the newly driven current: this is a directional inner product, not zero residual or a probability. The driven prediction still misses five individual current modes (peak error 0.00793% with enthalpy included). For the newly driven *energy*, dropping enthalpy gives 18.55% peak error; including it reduces this to 0.0581%, with 22 individual misses. No late pressure, phase adjustment or new coupling supplied the past-input forecast.
4. **Where to spend the next calculation.** All 13 remaining retained energy exceptions, but only one of the four current exceptions, also fail with *actual geometry and the reference wave supplied*. In this known-metric control, carrying the energy integration difference already present by 89.275 is sufficient to exceed tolerance on those 13 weak energy contrasts. None of the control's failing energy/current modes acquires an over-threshold error solely during the eleven scored centers. Across *all* modes rather than the failing subset, most of the signed fluid-error pattern accumulates between 89.275 and 89.83125. These statements use different denominators; no state was reset to get the quoted forecast.
