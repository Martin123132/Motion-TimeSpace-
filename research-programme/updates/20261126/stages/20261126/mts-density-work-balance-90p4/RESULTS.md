# Why the two gravity routes send different work to the photons

**The leading surviving contribution is the difference in how fast the explicit heavy field’s perturbation energy changes.** On the fine grid it contributes **+0.40447 percentage points** along the cumulative photon discrepancy. The full discrepancy remains **0.65971%** of the original small photon correction, against the pre-existing **0.5%** limit. No histories, parameters or tolerances were changed. These are comparisons of the prepared matched and resolved-wave evolutions, with the same cold comparator, not observations of a discrepancy in the sky.

## What the equations predict

Starting from each sealed segment, all 60 detailed replays (fifteen pieces, two grids, matched and resolved) reproduced their complete reference packets exactly, including the angular populations and all gravitational routes. We took the *rates of the states* from the equations; only prescribed-source and background coefficients required differentiation. The resulting matched-minus-resolved density residual predicts the previously frozen photon contribution to **0.00957% on p16 and 0.00819% on p32 of that contribution’s own norm**. In original small-correction units the fine-grid discrepancy of this reconstruction is 0.00003783%. The known discrepancy was not used as a source.

## Two explanations tested

- **Compensating current.** In the retained exchange equations the rapidly oscillating derivative of the heavy current cancels *exactly* between energy and current work. The reduced formula (see DERIVATION.md) agrees with the unsimplified term to 0.00053% or better pointwise, even though the derivative terms have a peak-sum over ten thousand times the net peak. The surviving dilution work nearly cancels the lapse contribution: their fine-grid combined vector has magnitude 0.00618%, despite individual magnitudes near 4.69%. A separate background bracket peaks at 15.86% of the constraint denominator minus k². It is reproduced by −3(v_h²−ρ₀/a)/2, independently integrating the oscillator on the averaged background; with the denominator-rate contribution its signed projection is +0.01697 points. This is a feature of the retained hybrid background/perturbation treatment, not a 15.86% photon error.
- **Error in the frozen geometric source U.** At the *same matter states*, the independently measured native input changes the new interval’s photon driving by only **0.000301%** in joint norm. At least **0.15971 points** would have to come off the present gap. The direct effect is more than 500 times too small, and points slightly in the wrong direction. This test includes energy and lapse driving with the inherited photon state fixed; it does not bound matter backreaction from changing the model.

## Precision and disposition

The equation-derived energy and current rates agreed with derivatives of the saved totals on all four trajectories (worst 0.00234% on the corresponding rate’s peak). The matched-minus-resolved instantaneous residual agreed with the earlier density-derivative test to 0.149%/0.215% on p16/p32. **Not every endpoint derivative is precise to 0.5%:** cubic versus quintic coefficient derivatives differ at the boundary by up to 1.272% on the *individual residual’s* scale; three of four pointwise order checks miss. Excluding only the two endpoints the worst is 0.344%. No endpoint was excluded from photon scoring, where the order change was below 0.007% of the relevant branch contribution. The full condition is not a pass just because the transported result is stable.
