# Replay order and archive notes
Use the saved Python 3.13 environment with NumPy and SciPy. Keep BLAS/OMP at one thread. Relative paths are with respect to this stage. No network, fit or catalogue access is required. Run verify_stage.py first for an intact archive; there is no need to regenerate the packets just to inspect the result.

1. first_pair.py and historic_replay.py produce the microscopic sources and an independent replay of the previously archived field from its prior endpoint. driven_pair.py freezes the transported field and stress before score_total.py reads the temporal references.
2. memory_history.py then memory_load.py reconstruct the physical trailing mean with its prehistory, retained integration constant and the lapse across the earlier missing memory-output segment. They do not fill absent stored memory entries as measured data.
3. causal_prep.py then score_causal.py test the causal fixed-metric initial-state extrapolation. metric_orbit.py then score_orbit.py separate the incoming phase from its conditional metric. Quadratic and cubic reconstructions are distinct; do not choose order by minimizing the pressure residual.
4. load_control.py takes three separate arguments in sequence: gravity, photon, score. It uses sealed original sources, **not** the candidate angular mean. The gravity output retains one receipt per interval. Photon transfers are frozen before the score.
5. virial.py records the original derivative-test method and intentionally exits on its first /640 miss. Do not discard that result or loosen the gate. virial_report.py evaluates all rows, including those misses, and separately reports independent derivative-order controls.
6. verify_stage.py --seal seals a successfully checked local stage; verify_stage.py without arguments checks the manifest and scientific gates without rewriting it. To reproduce computations use an independent matching workspace with equivalent parent seals rather than overwriting signed files. Mathematical results and signed summaries are described in RESULTS.md; raw numerical work arrays are local replay material, not a list of files to publish.
