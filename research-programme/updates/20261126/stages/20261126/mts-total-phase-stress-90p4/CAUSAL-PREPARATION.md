# A causal preparation test for the pressure cancellation
This diagnostic was designed after observing that the switch-only adiabatic phase prediction reproduces mean energy well but not the smaller pressure drift. It does not erase that failed prediction. Use the existing 89.65 Mpc saved boundary. Freeze ordinary gravity and all physical-orientation histories from the preceding stage. Do not initialize the phase from future mean pressure or current.

Let an early real field be A(theta) cos(theta-theta_a)+B(theta) sin(theta-theta_a). At lookback u_j=pi/2+j*pi the cosine vanishes and B(theta_a-u_j)=(-1)^(j+1) times the real field at that phase. Interpolate the *signed envelope*, not the oscillation, to the anchor using the unique Lagrange weights at 0. There are four past quarter-phase samples since 89.275. Constant, linear, quadratic and cubic continuations are explicitly different predictions. The coefficients are fixed by their phase coordinates, never optimized for pressure. Apply the same real-history functional to b,v,f,w on every leg and to the driven F,W on each saved metric. No extra population or adjusted frequency. The controls are powers 0,1,2; the highest supported power 3 is the primary, including if a lower degree looks better. Use the heavy preparation's phase integral. Exactly transport each frozen phase partner on its corresponding linear equations. Do not reset any actual field or curvature at the anchor.

Only forecast the supported centers reached *after* 89.65. The temporal reference remains the once-integrated real orientation, on the original full two-sided window and unchanged 0.5% thresholds. This asks whether a prior chunk of the retained wave identifies the appropriate orthogonal state, not whether it could have been selected without any such chunk at the earlier cutoff. Departures from a single slow envelope or high harmonics in the metric can defeat this prescription.
