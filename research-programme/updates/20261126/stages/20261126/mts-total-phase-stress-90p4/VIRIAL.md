# Pressure without subtracting the two large field stresses
Use the saved **physical orientation** as a diagnostic, not as a fitted phase model. The background obeys b'=v and v'=-2Hv-omega² b. For the driven field let F'=W+A and W'=-2HW-(K+omega²)F+B, with the same A,B and comoving K=k² as TOTAL.md. Differentiate the product V=(vF+bW)/2. The omega² terms combine, leaving

    P = V' + 2 H V + K bF/2 - (vA+bB)/2 + 3v² Phi + Qp.

The integrated version predicts V(t)-V(t0) given P and the nondifferentiated terms; the differential version uses only saved V, field, metric and sources, then compares with P. This is a product-rule identity, not a new equation of state. Independently differentiate the dense V samples by a fifth-order interpolating spline, check the integrated identity, and decompose the *same* temporal mean pressure change over the six supported centers. Score all terms on the same physical pressure-change scale; shares are complex contributions and absolute maxima need not sum to 100%. Compare the independent time samplings and the two gravity grids. No new source enters the photon calculation. No fitting to pressure.
