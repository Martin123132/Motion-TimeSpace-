# The causal constraint load, not just an instantaneous phase mean

The historical compensated gravity route uses a trailing phase average over pi of the normalized current j=J/rho, with rho=rho0/a. A common-phase ensemble average at one time does not generally equal this one-sided history. Take the native saved monotone phase coordinate for this check; do not adjust its speed. The old compensator satisfies delta=m-j, p_c=(delta'+H delta)/K, e_c'=-delta-3H p_c, K=k_out². Its share of the source for the energy constraint is L_c=(rho/2)(e_c+3H delta/K). Hence

    Y=2 L_c/rho = e_c + 3H delta/K,
    Y' = [-1 + 3(H'-H²)/K] delta.

There is no derivative of the rapidly oscillating current on the right. This is a consequence of the *existing* energy/current exchange equations and the metric constraint weights, not a fitted damping term or a new material component. Keep the inherited constant Y on entry. Test predicted Y changes against both routes' independently stored e_c and m histories, not merely Y at the final time. In the changed-source probe match the *total* E and J at the cutoff by subtracting the change of the real input from the carried memory. The offset in m must follow its individual incoming matching. Compare changes on their own scale; an almost unchanged total is not evidence that a small change was predicted. No opposite-sign or zero terms can be labeled a physical null unless the corresponding controls have actually been evaluated.

For a local alternative to integrating an entire past cycle, let j0,C,S be the mean, cosine and sine components at time theta of the transported pair. Write C=c cos(2theta)+s sin(2theta), S=-c sin(2theta)+s cos(2theta) so c,s vary slowly. Expand these and j0 about theta in the retarded integral from theta-pi to theta. At zeroth order m=j0. First order adds -pi j0'/2 - S_s'/2; second order adds pi² j0''/6+C_s''/4+pi S_s''/4. Subscript s means *only* the slowly varying c,s are differentiated, then rotated into the current basis; all primes here are native phase derivatives. The coefficients follow from integrating powers of lookback phase times 1, cos(2u), and -sin(2u). Test with past-only derivatives once an entire post-cutoff cycle exists; no fitting to the stored memory. The same formula applies to a complex spatial Fourier mode without treating its imaginary part as a temporal phase.
