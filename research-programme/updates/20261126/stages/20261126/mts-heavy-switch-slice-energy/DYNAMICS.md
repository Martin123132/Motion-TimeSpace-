# The jump law and a conserved alternative to a sudden moment overwrite

The native switch changes the meaning of two stored variables. Before the event they are the synchronous perturbation f and its conformal derivative w. Afterward they are the projected fractional density and longitudinal current d,j. It is their *stress*, not their storage slots, that gravity sees. All energies and currents below use the native rescaled Einstein-source convention. The zero of the synchronous residual gauge is the same on both sides and the incoming cold species fixes that clock.

At a=a_* set M=am, ρ=ρ₀/a, E=vw+M²bf, P=vw−M²bf, J=kvf, b′=v. The native table launches Ebar=ρd and Jbar=ρj. No other evolution variable, including etak, changes in the switch call. The published computational model supplies all of these inputs before reading post-event metric or photons. Define ΔE=Ebar−E and ΔJ=Jbar−J. In the flat solver z=(δρ/(2k)+etak)/H, σ=z+3δq/(2k²), α=σ/k, Φ=etak/k−Hα. Thus

    H₊²−H₋² = [ρ−(v²+M²b²)/2]/3
    α₊−α₋ = z₋(H₋/H₊−1)/k + ΔE/(2k²H₊) + 3ΔJ/(2k³).
    
These are two different density changes: ΔE and ΔJ are linear *mode* jumps, while the background density in the first line is a scalar. Φ₊ follows from the unchanged etak and the predicted α₊. When H is continuous, ΔΦ=−HΔα. Setting ΔE=ΔJ=0 at fixed H is the no-jump control, not how the current table was generated.

The canonical quadratic source uses F=f+vα and N=w−(3Hv+M²b)α. Expanding its densitized momentum gives qE=½(N⋆N+grad(F,F)+M²F⋆F)+3v Φ⋆N−(3/2)v²Φ⋆Φ, qJ=−dg(N,F), with the retained Fourier pair, gradient and divergence signs and phases. This follows by putting u=N−3vΦ into 3v Φ⋆u+(15/2)v²Φ⋆Φ. Evaluate this polynomial on both clocks using the very same incoming f,w. This predicts a *source-slot* jump without a microscopic impulse. On the original synchronous normal the conditional bare kinetic/gradient/mass quadratic pieces are unchanged at fixed b,v,f,w,a. These bare bilinears are not the entire second-order gauge transformation of the stress tensor; the genuinely second-order state also transforms.

**Is this merely a choice of time coordinate?** For a sharp GR matching without a surface layer the intrinsic metric and extrinsic curvature of the same hypersurface agree. See N. Deruelle and V. Mukhanov, [On matching conditions for cosmological perturbations, §III, equations 11–12](https://arxiv.org/html/gr-qc/9503050#S3). In synchronous conventions h′=2kz (also δ_c′=−kz), η_MB=etak/k and η_MB′=δq/(2k). With K_ij=−½ L_n γ_ij the scalar trace jump is −k[z]/a and the coefficient of the trace-free longitudinal tensor is −k[σ]/a on the fixed-time slice. A common first-order tilt of the surface adds the same trace-free Hessian on both sides; separate displacements T± must obey [H T]=0 from intrinsic scalar curvature continuity when etak is continuous. For the continuous nonzero H case this forces [T]=0. Equivalently the no-layer conditions imply continuity of the gauge-invariant longitudinal spatial potential Φ for these nonzero modes. A measured nonzero ΔΦ cannot be removed merely by relabeling synchronous time. A switch that approximates an average is not necessarily a literal physical transition: a failure here rules out the *sharp, no-layer interpretation with other states held fixed*, not a smooth effective theory.

**A repair which can be tested, without forcing the photons.** Retain the projected mean, but track the omitted coherent residual R=(E−Ebar,J−Jbar) with its prepared initial value. On a common metric let R_P=P−Pbar, Pbar=c_s²(Ebar+3H Jbar/k). Differentiating the Klein–Gordon moments and subtracting the fluid's own two equations gives

    R_E′ = −H(R_E+3R_P) − k R_J − (v²−ρ) k z
    R_J′ = −2H R_J + k R_P.

There is no inverse velocity or free surface coefficient. Independently evolve f,w to supply P, not a pressure chosen from R_E′ or from photons. The homogeneous part also has a residual: with exact scalar background density B=(v²+M²b²)/2, B−ρ starts at zero but (B−ρ)′=−H[(B−ρ)+3P_background]. Include it for the conservative metric test. Keeping *all* these exact residuals recovers the wave, as an identity on a given metric. This by itself is not a cheaper autonomous averaged theory and does not predict a new observational advantage. The first predictive test here is which jump, short-time shear and quadratic energy it yields from the single incoming state, compared with the actual moment-only native switch and resolved control. Large-time elimination or derivation of a decaying residual remains a different problem.
