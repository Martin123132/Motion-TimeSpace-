# A constructive alternative: conservation determines a compensating current

Instead of declaring the smooth switch impossible in general, relax precisely one restriction of FADE.md. On the chosen synchronous clock prescribe the coarse energy interpolation, keep its homogeneous conservation pressure, but let J=Jbar+W R_J+I and P=Pbar+W R_P+π. Do not add anisotropic stress. Evaluate the underlying scalar and fluid on the same trial geometry. Energy and momentum conservation require, without a choice of sound speed or new coupling,

    3Hπ = −W′R_E − k I − p_c k z,         p_c = −W′ΔB/(3H)
    I′+2H I = kπ − W′R_J
    I′+(2H+k²/(3H)) I = −k W′D/(3H).

Here I is additional to the *interpolated* longitudinal current, not the entire omitted wave current; it is zero on entry if there was no extra current beforehand. For a specified smooth W and common history, this equation has the unique retarded solution given by integrating its last RHS against exp(−∫[2H+k²/(3H)] dη). Its homogeneous kernel is damped for H>0. The k-dependent decay follows from prescribing the energy and eliminating the pressure, not a microscopic diffusion law. If this component gravitates, z and H must also be solved self-consistently. No observational prediction follows from choosing W as a convenient interpolation.

There is a testable coefficient independent of the switching speed: for an infinitesimal monotone change from the incoming weight, I/δW → −kD*/(3H*). In the continuous-background limit this equals (2/3) k³ [α], where the jump is the diagnosed OLD sharp-switch output, not an actual measurement of I. The relation holds also for a smooth start with W′ initially zero by continuity of the retarded kernel; it does not require dividing by W′ at the initial instant. The switch readout therefore fixes both sign and size of the current *required per unit weight change*, conditionally on this ansatz. On a decreasing fade the sign of I is opposite that coefficient. This gives a target for deriving the actual coarse-graining current from MTS rather than positing a new galaxy or cosmology parameter. It does not prove such a current exists in the original action.

Verification below substitutes exact analytic field, fluid and metric rates into all three conservation equations, for both signs of W′ and expanding metrics, including nonzero ΔB and z. The saved-coefficient comparison uses no fitted coefficient or photon readout. A kernel or microscopic stress mismatch at the next stage should reject the interpolation, not be tuned away.
