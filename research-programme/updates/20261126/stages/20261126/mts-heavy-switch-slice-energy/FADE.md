# Does a gradual fade provide a conservative physical switch?

Take the most economical proposed repair seriously. In synchronous gauge, on ONE common metric, let both the coherent residue and its homogeneous background be multiplied by a single differentiable weight W(η). W has no independent perturbation on this slice. The two endpoints are the retained scalar and the retained dust-like representation. Allow the transition any extra homogeneous pressure and any extra isotropic perturbed pressure. Do not introduce a new current, anisotropic stress, energy exchange with surrounding species or independent transition field. W is not chosen from the photons. No assumption on its width or shape is needed for the test below.

Write the native conformal, rescaled equations as B′=−H(B+3p), E′=−H(E+3P)−kJ−(B+p)kz, J′=−2HJ+kP. Dust has B_d=ρ, p_d=0 (its perturbative Pbar need not vanish), and the scalar has B_s=(v²+M²b²)/2, B_s+p_s=v². Let ΔB=B_s−ρ, R_E=E_s−Ebar, R_J=J_s−Jbar. Mixing as X_W=X_d+W(X_s−X_d), background conservation forces an additional homogeneous pressure p_c=−W′ΔB/(3H). Momentum conservation then forces the additional isotropic perturbed pressure P_c=W′R_J/k. Substituting BOTH forced pressures into the energy equation leaves the exact residual

    W′ D(k,η),   D = R_E + 3H R_J/k − ΔB k z/(3H).

The Hz coupling acts on total enthalpy, which is why discarding the last term away from the switch would be wrong. For any actual common metric the derivation compares the wave and fluid *on that same metric*, not two archived evolutions subtracted on different geometries. At the switch ΔB=0 up to background preparation accuracy. In the ideal continuous-H limit D* = −2 k² H [α]. Both sides use independently specified inputs: the left is a linear combination of prepared energy and current; the right comes from the fresh native shear jump. Relative corrections from finite ΔB and H are tested, not set to zero in the numerical D.

Consequences: for k,H nonzero, wherever D≠0 an evolving W and the no-extra-channel ansatz are incompatible. Setting W′=0 exactly at the join can suppress one instant, not allow an immediately varying fade on an open interval with nonzero D. A nonzero D on one populated mode is enough to obstruct a universal W there. This does not exclude later switching or a physical transition with its own clock perturbation, coherent current, exchange or anisotropic stress. No duration or transport coefficient is inferred. In the convention J′=−2HJ+k(P−S), holding the mixed E,J fixed and using the energy-required P yields a required divergence stress S_c=−W′D/(3H). This is a *requirement*, not a scalar-field prediction of anisotropic stress.
