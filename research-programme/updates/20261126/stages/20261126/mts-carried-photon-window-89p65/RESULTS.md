# Beyond 89.6: photon consistency survives the first new source interval

The compensated momentum and density gravity routes pass on both saved gravity grids in **89.6–89.65 conformal Mpc**, with no state reset. The cumulative joint gap rises from 0.1755% to about 0.2128%; the extension is not an improvement that was selected after seeing the scores. This short window was chosen in advance from the common end of the matched native forcing and the frozen spatial-U prediction. The independently available canonical wave padding alone goes farther, to 90.4, but cannot stand in for the matched inputs.

Each value is a difference between two signed, visibility-weighted predictions divided by the **original small reference correction** in that channel, not by a newly chosen scale. The limit is 0.5%. Increment and cumulative are norms of separately propagated signed vectors; their magnitudes do not add. On p32 the two cumulative correction sizes are 0.017065% and 0.017063% of the full joint launch (separate cap 0.163%). Without compensating stress the *new interval alone* gives a 5.3401% joint gap, and the cumulative gap is 514.33%, on the original small-correction scale.

**What actually continued.** For the matched perturbations, both routes carry their ordinary fields, curvature, all 77 × 69 angular moments, spectral shape and compensating stress from the prior final-partial-cycle packet. The homogeneous heavy wave continues from its own field and velocity. Each route obtains delayed current from its own preceding phase histories. The resolved comparison likewise uses its own two saved endings. The pre-existing shear prescription is evaluated on the saved wave histories at the required new phases; the other forcing uses the retained physical tables, including spatial-U, within their support. Both massless and massive incident angular populations are advanced from their endpoint states and reprojected to obtain their new sources; the largest source change upon doubling resolution is 0.000000114% on the source-refinement scale. Across eight physical continuations, the largest 14-channel entry replay error was below 1e-13%.

**Photon controls.** Replaying the prior gravity driving in the full scattering hierarchy reconstructs the incoming photon multipoles. They pass across 89.6 *unchanged*, including the higher moments; no new impulse is applied there. Old-only, new-only and combined driving propagate on the same mesh. The largest residual in their linear sum is 6.1e-12% of the new signal. All signed predictions and boundary multipoles are stored, and prediction-freeze.json was written before scoring. The 144 photon replay, temporal/angular refinement, source subdivision and gravity-grid comparisons pass on the fixed original scale (largest 0.00315%). The largest compensated route gap across these settings is 0.21280%. This tests consistency of the model correction on prepared histories; it does not extend an absolute cold-cosmology sky prediction or compare to new sky data.
