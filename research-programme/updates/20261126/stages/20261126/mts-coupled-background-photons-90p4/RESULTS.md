# A corrected second-order background reduces the photon discrepancy and identifies the next branch to test

**Question.** Would the earlier force repair work better if the resolved wave, ordinary matter, gravity and all 77 momentum bins also evolved on the corresponding homogeneous background? Yes on this saved 89.65–90.4 conformal-Mpc window. It removes most of the resolved constraint drift and improves the photons, but the next useful change is on the independently prepared matched branch. This is an internal consistency comparison, not a sky-data likelihood.

|---|---:|---:|
| Fine first-order forcing, archived second-order background | 0.655133 | 0.655144 |

The last two rows are projections of *saved* vectors with one contribution removed, **not new solutions**. The unchanged target is 0.5%. On p32, photon energy and current separately score 0.607116% and 0.606609%. The two routes are 0.018391% and 0.018376% on the separate full-launch scale (limit 0.163%). With the alternative complex entrance impulse the joint score is 0.607071%. Across both grids, both background tables and all three transfer settings the joint result ranges from 0.60700% to 0.60723%.

**What moved.** Ordinary thermodynamic densities and the conformal clock were read from the independently evolved native background. The retained heavy field and its velocity were evaluated on that clock, the Friedmann rate and pressure rebuilt, and the same scalar/metric quadratic forces then drove the full matter and gravity state, with different density and momentum routes. No incoming perturbation or curvature was reset. Phase, angular grids, incident radiation, remaining quadratic sources, external visibility medium, matched history and launch normalization were not changed; this is not a new radiation bath or CMB fit. Gravity and all photon controls use the unaltered reference files. **Which branch matters now?** On p32, pre-window photons carry 0.21277 points. The interval supplies 0.43773 points, at 45.06° to them, so their joint norm is 0.60700 rather than the scalar sum. Inside that interval the matched density-minus-momentum photon vector has norm 0.43556 points, versus 0.02307 for the corrected resolved wave. On the final direction, the matched potential-rate contribution is +0.40890 points, the matched lapse +0.00791, the two resolved terms together +0.00723, and the inherited contribution +0.18296. Independent branch, channel and time sums agree to better than 1e-7 points. Even the lower bound for deleting just the resolved contribution exceeds 0.5839%; refining that wave towards exact consistency cannot, by itself, meet 0.5% with these other inputs fixed. The no-memory matched control is not the independent cold cosmology.

**A concrete matched-branch mechanism.** Inspection and replay show the matched solver already integrates its own oscillator accurately. The issue is not a bad velocity interpolant there. Its homogeneous expansion is the averaged fluid’s; its wave contribution to the density constraint carries instantaneous enthalpy v² instead of the fluid’s rho. Direct differentiation gives T_host−T_fluid = 3(v²−rho)/2, with T=D−k²+3(H′−H²). The peak enthalpy mismatch is 99.99% of the heavy fluid’s enthalpy, *not* of the total cosmological density. T_fluid itself is below 7e-9% of the peak difference. This identifies a coefficient mismatch, not proof that it accounts for the entire matched photon vector.

Holding that same averaged geometry and all other matter fixed determines a background stress difference without fitting anything: E_c=rho−(v²+μ²b²)/2 and P_c=−(v²−μ²b²)/2. Thus E_c+P_c=rho−v². Integration of its energy equation from its own start reproduced the saved change to within 5.0e-9% on both grids. It is a difference between two representations of the heavy component, not evidence for an extra species. [Derivation and hypotheses](MATCHED-BACKGROUND.md) · [Independent derivative and integral checks](evidence-001.zip) (archive member: `matched-background-checks.json`).

**Next calculation.** Vary that stress difference on the same perturbed metric. Derive the work it owes the metric from its background enthalpy, separate the quadratic incident terms, and subtract only what the existing memory current and pressure already supply. Test the resulting density, momentum and potential rate on the independently prepared matched trajectories before repeating the photon projection. Do not simply remove the term from the denominator, introduce a fitted sound speed, or count the homogeneous identity as perturbative closure.

The calculation order is background, carry, visibility test, state budget and dense checks, window budget, orbit test, branch limits, matched background.
