# The matched stress balances; the remaining photon error is reproducible from the incident sources

**Result:** enforcing the live metric work of the heavy background stress difference did **not** fix the photon comparison. On the finer gravity grid it changed the joint gap from **0.607003% to 0.634325%**, away from the fixed 0.5% target. But the change also makes the source of the remaining discrepancy measurable: using the supplied forcing, incoming conditions and homogeneous coefficients, without the later matched state, a reduced calculation predicts **0.634283%**. This is a consistency test of one mode on a saved interval, not a cosmological likelihood.

**What changed.** With the existing phase-average current held to its own governing equation, the common-metric conservation laws determine the missing pressure and a normal-density evolution. They are not independent adjustable constants. The physical extra density starts at zero on each route, preserving its own incoming physical energy, while its normal-coordinate value is in general nonzero. The density numerator and its denominator change together, and the already-installed mean current and its pressure enter once. Both metric routes, 77 bins, angular moments and the delayed mean evolve live. The disabled replay and both null-memory trajectories are bit identical to their saved controls; the incoming curvature is not reset. This is conditional on the retained incident stresses and the fixed incoming physical stress at 89.65, not a derivation of the complete second-order source or primordial preparation. **Energy, momentum and projection.** Independently integrating both balances reproduced the compensated stress changes to within 3.3e-8% on either grid; the added physical density separately agreed within 2.8e-6%. Omitting the live metric work at the comparison stage gives at most about 0.0191% energy and 0.00757% current discrepancy on these same scales: detectable, but below the 0.5% gate, not an independent rejection at that tolerance. The frozen old-orbit work predicted the *change* of the momentum-route constraint drift to better than 3.1e-7% of that change; the actual drift grew by about 3.20%. On p32 the photon energy/current discrepancies are 0.634438% / 0.633933%; the separate full-launch sizes are 0.018391% / 0.018376%, within 0.163%. The optional entrance impulse gives 0.634398% joint. Across both background tables, gravity grids and three transfer settings the joint gap stays between 0.63432% and 0.63465%. No amplitudes were fitted. **Independent source test.** On the zero-state slice of the affine matched equations, the delayed mean rate cancels exactly between the energy row and the momentum row. We contracted the native operator and the actual bin sources in two independent ways. On the corrected branch the resulting source-only integral predicts the density-route constraint drift to 0.00391% on p16 and 0.00177% on p32; changing quadrature moved it by 0.02674% of the drift, so these point discrepancies are not a precision guarantee. The same test on the disabled branch misses by 24.94%. Holding only the route-current and slip differences to their incoming values, then evolving the stored-curvature difference and propagating both photon channels, gives the reduced predictions above. On p32, changing the integration grid moves the full projected vector by 0.000234% of its actual size. The disabled version misses the full photon vector by 9.14%. The resolved comparison and thermal transfer are supplied controls. This prediction was a follow-up to an already known discrepancy; it excludes later matched states as inputs, not the researcher's knowledge of the outcome.
