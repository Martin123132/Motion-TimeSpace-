# Independent momentum-orbit prediction and surviving sources

Use C=D(kappa−Phi_density) on the momentum orbit; equivalently C=K Phi+E_tot/2+3HJ_tot/(2K)−U, using *physical* conformally weighted perturbation energy. It need not vanish initially. Define S_E and S_J by moving all remaining energy and momentum forcing, including anisotropic and incident terms, to the right of the two physical conservation equations. Differentiation and Phi′=J_tot/(2K)−H Psi+Fq give

    C′+HC = [D−K+3(H′−H²)] J_tot/(2K) −HK(Psi−Phi)
             +D Fq + S_E/2 +3H S_J/(2K) − U′−HU.

In particular changing the chosen background enthalpy changes the first coefficient. On the old matched trajectory the work predicted from the metric stress is Y/2, where Y is independently integrated on that unchanged orbit; keeping the state fixed would give C_pred=C_old+Y/2. This includes the changed algebraic denominator once: the change 3W Phi/2 in D Phi and the normal-density change X/2 sum to Y/2. It does not use any new endpoint, does not erase C_old(launch), and is not the assumption that the other induced fields do not respond in the full run. Freeze this passive forecast, then quantify how much the fully coupled result differs from it relative to the *change*, not just the total level.

The direct term has a second representation: (Y′+HY)/2=(3W/2)[J_tot/(2K)+Fq]−(H−alpha)(E_m+3HJ_m/K)/2. Test it using the archived total current against the independently stored potential rate and lapse; no new free current. If direct metric work explains a large part of the remaining constraint drift, that is a physical localization on the saved branch, not proof all the remaining source terms satisfy the nonlinear Ward identity.
