# Metric work owed by the phase-averaged heavy stress

Use K=k² and signed current J in the native convention (an overdensity has E′ containing −J). All stresses include the native conformal a² factor. H is conformal Hubble rate, alpha=a′/a independently obtained from the clock. Phi is the spatial potential used by that orbit, Psi the lapse. For a conserved stress difference of background enthalpy W on the same metric, its perturbation satisfies, at fixed independently supplied quadratic sources and unchanged anisotropic-stress forcing,

    E′ + H(E+3P) + J = 3 W Phi′,
    J′ + 2H J − K P = K W Psi.

These are the time- and longitudinal space-components of the variation of the common-metric divergence. In conformal Newtonian gauge the spatial volume perturbation is −3 Phi and the lapse perturbation is Psi; these provide the two metric sources, with the same background enthalpy. Equivalently, apply the two matter equations separately to the averaged and wave backgrounds and subtract; density plus pressure multiplies both metric terms. At the retained second perturbation order these are the terms linear in the live metric. Products of two incident first-order perturbations are inhomogeneous sources and are not deduced from W alone. No separate shear is generated by a homogeneous isotropic stress under a scalar lapse or volume perturbation alone.

For the saved matched branch W=rho−v², rho=rho0/a. Write d=m−j_wave, J=rho d, P_m=rho(d′+Hd)/K and E_m=rho e, e′=−d−3H P_m/rho. These are precisely the pre-existing memory update, including the delayed current. The oscillator uses its own Klein–Gordon acceleration, not the derivative of an unrelated table. Consequently the memory *already* supplies the zero-enthalpy current work and pressure. Its momentum residual before adding the live metric work is (H−alpha)J, and its energy residual is (H−alpha)E_m. Do not add that current or its derivative again.

Holding J and the inherited anisotropic and incident forces fixed uniquely fixes the missing pressure for this test:

    Delta P = −W Psi + (H−alpha)J/K;    P=P_m+Delta P.

To keep the ordinary solver in its native variables introduce X=E−E_m−3W Phi. Substitution into the two equations gives

    X′ + H X = 3H W Psi −3(W′+HW)Phi −(H−alpha)(E_m+3H J/K).

Here W′=−alpha rho−2v v′ and v′=−2Hv−(am)² b. The reconstructed density numerator gains X exactly once; its denominator D gains 3W/2 exactly once from the 3W Phi term. In particular on the two memory-active orbits the new D equals the independently available fluid denominator. No term is subtracted from the momentum rate, shear, current, or stored curvature. X is zero on the unchanged null orbits. Algebraic density reconstruction on the momentum orbit and curvature evolution on the density orbit remain independent diagnostics, not constraints to reset.

An independent physical-variable check is Y=E−E_m=X+3W Phi. It evolves with source 3W(Phi′+H Psi)−(H−alpha)(E_m+3H J/K), with homogeneous decay −HY. Use the momentum rate for the momentum orbit only, differentiate the actual density-route potential for the other, and integrate from Y0=0. Separately integrate the full current equation from the actual incoming J with pressure recovered from the phase history. An improvement of the photon diagnostic measures the impact of this metric intervention, not a fitted closure for all quadratic and phase-dependent stresses.
