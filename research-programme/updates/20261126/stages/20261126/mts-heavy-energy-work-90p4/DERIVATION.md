# Work performed on the explicit heavy scalar

Use conformal time, κ=k², M²=(am_h)², B=M²b, background field/velocity b,v, and perturbation field/conjugate momentum f,w. H is the conformal expansion rate; Φ is the density-route curvature and Ψ is the lapse including slip and the live bin shear. w is not the coordinate-time derivative f′. The retained wave operator gives

    f′ = w + v(Ψ+3Φ) + q_f
    w′ = −2Hw − (κ+M²)f + B(3Φ−Ψ) + q_w.
    E_h = v w + B f + q_E.

Here q_f, q_w, q_E are the common native heavy field, conjugate-momentum and energy sources. They are not arbitrary functions inferred from a measured energy difference. Define background remainders r_b=b′−v, r_v=v′+2Hv+B. For the integrated matched oscillator they vanish analytically; for the tabulated reference differentiate the retained quintic interpolants in log time, including the conformal-time chain rule. Direct application of the product rule gives

    E_h′ = −4Hv w + (M²)′ b f − κv f + 6BvΦ
           + B q_f + v q_w + q_E′ + r_v w + M² r_b f.

Both the B w and the M²v f pairs cancel. More tellingly, the complete direct work of the metric is vB(3Φ−Ψ) + Bv(Ψ+3Φ) = 6BvΦ. This is exact for this retained energy and pair of equations; it does not assert that Ψ never affects the trajectory. Both potentials drive the independent evolution, and the measurement weights are unchanged.

**Separate incoming state from later forcing.** With y=(f,w), g=(Φ,Ψ), write y′=A y+C g+q. The rows of C are (3v,v) and (3B,−B); A has rows (0,1) and (−κ−M²,−2H). For the two branches use Δ for matched minus resolved and a bar for the arithmetic mean:

    Δy′ = Ā Δy + ΔA ȳ + C̄ Δg + ΔC ḡ + Δq.

Evolve the two original trajectories alongside six two-component responses (one nonzero incoming difference, five zero-initialized forcing differences). Separate ΔA into the H and M² pieces. Δq=0 because the same external table enters both retained evolution laws. Sum the six responses and independently compare their total with the original field difference. The incoming contribution starts at 89.65, not at primordial preparation; no claim about its earlier origin follows from this test.

**Two views of one signal.** The first table of signed terms is obtained by differencing the energy-work identity directly. For the second, let c_f=(M²)′b−κv+M²r_b and c_w=−4Hv+r_v. Apply Δ(c·y)=c̄·Δy+Δc·ȳ, using the six responses in the first term. Similarly split 6Δ(BvΦ), Δ(Bq_f), and Δ(vq_w). Both decompositions give the same ΔE_h′; they answer different questions and their rows must not be added across views. The photon source is −(1/D_m+1/D_w)ΔE_h′/4, with the same signed energy/current scattering transfer as previously. This is just the energy-rate contribution, not the whole constraint discrepancy.
