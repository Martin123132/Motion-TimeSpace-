# Two coefficients that identify what the gravity constraint is missing

Write F,W for the driven second-order canonical heavy field and velocity. Use K=k², μ=am, D the density denominator and R=eκᵀ-g as in ABSOLUTE.md. The field coefficients in R are c_F=(μ²b+3Hv)/(2D) and c_W=v/(2D). Since the stored-curvature equation sees the field current KvF, and the free field columns obey F'=W, W'=-(K+μ²)F-2HW, the exact transverse coefficients are

    B_F = c_F' -(K+μ²)c_W +v/2 -λ c_F,
    B_W = c_W' + c_F -(2H+λ)c_W.

Set A=H+λ+D'/D, T=D-K+3(H'-H²), R_b=b'-v, R_v=v'+2Hv+μ²b, and L=(am)'/(am)-H (the retained m is constant). Expanding gives

    2D B_W = R_v-A v,
    2D B_F = μ²R_b +3H R_v +2L μ²b +T v -A(μ²b+3Hv).

These identities do not assume conservation or tune a coefficient. The transverse scalar contribution vanishes if the canonical field equations, common scale-factor clock, homogeneous enthalpy identity T=0 and A=0 hold.
