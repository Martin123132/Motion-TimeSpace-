# How a discrepancy in gravity reaches the saved sources

For a synchronous native wave f,w on the background b,v let alpha=sigma/k, mu=am, Phi=eta_k/k-H alpha, F=f+v alpha and N=w+(-3Hv-mu²b)alpha. Use the saved spatial contractions at each wave-vector pair, not a product of separately averaged fields. Keep the five original output wavevectors fixed; the first is the pre-existing pressure experiment, the others are ancillary unselected controls. The original archive contains all five predictions on alternating time samples over this entire window. Test both the level and its change relative to launch. Do not overwrite the archive.

Independent gravity gives alpha_I from (a²alpha_I)'=a²(eta_k/k-Pi/k²) with the same launch. Hold native perturbations and the clock fixed to isolate the readout contribution, not to manufacture an alternative solution. For d=alpha_I-alpha, the changes in (Phi,F,N) are exactly (-Hd, vd, (-3Hv-mu²b)d). At fixed time Q is a homogeneous quadratic form in (Phi,F,N); in particular U=N-3vPhi is linear. Thus Q(x+d)-Q(x)=B(x,d)+B(d,x)+Q(d), with B its ordered bilinear form. Independently evaluate the directional derivative B(x,d)+B(d,x) using opposite signs of d or an analytic expansion and check the identity against direct projection. This separates the effect of the shear disagreement from actually changing the source solution. Q_h is absent in the cold control. Compare native heavy density and current against their direct canonical stress reconstruction and compare the two wave table resolutions; a change from the original is not necessarily an error.

There is a simple radiation control even in the cold model: delta_gamma^N=clxg-4Halpha and j_gamma^N=qg+4kalpha/3 in units of the background photon energy. Switching the gravity readout changes these by -4Hd and +4kd/3; delta_gamma^N-4Phi=clxg-4eta_k/k is unchanged. Report errors on the change over the window for both quantities on all modes, and the absolute scale for any nearly stationary mode. This is a linear collision-coupled photon test in the native solution, distinct from the later second-order 77-bin observed-window comparison.
