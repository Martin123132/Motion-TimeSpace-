# A null-cone test with the retained scalar state, rather than an imposed logarithmic profile

**Yes, the retained cosmological state produces focusing without a new motion amplitude. No, that focusing is not distinctive evidence for MTS.** On the primary Lambda-zero branch the scalar directly shortens the angular diameter distance at z=1 by 0.3655% in a fixed-expansion diagnostic. Its full distance is 1.5979% shorter than that of flat LCDM with the same present ordinary matter, radiation and H0. Ordinary GR with the *same* canonical scalar and state gives the same distance: the largest relative disagreement with the saved metric-distance calculation anywhere in the tested fixtures was 4.5e-15 or less, well within the existing 1e-6 tolerance. These are predicted differences between models or controlled calculations, not measurements.

## Source and equations

Use the retained flat scalar cosmology handoff (not bundled here) and only the saved Stage48 boundary values for the forecast. These states were calibrated using previous expansion data; the exercise does not independently determine mass or population. Method: take the null Raychaudhuri equation in [Chandrasekaran, section 3, eqs. 3.24–3.26](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#S3), but retain the canonical matter and radiation stress in the null Ricci contraction. In a homogeneous flat background an initially round, twist-free point beam has zero optical shear by isotropy. This gives a clean control without choosing a shear amplitude; it does **not** measure the shear in a real line of sight. No quantum measure or state from the preprint is imported.

Write w=1+z, V=dot(psi)/(sqrt(6) M_R H0), Y=m psi/(sqrt(6) M_R H0), mu=m/H0, and tau the past affine parameter in H0 units (c=1, photon energy today normalized to one). The radius is d=H0 D_A/c. Friedmann and Klein–Gordon give

    E² = Omega_m0 w³ + Omega_r0 w⁴ + Omega_Lambda + V² + Y²
    dV/dz=(3V+mu Y/E)/w,    dY/dz=−mu V/(w E)
    d tau/dz=1/(w² E),       (d psi/d tau)/M_R = −sqrt(6) w V
    d²d/d tau² = −[1.5 Omega_m0 w⁵ + 2 Omega_r0 w⁶ + 3 w² V²] d.

Initial d=0, derivative=1. The potential does not enter the null stress directly; it affects V and E. A cosmological constant enters E but not the square bracket. The stress integration uses a different scalar variable pair and a second-order area equation, not the reference's comoving-distance quadrature. Its equivalence to the reference follows from the common metric equations, not independent evidence that either describes the observed sky.
