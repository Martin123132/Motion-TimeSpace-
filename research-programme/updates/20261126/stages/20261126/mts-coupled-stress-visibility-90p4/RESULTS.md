# What the photons rule out, and what the heavy-wave phase predicts

**The angular feedback is real, but it cannot close the saved photon discrepancy. The wave equations do supply a useful replacement for independently guessing the averaged heavy stresses.** Everything below uses the original preparation, times, banks, normalization and 0.5% criterion. This is an internal consistency test, not a cosmological parameter fit.

## Actual scattered photons through 90.4 Mpc

Freeze the incremental quadratic stress after 89.275 Mpc, carry the earlier entry kick and the same retarded compensation and 77 bins through both gravity routes, then read photon energy and current over the original visibility window. The comparison is density-route minus momentum-route for the matched-minus-resolved correction; the ordinary cold history is untouched. The intervention is not a proposal to add the absolute coherent stress to an already averaged stress. The historical readout is the reference, not a claim about which angular approximation was used in its own first-order tables.

## A derived common-phase stress

On the existing coherent preparation, the local Klein–Gordon envelope fixes both damping and resonant forcing of the perturbation quadrature, including the term −X b/2. Project the quadratic product on the common phase of the background and wave, **not** the product of separately averaged fields. Freeze predictions for energy, stress trace, current and shear on each of the five triangles. Test them against the time history with the retained two-sided slow-intercept operator.

- On the eleven supported centers a rigid phase rotation passes **0/20** tests of mean-stress change; the derived local transport passes **20/20**. Its worst mean-level error is 0.000654%, its worst mean-change error 0.1524%. Both oscillating harmonics also pass in level and change. These are different channels and modes, not twenty independent astronomical observations.
- Differentiating the local ellipse correctly predicts the mean-current rate within 0.259%, and averaging the microscopic current rate predicts that rate's own temporal target within 0.135%. Nevertheless their difference produces a spurious term up to 0.236% of the mean rate. The temporal reference difference does not clear ten times the reference-discretization change on any triangle, so it supplies no measurable new current term.
- **Transport the two quadratures together instead.** Select the second quadrature once at the cutoff from the same derived envelope, and evolve both through the joint background/perturbation equation, including metric driving. This makes differentiation commute with the constructed phase ensemble without prescribing a compensating current. On both first-order resolutions, the transported prediction passes all 20 stress level and change tests for the mean and both harmonics, on the eleven original and ten interlaced centers. Worst mean-level error is 0.00104%; worst mean-change error 0.284%. Both projections of current rate pass on all five triangles (worst 0.327%). The difference between their rates disagrees with the temporal reference by **less than 0.0000012% of the mean rate**. The individual rates were slightly more accurate on some modes using the local ellipse; the decisive gain is their cancellation with no reset. Overlapping center windows are numerical checks, not independent holdouts.

The last result is a prediction of quadratic **first-order source contractions** on one matched cosmological background, at 89.83125–89.89375 Mpc. It is not yet the total second-order stress entering the Einstein equations. The complete heavy density also contains background velocity times second-order normal momentum, mass times background and second-order field, and the common-metric contribution. The complete current likewise includes the second-order field times the background velocity. Replacing only the six instantaneous microscopic slots with phase means would mix descriptions. Do not repeat that substitution or set the physical current difference to zero just because the ensemble differentiates exactly.

Check total work/flux and its independently computed current rate on the supported centers against both gravity routes before transferring a new stress to the photons. This tests a new physical source of the discrepancy, rather than extending the same angular calculation.
