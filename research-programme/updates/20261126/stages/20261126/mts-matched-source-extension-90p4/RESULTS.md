**Physical comparison.** The native matched solver was run at the 782 input wave numbers and the five external probes. **Precision and range.** All 48 preselected correction-value and rate comparisons pass (six quantities, two orders, four endpoints). The full-correction value / rate errors are 0.07796% / 0.07423% if normalized to the *old* peak rather than this interval's peak. **Incident populations and shear.** Both sets of 77 incident momentum bins, their angular populations and their common massless hierarchy were carried from the saved 89.65 endpoint to 90.4. The result is (a_wave/a_matched) × wave shear. This direct computation reproduces the earlier phase-based shear over their common support to 2.51e-10% on either grid; no continuation of a phase spline beyond its data is required.
