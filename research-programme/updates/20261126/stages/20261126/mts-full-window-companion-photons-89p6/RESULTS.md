# Full-window gravity histories projected to photon energy and current

Both corrected gravity routes predict the previous **prepared** photon readout to within 0.30% of its own vector, on both gravity grids. They also pass the absolute launch-size gate. But they do **not** agree with one another at the required 0.5% of the **original small photon correction**. The two facts are compatible: the prepared correction is about five times larger than that original reference. We did not change the denominator to obtain a pass.

The readout is the complex energy and longitudinal current across the retained visibility window after scattering. The gravity source covers the entire 89.1485038031–89.6 conformal Mpc window only. The comparison is to an existing numerical history, not sky data. No refit or new evolution of the matter or gravity was needed.

All errors are norms of **signed complex vector differences**, not differences of quoted amplitudes. Scoring uses the existing visibility measure. The joint column is the quadrature combination of energy and current. In all 12 corrected combinations of gravity, source subdivision and photon setting, the joint discrepancy was 1.56733–1.56759% of the original small correction. On the fine grid the earlier prepared calculation gave 0.08901% on that same denominator. The newer, simultaneous matter/gravity evolution does not preserve that earlier level of agreement.

**Why the switch is the next target.** With the complementary stress disabled, the new fine-grid joint route gap is 515.08% of the small correction. Switching it on reduces this to 1.56733%, so it genuinely improves the agreement. In the corrected result, the route-dependent entry impulse alone contributes 1.33690% and the continuous post-entry forcing alone 0.26188% on the same scale. These are vector norms, not additive shares. Both internal source corners have exactly zero stored jump in actual curvature. This points specifically to the matched and resolved entry curvatures, not to further photon time-step refinement. No saved reference amplitude was added to repair the new correction.
