# Late-time angular momentum: change the clock, then change the field

Start at tick 768 of the corrected, signed, q=1024 wave and matched cold preparations on the same 640 cells/Mpc mesh. Retain the 1,920 cells, shared/signed arithmetic, matter bank, stress forecast, proper-distance apertures and original 20 ppm tolerance. In the stored time unit one native step is 4,096π/(1,024μ). Compare the last quarter at that step and half of it on both host signs. Independently restart the **same native tick 960** at half and third steps on the positive host. At frequency 2μ the first two nominal step phases are 8π and 4π; a third-step interval is 8π/3. Agreement of the first two alone cannot exclude a sampling coincidence. This is a diagnostic, not a claim of a physical 2μ driving term. The split field evolution treats part of the fast generator analytically.

## Inject a regular gravitational perturbation only

For a separate pair of tests on each positive-host matter branch set s=r², w=0.02 Mpc, t(r)=A(r/w)⁴ exp(−s/w²), A=±10⁻²⁰ in the saved extrinsic-curvature units. This is an angular trace-free part of K, not new density or an occupation change. In the evolved chart δstate[7,1]=−2t changes only the physical angular curvature amplitude by +t; all metric components and matter variables, on both shared and signed states, must be unchanged at injection. The Cartesian radial coefficient map of (0,0,0,t) is (2t,−2t/s,t/s,t/s²); all are smooth functions of s. No singular centre, added surface layer or projection onto the constraints. This deliberately supplies a *nonzero* known constraint error. It is not a proposed physical MTS source and does not count towards the 20 ppm comparisons.

In the flat, vacuum principal part, with the angular covector coefficient multiplying ∂ₐY₂, the initial perturbations are Pᵣ=0, Pₐ=−2t ∂ₐY₂, H=0. Contracting the perturbed momentum constraint gives ∂ₜH=−2 div P=−24t Y₂/r² because ΔΩY₂=−6Y₂. The conjugate identity ∂ₜPₐ=−∂ₐH/2 implies ∂ₜ²H=ΔH. Thus the longitudinal part propagates while the transverse part is not artificially projected away. The continuum scalar amplitude has initial derivative proportional to r²exp(−r²/w²). With π=rPᵣ and τ=Pₐ/∂ₐY₂, its evolution gives ∂ₜπ=−rD_fH/2, ∂ₜτ=−H/2, and ∂ₜ²H=[D_b(r²D_fH)−6H]/r². ## Decision rule

The centre diagnostic includes the first cell and uses |P|/|J|, where J is the physical material current, as in the prior run. Also compare the norm of all residuals inside 0.3 Mpc and in the 0.6–2.1 Mpc shell. Track the signed residual itself: a change of sign is not convergence. A fixed-time, same-start step comparison measures time sensitivity of these runs, not a rigorous continuum error bound. A matching smooth pulse response would establish which part of the transfer is present without any tuned matter source; it would not alone identify the source of the unforced error. No altered time or pulse is allowed to replace the scored host/control. All outputs local.
