# One retained wave, two gravitational readouts: a flow-reversal experiment
**Result.** Two different initial wave preparations in the saved FIRE 590 environment give the same conclusion: conjugate the physical wave coefficients and the instantaneous density, occupation, potential and Newtonian force stay fixed; the projected radial and polar mass currents, time derivative of the potential, transverse metric shift and the current-driven piece of a light echo reverse. The computation regenerated the fields from each altered state. It did not negate the saved answers. It used an unchanged ordinary foreground and wave-off subtraction.

Three freely falling observers at s=6, 7.15 and 8.3 kpc, z=1.5 kpc; entries below are dimensionless fractional log echo rates for 2 E12 + 2 E23 - E13 using the flux construction:

| Wave preparation | Forward current contribution | Reversed current contribution |
|---|---:|---:|
| Reset | -1.19049911e-14 | +1.19049911e-14 |
| Coupled/inherited | +3.82109562e-13 | -3.82109562e-13 |

The long single link has a nonzero unchanged Newtonian term, -1.90103e-8 (reset) or -1.89606e-8 (coupled). It is the current coefficient, **not the whole emitted-to-received frequency ratio**, whose sign changes. The Newtonian part cancels in the three-link combination. A standing real wave gives a nonzero potential but no current term. Wave-off gives neither. Reversal and those two zero-current controls agreed to stored numerical precision. No physical fit or observed clock in either arm.

**Independent propagation check.** GR clock and light trajectories were integrated from the current-dependent metric, not from the endpoint sum. Both signs and both preselected test amplifications were run. All 16 comparisons (three links plus combination, both preparations, both gains) agree within 1.46e-8 relative, against a predeclared 0.005 threshold. Maximum endpoint miss 1.77e-12 kpc. This tests the coefficient and signs, not the accuracy of a finite-amplitude full-galaxy spacetime. Same source in GR gives the same result.

**A control that did not pass as originally implemented.** An arbitrary uniform phase change before numerical evaluation produced 18 above-threshold discrepancies out of the 576 total comparisons. All 18 were in that control. Worst was 3.21e-8 on the reset state's native changing-force term, with a 1e-9 scale-relative threshold. The reset three-link current rate changed by 5.33e-23 absolute in the native calculation. We did not loosen the threshold or replace the failing receipt.

**Diagnosis.** Using the same equations in a common phase convention, applied before precision was discarded, the 0.71-radian rephasing had zero resolved effect on the calculated fields and echoes; conjugation in that convention also passed. A quarter-turn requiring only a real/imaginary swap satisfied the original tolerance without that convention. When the arbitrary phase was rounded first and only then put into the common convention, a 3.48e-9 normalized residual remained. The canonical forward echo components themselves also differ from the legacy arithmetic by up to 2.94e-9 on the frozen scales. This distinguishes numerical representation of equivalent states from physical differences; it does not retroactively pass the original gate or guarantee accuracy for arbitrarily rounded input.

**What the hierarchy idea bought us.** Here one already-retained state determines two related physical sources with inequivalent responses under reversal. In terms of psi = sqrt(n) exp(i theta), the mass density reads n while the mass current reads n grad(theta), up to the fixed normalization. A rotation curve probing only density cannot choose between them on this slice. There is no extra current coefficient chosen from the clocks. This is an example of a testable parent-to-two-observables connection, not a derivation of all fields from one or a discrimination against other matter models with the same stress. The owner’s galactic state-preparation question remains relevant.
