# Reproducibility

Local Python 3.13, NumPy, SciPy and SymPy; Windows numerical process uses one thread. The saved clock, parent seals, and initial conditions referenced by polar_scope.py are required. A stock checkout without those local saved histories is not equivalent. No downloads, model training, telescope fits or GitHub operations. Coordinate components are in the retained simulation's gravitational units.

In this directory, use `python -B verify_polar_stage.py --replay-final` to verify the sealed inventory and recompute the final signed host-minus-zero proper readings from the three pairs of wave/cold raw states. Drop the replay flag for the shorter inventory and score check. No new trajectories are needed.

The original-run diagnostic consists of polar_balance.py, shell_flux.py, face_flux.py and compare_polar.py. Manufactured curved-polar checks and split-curvature algebra are independent tests: curved_polar_witness.py and qualify_split.py. The prospective freezes for the intervention are TRIAL.md, split-freeze.json, CONTROL.md, coarse-freeze.json. The revised evolutions live in this folder only; existing histories are not modified. `split_clock.py SIGN FLAG STOP` checks verified previous packets before advancing; `spatial_control.py` does the positive coarse control. FLAG=0 wave, FLAG=1 dust. Respect any owner hold.

corrected_budget.py evaluates raw independent H₂, radial and polar momentum, number and weak flux on the old and new clocks; source_partition.py evaluates the separately derived microscopic decomposition on saved states. source_budget.py integrates the real stresses. For the held-back-quarter diagnostic use predict_quarter.py freeze before predict_quarter.py score; the former uses only twenty earlier time-major rows per grid/sign. Fractions of very small net differences should not be interpreted as absolute probabilities. Full score tables include wave, cold and the additional wave-minus-cold contrast where meaningful.
