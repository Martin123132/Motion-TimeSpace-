# A polar force discrepancy traced to reference subtraction

Local 21 November 2026. Same q=1024 Milky Way-independent local gravity benchmark, common metric, heavy-wave versus cold control, signed/zero hosts, original formation histories, 4096 carrier turns, 1024 steps and unaltered two-aperture 20 ppm proper-tide prediction. This is a controlled route from the retained source to the GR/Newton limit, not a galaxy fit. Nothing was published.

**Diagnosis on the previous trajectories.** The polar geometric covector, read from its native face-based geometry, and the independent collocated physical-curvature readout differ. In the positive fine wave case at the final time, bulk RMS defect relative to actual coordinate matter current is 3.42×10⁻¹⁰ native vs 1.80×10⁻³ collocated. Replacing one by the other without explanation would hide the problem. Differentiating *after* averaging the face tilt, instead of directly differentiating the face tilt, predicts the signed difference to relative RMS 1.99×10⁻⁸; 93.9% of the Hann-windowed discrepancy power is above 80% of Nyquist. The negative case gives 2.18×10⁻⁸ and 86.6%. These are fixed-domain, finite-grid readout effects, not evidence that all high-frequency structure is unphysical. Transferring both runs onto the same 1/160-Mpc shells does not remove the positive-grid trend (volume-binned collocated defect/source: 1.82×10⁻⁵ → 2.57×10⁻⁴). Different conservative face-flux derivative orders confirm sensitivity: on the fine positive bulk, order 12 gives 2.11×10⁻⁹ versus 8.59×10⁻⁵ at order 24. Matching the evolution stencil alone does not validate a pointwise continuum measurement.

**Independent conservation check.** The full curved mixed-index stress divergence and a nonzero driven geometric/material constraint agree with the production polar transport routine to at worst 1.77×10⁻¹⁴ relative RMS on the fine manufactured fixture. On the saved previous actual histories the weak proper-volume law independently predicts momentum gained from radial flux and angular stress. Over five compact shells and all three previous grid/sign cases, the largest relative error of the whole-clock integrated *material* change is 9.89×10⁻¹⁰ wave and 5.01×10⁻¹⁰ cold, 3.97×10⁻⁷ in their smaller contrast. Each model has its own source and measure. This is not equally strong evidence about the tiny geometric-minus-material remainder: its five-time quadrature can be poor (e.g. 64% on the inner positive wave). Energy, surrounding work, and Hamiltonian constraints must not be inferred from only one clean momentum balance.

**Intervention.** We evolved the same equations but differentiated the angular increment directly instead of subtracting two reference-sized curvature stencils. In a manufactured example ordinary full-field subtraction loses all eight nonzero answers, while the rearrangement agrees with a separate 68-decimal calculation within 4.90×10⁻¹³ relative. Both species and both signs pass 32 launch/finite-amplitude qualification entries. On the unaltered positive first-quarter trajectories the independent bulk polar defect is reduced 1183-fold for wave and 1130-fold for dust, retaining all four detector comparisons. This triggered the predeclared full-clock runs. At final time the independent coordinate defect is **89.0× and 72.4× smaller** (positive wave and dust), **76.0× and 68.7× smaller** (negative wave and dust). No filter, change of material law or new charge. Both signs keep all 32 fine proper readings inside the original 20 ppm bands (worst absolute errors 10.08 and 13.78 ppm). These are paired numerical controls, not 32 independent observations.

**Where the physical signal comes from.** We derived the scalar contribution directly as comoving occupation times angular phase gradient, minus the normal-amplitude correction, then separately integrated the microscopic radial flux and angular stress. Across all fifteen grid/sign/window combinations, the heavy species supplies 99.88–99.90% of the change in the wave-minus-cold *total polar momentum*. Within the heavy species, the **phase difference** accounts for 99.99929–99.99943% of the change, the spatial occupation difference contributes about 5.72–7.13 parts per million, and the normal-amplitude current correction has the opposite sign. This is the split of the **momentum carried**, not a claim that scalar amplitude and pressure terms are negligible in the **forces generating** it.

For the last quarter we froze forecasts using stresses at ticks 256, 512 and 768 before the scorer used the independent endpoint currents. The quadratic stress forecast predicts all 15 heavy-sector momentum increments with at most **0.401 ppm relative error**; the linear forecast is comparably accurate and simply holding the last force constant misses by 18.3–22.9 ppm. Omitting the intrinsic scalar diagonal stress and radial amplitude-gradient flux **as a pair**, keeping phase-gradient transport and the background-stress connection on exactly the same histories, misses by **48.1–55.5%**. This diagnoses the pair's net work, not either term independently or a refitted pressureless model. The five shells overlap and the signs/resolutions are controls; these are numerical predictions, not new astronomical measurements. The fully integrated change of total wave-minus-cold material momentum agrees with the independently summed force to within 0.398 ppm on its change/absolute-work scale.

**Limits that set the next experiment.** The positive 320-cells/Mpc control still passes 7/16 proper readings, maximum error 39.64 ppm. Positive fine bulk polar defect relative to its physical orthonormal material source drops from 1.56×10⁻³ to 1.83×10⁻⁵ at final time. It is not strictly converged: for the coordinate covector the new independent order-24 face-flux ratio is 1.24×10⁻⁷ on the positive coarse grid and 9.38×10⁻⁷ on the positive fine grid. We did not retune any thresholds. On the matched signed 640 histories the final heavy-number profile in the tested interior is unchanged to recorded precision; the earlier cubic point-sampling mismatch remains.

The origin is the clearest remaining weakness. In the positive fine wave, the independent Hamiltonian RMS divided by the local density source is 1.61×10⁻³ over r<0.3 Mpc and 6.51×10⁻⁷ over 0.03<r<0.3 Mpc. In the first eight cells alone the absolute residual is 5.00×10⁻¹⁷ against a local source of 1.05×10⁻¹⁷. Four- and six-point even extrapolations of the geometric θ-current curvature already disagree at the last time (6–30 times the material coefficient in absolute discrepancy across the three stencils), unlike the launch. A smooth manufactured tensor reproduces the regular-origin formula; the real evolution is **not** certified by fitting a smooth series onto its central cells.

**Next:** use the derived regular Cartesian combinations (c−s₂/2)/r², (t₂−s₂/2)/r² and K₂/r² as evolved variables in an origin trial. Propagate a smooth nonzero centre shear and a deliberately nonzero angular constraint; do not force the geometric current to equal the material current. This addresses the actual central tensor evolution instead of trying another change of aperture.
