# Can the noisy centre itself account for the aperture difference?

Two budgets are retained: a deliberately conservative single maximum over the whole core, and a sharper bound obtained by summing the same positive polynomial weights separately between each pair of sampled radii. The latter integrates powers by endpoint differences and uses the larger absolute source at the two bracketing nodes; it therefore bounds the stated piecewise-linear reconstruction without extending the innermost spike across the whole core. Each is reported with and without cancellation between the signed and zero hosts. Neither bound uses cancellation between the two epochs or between wave and cold matter.
Do not discard or smooth away the innermost cell. Test the effects of the **measured** constraints on the scalar tide, not the unfounded assertion that a small constraint determines the entire metric error. For physical radius v below both windows, the density weight is exactly K=A v⁴, A=(6/5)∫W/u⁵. Fubini integration of each of the three terms in K gives I₀=∫K/u³=(−1/3+3/5+1/15)∫W/u³=(1/3)∫W/u³. The current weights for a single window at those radii are therefore

    K_r = 2 I₀ v³/5 − 2 A v⁵/7,
    K_t = 6 I₀ v³/5 − 3 A v⁵/7.

The weights for the original two-window difference are obtained by subtracting the two A and I₀ coefficients. This result is valid for the contribution from the centre even for individual windows; it does **not** specify the exterior contribution to their full values. Integrating the absolute polynomial terms through radius R gives a worst-sign density weight |A|R⁵/5 and current weights |I₀|R⁴/10+|A|R⁶/21 and 3|I₀|R⁴/10+|A|R⁶/14. No assumption that numerical errors themselves vanish at the origin is needed.

Use physical radius at each clock. For each branch and time keep the maximum absolute defect over the tap and its bracketing centres, with mirrored regular endpoints. Sum absolute bounds for initial and final, wave and cold, without relying on those cancellations; report host and zero-host independently when bounding the full signed comparison. This bounds the contribution on the specified piecewise-linear, sampled defect reconstruction, not between-mesh pathology, TT radiation, or constraint errors earlier than the two snapshots that already altered the evolved state.
