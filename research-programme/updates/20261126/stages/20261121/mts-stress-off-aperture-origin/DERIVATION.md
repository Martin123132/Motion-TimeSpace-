# Restoring the Newtonian tide without fixing the exterior by hand

**Inputs and conventions.** Use the local stress withdrawal (not bundled here) at gain zero with the same launch for wave and cold matter. Each quantity below is the quadrupole, host minus its own zero-host control, wave minus cold, and then the change since preparation multiplied by 1024. The evolution is not linearized in the spherical fields. We only linearize the homogeneous reference where explicitly indicated. Our sign is the freely falling tidal *acceleration*, minus the radial trace-free spacetime Riemann component. The underlying [3+1 Einstein constraints](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0703035) are imported GR, not new MTS claims.

**Two proper windows.** Denote the inherited temporal and spatial proper-distance readouts by T and S. Let A be the difference of the same two normalized windows on the homogeneous physical radius u=b(t)r, and P the difference of their Newtonian radial tide functionals on the same ruler. The parent already derived the density adjoint (not bundled here). Its kernels for an exterior quadrupole agree exactly. Rescale the integration to u separately at each clock. For a stored profile X, integrate (X_t−X_0) evaluated at u/b+X_0(u/b)−X_0(u) to include the change of sampled radius. The density is the evolving physical energy, not the comoving number or the sum of unsigned densities. Do not multiply it by a second expansion dilution.

**Spatial scalar vs the rest.** Write τ=tr K, σ=(Kʳᵣ)TF. The Hamiltonian constraint gives R₃/2=ε+C+D/2 with C=−τ²/3+3σ²/4 and D=R₃+2τ²/3−3σ²/2−2ε. Through the retained angular order the spatial Jacobi field is S_field=−Ric₃,rr^TF+V+π with V=−τσ/3+σ²/2. Products are harmonics of products, not products of quadrupoles. On flat homogeneous slices the contracted Bianchi identity gives div Ric₃^TF=grad R₃/6, so its longitudinal scalar part is (1/4) Hess^TF Δ⁻¹R₃. Thus P on R₃/2 is the scalar tide. Curved backgrounds and non-scalar shear need not obey that flat scalar formula; it is used to define a split, not impose the answer.

If N is the narrow-minus-wide matched Newton prediction with the stress withdrawn, the exact bookkeeping is

    ΔT − N = (ΔT − ΔS) + (ΔS − A S_field) + [P(ε) − N]
              + P(C) + A(V) + A(π) + B ,
    B = A(S_field − V − π) − P(ε+C).

Here Δ in front of a proper reading denotes narrow minus wide, not the Laplacian. All terms already include the common branch/time contrasts. The first two terms test independent temporal vs spatial readings and the effect of the actual perturbed ruler. The third uses the stored density rather than treating all deviations from Newton as propagating stress. In the second pass P(D/2) is projected independently from the archived high-precision constraint reader and subtracted from B, **not** from the observed tide. This last division has an interpolation sensitivity stated with the results. What remains is not thereby proved to be a traveling wave. Exteriors with arbitrary common tide cannot be diagnosed with the difference alone.

**Current without an assumed boundary.** In physical radius write the angular vector as J_r Y e_r + J_t ∇ΩY. For div J=Δj, a regular scalar j is only determined locally up to a multiple of u². The [three-window test](MOMENTUM-TEST.md) removes that ambiguity as well as an exterior constant tide. Put K equal to its combined scalar density kernel, I₊(v)=∫₀ᵛK(u)u²du and I₋(v)=∫ᵥ^∞K(u)/u³du. Solving the quadrupolar Poisson equation and integrating the radial divergence by parts gives

    P(j) = ∫ { [2v³I₋−3I₊/v²]J_r + 6[v³I₋+I₊/v²]J_t } dv / 5 .

Above the windows both weights vanish: I₋=0 by support and I₊=0 by the second-moment cancellation. The endpoint at zero is regular. Check a nonzero gradient current J=∇[u² exp(−u²)Y] and an independent divergence-free current; the second must give zero, not merely a small relative discrepancy against a large signal. The projected **material** currents are evaluated independently of the shear, including surrounding matter. The proper homogeneous expansion in the numerical clock is H=(d log b/dξ)/(32 b); ξ is the archived conformal time, while q=1024 scales the clock by √q. Compare changes in the products with H(t), not H_final times the change of a field. The shear reconstructed by the scalar momentum equation excludes divergence-free and curved-background contributions by construction. A near-null difference on this filtered observable does not establish the pointwise momentum constraint at the first cell.
