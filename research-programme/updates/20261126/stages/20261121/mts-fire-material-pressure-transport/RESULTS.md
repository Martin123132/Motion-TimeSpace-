# Which mass does the common metric actually couple to?

**The physical advance is a fork we can now test, not a new free constant.** A source weight can describe a real population with its own mass, inertia and stress. Or it can be a label used to observe a different, underlying population. These choices do not in general give the same equations. We have now evolved both choices, measured their internal momentum exchanges, and obtained an aperture-wide quantitative obstruction to mixing the two. All results are private calculations on the retained simulation preparation. [Equations](DERIVATION.md) · [replay](REPLAY.md).

## Pressure momentum closes on the gas that is actually moved

We reconstructed positive gas mass and thermal density from the saved native simulation supports in both clock neighbourhoods, then evolved the material vertices and pressure together for 0.24 Myr. The four trajectories per configuration use the same reset and coupled parent waves, with raw passive labels versus independently pressurized weighted gas. At launch the gas is at rest, as in the archived transaction control; the applied wave potentials are held fixed in time. No fitted velocity or temperature, no floor on a missing tag, no MTS coefficient adjustment.

- For an actual bounded subdivision, we evaluated each side's force independently. The unbalanced part of the first side's own pressure is exactly the momentum transferred from its neighbour; the complementary side loses the same amount. In the primary run the core momentum budget closes to less than 1e−14 of the relevant impulse scale. The integrated exchange work also accounts for the change of the labelled share's kinetic, internal and external potential energies. For example the coupled outer patch gives +0.00083347 solar masses kpc²/Myr² of work. A spatially constant tag gives the same trajectories under either pressure rule on both arms (difference below 8e−16 relative).
- Primary coupled, raw-gas pressure run: in the outer core the positive labels' generalized radial and vertical impulses from label exchange are **−0.6114, +1.3796** solar masses kpc/Myr. Own-pressure impulses are **+0.1599, −1.7131** and gravity contributes **−7.9012, −1.4973**. Their sum is the measured **−8.3527, −1.8308**. Units use the full reflected source, with coordinates defined on its upper meridional half; a vertical generalized impulse is not net Cartesian momentum of the galaxy.
- Shell-intersection integration and a derived fourth time derivative test the clock independently. On the original mechanical mesh the two-core clock double differences at 0.24 Myr are **+5.412e−22** and **+4.918e−22** (reported separately, not whole-galaxy signals). Time halving, extra guard cells and higher clock quadrature scarcely affect them. But the discrepancy is **not spatially converged**: on 2×, 4× and 8× meshes the inner value is +11.95, +15.29 and +14.48, and the outer +8.51, +5.29 and +1.16, all in units of 1e−22. The passive gas's paired clock itself changes by about 0.3% and 2.2% between original and 8× meshes. Source refinement reuses the same physical input; it does not add finer measurements.
- The failure has a diagnostic signature. Once the finest material cells separate the nearest radial label jump from the observer sphere, the launch fourth derivative changes sign in **both** patches. Direct evolutions at 0.00375 Myr recover those negative derivatives to **0.54% and 0.096%**. At 0.24 Myr the double differences are positive. Taking the short-time Taylor coefficient on an unresolved face and carrying it to finite time is not valid. ## A continuum front test distinguishes pressure physics from discretization

At a smooth pressure trace an advected **label alone** launches no wave. Treating the two label values as independently pressurized gas produces a shock and a rarefaction even though the two starting sound speeds are exactly equal. Under the local planar approximation we solved the classical Euler initial-value problem for the already identified faces and independently evolved conservative mass, momentum and energy on 256, 512 and 1,024 cells. Errors decrease at every refinement. For γ=5/3 and the cubic source the velocity-profile L1 error at 1,024 cells is about 1.1% of the contact speed; centre-plateau velocity errors are below one part in 10⁶. This is a control isolating the *artificial jump*, not a prediction that the simulated gas is perfectly uniform.

| Face, γ=5/3 cubic | Effective gas contact (km/s) | Passive-label contact from jump alone | First local acoustic arrival at probe |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Inner, trace A / B | +0.273 / +0.284 | 0 | 0.130 / 0.125 Myr |
| Outer, trace A / B | −0.0209 / −0.0220 | 0 | 0.477 / 0.453 Myr |

There is **no net added slab mass**. The local redistribution has a mass dipole; its Newtonian field can be computed from cumulative mass or directly from the momentum solution. At 0.24 Myr the inner A trace gives a spurious +9.87e−18 m/s² in the reset arm, and a coupled-minus-reset change of −1.69e−21 m/s², with both arms using the same pressure jump. The outer face's isolated perturbing field is still zero there at that time. This is not the 2D patch field, a causal speed for gravity, or measured anomalous acceleration. The off-front cancellation is special to the infinite plane and zero net surface mass. ## What if the wave is sourced by weighted mass but accelerates raw matter?

The action gives a decisive answer **before assuming a pressure law**. Using the saved stellar and gas mass within 30.091 kpc, we compared source work to inertial work on the *same* field, then allowed even the mathematically best single overall rescaling. The RMS mismatch is measured relative to the raw-matter RMS acceleration, weighted by raw mass. It is not a percentage residual of a galaxy observation.

| Driver | Best possible residual, joint gas + stars |
|---|---:|
| Reset wave field | 84.91% |
| Coupled wave field | 84.97% |
| Coupled minus reset wave field | **77.06%** |

For the difference driver, the gas-only floor is **75.61%**, stars-only **76.41%**. Both within-bin radial representations and orders 2/4 agree closely. This rules out repairing the hypothetical *mixed source/inertia interpretation* just by choosing a better universal multiplier. It does **not** apply this penalty to the archived reciprocal represented-mass model. If the raw matter is the gravitational source as well, the aperture's ordinary outer-minus-inner potential clock at launch is **1.28968e−7** rather than the tag's **4.69506e−8**, and the outer radial ordinary force is −1.4061e−10 instead of −6.0578e−11 m/s². These are gravitational contributions of two different **host mass maps**, with the exterior held common, not scores against the Milky Way.
