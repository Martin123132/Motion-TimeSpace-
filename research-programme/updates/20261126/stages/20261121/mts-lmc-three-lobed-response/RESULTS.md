# The three-lobed response to the Large Magellanic Cloud
       
Two fixed Galactic wave preparations, six satellite histories and the original eight signed force intervals. No perturbation added at the initial epoch. The previously calculated circular and two-lobed responses were retained once. The three-lobed density changes the spatial distribution without changing the total first-order occupation. At the actual observing meridian its contribution has the opposite sign to the value at the positive x-axis; see [derivation](DERIVATION.md).
       
**Sky result:** the new term closes only 0.0024–0.0048% of the remaining inner radial gap, and widens the inner polar gap by 0.0030–0.0064%. Neither the MTS nor matched static NFW count changes from 2/8 on either host or any history. The six histories are alternatives, not six independent sky observations.
       
**Energy test:** allowing arbitrary favorable shapes at each sector's source-energy ceiling, the signed force required from m=0,2,3 together is still 3.64–4.92 times the corresponding maximum projection. Giving the vertically odd response unrestricted help with the north/south difference leaves a factor of 3.53–4.79. An independent initial oscillation confined to these three sectors would need at least 6.15–13.69 times their combined driven-energy ceiling even under the latter relaxation (6.70–14.63 without it). These are bounds for the specified interpolated history and finite linear branches, not for the galaxy's formation energy or all angular sectors. See reference ground (not bundled here) and second ground (not bundled here).
       
**How could one look for it?** The [two ring combinations](MULTIPOLE-NULL.md) cancel arbitrary phases of the degree-three and degree-five regular vacuum tides. In the radial combination K, the residual direct satellite harmonic is 0.21–0.39 times the predicted wave. Nevertheless, the change in the archived *live collisionless* host exceeds that wave by factors of 22–51 across both field representations. The vertical combination Z suppresses the direct field further, but gives an even larger live-to-wave ratio. Static spherical NFW is an inadequate control for attributing such a harmonic; these live hosts are diagnostics, not a common-initial-state matched comparison. [All contrasts](evidence-001.zip) (archive member: `vacuum-null-test.csv`). Neither combination is an observed detection.
       
**Numerical checks:** the 96→128 radial-mode refinement and 16→32 physical time subdivisions each pass the unchanged 20 ppm target in all 48 meridian increments. The independent gradient agrees to 2.76e-12 relative. The larger-domain change reaches 0.122%, above target, without changing any band decision; no infinite-domain precision is claimed. All 192 MTS/control comparisons were checked, including the complete-degree source on both hosts; 962 inherited source/code files were checked against their prior receipts. Validation (not bundled here).
