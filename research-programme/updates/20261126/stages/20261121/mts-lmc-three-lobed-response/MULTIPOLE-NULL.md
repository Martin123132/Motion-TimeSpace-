# Two ring contrasts fixed before calculating their values
       
Can the weak three-lobed response survive a test that suppresses smooth external gravity without selecting coefficients from a simulation or the observed bands?
       
In a region where a potential is regular and harmonic, its lowest vertically even m=3 terms at any one time have the form
       
    Φ = Re{ exp(3iφ) R³ [A + B(z²−R²/8)] } + higher degrees.
       
A and B may have arbitrary complex phases and amplitudes. Indeed the transverse Laplacian of R⁵ exp(3iφ) is 16 R³ exp(3iφ), while differentiating z² twice gives 2. The azimuthal phase reference may be shifted to the known negative-x meridian without changing either null. These are the degree-three and degree-five regular solid harmonics, not assumptions about the field at a material source. From a_R=−∂RΦ and a_z=−∂zΦ we get the complex coefficients (omitting the common reference phase)
       
    a_R/R² = −3A − 3B z² + 5 B R²/8,       a_z/R³ = −2 B z.
       
For i=6 kpc, o=8.3 kpc and h=1.5 kpc form **both** controls, in acceleration units:
       
    Z = a_z(i) − (i/o)³ a_z(o),
    K = a_R(i) − (i/o)² a_R(o) + [5(i²−o²)/(16 h i)] a_z(i).
       
They both vanish for arbitrary A and B, including either sign, separately as complex numbers. On the even-in-height projected data, take cylindrical components at +h by rotating the original spherical radial/polar vectors at each ring. Freeze these weights, use both quadratures of the new 128-mode, 32-substep wave, and apply the same weights to the native direct satellite, independent archived subtraction, and both live collisionless field representations (final minus initial). Report both contrasts regardless of the result. Finite fields of degree seven or higher, local sources and nonvacuum overlap may leak: evaluate rather than assume cancellation. As a control on whether these are trivial identities, challenge with the next harmonic R³[z⁴−(3/4)R²z²+(3/80)R⁴] exp(3iφ). The construction suppresses the local tidal expansion; it need not suppress a local wave or a density-carrying collisionless halo. There is no new observed m=3 measurement, optimized signal-to-noise direction or post-hoc choice of the better contrast.
