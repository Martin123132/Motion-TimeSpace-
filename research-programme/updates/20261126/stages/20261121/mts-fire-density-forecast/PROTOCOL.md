# Blind-to-comparison density-rate forecast, FIRE launch 590

Freeze both prepared wave states (reset, coupled), separately, with both signs of their launch current and their unaltered occupation, common host and boundary. Use only the sealed forward launch metric fields and the independently checked conjugation identity to produce the two predictions. Do not read later density measurements or select directions until the numerical forecast is saved and hashed. These are conditional, local-in-time forecasts. This is not a claim that the larger research project never saw the FIRE data in development.

Use all three physical spherical radii through the existing clock positions (cylindrical radii 6, 7.15, 8.3 kpc, common height 1.5 kpc). Save both adjacent shell densities and their initial slopes: mean over shell volume, with all 35 retained even Legendre coefficients, no angular-mode selection. Also give enclosed masses and fluxes at the three faces. Positive mass rate means mass entering the sphere. A density slope in solar masses/kpc³/Myr is the coefficient of elapsed physical Myr, not a promise of linear evolution between widely separated snapshots.

Integration of the radial Poisson equation gives, for each conventional Legendre coefficient, 4πG D ρ_l = [r² V'_l]_a^b − l(l+1) integral_a^b V_l dr, D=(b³−a³)/3. Apply the identical linear functional to the saved potential rate, converting (km/s)/kpc to 1/Myr with the retained time unit. For l=0 this reduces to the difference of the two enclosed fluxes divided by the spherical shell volume. Use order-12 flux fields as primary and cubic Hermite integration on the saved radial knots (same unit, zero and coordinates for both signs); native equation and order 8 are numerical comparisons only. No additional smoothing or adjustable parameter.

A candidate independent history must (1) measure or independently evolve the same heavy-wave mass, or a separately isolated gravitational source with demonstrated equivalence; (2) match host, centering, frame, occupation and time ordering without estimating a normalization, sign or radial mapping from the answer; (3) resolve both shells or a predeclared identifiable subset (monopoles if angular information is unavailable); (4) supply a rate or a controlled finite-time approximation with temporal truncation and measurement uncertainties. A separate code using the same evolved states is not independent physical evidence. Gravity sourced by all matter, ordinary gas/stars, passive tags and collisionless dark-matter particles are not automatically the heavy wave.

Parity tolerance remains 1e-9. Keep the earlier 0.5% independent numerical comparison ceiling; do not reinterpret it as an observational error bar. For a matched history compare identical observables for both signs: maximum absolute residual divided by the peak absolute primary forecast across the two shells, per preparation (monopole test); for angular comparisons also use the shell-volume and angular weighted RMS norm of the full field. Report both residuals, do not choose a winner if uncertainties or cadence cannot distinguish them. Do not select a different time span or bin after seeing the result. If no independent source qualifies, say so. A saved, matched same-solver history may be used AFTER freezing as a labelled sign/short-time transport control, with no evidence promotion.
