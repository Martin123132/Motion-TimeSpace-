# Two ray distances separate the local photon coupling from the finite-range pion response

21 November 2026 · local analytic and numerical calculation · no fitted coefficient.

Taking the polarization difference at a single distance removes the common classical GR delay, but leaves the unknown local curvature coupling. Weight each polarization difference by the square of its impact parameter *before* subtracting the two. For an isolated weak point lens the local term then cancels, whereas the gapped matter spectrum survives with weight U(Wb)−U(WB). The [derivation](DERIVATION.md) gives units, sign, matching and which non-point lenses preserve that cancellation. This is a conditional response function; ordinary GR with the same quantum matter can also generate the loop. It is not evidence for a distinct MTS correction in sky data.

**What the saved pion calculation contributes.** At 1 and 2 femtometres, the lower signed elastic reference contributes +3.06418×10⁻⁵ GeV⁻², but the allowed higher window can reverse the sign of the two-window sum. At 2 and 4 fm the interval still straddles zero. At **3 and 6 fm** the 0.281–0.54 GeV reference contributes +2.11418×10⁻⁶ GeV⁻², and the 0.60–0.75 GeV angular-data bound (including the shared charged normalization) is [−0.66616,+0.71862]×10⁻⁶ GeV⁻². Thus **these two contributions combined** lie in [+1.44802,+2.83280]×10⁻⁶ GeV⁻², conditional on the saved low-energy wave and angular assumptions. Neither a complete prediction nor a confidence interval: the strip below 0.281, the gap 0.54–0.60, energies above 0.75, and other matter/cuts are not included. **Independent checks.** The point charged scalar's closed momentum-space loop integrated against an oscillating Bessel function agrees with the spectral transform within 1.33×10⁻⁷ relative on five test separations. The two scalar limits and the leading exponential threshold coefficient were checked separately. Quadrature refinement shifts the pion reference and outer endpoints by at most 1.16×10⁻⁹ relative. Direct integration of a circular extended lens reproduces the analytic exterior response; a noncircular counterexample leaves a 24.6% local residue (relative to the point local transfer at the nearer distance) under the same point weights. **Where the effect can matter.** The pion threshold sets a range of 0.707 fm. At the saved binary impact parameter of 6.11×10⁸ m the point-source threshold kernel alone has log₁₀ U ≈ −3.75×10²³. This is a scale comparison, not a model of the extended binary or a timing bound. The unsuppressed local tidal delay remains in ordinary single-ray observations. Searching for the nonlocal pion profile at pulsar distances would not calibrate that local coefficient.

**Next:** close the nearest missing low-energy contributions first: independently constrain the signed threshold slope and continue the two-pion wave from 0.54 to 0.60 GeV with sourced errors or an explicit outer bound. Propagate those into the same fixed 3/6-fm contrast, then ask how much unmeasured spectral norm could reverse it. Do not interpret the elastic window as a proof of positivity for mixed stress–photon spectral weight.
