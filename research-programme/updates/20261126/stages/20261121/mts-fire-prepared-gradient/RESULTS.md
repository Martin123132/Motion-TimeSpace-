# The wave remembers its earlier assembly; the inherited change is too small on these foregrounds

Private · 21 November 2026. The original late source lasts 425.18 Myr. We first measured the complex wave from the six saved FIRE-2 m12i gas-and-star epochs against each host's own final equilibrium, before scoring its force. We then compared two waves evolved through the **same** late gravity: one started at equilibrium at snapshot 572; the other carried in a fixed-occupation state evolved from 544, with the additional 558 source snapshot. That comparison separates memory of the initial state from disagreement about the late source. Neither state was chosen using the force data.

Rounded values at the finer original time step. The three dimensional columns measure the **squared spatial-gradient distance** from the host's own reference, expressed per unit wave mass in (km/s)². These are not total halo energies, missing matter fractions, or source work. Intervals are the six already-specified direct LMC alternatives, not six independent galaxies. The lower requirement permits arbitrary angular shapes at the measured gradient budget. On the simpler 544→572 endpoint interpolation without 558 the ratios are roughly 446–507, also insufficient. Removing this inherited preparation entirely gives 1,439–1,896. None of these ratios can be assigned to an arbitrary, accreting, or substantially excited halo.

**The initial state is not simply erased.** On the 558-resolved source, the squared gradient separation between inherited and reset waves changes over 572→600 by factors **1.855**, **1.316**, and **0.870** on anchorA, cut1 and cut2. Those are trajectory differences, not universal gains for arbitrary seeds. At the measured foreground, the inherited change to the inner radial force at 600 is *outward* by 0.00638 in units of 10⁻¹⁰ m/s² relative to the reset wave; it does not close the inward deficit. The untransferred FIRE analogue itself gives 0.21099 (km/s)² with the reset and its own ground; its gas+stellar density is not the Milky Way and receives **no Galactic force score**. The three local transfers retain the original occupation calibration, ordinary matter and source-path assumptions. Reflection symmetry alone does not make the two hemispheres' intervals incompatible. **Which forces carry the memory?** On anchorA, an integral estimated from six saved snapshots alone would have given **−1.848** instead of the actual **+0.314** (km/s)² increase in separation. We therefore replayed the original paired trajectories at every solver step (168 coarse, 336 fine), with no new external inputs. Every saved endpoint on both replays was recovered. The symmetric source identity gives this finer-step accounting:

| Contribution along this pair of trajectories | Change in squared gradient distance, (km/s)² |
|---|---:|
| Common ordinary gravity | +0.043913 |
| Shared part of the two waves' self-gravity | +0.116095 |
| Difference in self-gravity generated by the two densities | +0.154033 |
| Measured net increase | +0.314041 |

About **86% of that net increase** is carried by the two self-gravity terms together. This is a signed accounting identity along these two waves, **not** a claim that switching off self-gravity would remove 86% of the response; switching it off would change the trajectories. The terms reverse sign within the time window. No new coupling or population was fitted. The discrete budget closes to better than 10⁻⁸ (km/s)²; the integrated terms change by **0.18–0.29%** on time refinement, so do not read their displayed digits as time-converged to 20 ppm. **Checks and remaining uncertainty.** All 864 original paired coarse/fine signed totals across reset and both earlier paths meet their original 2×10⁻⁵ relative threshold with no band flips. The small reset gradient distances themselves change by 0.030–0.155% across all four hosts and do **not** meet that threshold. Two independent radial orders and an angular rule covering every retained density product recover the native force changes to within 2×10⁻¹¹ in the quoted force units. A spherical compact source and exterior shell separately check the signs and normalization. Six saved source evaluations cannot be used to apportion time-integrated growth on the two alternative foregrounds. No new fit, observational detection, or unique galactic initial occupation is implied.

**Next test to connect the source to the Newtonian limit.** Use the measured foreground and the already frozen 544→558→572 wave. Keep the reset wave fully self-gravitating, drive both with the same ordinary field, and in a separate control transport the inherited wave in the *reset wave's* gravity. Compare the signed radial/polar response and the distance between preparations with the true coupled result on the same time steps; if the two differ, that isolates what the independently generated density potential actually does, rather than interpreting the accounting fractions as causal. No new galactic coupling, selection of initial phases, or tuning to the bands. A bigger response would still need an independently supplied initial population or boundary flux.
