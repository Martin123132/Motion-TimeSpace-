# Frozen comparison

Start from the two saved transverse projectors and both their scalar and Dirac determinants. No refit. Let t=(epsilon/m)². Predict F(t)−F(t/4) without a local intercept. Use exact low-momentum moments and an independently evaluated spectral kernel; compare against direct logarithmic loops, at epsilon/m=1/8, 1/4, 1/2, 1 and 2, including both signs of an added intercept. Record dimension-eight and higher terms separately. Keep the prior absolute gate 2e−11 for dimensionless continuum quadrature comparisons and 2e−10 for exact null controls. Compare matched finite-spacing momenta on the same scalar torus, with an explicitly injected Maxwell contact, not a fitted subtraction of an unknown matter artifact. Do not treat fixed-spacing differences as continuum.

Separate real on-shell photons from the Euclidean off-shell family. Check published helicity-flip discontinuities against a direct two-particle angular projection for a charged scalar and the old scalar/Dirac sign anchors. If they match, use that bridge to replace the old two-sided W envelope by a signed *one-loop matter contribution* on the specified electroweak branch. Keep the parent coefficient and the full hadronic response separate; do not manufacture a physical QCD value from a pointlike pion.
