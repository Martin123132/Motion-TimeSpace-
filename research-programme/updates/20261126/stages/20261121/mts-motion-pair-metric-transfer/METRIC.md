## Force, bending and a comparison independent of the lens mass

Let \(\epsilon_\Phi,\epsilon_g,\epsilon_\alpha\) denote the changes in potential, inward force and unpolarized deflection divided by their leading GR values. In a static Newtonian gauge write \(g_{00}=-(1+2\Phi),\ g_{ij}=(1-2\Psi)\delta_{ij}\), \(\Phi_{\rm GR}=\Psi_{\rm GR}=-GM/r\). Dust-dust projections are \(2/3\) for \(P_2\), \(1/3\) for \(P_0\) and \(1/2\) for the Einstein numerator. A conserved null probe has zero trace. It follows that, \(W=\sqrt{s}\),
\[
 n(s)=(4a_2+2a_0)/3,\quad
 \epsilon_\Phi(r)=\int n e^{-Wr}ds,\qquad
 \epsilon_g(r)=\int n(1+Wr)e^{-Wr}ds.
\]
The spatial potential changes by \(\int (2a_2-2a_0)e^{-Wr}ds/3\) in units of \(\Phi_{\rm GR}\). Thus \((\Phi+\Psi)/(2\Phi_{\rm GR})-1=\int a_2e^{-Wr}ds\). The common time correction in units \(4t_M=4GM/c_{\rm light}^3\) is \(\int a_2 K_0(Wb)ds\). Taking its first derivative gives \(\epsilon_\alpha=\int a_2 Wb K_1(Wb)ds\). This agrees with integrating the two independently computed static metric potentials along the same ray. As a nontrivial external test, changing variables to \(W=2m(1+t)\) at \(\xi=0\) reproduces the polynomial \(3-(1+t)^{-2}+7/[4(1+t)^4]\) in [Fröb, Eq. (4.22a)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1607.03129), independently of the spin decomposition.

There is a sign result not needing a mass or fitted coupling: with impact \(b\) equal to orbital radius \(r\), \(0<\epsilon_\alpha<3\epsilon_g/4\) for a nonempty positive spin-two continuum of this form. For each mass \(y=Wb\), \(yK_1(y)\) equals the normalized line-of-sight average of \((1+Wr)e^{-Wr}\) with measure \(b^2 dz/r^3\) on \(z\ge0,\ r=\sqrt{b^2+z^2}\). This function decreases with \(r\); its average is less than its closest-approach value. The scalar trace supplies only additional nonnegative inward force. The effective mass inferred from light divided by that inferred from a circular orbit changes by \(\epsilon_\alpha-\epsilon_g\) to this order. This ideal-mass comparison cancels the leading source normalization; it is not an observational mass discrepancy for a real extended galaxy.

At \(mb\ll1,\ r=b\), for **one** minimally coupled canonical real field:
