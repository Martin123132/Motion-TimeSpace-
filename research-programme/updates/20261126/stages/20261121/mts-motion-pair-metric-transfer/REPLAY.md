# Reproduce the local calculation

Run `python -B verify.py` here with OMP_NUM_THREADS, OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS and MKL_NUM_THREADS set to 1. Uses the retained Python/NumPy/SciPy/mpmath environment; no downloads, long jobs, prior-stage writes or observing-data changes. Verifies the preceding 19-file seal and eight independent source files, regenerates this stage's tables, audits links, compares all file hashes with the local seal.

- [Baseline and scope](PROTOCOL.md) · [metric calculation](METRIC.md) · [intrinsic loop](CROSSED.md). Universal leading matter-loop physics is not counted as a newly derived fundamental coupling; see [Burns & Pilaftsis](https://arxiv.org/abs/1412.6021) and [Fröb](https://arxiv.org/abs/1607.03129). In particular Eq. (4.22a) of the latter supplies a distinct massive-potential representation in the minimal case. Its exact polynomial and normalizations are tested rather than only comparing a qualitative falloff.
- [Tensor checks](evidence-001.zip) (archive member: `angular-projections.csv`) vary energies, masses, helicities, azimuth and gauge representatives; the two-helicity amplitude squared is never substituted for the signed equal-helicity D projection. Two-source spectral shape (not bundled here) uses dimensionless entries multiplying ℓP²/r² or ℓP²/b²; the common time column also multiplies 4GM/c³. The finite-mass threshold variable is 2mb. The conformal control is not adopted as a change of parent operator.
- [Metric checks](evidence-001.zip) (archive member: `independent-controls.csv`) compare direct 3D potentials with the massive literature expression, a line integral of the metric with the time kernel, finite differences of the potential with the force and of the common delay with the bending and polarization split. Crossed cut (not bundled here) compares its analytic angular resolvent with direct on-shell projections including explicit changes of three subtraction coefficients. Normalized screen (not bundled here) uses both an upper crossed invariant and an upper pair invariant of 1 meV in energy. It does not extend the IR lepton coefficient through hadronic energies.
- [Numeric receipt](evidence-001.zip) (archive member: `checks.json`). The reduced mass scale is fixed by the same Newton constant as the source action. SI examples use the installed CODATA constants via SciPy, with ℓP² checked against the tabulated Planck length. The heavy and light masses are retained examples, not measured local spectra. Alpha, the cosmological occupation, the local photon coupling, and the crossed subtraction have not been fitted to these calculations.
