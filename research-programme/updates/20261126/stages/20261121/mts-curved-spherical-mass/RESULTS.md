# Curved spherical mass transport: the final two proper-ruler tides recover

Local controlled q=1024 experiment on the previously saved cosmological wave/cold launch. The separately requested fixed-occupation LMC calculation is here (not bundled here); the calculations have different sources, observables and gates.

**Prediction tested.** Signed host-minus-zero, wave-minus-cold proper-ruler acceleration, at four times through 4096 carrier turns, two widths, two detector expressions. Score relative to |Newton tide|, not to a tiny residual. The FLRW live-stress forecast, 20 ppm tolerance, clock, grid, common coupling, launch amplitudes, source histories and angular evolution were held fixed. Only the spherical evolution variables and their consistent feed into the matter equation changed; see derivation (not bundled here) and [prospective protocol](PROTOCOL.md). The geometric mass was not set equal to the integral of the matter density.

**Fine clock.** All 32 readings pass on the two signed 640-cells/Mpc runs; worst absolute errors are 10.076 ppm (+) and 14.483 ppm (−). The previous implementation passed the first 24 and missed all eight final readings. Recomputing the proper ruler in parts gives the same scores. Example: final positive, width 0.30, Gauss, old 1972.699 ppm, new 10.076 ppm. The sampled-field term changes by only 0.0038 ppm; its local Jacobian compensation stays put, while its integrated-distance term changes by −1962.62 ppm. The signed projection onto the previous localized 48-cell centre mode is reduced by orders of magnitude; the old mode was not subtracted from the readings. This compares the fate of one old mode, not stability for every possible perturbation.

**Source and shape checks.** On the actual final fine slices, across both signs, matter branches and the two radial regions, the largest relative RMS difference of the independently reconstructed and directly evolved areal accelerations is 1.14 × 10⁻¹⁰. In the positive wave core it is 3.06 × 10⁻¹¹, versus about 0.92 previously. The maximum relative mass-work difference is 7.97 × 10⁻¹¹ including the geometric energy and current defects, and 1.89 × 10⁻⁸ using material flux/pressure alone. The independent monopole current differs from the material current by up to 2.04 × 10⁻⁶ on this measure. These are signed host differences on saved slices with finite nonzero rates, not a general conservation law. The manufactured curved test has nonzero constraint residuals and independently supplied energy/momentum defects: all ten finest comparisons meet the predeclared 10⁻⁷ target; the worst relative error is 3.76 × 10⁻⁹. The centre momentum derivative is non-monotone under refinement; no measured convergence order is inferred there.

The actual angular constraints are a separate issue: at final time the largest core RMS residuals divided by the signed matter-source RMS are 0.357% (Hamiltonian), 0.310% (radial momentum) and 0.641% (polar momentum), even though the proper-aperture detector passes; the largest bulk polar ratio is 0.156%. The conserved-cell shell-number profile differs from the old forecast by 0.405% (+) and 0.350% (−) on the final 640 grid. The *integrated* shell profile is much closer, 0.00398% and 0.00379%, respectively. Do not exchange integrated and pointwise criteria.

**Matched 320-grid test:** 7/16 pass (+); the worst miss is 39.820 ppm. The first quarter passes all four, the second three, and neither later quarter passes. The original run at this resolution also passed 7/16; repairing the central instability on the fine grid does not make the coarser discretization accurate. At the final time the signed positive wave-minus-cold heavy-number change versus the old implementation on its own 640 grid is just 8.53 × 10⁻¹⁰ of its profile norm in the tested interior. But point-sampling versus the earlier freeze is still interpolation-sensitive: 0.694% linear vs 1.248% cubic (+), 0.545% vs 1.015% (−). No unconditional pointwise number pass is inferred.

**Next experiment toward local GR:** isolate the angular momentum balance on the two matched positive histories first. In the bulk the final wave polar defect/source RMS grows from 0.0108% to 0.156% on doubling the grid, while the angular Hamiltonian and bulk radial current remain much smaller. Compute the same covector at matched radial locations with the evolution stencil and the independent geometric readout, decompose the ordinary, wave and surrounding currents, and check the contracted angular transport equation with a nonzero manufactured source. Keep physical forces, occupations and proper rulers locked. That separates a readout/discretization mismatch from missing angular stress. Do not extrapolate to q=262144 from the two-aperture scalar result.
