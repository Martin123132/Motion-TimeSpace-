# Independent source search: enclosed mass versus its time derivative
Targeted primary-source search, 21 November 2026. Searched for Galactic enclosed-mass evolution and halo growth, observed radial stellar or gas mass flux near the solar circle, Galactic accelerations and jerk in pulsar timing, and the contents of the FIRE m12i release. This is a bounded search, not a claim that no possible dataset exists. Eligibility requires the frozen physical observable, host or independently justified host mapping, temporal response and foreground uncertainties. No points were scored simply for having overlapping radii.

| Primary source | What it actually supplies | Decision for this test |
|---|---|---|
| [Wetzel et al., public FIRE-2 release](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.06969); [archive](https://flathub.flatironinstitute.org/fire) | Time-ordered particle snapshots of the analog. The saved extraction has gas and stars; the public total also includes conventional dark-matter particles. | Calculated raw baryonic rates below. Total minus baryons in native FIRE is its own particle halo; worth a separate conventional-halo benchmark, not an independent measurement of a wave in either hybrid preparation. |
| [Bovy & Rix, SEGUE stellar dynamics](https://doi.org/10.1088/0004-637X/779/2/115) | Dynamical disk and dark-halo profile overlapping the requested radii, from stellar phase space. | Measurement of a profile, not its time derivative. Its publication date cannot be paired with a Gaia publication date to infer a physical change. |
| [Donlon et al., binary pulsar accelerations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.15808) | Relative line-of-sight acceleration of 26 binaries and local density inference including a baryonic budget. | Acceleration and density, not time derivative of sphere-averaged acceleration or baryonic mass flux. A measured nonzero acceleration is compatible with both signs. |
| [Liu et al., EPTA/PPTA frequency second derivatives](https://pure.manchester.ac.uk/ws/portalfiles/portal/110416649/Frequency_second_derivatives_and_timing_noises.pdf) | Real timing derivatives and uncertainties, including a detection for J1024−0719 consistent with a wide companion. | A derivative of the appropriate order, but also contains internal/companion, motion, differential-field and sightline terms. No enclosed heavy-wave mass rate on the three spheres. |
| [Halo-star mass-change feasibility study](https://academic.oup.com/mnras/article/521/3/4074/7081358) | Radial stellar streaming calibrated with simulated haloes in a 15–45 kpc shell, including FIRE analogs. The authors discuss non-equilibrium contamination of such inferences. | Useful tracer concept, not an observed 6–8.4 kpc pair of rates, and bulk stellar velocity is not the mass-weighted dark current. |
| [DESI DR2 halo K-giant kinematics](https://researchportal.port.ac.uk/en/publications/the-velocity-field-of-our-milky-way-outer-stellar-halo-based-on-d/) | Real tracer kinematics, including disequilibrium and reflex response; the fitted contraction/dipole addresses the outer halo. | Overlapping catalogue radii do not give a time series of enclosed mass. The stellar response to each potential and the selection function would have to be modeled independently. |
| [Fox et al., HST UV high-velocity cloud measurements](https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/missions/hubble/releases/2019/10/STScI-01EVSQSY6J2XT7G8DMCGY3JCKH.pdf) | Real gas inflow/outflow rates with distance, abundance and ionization assumptions. | Useful independent foreground information about a tracer, not all gas and stars crossing our three Galactocentric surfaces. Not a subtraction from the simulated host. |
| [Buist & Helmi, stream response to a changing potential](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201526203) | Simulated dynamical imprint of halo evolution on streams. | Historical, orbit-dependent response; no instantaneous shell derivatives. |
| [FIRE gas-flow study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.11472) | Simulated, spatially and temporally resolved gas transport in disk galaxies. | Independent gas dynamics analysis, not all-matter or heavy-wave flux through these closed spheres. |

Search result: no reviewed product supplies a matched total and ordinary-matter derivative with a shared error model, or independently identified heavy-wave flux. Do not interpret sign, quote a chi-square, or set the missing residual to zero.
