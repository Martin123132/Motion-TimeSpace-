# Ordinary Maxwell theory can masquerade as the Weyl term on a lattice

Work in lattice units with x=εa for physical momentum ε and lattice spacing a. The continuum readouts are calibrated to the same CFF convention as the parent projector (not bundled here). Apply them to the **ordinary noncompact plaquette Maxwell action**, not a dressed quark loop. At each oriented face the photon field strength is the forward curl; its weight is the metric factor in the plaquette action. Let f be the fraction of the external shear sampled from the arithmetic mean of corners, with the rest sampled at the geometric center of the face. Both placements have precisely the same zero-shear two-point function. For a time-containing face, f times the endpoint average plus (1−f) times its center has Fourier factor 1−f+f cos x relative to its center. Differentiating the two link curls and this weight gives the volume-stripped reading

    G_n(x) = 4(1−f+f cos x) sin²(x/2) − 4 sin²(nx/2).

Its sign and normalization are independently tested by multiplying forward differences of the two source fields at every site and sampling the real-space face. Its leading continuum value is (1−n²)x². Denote S_j=Σ_n w_n n^j. The Maxwell null, unit Weyl response and divergence null imply S₀=S₂=−1/16, S₄=−5/16; the three continuum columns are 1−n², −8(1+n²), and 6+4n²−2n⁴. It follows for **any** weights obeying these conditions on this slice that

    Σ w_n G_n(x) / x⁴ = (3f−2)/96 + O(x²).

At fixed regulator the two previous banks thus report the **same** spurious dimension-six coefficient from pure Maxwell: +Zγ a²/96 for corners or −Zγ a²/48 for midpoint. It vanishes in physical units as a→0, not as ε→0 at fixed a. Agreement between the two banks cannot by itself identify curvature physics. This is an artifact of this action, momentum slice and stress definition, not an anomalous photon speed.

**Two repairs actually tested.** (1) Calibrate ordinary Maxwell strength independently from the flat two-point reading at an unused momentum, then subtract its *lattice* three-source vertex, not its bare continuum expansion. Both placements and both banks on five sizes return the injected continuum Weyl coefficient −0.037 to better than 10⁻¹¹. This is a manufactured injection, not a determination of Zγ in QCD. A physical HVP would require a renormalization prescription and control of its momentum dependence.

(2) Requiring the common artifact to vanish uniquely fixes **f=2/3**, without a fit. In fact the two existing banks have exact finite-momentum expressions then. Writing u=cos x, before division by x⁴ the (0,1,2) bank is **identically zero**, and the (1,2,3) bank is **(1−u)³/20**. This follows by substituting cos(nx)=T_n(u); the second bank's divided residue is x²/160+O(x⁴). A separate site-space test at extents 10,18,26,40,64 confirms both identities. The improvement is local for a prescribed smooth external metric whose center value is known; it does not assume new dynamical metric variables. It improves this observable, not the full regulated stress tensor.

**Back on colored matter.** Change the gluon face placement in the *same* coupled finite action, leaving all quark inputs unchanged. Only S_g,h changes: at these nonzero momenta the exact response shift is [−<δS_g,h S_pk>+<δS_g,h S_p S_k>]/V. We predicted this without redetermining any quark coupling and compared it to brute-force finite gauge and quark determinant weights. On all 12 history/mode pairs the largest absolute error is 2.02×10⁻¹⁰. The two fixture readouts shift by absolute magnitudes 0.001685 and 0.000917. This is a change of stress discretization in a finite fixture, not a radiative correction to quote for QCD. The two placement choices are useful future cutoff controls; independent actual stress normalization and a continuum extrapolation are still necessary.
