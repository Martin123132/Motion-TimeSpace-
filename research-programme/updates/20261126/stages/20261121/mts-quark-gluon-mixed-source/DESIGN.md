The question is whether we can implement the current/current/shear response including the changes of the sea determinant and the gauge-action weight. This is a controlled noncommuting four-dimensional Wilson-link fixture. It is not an estimate of the physical QCD Wilson coefficient.

Require both longitudinal photon replacements and exact Bose interchange to 2e−10 per site. Check SU(3), noncommutation, finite color-gauge invariance, single-configuration and charge controls, and an intentionally incomplete estimator.
