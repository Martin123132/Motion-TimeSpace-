# Finite mixed stress–two-current measurement: prediction before loop evaluation

Retain the 4946 source derivative (metric, two vector potentials, all contacts) and the same full-index CFF used in the binary ray calculation. Test the Euclidean effective action −log Z. Specify R with positive scalar curvature on spheres, so R_0202=+q₀² h₂₂/2 for a plane spatial shear. Set epsilon as the common momentum unit, not a Wilson coefficient to be adjusted. Strip the space-time volume delta and differentiate twice in independent photon amplitudes (also if the momenta coincide). Use the positive Maxwell density F²/4 in Euclidean space. Keep explicit signs before converting conventions.

On a four-dimensional torus choose q=(2,0,0,0), p=(-1,n,0,0), k=(-1,-n,0,0) times epsilon, metric h₂₂=1, h₃₃=−1, both photon polarizations along 2. Maxwell and Ricci are proportional in this one slice: do NOT claim five independent measurements there. Derive an exact multi-reading Weyl coefficient canceling Maxwell, scalar curvature, Ricci and the covariant divergence-squared term. Separately test an enlarged bank including trace and another q magnitude; inspect rank, photon Ward, Bose, and the diffeomorphism contact terms off shell. A second noncollinear-only bank must agree at leading order. A finite contact proportional to CFF itself remains inseparable.

Known-source prediction: in units e²/[(4π)² m²], Bastianelli–Dávila–Schubert (arXiv:0812.4849, scalar Drummond–Hathrell form) gives −1/180 for full-index Riemann FF in their log Z convention, hence +1/180 in −log Z with the same Euclidean curvature. Build the scalar loop from its minimally coupled kinetic operator, NOT the matched effective action. Preserve tadpole and contact graphs, the order-two charge subtraction and higher powers in momentum. Test epsilon/m=1/2,1/4,1/8,1/16,1/32 for both banks, inverse mass squared/charge squared scaling and quadrature refinement. Report finite-momentum bias, not just the extrapolated value. If possible apply the same continuum extractor to a genuinely discretized field or independent microscopic spinor. A free charged species is a benchmark, not confined QCD. Do not assign a QCD coefficient or revise a sealed sign without an action-convention bridge.
