# Which term lifts the zero of the ordinary tensor tide?

Start with the retained low-energy action, checkpoint 4947 (not bundled here) on its stated zero-local-motion-source branch. We keep the displayed Einstein, Maxwell, W=C_abcd F^ab F^cd and curvature-squared terms, to quadratic order in prepared field amplitude and linear order in the higher-derivative coefficients. All results are in the *same* metric and detector frame. This is a response derivative about weak gravity, not evolution of the unreduced fourth-order theory with arbitrary extra initial data. Do not assign numerical physical values to the coefficients.

**Two geometrical sources.** Variation of +c_W W at flat metric gives T_W^ad/c_W=4∂_b∂_c Weyl[F^ab F^cd]. The Weyl projection first removes the four-form then the Ricci traces. This improvement is conserved and trace-free. Independently use a test metric with Fourier dependence exp(−ik·x): contract its linearized lower Weyl tensor with the *unprojected* field products and transfer the time derivatives off the metric by parts. A unit off-diagonal symmetric polarization measures its stress component; diagonal polarizations need a factor of two. The two formulae fix the signs and normalization; the second can drive all ten metric components without feeding in a precomputed tidal field.

Up to a four-dimensional Euler boundary term, C² = 2R_mn R^mn − 2R²/3. On a transverse-traceless metric perturbation the quadratic correction is a_C (□h_ij)^2/2. The kinetic Einstein term is h_ij □h_ij/(8κ). Hence □(1+4κa_C□)h_ij = −2κ T_ij in that sector. Reducing order on the massless branch gives the source T−4κa_C□T. The same full-tensor reduction for a conserved, traceless source follows by substituting R_mn=κT_mn and R=0 in the linearized Ricci-squared field variation: the divergence and scalar derivative terms vanish. R² gives no such source. No added massive spin-two pole is excited. Compare only at wavelengths and packet sizes where the derivative expansion is valid.

If d R_mn F^ma F^n_a is admitted as an additional fixed-frame matter matching term, its TT test variation gives −d□T, indistinguishable here from 4κa_C. The R F² contribution is absent in the TT variation; in this meridional-reflection-odd packet contrast ΔF² itself vanishes. These formulae cannot distinguish d from 4κa_C or a conventional contribution to c_W from an MTS contribution. Other unsupplied operators or changing the metric definition without transforming the apparatus are outside this inference.

**A derived launch coefficient.** Set K=kL along the equator, β=b², and take the critical b from the [launch null](NULL-DESIGN.md). Normalize out iABF_b where v=β/(1+β) and F_b=(2πv)^(3/2)L³ exp(−vK²/2). In the ordinary source the tensor coefficient is

    P = 2Kβ(K²β+2β²−2)/(1+β)³ .

At P=0 the wave data can have the same zero free differential tensor. The two higher-derivative coefficients in the *initial tidal acceleration* are

    W = −16Kβ/(1+β)² ,
    D = −8K(5β³−3β²−3β−3)/(1+β)³ .

Thus ΔA_TT/(iκABF_b) = (c_W/L²) W + [(4κa_C+d)/L²] D at launch through this order. This is a conditional prediction, not a numerical calibration. The expressions come from exact Gaussian moments of the two on-shell electric fields, their free time derivatives and the off-shell metric variation, not a polynomial fitted to data. [Symbolic calculation](evidence-001.zip) (archive member: `exact_vertices.py`) · full expressions (not bundled here). All momenta in these formulae are dimensionless; do not confuse K with the expansion curvature.

**Identifiability lemma on this family.** For every finite K>0 the unique critical β is in (0,1), W is nonzero and D is positive. Their ratio is 2β(1+β)/(5β³−3β²−3β−3). Its β derivative is −2(5β⁴+10β³+6β+3)/(5β³−3β²−3β−3)², strictly negative. β decreases strictly as K increases. Hence *any two distinct positive equatorial K*, each on its own critical preparation, give nonproportional columns at this order. Close scales can still be ill-conditioned. Unlike a numerical coincidence at a single zero this distinguishes the two specified source shapes. An absent interaction remains possible; this does not prove a detection.
