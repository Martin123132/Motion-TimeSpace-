# A metric test on the frozen Maxwell pair, without imposing the tidal answer

Use the finite plus and minus electric preparations of the previous sealed stage. Generate fields independently in real space, without importing its stress convolutions or tensor Green function. Fourier-transform all ten stress components of each preparation separately. Compare the saved equatorial modes kL=0.5,1,2; the three original oblique modes; and a rotated oblique mode (0.36,0.48,0.8). Integrate over t/L=0–2. Reserve the zero mode for conservation of energy and a check on **differences**: never invert k²=0 for a nonzero total mass. No galaxy or cosmology fit, no change to the low-energy action.

Use κ=1 as a common choice of units, not a coefficient calibrated to the desired tide. Reconstruct the two physical metrics individually. At launch use the spatially decaying Fourier constraint solution of their own nonzero energy with zero current, no free tensor perturbation, and zero initial shift/velocity. Compare a ten-component trace-reversed harmonic evolution with an independent synchronous spatial-metric/extrinsic-curvature evolution. Do not project the source or enforce gauge residuals by correction. Different gauge components need not agree; compare four-dimensional Riemann, Einstein and tidal readings. Also drive cold dust with the same launch density, no pressure and no current. It must reproduce the Newtonian acceleration gradient with that same κ.

Use the saved dimensionless tidal scale κABF(k), previously 2e-8 absolute on the independently differentiated tide and 5e-8 on source/quadrature refinement. Keep the density-contrast null at 1e-12 in ABF units. For the raw individual metric/gauge checks use a fixed absolute 2e-8 in source-normalized units (lengths in L); record maxima rather than only passes. On early oblique modes compare momentum reconstructed from the synchronous Einstein tensor (not supplied to the spatial evolution) to the saved finite-time values at 2e-5 relative. The leading cubic law is not exact at finite time. Keep every shared time, both signs and comparisons that fail. At t=2 on kL=1 the signed normalized tide must be negative although the source remains positive.

Negative control: remove only the differential momentum from the harmonic source, keeping its supplied stresses and energies. The oblique constraint must detect the nonconservation; a passing equatorial tensor projection alone cannot validate a source. This is an intentionally inconsistent diagnostic, never the physical branch. On the parity-even local branch the leading Einstein and Maxwell terms are shared with GR. This does not test a finite Weyl–photon coefficient, a nonzero motion background, or a quantum mass gap.
