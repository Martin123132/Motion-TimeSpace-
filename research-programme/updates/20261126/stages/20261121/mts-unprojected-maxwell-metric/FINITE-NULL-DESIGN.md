# Single-preparation, finite-aperture double null at launch

Keep the equal-width toroidal/poloidal pair, the original action and the shared initial metric data. Define p as Gaussian standard deviation along each Cartesian axis divided by L (not the three-dimensional rms radius); X is the centre divided by L. Sample the fixed Cartesian yz differential tidal acceleration, not a spatial Fourier coefficient. Average the individual local tidal readings on the background launch slice in parallel Cartesian frames. These are theoretical readouts, not an experimental sensitivity estimate.

After factoring out the common κAB source scale, the independently derived finite-aperture laws on X²=2/u are minimal=0, W=8√2 exp(−2)u² and D=W(2u²−1), u=1/(1+2p²), for the positive centre. W and D multiply c_W/L² and (4κa_C+d)/L² respectively; d=0 for the displayed matter action. Thus p*=√[(√2−1)/2], X*=√(2√2) suppress **both** the ordinary and the reduced spin-two responses in a single preparation, with surviving coefficient W*=4√2 exp(−2). This tests one common-metric effective action truncated as in ACTION-MATCHING.md; it does not identify the origin or numerical size of c_W. In particular this is not an all-operator null.

Freeze the following before direct quadrature at the double null:
- Integrate the free Cartesian fields directly at ±X*, orders 26 and 36. Compare minimal/W/D separately (absolute 2e−8 in reference units; minimal <1e−12 at exact centring). Halve the time stencil from .002 to .001. The two centres have opposite signed W; do not infer the negative reading by reflecting the positive one.
- A common translation d of both rulers leaks ordinary signal linearly in the *sum*, but not in the oriented half-difference. For Q(x)=2 C u x [(ux)²−2u], C=u^(3/2) exp(−ux²), Taylor expansion of that half-difference gives Q''(X*) d²/2. Test both .01 and .005 with the full Gaussian-averaged fields; the leading asymptotic ratio should approach four, not two. Compare the exact finite-offset expectations to the original 2e−8 tolerance separately.
- Test an independent error: move the two centres apart/together by ±.001 and leave the widths fixed. This does not cancel in the odd readout. Vary widths at fixed centres as a separate control. Registration rejection is not general immunity to calibration errors.
- Two outer-scale synthetic launch tidal readings of the independent metrics determine the two displayed linear-response amplitudes. Use only those two readings to predict all five channels at both directions, all times, all three preparations and both gauges, including the signed mixture. The predictions use the already frozen response columns; exclude the two input readings in reporting held-out data. Use the original channel thresholds.

No fits to observations, new couplings, refrozen older tables, changes to physical priors, or interference with the LMC calculations.
