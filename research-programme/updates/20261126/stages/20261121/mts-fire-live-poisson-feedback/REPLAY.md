# Local replay

Use the retained Python 3.13, NumPy, SciPy, h5py and requests. All numerical jobs here were single-threaded. No public upload or simulation-worker changes are needed. Verify the preceding seal named in `live_feedback_sources.py`; preserve all previous banks. Byte-identical writes are checked instead of replacing saved outputs.

1. `live_poisson.py` compares the direct pushed masses, analytical response, original source operator and controls on two wave grids and two radial representations. `wave_return.py` writes the predicted state/clock coefficients before advancing either comparison, then tests the actual Hamiltonian response on the common saved catalogue-nearby source.
2. `guard_motion.py` uses the eight locally archived gas/star phase-space packets. The two angular/radial interpolations are piecewise across the two exact clock radii. Keep the actual velocities and stored centres; the no-velocity calculation is a separate control.
3. `reciprocity.py` compares the finite-element force with the force derived from the actual quadrature Hamiltonian, then checks the latter against the independent source-position derivative. `closed_exchange.py --arm reset` and `closed_exchange.py --arm coupled` run the finite gravity-only pilot. The extra 16-step refinement can be reproduced by calling `closed_exchange.run(1,16,arm)` once per arm. `pilot_readout.py` and `pilot_readout.py --refined` calculate independent fixed-position controls at matching step sizes.
4. The four `thermal-s590-*.npz` packets are sufficient for the pressure calculation. Do **not** re-download verified inputs. Only if material is missing, `thermal_source.py` accepts an explicit future ISO-8601 cutoff less than four hours away; it checks native IDs against the saved raw hashes and reads the single additional FIRE gas-energy column per piece. `reconcile_mass.py` must precede `thermal_tide.py` when rebuilding: both original mass summation orders must match their respective stored arrays exactly. `thermal_tide.py` then checks independent determinant deformations and computes shear and compression.
5. `validate_feedback.py --seal` checks the derived limits, source and numerical budgets, both arms, step refinements, thermal receipts, and the complete local manifest. Work and energy entries in the closed-pilot tables are in solar masses times (km/s)². Work curvatures in the reciprocity tables have two additional inverse-Myr factors. Small residuals are measured against the ordinary kinetic energy gained, not against the larger rest energy or background binding energy.
