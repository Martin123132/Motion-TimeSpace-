# Can these pulsar timings distinguish opposite galactic flows?
**No, for either frozen preparation at its physical normalization.** This is an injection into mock pulse arrival delays at real positions and times, not a fit to the pulsars or evidence that the Milky Way is baryons-only. The stronger coupled preparation is still far below this experiment's sensitivity. The observed residuals remain unopened.

| Frozen preparation | Signed signal/noise after foreground removal | Best-case signal/noise¹ | Correct sign / wrong sign / unresolved² | Artificial 5-sigma sign recovered³ |
|---|---:|---:|---|---:|
| Reset | 8.28e-9 | 2.54e-8 | 1.25% / 1.25% / 97.50% | 99.70–99.72% |
| Coupled (inherited) | 2.28e-8 | 1.43e-7 | 1.25% / 1.25% / 97.50% | 99.69–99.73% |

¹ Exact foreground and sign pattern known, common noise absent, intrinsic noise and timing fit retained: an optimistic upper bound on this cubic channel for the same eight positions. ² Analytic probabilities for a known preparation. A forced binary choice is essentially 50:50. The unchanged 80% target requires S/N 3.083 at the predeclared joint false-alarm budget. These are not limits on all conceivable tracers. ³ Fraction across 65,536 paired score-space trials by sign, at deliberately unrealistic strength. No physical amplitude was tuned.

**What was injected.** Eight of the ten archived EPTA DR2 design records are inside the common support, giving 22,210 arrival epochs over individual spans of 9.27–11.01 years. J0613−0200 and J0751+1807 are outside the wave bank at nominal distance; neither was extrapolated. [The original public timing-design files](https://github.com/nanograv/discovery/tree/18ab4c010b2e8e09eb81fdb145b296da024659fa/data/epta_dr2) and [the pulsar geometry source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.15808) are not independent observations of a wave current. Every result here is conditional on the saved FIRE analog aligned with those sky coordinates, at nominal distances, not a measured Milky Way initial state. There is no direct shell-mass observation.

**Observation equation.** Let n point from observer to pulsar, d be their separation, v their relative velocity and v_perp its transverse part. The geometric range jerk is
`d''' = n·Δ[∂t g + (v_endpoint·∇)g] + 3 v_perp·Δg/d − 3 v_parallel |v_perp|²/d²`.
Only the first partial-time wave term changes sign here. It is minus the sightline projection of the difference of the full spatial gradients of the archived potential rate at pulsar and Sun. The angular Legendre derivative and the radial Hermite derivative both enter. In the slow-source, leading Newtonian approximation a jerk j gives a timing delay `j(t−t0)^3/(6c)` up to lower-order timing terms. This fixes the sign convention; this is not the previous oscillatory pressure/clock signal. Tracer velocities are a controlled assumption: common azimuthal motion with angular speed sqrt(G M_b(<r)/r³); at nonzero height it need not describe a circular equilibrium. For axisymmetric vectors the advective term on that slice is then Ω ẑ × g. We did not infer peculiar velocities, companion jerk, independent pulsar spin cubics, or a full retarded metric. Adding freely adjustable independent spin cubics could absorb the signal altogether.

**Ordinary foreground.** Gas and stars were accumulated at each endpoint from the 24 sealed FIRE packets at snapshots 589/590/591, with the same unequal-step centered derivative as the earlier shell calculation. All three earlier enclosed-rate controls reproduce at 1e-9 relative tolerance. This is a spherical ordinary-matter foreground, not an assertion of spherical wave stress or a measured Galactic force. The null contains only this ordinary foreground and noise. Each physical injection adds the same static wave contribution for that preparation and an oppositely signed current contribution. The nuisance projection removes the common clock cubic, the ordinary foreground pattern and the appropriate static wave pattern for all three competing hypotheses; sign identification cannot be purchased with the wave's static mass. There is no fitted halo normalization.

**Noise and recovery.** Used the released per-backend errors, and the retained intrinsic red, dispersion and chromatic noise, actual timing-model columns and fixed common red-process scenario. A cubic per pulsar is compressed after its own timing fit. The covariance of those scores includes the spatially correlated common process; this compression need not be optimal with common noise. Score = unit-variance projection onto the signed template after removing nuisance directions. Choose forward if above +2.2414, reverse below −2.2414 and otherwise unresolved. Splitting 5% across both preparations and tails controls family-wise false alarms without assuming independence (score correlation 0.9678). No noise settings fitted. At the physical amplitudes the noiseless scores get both signs right; with shared noise the forward, reverse and null classifications have identical counts in both ensembles. The raw-arrival tests independently synthesize the noise and add the physical timing delays before scoring, not after.

- **256 whole-array arrival-level draws:** baryons-only triggers a sign in either preparation in 6/256 (2.34%; approximate binomial SE 0.95 percentage points). The per-preparation unresolved counts are 251 and 250.
- **65,536 independent score-space draws:** baryons-only triggers either preparation in 2,047/65,536 (3.12%; SE 0.068 percentage points). For reset there were 809 positive and 836 negative calls; for coupled, 793 and 791. The same counts occur in the physical forward and reverse mocks. The noise draws are paired across hypotheses by design.
- The numerical checks distinguish a small signal from a canceled one: the two independently constructed field-derivative vectors agree at 1.99e-6 (reset) and 3.76e-7 (coupled), both below 0.005. Reversing the signed injection after including the ordinary and static contributions closes within 2.21e-13 relative, below 1e-9. The actual arrival-level score shifts agree with injected shifts to within 1.74e-14 in noise-sigma units. Even an oracle with the common red noise switched off cannot rescue this observable.

**Decision.** Leave real residuals blinded; neither sign is chosen. More draws on the same decade of timing would measure the false-alarm rate more precisely, not increase the physical signal. Next, screen one time-integrated observable such as the displacement or velocity of a dated stellar stream: first evolve both frozen histories against identical ordinary matter, then compare their separation with the stream's measurement errors and uncertainty in stripping history. Check that the histories cover the required age; do not extrapolate the launch jerk over millions of years. Commit to a data comparison only if that optimistic sensitivity is competitive.
