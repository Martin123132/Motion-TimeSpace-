**Why the phase mattered.** Simply setting the projected common phase to zero would give a maximum induced slope of 2.21–2.83 × 10⁻¹⁷/yr, comparable to the drift. Restoring the actual integrated forcing reduces it. Exponentiating the uniform phase alone gives 0.874–1.285 × 10⁻¹⁷/yr, consistent with the sign conclusion; this is a sensitivity check, not a nonlinear solution. The full ground cannot be dropped in a real measurement just because a matched control is convenient. No Galactic coherence fraction, measured foreground subtraction or matched undisturbed host is supplied by these data.
