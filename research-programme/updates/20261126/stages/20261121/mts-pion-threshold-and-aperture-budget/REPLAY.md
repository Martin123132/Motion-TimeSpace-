# Local replay

Run `python -B verify.py` in this directory with one numerical thread (OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS, OMP_NUM_THREADS and MKL_NUM_THREADS all 1). Requires the saved sibling stages and the retained NumPy/SciPy installation. Regenerates only this stage's numerical outputs; compares all files to its seal and the preceding stage's inputs to theirs. It does not download tables, rerun the galaxy calculations or start a service.

- [Frozen protocol](PROTOCOL.md), [equations](DERIVATION.md) and [results](RESULTS.md). The applied high-energy unitarity inequalities use a unit-flux incoming helicity column, not an extrapolated photon Born rate or a perturbative unitarity bound on fitted Wilson coefficients.
- [Crystal Ball Table 1, v1](https://doi.org/10.17182/hepdata.22923.v1/t1) from [Marsiske et al.](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.41.3324), neutral 50-MeV bin centers, acceptance 0.8. Local CC0 unmodified 4,029-byte table (not bundled here), SHA-256 0b926af9879652d5a6c2c8676ec4ee16d2477e43b3b7200a0325877c713ec924. Paper, Table 1 caption: 11% systematics below 0.8 GeV and 7% above, not contained in the data-table errors. Acquisition script is optional and refuses to overwrite an existing source file.
- [Mark II Table 1, v1](https://doi.org/10.17182/hepdata.22881.v1/t1), [Boyer et al.](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.42.1350), Table III and calorimeter-ID region from 0.540 GeV. Reused the saved charged table (not bundled here); reconstructed the contiguous native intervals from the published lower edge and centers: 30 MeV to 0.750, 10 MeV to 1.500, then 20 MeV to 1.600. Integrated acceptance 0.6, not a full-angle measurement. Do not add the limited-aperture total and angular results as independently measured moments.
- Threshold parent and normalization (not bundled here), [old two rays](../mts-photon-impact-transfer/RESULTS.md), old four-pion stress norm (not bundled here).
- Outputs distinguish rate boxes from signed-model variants. Grouped refinements, independent extremizers and the checks of the mixed-cut normalization are summarized in [checks.json](evidence-001.zip) (archive member: `checks.json`). Inclusive susceptibility (not bundled here) and vacuum correlator (not bundled here) are necessary-input budgets, not imported measurements. Published [thermal full-QCD stress data](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.02262) and [thermal pure gauge correlators](https://arxiv.org/abs/0806.3914) are not the requested vacuum total-QCD input.
