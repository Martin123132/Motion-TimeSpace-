## Check before importing a timing prior

In signature −+++ the principal equation on the physical polarization screen is k² a_i=8 c_W k^a k^b C_aibj a_j to first order. The factor eight comes from varying both field strengths and then antisymmetrizing the eikonal field strength. For Schwarzschild in a radial orthonormal basis choose electric Weyl diag(−2,1,1)q, q=GM/(c_light²r³). For a tangential ray with spatial wave number k along axis 3 the plane-wave quadratic action is

    (ω²−k²)(a1²+a2²)/2
      +4 c_W q [(−2ω²−k²)a1² + (ω²+2k²)a2²] .

Differentiating twice in the two amplitudes gives opposite slopes **of magnitude 24**, per c_W q, in ω²/k². The per-mode phase-speed slopes have magnitude 12; the polarization speed *difference* has magnitude 24. Exchanging Riemann or polarization sign conventions cannot change those magnitudes. An independent contraction of the weak-field metric curvature with an oblique null ray gives screen eigenvalues of magnitude 3q sin²θ (θ from radial), hence the expected radial zero. ## Same fixed source and receiver

At first order in c_W times weak curvature, the signed local slowness contrast has magnitude 24 |c_W| M_geom sin²θ / r³ in light-speed units.     |Δt| = 24 |c_W| M_geom /(c_light b²) |F(v_receiver)−F(v_emitter)| .

This includes the angular factor b²/r²; integrate b²/r⁵, not 1/r³. In the asymptotic two-sided limit the numerical prefactor is 32.
