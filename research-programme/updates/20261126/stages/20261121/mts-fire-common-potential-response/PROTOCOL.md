The exposed Milky Way bands, saved reset and full inherited tracks are engineering checks, not observational holdouts. No extension to other galaxies or public changes.

1. Start a passive wave at exactly the inherited state (up to phase and native norm roundoff). Propagate in the independently replayed reset wave's changing potential. This removes only the *additional* potential of its different density. The actual potential acting on this test wave is that of the reset; report what force its density would source separately, never add a second copy of the mass.
2. Differentiate the fully coupled Schrödinger–Poisson update at the reset, keeping both complex quadratures and fixed occupation. Use the shortest, phase-aligned great-circle path between measured initial states, with dimensionless displacement s=1 at inheritance. Freeze the signed radial and polar Taylor predictions (first order and through second order) and passive readouts for s = −1, −1/2, +1/2, +1 at every saved epoch and both resolutions before generating the nonlinear response tests. The intermediate and reversed preparations are numerical controls, not new physical fits. No coupling constant varies with s.
3. Check reset endpoint replay, original full inherited endpoint up to phase, linear common-potential overlap conservation, and the differentiated fixed-number identities. Predict force *differences* via the quadratic Poisson density, not a fitted gradient-to-force conversion. Use an independent full density-multipole readout, including exterior mass. Check the odd/even combinations and the separate radial/polar components rather than interpreting a small total-force error as a precise perturbation. Report what meets and misses the existing 2e−5 relative time-step tolerance.
4. Score full, passive, Taylor and static NFW on the unchanged signed force intervals using the same ordinary and direct LMC contributions. Do not use the six LMC alternatives as independent galaxies. If prediction checks fail, preserve results and diagnose them; do not tune the initial phase, change the tolerance or relabel a source term as a measured effective coupling.
