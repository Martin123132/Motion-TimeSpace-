# Charged and neutral pion data distinguish the helicity-two inputs

21 November 2026 · local · two real photons, both physical charge channels · the signed gravitational source, S waves, higher Born waves, datasets and apertures stayed fixed.

**Replacing the bare opposite-helicity D wave is not sufficient.** The signed dispersive reconstruction increases charged production where the measurements need less. A second, externally fitted amplitude reduces the excess in the higher-energy window but increases it in the lower one. The size of the discrepancy can now be localized independently of the two unknown isospin phases. No galaxy, gravitational or photon parameter was tuned here.

## Can phase changes resolve the discrepancy?

Summing charged and neutral rates over their overlapping range on a common angular aperture cancels the interference between isoscalar and isotensor D waves. Tested the full aperture, two halves and each of the six original Mark II angular bins. Take the charged 0.65–0.75 GeV and complete neutral 0.64–0.76 GeV windows, using the neutral-only strips rather than dividing or reallocating measured counts. In **0.2–0.3 in absolute cosine** the measured energy-integrated sum is **1.41146 nb GeV**. Even moving all points upward by their quoted pointwise errors (stat + sys where available) and applying the shared +7% to Mark II gives **1.70263 nb GeV**.

- The signed reconstruction's opposite-helicity lower bound is **1.87360 nb GeV** with *arbitrary phases*, even setting all like-helicity power to zero. Phase changes cannot repair this central-magnitude reconstruction on that bin. At least 4.67% further amplitude change in the pooled norm is necessary even relative to the favorable error box. This concerns the specified moduli plus higher-wave input, not exclusion of a field theory or a confidence statement.
- With the DP moduli the bound is **1.55872 nb GeV**: above the central sum but below the upper box. Keeping our specific like-helicity input adds 0.23197 nb GeV and makes even that lower bound exceed the upper box. The difference matters: for DP the like-helicity input still affects this conclusion.
- Over the full 0–0.6 aperture the signed lower bound alone is 10.8972 versus central 9.2286 and upper-box 11.0589 nb GeV. Pooling all angles would have missed the sharper diagnostic. All selections, including those with no decisive separation, are retained. This is a readout of exposed data with no look-elsewhere significance assigned. Joint comparisons (not bundled here).
