# A stress normalization that can be tested against measured charge running

The retained matter sector has an empirical test at two spacelike momenta. Taking the common metric seriously, the time component of the photon–photon stress response must be **(3.06 ± 1.01) × 10⁻³ GeV²** at the specified OPAL kinematics. An updated continuum lattice-QCD calculation of the charge running implies **(3.3355 ± 0.0139) × 10⁻³ GeV²** through the same identity. The uncertainty quoted for the latter is the propagated uncertainty of the published interpolation. These are two-current inputs; neither is an independent measurement of gravity. [Source convention, experimental conversion, spectral bounds and lattice-ready momenta](WARD-ANCHOR.md).

**Why this matters for MTS.** Before interpreting a transverse photon–curvature signal one should be able to reproduce this longitudinal stress using independent current insertions. The normalization of a local Maxwell term cancels at the chosen momenta. No MTS fit or cosmological prior enters. We also derived a subtraction that removes the same arbitrary Maxwell normalization at *other* momenta and even on the finite lattice for a specified Maxwell action. It does not remove an independent Weyl term or force an interacting stress test to pass.

### Which action was actually checked

- The [ILDG D450r010 record](https://www-zeuthen.desy.de/ape-cgi-bin/ildg-mdc.cgi?action=show&collection=ensemble&grid=ldg&id=mc://ldg/cls/nf21/D450r010) specifies a 128×64³ tree-level Symanzik gauge action and two degenerate light plus one strange Wilson–clover fermion. Parameters and three actual reweighting-factor checks are in production.json (not bundled here) and record-weights.csv (not bundled here). These are three configuration **metadata** records, not samples of the stress tensor. OpenQCD 1.6 is identified by the configuration metadata; 2.0 in the ensemble glossary is not its code version. The periodic run type in the [1.6 documentation](https://luscher.web.cern.ch/luscher/openQCD/openQCD-1.6.tar.gz) has periodic gauge links, antiperiodic temporal spinors and zero spatial twist here. The bare masses in this matrix normalization are 1/(2κ)−4; an overall source-independent rescaling of D does not enter the derivatives.
- The observable needs additional source choices not in the metadata. For TT use a unimodular shear, electric/magnetic rectangle factors sampled ⅔ at the averaged corners and ⅓ at the centre. For a time displacement use the actual proper-time difference across the temporal loop and an improved, four-point spatial-volume response. These are specified source completions, **not** a renormalized QCD stress tensor. For ordinary Maxwell with analogous rectangle coefficients the loop moments for powers 2, 4, 6 are 1, 0, −4. The leading spurious Weyl reading vanishes even before optimizing the corner fraction. At the ⅔ prescription bank 1 is exactly Maxwell-null on this slice; bank 2 has leading residual −(pa)²/40 in the divided readout rather than the old plaquette +(pa)²/160. Both symbolic and link-circulation tests use the inherited banks. The color action does not determine an external photon lattice action.
- For the unequal-frequency longitudinal Maxwell test, box sizes 32, 64 and 128 give relative stress defects 1.69×10⁻³, 1.09×10⁻⁴ and 6.84×10⁻⁶. The finite-source effect is visible, and decreases at fourth order on this sequence; the unimproved volume gives 4.82×10⁻³ at size 64. The flat current combination was calculated independently of the stress. The two periodic-momentum choices (not bundled here) show why a *continuum* Maxwell zero cannot simply be imposed on the finite-spacing data.

### What passed and what did not

The small controlled test uses two noncommuting, nonuniform color histories, their translation orbits, and prescribed zero-source weights 0.4/0.6. The matrices include the physical 2+1 flavor multiplicities and charges but neither the volume nor probability distribution of D450. In the convention σμν = i[γμ,γν]/2, the clover contribution for μ<ν is −c_SW γμγν F̂μν/2, F̂ the four oriented plaquettes minus their adjoints divided by eight. Photon phase on the Wilson hops is common to all tests. Compare color-only clover, independent Abelian clover (unit coefficient), and the Abelian phase inside the non-Abelian clover product. Only the last has a direct two-photon color-clover contact at zero external field, proportional to −Q² times the two plaquette fluxes and the anti-Hermitian color part. All three have exactly the same original flat zero-photon matrix.

- A uniform Abelian plaquette flux checks the clover sign and coefficient against sin(2π/5). The exact source tests include finite EM and color gauge transformations, pure-gradient insertions, flat γ₅ Hermiticity and interchanged photon legs. None tests the diffeomorphism Ward identity by itself.
- All twelve mixed derivatives, including contacts and changes of the normalized sea weight, match independent changes of the full source action. **Seven** of the sixteen original real-step estimates missed the unchanged 2×10⁻⁷ absolute gate. Six complex step directions cancel the lower-order step contamination in all twelve three-source cases, at two radii; maximum discrepancy with the analytic result 3.80×10⁻⁹. All four two-source cases already passed. Translation averaging makes single-source means zero; it does not license dropping products of single-source derivatives on individual histories.
- The two-history time-displacement Ward identity **fails**: stress = 0.7590−1.7256i, flat-current contact = +0.6048i, relative residual 2.238. Subtracting the finite-spacing Maxwell contamination still leaves a residual of magnitude 1.505 per site. This is a finite, prescribed measure with only translation symmetry restored, not a covariantly normalized continuum vacuum. It checks the calculation and exposes why this fixture cannot supply a physical normalization. No rescaling to force agreement.
- The same-transverse-momentum raw TT responses differ between the three completions even at identical zero-source action. Only the first TT bank is independently sampled on the tiny colored lattice (the last mode of bank 2 aliases); both banks were tested in the Maxwell control. The fixture numbers are not estimates of a physical CP-even vacuum coefficient. Raw comparisons (not bundled here).
