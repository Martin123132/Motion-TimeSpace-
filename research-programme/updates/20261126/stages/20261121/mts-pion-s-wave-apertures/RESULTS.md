21 November 2026 · private · 12 Mark II charged and 136 Belle neutral angular bins · retained spin-two photon and pion-stress inputs unchanged.

**The neutral rate failure is mostly a spin-zero spectator problem. The charged rate has a different obstruction: the opposite-helicity Born wave alone exceeds the entire measured central rate in both energy windows.** These are statements about these saved pion inputs, not a new comparison of galaxy theories. ## Charged observations isolate a missing correction without fitting a wave

In Mark II's 0.55–0.65 and 0.65–0.75 GeV windows the fixed opposite-helicity intensities alone contribute 110.41 and 98.66 nb, versus observed totals of 100.70 and 87.30 nb. The two helicities add **in intensity**. No choice of the like-helicity S or D, including arbitrary destructive interference between them, can fix that central-rate excess. The opposite-helicity aperture intensity must fall by at least 8.79% and 11.52% respectively to match those central numbers.

This gives an amplitude-level test. In each bin the square root of a helicity's intensity is its amplitude norm, with the positive phase-space weight. The norm of the required change cannot be smaller than the excess of the Born norm over the square root of the entire measured rate (zero lower bound if there is no excess). Combine these bin bounds in quadrature using angular widths and divide by the original aperture norm: the **minimum relative rms changes are 6.25% and 7.18%**. The bound is sharp for arbitrary binwise rescalings and free like-helicity power; it does not say the correction is a constant rescaling. Raising every measured bin by its quoted statistical error reduces the numbers to 2.22% and 3.27% (one and two bins respectively are still over). Unknown correlated normalization and systematic errors are not accounted for by these deterministic exercises. Charged positivity tests (not bundled here).

## A bound and an explicit sign ambiguity for the gravity integral

On the common charged/neutral interval **0.60–0.75 GeV** the frozen two-pion reference is **+3.6061 × 10⁻⁵ GeV⁻²**. Using the actual angular bins, allowing arbitrary complex S, both physical D waves and nonnegative opposite-helicity intensity, the signed moment lies within **[−5.0431, +5.5440] × 10⁻⁴ GeV⁻²** for exact central rates (rounded). With the available per-bin statistical and systematic upward shifts added linearly the outer interval is [−5.5508, +6.0517] × 10⁻⁴. These bounds assume the saved spin-two pion stress and the Born like-helicity waves above D, not a full-sky measurement of them. They concern only this window and do not cover a separate local curvature coefficient. Rates are not sliced in proportion to missing energy or angle. [Each bin contribution](evidence-001.zip) (archive member: `moment-bounds.csv`) · full precision (not bundled here).

This is not merely a loose bound straddling zero. At fixed **like-helicity** isotensor D and higher waves we explicitly reproduce every central bin with the candidate isoscalar D, its negative, **or zero** by allowing other helicity amplitudes to vary. A single fixed rule for the nuisance S admits scale factors −1.0030 to +3.6532 times the candidate; these are sufficient examples, **not** the complete allowed interval. The tightest bin for that rule is Belle 0.74–0.76 GeV, 0.65–0.70 in absolute cosine. Setting S strictly to zero instead does not admit the full negative candidate. The examples reconstruct bin powers after seeing the data; they are not replacement field-theory solutions. Explicit witnesses (not bundled here).

**Restricting neutral opposite helicity to D does not by itself settle the sign.** With only neutral S and D waves, physical intensity positivity and independent unknown nuisance moments per W window, all 10 windows admit positive, zero or negative isoscalar reference scaling within the stated statistical-plus-systematic boxes. Worst required fractions of those widths are 0.592 (+), 0.511 (zero), 0.569 (−). With statistics alone the worst requirements are 1.654, 1.628 and 1.734; even an unrestricted quadratic angular intensity needs up to 1.526. These are deterministic feasibility statements, not confidence levels or evidence for zero. Each reported minimum has a constructive upper and a separate lower certificate, with a gap below 7 × 10⁻⁷ box units.
