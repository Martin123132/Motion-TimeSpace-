# Free-fall echo and separation of sources
Use u=ct in kpc. Two observers start from the normals to the same launch slice at fixed physical axial circumference / 2π and distance above the reflection plane. Define the echo as log(emitted frequency / received frequency) for a coherently reflected pulse. The return leg starts at the actual reflection, not at a second time-zero launch. A coordinate-stationary or continuously supported clock is a different experiment.

For a first-order scalar potential V, leaving explicit inverse powers of c outside the coefficients, the launch coordinate displacement at cylindrical position (s,z) is k=(sV, integral from 0 to z of V dz). At time u the displacement is k-u² grad V/2, and its velocity is -u grad V. With first-order fields U and W, the mixed coordinate velocity has the form uL+u³J/6. Here H denotes the spatial Hessian:
- L = -H_U k_W - H_W k_U - 4(U grad W + W grad U) - grad Q - Bdot.
- J = H_U grad W + H_W grad U - grad A; A is the second time derivative of the mixed scalar potential at launch.

The mixed local log rate contains Q+u²A/2, the displacement of each first-order potential along the other trajectory, and the negative cross product of the two velocities. A flight of unperturbed length d along n changes the first-order arrival time by the emission delay plus n·(displacement_rec-displacement_emit) minus twice the scalar potential integral along the path. Differentiate the flight including direction changes, both endpoints and retiming; the dynamical integrals are -2 integral (u_emit+l)A dl and integral Bdot·n dl. See launch_forecast.py for the separately signed endpoint, Doppler and path terms. shoot_gr.py instead integrates the timelike and null Hamiltonians and intersects them at emission/reflection/reception. Its finite amplified coefficients must be extracted by a centered four-corner difference; for identical sources divide the mixed coefficient by two.

For a collinear chain use d13 E12/d12 + d13 E23/d23 - E13. Any piece proportional to d_ij times an endpoint difference cancels identically, including the leading Newtonian launch term. This does not cancel all the effects of ordinary matter. A jump between assumed radial density bins predicts [E_ij] = -2d n_r [Laplacian U] k_W,r/c⁴ for a receiver crossing that face. The singular Hessian in J cancels against the induced potential derivative, but the initial radial relocation in L survives. The jump is a property of that input representation.

For a *single* wave against wave-off, keeping the ordinary foreground common, density is proportional to |psi|² and mass current to Im(conj(psi) grad psi). The continuity equation supplies the scalar potential rate; the transverse part of the momentum supplies the shift B. At leading order c² E2 = 2d n·(grad V_i - grad V_j). At the next order c³ E3 = 2d Vdot_i - 4 integral Vdot dl + 2n·(B_i-B_j) + d² n·(2 grad Vdot_i - grad Vdot_j). Initial normal motion, both light flights and the time-dependent launch acceleration are all required. This lower-order single-state signal is *not* the four-state, mixed host–wave coefficient.
