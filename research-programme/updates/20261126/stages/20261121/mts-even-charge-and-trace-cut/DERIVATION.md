# A direct charge derivative is not an exchanged stress trace

Use natural units, a canonical real neutral fluctuation φ of pole mass m on the even branch, a canonical photon at the reference vacuum, and the conserved-source Einstein residue 1/M_R²=8πG. All ordinary charged matter and the same neutral scalar are present in the matched GR control. This stage isolates two different insertions. It does not fit α, G, a galaxy occupation, or a gravitational Wilson coefficient.

## 1. Match the two zero-momentum external scalar legs first

Hold the physical metric fixed before varying the effective photon kinetic term. In a unit-charge vertex basis write −B F²/4, so that e²=1/B(0), Z=B/B(0) and η=(ln Z)''=e²B'' at the reflection point. With a noncanonical coordinate ψ of kinetic coefficient K>0 the canonical second derivative there is K⁻¹∂ψ²; away from that point it is K⁻¹∂ψ²−K'∂ψ/(2K²). It is not a time derivative. For perturbative Dirac thresholds with a specified UV boundary,
    
    B_IR = B_UV + Σ b_i ln(Λ/m_i)/(8π²) + matching,
    η = e² B_UV'' − α/(2π) Σ b_i [ln(m_i/Λ)]'' + finite matching curvature,
    b_i = 4 N_i Q_i²/3.

Use total mass derivatives; any α feedback must be solved consistently as in the earlier threshold calculation (not bundled here). Do not count both a composite charged species and its resolved constituents in the same threshold interval. This is soft mass-insertion matching (compare [Kniehl & Spira](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/9505225)), not an MTS determination of charged masses. A common Weyl rescaling changes m, the matching ruler Λ and the physical probe Q by the same factor; m/Λ and Q/Λ do not change. Matching instead at a fixed coordinate energy requires running the boundary coupling to the transformed physical scale. Holding two inequivalent UV boundary conditions fixed is not a frame test; see [Nitti & Piazza](https://arxiv.org/abs/1202.2105).

4919 (not bundled here) and 4943 (not bundled here) specify a product action, measure and state at fixed public metric. The charged generating functional is then independent of the prescribed neutral field. With the same renormalization conditions, its normalized background photon curvature is **zero**, not merely below a clock bound. This is stronger than reflection, which only kills the slope. It is not factorization of a path integral *over* the metric. In the strictly shift-symmetric massless derivative-only block a constant shift also excludes φ²F². A quadratic soft breaking m²φ² permits, but does not generate by symmetry alone, a local term proportional to m²φ²F²/M⁴. Other explicit breakings need not obey that counting. A finite direct term or an independent charged-mass curvature defines a different matching choice. Neither is fixed by the measured charge at one vacuum. Graviton loops are not set to zero by the fixed-metric argument.

For one unit Dirac charge with true curvature κ=M_R²[ln(m_e/Λ)]'', and no independent UV curvature, the direct three-distance result at 1, 2, 4 cm is −4.48415×10⁻⁶ κλ/M_R² using the sealed response (not bundled here). This is a signed sensitivity, not a predicted nonzero κ. The direct result owned by the factorized branch is zero.

**A matching-independent check of the mechanism.** At fixed physical Q/Λ the one-loop mass term in e²B''(Q) gets multiplied by
K(x)=6∫₀¹ dz z(1−z)/[1+xz(1−z)], x=Q²/m_e². K(0)=1; K'(0)=−1/5. This follows by twice varying the inverse-charge determinant proportional to ∫z(1−z)ln[Λ²/(m_e²+Q²z(1−z))] on the even branch. Subtract two values of **B''**, not two logs with different uncalibrated denominators. A common local dimension-six subtraction cancels; derivative photon counterterms need not cancel. Changing variables w=1/[z(1−z)] gives K(x)=∫₄∞ dw σ(w)/(w+x), σ=12/[w²√(1−4/w)]. Independently the derivative of the Dirac pair rate (1+2/w)√(1−4/w) with respect to ln m_e at fixed timelike energy is −2σ. This cut cannot be shifted by a finite local polynomial. It is not a real-photon Compton amplitude and is not extrapolated from free current quarks through confinement. The numerical test varies both the physical matching ratio and a spurious frame curvature, and includes a genuine direct counterterm.

## 2. A nonzero channel with no direct charge derivative

At fixed charged mass, real photons still see a scalar source through the exchanged metric. Import the massive electron stress form factor ([Armillis, Corianò & Delle Rose](https://arxiv.org/abs/0910.3381)); the retained matching, sign conventions and transverse stress contraction are here (not bundled here). Isolate the **spin-zero** conserved photon stress, not the entire one-loop amplitude. It is proportional to (t g−qq) F₁(t) U, with U=F₁^{photon}:F₂^{photon}/2. Einstein exchange with a conserved scalar stress projects it onto −T_scalar F₁(t) U/(2 M_R²). The squared-momentum t in this vertex is the transfer between photons, not the total crossed invariant v. Define the signed unit a_e by the coefficient of (t+2m²)U at t=0, so |a_e|=F₁(0)/(2M_R²). Quoted signed projections are **relative to that unit**. They do not fix the sign of λ. For a minimally coupled scalar there is no improvement term in its stress trace.

Writing u=xy over x,y≥0, x+y≤1, the normalized massive form factor is
f(t)=F₁(t)/F₁(0)=(360/7)∫u(1−4u)/(1−tu/m_e²) dx dy. F₁(0)=7e²/(2160π²m_e²), and f'(0)=5/(49m_e²). This integral is finite at zero; the apparent massless anomaly pole cancels. It applies to two real photons, unlike K above. The isolated spin-zero helicity-flip vertex replaces η in the previous flip amplitude by a_e(t+2m²)f(t); this is an exchange, **not** a flat-space constitutive coefficient. Do not also insert an equivalent Ricci operator. Independent gravitational matching terms and the transverse spin-two response are separate.

**Crossed projection, newly evaluated.** For v>m² set D=(v−m²)²/v, r=(1−cos θ)/2, t=−Dr. The inherited tensor contact λ=8a_C Zψ/(K₀ M_R⁴) is helicity preserving and has only J=1. In the unitarity product it selects only J=1 of the exchanged helicity flip. The normalized angular weight is 3r² (one factor r from the vertex and one from the rotation matrix). Thus

    J(D) = 3∫₀¹ dr r² (2m²−Dr) f(−Dr),
    Im C(v)|trace × λ = λ a_e (v−m²)³ J(D)/(48π v).

This counts both intermediate helicity orderings, not two independent states. The reference unit includes no derivative contact. The previous contact × contact discontinuity is recovered by replacing a_e J by constant η. Each positive triangle element in J is proportional to (2m²−Dr)/(1+Dr u/m_e²), strictly decreasing in D for r>0. The projection starts positive for m>0, becomes negative for sufficiently large D (the D→∞ limit is negative, with integrable weight 1/u against the triangle measure), hence crosses once for the isolated one-loop amplitude. A UV extension of MTS is not used to claim a physical high-energy root. For the two retained masses the crossing is well inside the low-energy window. At D₀=8m²/3, the constant-form-factor part is zero; keeping the next term gives

    D_zero = (8m²/3)[1+(2/147)m²/m_e²+O(m⁴/m_e⁴)],
    v_zero/m² → (7+2√10)/3 = 4.4415… .

At m=0 the positive interval collapses: J(D)=−3D/4+O(D²/m_e²). There is no finite-energy massless turnover. The finite-mass sign change is a prediction about an interference component, not a negative probability. A nonminimal scalar improvement, an independent threshold contribution, or other tensor channels need not share it.

## 3. Transfer through the finite optical window, with the extra subtraction removed

Use the same pair and crossed upper energy Λ_w=1 meV, the same aperture transfer U_b(z)=z²K₂(z)/2, the same two very light pole examples and the massless control. Let L=Λ_w². Retain J=2m²−3D/4 first; quantify the electron-shape remainder below. The discontinuity now grows as v³ in this low-energy expansion, not v². Do **not** extrapolate it above the window. With three subtractions at zero a finite-interval representative for C, in units λ a_e L³ and with x=−v/L>0, h=m²/L, is

    (−x)³/(48π²) ∫_h¹ dw [2(h/w)(1−h/w)³−(3/4)(1−h/w)⁵]/(w+x).

Use the previous normalized D-wave projection B=15〈P₂[C(t)+C(u)]〉/(s²β²), s the physical pair invariant, β²=1−4m²/s. For an analytic quadratic plus cubic in the crossed variable the shift is c₂+3(m²−s/2)c₃. More generally a Bose-symmetric local polynomial of total degree ≤3 in the on-shell invariants projects onto this same affine subspace in s; it need not be uniquely assigned to a crossed channel. Thus **three distances no longer suffice** to eliminate the additional local term.

Average B over each consecutive pair of distances with weight sβ⁵[U_b−U_2b] ds, argument b√s in natural units, and call the first three averages B₁,B₂,B₃. Use four physical distances b,2b,4b,8b. Compute r_s=(〈s〉₃−〈s〉₂)/(〈s〉₂−〈s〉₁) from those same weights, not a fitted coefficient. The contrast (B₃−B₂)−r_s(B₂−B₁) removes **both** subtractions through cubic order. It does not remove arbitrary higher operators or a different interaction's own nonanalytic cut. In particular an independent RF² Wilson coefficient sewn into a loop can affect this projection; canceling an external polynomial is not cancellation of all UV matching in a discontinuity.

Independent limiting check: for m=0 and bΛ_w large, B(s)/(λa_e) has leading nonanalytic coefficient +3s ln(L/s)/(128π²). The Bessel moments give 〈s〉=(168/5)/b², so the four-distance contrast tends to −(3 ln 4/16)[3/(128π²)] × (168/5)λa_e/b². It is *not* the distance-independent logarithm of the direct two-contact case. Quadrature also tests the finite windows, a moderate-mass analytic fixture and deliberate addition of both polynomial terms.

Electron approximation: for 0≤Dr≤L, |f(−Dr)−1|≤Dr/(4m_e²). With ε=L/(4m_e²), the omitted normalized cut obeys |δ Im C|/(|λa_e|L³)≤w³(2h+w)ε/(48π). In the subtracted spacelike dispersion, (2h+w)/(w+x)≤3. Consequently |δC(−x)|/(|λa_e|L³)≤3εx³/(48π²); x≤s/L in either physical crossed argument. After projection and averaging, each |δB|/(|λa_e|L)≤[90ε/(48π²)]〈s/(Lβ²)〉. The weighted threshold limit is integrable. Summing all three bounds with the absolute contrast weights gives the tabulated integrated error (not bundled here), rather than assuming a relative bound survives a signed cancellation.

**Comparison and scope.** Standard GR with the same scalar and electron has the spin-zero Einstein vertex as well. Pure Einstein gravity without the inherited λ has no term of order λ a_e. A control also matched in the stress contact has *exactly the same* calculated term. The strictly factorized charged block supplies no intrinsic ηλ shape on either side. The 4878 cap on |a_C| is a perturbativity convention, not an observational measurement. Using it bounds only the isolated electron × inherited-contact signal. Quantum gravity, hadronic matching, other cuts at this order and independent local higher operators are not fixed by the four-distance test.
