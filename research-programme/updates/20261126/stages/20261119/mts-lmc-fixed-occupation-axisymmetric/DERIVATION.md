# Fixed number, not a fixed central density

Work on the previously prepared common-metric, positive quadratic Schrödinger–Poisson branch, in the same dimensionless time and mass-weighted radial coordinates. Its real stationary ground is g, H g=0, g†g=N. For a first-order **real axisymmetric** wave perturbation x+i y, the exact linear number constraint is g†x=0. Fix the arbitrary common phase by g†y=0. With P=I−gg†/N, B the saved multiplication by the ground in the Poisson coupling, and L the positive radial Poisson operator including exterior vacuum matching, the linear equations are

    x' = H y
    y' = −P[H x + B w + F(t)]
    L w = −2 B† x
    F = angular projection of g V₀,even / v².

The scalar removed by P in the second equation is the time-dependent phase rate. A potential constant at a given time acts only along g; it cannot change density. A spatially uniform force is either azimuthal order one or vertically odd and contributes nothing to this sector. No real density is subtracted by hand after evolution: d(g†x)/dt=(Hg)†y=0. Fixing N thus allows enclosed mass, local density and flattening to change. This is *first* order in the satellite field, distinct from the rate-squared/intensity mean that arose at second order for the bar. Keep the factor two in the Poisson source, not also in F.

**External field.** Translate each saved tesseral LMC potential following its measured-frame orbit and interpolate its shape in the satellite frame. Subtract the potential at the Galactic origin. For radius r and centre distance D rotate the axis to the centre direction; the separation s spans [|r−D|,r+D], with solid-angle measure s ds/(2rD) times the normalized azimuthal mean. Split integration at every satellite radial knot, using the unchanged values/derivatives and log-radius Hermite representation. In this frame the incident and displaced vectors have the same azimuth. Expand the source in harmonics through satellite degree six. By the addition theorem the azimuth integral for the Galactic axisymmetric harmonic l selects only the matching aligned cosine orders q≤min(l,6); sine terms integrate to zero. Sum only *even* l for this reflection-even sector. Shell coefficients are normalized with Y_l0=sqrt(2l+1) P_l and angular average one. The direct Cartesian source control performs the two angular integrations without rotating or using the addition rule.
