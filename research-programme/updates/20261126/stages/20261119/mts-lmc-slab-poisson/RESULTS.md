# What the fixed-occupation wave moves, and why amplification cannot fix the force
19 November 2026 · Local result, two saved preparations × six alternative LMC trajectories. No fit, new observations or changed force bands.

**The inner force has the wrong direction for an amplitude repair.** We independently reconstructed the present signed, linear axisymmetric wave density and evaluated its gravitational field. All 192 original radial/polar decisions (including the static NFW control) replay, with at most 1.90e-11 change in acceleration in units of 1e-10 m/s². Both models miss the radial bands at 6 and 8.3 kpc and both polar bands at 6 kpc, and satisfy the polar bands at 8.3 kpc. Static NFW gets the same satellite field but has no wave occupation or live halo response.

The fixed-number condition preserves the total wave population, not its distribution. The driven mode adds **0.384–0.814 M☉/pc²** between z=±1.5 kpc at R=6 kpc, and **0.209–0.458 M☉/pc²** at R=8.3 kpc. Its horizontal gravitational divergence partly cancels that extra vertical compression in all twelve preparations/histories. The previously retained force decomposition identifies the spherical induced density as dominant for inward gravity; a spherical perturbation gives exactly zero polar force.

Holding all other saved fields fixed at their final values, write a = a_other + s a_axis, with the driven state at s=1. At the **same northern inner probe**, the radial band demands s ≥ 40.83–85.63 (range of lower bounds). The polar band demands s ≤ −599.73 to −225.46 (range of upper bounds). The intervals are disjoint in each history, on both preparations, in both observing frames. No real multiplier, including a sign flip, puts *this shape* in both bands. This is an interval contradiction in the linear model, not a prediction that enormous excitations remain linear. It is stronger than reporting a poor fit, but it does not exclude different prepared profiles or dynamics.

## Where the two-radius difference comes from
For the previously saved two-radius diagnostic, compare the amount of extra vertical tide needed at 6 kpc with the maximum extra tide allowed at 8.3 kpc. A positive difference rules out a constant added vertical compression; a negative one leaves it feasible, not observed. MTS gives +0.02346 to +0.04113 and matched static NFW −0.02613 to −0.00931, in 1e-10 m/s² per kpc.

Of the **difference between those two model results**, **79.8–82.7%** comes from the radial contrast in mass columns, and the remainder from the horizontal-force divergence. Ordinary matter and the direct LMC contribution cancel in this subtraction; the ground and all retained live wave components are included. This is not a statement that 80% of an observed gravity discrepancy is missing mass. In fact at the reference inner probe the ground wave has a *larger* physical column (62.76 M☉/pc²) than its matched NFW counterpart (61.31), but **weaker** vertical compression: 0.1461 versus 0.2144. Horizontal geometry matters, and the explanatory fraction above involves *both* radii.

As a bookkeeping diagnostic only, substituting the NFW horizontal contribution for the MTS one leaves a positive tide gap on all 12 histories. Substituting just its column contrast crosses zero on nine histories and narrowly fails on three (up to +0.000286). Neither substitution is a solution of Poisson's equation with an independently chosen halo.

## Independent controls
An independent horizontal force calculation plus directly integrated physical column reconstructs all 48 two-radius compression values to **2.41e-8 absolute** or better in the stated tide units. The initial direct readout of the saved bar-potential spline had an absolute residual of 1.90e-5 at the inner probe. Reintegrating the source at the same angular degree removes the discrepancy; doubling the angular degree changes the horizontal bar tide by less than 4e-11. The spline readout is useful for its original endpoint forces, not equally accurate for their second spatial derivatives. The uncorrected comparison is retained in ordinary-independent-flux.csv. No archived potential or observation was replaced.

The bands are projected envelopes of the previously digitized tracer constraints, not six independent datasets or a joint likelihood. These results concern a first-order response on the two specified grounds. They do not fix galactic formation occupation. The next physically distinct lever is the *radial structure of the prepared wave*, not its amplitude: test whether an independently prepared, number-conserving redistribution can change the column contrast and polar force together while preserving the outer radial calibration. Keep the existing energy requirement as a gate; do not infer a new state from these bands and then score it on them.
