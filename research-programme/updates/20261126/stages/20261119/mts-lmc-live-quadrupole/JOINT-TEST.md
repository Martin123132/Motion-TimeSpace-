# A shared force test for the circular and two-lobed waves

Failure of each sector acting alone would not establish failure of their sum. Here the starting acceleration is reset to the *ordinary plus stationary wave foreground and direct satellite*, not the subtotal already containing either wave. When bounding possible sky signals put back the lowest m=2 mode as well. Work on the same fixed, linear stationary foreground, at the stated observing azimuth and radius. There is no transfer of freely adjustable energy from an unmodelled source.

Let A₀=2 S₀ be the fixed-number axisymmetric density readout, M₀ its stiffness restricted to the ground-orthogonal subspace, and G₀=A₀ M₀⁻¹ A₀ᵀ. E₀=xᵀM₀x+yᵀHy, without a factor 1/2 in the normalization of the axisymmetric real variables. For the entire complex m=2 sector, E₂=(d†M₂d+p†Hp)/2 and G₂=4 S₂ M₂⁻¹ S₂ᵀ. These definitions already account for the conjugate azimuthal order. For any real four-component direction u the largest possible projected correction at these separate budgets obeys

    u·a ≤ sqrt(E₀ uᵀG₀u) + sqrt(2 E₂ uᵀG₂u).

Use ceilings on energy, not just the energy observed in the propagated trial basis. For m=0 project the actual original incident bank at every time into the fixed-number tangent space. Certify λ>0 on that space by checking that the gravity-augmented shifted Hessian bordered by the norm constraint has just the one constraint-negative pivot. For f projected onto the tangent, ||f||_(M₀⁻¹) ≤ ||f||₂/sqrt(λ). The static shift is −M₀⁻¹ f (no factor 2). Thus the square of the sum of the norms of the two endpoint shifts plus total Bernstein variation bounds E₀. This intentionally conservative estimate uses the stored axisymmetric source and does **not** take the earlier reduced-wave energy to be an upper bound. For m=2 add the remaining-sector energy ceiling to a separately computed, conservative variation ceiling for the excluded lowest oscillator. Orthogonality makes their **energies** additive. All source-history data and coupling constants remain unchanged.

Let [l,h] be the original band box after subtracting the stationary and direct terms, imposing either signed hemisphere consistency or allowing an arbitrary reflection-odd repair. Its least possible projection along u is b(u)=Σ max(uᵢ,0)lᵢ + min(uᵢ,0)hᵢ. To find a useful u we use a deliberately looser ellipsoid since (a+b)²≤2(a²+b²); the reported witness is then recomputed with the **separate square roots**, not that loose ellipsoid. If b(u) exceeds the sum of the two supports for just one u, no combination of the *whole two sectors* at these bounded budgets can fit the four bands. Conversely, a ratio ≤1 for this witness is inconclusive. The saved score rows include all four witness components so the calculation is independently checkable.

Scope: zero added initial oscillation, two specified stationary grounds, six independent choices of simulated LMC history, quartic reconstruction between time samples, numerical spatial meshes, first-order gravity of the linear wave. A different formation state, independent source or live ordinary Galactic component is not bounded by this argument. The finite-box physical readout and these bands are not a survey-wide likelihood or a global exclusion of MTS.
