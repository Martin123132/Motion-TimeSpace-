# The rest of the LMC-driven two-lobed wave: a force calculation and a joint exclusion test

**Local result, 19 November 2026.** Starting from the two saved stationary grounds with no added oscillation at the first archived epoch, propagate the vertically even complex m=2 response to all six [Vasiliev MW–LMC simulations](https://zenodo.org/records/8015660). These are source scenarios, not six independent measurements. Nothing was fitted. We independently recovered the previously counted lowest two-lobed mode: the worst complex position-and-velocity disagreement is 2.62 × 10⁻⁸ relative. Only the *other* modes were added to the previous result.

## What if the circular and two-lobed waves help each other?

First isolate m=2 beyond the lowest mode. Reaching all four radial/polar requirements with this remaining sector alone would need **11,970–24,950 times** the excitation energy actually propagated in it. A stronger check does not assume the propagated radial basis captured all available energy. On each full spatial mesh, we integrated an *upper bound* from the LMC source history, including modes not propagated in that basis. Even this deliberately generous ceiling is below the minimum energy by factors **2,970–5,256**. Permitting arbitrary odd-in-height assistance for the hemispheric differences still leaves factors 2,721–4,859. These are energy ratios, not force ratios.

Then reset the starting forces to the stationary foreground plus the direct satellite pull. Give both the circular wave and the *entire* two-lobed wave (lowest included once) the benefit of their full source-energy ceilings; let their force patterns cooperate. There is a signed linear combination of the four force readings whose required correction exceeds the *sum of both maximal contributions*. For the primary represented source the margin is **3.91–5.30 times in force**, or **3.79–5.17 times** even if the north/south difference gets arbitrary reflection-odd help. Each orbit/ground has its own explicit witness, not a product of two separate exclusion factors. This test allows far more freedom in redistributing excitation than the actual LMC equations do. See joint-even-source-budget-0.csv and joint-even-source-budget-1.csv, the associated four-by-four matrices, and JOINT-TEST.md.

**Independent full-angular challenge.** The finite ground and wave bases give an exact angular product limit: if the ground ends at degree L, products with the m=0 wave end at 2L, and with the m=2 wave at 2L+2. We recalculated both incident sectors through these limits (36/38 on the reference, 68/70 on the added-stellar ground), using an independent radius/time sampling, 48-point split quadrature and every native satellite multipole. Instead of removing the lowest wave or estimating which modes get populated, this challenge bounded the *entire* m=2 input directly with a conservative stiffness estimate. It reused the original four-component witness directions. With eight time steps per native interval, the required force combination remained **3.82–5.18 times the joint ceiling**, or **3.71–5.06 times** allowing arbitrary odd-in-height help. At four steps the smallest relaxed margin was still 3.63. Direct Cartesian checks through the full angular limits agree to 8.46 × 10⁻⁹ relative. This checks angular completeness for the saved spatial grounds, not convergence of arbitrary finer angular grounds or all azimuthal sectors.

**Overall LMC contribution so far.** Relative to the stationary MTS foreground, the direct LMC pull plus the circular and two-lobed wave responses close **2.76–4.88% of the original 6-kpc radial gap** across the two hemispheres. They enlarge the 6-kpc polar gap by **1.46–4.14%**. These are signed changes against the original nearer band edge, not fractions of observed gravity. The separately attributable terms, controls and totals for all 192 primary comparisons are in combined-force-ledger.csv.

## Numerical precision versus model limits

- Eight independent Cartesian source samples retain both complex phase components. A translated analytic quadrupole and a constant-plus-uniform-acceleration null agree to near machine precision. On 24 samples away from both the source time epochs and numerical radii, the maximum relative shell error is 4.82 × 10⁻⁶ (6.19 × 10⁻⁶ restricting to the inner two probes). For six modes on each foreground an independent ODE solution agrees to 1.90 × 10⁻⁷ in final phase space.
- Increasing the force-enriched radial wave basis changes the added signed force by at most 9.51 × 10⁻⁸ relative; half the source time resolution, half the radius resolution and incident degree 16 instead of 24 change it by at most 1.03 × 10⁻⁶, 1.90 × 10⁻⁷, and 4.79 × 10⁻⁹ respectively. Extending the outer wave boundary from 30 to 40 ground lengths changes it by up to **0.0268%** (absolute 5.97 × 10⁻⁷ in table units). The latter is above the original 2 × 10⁻⁵ relative target; no band classification changes. These comparisons do not supply an infinite-domain theorem.
- The free-energy ceiling assumes zero initially added waves and bounds the source’s represented time history on each discrete stationary model. A pre-existing unrelated oscillation, nonlinear coupling, different baryonic foreground, or an independently populated formation state is a different experiment. The alternative initial equilibrium plus slope for the remaining modes is saved separately, not disguised as a zero-start run. The m=0 and m=2 sectors are not all possible azimuthal modes.

The derivations, full-angle cross-check, reproduction steps, and signed tables are kept in this directory. This is a local physical test, not a new fit, publication, or claim against the entire MTS framework.
