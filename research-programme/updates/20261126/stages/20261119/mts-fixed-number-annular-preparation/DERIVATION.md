# A fixed-number, outer-force-preserving preparation

Use the common-metric positive quadratic heavy branch and the real prepared Schrödinger–Poisson ground g at fixed N, not a fitted halo mass. Coordinates and energies are the retained dimensionless wave coordinates (energy convention without an extra 1/2). The imposed sources V₁,V₂ are the physical potentials per unit fractional change of the living stellar disk in 4–7 and 7–8 kpc. All other ordinary components are unchanged. Poisson superposition is exact for these ordinary sources. The gravity of the wave is solved with them. Absolute source coefficients and changes from an initially prepared state are distinguished.

## Which source histories are admitted?
At each measured radial knot the absolute stellar multiplier is 1+e_i·p with e_i=(1,0), (0,1) or (0,0). Require one positive common comparison calibration c satisfying c L_i≤1+e_i·p≤c U_i, where L_i=max(0,1−2σ_i/Σ_i), U_i=1+2σ_i/Σ_i. Eliminating c gives (L_j e_i−U_i e_j)·p≤U_i−L_j, plus positivity. The living stellar mass is affine in p. The half-mass aperture limits are also two linear inequalities in p because enclosed mass at each fixed aperture is affine. Intersect the constraints inherited from the annular census. The result is a six-vertex polygon, *not* six measurements or a probability distribution. Cross-check its supports against separately solved linear programs and its vertices against the original calibration inequalities. The zero-change point on the upper preparation is at a boundary; do not force a positive branch there.

## Stationary construction
Let H(g,p) be the wave Hamiltonian, including the change in the wave's own potential via Poisson. Solve simultaneously
    [H(g,p)−μ]g=0,   g†g=N,   Δa_outer,matter(p)+Δa_outer,wave(g)=0.
All accelerations are physical; the outer component here is positive inward. The first variation with respect to each source is the constrained static susceptibility X_i=−K⁻¹ P V_i g on the number tangent, with K=H₀−2 B L⁻¹ B† and P removing g. The inverse is implemented by the saved bordered wave–Poisson system, not by dividing by a zero frequency. See the archived symmetric inertia checks for both meshes, and the independent ground-number and force readouts in this stage.

If a=w·p is the linear drift of the outer inward acceleration including both matter and wave, the anchor-preserving tangent for changes (u,v) has v/u=−w₁/w₂. Its denominator is computed and nonzero. Intersect this line through the prepared starting point with the photometric polygon first. Test 90% of each available signed length. Hold u fixed and solve for g, μ and v *nonlinearly*, rechecking the original mass restrictions. No inner force bar or residual is an input. Coefficients are correlated by the anchor, **not** by enforcing exactly constant ordinary stellar mass. Stellar mass changes remain recorded.

For the energy cross-check write d=g−g₀, S the difference of full wave densities in Poisson coordinates. The change in the unforced energy on the number surface is d†H₀d−S†L⁻¹S/2, up to the initial stationarity residual. Adding the imposed potential gives this plus Σ p_i d†V_i(g+g₀) for *changes* p from the original sources. Both are evaluated, not inferred from sky projections. The original archived norms exceed the specified N by ~1.3–1.4e-12 in native units; the new solves enforce the specified N to <5e-15. The integrated radial number difference accounts for this signed starting defect.

## Arbitrary monotonic histories (linear response on the full discrete spatial tangent)
Let U(t) be homogeneous phase-space evolution, preserving ||(x,y)||²_E=x†Kx+y†H₀y with fixed phase and fixed number. Variation of constants about the instantaneous equilibrium gives the endpoint state
    Z(T)=(Σ X_i p_i,0) − Σ ∫ U(T−t)(X_i,0) dp_i(t).
A step contributes an impulse of equilibrium displacement, not newly created wave number. Each coefficient starts at zero relative to the saved preparation. For two independent monotonic changes, of either sign and at any rates, triangle inequality gives
    ||transient||_E ≤ Σ |p_i| √(X_i†KX_i).
This does not require synchronized ring histories, a specific duration, or a truncated normal-mode sum. It does require a stable linearized ground, no free initial perturbation in this extra sector, and monotonicity in each ordinary template. Extending the set to all paths with these endpoints is conservative when intermediate photometric restrictions are present.

Let O be the wave-force readout, S=D+OX the total stationary derivative including direct ordinary gravity, G=OK⁻¹O† on the number tangent. For arbitrary output vector v the added force satisfies
    v·Δa ≤ max_p [v·Sp + √(v†Gv) Σ |p_i|√E_i].
The right side is convex in p and its maximum occurs at a vertex of the shifted polygon. To bound an individual sky component preserving the outer force, apply this to v−λ e_outer: any real λ gives a valid certificate. The saved multipliers tighten this upper bound; they do not select a formation history or fit a model parameter. Energy-witness files also give lower limits on the energy required just to bridge one missed band after giving the static change its most favorable permitted value. They compare to an upper bound on *additional transient wave energy*, not to the orbital energy of the Galaxy.

## Finite-duration checks
For each source prescribe f(t)=[1−cos(πt/T)]/2 on [0,T] and constant outside. T is one or two outer circular periods given by the *previous* 17.58 kpc and 224.03 km/s anchor. These are checks, not observed assembly durations. A mode with frequency ω has q(T)=q_stat(1−Re J), q'(T)=ω q_stat Im J, where J=∫f'(t) exp[iω(T−t)]dt. The implementation uses a two-sinc expression continuous at ωT=π. It is verified against independently integrated oscillators and a piecewise-linear history integral on the actual spectrum. Use the finite-duration outer response, not the static slope, to determine v/u. Save displacement and conjugate tangent; check occupation, energy and the force readout. Matching the anchor at the endpoint is not an assertion that it remained constant in the past.

## Comparison convention
All reported force increments are in 10⁻¹⁰ m/s². Test northern and southern radial/polar bands individually and keep the two original frame conventions; these are not independent observations. The full finite stationary readout is relative to its own unperturbed ground. Adding an increment to the saved LMC histories freezes their previous drive response: it neglects mixed ring × satellite terms and does **not** evolve the satellite on a changed nonlinear host. The monotonic-histories theorem applies to the linear superposition. NFW is tested at the *same baryonic inputs* with both fixed normalization and the original outer force restored by one explicit amplitude change. Neither version is a live collisionless halo.
