# Local preparation challenge (before readout)
Keep both retained wave populations and the common metric coupling fixed. Use the published living-star radial profile and its previously used 4–7 and 7–8 kpc source templates; the other matter stays fixed. Allow independent changes to those two coefficients, rather than equating constant stellar mass to constant outer force. Bound columns with the original two marginal widths and a single common calibration, and retain the integral stellar mass and half-mass-radius checks. These are a sensitivity corridor, not a confidence region.

First derive the stationary outer-force-preserving slope from the field equations, without the 6/8.3-kpc bands. Use 90% of each nonzero signed interval along that slope within the foreground corridor as physical-source challenges. Solve the finite stationary wave equation with fixed number, inner ring coefficient fixed, and donor coefficient determined by the same outer-force constraint. Independently assess sky readout, occupation, residuals, radial changes and energy. Do not replace the donor solution with an observed-velocity fit. Check the matched NFW control both at fixed halo amplitude and with the original outer calibration restored explicitly.

For a broader test derive an upper bound in the full linearized spatial space on the excitation produced by two separately monotonic ring histories between the saved baseline and any permitted endpoint, with no rate or relative phase fitted. Test both foregrounds and all six existing LMC skies. This bound does not apply to cyclic pumping or arbitrary unknown formation fields. Superposition with saved histories is a first-order comparison; do not call it nonlinear joint wave–LMC evolution. Record remaining limitations and whether a calculation actually changes a signed-band decision.
