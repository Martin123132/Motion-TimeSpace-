# Relative motion from the common-metric matter equations

## Linear evolution and the part gravity cannot imitate
Use conformal Newtonian gauge with lapse Ψ, spatial curvature Φ and dimensionless peculiar velocity v (multiply by c for km/s). Let H be the physical Hubble rate in inverse Mpc with c=1, ℋ=aH, θ=div v, and prime here mean d/d ln a. For species i let D_i=δ_i,N−3Φ and p_i=a θ_i. D is the metric-volume-corrected contrast of the retained averaged density; with finite pressure it should not be treated as an exactly advected dust particle count. Both the residual cold constituent c and the averaged heavy field h have dust-like background dilution in this interval, but the heavy *perturbation* has pressure Π_h=δP_h/ρ̄_h. The original averaged stress, not a fitted galaxy equation, gives

    D_c' = −p_c/(a²H),                    p_c' = k²Ψ/H,
    D_h' = −p_h/(a²H) − 3Π_h,             p_h' = k²(Ψ+Π_h)/H,
    Π_h = c_h² [δ_h,N + 3ℋθ_h/k²],
    c_h² = [x/(sqrt(1+x²)+1)]²,          x=k/(a m_h).

These relations assume separate linear stress conservation, no anisotropic stress of h, the retained averaged branch, and common metric minimal coupling. The pressure is gauge-invariant here because its background value is zero. It is *not* an exact closure for arbitrary late nonlinear superpositions of real fields. The microscopic and averaged specifications are distinguished in the formation-interface derivation (not bundled here); prior metric constraint and radiation correction (not bundled here).

Define r=D_h−D_c, j=p_h−p_c, C=δ_c,N+3ℋθ_c/k². In the native CDM-comoving synchronous chart, C is simply the saved synchronous CDM density contrast. Subtracting the two equations gives a closed **forced** two-variable system:

    Π = c_h² [ C + r + 3Hj/k² ],
    j' = k²Π/H,                           r' = −j/(a²H)−3Π.

No separately normalized force, fitted scale, or metric reconstructed from future wave velocities appears. The same Ψ acts on both species; relative motion constrains stress and state preparation, not the absolute gravitational force. If c_h²→0, j is constant, and an initially identical state stays identical; an inherited relative velocity does not vanish just because gravity is universal. The C history still carries the gravity of **all** the material, including the wave. This equation cannot be closed on the ordinary component of a *different* spacetime without changing the problem.

Independent checks: read Π directly from the saved heavy pressure divided by its background density; also reconstruct it from the synchronous density and momentum using the retained sound speed. The accuracy of an integrated shared numerical equation is not an experimental bound on a fifth force. Solving the forced pair from *only* initial r,j and time-sampled C,H, instead of reading wave trajectories at intermediate times, tests the closure independently of reading final Π. The signed projections of the three superposed contributions onto final r are 32.43% from initial r, 19.17% from initial j and 48.40% from the subsequently supplied ordinary rest-density history on this band. These are signed projections in one fixed inner product, not independent variance fractions: e.g., contributions to final j can oppose one another. Never substitute the paired cold cosmology's C for the same-metric C without labelling the resulting large source uncertainty.

## Leading nonrelativistic and nonlinear checks
With coefficients C_i=8πG a³ρ̄_i fixed by the parent, the reduced common Poisson source is k²Ψ_mat=−Σ C_i D_i/(2a). The leading wave force-divergence term is k⁴D_h/(4a²m_h²). Baryon pressure, radiation perturbations, neutrinos, volume and other relativistic effects are not silently contained in this reduced Poisson source. Removing the baryonic force is a diagnostic intervention, not a permissible model recalibration.

A further check inserts the realized linear fields into the second-order **Newtonian** number and irrotational Euler sources.
