# Before evaluating tidal histories or launching any test stars

Use the sealed Pal 5 RR-distance cluster centre at 20.6 kpc and its measured bulk motion, same original ordinary potential and both frozen signed wave preparations over 586→590→600. No new fit. Compare ordinary host alone, reset forward/reverse, inherited forward/reverse. Compute both the direct field effect at identical points on the host baseline and the full effect following each field's own orbit conditioned on the same present cluster centre. Distances/times here are in a Galactic analog, not observed time-series measurements of the Milky Way.

Derive the Cartesian tidal acceleration tensor (minus the Hessian of the saved potential), check with independent central differences of the unchanged external acceleration, and compare the linear prediction with differential pulls across the observed projected half-number scale and twice that scale. Keep analytic curvature distinct from actual finite extent effects and C1 radial interpolation knots. Retain the 0.5% numerical vector/history refinement tolerance and <1e−9 centre return threshold. Record per-epoch deviations, not only global norms. Include singular cases and failed checks. Check the potential bank and orbit refinement independently at the available (2,1),(4,1),(4,2) settings. Measure maximal stretching, rotation and Euler term; a spherical cluster in a frozen rotating linear tide gives a force-balance scale, not a time-independent escape boundary.

Stellar constraints fixed independently of MTS: King/profile + shallow mass-function mass 4297±98 solar masses, Ibata et al. 2017 (https://arxiv.org/html/1708.06360v1, §§V.2–V.3); alternative Salpeter star-count mass 16700 solar masses, sensitivity only. Projected half-number radius 3.21±0.06 arcmin from Gieles et al. 2021 (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7617085/, Methods), partly based on the same imaging. Use this angle at 20.6 kpc, not 18.7 pc from a different adopted distance. No MTS calibration; mass does not independently measure unseen remnants. Fixed Plummer mass-following-light illustration with scale equal to projected half-number radius, while half-3D mass radius is larger. Use present structure as a *conditional* constant through the 111.16 Myr; not a reconstructed time-dependent stellar system.

To test time dependence directly, place tracers at one, two, three projected radii on six Cartesian axes with four perpendicular circular internal velocity choices each; zero draw, no assigned release dates, no body restarts or sign-selected stars. Start identically relative to each of the five backcast centres. Integrate finite external force plus the same moving Plummer potential, with isolated-cluster control, using the retained field and orbit resolutions. Track separations and cluster-only binding-energy sign flips/returns. They are not dynamically invariant escape labels, a population frequency, measured stripping times, or evidence for black-hole content. Test predicted radius/energy and signed differences at matched tracer labels; only robust quantities should be advanced.
