# What actually supplies the radiation-era gravity?

**The old compensated initial surface carries energy and momentum not supplied by its named matter variables. That is mostly a starting-condition effect, not a growing violation of energy conservation.** We can remove the unassigned component: initialize the metric from the named fields and particles, then propagate the trace equation without enforcing either constraint again. On all five saved modes both constraints remain accurate on both cold and heavy backgrounds. This does **not** preserve the old potentials. It changes what the model predicts, rather than polishing the precision of the old answer.

Constraint errors use the maximum absolute discrepancy over the interval divided by that probe's peak required energy or current; the table then takes the worst probe, with different energy and current units handled separately. Endpoint comparisons use the complex difference relative to the nonzero reference final potential. These are *perturbation* ratios; the larger values do not mean the total gravitational field changed by those factors. On the designated (29,1,1) primary, switching to the named-material launch changes the final lapse/Weyl by 112.93%/103.08% cold and 44.24%/44.77% heavy. Repeating with the 32-cell incoming neutrino distributions leaves the same conclusion. Distinct 16- and 32-cell original normal/coordinate branches are reproduced read-only; a new named-material start is unique for the same canonical matter state, not a new fit.

With the old total compensation the instantaneous unassigned current can reach **16.36 times** that cold probe's peak required current; the analogous heavy energy can reach **17.32 times** its peak required energy. Large pieces cancel. Evolving only the initial discrepancy as a shear-free, trace-free acoustic disturbance, adding it to the actual matter constraints and evolving the matter independently recovers the old gravity to the precision in the table. Holding the discrepancy frozen instead gives maximum errors of 1,029% cold and 134% heavy. The reconstruction does not read the later discrepancy. It identifies the old boundary's mathematical effect; it is not a discovery of an additional fluid.

### Can a real constituent do the same job?

At the original initial metric, we assigned **the same required energy and longitudinal current** to three explicit material preparations. The photon and massless-neutrino monopole/dipole assignments are fully fixed by their energy and momentum weights; the common displacement assigns energy and current in proportion to enthalpy, including the heavy scalar and massive thermal neutrinos when present. All reproduce both initial potentials. All evolve with the actual component equations; no fitted coupling or late-time information determines the input.

| Actual carrier | Cold: largest final change from old | Heavy: largest final change from old |
|---|---:|---:|
| Photons | 34.57% | 13.44% |
| Massless neutrinos | 411.69% | 87.17% |
| Common fractional density and bulk velocity across named matter | 92.41% | 67.58% |

Their subsequent energy/current constraints remain accurate to **0.01251%** or better across all tested preparations. These differences are not numerical breakdowns. No convex mixture of these three allocations reproduces the old answer on every probe: a separating projection of the complex responses guarantees at least **34.56%** on the cold (9,-2,4) Weyl mode and **13.44%** on the heavy (29,1,1) lapse mode for the original incident preparation. Treat this as a bound on this family of initial states, not all possible radiation or matter.

An independent native regular adiabatic mode was calculated at the **exact five output wavelengths**, not interpolated from the galaxy or input-wave archive. An arbitrary complex amplitude for *each output wavelength* was allowed purely to test possibility. It still cannot simultaneously supply the required energy and longitudinal current: the best equal-fraction joint RMS discrepancies range from **70.6–70.9% cold** and **50.1–82.2% heavy**, versus the unchanged 0.5% gate. Independently, the relative phase of required energy and current leaves at least an 8.03% joint mismatch even allowing an arbitrary real transfer ratio at every probe. This rules out adding a single homogeneous regular mode as the **entire** compensator. It does **not** rule out standard adiabatic initial conditions evolved with the earlier *quadratic* sources. Those sum many complex wave pairs and need not have a single mode's phase.

### Accuracy, provenance and limits

- The inventory reads the canonical scalar energies/currents, rest-mass and kinetic terms for CDM/baryons, photon coordinate moments and massive/massless neutrino phase space separately. Trace/shear sums and total coordinate readouts provide cross-checks. Independent unexpanded matter formulas check 96 local cases. Omitting photons or CDM breaks the 0.5% constraint check in both backgrounds. Dropping just massless neutrinos does **not** cross that gate on the cold bank: a dropout is not guaranteed detectable on tiny channels.
- The old first-order aggregate quadratic forcing and the sum of species forcings are *not assumed identical*. The origin ledgers (not bundled here) keep the coordinate conversion, transported initial residual, forcing difference and per-species balance defects separate. On the thinned integration some channel errors exceed the very small net material residual; do not attribute the last few parts in a million uniquely to one species. Two of six independently reconstructed heavy aggregate contractions miss a strict 2e-13 roundoff comparison (up to 9.13e-12 relative); the authoritative archived forcing and rebuilt forcing audits are both retained. Unprefixed preliminary balance tables match one of the two tagged copies and are not extra experiments.
- The 0.5% gate tests numerical/conditional prediction agreement; it is not a statistical confidence interval. No change to data, Gaussianity assumption, bank, amplitudes, Galactic flow choice, or public repository. This is early-time scalar gravity in the existing frame, not a revision of the Pal 5 result.
