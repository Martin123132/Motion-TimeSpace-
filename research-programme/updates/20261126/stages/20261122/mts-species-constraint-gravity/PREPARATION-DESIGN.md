# Before evaluating any repaired matter predictions

Keep the original compensated launch and named-material launch as separate controls. Compute their **unassigned normal energy and covector-current divergence** at the original launch from the component ledger and the two metric constraints, using no later readouts. First evolve that initial difference as a trace-free, shear-free conserved test stress proportional to a^{-4}, with harmonic density/longitudinal momentum transport. Insert it into both material constraints and evolve the named particles independently. Fixed or reverse-sign transport are controls. This is a test of the origin of the discrepancy, not a licence to add a new substance.

Second, supply that exact same initial energy/current through actual existing species instead: photon only; relativistic neutrino only; all retained species sharing a fractional adiabatic density displacement and a common velocity potential. In the last model derive the allocation from enthalpy and the canonical scalar perturbations, including the massive thermal velocity factor. No fitted amplitude or new coupling. Keep other initial moments fixed and compare the later lapse, Weyl and constraints with the original at five modes. At fixed slicing test whether a *single background time displacement* would already suffice or the initial comoving density combination is nonzero. A common fractional displacement on one slice is not the primordial growing-mode solution; distinguish those claims. Test the full incoming distribution at 32 cells as well as the original neutrino launch. Retain the earlier 0.5% gate.
