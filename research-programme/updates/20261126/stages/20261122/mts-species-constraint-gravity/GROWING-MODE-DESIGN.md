# Independent regular-mode test, before opening the exact transfers

The constraint discrepancy is a complex pair on each scalar output wavevector. Test the well-defined possibility that the missing material state is an additional copy of the cosmology's retained regular linear adiabatic mode. For differences of order-two solutions sharing the first-order source the field and transport equations are linear; a homogeneous adiabatic mode therefore has the native transfer's density/current ratio, irrespective of its primordial amplitude or phase. Keep the first-order source frozen. Use its material density **at fixed original order-two potential** (subtract the potential dependence of the canonical matter variables), and its physical longitudinal current. Compute the two exact-wavelength transfers with the native solver rather than interpolate over the missing last shell. Verify the model initial-condition setting and two native precision settings. Check the independent required density/current pair for collinearity in the complex plane; use the same numerical tolerance and report the normalization. Do not adjust the mode phase separately for density and current. A failure rules out an added single regular mode as the whole compensator on this slice, not adiabatic first-order initial conditions or second-order prehistory forced by two first-order waves.
