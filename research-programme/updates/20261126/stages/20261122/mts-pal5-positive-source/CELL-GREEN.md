# Untruncated force of the archived finite-volume mass cells
Follow up on the positive direct counts: verify that they belong to an actual Newtonian field, not merely a positive density with no dynamics. Treat each mirrored axisymmetric cell as constant in physical volume. Integrating the derivative of the Newton kernel by parts, its field is given by surface integrals of the *potential* kernel weighted by the density jumps across the cell faces. The r faces have area r² dμ dφ and normal (sqrt(1−μ²) cosφ, sqrt(1−μ²) sinφ, ±μ); the |μ| faces have area r sqrt(1−μ²) dr dφ and normal (−μ cosφ, −μ sinφ, ±sqrt(1−μ²)). Internal faces cancel for a constant density. Use the isolated Green function, regular at the origin and decaying at infinity; no fitted multipoles or angular cutoff. Integrate azimuth analytically, the remaining coordinates by quadrature. No wave or orbit evolution.

Predeclare the two launch sites, test the full-volume field and signed increment, and supply both cylindrical components. Check against a uniform ball, a far-field multipole calculation, resolution doubling and the local cylindrical Poisson identity div(a)=d_R a_R+a_R/R+d_z a_z=−4πGρ. Use symmetric derivative steps smaller than the distance to any local source-density interface and halve them. Existing 0.5% threshold; normalize a nearly cancelling divergence by the actual local density, not by a Galactic acceleration. Retain failures. A finite-volume field for the apodized foreground is not the absolute full Galaxy, and no representation change may silently enter an already saved history.
