# Palomar 5: what the stream can see, and where the wave needs more spatial resolution

**The measured stream does not distinguish the two frozen flow directions for either preparation in this test.** The signed histories cover 111.159 Myr: 5.73% of the estimated 1.94 Gyr trailing-debris drift time, or 2.22% of a 5 Gyr disruption calculation. These are two reference times, not bounds on a measured lifetime. The 425 Myr common and 1.066 Gyr inherited forward archives are not additional two-direction stream predictions. See [Odenkirchen et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0307446), [Fritz & Kallivayalil](https://arxiv.org/abs/1508.06647), and the [saved measurements and forecasts](../mts-pal5-joined-flow-586-600/RESULTS.md).

At 22.05 kpc the difference in line-of-sight velocity gradient (forward minus reverse) is **+0.06511** for reset and **−0.07981** for inherited, in km/s per degree, compared with a quoted error of **0.15**. Over all three distances the largest split is 0.645 of that error. A star separates by up to 68.08 pc in three dimensions, but no single sky bin shifts by more than 0.448 of its error. The largest independent-bin track contrast is 1.263 error units, falling to 0.168 if a free tilt is removed. This is forecast separation, not evidence measured against residuals.

**Can changing the stripping date mimic that signal?** With release varied ±10 Myr, the opposite sign individually brackets 68 of 72 coordinate targets (not bundled here). But each star can only have one release date. Using the saved three dates, we tested a stricter scenario: one common date shift for both synthetic tail launches, their two sky coordinates and their line-of-sight velocities. On each straight segment between dates we minimized the largest absolute residual divided by its own same-date sign difference. The maximum of six absolute affine functions reaches its minimum at an endpoint or intersection of two signed lines; both segments and all candidate minima were evaluated. This deliberately uses **no assumed observational covariance**; 1 means no improvement, not one standard deviation.

Across two preparations, three distances and both mock injected signs, one common date improves all six coordinates in **6 of 12** configurations. In every configuration at least one coordinate retains **69.6–100%** of its original difference at the best common date. The same classification holds on both saved orbit grids. Thus matching individual coordinates does not erase the joint physical signal, but the existing error comparison does not detect it. This is conditional on linear interpolation between three sample dates and an intentionally restrictive common-release pair; actual stars need not leave together. Both grids, individual and paired releases (not bundled here).

## Gravity responsible for stripping: corrected ordinary source versus original, not one flow minus the other

Starting from identical wave states, changing only the spatial representation of ordinary gravity tests an independently predicted change in the initial rate of wave redistribution. The old global wave space admits too little small-scale angular structure. In the 19.235 pc ball it gives the following **curvature of the mass change** (solar masses/Myr²); these are not stream velocities or a new orbit fit.

| State | Frozen continuum | Global wave basis | Locally resolved |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Reset forward | −0.098252 | +0.002150 | −0.098289 |
| Reset reverse | −0.097740 | +0.002139 | −0.097778 |
| Inherited forward | −0.097548 | +0.002135 | −0.097586 |
| Inherited reverse | −0.097956 | +0.002143 | −0.097994 |

All twelve global moving-aperture responses miss the unchanged 0.5% threshold. The much finer source quadrature changes these twelve discrete derivatives by at most 0.000061%; it cannot fix their sign. One whole-domain correction comparison still misses narrowly (0.5064%). Time refinement of the saved longer evolution misses all 36 signal comparisons (worst 16.11%). Propagating paired amplitudes for much shorter times instead agrees with the global discrete derivatives to 0.0144%: time convergence alone is not evidence that the spatial operator is right.

The retained angular product through degree 68 represents the change in ordinary mass inside the innermost sphere as **+3.57** solar masses, versus **−114.32** from the ordinary source itself. In the tested sharp harmonic expansion the last failing even degrees were 918, 658 and 522 from smallest to largest sphere. These numbers diagnose that particular representation; they are not minimum requirements on every solver. Angular tests (not bundled here).

Independent meridional propagation of the actual wave and its density, with physical source gravity resolved before projection, recovers all four states and all three apertures to **better than 0.089%**, over **0.03125–0.125 year**. The finite-time kinetic experiment uses fixed centres; the moving readout adds the independent initial observer-acceleration term. It does not integrate the repaired field over 111 Myr, or even the 62,500-year long coarse test. The instantaneous gravity average is determined by enclosed mass, not the full acceleration vector.
