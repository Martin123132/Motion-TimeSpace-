The tidal potential is A(R²−2z²)/2: its Laplacian vanishes, its plane circular-speed-squared term is A R², and its vertical potential rise to height h is −A h². Thus an outward radial tide (A<0) costs vertical compression, not vertical support of the opposite sign. Test whether any tide and sphere work, including arbitrarily thin positive shells.
