## A coupling we can now calculate

The stellar runs above reuse the saved waves. To determine the leading effect of gravity back on the wave itself, we derived the initial change in **wave mass inside a sphere moving with the cluster**, from continuity and the wave momentum equation. It is a density-weighted surface integral of the change in relative ordinary acceleration; no new coupling or fitted occupation is required. The corresponding sphere-averaged gravitational acceleration follows from Gauss's law. [Derivation](DERIVATION.md).

At radius 19.235 pc the four prepared balls initially contain 449.14–452.38 solar masses of wave matter in this model. The corrected-minus-original enclosed wave-mass curvature ranges from **−0.09825 to −0.09755 solar masses/Myr²**; the fractional curvature is about **−2.172e−4/Myr²** in all four. Reset-forward implies −0.001142 (km/s)²/kpc/Myr² in the curvature of the sphere-averaged inward wave acceleration. This is not an observed mass, an instantaneous mass decrement, or a later-time wave replay. All 48 surface, initial-state refinement, potential and mass-decomposition checks pass the same tolerance; 27 independent exact-Gaussian evolution checks pass.
