# Weak-field number current and Newtonian force: two independent tests

**Frame and surface.** Use the original 544 → 558 → 572 axisymmetric complex wave in its terminal-axis frame, on the old interpolated ordinary forcing with self-gravity. The independently reconstructed catalogue potential V is used to define a diagnostic control surface, *not* as an additional force acting on those saved trajectories. An interior-ball bound, a rigorous enclosing-sphere potential lower bound above E and a positive lower bound on the radial derivative in between establish a host-connected star-shaped surface in the reconstructed field, not just sampled ray intersections. For a monotone truncated spherical density, the force-gradient operator norm at separation d is ≤2Gm/d³ (use the radial and transverse eigenvalues and ρ≤mean interior density). **Independent force bound at the observation epoch.** Use the archived GD-1 observable at r=√(11.9²+7.3²) kpc and its SHAPE, not a spherical enclosed-mass assumption. With wave density unchanged inside R>r and at most the same existing exterior mass M redistributed outside R, write Q_old for the signed *old exterior* inward force times r²/G. The largest possible decrease of the total inward proxy is Q_old+M K_max. The observed missing decrease is already fixed by the saved total plus the corresponding stars minus the published upper interval. No stream age extrapolation.

- Arbitrary positive exterior in a fixed frame: K_max=r²/(R−r)². In a host-centred differential frame it is r²[(R−r)⁻²−R⁻²]. Both upper bounds follow from the Newton kernel and are attained as suprema by concentrating at the nearest aligned point (not a realizable wave requirement).
- Inversion symmetry about the host: K_max=(r²/2)[(R−r)⁻²−(R+r)⁻²]. The inward/outward radial Legendre series for an antipodal pair has only even degrees and each even Legendre polynomial is at most 1. Equality is approached by the aligned pair. The saved wave has zero force at the centre, so Q_old is the same in both frames.
- For density axisymmetric about the saved axis *and* inversion symmetric: a mass ring and its reflected ring at source inclination cosine η yield the outward kernel Σ_{ℓ≥2 even} ℓ(r/R)^(ℓ+1)Pℓ(μ_probe)Pℓ(η).
