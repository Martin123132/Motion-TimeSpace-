# Direct Newtonian test, specified before computing accelerations
At the present data-conditioned positions use the endpoint of the retained late 590→600 bank. Compare the two signs at the *same* point and velocity, not the two separated mock trajectories. In heliocentric inertial kinematics with an accelerating solar origin, d(v_los)/dt=n·(a_star−a_sun)+|v_rel,perp|²/D. The perspective term cancels between signs at the same state. The common ordinary host also cancels. Do not evaluate the solar acceleration outside the radial field table. Remove an arbitrary constant observer acceleration by projecting the vector of LOS accelerations off the three Cartesian sky directions; optionally also remove one common spectrograph drift. Whiten by independent quoted per-star errors for an explicitly hypothetical pair of spectra with equal single-epoch errors, not a claim of existing repeated measurements. Report whole forward-minus-reverse split and the half-split versus either sign midline. Include raw and observer-independent magnitude, unweighted and precision-weighted removal. Forecast a ten-year velocity change by a first-order Taylor expansion at fixed present state, not by extrapolating 111 Myr or by fitting the observed velocities. Compare original resolution factors 2 and 4 with the retained 0.5% vector criterion. Independently difference Newtonian potential along the sightline to check sign, geometry, time and unit factors. No isotope, line-formation, binary, calibration or membership noise modeled; optimistic sensitivity only.
