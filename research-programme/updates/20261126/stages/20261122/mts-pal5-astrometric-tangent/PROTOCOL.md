# Frozen design — Palomar 5 line-of-sight velocity from five coordinates
Before importing any velocities from the saved Kuzma extended table: pin the 56-file RR experiment and observer. Use only its 17 published-tail RR positions/distances/PMs for the construction. The two MTS signs and both preparations share this experiment: neither supplies a velocity or gets its own optimized curve. This tests the missing initial-state prescription, not directly a force model.

With right ascension as parameter, fit declination and distance separately by ordinary least squares to global quadratics and both PM components to lines (centred/scaled RA). No RV, orbit or progenitor anchor. These are empirical ridge estimates, not derived matter parameters. Use the same sky frame and solar velocity throughout. From the Galactic-rest tangency condition project measured transverse space motion onto the sky tangent, predict heliocentric LOS speed using the distance derivative, and report the remaining normal velocity. Check the normal component on the individual RR PMs and distances, not just the smoothed velocity curve. This is a necessary consistency condition only. For the external catalogue use smoothed RR PMs and distances, sky ridge at each target RA, not the target star's PM, distance or velocity. Targets must lie within the measured RA span on their own side of the cluster and outside 11.2 arcmin; do not select on agreement, impose no new velocity or perpendicular offset cut. Report offsets from the ridge. Freeze per-row predictions, coverage and data-independent checks to disk before decoding target velocity columns. Include separate per-arm, per-source and K22-only comparisons, unweighted bias/RMS/median absolute residual plus quoted instrumental errors. Published membership used velocities, and older surveys overlap the legacy gradient. Distinct stars and withheld values for this calculation do not make a blind population sample. The published -58.7+1.20*(RA-229.018) serves only as an exposed comparator. No refit, slope tuning, ranking freedoms or outlier removal after unblinding.
 
Predeclared alternatives: same sky track/PM with a linear and cubic distance ridge; same distance/PM with cubic sky ridge; quadratic rather than linear PM ridge. All are controls, not candidates to select. Primary empirical sensitivity: leave out each of the 17 RR in turn and recompute the entire predicted curve; mark targets no longer inside that arm's support. Report all and bracketed-only deletes. Separately propagate the published individual 1% random distance approximation, both correlated DR2 PM components, plus a shared ±2% distance scale; input-propagation errors are not total prediction errors. Preserve shared-track covariance across targets. For individual cross-track offsets include direct PM measurement uncertainty; report sensitivity to track geometry separately. Check the central finite differences with half and quarter steps, with 0.5% relative tolerance in the RMS of the marginal uncertainties. Require identical frozen products before comparing spectra; do not treat a missed precision check as evidence. No numerical wave bank or orbit evolution changes.

Also preregister a post-unblinding diagnostic: at the observed radial velocity infer the distance derivative necessary for tangency, keeping the original track, transverse rate and observer. Near-zero rates cannot be inverted; flag |sky-tangent speed| <10 km/s rather than dividing. Do not feed this derivative back into the frozen forecast or count it as a prediction.
