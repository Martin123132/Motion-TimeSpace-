# Reading the numerical refinements
Keep the retained 0.005 relative criterion. No acceptance based on a large common background when the target is a small difference. Report a zero as a null only for a symmetry or explicitly tested zero, not for a missing source.

- Compare the physical vectors at the 25 sites (centre included), and separately compare the 24 star-minus-centre vectors for each preparation and sign. The fixed and accelerating-frame fields answer different questions. Report actual individual release-star work coefficients as well as the RMS within each 24-star sphere; a convergent vector norm does not automatically resolve a tiny component along a velocity.
- Preserve the old absolute Newton-source orders 10 and 14 and the original lower global angular resolutions. Before scoring order 18 use the same positions, numerical tolerance and outputs for every sign/arm. The new direct integration pools both position lists, includes their union as quadrature breakpoints, and therefore checks order plus integration partition. It is not an independent observation or a formal error enclosure.
- For each final signal check direct 14 versus 18, whole-domain angular 476 versus 612, changed radial/angular quadrature at degree 476 against the final setup, and saved input waves of 140 versus 280 steps. For the latter two angular/radial tests compare on the common order-14 local drive, preserving exact comparison settings; compare the finest direct change separately. For sensitive signed differences compare *differences of the two signs* before applying the criterion. Treat a row as supported by these tested refinements only if every relevant comparison passes; retain each individual failure.
- For the leading weak-metric redshift at fixed clock positions in the chosen frame, report the potential difference with the linear term from the centre acceleration removed. It has units Myr⁻² after dividing by c². Check both the direct potential formula and integrating the solved vector along a straight path, with a broken path where available. Do not interpret this as a measured redshift, a free-star clock history, or a replacement for relativistic photon propagation. A failed near-cancellation stays unresolved.
- For the boundary match use both the isolated potential and its radial derivative, two different matching radii, and independent direct integration of the source moments. An algebra error in the first implementation of the boundary-force route, substituting l+1 for l in D(B), is retained in the original transfer outputs. The corrected matched outputs and their checks supersede that route. Moving the matching surface changes which material is exterior, so compare the total fields, not their decompositions alone.
