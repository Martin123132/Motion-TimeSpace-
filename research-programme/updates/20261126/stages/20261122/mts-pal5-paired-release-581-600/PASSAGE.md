# Separate, dynamics-defined release-clock test

The midpoint injections in the primary test are controls, not pericentre identifications. Before examining the orbital turning points or their velocity contrasts, define an alternative: find every interior inward-to-outward crossing of r·v=0 in 581→586. Use the first local minimum only if both signs and their mean have a single matching minimum; report absent/ambiguous/boundary-limited cases. Infer the times from the simulated orbits, not observed velocity differences. Keep the nominal 22.05 kpc for this additional direct-time test, both preparations, same fixed trial-star launch, no new parameters. Compute endpoint velocities for (i) common mean-orbit passage time and (ii) each sign's own passage time, and ±10 Myr offsets where both clocks are supported. Interrogate both signs under matched assumptions; this is not a declaration that all stars escaped at pericentre. Verify turning-point stability and the freshly computed velocity contrasts on the two finest orbit step choices, with the same 0.005 relative rule. Test the effect of allowing one lag common to the two stars; do not add this forecast as an independent observation.
