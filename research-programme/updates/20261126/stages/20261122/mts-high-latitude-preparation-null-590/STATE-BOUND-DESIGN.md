# Positive result to attempt after projection of the saved histories

The source updates change the radial GD-1 force in the wrong direction, but this by itself is not a no-go for other sources. Bound the change in that same *full radial force*, not just the enclosed monopole, caused by any normalized state within the phase-angle radius actually attained by the saved source interventions. Do this in the retained even axisymmetric angular space; do not assume that an arbitrary future state has the small quadrupole seen today. Include the exterior mass and exact Green weights. Prove the inequality for pure states, test by direct readout of saved formation states and by unrelated positive controls, and derive a lower bound on the state displacement needed by GD-1. A lower bound on the entire wave-state displacement is not a numerical bound on coupling, a prohibition of different earlier formation, or a posterior probability. Do not declare the sampled formation changes to be a universal maximum.
