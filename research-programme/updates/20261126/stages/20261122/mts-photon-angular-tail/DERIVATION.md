# Scalar output of the photon angular hierarchy

Take one ordered input pair with angular field leg **p**, metric or velocity leg **q**, output **k**=**p**+**q**, and h the cosine between k and p. The intensity basis is (−i)^j(2j+1)P_j. The electric polarization screen basis is (−i)^(j−2)(2j+1)(1−μ²)P''_j/[j(j−1)]. Rotation to the output axis gives the scalar diagonal projection P_l(h). Multiplication by the photon direction contracted with a wavevector connects only j=l±1. Write e=+1 for j=l−1, −1 for j=l+1. The real coefficients in the Fourier recurrence are Np=e β p P_l(h) and Nq=e β k P_j(h)−Np. For the lower neighbour β=l/(2l+1); for the upper neighbour it is (l+1)/(2l+1) for intensity and (l+3)(l−1)/[(l+1)(2l+1)] for polarization. For a screen gradient contracted with the gradient of the dipole the sphere product rule multiplies Nq by d=[2+j(j+1)−l(l+1)]/2. The constant spin shift cancels in the difference of Laplacian eigenvalues.

For l≥3 sum the ordered terms G [Np+(4−d)Nq] A_j and opacity × V_b Nq A_j, and the diagonal rate term −opacity × R P_l(h) A_l, with the metric/velocity/rate on q and A on p. G=A−B in the retained metric notation, R is the lapse plus fractional electron density, with no independent ionization perturbation here. The electron-rest-frame scattering gain has only a monopole and quadrupole. Boosting the outgoing linear quadrupole adds opacity × V_b (3−d) Nq (M2/10+3E2/5) for (l,j)=(3,2) only. These are low-energy Thomson, scalar-output statements; thermal scattering, magnetic incident polarization and independent vector/tensor output are not inferred. See [implementation](evidence-001.zip) (archive member: `multipoles.py`) for Fourier signs and polarization normalizations and [Pitrou](https://arxiv.org/abs/0809.3036) for the general second-order polarized Boltzmann framework.

[Incident evolution](evidence-001.zip) (archive member: `incident_tail.py`) starts before the comparison, using the actual native third moments. The l=4 result is independently reconstructed from the native third-moment time derivatives. [Independent controls](evidence-001.zip) (archive member: `angular_independent.py`) project angular functions and the unexpanded moving-electron kernel, not just the same coefficient recurrence. [Response](evidence-001.zip) (archive member: `respond_tail.py`) evolves both sectors on identical launch conditions, increasing direct source order from 2 to 16, and compares the actual endpoint change with the saved octupole kernels. Neither early opacity attenuation nor these small response errors bound collisionless species.
