# Higher photon moments in the saved gravity histories

On the five preselected radiation-era modes, higher photon moments do not shift the final lapse or Weyl perturbation across the frozen 0.5% threshold. Relative to the quadrupole-only reference on the primary (29,1,1) mode, the lapse changes by 0.10054% (cold) and 0.06927% (heavy); the Weyl changes by 0.09090% and 0.06950%. Percentages are relative to the corresponding nonzero final perturbation, not relative to the total gravitational field.

The largest complex endpoint change over the five modes and both observables is 0.12331% cold or 0.06951% heavy (rounded upward). On the heavy primary the change in the paired intensity/polarization octupoles reaches about 0.804 of the reference octupole scale; the frozen gain bound requires at least 1.99 times that scale for a 0.5% lapse effect. The inferred force was checked independently by integrating the earlier causal response kernels against these actual signed octupoles. The angular-order, timestep and conserved-equation checks are in the cold endpoints (not bundled here) and heavy endpoints (not bundled here); compare the complex difference, not just its real projection.
