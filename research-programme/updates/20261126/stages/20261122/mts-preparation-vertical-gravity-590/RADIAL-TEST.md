# Companion radial consistency test, before calculating model speeds here

The same potential that gives the vertical rise must give v_c²=R ∂RΦ on the plane. Compare all Ou et al. MNRAS 2024 (arXiv:2303.12838), Table 1 central radii within 1 kpc of their own R_sun=8.178 kpc. The published table is open and the 8.19 kpc velocity was seen during source selection: not blind. Use all four published bins, no interpolation to a favorable point, no new wave, foreground, distance or amplitude fit, and no independent-Gaussian combination of the radial and vertical catalogues. Map the common-source no-wave control, both preparations/signs and both native vs physical positive-source representations. Report conditional readout-only results: the positive-source comparison is not a newly formed wave state. Recompute force from the full-domain instantaneously frozen wave and the positive-source Poisson solve. Check force interpolation and positive-solve vs native-plus-defect on both fine grids.

Slope check: area-average the radial curvature operator in the Galactic plane between the two extreme centres and on each adjacent pair: [v_c²(out)−v_c²(in)] / [(R_out²−R_in²)/2]. This is an exact identity for an axisymmetric potential; not a point derivative, density measurement, or vertically averaged rotation correction. Apply point uncertainty endpoints in both opposing directions as a sensitivity envelope; do not pretend missing covariances or differential systematics vanish. Report how much fractional change in the *relative* endpoint velocity would reconcile any shape discrepancy, separately from a shared calibration. Do not use discrepancies to select/delete bins or deblind pulsar data.
