All Fourier products are the signed sum of the two long-short orderings; homogeneous self-products and other triangles are not included. Write h=a'/a, F=e^{-A}(h+B'), L=Ψ₂−Φ₂, e=8πGa²E, p=8πGa²P, D=e−3p. Units are comoving Mpc and conformal time in Mpc with c=1.
