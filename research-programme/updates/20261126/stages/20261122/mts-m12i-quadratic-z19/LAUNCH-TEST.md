# Can an unknown second-order launch erase the material result?
Before evaluating: use the original homogeneous multi-species response in exactly the reduced chart used to generate the particular solution, on the same finite Fourier support; do not set a distribution for the unknown initial wave. At each predefined material cut test the host-normalized change between epochs (not the final absolute occupation). Derive the dual norm and the least joint rms of an inherited second-order launch vector required to cancel that change. Density and scaled peculiar velocity divergence are dimensionless; at launch w=-theta/(a_i H_i). Report all-input, density-only, velocity-only and species-identical input subspaces, both for the full generated support (no zero mode) and the initially populated band. Pin the known coefficients; no measured final halo, sign, or observed residual constrains the unknowns. These are lower bounds in a specified norm and deterministic sensitivity thresholds, not estimates of actual initial power, odds or independent initial conditions. Check the optimal perturbation attains the bound and compare selected retarded propagators with independent forward ODE solutions. An exactly silent channel should be labelled, not divided by numerical noise.
