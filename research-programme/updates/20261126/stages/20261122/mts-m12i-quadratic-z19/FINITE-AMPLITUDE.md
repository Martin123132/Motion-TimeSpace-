# Independent nonlinear test (chosen before its results)
Test the reduced quadratic operator independently of CAMB or its first-order interpolant. Use two same-axis Fourier waves with box indices 9 and 22, phases +0.37 and −0.81, scaled to 0.014 per complex launch coefficient at the largest density of each mode. Predict both harmonics and both sum/difference modes (18, 44, 31, 13). Use the actual branch masses, density fractions, backgrounds and corrected linear launch transfers. Compare a retarded coefficient from ordered Fourier pairs with the even part of positive/negative, finite-amplitude 1D nonlinear evolution at amplitudes 0.4, 0.2 and 0.1. For MTS evolve the complex Schrödinger envelope itself with the common Poisson potential; its launch amplitude and phase are set to give zero quadratic Eulerian density and zero quadratic velocity. Cold components evolve under the same potential. Track conserved mean wave norm and both velocity divergence and the physical number current. No standalone radiation driving in this reduced-equation comparison and no use of stream observations.
