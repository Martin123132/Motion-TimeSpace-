# Palomar 5: both frozen directions through 111.16 million years

The two model streams separate much more on the additional, earlier part of the orbit. The largest cluster-relative 3-D difference is **68.08 pc**, versus 0.1273 pc when only the late 22.19 Myr differs. This does **not** mean the directions have been distinguished on the sky. The largest full velocity-gradient split is 0.09679 km/s per degree, **0.645 of the published gradient uncertainty**. No observed residual was used to prefer a direction.

The **opposite gradient signs between preparations** matter: there is no preparation-independent sign forecast here. The dominant contribution in each is the earlier 88.97 Myr, not an extrapolation of the weak late force. At 22.05 kpc the late-only term slightly opposes the early term for reset and slightly reinforces it for inherited. The full signed velocity difference differs from the sum of the two isolated contributions by less than 0.0001% in vector norm across the reported distances. The trajectories reach Galactocentric radii as small as 4.06 kpc in the earlier interval; the late segment only samples radii above 14.56 kpc. That establishes exposure to a different part of the field, not that pericentre alone produced the signal.

## How much of the stream and what the errors allow

- The signed stellar trajectories span **111.158776 Myr**, 5.73% of the [1.94 Gyr old trailing-debris drift estimate](https://inspirehep.net/files/3329b4b594051c3042f68d6a9524ac72) or 2.22% of the [5 Gyr disruption model](https://doi.org/10.1088/0004-637X/811/2/123). They are not a calculation of stream formation over either lifetime. The longer forward formation archives are not extra signed debris trajectories.
- Across three common distance assumptions the maximum individual bin shift is **0.448 of its positional error**. The largest nominal joint track difference is **1.263** in independent-bin error units, for inherited at **23.2 kpc**. This is separation of two model means, not chi-square against data or discovery significance. It is sensitive both to common geometry and to bin correlations we did not invent.
- **Supplementary orientation test, not part of the original selection.** Project the vector of track differences divided by the bin errors onto the single track-tilt vector (RA−cluster RA)/error. No observational residuals enter. In that 23.2 kpc case **98.24% of squared separation** is a tilt of 0.00357 degrees per degree about the cluster. If this single orientation is left free, independent-bin separation drops from **1.263 to 0.168**. This does not establish that such a tilt is physically allowed by a measured orientation prior. At the nominal distance inherited drops from 0.497 to 0.200 and reset from 0.1243 to 0.1242. All tilt projections (not bundled here).
- The recent-release experiments use a common illustrative inner/outer escape offset and vary release at the earlier interval midpoint by ±10 Myr, motivated by the [published stripping model](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stx1208). The early-to-late timing change is **at least 15.7 times** the same-date forward/reverse change across these tails, coordinates, distances and preparations. This is a scenario, not a timing likelihood for the old observed stream. The 1,845-fold comparison in the earlier report concerns different, much more recent releases. ## Verification and physical scope

We continued **backward from the same signed 590 wave states** on the original 586→590 interpolated ordinary drive, with self-gravity and the published-in-project isolated Newtonian Green operator. All four earlier states at each resolution reproduce their saved inverse endpoints. At the shared radius/time grid at 590, both the later field and its physical-time derivative agree with the saved late bank. The earlier wave potential and radial derivative are evaluated on the actual global solution from 2 to 35 kpc, not extrapolated from 12–29 kpc. Time reversal of the drive or conjugation of each forward frame would not do this calculation. The common matter source is prescribed for both waves (not a live Galaxy); see the [formation test](../mts-formation-cold-source-586-590/RESULTS.md) showing that the reverse is a counterfactual, not the outcome of either actual archived 586 preparation on the tested drives.

On each frozen field we integrated the same mock track and cluster, back-initialized at 586 in the sign-mean field. In signed segments we chose the full corresponding force; in the other segments we retained the **mean**, not zero gravity. We returned the stars to the same observation epoch, removed cluster bulk motion and compared at fixed right ascension. All tested paths stay inside both tables. At the original late orbit step, the late-only control recovers the sealed short-window position/velocity difference vectors within 3e−9 relative at the finest field resolution. The refinement survey covers both wave/field resolutions and an additional orbit-step halving at fixed fine field. All **180 comparisons pass the unchanged 0.5% tolerance**; largest changes 0.294% and 0.081% respectively. All mean-orbit round trips pass 1e−9. Numerical errors are not an uncertainty model for the Galaxy.
