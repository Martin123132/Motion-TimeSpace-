# Where the inner halo actually came from — m12i at 544

**The old wave's central concentration is not inherited simply by tracing the host's ordinary cold dark matter.** We counted all 70,514,272 fine dark particles in the snapshot, selected the 27,493,118 inside 287.005 kpc, and traced those IDs to their original MUSIC phase-space records. The simulation's parameter file names the exact input binary used here. An independent 137,744-marker check at z≈19 finds the correct early positions within 3–5 comoving kpc RMS of a first-order displacement, versus approximately 3 Mpc if initial records are shuffled. This is a result about one simulation and one saved MTS preparation, not observed Milky Way initial conditions.

| At snapshot 544 | Classical fraction of host dark mass | Original wave fraction | Required excess over tracing cold mass uniformly, same total wave mass | Required mean relative birth weight, inner vs rest |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Inside 13.96 kpc | 8.12% | 54.15% | 98.47 billion solar masses | 13.36× |
| Inside 16.6 kpc | 9.98% | 68.07% | 124.27 billion solar masses | 19.24× |

The two rows are nested, not additive. The first compares 78.55 billion solar masses of simulated dark material with 115.84 billion in the old wave; the fractional comparison independently normalizes both by their own mass in the outer sphere. **This is a test of the old starting assumption, not a requirement that MTS reproduce it.** A less concentrated formation-derived state is a legitimate alternative. This test does not tell us its later nonspherical force at GD-1.

**What the starting patch looked like.** Half the inner-tag mass started within 0.890 comoving Mpc of its own centre, and 90% within 1.270 Mpc. An initial sphere around the whole host patch centre enclosing the same number of host particles identifies only 28.9% of the eventual inner group. Even if birth weights could magically depend on the final destination, their rms departure from unity would have to be at least 1.685 (inner aperture) or 1.939 (16.6 kpc). Requiring nonnegative weights constant in 0.334-Mpc initial cells raises the first minimum to 3.61–3.76, depending on origin. This is neither an information-theoretic ban on dynamical capture nor a confidence interval.

**An actual input for the wave equations.** The two first-pass inversions use the same simulated realization, not another random spherical peak. On 0.2925–2.4 Mpc⁻¹, parent number-density and spatial-phase transfers produce two saved three-dimensional complex Fourier packets. At z=99 the inner tags' estimated mean wave overdensity is 1.94% (from initial density) or 1.97% (from initial momentum) on the finer mesh. Reweighting the classical trajectories at launch changes their core share by **−0.231 or −0.229 percentage points**, versus the **+46.03 points** required for the old stationary seed. The relative current in the common MTS metric also initially points toward decreasing that share: about −0.000174 per e-fold in fraction units, not a measured late mass flow. These numbers apply to the tested band at first order; they are not a bound on omitted modes, phase interference or later transport.

**Why two packets, not one accepted restart.** The broad-band density- and velocity-inferred matter modes have correlation 0.9928 but **12.0% relative rms disagreement** on the finer mesh. Exact sums over the initial particles show 0.60%–6.60% disagreement on the four checked individual modes even without gridding. Gridding also matters: over these four the interlaced 192-grid density differs from direct sums by 0.854% in norm. None of the 27 window checks pass *all* the frozen 0.5% criteria. We kept both results rather than averaging. The mode mismatch cannot all be assigned to the time between redshifts or to CIC gridding. The full initial file contains second-order displacements; the two inversions deliberately assume a first-order growing mode. Other contributions remain possible.

**An important source correction.** The published file under slot 000 tested here has 96,239,056 fine dark particles (z=99). The verified run input at z=100, and slots 001, 020 and 544, have 70,514,272. Particle mass per marker alone would not have exposed the mismatch. No slot-000 ID was joined and no conclusion is drawn about the reason for that file's contents. This is not evidence that the whole release is wrong.
