# Before reconstructing the modes

The MUSIC input is actually z=100. Its saved 2LPT run log records an Eisenstein-Hu transfer, original cosmology, dplus and normalization. Recover **a conditional common random realization**, not a new universal MTS coupling: independently deconvolve the mass density and the negative divergence of its peculiar momentum at that epoch. They would agree in linear growing dust, not identically in the actual displaced 2LPT particle realization. Whiten by the MUSIC matter spectrum, recolour independently by the original MTS primordial spectrum and apply its existing z=99 number-density and phase transfers. Keep baryons and coarse cells in the MUSIC total mass; do not silently use the parent's cold transfer as the FIRE transfer. Archive both estimates, not their average. Do not promote a unique field if they fail the original 0.5% / 2e−5 accuracy checks.

Use all integer box modes in the existing retained band with k ≤ 0.75, 1.5 and 2.4 inverse comoving Mpc (the original 128-cell coarse lattice has Nyquist 4.70/Mpc). The cuts are resolution diagnostics, not a fitted smoothing radius or an estimate of the missing high modes. Compare 128 and 192 mesh points per axis with a second mesh displaced half a cell in all axes to suppress odd alias images. Count the wave in place of passive cold labels only as a counterfactual: the measured MTS phase induces a different velocity field, and equal labels do not impose equal nonlinear trajectories. Use the actual initial positions and a separate growth displacement to z=99 for a clock sensitivity check; the stored snapshot numbered zero has incompatible particle counts. No selection by later stream fit.
