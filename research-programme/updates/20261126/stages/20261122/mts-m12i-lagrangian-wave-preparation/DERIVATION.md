# What a host-conditioned wave starting state would need to explain

The target in this experiment is the **earlier stored wave state**, not a measurement that the Galaxy must satisfy. Snapshot 544 has a stationary fixed-occupation wave seed; later histories were built from it. At a physical radius r*, compare *volume-integrated mass* in both models, not the force-equivalent mass of a nonspherical distribution. Integrate the original reduced radial wave on its finite elements, splitting at all requested radii, summing |u_l|² with the stored mass and length scale. Polynomial quadrature orders 18 and 26 agree and the inner readout independently reproduces origin-trace.csv. In FIRE, count equal-mass high-resolution dark particles on spheres about the saved galaxy centre. The outer reference is the central Rockstar halo's published R200m, not the halo finder's bound-particle list. Its spherical particle mass need not equal its catalogue mass; this is a fixed conditional population, not a tidal capture law.

**A necessary transport or reweighting budget.** Let I be the binary indicator that a uniformly drawn host marker ends in the inner aperture on its actual cold trajectory; p=E I. If wave occupation is attached passively to those same trajectories, let w≥0 denote its starting number per marker, divided by its host mean. Then F=E(Iw), E w=1. Cauchy gives |F−p| ≤ sqrt[p(1−p)] rms(w−1), with equality for the two constant weights F/p and (1−F)/(1−p). Their ratio F(1−p)/[p(1−F)] is the *actual ratio of the required mean weights* between the two groups, independent of how finely the weight varies. Relative to passive uniform painting, the required signed mass transfer at fixed host total is M_H(F−p). This is not proof of influx through a particular material boundary over time; actual wave trajectories do not carry immutable dark-particle IDs. If both species' initial density contrasts are pointwise ≤ε in absolute value, attaching weight proportional to (1+δψ)/(1+δd) can supply a max/min ratio at most [(1+ε)/(1−ε)]². The epsilon threshold is necessary in this scenario, not a measured universal perturbation bound.

**Can spatially resolved birth weights do it?** For cells in the actual initial particle cloud let h=E(I|cell), with probabilities q set by all host tags. If w is constant per cell the extremal F is max h and the exact least-rms nonnegative weights are affine in h clipped at zero (solve the two Lagrange constraints). The output keeps infeasibilities instead of replacing missing weights with zero, and compares against independent constrained minimization on known controls. Changing a grid origin changes the cell family: do not infer a sharp physical smoothing length. More generally, for a within-cell Lipschitz bound L, decompose Cov(I,w) by conditioning on the cell. The first term is ≤sd(h) sd(w). Within a cell the difference of the two conditional means of w is ≤L sqrt(3)Δ, hence the extra term is ≤L sqrt(3)Δ E[h(1−h)]. This latter inequality does not require weights to be constant. The coefficient is in initial comoving Mpc.

**Matched primordial realization, two conditional inversions.** For an adiabatic, growing pressureless mode, at linear order the total density contrast d and longitudinal peculiar momentum estimate −i k·[(1+δ)v]_k/(aHf) coincide. MUSIC's actual input uses second-order displacements and finite multiresolution particles; the identity is not exact for those arrays. Use GADGET comoving lengths ckpc/h and physical peculiar v=sqrt(a) v_file, periodic full-box constraints, total mass of all three particle species (including variable coarse masses). Ignore neither gas nor the exterior zoom cells. Deposit at 128 and 192 sites per axis with cloud-in-cell; shift another grid by half a cell in all axes, correct for the shift and CIC window *before* averaging complex modes. Compare four non-axis-aligned modes with weighted direct exp(−ikx) sums over **all** original particles and separate sums over each tag class. Sampling the patch uses the conjugate window coefficient. Count real degrees with the conjugate partner for positive k_z. These are Fourier coefficients normalized by box volume, not shell power averages.

For each mode in the stated band use the logged FIRE cosmology, Eisenstein–Hu transfer shape, n_s, D_init/D_0 and σ8 to compute P_FIRE(k,a_init) in Mpc³. Normalization is independently recovered from σ8 with the spherical top-hat; converting to the MUSIC h-unit/Fourier convention recovers the archived pnorm to 4.3×10⁻⁸ fractionally. The programme used MUSIC 1.51 (logged), not a modern recalculation with another cosmology. The [transfer fit](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/9709112) and [MUSIC method](https://arxiv.org/abs/1103.6031) are imported, not derived here.

Whiten each complex estimator by sqrt(P_FIRE); recolour by sqrt[2π² P_R,MTS(k)/k³], fixing the relative global sign with the parent's paired cold density transfer. This preserves an estimate of the standardized adiabatic random draw, **not identical background parameters, raw density, or hidden simulation phase data**. Multiply the result by the original z=99 transfer functions for the canonical coordinate number δ_N−3Φ and phase S=−am θ/k². Store both independent complex fields. They have no hand-picked orientation and no fitted amplitude; all untested Fourier bins are absent, not inferred zero in the universe. Wave peculiar velocity is i c k S/(am). The Lagrangian window is evaluated both at actual initial positions (z=100) and after a 1%-in-growth first-order displacement to z=99; neither is sold as the rejected snapshot 000. The actual z≈19 intermediate positions independently test ID matching and growing displacement over a much longer interval; they do not certify the small intervening step to arbitrary precision.

For passive number weights, the first-order change in the fraction ending in the core is p[mean_inner(δψ−δref)−mean_host(δψ−δref)], not p times the raw halo overdensity. Distinguish the linearized FIRE reference at z=99, the separate paired cold cosmology, and the residual CDM *in the same heavy cosmology*. For the last case the leading conserved nonrelativistic number continuity law on a common metric gives d(δψ,coord−δc,coord)/d ln a=(θc−θψ)/ℋ. The coordinate volume perturbation cancels. This is an initial rate on the tagged window; not the integral of future capture, nor the exact curved-spacetime energy-density continuity law with nonzero pressure. We do not identify a classical velocity with the heavy-wave phase or multiply this initial rate by the age of a galaxy.
