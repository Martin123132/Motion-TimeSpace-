# Time, the trace and the matching check

For a positive-frequency perturbation of a stationary homogeneous free real field in flat space, the bilinear energy and pressure between the frequency m and ω=√(m²+p²) are proportional to m(ω+m) and m(ω−m). Their ratio (ω−m)/(ω+m)=(p/m)²/(1+√(1+(p/m)²))² gives the native beat sound speed with p=k/a. Applying it to the comoving density of a slowly varying expanding background is an approximation; reproducing the post-switch implementation is not an independent derivation of its expansion corrections. Another mistake would be to gauge-transform into the instantaneous scalar rest frame at every turning point and then average. That is not the rest frame of the averaged distribution.

**What the canonical equation actually predicts.** Work on the Newtonian slice in proper time, dots denoting local background cosmic-time derivatives. For b=homogeneous field and f=δφ_N, the linearized KG equation is
f̈+3H ḟ+(m²+k²/a²)f=ḃ(Ψ̇+3Φ̇)−2m² bΨ, with b̈+3Hḃ+m²b=0.
δp_N=ḃ(ḟ−ḃΨ)−m²bf. Applying (∂t²+3H∂t) to bf produces 2δp_N as a sum of time derivatives, the spatial-gradient term and explicit metric sources; no pressure law is inserted. In the dimensionless carrier phase s=m t use z=a^(3/2)m b/√C, v=a^(3/2)ḃ/√C, d=a^(3/2)m f/√C, u=a^(3/2)(ḟ−ḃΨ)/√C. The dimensionless pressure is vu−zd; density is vu+zd. With h=H/m and ξ=k²/(am)² the weak identity is given explicitly in [the preregistered additional check](VIRIAL-TEST.md). All derivatives of the compact taper are with respect to s. Endpoints have W=W'=0. Averaging scaled variables defines the clock and normalization used here; it is not a unique covariant averaging operation for all orders.

For the independent evolution set y=u−3vΦ. Then
z'=v+3hz/2, v'=−z−3hv/2,
d'=y+3hd/2+v(Ψ+3Φ), y'=−(1+ξ)d−3hy/2+z(3Φ−Ψ).
This form needs the metric but **no derivative of a noisy potential**. Start from the first saved field and normal momentum, supply a(s),Φ,Ψ, and advance without subsequent microscopic state data. The comparison is conditional on the externally supplied linear metric; it does not re-solve the entire cosmology. The physical clock is m∫a dη, not the index in a nominally uniform array. In the earlier eight-cycle archive the two clocks differ by only 1.98e−8 fractionally over the whole window; using the actual kernel derivatives at the measured times matters because the oscillatory terms being subtracted are much larger than the pressure. Both clocks' diagnostics are kept.

For comparison in the effective rest frame *after filtering*, let V=⟨vd/(am)⟩/(ρ₀+p₀) with ρ₀=⟨(v²+z²)/2⟩, p₀=⟨(v²−z²)/2⟩. For scaled background variables the conformal derivative is am∂s⟨X⟩−3ℋ⟨X⟩. Subtract the respective density or pressure derivative times V. The exact beat relation from flat space is tested against these two quantities, not against high-frequency point values. A separate diagnostic resolves background and perturbation differential defects of the archived trajectories. The part using residuals of the measured perturbed velocity is **accounting**, not a new independent prediction. The field replay with a displacement-only virial readout is the stronger test.
