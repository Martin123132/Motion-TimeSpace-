# What the earlier mixed tide and transition stress measure

Use the parent background, common adiabatic phases, split of input modes and host tag windows from the sealed compression calculation (not bundled here). No stream positions or forces are used as formation data. Cold compares baryon and CDM compression; heavy compares wave and ordinary CDM on the same metric. The early integral is a particular response with zero *additional* dynamical compression and velocity at its lower limit. It is not a primordial initial condition.

## Source and its projection

On a Newtonian-gauge slice let D be the first-order coordinate-volume density (energy-density convention on the scalar side), use the longitudinal displacement s(k)=i k D/k², and denote the symmetric long-short product by [AB]=A_L B_S+A_S B_L. For pressureless species this is the longitudinal continuity displacement; treating it as an exact number displacement for the finite-pressure averaged wave is an approximation. The induced coordinate density is K=∂i∂j[s_i s_j]/2. The material compression equation has a common matter term −Σ C_j K_j/(2a). Evaluating the first-order acceleration on the displaced coordinates supplies [s·∇T]−[(Pij D)(Pij T)], where T(k)=k²Ψ(k), Pij=k_i k_j/k². Decompose the latter into −[DT]/3, the traceless contraction with k²(Φ+Ψ)/2, and the traceless remainder. Labels “Ricci_trace” and “Ricci_shear” in the tables refer to this decomposition of the *spatial lapse Hessian*, not an extraction of the entire tetrad Riemann tensor in a time-dependent relativistic metric. A uniform acceleration cancels in the difference of two freely falling points; a spatial force gradient does not. This does not license dropping gradients in the long/short pairs.

For a long q and short p, let μ be the cosine of their angle, k=q+p. K has kernel 1+μ²+q·p(1/q²+1/p²). For each ordered density/metric pair the advection kernel is −q·p/q² and the shear is −(μ²−1/3). Expanding Δ√(1+D)/√(1+D) through quadratic order gives (1/2)ΔD−DΔD/2−|∇D|²/4. With the linear force evaluated at displaced positions and its spatial derivative transformed, the wave source in the code is (k⁴K+Q)/(4 a²m²); the full Q pair kernel is −k²(p²+q²+q·p)−(q·p/q²+μ²)p⁴−(q·p/p²+μ²)q⁴. The saved independent enumeration verifies each term against the spatial transform, including phases and normalizations.

Integrate in ln a against the late differential measurement row e(G_final,initial−I)G(initial,.003)G(.003,a). Each driving divergence enters the scaled velocity slot with coefficient −1/(B H). Read out the change of normalized share during the later z99→19 segment, in percentage points; it is not the absolute value of the first-order density at .003. The all-mode result includes the mixed input pairs only and in-band plus generated output on the mixed support. The previous-screen result masks *outputs* by the later era's criterion. Native linear metric and first-order baryon density include radiation; the second-order common curvature, photon/neutrino stress and baryon acoustic response are not obtained just by inserting these linear sources into a matter-only evolution. The baryon-locked alternative is a sensitivity, not an error bar.

## Exact scalar ingredient at the transition

A canonical, minimally coupled scalar on a homogeneous background has 8πG a² δ²T^i_{j,TF}=[∂^i δφ_N ∂_j δφ_N]^{TF} in the retained parent normalization and Newtonian scalar chart. Its second-order perturbation does not multiply a background spatial gradient. This particular tensor component is fixed without solving for δ²φ. It is only one component of the metric source. Project it with (3/2)(k_i k_j/k²−δij/3). Exclude k=0 from this directional projection.

With C the heavy background density coefficient, d=a^(3/2)m δφ_N/√C, u=a^(1/2)δφ'_{normal,N}/√C, and h the analogous complex homogeneous carrier, define f=(d+i u)/h. The real field is d=Re(h f). Thus the mixed Gram kernel from the data contains both (1/2)Re⟨|h|² f_L f_S*⟩ and (1/2)Re⟨h² f_L f_S⟩. The latter need not vanish over a finite cycle. Average in proper carrier time, restore C/(a³m²) at the common reporting epoch. The leading averaged wave prediction uses X_L X_S+Y_L Y_S, where X=δ_N/2=(D+3Φ)/2 and Y=−amθ/k²; bulk alone is insufficient. All compared fields retain identical phases. The edge estimator 2 × (last-cycle mean) minus (last-two-cycle mean) removes linear slow-time drift at the last canonical epoch. The canonical and first averaged epoch differ by 0.0667% in scale factor. An endpoint comparison alone cannot distinguish a small clock offset from a missing order in the averaged stress. The held-out last-cycle prediction supplies the homogeneous background at test epochs, but no perturbation from those epochs. Its success is a pre-transition transport check, not validation after the switch.

The strict earlier scale test asks for both (aH/k)² and [x/(1+√(1+x²))]² below 2e−5 with x=k/(am). Test both members of a pair. A smaller time-integration error does not repair violation of either approximation. No claim here that the lapse at second order follows from this scalar contribution alone.
