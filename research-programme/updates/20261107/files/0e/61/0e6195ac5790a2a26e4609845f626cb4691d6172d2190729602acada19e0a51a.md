# Fixed-support radial shape work and the current residual

Private derivation and predeclared test, 27 September 2026. The completed
third-grid primary/reference calculation is the input; no trajectory,
interaction coefficient, support node, stress prescription or tolerance
from that calculation is changed.

## 1. Remove the actual source-motion shape before testing arbitrary motion

Use the inherited map r=chi(x,b), J=chi_x, d=chi_b and g=u_x/J. For each
compact radial test xi(r), define

    xi_b = xi(b),       eta(r) = xi(r)-d(r)*xi_b,
    eta_r = xi_r-(J_b/J)*xi_b.

Eta vanishes at the moving zero-field anchor and at the fixed exterior
endpoints. Thus eta*g is an admissible field test after projection onto
the continuous spatial/material trial space; it does not move the pinned
field independently of the source. The source component of the associated
canonical test is -xi_b.

Let E=-pi_t-H_x on each cell and f=(beta-alpha*W^2)(g_plus-g_minus)
at each interior face. Define A[v]=integral E*v dx + sum_faces f*v_face,
with the explicit average-gradient trace when v=eta*g or d*g.
The already derived source identity gives

    E_wave,b = -A[d*g],
    E_source = E_wave,b + F_G,b - (p_d,t-F_d),
    F_G,b = -sum_i gamma_b,i*load_i.

Consequently the old source/spatial part of the radial residual is

    C_xi + F_xi + dust_Euler*xi_b + W_G[xi]
      = A[eta*g] + W_G[eta] - xi_b*E_source.

This is an off-shell identity, not an assumption that E_source vanishes
pointwise. Material Galerkin stationarity only constrains its projections.
Numerical source-identity defects and the explicitly evaluated exterior
terms are retained in the implementation.

## 2. The action gives a new, independently testable shape-work functional

The retained potential (minus the Gram Lagrangian) is

    U_G = 1/2 sum_a (S gamma)_a e_a^2,
    e=D u,   load=S^T(e^2/2),   gamma=r^2*N*sqrt(F)/J.

Take an independent virtual displacement of support r_i -> r_i+epsilon*eta_i,
J_i -> J_i*(1+epsilon*eta'_i), and nodal field u -> u+epsilon*v.
Hold the Eulerian metric profiles and D,S fixed. This is a probe of the
retained functional, NOT the introduction of new embedding equations.
Ordinary differentiation yields

    B_G[v,eta] = d_epsilon U_G |0 = G[v]+W_G[eta],
    G[v] = sum_a (S gamma)_a e_a (D v)_a,
    W_G[eta] = sum_i gamma_i load_i
       [-eta'_i + (2/r_i+nu'_i+F'_i/(2F_i))*eta_i].

For v=eta*g, this is the response when field samples follow a fixed
Eulerian field while the support is displaced. For projected v it includes
the explicit projection change, separated below. The earlier W_G formula
alone did not test this combined field-and-support response.

## 3. Do not silently apply a continuum product rule to D

At support nodes choose the explicit average physical gradient gbar at
ordinary P2 interfaces. At cell midpoints it is the ordinary gradient.
The source anchor is absent from the free field basis and eta vanishes there.
Define the exact rectangular product defect

    C_D(eta,gbar) = D(eta*gbar) - (S eta)*(D gbar).

This definition does not say that the remaining factor (D eta)*gbar is
zero or assert a usual differential Leibniz rule. It is the exact difference
between two specified matrix operations. It gives the identity

    G[v] = sum_a (S gamma)_a e_a [
           (S eta)_a*(D gbar)_a + C_D(eta,gbar)_a
           + D(v-eta*gbar)_a ].

All three terms are evaluated, including cancellations. A finite-dimensional
quadratic potential does not possess invariance under arbitrary support
motion merely because it is positive or has antisymmetric energy transfer.
An exact rational counterexample and a zero-interaction reference control
test this distinction. Such a counterexample is not a verdict on the MTS
continuum completion.

## 4. Close the complete residual without defining a correction from it

Let v be the spatial/material projection of eta*g and z the material
projection of xi_b. The decomposition is

    R = B_G[v,eta] + d_t(J_horizontal-J_minimal)
        + integral E*(eta*g-v) dx
        + sum_faces f*(eta*gbar-v_face)
        - (xi_b-z)*E_source
        + [A[v]-G[v]-z*E_source].

The last bracket is independently compared with the complete stored
canonical Euler covector contracted against (v,-z), using the saved Hessian,
coupled acceleration and live-metric momentum response. Its 48/64 material
quadrature difference and assembly discrepancy are retained separately.
The old current-correction derivative is reused in full, not just its Gram
bond term. The direct/source-decomposition and component-sum gaps are
reported rather than absorbed into B_G.

Thus both B_G and the projection/field/source defects have independent
definitions; none is introduced as minus the observed whole residual.
An explicit shape reaction can be physically meaningful if its support is
material. Making it vanish, adding its dynamics, or completing its metric
variation are different tasks and must not be conflated.
