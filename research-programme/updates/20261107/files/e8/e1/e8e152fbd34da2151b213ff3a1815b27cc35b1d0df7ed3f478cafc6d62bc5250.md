The piecewise-linear three-anchor interpolant satisfies exactly

This interpolant is continuous; the jump is an approximation diagnostic,
not a physical discontinuity being postulated.

If the actual source is twice continuously differentiable in u, the
midpoint defect has the integral representation
Delta=-(1/2) integral_[u0,u1] (v-u0) R''(v) dv

This is conditional: one observed Delta does NOT give an upper bound on
sup||R''||, or certify interpolation at every other time. A vector-valued
defect need not share a single scalar mean-value point.
This identity follows by two integrations by parts; no external theorem
or literature acquisition is needed.
