# Stage80 — an emitting density response, derived and tested

8 October2026. Private/local continuation of Stage79.

## What moved forward

We now have a solved perturbation of the emitting background in both existing
MTS parents, rather than just a background calculation or a list of missing
inputs. The new result is its response to a small change in baryon density
at fixed reference expansion. It is a source-sector building block for the
existing spatial radiation/gas/metric hierarchy.

The derivation identifies a coupling that the old Compton-only formula would
miss: bremsstrahlung changes with both electron and ion density. One density
factor cancels when converting energy exchange to heating per gas particle;
the other must remain. The photon and gas equations now carry that same term
with consistent signs.

## Other derivations completed

The common-temperature emission column is now derived at leading heat order.
Although the14.16 DC correction was supplied only on the Planck equilibrium
family, its needed common-temperature derivative is independent of any smooth
off-equilibrium extension at this order. This removes the need to invent a
fitted radiation temperature for that column.70-digit checks at the physical
heat parameter agree with the formula to2.35e-9 relative or better.

The source's velocity transformation is derived from the invariant collision
scalar, with the finite-frequency-window boundary kept explicit. Energy and
momentum moments are paired with the gas; the tested source-frame identities
agree to9.65e-9 or better. Closed first-heat-order Compton pair coefficients
also agree with the inherited finite-heat expressions on both emitting histories.
