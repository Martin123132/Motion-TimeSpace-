Six raw photon/gas or spectral energy-moment tests failed the unchanged1e-10
relative threshold on the small endpoint increments.

It reproduces residuals already present in the stored event values rather than
removing them. Thirty-three of the48 per-column atomic moment checks fail.
The largest photon/gas relative defect is1.36614e-7 on the much smaller5-to6 energy
difference; the largest spectral energy-moment defect is6.55937e-8.
All failures are retained, and endpoint propagation is blocked.

The ranked real D-event defects led to an actual arithmetic repair, not a new fit.
For rest-frame incident energy e, signed soft energy s,
A=1-cos(hard), B=1-n_h.n_s and C=1-n_s,z, derive
h+s-e=[-e*s*C-(e-s)*(e*A-s*B)]/(1+e*A-s*B).
This avoids subtracting nearly equal photon energies. Its Born limit is
h-e=-e^2*A/(1+e*A). The independent electron recoil calculation is retained.
The low spectral test moments follow their exact constant/linear definitions.
