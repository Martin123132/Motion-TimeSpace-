## 1. Model, assumptions and the calculation performed

Keep the same closed complex-scalar candidate and fractional-quartic potential:

    C=f(r) exp(i omega t)/sqrt(2),
    V(f)=3g f^(4/3)/4+lambda f^4/4, g,lambda>0.

The physical charge is the candidate's global U(1) charge, not electric charge.
Its U(1) and quartic stabilization remain explicit hypotheses of the isolated
condensate route. No replacement of the original real MTS scalar/bath is made.

The gravity used here is the minimal Einstein-Hilbert part of the previously
retained parent metric action, with the same G_R. It is not inferred from the
scalar acoustic metric. In the parent convention M_R^2 G_munu=T_munu,
G_R=1/(8pi M_R^2). The actual parent action/source references remain recorded
in `../conserved-stress-20261003/results/stage-integrity.json` (4876,4916,4960).

For this isolated asymptotically flat test, omit other matter, cosmological
curvature and higher-curvature/nonlocal terms. Applying it to a physical object
requires those terms to be negligible on both the body and interface scales;
in particular the body must be small compared with the cosmological curvature
radius. This is a specified parent limit, not a demonstration that all parent
corrections vanish or that the value of G_R is derived.

We solve at fixed total charge, not fixed central field or radius. This lets
us compare the same body before and after gravitational binding is included.

## 2. Covariant source, charge and dimensionless equations

With signature (-,+,+,+), hbar=c=1, write

    ds^2=-N(r)^2dt^2+dr^2/F(r)+r^2 dOmega^2,
    F(r)=1-2G_R M(r)/r, N(infinity)=1.

The full amplitude/phase action gives

    rho=F f'^2/2+omega^2 f^2/(2N^2)+V,
    p_r=F f'^2/2+omega^2 f^2/(2N^2)-V,
    p_t=-F f'^2/2+omega^2 f^2/(2N^2)-V,
    Q=4pi omega int [r^2 f^2/(N sqrt(F))] dr.

The charge factor is both the proper-volume factor 1/sqrt(F) and the conversion
from coordinate to local phase frequency omega/N. Dropping either changes the
fixed-charge problem. There is no radial charge flux for this stationary ansatz.

Retain v_*=(g/lambda)^(3/8), mu_*^2=2g v_*^(-2/3). Define

    s=mu_* r, h=f/v_*, w=omega/mu_*, nu=ln N,
    W(h)=3h^(4/3)/8+h^4/8,
    m=mu_* M/(4pi v_*^2), alpha=4pi G_R v_*^2,
    F=1-2alpha m/s.

Here rho,p_r,p_t are henceforth in v_*^2 mu_*^2 units, with f->h,
omega->w and V->W. The coupled equations are

    m'=s^2 rho,
    nu'=alpha(m+s^3 p_r)/(s^2 F),
    F'=2alpha(m/s^2-s rho),
    h''+(2/s+nu'+F'/(2F))h'=[W'(h)-w^2 exp(-2nu)h]/F,
    j'=w s^2 h^2 exp(-nu)/sqrt(F).

The dimensionless total energy and charge are e_ADM=4pi m(R), q=4pi j(R).
Physical E_ADM=(v_*^2/mu_*)e_ADM and Q=(v_*^2/mu_*^2)q, with the same unfitted
scale conversions as the flat daughter. No astrophysical units are assigned.

Boundary data: regular center; compact outer h=h'=0; exterior Schwarzschild
matching; N(infinity)=1; q fixed to a previously solved flat body. The support
radius, frequency at infinity and total mass are solved parameters.

## 3. The compact edge survives curved geometry

Let R be the dimensionless support radius, C_s=2alpha m(R)/R and F_s=1-C_s>0.
For inward coordinate distance d=R-s, the nontrivial positive endpoint is

    h~(12F_s)^(-3/2) d^3.

In proper radial distance ell this is h~12^(-3/2) ell^3. Thus gravity changes
the coordinate coefficient, not the leading proper-distance power law. The
field remains C^2 when joined to vacuum, with vanishing surface stress.

Write h=(12F_s)^(-3/2)u(d)^3. In the vacuum-edge expansion,

    F_vac(d)=1-C_s R/(R-d),
    B_vac(d)=2/(R-d)+C_s R/[(R-d)^2 F_vac(d)],
    3u u''+6u'^2-3B_vac u u'
       =6F_s/F_vac-w^2u^2/F_vac^2+u^8/[3456 F_s^3 F_vac].

The derived first correction is

    u=d+(2+C_s)d^2/[10R(1-C_s)]+O(d^3).

All coefficients through d^5 are generated symbolically and reduce exactly
to the earlier flat coefficients when C_s=0. Matter changes the u-series first
at d^6: rho= d^4/(192F_s^2)+O(d^5), so the omitted mass from an edge distance
delta is R^2 delta^5/(960F_s^2)+O(delta^6). The solver integrates this tiny
tail using its series rather than extending an exponential through vacuum.
The scalar quartic force enters the transformed u-equation at d^8.

Matching N to the exterior expression at the small-amplitude numerical cut
neglects a matter correction of order alpha*delta^6. Mass and charge tails
are integrated separately. The changed endpoint floor and a third resolution
test verify that this approximation is below the reported observable errors.
This is controlled numerical endpoint treatment, not an exact exterior metric
asserted throughout the nonzero scalar tail.

## 4. Conservation and independent mass checks

The scalar equation gives the anisotropic hydrostatic identity

    p_r'=-(rho+p_r)nu'+2(p_t-p_r)/s.

The flat integrated pressure cancellation is therefore not reused unchanged
in a gravitating body. The appropriate stationary mass check is instead

    d/ds { N(m+s^3p_r)/sqrt(F) }
       =s^2 N(rho+p_r+2p_t)/sqrt(F),
    m_ADM=int s^2 N(rho+p_r+2p_t)/sqrt(F) ds.

The second equality uses regularity at the center, zero boundary pressure,
and exterior time normalization. It is the Komar/ADM relation for this
stationary, horizonless Einstein-matter system. Here

    rho+p_r+2p_t=2w^2 h^2/N^2-2W.

This gives an independent integrated check against m'=s^2rho. We also evaluate
the angular Einstein equation from the saved spline derivatives:

    F(nu''+nu'^2+nu'/s)+(F'/2)(nu'+1/s)=2alpha p_t.

Although related analytically through conservation, it is not one of the
collocation equations imposed directly. Its finite-resolution residual must
be distinguished from the tighter integrated mass/charge residuals.

## 5. Derived weak-binding law at fixed charge

Let h_0,m_0,rho_0,p_r0 denote a flat stationary body. Expand in alpha at fixed
q while keeping the dimensionless matter potential fixed:

    F=1-2alpha m_0/s+O(alpha^2), N=1+alpha n_1+O(alpha^2).

Stationarity of e_0-w_0 q_0 cancels the first-order profile and frequency
variations, including motion of the zero-field boundary. For the explicit
metric dependence of the energy density minus w_0 times charge density,

    d/dalpha [ F h_0'^2/2+w_0^2 h_0^2/(2N^2)
               -w_0^2 h_0^2/(N sqrt(F)) ] at alpha=0
       =-(m_0/s)(h_0'^2+w_0^2 h_0^2).

The lapse perturbation cancels. Since h_0'^2+w_0^2 h_0^2=rho_0+p_r0,

    e_ADM(alpha,q)=e_0(q)+alpha B(q)+O(alpha^2),
    B(q)=-4pi int s m_0(s)[rho_0(s)+p_r0(s)] ds.

This is a derived coefficient, not fitted to the gravitating outputs.
The density-only Newtonian self-energy comparison is

    B_Newton=-4pi int s m_0 rho_0 ds.

Weak gravity alone does not imply negligible internal pressure. In the flat
body p_r0 decreases to zero at the edge and is positive inside, so B is more
negative than B_Newton. Both estimates use the same flat source and integration
procedure. A disagreement of the density-only approximation for a pressurized
body is not failure of GR or a pipeline defect.

The last column compares the prior-flat-profile prediction to an extrapolation

also agree. The pressure correction decreases toward the large-body regime.

A simultaneous large-body/weak-gravity limit is required: for a nearly uniform
critical core, m~R^3/3 and compactness~2alpha R^2/3. The useful window is

    interface thickness << R << alpha^(-1/2),

up to order-one factors. Sending R to infinity at fixed alpha is not a
Newtonian limit. The exterior monopole is Schwarzschild with the same G_R,
and its weak-field potential is -G_R E_ADM/r; the density-only approximation
inside also requires small pressure relative to energy density.

## 6. Numerical continuation and refinement

Three fixed-charge families were continued from flat support radii 8,20,80.
For each, seven coupling values correspond to nominal flat compactness
0,1e-5,1e-4,.001,.003,.01,.03, where the label is 2alpha m_flat/R_flat.
The actual gravitating compactness is an output, not fixed to the label.

All 21 coarse and 21 fine points converged. Coarse/fine endpoint floors were
1e-8/1e-11 and collocation tolerances 2e-7/2e-9. Maximum relative change across
radius, frequency, ADM energy, central amplitude and central lapse: 3.43e-9.
Maximum fine errors: charge8.88e-14, mass quadrature1.24e-13,
Komar/ADM1.66e-13, normalized scalar residual4.56e-6,
normalized angular Einstein residual6.49e-5. The latter is not 1e-13 accuracy.
For the worst angular case (R_flat=8, nominalcompactness.03), a third run with
floor1e-12 and tolerance2e-11 reduced that residual to1.03e-6, while leaving
the mass unchanged to better than1e-10 relative.

At fixed alpha, twelve additional nearby-charge solutions check d e_ADM/dq=w
using two symmetric charge steps. Maximum fine-step relative error2.32e-9;
the errors decrease with the charge step. This also checks that the frequency
is normalized at infinity rather than mistakenly using a central clock.
There were four initial pilot solutions and two third-resolution solutions;
they are retained separately rather than counted as new theory evidence.

## 7. Example and interpretation

For the q=1860240.3061617 family at nominal compactness .03:

    R: 80 -> 79.1249841359,
    e_ADM: 1873746.6414611 -> 1855730.2265537,
    actual surface compactness: .03004011425,
    minimum interior F: .9689652345,
    N_center: .9757517401,
    w_infinity: .9886875450,
    w_center_proper=w_infinity/N_center: 1.0132572706,
    e_ADM/q: .99757553925.

The radius shrinks1.09377% and the asymptotic energy drops0.961518% at unchanged
charge. The frequency at infinity is below the flat coexistence value, while
the local central frequency remains above it. This is gravitational redshift,
not reversal of time dilation. The flat-space bound e>=q is not an ADM bound
when gravitational binding is included, so e_ADM/q<1 is not a contradiction.

All sampled objects remain well away from F=0. Convergence of stationary
gravitating solutions is not a test of their coupled dynamical stability;
the previous flat fixed-charge Hessian cannot simply be recycled unchanged.

## 8. Primary-source context

[Liebling and Palenzuela, Dynamical Boson Stars](https://arxiv.org/abs/1202.5809)
provides the established Einstein-complex-scalar context. These are candidate
soliton/boson-star solutions, not a newly invented kind of gravitational object.
[Hartmann et al., Compact (A)dS Boson Stars and Shells](https://arxiv.org/abs/1310.3632)
studies gravitational back-reaction for a different, V-shaped compact-support
potential. Its profiles and stability claims are not transplanted into our
fractional-quartic model. Source records checked3October2026.
