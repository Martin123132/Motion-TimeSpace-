# Native-radius Gram action: a radial-covariant areal lift

## Why this extension is being tested

The native-kernel stage supplies an actual geometric covector, rather than
only a coordinate conjugation. Its source-motion power on the saved seeds
agrees with the old Jacobian term. Do not add the two powers. The remaining
question is where its general shape response enters the metric equations.
This note constructs a SPECIFIED extension of the Gram sector before
varying the angular metric. It does not infer a unique parent action from
gauge-fixed data or change the existing live equations silently.

## 1. Restore the areal scalar and its coframe

In a monotone radial patch use

    ds^2=-N^2 dt^2+dr^2/F+A(t,r)^2 dOmega^2, A_r>0, F>0.

Coordinate mesh edges r_e=chi(x_e) have physical areal radii R_e=A(r_e).
Retain nodal scalar values q and the same native-index B and S. Rebuild the
compensated P2 kernel D_R in R, including every interface and the source
hinge as in the preceding construction. Define

    C_A=A^2 N sqrt(F) A_r,
    U_G[A,N,F,q,chi]=(1/2) sum_a (S C_A)_a (D_R q)_a^2.

This reduces exactly to the physical native-radius candidate at A=r.
The A_r factor is necessary for this stated coframe choice: under an
orientation-preserving radial relabelling r'=f(r), F'=F f_r^2 and
A_(r')=A_r/f_r, so sqrt(F') A_(r')=sqrt(F) A_r. Thus C_A and R are
scalars at transported mesh markers; B/S remain fixed index maps.

The resulting Gram functional is invariant under spatial radial
relabelling of all these arguments in this patch. Omitting A_r fails
that covariance test. This is not yet a spacetime-covariance proof for
the combined wave/source/horizontal-leaf action; in particular A_t and
the nonzero-shift extension need their own variation. No independent
support inertia or adjustable coefficient is introduced.

On the discrete tests A is affine on each coordinate field cell and its
native-node A_r is the arithmetic mean of its two cell traces (one trace
at an endpoint). This is a declared finite-element convention. It must
be transported with the cell traces in any relabelling test; no unique
classical derivative is asserted at a C0 cell face. The analytic smooth
formula below and its specified weak discrete realization are distinct.

## 2. Derive the angular stress before imposing A=r

Let beta=r^2 N sqrt(F), load=S^T[(D_R q)^2/2], h_j=beta_j load_j,
and g_e=(partial U_G/partial R_e)_(C_A,q), the COMPLETE kernel covector
including native-knot scatter and ordinary/source interfaces. At A=r,
vary A at fixed coordinate markers, N,F and nodal q:

    delta_A U_G = sum_e g_e delta A(r_e)
                +sum_j h_j [2 delta A(r_j)/r_j+delta A_r(r_j)].

N and F variations leave R and the kernel fixed, and retain the same
Gram energy density and radial pressure as the physical candidate:

    rho_G=p_r,G=sum_j F_j load_j delta(r-r_j).

The reduced action convention is delta L/dA=2N r p_perp/sqrt(F),
with the angular integration normalization inherited from the old work.
Since L_G=-U_G, its weak angular response is

    2N r p_perp,G/sqrt(F)
      =-sum_e g_e delta(r-r_e)
       -sum_j (2h_j/r_j) delta(r-r_j)
       +sum_j h_j delta'(r-r_j).

Here delta' is defined by <delta'_j,eta>=-eta'(r_j); at finite cell
faces use the declared mean trace functional. Products with metric
prefactors are distributional products, not constant-prefactor shortcuts.
This is the ordinary area tension PLUS a computed kernel force and
coframe derivative stress. None is obtained by fitting a Ward residual.

## 3. The spatial Ward identity now uses the actual kernel shape

The old area-only Gram spatial moment on the same seed was

    W_old[eta]=sum_j h_j[-eta'_j+(beta_r/beta)_j eta_j].

The new angular term changes it by

    Delta W[eta]=sum_e g_e eta_e+sum_j h_j eta'_j.

Consequently

    W_new[eta]=sum_e g_e eta_e+sum_j load_j beta_r,j eta_j,

exactly the derivative of the native-radius potential under physical
marker displacement at fixed q and Eulerian metric. The old -h eta'
piece cancels the coframe derivative term; keeping both as extra source
forces would double-count it. The field virtual work adds U_q delta q
separately. W_new by itself is NOT the temporal mixed-current derivative.

For a stencil and its coefficient sampling entirely on one affine branch,
R=Jx+a gives D_R=J^(-1/2)D_x. Thus its shape power is

    beta S^T[(D_R q) (partial_t D_R)q]
       =-beta (J_t/J) load.

Cross-branch rows need the full kernel; do not assume this identity there.
Test it directly on the actual saved probes, where it explains why the
source power can already be present in gamma_t for gamma=beta/J.

For each pinned test use the exact finite A_e=r_e+epsilon eta_e and
