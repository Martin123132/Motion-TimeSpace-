# Stage180 — linear incident photon and gas channels

At each saved anchor event, all operator quantities are fixed. The Born driving
jump in the inherited implementation is

    B[phi,g] = phi(x_out)-phi(x_in)+r0*g,
    A0[phi,g] = -w0*c0*L0*B[phi,g],

where r0 is the anchor recoil-energy/temperature factor, L0 the test-function
jump with the inherited repaired primitive moments, w0 the full event weight,
and c0 the cached virtual/vacuum coefficient. Thus

    A0[phi,g] = A0[phi,0]+A0[0,g].

These are diagnostic incident channels. Each output has both photon energy and
the opposite gas-energy transfer. Incident gas-only does not mean gas output only.
Neither isolated incident channel replaces the saved physical history.

For half-width s=.01 or .005, define

    U_photon_s = C_s[A0(phi,0)], U_gas_s = C_s[A0(0,g)],
    U_s = U_photon_s+U_gas_s.

At fixed A0, K_g=A0[0,1] is independent of epoch and history. Therefore

    U_gas_s = K_g * C_s[g],
    DeltaU_gas = K_g * (C_.01[g]-C_.005[g]).

Evaluate K_g directly using unit incident gas departure, not by dividing a
response by a fitted gas amplitude. Sum event kernels with their original anchor
weights. The kernel columns must be identical across the two background/join
choices; the actual gas scalars retain their original heavy/cold histories.

Reconstruct event components, both curvatures, their discrepancy and the unit-gas
prediction at80digits; repeat at100digits. Retain the original high/low arrays.
The arithmetic residual combined-photon-gas is measured, not assigned to either
channel to force a pass. Normalize closure by the same saved combined norm at
each width/discrepancy and retain the1e-10 gate. Exact arithmetic cannot remove
errors already present in floating-point source evaluations.

For the full matched discrepancy, retain the Stage179 fixed and mixed virtual
terms, average-operator correction and nonvirtual matched response. Replace only
its anchor-history component by the two evaluated channels and measure closure.
Report signed projections and norms in the inherited mass-dual photon metric.
This locates the contribution in saved histories; it does not alone identify a
physical versus discretization origin. Baseline controls and original1% source
gate outcomes are reused unchanged.
