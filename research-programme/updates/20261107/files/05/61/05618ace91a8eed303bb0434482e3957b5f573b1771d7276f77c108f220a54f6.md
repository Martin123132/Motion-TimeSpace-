# Source-hinge negative-control correction

The first native-kernel validator passed its independent direct-stencil,
geometry-tangent, source-power, interval-current and signed action-derivative
checks, then failed its final requirement that removing the source hinge
must visibly change the energy of these saved seeds. That requirement was
not justified. The six actual MTS probes show no detectable energy change
from this omission; removing curvature atoms DOES change their energies.

Preserve validator attempt01 as failed. Do not describe its final gate as
a successful test or silently change an executed script.

Validator v2 replays all original derivative tests with unchanged tolerances.
It records the actual hinge-insensitivity and adds an explicit, labelled
manufactured control on each actual MTS mesh: set only the midpoint field
in the cell immediately right of the source to one. The source slope jump
is then exactly 4/H_right. Independently rebuild the compensated and
uncompensated four-native-knot operators and require their nonzero factor
difference to equal that known jump times the divided-difference hinge
response. This tests the compensation when there is a source jump to
compensate. It is a numerical negative control, not new physical data.

Actual-state evidence and manufactured-control evidence remain separate.
No action coefficient, saved physical state, previous output, or derivative
tolerance is changed. The v2 run records both flags and control magnitudes.
