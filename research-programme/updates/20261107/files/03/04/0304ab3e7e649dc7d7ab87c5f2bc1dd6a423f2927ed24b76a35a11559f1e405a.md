## 5. Positive phase and coupled derivative squares

Let
```text
D = 3(beta+y)^2+x(3y+2F).
```
Exact symbolic simplification gives
```text
d_phase = D/(3Omega^2)
A_ZZ = 9beta^2(3y+2F)/(4alpha r^2 D)
A_Zw = 3F h' beta(3beta-2F)/(2D)
det A = 9F^2 beta^2/(2alpha r^2 D).
```
A is the derivative block of S in the ordered variables (Z',w').
Equivalently,
```text
(Z',w') A (Z',w')^T
 = A_ZZ (Z'+eta w')^2 + [2F^2/(3y+2F)] w'^2

eta = 2alpha r^2 F h'(3beta-2F)/[3beta(3y+2F)].
```
For real h,h',s, x,y>=0. For r,alpha,F,beta>0, D and both square
coefficients are strictly positive. This proof includes the mixed derivative
coefficient, with either sign of h', rather than assuming it is small.

Furthermore
```text
d_phase >= [(beta+y)/(beta+x+y)]^2
        >= [beta/(beta+x)]^2
        >= (1004/1125)^2 = 0.7964570864...
```
in the stated box. The middle inequality follows because adding y>=0
increases the fraction. This is not an improvement to the previous 77% bound
in the same norm: pibar and the original pi_v are different variables.

A conservative bound y<=2alpha r^2 rho<=492/625 and F>=461/625
also gives the final scalar derivative pivot >=0.2835976647. Tighter inherited
bounds are possible but unnecessary for its sign. These are coefficient

a frequency or wave equation. See `results/vacuum-join-v2/status.json`.

the absent scalar sector; it does not assign it a zero physical mass. Limits
of its interior products and the endpoint domain are to be taken from inside.
