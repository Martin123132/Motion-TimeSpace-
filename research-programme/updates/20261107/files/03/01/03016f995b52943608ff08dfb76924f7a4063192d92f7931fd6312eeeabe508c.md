# Why source-flat visible fields cannot reuse the exact Gram-null shortcut

This is an algebraic statement about the retained native operator, independent of the frozen-metric root searches.

Let X_0<...<X_(n-1) be the physical native knots. Each row of D is a nonzero positive multiple of the third divided difference on four consecutive knots. If Dv=0, interpolate the first three values by the unique polynomial P of degree at most2. The first four-point equation forces v_3=P(X_3), since the coefficient of v_3 is nonzero. Induction on consecutive rows forces v_j=P(X_j) for every j. Conversely every quadratic gives Dv=0. Thus ker D consists precisely of sampled quadratics, even on the nonuniform physical mesh.

The retained template B contains a diagonal row sqrt(m_j) for each divided difference, with m_j>0, plus nonnegative-square adjacency and endpoint contributions. Therefore B is injective on the divided-difference vector. With positive sampled metric weights W, U_G=(BDv)^T W(BDv)/2 vanishes exactly when Dv=0.

For a source-flat state, the compensated source jump J is zero, so v is the uncompensated native sample vector. A zero neighbourhood contains at least three distinct native knots in these fixtures. If U_G=0, its sampled quadratic has three distinct zeros and is identically zero. Hence all native field samples vanish.

**Consequence:** a source-flat field that is nonzero on the physical native support cannot simultaneously use the old exact-zero-Gram-energy shortcut. This holds at every positive metric and for every ordered native mesh in the stated chart; tuning the metric does not evade it.

**Limits:** the native operator samples only a finite support. Vanishing there does not imply that every field midpoint or every continuum field value vanishes. Inter-knot bubbles can be invisible to the finite operator; this argument does not discard those degrees of freedom or claim their dynamics are consistent. Nor does U_G>0 imply B_i!=0 for some shape test: a nonzero-energy constrained stationary point is logically possible. The separate quadratic-form certificates address only their declared finite trial spaces and fixed geometry.

This rules out a specific shortcut, not source-flat data in general and not MTS. The next joint construction must either find a genuine nonzero-energy constrained state or use compatible nonflat source jets without imposing the previous common-curvature restriction.
