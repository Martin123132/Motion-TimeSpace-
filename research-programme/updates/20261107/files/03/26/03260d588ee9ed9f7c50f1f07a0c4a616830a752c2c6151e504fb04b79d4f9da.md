# Stage92: the frequency-dependent source and its insertion

This extends the same MTS radiation/gas/metric equations. Stage91 supplied
an energy contraction, not a source shape. Retain its sourced standard-
matter amplitudes and physical constants; no new parent interaction or
fitted parameter enters here.

## 1. The rectangular weak form

For each original photon test B_a and each saved physical departure
Phi_j with gas slope b_j, write for emission or inverse absorption

    d_a = B_a(x_h) + sign(s) B_a(|x_s|) - B_a(x_in),
    d_j = Phi_j(x_h) + sign(s) Phi_j(|x_s|) - Phi_j(x_in)
          + b_j d_e,
    C_aj = -integral W d_a d_j.

The gas test is d_e=(E_out-E_in)/T. Independent x and gas readouts are
kept beside all96 spectral tests. The energy identity d_x+d_e=0 holds
before integration. Elastic terms use the corresponding two-photon jump.
The source is98-by4, not an unnecessary full96-by96 response operator.

The real/inverse, matching subtraction, finite vacuum and finite thermal
virtual factors are inherited unchanged. Every thermal amplitude is read
from its exact Stage91 node, never estimated by kinematic rescaling.

For the displaced state the product rule is essential:

    delta(d_a d_j) = delta d_a d_j + d_a delta d_j.

The shifted photon frequency changes BOTH the test and the departure.
Canonical momentum and normalized electron-density response remain. One
may not multiply a scalar energy correction by an assumed temperature
template to replace these spectral contractions.

## 2. Infrared integration is stricter for number than energy

With the solved regular tail Phi(x)=b_IR x+..., a constant photon test has
real jump1, whereas the physical departure jump is O(x_s). Thermal soft
weight behaves as dx_s/x_s^2. Its number-source integrand can therefore
behave as dx_s/x_s. The additional soft energy factor makes the energy
readout less sensitive. A successful energy test does not demonstrate
convergence of number or all96 modes.

Keep the positive physical floor explicit. On each positive interval,

    x_s=exp(t),  dx_s=x_s dt.

This is an exact change of integration variable, not a physical infrared
closure. Compare logarithmic panels and their refinements with the
inherited linear panels. The old calculations are retained as controls.
Neither transformation integrates through the unresolved collective
region or proves a zero-floor limit. Results decide which finite-domain
source representation is numerically usable.

## 3. Leading overlap, same state and domain

For the leading recoil-free Gould process, z=h+s and0<s<=z/2,

    C_G[A] = -4 alpha theta^2/(3 pi I3)
       integral dz ds [z^4 G(s/z) n(z)(1+n(h))(1+n(s))/s]
       [A(s)+A(h)-A(z)] [Phi(s)+Phi(h)-Phi(z)].

Use the SAME joined physical profile and explicit seed/soft domains when
subtracting it from the matched source. Its energy jump is analytically
zero; its number jump is1. This is the overlap already present at leading
order, not a second positive correction to add on top of it.

The original basis columns are1/norm0 and(x-mean)/norm1. Their Gould
jumps are exactly1/norm0 and-mean/norm1. Implementing these event
identities avoids subtracting nearly equal energies numerically; it is
not an output projection to force conservation.

The existing parent differs both in its soft floor1e-5 and in using the
original in-band profile. Consequently keep three quantities distinct:

    recoil_or_finite_part = C_matched(joined,Dnew)-C_G(joined,Dnew),
    domain_state_change = C_G(joined,Dnew)-C_G(original,Doverlap),
    replacement = recoil_or_finite_part+domain_state_change
                = C_matched(joined,Dnew)-C_G(original,Doverlap).

Doverlap is the part of the old domain explicitly covered by this
calculation. The old source outside it is retained, not silently removed.
There is an important finite-window detail: in the reverse channel the
sampled incoming hard photon is h, not z=h+s. At leading order, detailed
balance rewrites both weights using the same three-photon Planck factor.
The Stage91 ordered weak integrals therefore select a reaction with weight

    window_reaction = (indicator_window(z)+indicator_window(h))/2.

Use the original half weights of the emission and inverse terms. A simple
z<=24 cutoff would omit reverse-channel events with h<=24<z<=48 and
mislabel a domain mismatch as recoil. The overlap evaluates both windows:
integrate(z,s) in emission and(h,s) in the reverse channel (unit Jacobian).
The original full-parent bank control, by contrast, retains its own z<=60
definition and is not silently changed into that averaged window.

The first difference is not the same as the second. If the newly included
tail is evolved, its associated Compton/BR terms and coupling must be
treated consistently too. A standalone added DC tail can destroy the
derived combined low-frequency number cancellation.

## 4. Insertion without changing the underlying parent

For source vector c, the retained spectral coefficients a obey

    M a' = R K(a-b t) + R c,

where K contains the old Compton and emission terms, t represents x, and
the supplied c must be the REPLACEMENT difference defined on the actual
parent domain. Its photon energy is t.c, and gas receives its negative.
The gas energy equation must retain finite heat capacity, not set b by
an equilibrium closure. The mass-matrix solve converts a weak source to
coefficient rate; it does not create a spectrum from energy alone.

For a first correction evaluated on a saved parent, c is an external
forcing at that perturbative order. Feeding the resulting corrected
state back into c is a higher iteration requiring a sourced response.
Neither interpolated time snapshots nor a frozen-time response establishes
a converged corrected cosmological history by itself.

## 5. Numerical representation

All96 original tests are retained. A dense log-frequency interpolant is
checked against direct recurrence and its derivative; the first two
columns retain analytic definitions. Reconstructing tiny x from
mean+(x-mean) is cancellation-conditioned. The preserved raw readout test
used an overly strict relative tolerance; the companion test uses the
derived absolute floating-point allowance20 eps(sum absolute products+|x|).
This changes no source-vector or energy-transfer tolerance.

Projection precision is measured in the mass-dual norm of the actual
96-component source. Counts of checks are not proofs of the parent theory.
No phenomenological refit or new gravitational action is introduced.

The constant-test moment uses the inherited energy normalization I3.
Convert it to a physical photon-number fraction per Thomson time by
multiplying chi I3/(2 zeta(3)); energy instead multiplies chi directly.

## 6. Leading subtraction/addback is an integration identity

Let C be the full matched source and G its known leading Gould functional
on the SAME joined state and forward/reverse reaction window. Then

    C = (C-G)+G,
    C_est = Q_C[C]-Q_G[G]+Q_fine[G].

The coefficient1 is fixed by this identity. No projection of unwanted
high-frequency modes is permitted. Q_G retains the inherited half-weighted
spontaneous/thermal/inverse decomposition and the corresponding positive
soft panels; its angular leading integral is known analytically. The
matched recoil and thermal-velocity departures remain in the difference.

Because the two finite quadratures are not identical continuum integrals,
the identity alone does not prove numerical accuracy. We independently
refine the full estimator, and separately verify that the refined sampled
leading term approaches the independent addback. Both outer and real
FULL-source norm changes pass1%; the raw estimator fails and is retained.
The small difference C-G can still have substantial relative error.

For a constant-epoch linear response use

    A = alpha M-R K,
    f = A^(-1) R c,  r=A^(-1)(-R K t),
    delta a=f+r delta b,
    delta b=[R(t.c+c_g)-alpha E.f]/
            [chi(alpha+1)+alpha E.r],  E=t^T M.

This follows by adding the photon and finite-capacity gas energy equations;
it does not set the gas to equilibrium. The independent gas source c_g is
retained, including any floating-point pairing residual. The implicit-step
work is duration times chi delta b. Direct equation and energy/work
residuals are checked. Here this is a diagnostic on the unchanged parent
linear system, not a newly evolved physical cosmological history.
