## What is derived

A local matching calculation gives the heavy mode's contact interaction, its long-range forces, and its conversion into lighter modes from the same parent coefficients. A second calculation reconstructs its one-particle density matrix from field and momentum correlations. Together they separate three questions: how much matter is present, whether it is coherent, and whether its interactions preserve that population.

The most useful new restriction is that a single inherited quartic potential couples repulsion and decay. Increasing repulsion is not a free stability adjustment. The measured cosmology response in Stage51 is unchanged.

## 2 Interaction coefficients after rotation

Define the vertices by factorial-normalized potential terms. Direct differentiation gives
\[
 g_{hhh}=-r^{3/2}U_3,\qquad
 \gamma_{\ell hh}=\sqrt Z\,rU_3,\qquad
 g_{h\ell\ell}=-\sqrt r\,ZU_3,
\]
\[
 \lambda_{hhhh}=r^2U_4,\qquad
 \lambda_{h\ell\ell\ell}=-\sqrt r\,Z^{3/2}U_4 .
\]
For example \(V\supset\gamma_{\ell hh}\ell h^2/2+
g_{h\ell\ell}h\ell^2/2+\lambda_{h\ell\ell\ell}h\ell^3/6\).
The mixed vertices are inherited with the self-interaction; omitting them would change the action.

For the leading nonrelativistic carrier
\[
 h=\frac{\Psi e^{-iMt}+\Psi^*e^{iMt}}{\sqrt{2M}},\qquad n=|\Psi|^2 ,
\]
the physical local number density has dimension three. Averaging at fixed slow envelope yields
\[
 \langle h^4\rangle=\frac{3n^2}{2M^2},\qquad
 \langle\dot h^4\rangle=\frac32 M^2n^2 .
\]
Writing \({\cal L}_{{\rm NR,int}}=-g_{\rm contact}n^2/2\),
\[
 \boxed{g_{\rm contact}=r^2\left(\frac{U_4}{8M^2}+3C_2M^2\right)},\qquad
 \lambda_{\rm th}=8M^2g_{\rm contact}=r^2(U_4+24C_2M^4).
\]
An independent all-incoming four-leg vertex gives the derivative contribution
\(8C_2r^2\sum_{\rm pairings}(p_i\cdot p_j)(p_k\cdot p_l)
=24C_2r^2M^4\) at threshold, with the common amplitude convention
\({\cal M}=-\lambda_{\rm th}\).
Without cubic exchange, short-range repulsion requires \(U_4+24C_2M^4>0\).
The leading Born scattering length is \(a_s=Mg_{\rm contact}/(4\pi)\);
this is not an assertion that the microscopic potential is hard-sphere repulsion.

## 3 Exchange forces and a stability equation

Cubic exchange cannot be omitted merely because a quartic is present. The heavy \(s,t,u\) propagator sum at threshold is
\[
 \frac1{4M^2-M^2}+\frac2{-M^2}=-\frac5{3M^2}.
\]
Keep soft light exchange explicit and integrate only the hard contribution:
\[
 \boxed{g_{\rm hard}=
 \frac{\lambda_{\rm th}-5g_{hhh}^2/(3M^2)
       +\gamma_{\ell hh}^2/(4M^2-\mu^2)}{8M^2}},
\]
\[
 \widetilde V_\ell(q)=-\frac{\gamma_{\ell hh}^2}
                           {4M^2(q^2+\mu^2)},\qquad
 \widetilde V_G(q)=-\frac{4\pi GM^2}{q^2}.
\]
Here \(q\) is physical spatial momentum, not a quotient map. These are leading weak-coupling, static, weak-gravity expressions away from the \(2M=\mu\) resonance. In particular the attractive light force follows by minimizing
\[
 E_\ell=\tfrac12(q^2+\mu^2)\ell^2+\frac{\gamma_{\ell hh}}{2M}n\ell .
\]
Only for \(q\ll\mu\) may the soft force also be approximated by a contact. Then
\[
 g_{\rm all\ local}
 =\frac{\lambda_{\rm th}-5g_{hhh}^2/(3M^2)
 +\gamma_{\ell hh}^2[(4M^2-\mu^2)^{-1}-2/\mu^2]}{8M^2}.
\]
Using this coefficient and separately adding the same Yukawa force would double count. A cosmologically light \(\mu\) generally requires the explicit mediator instead.

The isolated heavy-fluid density response in a local static patch is
\[
 \boxed{\omega^2(q)=\frac{q^4}{4M^2}
       +\frac{nq^2}{M}[g_{\rm hard}+\widetilde V_\ell(q)+\widetilde V_G(q)]}.
\]
With \(U_3=U_4=C_2=0\), it returns the Stage49 Jeans expression
\(q^4/(4M^2)-4\pi GMn\). This is a useful limit check, not a substitute for the expanding multi-component perturbation equations. Even a positive short-range coefficient cannot by itself remove long-wavelength gravitational instability. Background-dependent derivative vertices and a time-dependent light response are outside this local static calculation.

 \boxed{\Gamma_{h\to3\ell}
 =\frac{U_4^2rZ^3M}{3072\pi^3}} .

## 5 Coherence from the retained canonical variables

For a regulated finite set of canonical heavy modes, define
\[
 b(x)=\sqrt{M/2}\,q_h(x)+\frac{i\,p_h(x)}{\sqrt{2M}},
 \qquad \alpha(x)=\langle b(x)\rangle .
\]
Let \(C_{qq},C_{pp},C_{qp},C_{pq}\) be the connected symmetrized equal-time covariance kernels. The canonical commutator then gives
\[
 \boxed{N_1(x,y)=\langle b^\dagger(x)b(y)\rangle
 =\alpha^*(x)\alpha(y)+\frac M2C_{qq}(x,y)
 +\frac1{2M}C_{pp}(x,y)
 +\frac i2[C_{qp}(x,y)-C_{pq}(x,y)]
 -\frac12\delta(x-y)} .
\]
The subtraction is the canonical commutator contribution, not an independently chosen negative population. In a finite orthonormal mode basis it is simply half the identity. Continuum observables require a specified regulator, mode projection and renormalized state; do not trace an unregulated coincident delta function. A relativistic momentum-dependent vacuum uses \(\omega_k=\sqrt{M^2+k^2}\) rather than this leading low-momentum reference \(M\).

For FLRW, \(q_h=a^{3/2}h\) and its boundary-transformed conjugate \(p_h=\dot q_h\) give the comoving canonical version. The late adiabatic/nonrelativistic regime supplies its approximate particle interpretation. It does not define a unique exact particle number during onset.

For \(N=\operatorname{Tr}N_1>0\), evaluate
\[
 f_0=\frac{\lambda_{\max}(N_1)}{\operatorname{Tr}N_1}.
\]
The connected field-only covariance in 4873 is not enough: momentum correlations and the mean must be retained. A coherent state has connected vacuum fluctuations but nonzero \(\alpha^*\alpha\); discarding the mean would discard its population.

An explicit counterexample keeps \(N=1000\) while comparing one occupied mode with uniform occupation of 8,32,128 modes. The fractions are respectively 1 versus 0.125,0.03125,0.0078125. They have equal leading rest-mass density, not necessarily equal gradient energy or full stress. Consequently neither the cosmological energy fraction \(F\) nor coordinate overlap \(r\) determines \(f_0\). A supplied covariance and preparation could be used to compute it rather than guessing it.
