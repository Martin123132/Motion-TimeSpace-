# Bounded recovery of the position-reaction diagnostic

This is execution plumbing, not a new physics result or relaxed numerical gate.
The slower refined-grid calculation must not force another four-hour overrun
or require the completed reference controls to be recalculated.

The versioned recovery runner is
`scripts/diagnose_annular_position_reaction_solve_v3_20260929.py`.
It requires a settled diagnostic parent, rehashes its inputs and outputs,
and preserves every completed case and its original accuracy flags.
It also reuses complete, hashed material48/material64 archives from an
interrupted case; finite values, shapes, and quadrature orders are checked
before use. A partly filled in-memory integration is not a saved archive.

The original momentum oracle, native step-halving check, live solve,
independent corrected Euler evaluation, and sensitivity solve are unchanged.
The runner does not infer the corrected Euler check from a small linear
solver residual. A saved reaction with no completed re-evaluation remains
unfinished and is not promoted into the completed-case list.

By default it adds at most ONE newly completed control, then writes an
explicit remaining-case list. `state=complete` means that bounded diagnostic
invocation completed; `all_four_diagnostics_complete` separately states
whether all four controls exist. `accepted_for_C0` and
`numerical_acceptance_passed` remain false in either case. Runtime guards
may still interrupt an individual case; complete archives remain reusable.

Use `--dry-run` with a unique evidence label after the parent has settled
to verify all hashes and record the recovery plan without launching a
numerical calculation. Dry-run output is explicitly marked `run_kind=dry_run`
and cannot itself be used as a numerical recovery parent.

Private operation only. One numerical core, below-normal priority, no
subagents, no GitHub actions, and no original-workbench writes. Do not alter
any previously executed scientific source or result to make recovery pass.
