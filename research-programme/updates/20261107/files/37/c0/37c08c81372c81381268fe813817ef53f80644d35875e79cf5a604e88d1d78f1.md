# What the saved soft matrices mean

Both `banks/polarized-singular104` and `banks/finite-shift-soft104-l4` describe
the **unresolved hard-process pole**. Their real and virtual signs cancel for
the same event, bath occupation and resolution definition. This does not mean
that the entire double-Compton reaction cancels or that photon number is
conserved by it.

`banks/polarized-singular104` is the cold/leading-energy eikonal result. Its
formula label `C_real,pole` must be read with the hard-process qualifier here.
It extends the temperature-template singularity checked in Stage81 to hard
polarization channels. It is NOT the singular coefficient of the unrestricted
three-photon operator for every possible soft-frequency trial function.

For an infrared-safe detector/test profile, the weight assigned to a photon
vanishes as its energy tends to zero. For photon number the weight is1, not0.
Its event difference is1-1-1=-1 even when the hard event's difference1-1=0.
Number-changing emission/absorption must remain in the resolved kinetic sector.
Applying this virtual pole as a blanket cancellation of DC photon creation
would be an error.

The finite-shift bank uses the invariant electron-pair current on the actual
Juttner/Compton event quadrature. It does not use1/(1+14.16theta). Do not combine
its virtual matrices with Stage81's scalar-corrected Gould real pole: those
coefficients differ and would leave a spurious inverse-resolution term.
Stage81 backgrounds are preserved as approximate backgrounds, not silently
relabeled as a complete finite-temperature inclusive collision calculation.

The finite soft characteristic functional is normalized and cutoff-convergent
for a thermal soft bath. Its separator from hard radiation remains explicit.
No arbitrary finite hard virtual term was chosen, and no full spectral wave
calculation is authorized by these pole checks alone. The first-heat emitting
background also needs its low-frequency occupation controlled in the matching;
a finite-window spectral fit does not prove that control at zero frequency.
