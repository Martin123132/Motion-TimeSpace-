## Arithmetic versus time discretization

At each fixed grid and probe rule, the exact bookkeeping identity is

    new80 - saved_old = (new80 - increment64) + (increment64 - saved_old).

For the quarter-grid candidate D:

| Background | Increment64 minus saved old | New80 minus increment64 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| heavy | +2.66861139704e-8 | -1.31018244145e-10 |
| cold | -1.70900039689e-7 | +7.29001995646e-12 |

Thus most of this correction is already recovered by the well-conditioned
increment formulation in ordinary precision. The first term includes arithmetic
ordering, assembly and solver representation, not solely state storage: the old
positive-definite solve may use one triangle, whereas the new general solve uses
the full inherited matrix, as Stage188 did. We have isolated a numerical
representation effect, not uniquely identified one floating-point operation.

The new80 versus increment64 gas-history differences remain below 6.27e-15
across all eight histories; old versus new80 differences reach 3.11e-12.
Small state differences matter because D has weights up to 60000.

After carrying precision, the candidate single-partial absolute half/quarter
D differences are 9.65928e-10 / 1.37733e-9. These are observed refinement
differences, not rigorous bounds on the exact continuum solution.
An independent 100-digit full trajectory was not run: the 100-digit controls
cover the initial pilot block and quarter probe evaluations. Initial-state
and physical-quadrature uncertainties remain inherited.
