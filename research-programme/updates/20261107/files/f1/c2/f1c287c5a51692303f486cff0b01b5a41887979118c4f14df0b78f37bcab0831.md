# Sufficient expanding-branch condition in the retained physical weak model

This supplements the actual implemented Jacobian/root checks, not replaces
them. Stage227 supplied the physical Compton/DC/BR passivity structure,
including between-knot BR controls. Assume that same exact symmetric weak
form, positive physical mass M and its stated energy-row defect. No new
attraction gap, zero-coupling axiom or fitted derivative is assumed.

At fixed previous states, define gamma=C(alpha-1)+2,
G=diag(alpha M,chi gamma), and b as in DERIVATION.md. Let

    B=||b||_(G^-1), epsilon=||(Erow-t^T M)M^-1/2||,
    eta=sqrt(alpha/(chi gamma))*epsilon < 1,
    L=sqrt(Erow M^-1 Erow^T/alpha+chi C^2/gamma).

The resolvent argument gives a uniform heat bound and logarithmic derivative:

    |q_heat| <= Q=chi[A0+L B/(1-eta)],
    |q_heat,lnH| <= T=chi^2 |C-2| B/[4alpha sqrt(chi gamma)(1-eta)^2].

For each field let b_j=(m history_phi,history_pi/a). The positive symmetric
part of its implicit matrix bounds rho_j by |b_j|^2/(2alpha^2). Write their
sum as P. With rho_bare=rho_reference and rho_floor=rho_bare-rho_star Q>0,

    H_lower=a sqrt(rho_floor/3),
    H_upper=a sqrt((rho_bare+rho_star Q+P)/3).

All positive roots lie inside these limits: the field energy is positive,
bounded above by P, and heat has magnitude at most rho_star Q. At these
endpoints F has opposite weak signs. Within the bracket,

    |rho_fields,lnH| <= D=sum_j |b_j|^2 (a m_j/H_lower)/alpha^3,
    J >= 1-(D+rho_star T)/(2rho_floor).

A strictly positive last expression supplies existence and uniqueness of the
expanding step root within this physical weak-model class. It is a sufficient
condition, not a fitted test threshold. It is evaluated at every saved step
for NR/EOS on both parents and both time resolutions.

Important scope: saved floating coefficients evaluate the sizes entering this
physical theorem. M is interpreted as its symmetric physical Gram form.
The theorem is not an outward-rounded interval certificate of every stored
binary matrix, including asymmetric roundoff in the original implementation.
The code continues using the inherited upper-triangle convention, without
editing the bank; independent80-digit coupled-block checks and actual numeric
Jacobians cover that represented solve. Do not conflate these two claims.

The physical weak form is the same approximation being evolved, not a new
easier model substituted for MTS. This theorem says nothing about arbitrary
nonlinear collision operators outside that named approximation, or every
possible initial condition. Its coefficients here come from the actual
computed histories and known positive density components.
