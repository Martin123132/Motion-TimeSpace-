# Stage97: changing directions without changing the source

The hard scattering-angle integral in the SAME parent source is

    integral_-1^1 dc W(c) [D(c)+V(c)+S(c)+C0(c)] - Gsampled + Gaccurate.

The normalized Born factor, Doppler factors, recoil, physical profiles and
gas partner remain in W and the component functions as in the parent. The
three-point rule is replaced by a five-point Gauss rule, not by a fit to data.
All new directions require their own actual-node virtual/state coefficients.

## What can legitimately be reused

For a node with identical incoming frequency, electron velocity and hard
cosine, the component array is independent of its quadrature weight. Therefore
the array can be reused with a NEW weight if its identity and sealed hash match.
This is particularly useful at zero cosine shared by the odd-order rules.
Reusing a coefficient from a different direction is not justified.

The source retains the separate incoming resolutions established in Stage96:
compact128 for K=C0-Gsampled, seed4 for D/V/S. These are estimators of linear
integrals over the same physical source, not interpolated effective coefficients.
The leading subtraction has an analytic hard-direction integral; it does not
change when the numerical hard rule changes. We check that its saved array is
identical, keeping the forward/inverse windows unchanged.

## Refinement budget and its coverage

For each component define Delta_hard = value_hard5-value_hard3 at the SAME
component-specific incoming rule. The complete hard comparison is the sum of
those changes. Its norm is bounded by the sum of the computed component norms.
We add the separately retained Stage96 incoming differences, normalizing every
term by the NEW assembled remainder norm, not the old unresolved estimate.

The original frozen-background photon/gas response is linear in both sources.
Each difference is propagated through that same coupled response, retaining
the independent gas and energy/work equations. Photon and gas component-sum
ratios each face the1% observed threshold.

These are differences between computed rules, not bounds on the exact continuum
error. In particular, the inherited incoming differences were measured at hard3.
The mixed difference

    (J_seed4,hard5-J_seed3,hard5) - (J_seed4,hard3-J_seed3,hard3)

for expensive J=D+V+S is not computed merely by testing hard5 at seed4. Do not
call the combined ledger a uniform two-dimensional error theorem. Its exact
scope, and any need for that cross-check, must be stated with the results.

Electron-grid, internal-loop, frequency-domain/tail and physical-history tests
remain separate. No field, coupling, source mode or gas variable is removed.
