# Microscopic curvature, not a fitted temperature curve

Write a = D ln W, P' = D P, h' = D h and D = d/d ln(theta).
The source functional F(P,h) is linear in the incident photon/gas departure h.
Directly differentiating the first-derivative law gives

    D^2 S = -sum W {
        (a^2 + D a) F
        + 2 a [F_P P' + F_h h']
        + F_PP[P',P'] + F_P P''
        + 2 F_Ph[P',h'] + F_h h''
    }.

There is no F_hh[h',h'] term because F is linear in h. In the fixed-incident
control h'=h''=0. In the moving-history calculation the native saved h and
its variation are external inputs, not newly solved physical backgrounds.
The numerical second differences evaluate this full curvature, not just
the explicit thermal mass coefficient. Neither prediction fits a new exponent.

## Closed electron-population factors

Let r=theta*y, gamma=1+r, beta=sqrt(r*(2+r))/gamma, and

    b = D ln beta = 1/[gamma*(gamma+1)],
    D b = -r*(2*gamma+1)/[gamma^2*(gamma+1)^2],
    D beta = beta*b,
    D^2 beta = beta*(b^2 + D b).

For the unnormalized radial weight q*gamma*sqrt(2+r), let

    a_j = r_j/gamma_j + r_j/[2*(gamma_j+1)],
    D a_j = r_j/gamma_j^2 + r_j/(gamma_j+1)^2,
    c_j = a_j - <a>_w.

Normalized weights satisfy

    D w_j = w_j*c_j,
    D<a>_w = <Da>_w + Var_w(a),
    D^2 w_j = w_j*[c_j^2 + D a_j - <Da>_w - Var_w(a)].

Both sum D w_j and sum D^2 w_j vanish. The event measure also includes
the Born differential factor sigma. At fixed comoving seed and angular nodes,

    D ln W_j = c_j + D ln sigma_j.

Differentiating only the bare thermal weight would miss its normalization
and the Born factor. Born normalizations inside F must move consistently too.

For a Bose factor n(z)=1/(exp(z)-1),

    D^2 n = n*(1+n)*[(1+2*n)*(D z)^2 - D^2 z].

Here z itself changes through recoil, boost and physical integration limits.
These identities do not reduce the matched source to a single temperature power.

## Second derivative of the matched integral

For the finite spontaneous remainder R and A=a_pole*P0,

    I = (1/2) integral_l^b R(xi,omega) d omega + (1/2) A ln(2b).

Its curvature is

    2 I'' = integral_l^b R_xixi d omega
            + 2 R_xi(b)b' + R_omega(b)(b')^2 + R(b)b''
            - 2 R_xi(l)l' - R_omega(l)(l')^2 - R(l)l''
            + A'' ln(2b) + 2 A'b'/b
            + A*[b''/b - (b'/b)^2].

The thermal part obeys the same rule with its Bose factor included.
Artificial adjacent-panel boundary terms cancel where the integrand agrees.
Physical emission/absorption thresholds retain boundary terms; a change in
ordering must be treated piecewise, not silently differentiated through a max.
The actual nearby-temperature evaluations rebuild the original limits.

## What the test establishes

P1 and P2 are Taylor predictions from the local microscopic source evaluations.
For a three-times differentiable source, the second-order remainder is bounded
by |delta|^3 sup ||D^3 S||/6 on the entire interval. This stage does not
establish that supremum; two step sizes alone do not certify it. The independent
temperature test, derivative-stability gates and retained event/moment checks
are therefore all necessary and are reported separately.
