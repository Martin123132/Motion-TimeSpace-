# Why the first attractive overlap result is not the result to promote

The original band solve and the saved inner solve use the same
two-point invariant-preserving transport interpolation. They can agree
closely because they share an error. Preserving constant chemical
potential and equal-temperature equilibrium is necessary, but does not
make that interpolation exact on curved spectra.

The old coarse/fine overlap comparison changes the result by up to
2.784 times the ORIGINAL inner-minus-outer band mismatch. That cannot be
hidden behind a small error relative to the much larger total spectrum.
The first candidate's roughly 99.7% endpoint agreement is therefore not
evidence that it predicts the physical overlap at that precision.

## Direct check, not just an inference from grid dependence
`results/background-scattering/status.json` evaluates the known outer
spectrum at actual saved Doppler shifts, at four frequencies, three
epochs and both frequency grids. All six baseline/correction columns
are tested. This reference does not deposit shifts on the transport mesh.

Four-point background interpolation has at most 0.000177777 times the
two-point source error in these checks; six-point has at most
0.00000275409 times that error. These are maxima over the executed
tests, not global mathematical bounds or observational improvements.

The defect-only solver uses this better-known forcing while retaining
the positive low-order generator on the SMALL unknown difference.
It changes no MTS coefficient, physical source rate or joining window.
Its grid/time/forcing controls are separate from the old failed controls.

## What survives and what needs recalculation
- The collision identities, number tests, orthogonal projection identity
  and exact bulk/join decomposition remain valid mathematical statements.
- Saved parent/baseline histories, action work and empirical results are
  unchanged. No new data fit or physical-model preference follows here.
- The original low-frequency reconstruction and any subsequent small
  feedback using it retain their conditional status; their numerical
  convergence on the much smaller joining-correction scale is not
  established by their earlier full-spectrum checks.
- This common-band repair is a diagnostic and a tested numerical method,
  not an already-updated full inner solution. Its two-sided saved outer
  exterior is not substituted for the derived infrared boundary law.
- A proper next calculation must use the improved background treatment
  on the original inner domain, retain DC/BR, memory and the derived
  lower exterior, then recompute the stored-moment-subtracted feedback.
  It must also carry the bridge; discarding it is not a physical repair.

Do not describe disappearance of a grid artefact as disappearance of an
MTS physical effect. Conversely, do not count that artefact as MTS evidence.
Baseline and corrections exhibit the same numerical mechanism.
