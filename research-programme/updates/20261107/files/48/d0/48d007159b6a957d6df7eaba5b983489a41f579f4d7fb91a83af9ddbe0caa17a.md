# Source-flat native-state search: live metric feedback

The inner search uses the actual native interaction but freezes the radial geometry of the balanced source-flat state. That is an inner solve, not a reason to accept or reject a state without checking its own geometry.

Predeclare a feedback probe on the best fit from the largest (24-mode) family at material order48, on both retained spatial grids. Use it whether or not it is a root. Do not select a different family using withheld-test performance, tune the source acceleration, or reduce the nonzero field norm toward zero.

Reconstruct the trial field from the saved Decimal basis and coefficients. Retain the source coordinates and zero rates. Recompute the native radial equations with the physical native load, not the old fixed-reference factor. Evaluate all33 source/end-pinned shape forces on that geometry at material orders32 and48. Separately replay the same field on the original frozen geometry against its stored quadratic forms.

For the field coefficient vector a, the same action gives

    B_i(a;g) = a^T Q_i(g) a.

Because the source map is fixed, the native image and its shape tangent are metric-independent in this probe; Q depends on the metric through beta and beta_R. Thus the feedback comparison is between Q(g[a]) and Q(g[balanced]), not between different physical operators. Native radial energy must agree with direct material energy. A scalar-adjoint oracle independently checks the fast tangent contraction.

Use fixed scales from the full24-mode frozen matrices to compare forces between family sizes and the two metrics. Size-dependent conditioning scales from the search cannot by themselves demonstrate convergence as the trial space grows.

A positive combination sum_i lambda_i Q_i - kappa H proves an obstruction only for those stored finite matrices and their fixed geometry. At a new metric it persists if the H-normalized operator norm of sum_i lambda_i delta Q_i is smaller than kappa. This sufficient perturbation criterion is derived from the Rayleigh quotient; it is not assumed satisfied by a small change in one scalar field evaluation. The scalar feedback probe does not certify that operator bound.

An unsuccessful frozen root search alone is not an exclusion. Conversely, a source-compatible profile or a small change after metric feedback is not a root. Do not start physical evolution until the actual constraints and their preservation have been addressed.
