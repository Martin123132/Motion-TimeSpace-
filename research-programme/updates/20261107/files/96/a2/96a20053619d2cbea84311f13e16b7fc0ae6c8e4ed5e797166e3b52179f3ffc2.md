# Independent product-rule evaluation of the position force

The Eulerian force derivative contains a large transported-gradient
contribution which cancels its moving-face integral. Rather than treating
agreement with this ill-conditioned subtraction as the sole authority,
verify the material force through the independent explicit product-rule
derivative already derived in annular_position_support_column_20260930.py.

This new check evaluates that derivative on the actual complete integral,
not just on selected points. It does not call either density function or
differentiate the material density with a complex step. It builds the
baseline kinematics and their tangents, differentiates alpha, beta and
their radial/time coefficients by the explicit product rule, and adds
the U_R times baseline-force measure term. The input position, velocity
and reaction fields are unchanged.

Both implementations are consequently compared AFTER analytically
cancelling the known transport total derivative, not after fitting a
correction to a residual. Their mathematical equivalence follows from
the transport identity; their arithmetic is independently evaluated.
96-digit finite-family point checks anchor the derivative, and24/28 and
32/36 full integrations test both reference and MTS without changing
the2e-17 numerical tolerance.

The same already computed radial-cut atoms and native contribution are
added to both force columns. Source-clock and gravitational entries are
current rows, not added to the force. This shared-boundary check tests
the force bulk independently; it is not an independent rederivation of
those reused boundaries. Original Eulerian cross-method/paired failures
remain historical failures if their numbers still exceed the gate.
