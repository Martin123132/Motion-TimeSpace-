## What actually explains the residual

For the warm6-5 first-column pair defect, the real-collision contribution is-4.36076e-10, the virtual term-9.14394e-13 and the state term-2.07613e-11, all normalized to the total energy difference. The real term dominates.

Eight worst-event tests show that compensated radial/angular summation alone leaves that defect essentially unchanged. The issue lies before summation, in subtracting the Born limit from the soft event.

`SOFT-SUBTRACTION-DERIVATION.md` derives a correlated difference evaluation. It computes the hard-photon shift and independent electron recoil-energy shift directly, then subtracts the Born product algebraically before quadrature. It changes neither the collision amplitudes, cutoff nor weights, and never sets electron=-photon.

On the same eight worst events, the follow-up probe reaches pair defects no larger than7.56143e-16. Nonzero residuals improve by743.67–11499times; five column residuals round to zero and are not described as infinite-accuracy results. These are eight-event probes, not a completed correlated-source calculation. The original full-run gate remains failed.
