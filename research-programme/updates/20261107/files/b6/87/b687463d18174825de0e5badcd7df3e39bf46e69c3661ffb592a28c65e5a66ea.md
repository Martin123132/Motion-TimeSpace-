# Exact inner counterpart of the source correction

This derives the missing operator link;it is not yet an installed time step.

For one unit-rate event,u=1/r,the source action is
F_u v=[u v(x/u)+u^2 v(ux)-(u+u^2)v(x)]/2.
Its inner counterpart under integral x v f dx is
H_u f=[f(x/u)+u^3 f(ux)-(u+u^2)f(x)]/2.
Changing variables proves H_u*=F_u on the full domain with integrable
fields. Differentiation in u commutes with this adjoint when the fields
and derivatives are dominated sufficiently to interchange operations.

Consequently the equation-derived inner defect is
D2_inner=sum w V partial_u^2 H_u/2,
D3_inner=sum w mu3 partial_u^3 H_u/6,
and its weak adjoint is exactly the D2,D3 measured in this stage.
The corrected operator is H_grid/c-D2_inner-D3_inner,not an independently
chosen new energy term or fitted coupling.

The explicit derivatives are:
H''f=[2x u^-3 f'(x/u)+x^2 u^-4 f''(x/u)
      +6u f(ux)+6u^2 x f'(ux)+u^3 x^2 f''(ux)-2f(x)]/2,
H'''f=[-6x u^-4 f'(x/u)-6x^2 u^-5 f''(x/u)
       -x^3 u^-6 f'''(x/u)+6f(ux)+18u x f'(ux)
       +9u^2 x^2 f''(ux)+u^3 x^3 f'''(ux)]/2.

For f=x^p,the symbol is
h_p(u)=[u^-p+u^(p+3)-u-u^2]/2.
For a source test x^q,its symbol is h_(-q-2),identically.
The chemical mode p=-1 and adjoint-number mode p=-2 vanish eventwise;
p=-3 is the source energy eigenvalue. All u derivatives inherit these
identities. This does not establish positivity of the corrected stencil:
subtracting derivative defects can introduce signed terms.

## Finite boundaries,not a silent zero-flux assumption

On [a,b],with a physical extension of f wherever shifted arguments need it,
the exact identity is
<v,H_u f>_[a,b]=<F_u v,f>_[a,b]+B_u(v,f),
where
B_u = u^2/2*(integral_(a/u)^(b/u)-integral_a^b)
                  y v(uy) f(y) dy
    + u/2*(integral_(au)^(bu)-integral_a^b)
                  y v(y/u) f(y) dy.

Thus a derivative-defect correction carries the corresponding
V partial_u^2 B_u/2 and mu3 partial_u^3 B_u/6. The derivatives include
moving-boundary endpoint terms. Zero extension is one boundary condition,
not a physical derivation. The matching window also must be handled:
H_u(W f)-W H_u f =
[(W(x/u)-W(x))f(x/u)+u^3(W(ux)-W(x))f(ux)]/2.
Its u derivatives determine the matching-window commutator corrections.
These boundary/window pieces must accompany any inner implementation.

test_inner_counterpart.py checks the derivative formulas and symbol
duality with exact rational power tests,and the finite-boundary identity
against explicit rational integrals. It is not a full generator-stability
or positivity test and does not replace continuous later-state evolution.
