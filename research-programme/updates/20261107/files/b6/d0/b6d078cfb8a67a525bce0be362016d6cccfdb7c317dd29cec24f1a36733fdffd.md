# Spatial correction from the event deposition law

The physical weak source for one event,in u=1/r,is
F(u;x)=[u v(x/u)+u^2 v(xu)-(u+u^2)v(x)]/2.
The saved positive deposition replaces u by a two-point variable U with
E[U]=u and endpoints a>=u>=b. Set
V=(a-u)(u-b),
mu3=V(a+b-2u).
Then E[F(U)]-F(u)=V F''(u)/2+mu3 F'''(u)/6+R4.

The derivatives for ANY sufficiently differentiable test v are:
F''=[x^2 u^-3 v''(x/u)+2(v(xu)-v(x))
     +4xu v'(xu)+x^2 u^2 v''(xu)]/2,
F'''=[-3x^2 u^-4 v''(x/u)-x^3 u^-5 v'''(x/u)
      +6x v'(xu)+6x^2 u v''(xu)+x^3 u^2 v'''(xu)]/2.

Let D2=sum w V F''/2,D3=sum w mu3 F'''/6.
Stage130's normalized source A_grid=c A_deposited,where
c=lambda_physical/lambda_deposited is already fixed by the kernel.
Define corrections without using the measured physical-reference matrix:
A_2=A_grid/c-D2,
A_3=A_grid/c-D2-D3.
Hence A_2-A_physical=D3+R4 and A_3-A_physical=R4.
No data fit or empirical tuning enters these coefficients.
The reference matrix is used only for independent comparisons.

For v=1,every term vanishes. For v=x,F is a cubic in u:
F=x(1-u-u^2+u^3)/2.
Its exact eigenvalue-deposition defect is
delta lambda=sum w V(a+b+u-1)/2.
D2 has energy moment sum w V(3u-1)/2;
D3 has energy moment sum w mu3/2.
Their sum equals the exact bias,so A_3 preserves the physical energy
moment exactly in arithmetic,while variance-only A_2 need not.
No energy row of A_2 is silently forced to agree with the reference.

For twice Gram-Schmidt-projected saved rational basis functions,
differentiate the stored recurrence analytically. Compact coordinate
z=(x-p)/(x+p) has derivatives2p/(x+p)^2,-4p/(x+p)^3,
12p/(x+p)^4. Leibniz coefficients determine the first three derivatives.
Use80-digit independent numerical differentiation for preflight,not
finite-step tuning of the physical calculation.

The unnormalised weak quadrature includes1/I3:the factor1/2 is already
inside F and its derivatives. compact_defect supplies x times the slope
and fixed matching window. Keep the existing convention and grid.

For the Taylor remainder,
|R4|<=sum w E[(U-u)^4] sup_(t in [b,a])|F''''(t)|/24,
provided that fourth derivative is bounded on each cell.
This is a conditional analytic bound;no global supremum certificate is
claimed from selected samples. Direct physical-event comparisons measure
the residual on the actual saved96-mode space instead.

The source correction can have signed contributions. This test does not
silently insert it into the positive inner time-evolution generator.
Deriving its matching inner correction and finite-boundary handling is a
distinct subsequent step. The current target is an explained,measured
source-accuracy improvement at fixed states,not a new closure assumption.
