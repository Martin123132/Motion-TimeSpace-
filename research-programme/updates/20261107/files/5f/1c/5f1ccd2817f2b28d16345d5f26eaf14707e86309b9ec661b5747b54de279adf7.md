## 1. Derive the three-frequency form, not a local sink

Use dimensionless photon frequencies z=s+h in the resting-electron,
leading low-energy Gould limit, with 0<s<=h. Electron recoil energy is
higher order in this limit. Set w=s/z, y=w(1-w), and

    G(w) = [1-3y+(3/2)y^2-y^3]/(1-w).

This is the same Gould factor already present in the imported emission
model. The exact six-diagram amplitude approaches its angular-integrated
cross section as the incoming energy decreases; the independent check is
`results/exact-amplitude-limit/status.json`. This is a leading limit, not
an assertion of exactly zero recoil at finite electron mass.

The forward/inverse occupation bracket is

    n(z)(1+n(h))(1+n(s)) - (1+n(z))n(h)n(s).

At a Planck reference it vanishes because z=h+s. With
delta n=n(1+n)Phi its first variation is

    -n(z)(1+n(h))(1+n(s)) [Phi(s)+Phi(h)-Phi(z)].

Consequently for arbitrary test A the weak collision form, per electron
Thomson time, is

    C_DC[A] = -4 alpha theta^2/(3 pi I3)
       integral ds/s integral_(2s)^infinity dz
       z^4 G(s/z) n(z)(1+n(z-s))(1+n(s))
       [A(s)+A(z-s)-A(z)] [Phi(s)+Phi(z-s)-Phi(z)],

where I3=pi^4/15. The harder/softer ordering removes identical-final-state
double counting. A=1 has reaction jump1; photon number is not conserved.
A=x has reaction jump0; PHOTON energy is conserved by this particular
leading recoil-free DC contribution. Electron energy transfer from exact
Compton, BR and higher-order DC recoil is not declared zero.

The retained normalization DC_CONSTANT=(4 alpha/(3pi)) I4, I4=4pi^4/15,
is factored out of the stored matrix. Discarding the hard/seed test legs
recovers the old integrated local absorption matrix numerically. This

## 3. Derive the infrared source slope from the hard spectrum

For a tracked soft photon, its finite-s target in this leading kernel is

    Phi_target(s) = weighted_average_z [Phi(z)-Phi(z-s)],
    weight = z^4 G(s/z) n(z)(1+n(z-s)),  z>=2s.

For a smooth hard spectrum the limit is

    Phi_target(s) = s b_DC + higher powers,
    b_DC[Phi] = integral_0^infinity dz z^4 n(z)(1+n(z)) Phi'(z) / I4.

The normalization recognizes Phi=b_gamma x exactly: b_DC=b_gamma. It
does not generally equal b_e. This is why freezing all hard photons to the
electron-temperature family was restrictive.

The leading soft coefficient is kappa_DC=DC_CONSTANT theta^2. The
sourced free-free coefficient kappa_BR(s)=s^2 Gamma_BR(s) depends slowly
on frequency through the H/He Gaunt tables. Combining both sources gives

    partial_tau Phi |_source =
      -(kappa_DC+kappa_BR(s))/s^2 *
       [Phi(s) - s b_IR(s)] + subleading terms,
    b_IR(s)=[kappa_DC b_DC+kappa_BR(s)b_e]/
                                       [kappa_DC+kappa_BR(s)].

Thus the leading infrared slope is a nonlocal functional of the photon
spectrum AND the gas, with both coefficients sourced. It is not a new
free boundary constant. A complete time-dependent tail must still retain
transport, finite heat capacity and the subleading terms.

## 4. The soft Compton transport is also available without guessing

In the zero-photon-energy limit at fixed electron velocity, let
r=omega_out/omega_in be the exact Doppler ratio. The leading Thomson
kernel acting on u=Phi-b_e x is

    L_soft u(x) = average_(gas,rest solid angle)
       [(1-v.n_in) (3/(16pi))(1+c_rest^2)/r] [u(r x)-u(x)].

The1/r is the Rayleigh-Jeans Bose enhancement ratio. For u=x^p its Mellin
coefficient is the same positive-weight average of r^p-1. The numerical
velocity/angular integral retains exact boosts and the Maxwell-Juttner
population. It obeys lambda_p=lambda_(-1-p) in the tested quadrature and
approaches theta p(p+1) at small theta; that diffusion limit is CHECKED,
not substituted for the finite-shift kernel in the actual evolutions.

Together the leading tail equation is

    partial_tau Phi = L_soft(Phi-b_e x)
                -kappa_total(x)/x^2 [Phi-x b_IR(x)] + remainders.

In a region with slowly varying b_IR and absorption faster than evolution,
the first forced correction has order x^3/kappa_total, with numerator
L_soft[x(b_IR-b_e)]/x - partial_tau b_IR. Logarithmic Gaunt variation must
be kept when evaluating this expression. This is the derived route to a
tail solve, NOT permission to set that correction or transient to zero.
