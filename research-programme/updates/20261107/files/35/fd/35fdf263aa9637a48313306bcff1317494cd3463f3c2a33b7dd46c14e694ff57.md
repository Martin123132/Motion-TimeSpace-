## 2. Source work follows from the action, with its sign fixed

Use the same real canonical exterior master U as stage42 and add

    S_source = N integral dt J(t) U(t,x_s),

to the bulk action on x >= x_R and the boundary action

    S_boundary = (N/2) integral dt [A U_t(R)^2 - B U(R)^2].
    A=-s2=120.2024346430085, B=s0>0.

The field and boundary equations are

    U_tt - U_xx + V U = J(t) delta(x-x_s),
    U_x(R) = B U(R) + A U_tt(R).

U is continuous at the shell and its derivative jumps by

    U_x(x_s+) - U_x(x_s-) = -J(t).

Consequently the source power is +N J U_t(x_s), not its negative.
The local energy density and outward energy flux are

    e = N/2 [U_t^2 + U_x^2 + V U^2],
    F = -N U_t U_x.

Away from the shell, e_t + F_x = 0. Integrating on the two sides of the
shell gives the source-work term through that derivative jump. The boundary
energy and its exchange with the bulk are

    E_b = N/2 [A U_t(R)^2 + B U(R)^2],
    dE_b/dt = N U_t(R) U_x(R).

For a finite outer detector x_d this yields the sourced balance

    d(E_bulk[x_R,x_d] + E_b)/dt + F(x_d,t) = N J(t) U_t(x_s,t).

The potential is the same inherited Schwarzschild polar l=2 potential used by
the saved response calculation, and is positive in this exterior. Thus the
retained bulk and boundary energies are nonnegative. This assertion concerns
this canonical effective system, not the sign of every constrained interior
gravitational degree of freedom.

    z=(Omega-.018)/.012,

The packet has pre-event tails. This experiment is not a sharp-front causality

## 4. Exterior transfer and independent energy channels

Existing saved orbital transfers use different source radii and nonuniform
frequencies. They are not a complete transfer for this fixed-shell packet.
We therefore solve only the modest exterior equations at the required frequency
nodes; no background or interior field solution is recomputed.

Let h,d be real homogeneous exterior solutions with

    h(0)=1, h_y(0)=alpha; d(0)=0, d_y(0)=1,
    f/f(0)=h+i beta d,
    C=alpha-(b-a Omega^2), u=h-C d.

Here f is the outgoing solution, normalized to unit asymptotic amplitude;
beta=I_out/|f(0)|^2 follows from its conserved Wronskian current. In the exact
outgoing normalization I_out=Omega. The Green function for a positive unit
canonical source is

    G(y,y_s) = -u(y_<) [h(y_>)+i beta d(y_>)] / (C+i beta).
    C_infinity = -u(y_s) / [f(0)(C+i beta)].

This choice gives derivative jump -1. Real and imaginary parts are evaluated
separately to avoid losing the very small radiative part to cancellation.
We check the raw integrated outgoing current and direct complex reconstruction
as independent controls; the algebraically imposed optical identity alone is
not treated as an independent numerical success.

In particular,

    Im G_ss = I_out |C_infinity|^2,
    W_source(all time) = pi integral Omega |A|^2 Im G_ss dOmega,
    E_infinity = pi integral Omega^2 |A|^2 |C_infinity|^2 dOmega.

Both are positive for a nonzero radiating pulse. We separately reconstruct
time-domain source power, finite-detector flux, exterior volume energy and
boundary energy. Their time derivatives are taken spectrally, while the
spatial energy integral uses Gauss quadrature split at the source.

Infinity power is evaluated at retarded time. Its cumulative integral is NOT
subtracted from finite-detector stored energy at equal coordinate time; that
would mix two different energy accounting regions. The finite-volume balance
uses the actual detector flux. That flux may briefly be negative because
reactive near-zone energy can cross a finite sphere inward, whereas the
asymptotic outgoing power is a nonnegative square.
