# Stage101: the exact four-corner frequency/geometry increment

The complete source remains C=Gaccurate+K+D+V+S. Stage100 already computed
its fine frequency rule at the current gas/hard-angle grid. This stage computes

## Four-corner identity

For each actual98x4 component vector j in {D,V,S},write A_j(s,g),where
s=3 or4 denotes the existing seven-panel incoming seed rule. Let g0 be
radial2/polar2/azimuth4/hard3 and g1 be radial3/polar3/azimuth8/hard5.
The physical temperature,finite window [1e-5,24],profiles,spectral basis,
internal rules and coefficient prescription are held fixed.

    delta0_j = A_j(4,g0)-A_j(3,g0)
    delta1_j = A_j(4,g1)-A_j(3,g1)
    M_j = delta1_j-delta0_j
        = [A_j(4,g1)-A_j(4,g0)]-[A_j(3,g1)-A_j(3,g0)].

This is an exact identity of the four finite numerical integrals. It does
not assume separability of frequency and electron/hard-angle dependence.
Only A_j(3,g1) is missing. Its4725 representative evaluations complete the
comparison while reusing all6300 fine-grid coefficients from Stage100 and
the original Stage95 seed3/4 arrays. No interpolation replaces a coefficient.

The change g0->g1 combines hard,azimuth,polar and radial refinement. M is
the mixed increment for this composite change,not a separately identified
frequency-by-radial derivative or a theorem covering every grid combination.

## Budget replacement,not addition

For source norm ||.|| on the full96-mode space,retain all other observed
differences E_k and form

    B_updated = [sum_other ||E_k|| + sum_j ||delta1_j||] / ||R_fine||.
    B_old     = [sum_other ||E_k|| + sum_j ||delta0_j||] / ||R_fine||.

M_j is reported separately. Do NOT add both delta0_j and M_j to delta1_j.
The comparison also obeys the diagnostic inequalities

    ||delta1_j|| <= ||delta0_j||+||M_j||,
    |B_updated-B_old| <= sum_j ||M_j||/||R_fine||.

Neither inequality bounds the omitted continuum error by these finite
differences. The unchanged inherited compact-control and other-grid estimates
retain their original scope. No small leading-source ratio waives a gate.

At fixed saved parent background,the existing coupled photon/gas response
is linear in the independent photon/gas source pair. Apply that SAME response
to every difference,including M,so the four-corner identity and triangle bound
hold in its photon norm and absolute gas norm too. Check the original equations
and energy/work balance in every solve. The gas response is not discarded or
forced into equilibrium to make the budget pass.
