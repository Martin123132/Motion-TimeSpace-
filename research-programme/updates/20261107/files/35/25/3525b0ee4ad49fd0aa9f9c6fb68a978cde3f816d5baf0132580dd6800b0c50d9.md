# Full actual-background derivative of the joint canonical map

This continues, rather than replaces, the retained quadratic scalar–gravity branch in
../mts-joint-gravity-hamiltonian-20261107/DERIVATION.md and CANONICAL-DERIVATION.md.
The scalar potential, fitted masses, normalization, GR action, rest-species interface,
saved physical grid and accepted numerical thresholds are unchanged.

## 1. Time-dependent canonical action

Use N=ln(a), conformal H, and the preceding canonical coordinates

    Z=(d_s, pi_s, Re B, Im B, h, P_h, eta, P_eta)
    J=diag(J2,J2,J2,J2), J2=[[0,1],[-1,0]].

The quadratic N-time Hamiltonian along the retained background is

    H_N(Z,N) = Z^T K(N) Z/2 + l(N)^T Z.

The rest-species pressure and anisotropic stress are the saved, current source
interface in l. This does not replace their collision hierarchy with a closed
Hamiltonian. Background discretization and represented candidate defects remain
separately recorded residuals, not changes to this action.

Let G=G^T and S=exp(JG). Substituting Z=S(N)W into the symmetrized first-order action

    I = integral[-Z^T J Z_N/2 - H_N] dN

gives

    K_new = S^T K S + S^T J S_N
    l_new = S^T l
    W_N = J(K_new W + l_new) + S^-1 r,

where r=Z_N-J(KZ+l) is the separately measured/derived saved residual.

The sign of the connection term follows from the action, not a fitted cancellation.
Differentiating S^T J S=J proves that S^T J S_N is symmetric. The construction
transforms the metric canonical pairs as well as the scalar pairs. No new free
coupling is introduced.

## 2. Analytic harmonics, including the coupling square

Retain the parent's exact rotating heavy mode and phase. Define

    A=a(sqrt(omega_h) phi_h + i v_h/sqrt(omega_h)) exp(i theta)
    omega_I=a m_I, mu=theta_N, gamma=k^2/(2 omega_h H)
    g=(0,0,0,0,0,-6,0,1)
    F=f(theta)^T Z
    f=f0+f2c cos(2theta)+f2s sin(2theta)
    f0=(v_s,0,Im A/(2a^2),-Re A/(2a^2),0,0,0,0)
    f2c=(0,0,Im A/(2a^2),Re A/(2a^2),0,0,0,0)
    f2s=(0,0,-Re A/(2a^2),Im A/(2a^2),0,0,0,0).

The joint parent contains G_metric F/(2H)-3a^2 F^2/(4H), with
G_metric=P_eta-6P_h. Thus its symmetric Hessian is

    K=K_base + (g f^T+f g^T)/(2H) - 3a^2 f f^T/(2H)
     =K0+sum_{n=2,4}(Knc cos(n theta)+Kns sin(n theta)).

The explicit coefficients and their product-rule derivatives are in canonical_math.py.
They include the fourth harmonic from the gravity-induced F^2 term. The direct
preceding Hamiltonian is retained as an independent algebraic comparison.

## 3. Replace the frozen derivative, not the background

Take the same zero-mean generator as the preceding stage,

    G=sum_{n=2,4}[Knc sin(n theta)-Kns cos(n theta)]/(n mu).

Its FULL derivative is

    G_N = K-K0
        + sum_{n=2,4} {
            sin(n theta)[Knc,N - (mu_N/mu) Knc]
          - cos(n theta)[Kns,N - (mu_N/mu) Kns]
          }/(n mu).

The first term alone was the frozen-coefficient derivative. The second term is
required by the actual retained background, including its micromotion. Calling
these coefficients slow is not a reason to set their derivatives to zero.

The derivative of the exponential is the Frechet derivative

    S_N = L_exp(JG, JG_N)
        = integral_0^1 exp[(1-t)JG] JG_N exp[t JG] dt.

This is not replaced by exp(JG)JG_N: the matrices need not commute.

Actual A_N is evaluated without fitting:

    A_N = exp(i theta) {
            a sqrt(omega_h)(3 phi_h/2 + phi_h,N)
          + i a/sqrt(omega_h)(v_h/2 + v_h,N)
          } + i mu A.

The field and velocity values AND derivatives come from the saved DOP853 dense
polynomials. We do not silently substitute the ideal background ODE for the
retained numerical derivatives. The carrier is the existing cubic and its
antiderivative, including any small omega_h/H-mu mismatch.

For the background density representation used off the original nodes,

    R_I=(v_I^2+omega_I^2 phi_I^2)/2
    R_I,N=v_I v_I,N+omega_I^2(phi_I^2+phi_I phi_I,N)
    H^2=(R_rest+sum R_I)/3
    H_N/H=(R_rest,N+sum R_I,N)/(6H^2).

Only the already saved R_rest needs a declared interpolation derivative. A cubic
through all its saved nodes is compared with every-second-node interpolation.
This is a numerical representation, not a newly derived rest-fluid law. Exact
node agreement, independent derivative checks, and refinement bounds are recorded.

## 4. Conditioning is not a new time transformation

For numerical conditioning use a symplectic diagonal D at each evaluation:

    D=diag(1/s_s,s_s,1,1,1/s_g,s_g,1/s_g,s_g)
    s_s=a(k^2+omega_s^2)^(1/4), s_g=a sqrt(k).

The code evaluates exp(D^-1 JG D) and the Frechet direction D^-1 JG_N D.
It returns D^-1 S_N D, NOT the derivative of D^-1 S D. No D_N term belongs
in this representation of the physical derivative. Independent finite differences
first reconstruct physical S at each shifted time, then compare in the central D.
The integrated replay evolves the physical W, not W/D.

## 5. The constraints travel with the coordinates

Write D_scalar=density_row^T Z and F=source_row^T Z. Then

    C_H=k^2 eta-H(P_eta-6P_h)/(2a^2)+3HF/2
        +(D_scalar+D_rest)/2
    C_M=P_h/a^2-Q_rest/(2k).

The latter uses Q_scalar=kF and eta'=P_h/a^2+F/2.
For any linear constraint C=r^T Z+c, the transformed constraint is
C=(r^T S)W+c. Dropping this row transformation would compare different
observables. Density/current reconstruction, both pullbacks and their retained
residuals are tested.

The original metric momenta were reconstructed from the saved constrained
history. Therefore their constraint agreement is an algebraic and implementation
test, not an independent proof of constraint propagation by a newly evolved model.

## 6. What the tests establish

- Independently finite-difference coefficients, G, and physical S at 97 interior
  probes, with steps 1e-6 and 5e-7 in N, using the retained background.
- Reuse all 42 saved histories, including heavy/full, heavy/reduced, cold and zero
  controls, for half, balanced and double wavelength settings.
- Test exact coordinate/inverse/action/source identities at 1e-10.
- Test raw rates, constraints and integral reconstruction at the unchanged 2e-5
  numerical gate; also record the residual-accounted reconstruction separately.
- Compare integral replays with strides 1,2,4, without altering physical grids.
- Preserve the omitted-coefficient-derivative result as an ablation.

A saved-history replay checks the transformation and implementation. It is not
an independent prediction from a reduced, closed mean model. The next calculation

and constraint pullbacks, then freeze and forward-test that model against the
existing full controls. The microscopic/scattering bank remains untouched.
