# 29. Causal exterior coupling and finite-history energy

5 October2026. Private daughter of the condensate branch. Stage28's
interior response is now joined variationally to exterior waves and driven
by the same finite forcing for the condensate and the same-mass BH.
The retained Einstein-Hilbert/G_R background and body are unchanged.

## 1. What this calculation adds

The previously clamped surface is released: its displacement is now solved
together with the interior modes and the exterior field. No fitted surface
force or delay is added. We derive and test

    source work = stored interior energy + stored exterior energy
                  + escaped outer energy + absorbed horizon energy.

The body has no horizon term. The same-source difference is

    Delta W = Delta E_stored + Delta E_escaped - E_H(BH).

Unlike a steady harmonic calculation, the stored term is time-dependent
and cannot be dropped. In this experiment, omitting it produces a maximum
error about1.42 times the peak body-minus-BH work difference.

This is a linear wave experiment with an externally powered master source.
It supplies the interior-to-radiation link, not yet a derivation of an
evolving companion's stress tensor. That distinction determines the next step.

## 2. Coordinates, normalization and source

Use tau=t/R and zeta=(rstar-rstar(R))/R, with the surface at zeta=0.
Let x=r/R and c=2M/R=0.03005796484196544. Then

    zeta=x-1+c*ln[(x-c)/(1-c)],
    dx/dzeta=1-c/x,
    Vhat=(1-c/x)*(6/x^2-3c/x^3).

The stable inverse uses the real Wright omega function:

    w=omega_W[ln((1-c)/c)+(zeta+1-c)/c],
    x=c*(1+w), f=w/(1+w).

This evaluates f near the horizon without subtracting nearly equal numbers.
It is a coordinate inversion, not a new physical field or coupling.

For one real quadrature, the canonical master equation and energy are

    psi_tt-psi_zz+Vhat*psi=g(tau)*delta(zeta-zeta_s),
    Ehat=1/2 int[psi_t^2+psi_z^2+Vhat*psi^2] d zeta.

Dots/subscripts here refer to dimensionless time/coordinate. For the
m=+/-1 pair sum both quadratures; physical energy is kappa/R times Ehat,
with kappa=3/(4pi), preserving stages25-28's power normalization.
Our finite pulse occupies one real quadrature. All numerical energies
reported here are Ehat, for unit test-source amplitude.

The source is at x_s=2.5, zeta_s=1.5280955900186792. Its prescribed drive is

    g(tau)=sin[pi*(tau-2)/12]^6*cos[0.5*(tau-2)], 2<tau<14,
    g=0 otherwise.

Its amplitude, duration and carrier are chosen benchmark settings, not
fitted physical response coefficients. The delta source gives continuous
psi and the derivative jump [psi_z]=-g. Its energy is finite, unlike a
naively squared discontinuous delta-prime field. This test does NOT
silently replace the full orbital delta-plus-delta-prime source by a
physical point-particle model; it is a clean diagnostic source.

## 3. Derive the coupled interface

The body-side variables are the actual stage28 Dirichlet modes eta_n and
the shared surface value q. Their quadratic forms are

    M_in = [[identity, a],[a^T,I]],
    K_in = diag(lambda_n,Y0).

They implement
eta_n''+lambda_n eta_n=-a_n q'' and
p_in=Y0 q+I q''+sum a_n eta_n'', with no new parameter.
The inherited spectral coefficients are read from
`../causal-axial-20261005/results/spectrum/body-256.npz`.
Keeping I when a finite set of modes is retained preserves the positive
kinetic remainder I-sum_retained a_n^2.

The exterior action is the same canonical wave action on zeta>=0.
Its lower-surface variation is +kappa*p_out*delta q, while the interior
contributes -kappa*p_in*delta q. Consequently stationarity gives

    psi_in=psi_out=q, p_in=p_out.

The surface value is one shared degree of freedom; the traction condition
follows from its equation of motion. It is not an extra response axiom.

For the BH comparison replace the body interior by the Schwarzschild
region from the inner extraction surface to R. Keep the same mass,
exterior mesh, source location, drive, timestep and outer condition.
There is no selective tolerance or forcing advantage for either object.

## 4. Local, regional and total work identities

For e=(psi_t^2+psi_z^2+Vhat psi^2)/2 and flux=-psi_t psi_z,

    e_t+flux_z=S*psi_t.

At the source, Wdot=g*psi_t(zeta_s). Interface work has opposite signs
in the two regions:

    E_in'  = q'*p - P_H,
    E_out' = Wdot - q'*p - P_outer.

Thus the interface cancels from total work, and it also cancels in the
body-minus-BH comparison. P_H is zero for the body, not for the reference.
Initially the field and its energy vanish.

The driver is prescribed externally: W is the work supplied to the wave
field, not an already-derived loss of orbital energy. No point-particle
energy is smuggled into this budget.

The time runs use a finite outer surface zeta=40 or60 with
psi_z=-psi_t there. The BH has psi_z=+psi_t at its inner surface,
zeta=-2 or-2.1. Each boundary is passive: its outward/absorbed power is
the square of the corresponding velocity.

The outer Sommerfeld condition is NOT the exact nonlocal transparent
condition for the Schwarzschild ell2 potential. Therefore the reported
time-domain outer flux is flux through a finite extraction surface, not
automatically an exact flux at infinity.

Independent monochromatic ODE calculations compare:

## 6. Numerical method and a non-circular check on bookkeeping

Quadratic finite elements discretize the exterior and the BH inner region.
The condensed body matrices are coupled directly at the surface. The
semidiscrete equation is

    M u''+C u'+K u=b*g,

with positive energy and nonnegative boundary damping C. Implicit midpoint
gives the exact discrete relation, up to linear-solve/rounding error,

    E[n+1]-E[n]=dt*(v_mid^T b*g_mid-v_mid^T C v_mid).

This structural property is useful but is NOT independent proof that the
physical coefficients are correct. We therefore also test the coupled
Green function against separately integrated continuum radial equations.
Removing the required body surface contact3f(R) still conserves the
discrete energy, but changes the response and fails that independent
comparison. Conservation alone does not rescue the wrong model.

## 7. Independent true-infinity work calculation

For the Fourier convention g_tilde(k)=int g(tau)exp(ik*tau)d tau and
retarded diagonal Green function G(k), Parseval gives

    W=(1/pi)*int_0^infinity k*ImG(k)*|g_tilde(k)|^2 dk.

For each frequency, the body response comes from the original radial ODE,
not the finite-element/midpoint evolution. The exterior is truly outgoing,
and the BH is truly ingoing. Surface imaginary slopes are reconstructed
from conserved currents, preserving stage27's normalization repair.
Subtracting logarithmic derivatives before forming the Green difference
avoids unnecessarily subtracting two nearly equal source responses.

Gauss quadratures24 and36 per band agree; extending the frequency cutoff
from9 to12 adds9.30e-16 to body work and5.55e-17 to the work difference.
These are numerical cutoff controls, not a rigorous bound on the remaining
infinite-frequency tail.

The continuum spectral calculation gives

    W_body = 0.265982103032252,
    W_BH   = 0.26601558226022815,
    DeltaW = -3.347922797601462e-5.

The finest time run gives

    W_body = 0.26598247303871386,
    W_BH   = 0.2660159521978862,
    DeltaW = -3.34791591723449e-5.

Relative errors are1.40e-6 or less for each total and2.06e-6 for the
smaller difference. They decrease with timestep refinement; no response
coefficient is adjusted to enforce agreement.

For a harmonic real source the identity is

    <Wdot> = (k/2)*ImG*|g0|^2
           = <P_outer>+<P_H>.

For the full quadrature pair the factor1/2 disappears. With
F_ret,pair=kappa*g0^2*DeltaG/(2R), this recovers
2Omega ImF_ret,pair=Delta P_out-P_H, the stage27 optical normalization.
The finite-history stored-energy term is what the period average removes.

## 8. What the pulse comparison says

The finest time run's same-source budget at tau90 is

    DeltaW       = -3.34791591723449e-5,
    DeltaE_store = -3.629506094470797e-11,
    DeltaE_outer = -3.347911581974561e-5,
    E_H(BH)      =  7.0586416136113e-12.

Their signed combination agrees to about1.1e-15 absolute at that endpoint.
The maximum per-object budget error over the evolution is below1.2e-13
of peak work, which reflects the method's structural energy property.
The independent spectral agreement, not that tiny bookkeeping residual,
sets the stronger practical accuracy check on the physical work result.

Dropping the horizon term is detectable even though it is small.
Dropping storage during the pulse is much worse: its difference-budget
error exceeds the entire peak work difference. Thus a steady optical
identity cannot simply be applied instantaneously to a changing source.

The sign of DeltaW means this body takes less work from this particular
imposed pulse than the same-mass BH. It is not a universal sign theorem,
a binary phase prediction, or a preference established by observations.
