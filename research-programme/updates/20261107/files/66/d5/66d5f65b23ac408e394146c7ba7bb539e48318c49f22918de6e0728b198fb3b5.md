# Which temperature-dependent quantity is predicted

The event calculation uses dimensionless theta=k_B T/(m_e c^2), photon
energies in m_e c^2 units, and fixed x=h nu/(k_B T). The electron measure is
normalized; the Born differential factor is normalized to the Thomson
cross section. S here is the same weak source used in the saved source bank.
It is not by itself a proper-time energy density injection rate.

In the inherited transport equation the source enters as r(theta)*S(theta),
where r is the separate, unchanged expansion/scattering prefactor. Therefore

    D[r S] = r D S + (D r) S,
    D^2[r S] = r D^2 S + 2 (D r)(D S) + (D^2 r) S.

The source-only prediction test must not be relabelled a prediction of the
whole forcing or final spectrum without carrying this prefactor, the photon
and gas response, and all other saved source windows through the transport.
No density, Hubble history, normalization constant or fitted parameter is
changed to improve the present test. Both branches use the same normalization.
