# Sharpen the gravitational source bound using conserved exchange

The source-free tail in DERIVATION.md has

    q=chi(E+chi g), q_s=-chi^2 g,
    V^2=p^T M p+chi g^2,
    (V^2/2)_s <= -chi g^2.

Starting at the saved physical restart s0, let V0=V(s0), q0=q(s0) and
L=S-s0. Pointwise contraction gives |g|<=V0/sqrt(chi), while its integrated
dissipation gives integral_s0^S chi g^2 ds <= V0^2/2. Hence

    sup_[s0,S]|q(s)|
      <= |q0|+chi^(3/2) V0 min(L,sqrt(L/2)) = Q_work.

The earlier dual-norm inequality also holds. Use Q_sharp=min(Q_work,Q_dual).
This is a derived intersection of two upper bounds, not selection between fits
or a relaxed error gate. It uses the opposite gas/radiation exchange and gas
expansion work, which explain why gravitational density can be much smaller
than the spectral-shape norm. It does not infer one from the other.

Insert Q_sharp into the already derived coupled-field bound:

    |delta H/H| <= G_source Q_sharp + G_incoming ||z(s0)||.

The incoming-field term remains explicit. The zero-incoming number is the
attributed metric response generated AFTER this restart, not the complete
earlier source-window response. Unknown external injection would add its
integral to q and an input term to the kinetic energy inequality; it cannot
be included while retaining the source-free bound unchanged.

Evaluate q0 from the actual physical photon moment Erow p and gas state,
not by discarding a tiny discrepancy with a separately carried energy row.
M's symmetric part is its exact quadratic form for this calculation; the
scattering/emission bank and evolution are not modified. Numeric coefficients
are conditional on the retained dissipative model and saved initial packet,
with their inherited approximation uncertainties. This is not an interval
certificate of continuum source assembly or a bound on the uncomputed history.
