# Stage119 derivation: one numerical change only

Inputs are the sealed Stage118 predictions and responses in
`../next-grid-and-precision-evolution-20261013/results/stage-integrity.json`.
The physical inner equation and lower/upper boundary laws are unchanged.
The saved positive two-point operator acts on the defect e; the identical
four-point transport acts on the prescribed outer field, with the same
lower-boundary order reduction. No new scattering amplitudes are generated.

For outer field u, defect e, generator L and physical forcing f:
a e_n - h L_n e_n = -b e_(n-1) - c e_(n-2)
                      + h f_n - (a u_n+b u_(n-1)+c u_(n-2)).
Startup uses (a,b,c)=(1,-1,0); subsequent steps use (3/2,-2,1/2).
Only h is halved. The coefficient/BR bank and all frequency nodes are copied
exactly; high-order outer transport uses the same saved events and spacing.
The two histories retain the same 65 saved epochs.

The matched correction remains q=x W e, m=V^T measure q, p=M^-1 m.
The driving residual is the physical collision source on q minus K p.
The same 2048-step coupled response is recomputed, with physical readout
V z + q - V p and gas component b. No joining term is dropped.
The energy ledger remains radiation energy + chi*b + gas work = 0.

Time differences compare paired Stage118 and Stage119 arrays. Peak-normalized
gas differences and endpoint-normalized differences are distinct outputs.
The last-quarter interval is fixed in advance. A late zero crossing can
make endpoint-relative changes large without a large history-norm change;
both signed endpoints and the actual denominators must therefore be saved.
Two time levels estimate sensitivity only. Response time step, 65-knot
forcing interpolation, window choice, physical approximations and earlier
frequency-grid errors are not removed by this test.
