## 2. Coupled equations and units

Let N=ln a, nu=M/H0, k denote a **comoving wavenumber in Mpc^-1**, h_E=dlnE/dN and F the fraction of the existing pressureless density assigned to the heavy wave. F is not Stage49's motion-field overlap r and not the cosmological slow-scalar fraction f_scalar.

The remaining pressureless component is denoted c. In this calculation it stands for all other pressureless matter, not a separate baryon transfer calculation. Both components source the same inherited Newtonian potential. Write

\[
 \delta_m=(1-F)\delta_c+F\delta_h,\quad
 S(N)=\tfrac32\Omega_m(N),\quad
 Q(N,k)={(ck/H_0)^4\over4\nu^2a^4E^2}.
\]

The Stage49 equations become

\[
 \delta_{c,NN}+(2+h_E)\delta_{c,N}=S\delta_m,
\]
\[
 \boxed{\delta_{h,NN}+(2+h_E)\delta_{h,N}+Q\delta_h=S\delta_m.}
\]

Consequently

\[
 \delta_{m,NN}+(2+h_E)\delta_{m,N}-S\delta_m=-FQ\delta_h,
\]
\[
 (\delta_h-\delta_c)_{,NN}+(2+h_E)(\delta_h-\delta_c)_{,N}=-Q\delta_h.
\]

This matters observationally: the pressure acts on the heavy component, not directly on the total matter or ordinary tracers. Their density and velocity responses are saved separately. Replacing the right-hand side by a freely chosen suppression function would discard the action-derived prediction.

The dimensionless conversion uses c=299792.458km/s and inherited fiducial H0=70km/s/Mpc. Quoted mass energies use hbar=6.582119569e-16eV s. H0 is a coordinate calibration for this screen, not a new measurement. Numerical k is not k in h/Mpc; a conversion requires the specified little-h. Physical wavelength is2pi a/k, distinct from the gradient scale a/k.

\[

\[

There is no single hard cutoff of all matter when F<1. At frozen coefficients the gravitational/pressure matrix has characteristic polynomial

\[

For S,Q>0 and F<1 its determinant is negative, retaining a positive instantaneous gravitational eigenvalue. In the high-pressure regime the clustering part continues growing more slowly rather than all perturbations disappearing.
