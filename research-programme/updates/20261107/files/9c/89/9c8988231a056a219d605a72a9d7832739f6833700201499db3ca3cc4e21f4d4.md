    e' = A_low e + S_high[g,b,beta_H] - g'.
    S_high = R [M_high g+lambda1(g-b)
                +Gamma_DC(beta_H-g)+Gamma_BR(b-g)].

The two exterior collars for e are zero. Evaluate the known background
at saved physical shifts with four-point interpolation in y=1/x,
then repeat with six points. Both exactly reproduce constant slope
and constant chemical potential (f=constant or f proportional to y).
The grid changes only the accuracy of this representation; no amplitude
is fitted. M_low remains the positive inherited operator on the small e.
Negative high-order weights act only on the known forcing, not the
homogeneous error generator. This separates interpolation of a large
known field from interpolation of its much smaller unknown correction.
