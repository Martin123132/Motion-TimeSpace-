# 39. Matched finite-mass near-circular tidal balance

Private daughter, 6 October 2026. Immediate parent:
`../conservative-eccentric-action-20261005/results/stage-integrity.json`.

This step derives the matching radiation from BOTH the induced quadrupole and
the tidally changed orbital motion. Combined with the same conservative action,
it supplies consistent near-circular shrinkage, circularization and azimuthal
chirp laws at leading weak-field electric-tidal order. No new coupling is fitted.

## 1. Choose one approximation and keep its orders matched

Retain the original body and its static lambda, global charge, Einstein action
and G_R. The calculation here is a controlled weak-field daughter of the
finite-frequency response, not a replacement for stage38's full ell=2 result.

Let M be the deformable body's mass, mu the point companion's mass, MT=M+mu,
mu_red=M mu/MT and q=mu/M. Geometric masses and time have length units.
Only the body M is deformable. Work to linear order in lambda and first order
in the near-circular radial action, KEEPING the mixed lambda*eccentricity term.
Keep the exact Newtonian mass factors rather than replacing mu_red by mu early.

The static adiabatic electric tide has already been matched to the action:

    L = mu_red (r_dot²+r² phi_dot²)/2 + M mu/r + 3lambda mu²/(2r⁶),
    I_ij = [mu_red r² + 3lambda mu/r³] n_<ij>.

The first quadrupole term follows the perturbed orbit, not a frozen Kepler orbit.
The second is the induced body quadrupole. Its coefficient is the same lambda
as in the conservative interaction. Define

    alpha = 3lambda mu²/(2mu_red),
    h=J_phi/mu_red, j=J_r/mu_red,
    d=h²/MT, z=2j/h,
    ell=lambda/d⁵, k=alpha/(MT d⁵)=(3/2)q ell,
    b=3lambda mu/(mu_red d⁵)=3(1+q)ell.

d is an ACTION-defined radius/semilatus label. It is not the actual perturbed
circular separation or the frequency radius. z agrees with Kepler e² only
through first order in eccentricity at zero tide. Defining it through actions
avoids an arbitrary eccentricity convention for the non-Keplerian orbit.

The unperturbed frequency is Omega_N=sqrt(MT/d³). These laws assume a stable
near-circular orbit, weak fields, a small linear tide, and sufficiently slow
tidal forcing for the static response approximation. They omit absorption,
dynamic response corrections, magnetic tides and higher PN orders. They do not
combine those omitted terms selectively with a higher-accuracy phase claim.

## 2. Derive the perturbed orbit moments

At fixed h, radial motion obeys

    r_ddot=h²/r³-MT/r²-6alpha/r⁷,
    U_eff=h²/(2r²)-MT/r-alpha/r⁶.

For the local derivation scale d=MT=mu_red=1, so h=Omega_N=1. To first order in k,

    r_c=1-6k,    Omega_phi,c=1+12k,
    omega_r=sqrt[U_eff''(r_c)]=1-3k,
    U_eff'''(r_c)=-6+120k.

Put x=r-r_c. Harmonic action and the averaged radial equation give

    <x²>=j/omega_r+O(j²),     <v²>=j omega_r+O(j²),
    <x>=-U_eff'''(r_c) j/(2 omega_r³)+O(j²).

The last identity follows by averaging r_ddot=-omega_r² x-U_eff''' x²/2+...
over a radial cycle; the averaged acceleration is zero. Thus a time-reversal
even local observable G(r,v) has the expansion

    <G>=G_c+z[-U_eff''' G_r/(4omega_r³)
                         +G_rr/(4omega_r)+omega_r G_vv/4]+O(z²).

At this order the three weights are 3/2-33k/2, (1+3k)/4, (1-3k)/4.
They account for the mean radial displacement as well as the oscillation.
Ignoring that displacement would give the wrong eccentric radiation coefficient.

Applying the same averaging to h/r² gives

    Omega_phi/Omega_N = 1+12k+z(-3/2+39k/2).

This is independently recovered from the action Hamiltonian

    E/mu_red = -MT²/[2(h+j)²] - alpha/d⁶ [1+3j/h+O(j²/h²)] + O(lambda²).

Its derivatives give Omega_phi and the circular-limit Omega_r/Omega_N=1-3k.
The circular-limit radial frequency is sufficient for the O(z) radial-action
loss because that loss itself vanishes at z=0.

## 3. Compute the radiation instead of assigning a correction factor

Use the leading mass-quadrupole formulas, with a positive outgoing flux:

    F_E = <I_ij''' I_ij'''>/5,
    F_L = (2/5) epsilon_zab <I_ac'' I_bc'''>.

`derive_balance.py` differentiates the full STF tensor in a rotating basis.
If R is the planar antisymmetric rotation matrix, its derivative operator is

    D T = v partial_r T + r_ddot partial_v T + (h/r²)(R T-T R).

Apply it three times to the orbital-plus-induced quadrupole, retain terms linear
in the tide, then use the derived orbit moments. There is no fit to eccentric
orbit samples and no imported eccentric tidal flux coefficient.

Normalize by

    P_N=(32/5)mu_red² MT³/d⁵,       L_N=P_N/Omega_N.

The result in separate orbital-feedback k and induced-response b contributions is

    F_E/P_N = 1+2b+48k + z[37/24+199b/4+3627k/8],
    F_L/L_N = 1+2b+36k + z[-5/8+105b/4+1605k/8].

At z=0, F_E=Omega_phi,c F_L to retained order. The distinct k coefficients
show why evaluating only the induced quadrupole on an unchanged orbit was not
enough to match the previous conservative calculation.

## 4. Action balance closes shrinkage and circularization

Use the same Hamiltonian/action frequencies in

    h_dot=-F_L/mu_red,
    j_dot=-(F_E-Omega_phi F_L)/(mu_red Omega_r),
    d_dot=2d h_dot/h,         z_dot=2j_dot/h-z h_dot/h.

The radial flux coefficient is

    (F_E-Omega_phi F_L)/(P_N z Omega_r/Omega_N)
        = 11/3+53b/2+1223k/4 + O(z,lambda²).

Its nonzero circular pieces cancel before dividing by z. Energy and angular
momentum balance therefore keep exactly circular orbits circular at this order.

Substitute b=3(1+q)ell and k=(3/2)q ell. Define

    d_dot_N=-(64/5)mu_red MT²/d³,
    Gamma_N=-(608/15)mu_red MT²/d⁴.

The matched evolution laws are

    d_dot/d_dot_N = 1+(6+60q)ell
                  +z[-5/8+(315/4+6075q/16)ell],

    z_dot/(Gamma_N z) = 1+(459/19+12195q/76)ell,

    Omega_phi/Omega_N = 1+18q ell+z[-3/2+117q ell/4].

The dimensions are consistent: d_dot is dimensionless, Gamma_N and Omega_N
have inverse-length units. The formula for z_dot is understood continuously at
z=0, where z_dot=0, rather than by numerically dividing zero by zero.

The same expansion gives

    F_E/P_N = 1+(6+78q)ell
                 +z[37/24+(597/4+13269q/16)ell],
    F_L/L_N = 1+(6+60q)ell
                 +z[-5/8+(315/4+6075q/16)ell].

Both fluxes use the same masses, lambda, orbit and averaging convention.
These are the leading static electric contributions, not a complete PN flux.

## 5. Derive the observable-frequency chirp consistently

Differentiate Omega_phi(d,z) using BOTH d_dot and z_dot. At fixed action labels,
with Omega_dot_N=(96/5)mu_red MT² Omega_N/d⁴,

    Omega_dot_phi/Omega_dot_N =
      1+2b+88k+z[25/24+195b/4+10685k/24].

For a common observable azimuthal frequency define D=(MT/Omega_phi²)^(1/3),
ell_D=lambda/D⁵ and k_D=(3/2)q ell_D. Solving for the action radius gives

    d/D = 1-z+8k_D+57k_D z + O(z²,lambda²).

The mixed 57k_D z term matters. Change the baseline d^-11/2 factor AND the
tidal d^-5 factors when converting the chirp, not just the leading frequency.
The final expression is

    Omega_dot_phi / [(96/5)mu_red MT^(2/3) Omega_phi^(11/3)]
      = 1+(157/24)z + 6(1+12q)ell_D
                       +[837/4+20721q/16]ell_D z.

Here z is still the ACTION eccentricity 2J_r/J_phi, not a newly defined orbital
turning-point eccentricity. This expression is accurate only at the retained
linear orders and should not be promoted into a resummed high-eccentricity law.

## 6. Required limits and independent tests

All three planned limits close:

1. Zero tide: point quadrupole P_z/P_N=37/24, L_z/L_N=-5/8 and the familiar
   fixed-frequency chirp coefficient157/24 are recovered.
2. q -> 0: the outgoing electric-tidal shrinkage coefficient315/4 fromstage37
   and circularization coefficient459/19 fromstage35 are recovered exactly.
   Their separate BH-absorption terms are deliberately not imported here.
3. z -> 0, fixed frequency: stage20's flux increment6(1+3q)ell_D and chirp
   increment6(1+12q)ell_D are recovered exactly. The different fixed-action
   circular coefficient6+60q is not mistaken for a conflicting fixed-frequency
   result.

The source tensor and evolution laws also satisfy the retained-order action
first law F_E=Omega_phi F_L+Omega_r F_Jr exactly.

Numerical validation is independent of the orbit-moment averaging method.
It integrates the radial central-force equation from periapsis to apocenter,
measures J_r from the integral of v² dt, and averages local radiation over time.
The local radiation implementation uses a scalar complex quadrupole instead
of the symbolic rotating matrix:

    A=r²+b/r³, Z=A exp(2i phi),
    F_E=A'''²/30+|Z'''|²/10,
    F_L=Im(conjugate(Z'') Z''')/5.

54 small-amplitude control orbits are saved: two integration settings, three
radial amplitudes, and nine auxiliary tidal-parameter settings. Signed auxiliary
values and the higher powers generated by the exact truncated equations are
ALGEBRA CONTROLS, not alternative physical bodies or higher-order predictions.
Finite-difference/extrapolation results validate the already derived coefficients;
they never define or fit them.
