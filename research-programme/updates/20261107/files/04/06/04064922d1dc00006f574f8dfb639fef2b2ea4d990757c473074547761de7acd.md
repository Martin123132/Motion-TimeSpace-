# Stage86: moving-electron, polarization-resolved thermal source

## Continuity and conventions

This extends the retained MTS radiation/gas source, not the parent action.
The input is the Stage85 finite conditional thermal source and the Stage84
dressed amplitude. Use metric (+---), bare electron mass one, c=hbar=kB=1.
E and T therefore mean physical energy and temperature in electron-rest-energy
units; velocities are in units of c. Mueller matrices use Stokes order I,Q,U,V
with Pauli basis (identity,sigma3,sigma1,sigma2). Unpolarized electron spins
are averaged. A conditional event is tagged by incoming photon energy and
outgoing harder-photon LAB direction. Exactly collinear rays are explicitly
excluded from this scattering-plane coordinate chart.

The inherited dynamical history is
`../emitting-thermal-background-20261008/backgrounds/heavy-ir96-integrated-6400/history.npz`.
Its initial E=T=0.00045962015785263325 sets the numerical benchmark. The
selected electron velocity is a quadrature probe, not a fitted bulk velocity
or a replacement for the saved electron distribution.

## 1. Exact real-amplitude reduction, not a soft approximation

For any of the six orderings let signed photon transfers be t0=k0,
t1=-k1, t2=-k2 and transverse polarization vectors eps_j. Using both external
Dirac equations and eps slash anticommutation gives

    S(p+t_first) slash(eps_first) u(p)
      = [2 p.eps_first + slash(t_first)slash(eps_first)] u(p)/(2 p.t_first),

    ubar(p') slash(eps_last) S(p'-t_last)
      = ubar(p') [2 p'.eps_last - slash(eps_last)slash(t_last)]/(-2 p'.t_last).

Insert the middle vertex between these two reduced factors and sum all six
orderings. The result is exact on shell, for emission and crossed absorption;
there is no truncation in photon energy. It replaces cancellation-prone
propagator mass-shell subtractions with linear scalar products. The full
incoming/hard/second-photon polarization and electron-spin tensor survives.
`verify_reduced_amplitude.py` compares it with the earlier high-precision
six-diagram implementation, including three Ward substitutions and a missing
diagram control. This is the basis of the faster single-worker calculation.

## 2. Polarization is a matrix channel

For each unobserved spin and second-photon polarization, define the 2x2
Kraus matrix A with hard polarization as row and incoming polarization as
column. The tree/real Mueller response is

    M_ab = (1/4) sum Tr[sigma_a A sigma_b A_dagger].

The factor is one half from the electron spin average and one half from
rho=(I sigma0+Q sigma3+U sigma1+V sigma2)/2. For the one-loop correction,

    delta M_ab = (1/2) Re sum Tr[sigma_a delta A sigma_b A_dagger].

This retains interference phases. It is not an intensity multiplier for all
Stokes components. For the tree intensity, M_II=2U with

    U=1+c^2+E^2(1-c)^2/[1+E(1-c)].

Keeping both emitted photons instead gives a 4x2 Kraus matrix. Its two
partial traces give the hard and second-photon readouts. The adjoint channel
uses the same tensor, with Tr[O E(rho)]=Tr[E_dagger(O) rho].
`verify_joint_readout.py` checks these identities with complex polarized
input states. No independent inverse-reaction amplitude is invented.

## 3. Moving bath and observed angular measure

Let L map a scattering-aligned incoming-electron rest frame to the lab bath
frame. Transform both observed rays to construct this frame. The bath
four-velocity there is u=L_inverse (1,0,0,0). Occupation is the scalar

    N(q)=1/{exp[(u.q)/T]-1},
    T_direction=T/[u.(1,n)].

The radial thermal functions from Stage84 can thus be used at T_direction
inside their angular integral. This is not a fitted effective temperature.
The angular identity integral dOmega/[u.(1,n)]^2=4pi gives the same scalar
leading mass-squared shift d=2 pi alpha T^2/3. It does NOT make the other
angular thermal terms isotropic.

Boost each polarization vector, remove its time component by adding a
multiple of the null photon momentum, and project on the chosen lab
transverse basis. The resulting real orthogonal Jones map J induces
R_ab=Tr[sigma_a J sigma_b J_dagger]/2. A source matrix transforms as
R_out M R_in_transpose. Its four-vector moment indices transform separately
with L. This separates polarization basis transport from physical four-force.

For an electron with lab velocity v the rate per lab photon flux per electron
contains relative flux 1-v.n_in and outgoing solid-angle Jacobian

    dOmega_rest/dOmega_lab=1/[gamma^2(1-v.n_out)^2].

The Born conditional reference, in units of the bare Thomson cross section,
is 3/(16pi) (h/E)^2 U (1+N(h_lab)) times these two factors. This reference
must multiply the reported normalized correction before electron averaging.
`verify_moving_geometry.py` checks the Jacobian by differentiating the
aberration map independently.

## 4. The ordering boundary must stay in the bath frame

Simply imposing h_rest>=q_rest would change which photon is called observed
when the electron moves. Instead impose h D_h>=q D_s, where
D_h=u.(1,n_h), D_s=u.(1,n_s), r=D_s/D_h. For

    h(q)=[E-q-E q a02]/[1+E a01-q a12],
    aij=1-ni.nj,
    B=1+E a02+r(1+E a01),

the allowed emitted second-photon energy ends at

    q_max=2E/[B+sqrt(B^2-4 r a12 E)].

This stable smaller root obeys equal LAB photon energies at the boundary.
The absorption branch remains separate and uses negative signed q.

## 5. Finite matrix-valued matching

Apply the Stage85 whole-axis subtraction to every Mueller/source entry.
The direction-dependent pole factors into the normalized Born Mueller map,
the Born event moment and

    a_Omega=alpha/(4pi^2) |p_recoil cross n|^2/(E_final-p_recoil.n)^2.

Below q_max the paired thermal integrand contains

    N(q,T_direction) [R(q) H(q) F(q)-R(-q) H(-q) F(-q)
                     -2 a_Omega F_Born M_Born chi(q)/q]
      +R(q)[H(q)-1]F(q).

Here R is the signed real Mueller density and H is the ratio of final-hard
Bose factors at actual and Born LAB photon energies. Above q_max only the
crossed absorption and reference subtraction remain. A changed profile chi
requires the same finite reference conversion as Stage85. Testing
chi=exp[-(q/(0.2 T_direction))^2] against chi=1 checks this matrix matching,
not just its intensity entry. The finite virtual map, dressed phase-space
derivative, hard-Bose derivative and kinematic moment shift are then added.

The moment array is event count followed by photon (E,px,py,pz) and electron
(E,px,py,pz). Its first entry is NOT net photon-number production. Photon
energy/momentum includes both photons in a real event. Hard Stokes tags on
electron moment rows are joint diagnostics, not an electron polarization.
For the gas four-force use the outgoing-intensity row and the appropriate
incoming radiation density matrix.

## 6. Fixed canonical momentum, not fixed velocity

The rest-frame construction follows an electron at fixed v when its thermal
mass changes. A gas distribution is integrated at fixed lab canonical p.
Since v=p/sqrt(p^2+m^2), at bare m=1

    delta v = -v d/(2 gamma^2).

Let B(v) be the complete Born LAB source including angular/flux factors,
polarization rotations, Bose occupation and moment weights. Then

    delta B_at_fixed_p = delta B_at_fixed_v
                        -d v.grad_v B/(2 gamma^2).

This is a chain-rule term, not a new fit coefficient. A fourth-order centered
directional difference evaluates the derivative. It is independently checked
using the exact massive Born family B(m,v)/bare_sigma_Thomson, where the
cross-section factor scales as m^-2, E and T arguments scale as m^-1 and
four-vector moment weights scale as m. Direct fixed-p differentiation agrees
with fixed-v differentiation plus the derived velocity term. Halving the
difference step checks the numerical derivative. This reparameterization does
not replace the other, genuinely matrix-valued thermal corrections.

## 7. What this gives the existing parent next

The source now accepts electron motion and arbitrary incident Stokes data,
with the two-photon real amplitude available for both readouts. The next
operation is a frequency/angular weak collision projection with inverse
weights and electron-distribution averaging, followed by the existing gas/
radiation finite-wavenumber evolution. Use the Stage85 microreversible weak
form; do not double count inverse DC as a second independent reaction.

The retained Feynman virtual pole prescription produces phase-sensitive
conditional entries. They must not be advertised as cosmic circular
polarization: reflected electron velocities pair under a parity-symmetric
gas, and the kinetic cut/gain-loss interpretation still has to be applied.
The soft-photon frequency readout, finite-density/collective correction and
unresolved infrared occupation must likewise be treated at the projection,
not set to zero because a conditional event rate is finite.

The inherited vacuum result was an unpolarized resting-electron benchmark.
This stage does not silently promote it to a full moving polarized vacuum
loop. The thermal correction refers to the stated lab-ordered observable.
No new free parameter, empirical fit, cosmological background, Newton
constant or electron mass is derived by these source computations.
