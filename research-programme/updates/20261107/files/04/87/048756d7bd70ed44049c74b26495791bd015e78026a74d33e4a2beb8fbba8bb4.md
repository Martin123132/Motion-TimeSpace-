# Resolved acceleration current and third-grid C0 sensitivity

At fixed instantaneous positions and velocities, vary only the material field acceleration, map acceleration and metric time derivative H. Write g=q_R, T=qdot-Wg, A=momentum_rate, E=(alpha*T^2+beta*g^2)/2. Then

    delta alpha_t = alpha*(H_mu/(R*F)-H_nu),
    delta A = alpha*(delta qddot-delta Wdot*g)+delta alpha_t*T.

The inherited force/current densities simplify exactly to

    delta f = delta A*(z-eta*g),
    delta j_h = delta A*(T*I_h-V_h).

Indeed delta beta_t=-beta*delta alpha_t/alpha and
delta[Edot+(W_R+alpha_t/alpha)E]=T*delta A. This cancels the apparent extra energy terms without dropping them. The gravitational addition is integral N*H_mu/(kappa*sqrt(F))*h dR. No moving-cut atom arises: the positions, boundaries and instantaneous metric are fixed. Native and source-clock currents depend on their instantaneous configuration/velocities, not these accelerations, and therefore have zero variation in this channel.

For C0 reaction sensitivity use the owned uncertainty acceleration and H from the matching THIRD-grid response. For C2 use the existing compensated field acceleration, delta Wdot=eta+D*e_source, and its existing live velocity-metric response V. Nothing is newly fitted or prescribed.

Evaluate the cancellation-prone weight using the exact nodal identity

    T*I_h-V_h = sum_i b_i*v_i*(I_h(R)-I_h(R_i))-W*g*I_h(R).

Use polynomial divided differences and unrounded nodal separations, as in the validated stable C0 current. Keep fixed-metric and metric-induced delta A terms separate during accumulation. This re-integrates C2 on the resolved radial/label cuts instead of assuming its old material quadrature is sufficiently accurate. Its force and currents are compared to the old owned column; disagreement is retained, not relabelled as a pass.

Both channels share one radial24/32, label28/36 traversal. Save compensated sums and incomplete markers at safe boundaries. The actual-point oracle evaluates signed changes in the original unsimplified density with96-digit arithmetic on the same supplied binary64 inputs. In that oracle, the map-acceleration term can equivalently be inserted as a local field-acceleration shift -delta Wdot*g, because all other kinematics are fixed. The algebraic identity itself is tested at high precision; binary64-versus-oracle errors are recorded separately. This is numerical validation, not physical accuracy to96digits.

C0 sensitivity retains the existing2e-11 relative pointwise gate. For the independently re-integrated C2, the new predeclared binary64 pointwise relative gate is2e-8, reflecting subtraction of nearly-null compensated accelerations. It does not replace or relax any old C2 replay gate; retain all old warnings. Integrated paired differences, oracle differences and source differences are reported, and no smaller coefficient uncertainty is accepted merely because one diagnostic is small.

Before any compatibility promotion, require a complete higher-grid C0 pair and its matching sensitivity. C2 comparisons may be used to localize the shared discrepancy. No historical baseline is subtracted and no reference residual is removed from MTS.
