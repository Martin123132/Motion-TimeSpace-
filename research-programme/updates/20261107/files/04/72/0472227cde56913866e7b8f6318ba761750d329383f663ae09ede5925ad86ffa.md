## 2. Second-order orbit expansion

Use the Darwin anomaly chi, r0=M p and circular frequencies
Omega_phi0=1/(M p^(3/2)), Omega_r0=Omega_phi0 sqrt(1-6/p).
Define

    a=2/(p-2)-2+1/(p-6),
    b0=-2/(p-2)^2,
    b2=4/(p-2)^2+3+3/[2(p-6)^2]-4/(p-2)
       +2/[(p-2)(p-6)]-2/(p-6),
    t2=b0+b2/2,
    phi2=3/[4(p-6)^2].

The time and phase Jacobians expand as

    t'(chi)=t0'[1+e a cos(chi)+e^2(b0+b2 cos^2(chi))]+O(e^3),
    phi'(chi)=phi0'[1+e cos(chi)/(p-6)
                         +3e^2 cos^2(chi)/{2(p-6)^2}]+O(e^3).

Consequently

    Omega_r/Omega_r0=1-t2 z+O(z^2),
    alpha_phi=phi2-t2
             =-3(p^2-10p+22)/[2(p-6)(p-2)].

The Newtonian fixed-p limit is alpha_phi -> -3/2, not zero.
The normalized integration weight is
1+e a cos(chi)+e^2 b2[cos^2(chi)-1/2]; normalizing by the radial period is
essential. Also

    r=r0[1-e cos(chi)+e^2 cos^2(chi)]+O(e^3),
    v=e r0 Omega_r0 sin(chi)
       -e^2(2+a)r0 Omega_r0 sin(chi)cos(chi)+O(e^3),
    E=E0+z E2+O(z^2),   E2=E0{1/[2(p-3)]-2/(p-2)^2},
    h=h0+z h2+O(z^2),   h2=h0/[2(p-3)].

All expansions are fixed-p, not fixed periapsis.

## 3. Derive the carrier-source correction

Strip exp(-im phi) from the inherited weak source and write its contraction
with a homogeneous master solution as T(r,v,E,h;omega). The carrier has
omega0=m Omega_phi0. Its first-order phase wobble is

    kappa=2 sqrt[p/(p-6)] (p-3)/(p-2),
    omega t-m phi=-m e kappa sin(chi)+O(e^2).

The second-order periodic phase term is proportional to sin(2chi); its average
against the circular source vanishes. Direct normalized anomaly averaging gives

    I_geo,2 =
        E2 T_E + h2 T_h
        + r0(1-a)T_r/2 + r0^2 T_rr/4
        + r0^2 Omega_r0^2 T_vv/4
        - i m kappa r0 Omega_r0 T_v/2
        - m^2 kappa^2 T0/4.

The derivatives here hold omega,E,h fixed unless explicitly indicated.
Radial derivatives include the radial dependence of the homogeneous solution,
not just the explicit delta-source coefficient.

At fixed omega, use the canonical form

    T=(A+i v D+v^2 G)y - (C+v^2 C2)y_x,
    x=r_*, f=1-2M/r, U=(V-omega^2)/f.
    T_r=(A'-C U)y+(A/f-C')y_x.

Differentiating once more gives

    T_rr=[(A'-CU)'+(A/f-C')U]y
          +[(A'-CU)/f+(A/f-C')']y_x.

A,C,D are stage35's a,c,d (capitalized here to distinguish them from the
time-Jacobian coefficient a). In the polar sector, define L=2+3M/r,
S=8pi E Y/f and B=8+12M/r+84M^2/r^2. The additional coefficients are

    G=S{f'/(3Lf)+2/(3Lr)+2M/(L^2 r^2)
                       +B/(12L^2 f r)-L'/(3L^2)},
    C2=-C/f^2.

Thus T_vv=2G y+2C y_x/f^2. In the exact integrated-by-parts odd source,
there is no v^2 term, so T_vv=0. Both sectors retain their linear-velocity
contribution. Independent comparison with the original polar canonical
source verifies these coefficients.

### Frequency shift of the entire wave response

The carrier frequency also changes:
omega=omega0+z omega2+..., omega2=alpha_phi omega0.

For any physical amplitude,

    A_wave,2 = A_geo,2 + omega2 partial_omega A_wave,0.

The derivative acts on the entire matched wave response: interior, propagation,
outgoing normalization and the frequency-dependent source functional.
It is not legitimate to differentiate a source coefficient while freezing
the body response.

The source correction is analytic. The frequency derivative is evaluated
numerically by a five-point centered stencil, with fractional steps0.001
and0.0005, through actual interior/exterior solves. This is a controlled
response derivative, not a fit to finite-e data.

## 4. Flux coefficients and the action-derived P2

For pair power P=c omega^2 |A_wave|^2, c=3/(4pi),

    P_flux,2 = 2c omega0^2{
                 Re[A_wave,0* A_wave,2]+alpha_phi |A_wave,0|^2}.

An asterisk denotes complex conjugation. For the small body-minus-BH
difference, expand interference terms directly rather than subtracting large
powers. The BH total includes absorption; the candidate remains nonabsorbing
in the retained linear model.

The second-order flux sums contain:
- each carrier's P_flux,2;
- the already derived first-sideband powers, whose amplitudes start at e.
Modes with |n|>=2 first contribute power at e^4 and are not needed for P2.

For angular-momentum loss, the carrier coefficient includes the frequency
denominator correction:

    L2_carrier = (m/omega0)[P_flux,2-alpha_phi P_flux,0],
    L2_sideband = (m/omega_mn) P_flux,mn^(2).

The polar m=0,n=1 sideband contributes energy but no angular momentum.

Expand the specific angular action as h(p,z)=h0(p)+z h2(p)+... .
Then the O(z) angular balance is

    h0' P2 + h2' P0 + h2 Gamma0 = -L2,
    P2 = [-L2-h2'P0-h2 Gamma0]/h0'.

Independently the energy balance gives

    P2 = [-F2-E2'P0-E2 Gamma0]/E0'.

These agree numerically at every sampled radius and in each parity/channel.
The two expressions test the energy/angular implementation; they do not by
themselves replace the independent finite-e source check.

## 6. Consistent timing and phase to first order in initial e^2

Let zi=e_initial^2 and write z(p)=zi Z(p)+O(zi^2), where

    Z(p)=exp{integral[p_initial,p] Gamma0/P0 dp'}.

The clock and phase are expanded strictly as

    T=T0+zi K_T+O(zi^2),
    Phi=Phi0+zi K_Phi+O(zi^2),
    T=eta t/M,   Phi=eta phi,   eta=mu/M.

Their new coefficient kernels are

    dK_T/dp = - Z P2/(M^2 P0^2),
    dK_Phi/dp = [Omega_phi0/(M P0)] Z [alpha_phi-P2/P0].

Z differs between the candidate and BH; this difference must also enter the
difference of the clock coefficients. The implementation carries that
difference using expm1 of the difference in ln Z, together with stable
differences of P2/P0 and 1/P0.

In the Newtonian zero-tide limit, Z=(p/pi)^(19/6). The closed forms are

    K_T,N = 75[pi^(43/6)-p^(43/6)]/[11008 pi^(19/6)],
    K_Phi,N = 105[p^(17/3)-pi^(17/3)]/[8704 pi^(19/6)],

where pi denotes the INITIAL SEMILATUS, not the constant3.14159.

A leading differential finite-size check is also derived. Set
u=lambda/(M^5 p^5), h_abs=p^-4 and
DeltaL=-23lambda(p^-5-pi^-5)/(2M^5)+35(p^-4-pi^-4)/24.
The corresponding first-order differential coefficient kernels are

    dDeltaK_T/dp=(5/64)p^3 (p/pi)^(19/6)
                 [(345/4)u-(29/4)h_abs-(5/8)DeltaL],
    dDeltaK_Phi/dp=-(5/64)p^(3/2) (p/pi)^(19/6)
                 [-(309/4)u+(23/4)h_abs-(7/8)DeltaL].

Their analytic primitives are saved in results/weak-field/status.json.
The initial-semilatus symbols are explicitly supplied when evaluating them;
they are not silently parsed as the mathematical constant pi.
