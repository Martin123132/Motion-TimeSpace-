# Reading the leftover physically

The saved primary mu/y amplitudes are held FIXED. This readout does not
refit them when inspecting different frequency intervals.

Use Stage108's stable physical spectrum for the16 window columns.
For the parent baseline evaluate the same inherited basis with the same
Stage10865-digit readout. Apply the primary energy-matching convention
and the standard template normalization to both.

Store temperature,mu,y and residual in occupation and specific intensity,
with frequency in Hz at the actual saved epoch. At frequencies where
large signed components cancel, verify reconstruction against256eps
times the sum of absolute component magnitudes. Do not interpret loss
of relative precision near a zero as a physical residual.

Locate the residual squared norm in six intervals:
[1e-5,1e-3], [1e-3,1e-2], [1e-2,1e-1], [1e-1,1], [1,10], [10,60].
Use300 and600 logarithmic Gauss nodes PER interval; their sum must
reproduce the primary whole-band residual squared norm within1e-8.
This is a cheap integral of SAVED spectral functions, not a collision
integral or source reevaluation.

Unlike refitting after a cutoff, these bins diagnose where the existing
fixed-template residual is located. They do not identify its physical
cause, supply an uncomputed infrared tail, or produce a present-day
observable. Negative residual values are legitimate signed distortions.
