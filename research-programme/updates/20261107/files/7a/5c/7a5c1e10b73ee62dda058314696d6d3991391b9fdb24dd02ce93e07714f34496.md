# Final-epoch budget and residual-shape conventions

Let P be the saved linear response-to-spectrum map at the final epoch. The
saved weighted readout splits potential f into a temperature contribution,
two energy-orthogonalized mu/y templates, and a residual r. The stored energy
coordinate fixes the temperature projection; eta_standard includes the template
temperature shifts. Everything uses the original700-node measure. Thus a
response increment has its own linear scalar amplitudes and residual; compute
these directly rather than subtract two nearly equal large observables.

For the saved endpoint repair a and physical increments d54,d65:

    f4 = f_saved + a
    f5 = f4 + P(d54)
    f6 = f4 + P(d54) + P(d65) = f4 + P(d64).

The saved baseline b is separate. The three quantities b, f6, b+f6 all use the
same readout. In high precision R[(b+f6)-(b+f5)] = R[P(d65)]; numerical
cancellation of a shared baseline is not a certificate of its physical accuracy.

For latest matched increments r_k and the final time-step change r_t define

    E(x) = sum_k |r_k(x)| + |r_t(x)|,
    S = sum_k ||r_k||_w + ||r_t||_w.

Then ||sum r_k+r_t||_w <= ||E||_w <= S by the triangle inequality.
These statements bound THESE RECORDED CHANGES. They bound the unknown numerical
remainder only if separate arguments establish corresponding component bounds.
We do not assume such arguments or statistical independence. Scalar amplitude
allowances use absolute sums by the same rule. Twofold low parts and arithmetic
repairs are diagnostic contributions, not independent physical errors.

The latest incoming-band increments occur at interior temperature anchors;
endpoint6-5 spans the saved endpoint windows. Older overlapping refinements are
not added again. Available interior-site responses provide a separate
de-correlated-site sensitivity scenario. Endpoint6-5 remains a matched-endpoint
change; a sitewise endpoint6-5 bound is not available from these saved columns.

Frequency localization is performed on output x using the already used1,3,8,20
boundaries, plus the fixed basis endpoints. These masks do not alter any
quadrature nodes or incoming integration bands. Input-band provenance and output
spectral-frequency regions are different and are explicitly named separately.

Local/cubic and matching/join comparisons remain alternative saved readouts,
not fitted error distributions. Their spreads are reported separately rather
than being counted as certified integration errors. Other incoming bands,
angular/electron/loop/real-emission rules, frequency-basis truncation, inner-history
integration, baseline uncertainty and temperature-law truncation remain outside
this partial propagated budget unless independently supplied.
