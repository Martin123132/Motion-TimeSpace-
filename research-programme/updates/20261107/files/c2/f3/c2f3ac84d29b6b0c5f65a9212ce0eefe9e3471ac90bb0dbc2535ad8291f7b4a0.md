## 2. New exact acceleration-current reduction

At fixed positions and velocities, the acceleration-only variation of the original force and three currents reduces to

    delta A = alpha*(delta qddot-delta Wdot*g)
              + alpha*T*(H_mu/(R*F)-H_nu),
    delta force = delta A*(z-eta*g),
    delta current_h = delta A*(T*I_h-V_h).

The apparently separate energy terms cancel exactly: delta[Edot+(W_R+alpha_t/alpha)E]=T*delta A. This identity removes no physical term. The gravitational current variation is added separately. The cancellation-prone weight is evaluated through nodal primitive differences, not by subtracting two nearly equal integrated expressions.

Two channels use this identity: the actual third-grid candidate-reaction sensitivity for C0, and an independent resolved-cut integration of the existing C2 acceleration column. Both retain their existing parent-owned acceleration and metric responses; there are no new fitted coefficients. See DERIVATION-20261001-resolved-acceleration-current.md and its four cited derivations.

Symbolic cancellation and an omitted-term negative control pass. On twelve actual points per branch, a 96-digit signed perturbation of the original unsimplified expression tests the reduction independently. For both branches, binary64 relative differences are approximately 1.315632e-15/reaction and 1.355588e-9/C2. The high-precision algebraic differences are much smaller; this is numerical validation on supplied inputs, not physical precision to 96 digits.
