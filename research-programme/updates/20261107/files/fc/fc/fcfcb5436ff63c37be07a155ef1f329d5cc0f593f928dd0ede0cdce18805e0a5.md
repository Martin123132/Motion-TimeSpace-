# Stage94: resolved logarithmic recoil/motion control

## Same parent, same calibrated source

Use Stage93's exact identity at the same physical population and angular nodes:

    R = D + V + S + (C0 - Gsampled),
    Cphysical,est = Gaccurate + R.

D is the exact-amplitude difference, C0 the leading intensity evaluated with
exact recoil and motion, V the virtual term and S the state/population term.
The old physical profiles, all96 tests and the independent gas partner remain.
This step improves integration of C0-Gsampled; it does not introduce a new
action, new response spectrum, new coupling or new empirical fit.

## Why the large global rule was inefficient

Stage93 resolved the expensive amplitude difference to0.19324% in its last
radial comparison, but the kinematic bracket still changed12.363%. Its drift
norm was98.975% of the total remainder drift norm. Each large term in the
bracket changed by about1.8–2.0e-9; subtracting them left about1.57e-10.
The issue was therefore not floating-point subtraction alone: the separate
global rules were still integrating oscillatory high-mode products unevenly.
The retained basis is a stable recurrence in (x-pivot)/(x+pivot), not a set of
independent smooth low-degree polynomials in log frequency. A large global
Gaussian order does not provide a useful local resolution guarantee.

## Exact change of variable and panel measure

For each inherited positive interval [a,b] let y=log(s). Split [log(a),log(b)]
into equal panels with maximum width h, keeping both exact endpoints. With
Gaussian nodes xi and weights wi on [-1,1], panel midpoint m and halfwidth d,

    s_i = exp(m+d xi),
    integral_a^b F(s) ds -> sum_panels sum_i d wi s_i F(s_i).

The s_i factor is the exact Jacobian, not an adjustable normalization. All
weights are positive and all nodes are strictly inside their original interval.
Caching the dimensionless Gaussian rule changes no nodes or weights.

Apply this transformation independently inside the inherited spontaneous and
thermal pieces. Keep exact moving ordering boundaries, absorption boundary,
soft floor, thermal knots, tail cutoff, Bose factors and logarithmic pole
addbacks. Both C0 and Gsampled use the same panel policy, while retaining their
own documented physical endpoints and exact Jacobians. They are not claimed
to be an identical finite quadrature or an already pointwise-subtracted kernel.

## Independent error budget without a physical closure

For high/low panel rules, form deltaK=Khigh-Klow. Independently retain the
saved deltaD=D192-D96 and the leading-addback refinement deltaG. In the original
mass-dual norm the triangle inequality gives

    ||deltaK+deltaD+deltaG|| <= ||deltaK||+||deltaD||+||deltaG||.

This is an exact norm inequality for these computed differences. Calling its
right-hand side an observed refinement budget does NOT make it a rigorous
bound on the unknown continuum quadrature error. It is conditional on the
fixed angular/population grid and on the saved virtual/state resolution.
Those terms are reused, not proven exact by being unchanged in this ladder.

Test the corresponding linear finite-heat photon/gas response separately.
Linearity permits propagating deltaK, deltaD and deltaG independently and
using the same norm inequality on their response changes. No gas equilibrium
condition, thermal template or high-mode deletion is allowed to improve a gate.

The acceptance target is1% on both full96 remainder and response. Global-to-
panel disagreement is kept as a diagnostic even if successive panel refinement
passes. Angular, outer population and consistent domain/tail companion terms
must be checked before feeding any new source into full histories.
