# Preserve the mass solve in a high/low representation

The first Stage241 port trials are retained without alteration. They have
46,44 and56 failed checks at warm,middle,cold. The stricter cubic-response
identity exposes cancellation in the rounded photon solve, even though the
native stress-row comparison itself passes.

The diagnosis separates three quantities, rather than assigning the failure
to physics: the stored direct energy row; the compensated energy contraction
of the stored radial weak rows; and the compensated energy contraction of
the mass-solved photon coefficients. Their worst relative differences are
3.9722e-11 (weak versus direct),8.9174e-11 (solve versus weak), and1.0394e-10
(solve versus direct). The first two radial coordinates cancel by a factor
up to568534. The mass matrix condition number is approximately1: this is
loss of the small response in a rounded coefficient vector, not an unstable
mass matrix. Replacing the gas row or widening a tolerance would not fix it.

Let the unchanged effective mass be M=I+D. Because ||D|| is tiny for this
already orthonormalized basis, an algebraic inverse is

    M^-1 = I-D+D^2 + R3,
    ||R3||_infinity <= ||D||_infinity^3/(1-||D||_infinity).

Keep the weak source as the high coefficient and -D W+D^2 W as its low
coefficient. Apply the exact retained opacity/angular scaling with a
two-product high/low expansion. Do not collapse the pair before energy,
pressure or metric readout. Include both halves in native stress rows.
The mass, source matrices, gas recoil, opacity and EOS remain unchanged.
This is an algebraically controlled way of solving the same mass system,
not an energy projection or an imposed exchange identity.

The implementation explicitly rejects a mass matrix outside its near-
identity bound. Before the complete rerun, compare selected cancelled
columns to an independent80-digit inverse and contraction. The test uses
the actual frozen matrices and the unchanged1e-10 identity gate. A separate
conservative floating-point plus Neumann remainder estimate is tested as
a solver error bound, not substituted for the physical acceptance gates.

The new candidate is stored in `precision/`, alongside the untouched failed
trial. Its runner reuses the frozen original assessment body, changing only
the physical photon representation and native readout to carry its low half.
The original angular refinement, component additivity, independent recoil,
negative controls and local metric checks are all rerun unchanged.
