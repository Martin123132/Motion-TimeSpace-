# Support/shift response: the geometric time derivative changes the answer

## Outcome

We derived the actual support/shift mixed current from the inherited horizontal action, including its temporal endpoint, wave, native Gram and proper-clock terms. A large apparent response also appeared in the reference model. It was a legitimate partial derivative with acceleration held fixed, but not the derivative along geometric field transport. Completing that transport by the chain rule removes almost all of this common large response without fitting a coefficient.

The corrected reference response decreases on the finer grid. A smaller MTS-specific response remains, resolved above the numerical derivative and quadrature discrepancies. We have therefore obtained the appropriate coupling coefficients for the next constrained calculation, rather than mistaking an incomplete perturbation for new support inertia.

These are weak current-response moments in the existing code normalization, not SI measurements, a new evolved solution, or the support force itself. The previous coupled Euler-force result remains unchanged. Work is private; no GitHub action, subagents or new nonlinear trajectories. Main and validation workers have finished.

## 1. Actual current, not an assumed minimal substitution

Protocol: `DERIVATION-20260928-support-shift-current-jet.md`.

Use the same four saved reference/MTS-primary fine/third states and source/endpoints-pinned test1. The current tests are the separate connection profiles h=1,(R-6),(R-6)^2 with I(R)=integral_b^R h. They are not the support profile eta. Original and optimally field/source-compensated velocity directions are both retained in the frozen-acceleration stage.

Let g=q_R, W be mesh velocity, T=qdot-W*g, alpha=R^2/(N*sqrtF), beta=R^2*N*sqrtF and a_h=P2(qdot_i*I_i). A dot denotes a cell-material time derivative; beta_t is Eulerian. Then

    E=(alpha*T^2+beta*g^2)/2
    A_dot=(alpha_dot+W_R*alpha)*T+alpha*Tdot
    C=alpha*W*T+beta*g
    X=E_dot+W_R*E-(beta_t/beta)*E
    B_wave=integral (alpha*T*a_h-E*I) dR
    K_wave[h]=-integral (A_dot*a_h+C*(a_h)_R) dR
               +integral (X*I+E*W*h) dR.

This is the raw inherited horizontal variation minus d_t B_wave. The moving-source primitive terms cancel between those two expressions; the source is not silently frozen in the compensated test. Cell geometry, interface transport and material interpolation are retained.

An independently checked identity explains why simply substituting the minimal ADM wave current would be incorrect off shell. On each smooth cell, with E_q,strong=A_dot-C_R,

    X=E_q,strong*T+partial_R(beta*T*g+W*E),
    K_wave[h]=-integral beta*T*g*h dR
               +integral E_q,strong*(T*I-a_h) dR
               +sum_cell_boundaries [(beta*T*g+W*E)*I-C*a_h].

Each cell boundary has its oriented trace. Do not discard internal faces or exterior terms, and do not equate a weakly solved finite-element equation with pointwise E_q,strong=0. This formula is a decomposition, not a subtraction prescription.

For velocity perturbation (chi,zeta,deltaV), the separately derived acceleration block is

    C2[h]=integral alpha*(chi_h-zeta_h*g)*(T*I-a_h) dR.

For the native kernel, f=D_R*q, load=S^T(f^2/2), energy=beta*load, and

    delta(dotload)=S^T[f*(D_R*chi+D_R'[zeta]*q)]
    C_Gram[h]=-sum U_q*chi*I(field nodes)
               +sum [beta*delta(dotload)+beta_R*zeta*load]*I(native nodes)
               +sum energy*zeta*h(native nodes).

The source proper-clock block is N_b^2*F_b*inertia_b*deltaV*h(b), zero in the original pinned direction but retained in the compensated one. Gram has no acceleration dependence in this specified extension.

## 2. The required geometric completion is derived

Protocol: `DERIVATION-20260928-geometric-support-jet-completion.md`.

For a fixed material edge direction eta, take an infinitesimal support history

    delta R=s(t)*eta,
    delta q=s(t)*z(t),
    z_i=eta_i*average_adjacent(g_i).

At s=0, sdot=1, sddot=0, differentiation requires

    delta W=eta,             delta qdot=z,
    delta Wdot=0,            delta qddot=2*zdot,
    zdot_i=eta_i*average_adjacent(qdot_R-W_R*g)_i.

The factor2 is a chain-rule coefficient, not a fitted cancellation. It was checked symbolically and by independent real finite perturbations of field and physical cell geometry at all15 material cardinals in each of the four cases. The resulting acceleration direction is interpolated in the original material space.

Thus the original-direction geometric coefficient is

    C1_geometric[h]=C1_frozen[h]
        +integral alpha*(2*zdot_h)*(T*I-a_h) dR.

Gram is unchanged by this acceleration completion. In a smooth patch this gives delta Tdot=eta*T_R and leaves the physical Eulerian temporal rate unchanged. The finite-space defect is measured, not set to zero.

This follow-up completes the ORIGINAL direction only. Completing the optimally compensated direction would additionally require the time derivative of its state-dependent Hessian projection. That derivative was not assumed zero. The saved frozen-acceleration results remain valid as partial derivatives and are not retrospectively relabelled.

## 4. The gravitational equation and the next calculation

The local ADM action in `scripts/derive_annular_adm_clock_quadratic_20260909.py` supplies R*v*L_t/(kappa*N) at linear order in physical shift, with L=1/sqrtF. In the current convention delta v=N^2*F*h,

    Q_gravity=mu_t/(kappa*N*F^(3/2)),
    K_gravity=N*mu_t/(kappa*sqrtF),
    K_gravity+K_matter=0.

The normalization was re-derived symbolically from that saved action. Kuchar's [Geometrodynamics of Schwarzschild Black Holes](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9403003) is convention background, not the source of our computed numbers or a claim that the present finite action is covered by that paper.

This also gives a precise starting row for the coupled metric variation. At fixed areal coordinate, F=1-2mu/R and G=N/(kappa*sqrtF),

    delta K_gravity=G*delta(mu_t)
        +G*mu_t*(delta N/N+delta mu/(R*F)).

Retain the metric dependence of K_matter too. Replacing this row by a freely chosen compensating shift or by just G*delta(mu_t) on a changing background would drop actual action terms.

For general amplitude history, the already defined support direction has

    delta qdot=sdot*z+s*zdot,
    delta qddot=sddot*z+2*sdot*zdot+s*zddot,
    delta W=sdot*eta, delta Wdot=sddot*eta.

Within each cell, gddot=qddot_R-2*W_R*gdot-Wdot_R*g; therefore zddot is eta times the same adjacent-trace average of gddot. The completed calculation supplies C1, and the acceleration test supplies C2, in

    delta K_matter=C0*s+C1*sdot+C2*sddot+metric-response terms.

The next bounded construction is to assemble C0 from the SAME geometric history, include the native-kernel changes and all metric/lapse responses, and solve the linearized coupled constraints before reevaluating the SAME support Euler test. Do this on reference and MTS first, with a saved small-response/rank diagnostic, rather than launching another day-long refinement run. These three current moments are diagnostics, not a complete spatial constraint basis or a rank proof.

There is a useful safeguard against inventing an inertia. In a frozen FIRST-ORDER block L_mix=v^T*(G*mudot+C*sdot), eliminating the common vdot leaves

    E_support,0-C^T*G^(-T)*E_metric,0=0.

The mixed velocity map still has direction (-G^(-1)*C,1) in its kernel. This finite-dimensional identity was checked explicitly; it is not a no-go theorem for the full variable-coefficient/higher-jet system. In that system the support reaction from v*(C0*s+C1*sdot+C2*sddot) is instead

    -C0*v + d_t(C1*v) - d_t^2(C2*v).

This full adjoint, including coefficient derivatives and boundary terms, is the action-derived route to test. Do not turn the earlier nearly singular fixed-metric support mass into a physical cure, and do not simply impose a support rule.
