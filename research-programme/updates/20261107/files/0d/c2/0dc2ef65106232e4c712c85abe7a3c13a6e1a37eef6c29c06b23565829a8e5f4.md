# Stage125 — initial transient law and initialization ownership

Coordinate t is the saved expansion coordinate, not proper time.
Let u(t) be the original outer reconstructed slope and e(t) its unresolved
inner correction on the fixed5265-node grid. The saved implementation is

    e' = L(t)e + f(t) - u'(t),      e(t0)=0.

L is the retained positive-stencil Compton plus emission inner generator;
f includes the distinct fourth-order outer transport, source terms and
fixed boundary continuation. They are not interchangeable operators.

Write b0=f(t0)-u'(t0+), L0=L(t0). The right derivative uses the earliest
next original baseline/window knot, not the65-epoch source interpolation.
The exact finite-matrix frozen-coefficient initial-value solution is

    efrozen(t0+tau) = exp(L0*tau)e0
                    + integral_0^tau exp(L0*(tau-s))*b0 ds.

This integral remains valid when L0 is singular. In particular
e'(t0+)=b0 for zero matched initial defect. A linear source map S=A(t)e
then obeys S(t0)=0 and S'(t0+)=A(t0)b0; a rapid rise is not excluded by
a zero initial value. No inversion of L0 or invertibility assumption is used.

For the actual coefficients define delta=e-efrozen. Exactly,

    delta' = L0*delta + (L(t)-L0)e + (b(t)-b0), delta(t0)=0.

Thus the same semigroup convolves the coefficient/driver mismatch.
Freezing is a controlled reference to compare, not automatically accurate.
The implementation computes its BE/BDF2 discretization, not the analytic
exponential. A measured match is neither a rigorous global error bound nor
a derivation of physical initial conditions.

The first actual backward-Euler step with e0=0 obeys exactly

    (I-h L1)e1 = h f1 - (u1-u0)
              = h [f1-(u1-u0)/h].

This resolvent law, not e1=h*b0, is appropriate for a stiff first step.
Subsequent steps use the unchanged BDF2 rule. Per-column residuals and old
quarter/end states are checked; the frozen reference gets separate residuals.

## Initialization audit

The consumed source is ../inner-time-heavy40960-20261013/refine_inner_again.py,
lines70–74: outer comes from the saved driver, error is zeros_like(outer),
and the initial total slope equals outer. The cold script inherits the same
construction. ../driven-low-frequency-turnover-20261012/turnover_common.py
constructs the earlier initial slope from profile_at(start)/frequency.

This is matching the unresolved correction to zero at the chosen start of
a conditional reconstruction. It is not a proof that a physical cosmology
or parent action requires zero microscopic memory. For nonzero admissible
e0, the frozen law carries exp(L0*tau)e0 and the initial source A0 e0.
We do not introduce or tune such a state here; determining it requires a
consistent prehistory/matching condition. This audit concerns the consumed
implementation, not a claim that no other corpus document addresses it.

## Experiment and separation

Only interval0 source sampling changes, subdivisions4 to8 to16. Fresh
states reuse step0 restarts and all Stage124 points elsewhere remain fixed.
The assembled source retains the65-bank adjoint interpolation; the direct
source uses physically evaluated adjoints at its additional temperatures.
The inner coefficient bank is not regenerated in either case. The frozen
state is source-evaluated diagnostically, not substituted into the actual
response. Radiation readout and gas response use the unchanged2048-step
response integration for every comparison.

Percent changes refer to the small conditional correction, never to total
gas temperature. Save signed components, peak/late/endpoint denominators,
cancellation factors, absolute differences and sign flags. A shrinking
difference at4/8/16 is observed contraction, not an asymptotic-order proof.
