## Angular calculation

Write t=x0, x=x2, v=x1=t-x, w=x/t. Label the lower-energy outgoing photon as2,
so 0<w<=1/2 and each unordered outgoing pair is counted once.
After integrating the companion direction, the intensity angular polynomial is

    K(c,w)=A(w)+B(w)(1-c^2)-10w^3 c(1-c)^2,
    A=2(1-w)^2(3-7w+13w^2-7w^3+3w^4),
    B=11-21w+30w^3-21w^5+11w^6.

Our Legendre projection gives

    k0=A+2B/3+20w^3/3,
    k1=-16w^3, k2=-2B/3+40w^3/3, k3=-4w^3,
    lambda_l(w)=k_l/[(2l+1)k0], lambda_l=0 for l>=4.

For y=w(1-w), k0=(40/3)(1-3y+3y^2/2-y^3).
The soft incident/emitted moments are [1,0,-11/100,0,...].
The three direction-pair averages are

    <P_l(n0.n2)>=lambda_l(w),
    <P_l(n0.n1)>=lambda_l(1-w),
    <P_l(n1.n2)>=lambda_l(-w/(1-w)).

The second relation exchanges outgoing photons. The third crosses the incoming
and companion legs in the symmetric squared amplitude; angle-independent
phase-space factors cancel in the normalized moment. The crossed expression
also obeys k0(-w/(1-w))*(1-w)^6=k0(w).
Do not infer a polarization kernel by applying these intensity averages to E/B.

## Bose factors determine the energy ledger

At the retained recoil-free order the electron forward/reverse factors agree:

    F=n0(1+n1)(1+n2)-(1+n0)n1n2
     =n0+n0*n1+n0*n2-n1*n2.

At a Planck state with t=v+x, write delta n_i=nP_i(1+nP_i) f_i.
Then delta F=F_eq*(f0-f1-f2), F_eq=nP(t)(1+nP(v))(1+nP(x)).
The forward event removes the incident photon and adds BOTH outgoing photons.
For a test function phi its event difference is

    Dphi=phi(t)-phi(v)-phi(x).

Consequently D(x)=0, whereas D(1)=-1. Photon energy is conserved by this
recoil-free reaction; photon number is not. There is no projection to force
either result. A soft-only production operator cannot be promoted to the
complete microscopic energy source by depositing its soft-photon energy in gas.
Compton and BR still exchange energy with the finite-capacity gas.

In the inherited normalization I3=pi^4/15 the positive event measure is

    dW=t^4 G(w) F_eq/(I3*x) dx dt,
    G(w)=[1-3y+3y^2/2-y^3]/(1-w).

For basis Phi and signs s=(+1,-1,-1),

    C0=-integral dW D(Phi) D(Phi)^T,
    C_l=-integral dW sum_ij s_i s_j lambda_ij Phi_i Phi_j^T/(2l+1).

Here lambda_ii=1. The direction Gram matrix is positive, so Cholesky assembly
preserves dissipation without eigenvalue clipping. The scalar multiplier per
Thomson depth is 4alpha/(3pi)*theta_star^2/(1+14.16theta_star). The denominator
is a retained source approximation, not a newly derived general angular law.
The soft-soft diagonal independently reproduces the previous absorption bank.

## Derived mixed Bose response

Let w_i=nP_i(1+nP_i), n_i=nP_i+chi*w_i*B_i+epsilon*w_i*f_i.
The exact coefficient of chi*epsilon in F is F_eq*sum_i A_i f_i, with

    A0=w0(w1 B1+w2 B2)/F_eq,
    A1=w1(w0 B0-w2 B2)/F_eq,
    A2=w2(w0 B0-w1 B1)/F_eq.

Thus the intensity mixed operator is obtained by replacing the right signed
event vector in C_l by A_j Phi_j. Its energy LEFT null survives because the
left test-function difference is unchanged. It need not be symmetric or
negative on a nonequilibrium background. No such false acceptance test is used.
For the Planck temperature path, Q2(x)=x^2*coth(x/2)-2x obeys

    sum_i A_i[B=x]*x_i + D(Q2)=0.

This verifies the curvature of the equilibrium family as well as its tangent.
The physical-chi test compares with independently evaluated70-digit Bose
derivatives, not a difference of two double-precision nearly equal operators.
The scalar rate correction is frozen at the reference temperature here. Its
general off-equilibrium derivative is not supplied by this Hessian.

## A physical infrared matching result, not a cutoff convention

The real resolved reaction is integrated over xmin<=x, v and t<=60.
Its cutoff is a numerical resolution, not a derived plasma scale. For an
angular temperature perturbation f=x P_l, l>=1, its Rayleigh relaxation is

    beta_l(xmin) = [40*pi^2/7]*(1-lambda_l(1))/xmin + subleading terms.

Derivation: F_eq ~ nP(t)(1+nP(t))/x, G->1, and the squared event difference
averages to 2*t^2*(1-lambda_l(1)). Use integral t^6*nP(1+nP)dt=720*zeta(6)
and temperature-template mass integral x^4*nP(1+nP)dx/I3=4.

prediction. The primary paper explains the matching to unresolved processes

medium treatment on both sides. This stage has not supplied that finite

fix the physical finite part. Keep this sector out of a claim-bearing wave run
until the matching is derived or its total effect is independently bounded.
