## Physical question

Can a collective state of explicit underlying variables produce an effective
spacetime and its gravitational response, instead of putting the metric and
the Einstein action in as independent starting ingredients?

The particle Standard Model is a target to recover, not dark energy. Its
adopted action in the existing framework is preserved for comparison, not
claimed as an emergent result. A condensate analogy alone does not replace it.

## First calculation

Start with the actually declared conservative real scalar from checkpoint
4916. Derive its fluctuation operator about a general background and test
whether a nontrivial causal metric emerges. If that minimal candidate cannot
do this, test the smallest clearly labeled phase/amplitude extension, deriving
the effective phase action rather than choosing a metric by hand. Keep the
underlying background geometry explicit. Compare directly with the previous
clock/coframe and P(X) results, especially 5184 and 5188, to avoid rediscovering
an old obstruction and calling it progress.

No original action is silently modified. New symmetries, fields, potentials,
state choices and approximations must be named as assumptions. A scalar
acoustic metric is not automatically a dynamical spin-two gravity sector.
