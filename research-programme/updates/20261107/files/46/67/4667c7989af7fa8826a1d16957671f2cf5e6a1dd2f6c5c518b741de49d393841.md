# Stage169 — a temperature-dependent kinetic forecast

## What advanced

The saved collision and emission equations give a driven temperature law,
not an autonomous power law for the remaining low-frequency shape. The coupled
photon/gas state and its preceding history matter. Keeping them, the frozen
equation-driven forecast reproduces the saved spectrum at an intermediate
temperature within the unchanged 1% gates for both baseline and MTS.

This is a test of **transport conditional on the supplied microscopic source**.
It is not an independently derived replacement for that source's temperature
curve. In particular it does not erase Stage168's 1.674470--1.677507% held-out
source discrepancy or its propagated final-spectrum sensitivity. No current
history is replaced or refitted here.

## Derived law

With u=ln(a/a_start), theta=k_B T/(m_e c^2), and fixed comoving frequency x,
d ln(theta)/du=-1. For photon coefficients p and scaled gas temperature g,

    dp/dln(theta) = -r M^-1 [L(theta)(p-t g)+J_p],
    chi dg/dln(theta) = chi g + r t^T L(theta)(p-t g)-r J_g+b,
    L = K_Compton - D_DC - D_BR.

For the fixed temperature/mu/y residual projector Q and readout V,

    dR/dln(theta) = -r Q V M^-1 [L(theta)(p-t g)+J_p].

A numerical counterexample changes the resolved temperature component without
changing R, but changes its future derivative. Therefore R alone does not
specify a closed evolution law. This rules out inferring a universal R~T^n
from the absorption exponent alone within this inherited finite system.

The temperature dependences that follow directly from the implemented rates
are, at fixed x and before the common expansion/scattering factor r(theta):

- nonlocal-leading double Compton: D_DC proportional to theta^2;
- free-free emission/absorption: Gamma_BR proportional to
  theta^(-1/2) G(theta,x)(1-exp(-x))/x^3;
- its exact logarithmic slope is -1/2 + dln(G)/dln(theta), retaining the
  existing Gaunt-table interpolation rather than fitting an exponent.

At the target, the free-free slopes at x=.001,.01,.1,1 are respectively
-0.451446, -0.442474, -0.450526 and -0.514167. These are not the slopes of
the complete residual, whose drive and memory remain present. The forecast
uses the full inherited Compton matrix, not a soft approximation over all x<1.
See DERIVATION.md and the 16 passing rate/closure checks in
`rate-audit/results/rate-derivation`.

## Preserving the earlier physical state

The saved full spectrum is hybrid: an old native contribution, resolved
feedback, later source windows, and an unresolved low-frequency carrier.
The first mapping omitted part of that history and failed two endpoint
identities. The corrected mapping reconstructs the Stage166 baseline and MTS
readouts with maximum identity diagnostic 6.712e-15, below the fixed 1e-10 gate.
Earlier contributions have not been discarded to improve the comparison.

The unresolved carrier is supplied identically to forecast and reference;
it is **not predicted** here. Both represented-only and hybrid errors are
reported so it cannot mask a bad forecast of the evolved degrees of freedom.

Four first-attempt tiny energy/gas subtraction checks also fail when recovering
an increment from separately rounded large totals. Their failures remain.
Paired baseline/increment output avoids that information loss; 80-digit and
exact-rational arithmetic checks verify the representation. This is not a new
independent physical linearity result.

Four legacy feedback gas-relative diagnostics remain failed (approximately
6.3033e-6, 2.6414e-6, 3.3660e-5 and 5.6052e-7 versus 1e-7). The replay checks
only establish exact reproduction of the saved reference; they do not certify
its gas accuracy. Forty inherited raw source-moment failures also stay visible.
The transport calculation uses the previously declared canonical numerical
source, not a newly proven conserved raw collision source.

This target existed in the earlier calculations. Moreover, the corrected
mapping was made after inspecting the first comparison. Describe the result
as a retrospective, conditional prediction with a deterministic mapping repair,
not new blind experimental evidence or an independent microscopic source test.

## Next physical derivation

The next calculation should act on the source, not fit another temperature
polynomial to the residual. For the saved operator correction and incident state,

    J(theta) = DeltaK(theta) h(theta),
    dJ/dln(theta) = (dDeltaK/dln(theta)) h
                   + DeltaK dh/dln(theta).

Derive the first term from the finite real/virtual/state event integrand with
its matching soft subtraction, and obtain the second from the incident-state
equations. Both baseline and MTS must use the same differentiation and error
checks. Freeze that source-level prediction before a separate physical
temperature evaluation; then propagate through this tested transport map.
The present agreement establishes which transport machinery can be reused,
not the value of the missing source derivative.
