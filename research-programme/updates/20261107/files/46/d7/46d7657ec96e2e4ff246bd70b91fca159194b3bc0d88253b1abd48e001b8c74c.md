# Stage114 — what actually crosses the numerical join

## 1. Exact product rule,not an extra hypothesis
Write h=Phi_inner-Phi_saved and q=W h. For a physical Doppler ratio r,
Delta f=f(rx)-f(x),bar f=[f(rx)+f(x)]/2. Then identically

    Delta(W h)=bar W Delta h+bar h Delta W.

With c=1/(2 I3) and k_e=w_e/r_e,the selected Stage113 weak functional is

    C[A;Wh]=-c sum_e k_e integral dx Delta A Delta(Wh)=B[A]+J[A],
    B[A]=-c sum_e k_e integral dx Delta A bar W Delta h,
    J[A]=-c sum_e k_e integral dx Delta A bar h Delta W.

B transports gradients of h through the average window;J is supported
on events whose endpoints see different W. Neither is generally a
separately dissipative operator. The sum retains the original Dirichlet
form. Each annihilates a constant test,so each has zero Compton number
moment. Each energy moment is paired with the opposite gas transfer.

Important control:if h is constant,B=0 but J=C[A;W h] need not vanish.
Discarding J would falsely turn a windowed chemical-potential change
into a Compton equilibrium. The join term is necessary,not optional.

## 2. Compact-domain evaluation at saved physical shifts
Use d_plus A=A(rx)-A(x),d_minus A=A(x/r)-A(x),and likewise for h.
Changing variable in the terms containing W(rx) yields

    B[A]=-1/(4 I3) integral_support(W) dx W(x) sum_e k_e
       [d_plus A d_plus h+d_minus A d_minus h/r_e],
    J[A]=+1/(4 I3) integral_support(W) dx W(x) sum_e k_e
       [d_plus A (h(rx)+h(x))+d_minus A (h(x/r_e)+h(x))/r_e].

Adding these returns exactly Stage113's compact adjoint formula.
No frequency ratio is deposited on a grid. Evaluating the saved spline
at actual shifted frequencies reuses the prescribed h;it does not
remove the inherited interpolation already inside h. No extrapolation
is allowed. The sampled ranges are checked at every temperature.

Both fixed windows reuse the union of their original source quadrature
nodes,not a new match parameter. Geometry and basis evaluations are
shared for both backgrounds;all source histories remain distinct.

## 3. Do not hide represented photons in the split
The complete matched forcing was

    D=C_C[q]+C_DC[q]+C_BR[q]-sum_a K_a p, p=M^-1 <V,q>.

Use three forcing groups:
    bulk=B,
    join=J,
    remainder=C_DC[q]+C_BR[q]-sum_a K_a p.

Thus D=bulk+join+remainder exactly. The remainder is a bookkeeping
group,NOT a new scattering process. The parent linear response solver
propagates each group with the same96-mode operator and gas coupling.
The physical final change also includes q-Vp,which is not silently
assigned to either Compton group.

For two windows,report the four vector differences and signed projections
on their total difference. Large individual norms may cancel. A norm
ratio is not a percentage of a measured signal.

## 4. Derived common-overlap residual for the next step
Let L_full be a consistently defined full-frequency linear photon/gas
evolution operator and L_soft its inner approximation,on a common state
space. First consider the photon block and a fixed-in-time window W,
with scalar rate R(u). Define

    r_h=(partial_u-R L_soft)h, Delta L=L_full-L_soft.
    (partial_u-R L_full)(W h)
       =W r_h-R[L_soft,W]h-R Delta L(W h).

This follows by adding/subtracting W L_soft h;it is exact on functions
in both operator domains. A time-dependent W adds (partial_u W)h.
Gas/number moments must accompany the collision terms as in the
existing paired response. This identity does NOT assume r_h=0:inner
and outer histories can have different drivers and approximation errors.

The symmetric weak join term J above is NOT itself identical to the
strong commutator [L_soft,W]h. They are different decompositions.
Do not subtract J as though it were automatically a residual correction.

For an outer approximate solution with residual r_outer,the composite
residual is r_outer plus the right-hand side above. A consistent defect
correction must solve the full driven residual problem with specified
initial/boundary data. An arbitrary cancelling counterterm is not a
derived MTS prediction. In particular,if an exact outer solution and
identical zero-change initial data already solve the same unique full
problem,cancelling an arbitrary Wh cannot create a new physical effect.

Our96-mode outer history and inner spline do not yet supply a full
common-space finite-frequency remainder. The calculations here identify
which joining contributions must be kept while constructing that overlap.
They do not declare Delta L zero or promote the soft kernel to full recoil.
