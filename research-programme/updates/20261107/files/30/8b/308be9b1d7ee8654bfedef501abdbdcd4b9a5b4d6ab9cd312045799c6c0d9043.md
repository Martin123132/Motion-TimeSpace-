# Explicit third-jet update after the curvature repair

At an ordinary native interface, use the time-symmetric assumptions and exact moving laws in `DERIVATION-20261002-moving-native-transmission-and-third-jets.md`. Set j=[g], m=(g_plus+g_minus)/2, a_X=X_ddot and E=(c^2)_R+d+a_X. Keep the constructed value, first-jet and second-jet data fixed. The regular bulk equation gives

    g_ddot_minus/plus = c^2*ell_minus/plus + E*k_minus/plus + d_R*g_minus/plus.

The two required second time derivatives are fixed by the action covectors:

    j_ddot_required = (A_ddot-(beta_ddot-2*alpha*a_X^2)*j)/beta,
    m_ddot_required = -(A_ddot*m+C_ddot)/A.

Thus the update is explicit:

    ell_minus_required = (m_ddot_required-j_ddot_required/2-E*k_minus-d_R*g_minus)/c^2,
    ell_plus_required  = (m_ddot_required+j_ddot_required/2-E*k_plus-d_R*g_plus)/c^2.

This local two-by-two map is invertible for c^2>0 and A!=0: take the mean and jump of the first equation. The velocity-squared term is retained; no source acceleration is fitted. This gives target third jets once the coupled right-hand sides have been evaluated.

For each unchanged physical interval, the previously derived degree-seven endpoint lift independently changes outgoing/incoming third derivatives while preserving both endpoint values and first/second derivatives. On an interval containing the distinguished source there is an additional source-value constraint. A degree-eight bubble x^4*(1-x)^4 has zero endpoint derivatives through order three and is nonzero at every strictly interior source point. Subtract the induced source value times this bubble divided by its value at the source. This preserves all native jets through order three AND q(b)=0. Source one-sided trace conditions still require explicit rechecking.

The metric second derivative, complete source acceleration, A_ddot and C_ddot respond to the changed bulk profile. This local update is therefore conditional, not a licence to freeze the coupled problem and declare success. After constructing the lift, recompute radial gravity and the complete action acceleration, then test the moving field-jump and shape-preservation equations together. Retain non-silent compensated source-hinge terms. Finite material samples do not certify all labels.
