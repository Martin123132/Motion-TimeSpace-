## The new derivation

At fixed baseline, position displacement and first time tangent, changing
the computed reaction by delta k and its mixed metric response by delta H
changes the position-response densities by

    delta A = -alpha*(delta H_nu-delta H_mu/(R*F))*T
              +alpha*(delta k_field-D*delta k_source*g),
    delta C0_force_density = delta A*(z-eta*g),
    delta C0_current_density = delta A*(T*I_h-V_h).

The gravitational current contributes the integral of
N/(kappa*sqrt(F))*delta H_mu*h. Native, source-clock and boundary terms
have zero sensitivity to THESE reaction arguments, not zero original
position contributions. The derivation is in
`DERIVATION-20260930-C0-reaction-sensitivity.md`.

Manufactured validation passes24 checks across pure field, source,
metric and combined changes, including finite-linearity and omission
controls. Actual active-support comparisons with the complete density
formula have relative disagreement at most6.36e-15 on sampled points.
They are explicitly non-vacuous active-support samples, not only an
outside-support test.

## Boundary and native handling

Eulerian integration uses moving-material-face terms and outer endpoints.
Material integration instead uses the regular material derivative plus
the measure derivative and fixed-radial-cut atoms +U[L]. These two sets
of boundary terms are never added together. Ten manufactured boundary
checks and eight material-density checks pass, including omitted/wrong
boundary and deliberately rounded tiny-jump controls.

Decimal arithmetic before conversion to binary64. This does not turn

kinematics retain their inherited precision. The measured gap between
