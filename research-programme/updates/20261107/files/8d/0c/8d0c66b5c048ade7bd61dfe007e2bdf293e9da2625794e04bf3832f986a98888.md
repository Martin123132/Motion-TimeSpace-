## 4. The canonical gravitational surface conversion

    K = (6r²+6Mr+6M²) Z/[r²(2r+3M)] + V,

## 5. Why A_action must match -s2

At fixed surface master z(t), the canonical on-shell interior action has
Fourier kernel

    S_int,on-shell = -(N_Z/2) integral s(omega) |z(omega)|² [Fourier measure].

Therefore

    s(omega)=s0+s2 omega²+...,
    S_int,on-shell=(N_Z/2) integral dt [(-s2) zdot²-s0 z²]+... .

The action calculation above evaluates precisely this order-omega² term,
including the conversion to the master variable. It yields

    A = -s2.

The coefficient of zdot in the reduced canonical momentum is N_Z A. This
also identifies the leading boundary symplectic/energy normalization. It is
a low-frequency reduced statement on the driven branch, not a diagonalization
of the full nonspherical interior Hamiltonian.
