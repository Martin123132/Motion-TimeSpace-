# Direct original-variable check, without changing a tolerance

The first independent validator contains 100 checks, of which 60 fail. All 60
are retained in `results/validation/status.json`. They are the sampled Radau
stage-derivative reconstructions and the photon/gas equations evaluated through
those rounded derivatives. The successful spectral forecasts do not override
these failures.

The reconstruction infers a first-stage derivative from the difference of two
stored endpoint states and a stiff float64 generator. Subtracting nearly equal
values amplifies rounding; evaluation of the original gas cancellation is
also sensitive. The failed diagnostics reach order unity for the inferred
first stage, despite tiny changes of endpoint spectra. The second-stage
original-equation diagnostics fail too. Neither is relabelled a pass.

## Independent repair test

Instead of reconstructing a stage backwards, solve the two collocation stages
directly in the original photon coefficients and gas variable z=(p,g). Retain
the original supplied mass, collision and emission matrices as exact binary64
inputs to an 80-digit calculation:

    D = diag(M,chi),
    B = [[r L, -r L t],[-r t^T L, r t^T L t-chi]],
    F = [r J_p, -r J_E-b].

The existing canonical source is converted back to spectral components by its
explicit pivot formula at high precision; raw source qualification is unchanged.
For each Radau stage i,

    D Z_i - h sum_j a_ij B_j Z_j = D Z_0 + h sum_j a_ij F_j.

The high-precision block system is solved with float64 LU as a preconditioner
and high-precision residual refinement. Three residual evaluations suffice in
each tested case. Derivatives follow from the inverse tableau acting on the
high-precision stage differences, rather than rounded endpoints. Check
D Z'_i=B_i Z_i+F_i directly, and separately check radiation energy plus gas
plus expansion work, using the original photon energy row t^T M.

The checks use the SAME 1e-7 photon, 1e-8 absolute gas and 1e-7 energy/work
thresholds. The 1% endpoint agreement gate is also unchanged. No fit, rate,
source curve or saved forecast is adjusted.

## Results and limits

The smoke has 11 passing checks. Production checks first, middle and last of
the 256 steps for both backgrounds and both matching choices: 12 one-step
problems, 24 stage pairs of original photon/gas equations, 132 checks passing.
The maximum photon-equation diagnostic is 6.067e-42, gas absolute residual
1.336e-45 and relative energy/work diagnostic 4.640e-47. These are arithmetic
residuals for the supplied finite matrices, NOT physical accuracies or exact
continuum results.

Compared with the saved float evolution step endpoints, the maximum relative
spectral-potential difference is 1.004e-9 for baseline and 4.948e-13 for MTS;
the gas difference is 8.782e-7 and 2.039e-9 respectively. Thus the independently
solved original-variable samples support the unchanged forward-accuracy gate.

This is a sampled one-step audit starting from saved states, not a high-precision
rerun of all 256 steps or a global error certificate. The raw float derivative
checks remain failed. The final validation accepts the conditional forecast
and this explicitly bounded audit scope, not a claim that every diagnostic
in this child passed. The four older replay gas diagnostics and forty raw
physical source-moment failures also remain separate.
