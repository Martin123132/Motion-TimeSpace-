# Construct the existing fields' forced reaction

This private continuation takes the sealed 28 September position/time jet as
input. It retains both matched theories and both spatial grids. No empirical
fit, new freely chosen inertia, or subtraction of the reference residual is
introduced.

## Material covectors

At fixed material label and reference coordinate, write J=R_xi,
g=q_xi/J, W=R_t, T=q_t-Wg, alpha=R^2/(N sqrt(F)), and
beta=R^2 N sqrt(F). The independent support shape eta is reference-fixed.
The existing source coordinate moves the map by D, with D_R=D_xi/J.
For the compensated direction e and total movement U=eta+D e_source,

    delta_position g = e_field,R - g U_R,
    delta_position T = -W delta_position g,
    delta_position alpha = alpha_R U + alpha_Y X.

Thus the field momentum position density is

    delta(J alpha T) = J [(alpha U_R + delta alpha) T + alpha delta T].

The source momentum includes the differentiated basis -Dg, as well as
the exact proper-clock source momentum. Positions do not change the
velocity coefficients in this partial derivative. This defines Ppos.

The velocity direction has delta W=U, delta T=e_field-Ug and delta Y=V.
The old field force is the weak integral of
-(alpha T W + beta g) times the physical derivative of its test basis.
The source wave-force density is

    (D alpha_R + alpha D_R) T^2/2
    + alpha T W g D_R - (D beta_R - beta D_R) g^2/2.

Differentiate these expressions, the exact source-clock force and the
physical native force at fixed positions. Native velocity dependence
enters only through the live metric coefficients beta and beta_R; its
field and edge adjoints are linear in beta. Their signed coefficient
derivative is evaluated with 70-digit arithmetic, retaining the source
edge term and the radial-coefficient term. This defines Fvel.

## Forced solve and independent Euler check

Use the inherited operator L_live=M+P_metric A_old, without treating the
metric-substituted equations as a newly postulated reduced action:

    L_live b1 = Ppos - Fvel,
    additional acceleration a1 = -edot-b1,
    total acceleration variation = edot-b1,
    corrected metric-time variation = X+Vdot-A_old(b1).

Independently differentiate the actual material momentum rate, including
moving measure, basis rate, source acceleration and metric-time response.
At the original trial jet this must agree with Ppos+L_live edot. At the
corrected jet its difference from Fvel must vanish to the declared
numerical tolerance. The tiny inherited compensation residual and its
time derivative remain numerical, not exact identities.

The calculation compares material orders 48 and 64, records inverse-
weighted discrepancies and separately propagates them through the live
linear solve. Existing position-density and mixed-time precision flags
are inherited unchanged. No residual test silently promotes them.

After this response is solved and checked, its acceleration and metric-
time fields can be inserted into the support force and three current
conditions alongside the existing acceleration column. That combined
compatibility test is a subsequent calculation, not implied by solving
the old-field equations alone.
