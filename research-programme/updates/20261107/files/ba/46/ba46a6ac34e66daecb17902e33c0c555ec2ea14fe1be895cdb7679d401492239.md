## 2. First law derived from stationary variation

Let H=m_infinity be the constraint-eliminated ADM Hamiltonian and
Qbar=q/(4pi). On a differentiable stationary family z_s,

\[
 DH(z_s)-w_s D\bar Q(z_s)=0.
\]

Contract with the family tangent \(\dot z_s\), not with an arbitrary
fixed-charge perturbation:

\[
 \frac{dH}{ds}=w_s\frac{d\bar Q}{ds},\qquad
 \boxed{\frac{de}{ds}=w_s\frac{dq}{ds}}.
\]

Thus, when w is finite and nonzero, a charge turning point is also a mass
turning point. This is derived for the retained action, not imposed as a
stability diagnostic and not borrowed from a different potential.

For clarity, changing alpha adds a work term. At fixed canonical fields
the spherical constraint has the form

\[
 m'+\alpha rKm=r^2(K/2+W),\qquad
 \Xi'/\Xi=\alpha rK,\qquad\Xi(\infty)=1.
\]

Differentiate the constraint with respect to alpha and multiply by Xi:

\[
 (\Xi\partial_\alpha m)'=-\Xi rKm,\qquad
 (\partial_\alpha H)_z=-\int_0^\infty\Xi rKm\,dr.
\]

Consequently, the corresponding stationary-family identity is
\(de=w\,dq-4\pi\int\Xi rKm\,dr\,d\alpha\).
The extra term vanishes along this stage's fixed-alpha sequence. This
algebraic extension is not a newly fitted strong-coupling law.

## 3. Why the family tangent becomes a radial zero mode

Differentiate the stationary equation. With
\(L_s=D^2(H-w_s\bar Q)\),

\[
 L_s\dot z_s=w_s' D\bar Q.
\]

At \(q'(s_*)=0\), the tangent belongs to the charge-preserving space.
For every other charge-preserving variation v,

\[
 \langle v,L_{s_*}\dot z_{s_*}\rangle
 =w'_{s_*}D\bar Q[v]=0.
\]

After quotienting the constant phase symmetry, the constrained even Hessian
therefore has a null tangent. The tangent is not zero: the central-amplitude
parameter makes its central amplitude component equal to one.

The previous stage derived actual radial dynamics,

\[
 \dot e=D_q z,\quad\dot z=-H_q e,\quad
 D_q=L_dL_d^T>0,\quad
 P=L_d^TH_qL_d,\quad P y=\Omega^2y.
\]

Hence the charge-preserving family tangent gives a pulsation zero,
\(y_*=L_d^{-1}e_*\), under the regularity and domain assumptions just
stated. A negative resolved eigenvalue of P gives a growing/decaying pair
\(\pm\sqrt{-\Omega^2}\), not an imaginary oscillation frequency to be
discarded. The numerical code retains these signs explicitly.

This argument identifies a zero; by itself it does not count unstable
modes, exclude earlier unsampled crossings, or establish nonlinear fate.
We calculate the spectrum on both sides and check the next eigenvalues.

## 4. Moving compact edge: the domain is part of the derivation

The potential is not C2 at vacuum. Let d=R-r and F_s>0. The inherited edge is

\[
 h\sim C d^3,\quad C=(12F_s)^{-3/2},\quad
 \partial_s h=3C R' d^2+O(d^3).
\]

Since \(W''\sim 2F_s/d^2\), its contribution to the tangent's quadratic
form is O(d2), hence integrable. The gradient term is also integrable.
The canonical density is O(d6) and its tangent O(d5). The charge-density
quadratic term \(A\sigma^2/(r^2h^2)\) is O(d4).

The unperturbed energy density is O(d4). Sweeping a thin end interval by
deltaR contributes O(|deltaR|5) to energy and O(|deltaR|7) to charge.
There is no first- or second-order wall contribution on this family.
These local estimates justify the formal family-tangent calculation;
they are not an arbitrary-vacuum-perturbation well-posedness theorem.

To compare profiles with different radii numerically, the finite difference
is taken at the SAME physical r, with h and pi exactly zero outside each
profile's support. Differencing at equal r/R would mix in a coordinate
deformation and would not test the Hamiltonian tangent.

## 5. Independent numerical checks

`derive_turning_relation.py` checks five symbolic chain-rule/edge identities.
These are identity checks supporting this derivation, not five independent
physical predictions or a machine-certified continuum proof.

`locate_turning_point.py` fits local charge and mass curves independently,
compares polynomial orders, and tests both differential and integrated
de=w dq. `test_turning_tangent.py` compares the family tangent against the
lowest actual pulsation mode, including the initially unprojected charge
error. It does not quietly project a large charge error away.

`test_growing_mode.py` independently diagonalizes the first-order canonical
generator on the unstable side. `refine_spectra.py` keeps signed frequencies
through spatial refinement. Shared backgrounds and operators mean these
checks are complementary, not independent experiments.

At an exact zero, relative error divided by Omega2 is ill-conditioned. We
therefore report signed values and absolute mesh changes, and distinguish
mesh error from the finite-dimensional eigen-equation residual. Mode overlap
and a vanishing Rayleigh value do not by themselves control the strong
operator residual, which is separately recorded for the family tangent.
