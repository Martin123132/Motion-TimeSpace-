# Source repair and reference accounting

The soft-subtraction identities are derived in the immutable Stage163 `SOFT-SUBTRACTION-DERIVATION.md`. For signed soft energy s, d0=1+eA and d=d0-sB:

    dh=-s[(1+eC)d0-eB]/(d0*d)
    dh+s=s[e(d0(A-C)+B)-s*B*d0]/(d0*d).

With p0 the Born recoil and dp=-dh*n_h-s*n_s, the independent electron rest-energy shift is [2*p0.dp+dp.dp]/[sqrt(1+|p0+dp|^2)+sqrt(1+|p0|^2)]. The Lorentz-transformed photon and electron shifts remain independently evaluated. Subtract the Born product using LR-L0R0=(L-L0)R+L0(R-R0), then combine pole coefficients before quadrature. The source law and infrared cutoff are unchanged.

Let R0 denote the saved order4 endpoint reference, R4 the same reference with its saved-event arithmetic repaired, and R6 the order6 result evaluated by the same repaired kernel. Define a=R4-R0, q=R6-R4 and u=R6-R0. Then u=a+q. This identity also holds for the linear diagnostics C(S)=S_photon+S_gas and E(S)=temperature.S_spectral-S_photon:

    C(u)=C(a)+C(q), E(u)=E(a)+E(q).

It is a category error to call a conserved physical perturbation the entire u when a includes correcting the old numerical defect. Conversely it is incorrect to silently discard a or force its gas component to minus its photon component. Keep a explicitly and test its accounting against the saved input errors. The raw legacy1e-10 tests remain visible, even if the repaired physical difference q passes.

The source remainder also includes unchanged terms outside the real/virtual/state bank. Recover these as saved full source minus its saved exact D/V/S sum, so no control or leading term is lost. Reconstruct new full source = recovered unchanged part + repaired D/V/S. Check the full source independently. Frozen higher spectral rows are reused, not refitted.

Source reconciliation is not automatically a rebase of previously saved readouts. The actual historical solver's source-coordinate conversion and response owner must be inspected before claiming that a has been propagated. The shared warm endpoint also terminates the earlier window; a terminal-window-only diagnostic is not a global-history update. No precision gate is waived on these grounds.
