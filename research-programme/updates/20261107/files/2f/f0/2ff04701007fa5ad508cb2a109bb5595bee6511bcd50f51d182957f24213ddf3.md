## Checks that discriminate between implementations

- The reduced six-diagram amplitude agrees with the earlier high-precision
  amplitude to maximum relative error7.72e-16 over the tested energies,
  emission/crossed branches and soft shares. Removing a diagram breaks a
  Ward test.
- The unpolarized resting limit reproduces Stage85. For the moving example,
  real angular/radial refinement from6/40 to8/56 changes the event matrix
  by1.66e-13 in relative Frobenius norm and force matrices by9.79e-14.
  Virtual angular6 to8 changes its Mueller matrix by2.65e-14. These are
  convergence observations at the benchmarks, not uniform error bounds.
- Changing the subtraction profile cancels in the full matrices when its
  finite conversion is included. Omitting it changes the result by41.0% in
  the tested array norm.
- Replacing the full event Mueller correction by its intensity correction
  times the Born map gives183% relative matrix error. Polarization cannot be
  filled in by an intensity-only rescaling.
- The fixed-p chain-rule term has0.246% of the full thermal array norm for
  the moving probe. It is small but derived and retained, not fitted away.
- The total Born-plus-thermal conditional Choi matrices are positive at all
  three tested cases; their minimum eigenvalues are8.71e-8 or9.07e-8.
  This is not a global positivity theorem for a truncated perturbation series.
- Hard and second-photon readouts follow from one joint density matrix; the
  reverse channel obeys the exact observable-adjoint identity.
- Reflecting the electron's out-of-plane velocity gives the required Stokes
  and four-force reflection. Odd entries, including I-to-V, cancel for this
  symmetric pair. Conditional phase-sensitive entries are therefore not a
  claimed isotropic cosmic circular-polarization signal.
