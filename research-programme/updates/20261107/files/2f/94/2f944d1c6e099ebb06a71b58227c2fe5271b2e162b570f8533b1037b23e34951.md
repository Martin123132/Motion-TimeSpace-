# Radial accuracy of the expanded support

The new hard angles cannot simply inherit an untested radial error estimate
from event0. Before calling their combined source qualified, evaluate the
original radial18 rule against radial26 at the finer angular6 rule for all
six new rows. radial240.py and radial-configuration.json preserve the same
domain, panel boundaries, histories, components and0.002 tolerance.

This adds432 rays, scheduled after the current624-ray angular extension and
the matched virtual/state calculation as slots become available. Each ray
is restartable. Runtime minutes are scheduling controls only; they do not
change any physical input or acceptance gate.

The three-angle sum retains event0's earlier qualified18-to26 radial test
at angular4 and its independent angular refinement. The new events have
their radial comparison at angular6. Do not present this as a formal
uniform quadrature-error bound over missing electron or frequency nodes.
Keep the actual per-event discretizations and convergence tests visible.
