## Equivalent equations,not a new physical approximation

At fixed inherited background the discretized equations are linear.
Write their full BDF state,including photons,gas and expansion work,as X.
At each step their matrix and history maps are identical for both forcings:

    A_k X4_k = H_k[X4] + F4_k
    A_k X5_k = H_k[X5] + F4_k + deltaF_k

Subtracting the EQUATIONS,not two computed large solutions,gives

    A_k dX_k = H_k[dX] + deltaF_k, dX_initial=0
    X5 = X4 + dX.

The map (X4,X5) <-> (X4,dX) is constant,invertible,and retains every
photon mode,gas variable and energy/work relation. The same argument holds
for the derived number bookkeeping. It requires the current first-order
fixed-geometry linear equations; no nonlinear extension is assumed.

Compute dX independently from the SUM of the three new source components.
Separately compute each component response. The test dX=sum_j dX_j
is not tautological: it compares four separate solves on the same operator.
Keep exactly the original1e-8 relative identity gate and all other gates.

Reconstruct the full candidate only for storage. Its equation residual is
the sum of central and increment equation residuals by the displayed
linear identity. The original independent full-candidate calculation and
its failed subtraction tests remain available as a conditioning diagnostic.

## Why change the representation

This refinement is only around1e-5 of the central response. Forming its
value by subtracting two approximate central-size histories amplifies their
numerical errors when normalized to the tiny increment. Report the actual
raw-versus-direct discrepancy both absolutely and relative to the increment
AND central scale. Do not claim a rigorous roundoff theorem merely from
these ratios or change the old failed tests into passes.

The stable run must also replay all central and six individual component
histories from the raw run exactly. Its total-increment consistency is
required to pass; individual physical contraction failures remain failures.
No source quadrature or event integral is rerun for this repair.
