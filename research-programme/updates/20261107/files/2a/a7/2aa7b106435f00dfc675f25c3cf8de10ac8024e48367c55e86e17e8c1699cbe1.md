# The residual is identifiable gravitational physics

All quantities below are evaluated on the SAME current GR reference state;
no target-trajectory density difference is supplied.
Define M=3H Qg/(2k^3), S=Pi_g/k^2, P=-Dg/(2k^2),
g=3H W/(2k^2), Wq the actual native quadrature enthalpy.
Primes denote conformal time, not N.

For both candidate branches, r_alpha=Psi_n-Psi_g=M+S exactly.
The difference in metric expansion follows from z=k alpha-3eta'/k:

    dust-force: r_z=3Phi_g'/k-3H(M+S)/k.

For Poisson-spatial the derivative of the CURRENT density feeds back:

    P_n'=P_g' + Wq r_z/(2k) + g r_alpha
    r_z = [3(Phi_g'-P_g')/k-3(H+g)(M+S)/k]
          /[1+3Wq/(2k^2)].

Here P_g' means the derivative of -D/(2k^2) ALONG THE GR equations,
not the derivative along the candidate. It is calculated from the retained
native density rate, g', alpha and alpha_g'; there is no missing closure.

Writing r_z=T+Y(M+S), define adjoint gains G_alpha=lambda^T b_alpha,
G_z=lambda^T b_z. The final error integrand is

    (G_alpha+Y G_z) M + (G_alpha+Y G_z) S + G_z T.

This separates omitted current, anisotropic stress and spatial-metric time
response. The scalar's fast pressure/phase remains in the reference rates,
the metric channels and the fully coupled adjoint. The split is checked
against the independently constructed rank-two operator at every saved node.
It is not an attribution inferred from correlation of two plots.

Bounds on the individual absolute channel integrals may be much larger than
the signed sum. Their difference exposes physical cancellation instead of
using a fitted correction to enforce agreement. These terminal-response
budgets do not establish a uniform limit or a mass-gap result.
