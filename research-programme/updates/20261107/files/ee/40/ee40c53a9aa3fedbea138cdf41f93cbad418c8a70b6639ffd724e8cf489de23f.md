## 2. Status definitions and evidence scope

- **CONFIRMED USE:** saved calculations, documented research decisions, implementation plus run metadata, or explicit user report establish use. The evidence type is specified. This does not certify historical statistical correctness.
- **CONFIRMED UNUSED WITHIN SCOPE:** a specific closed likelihood/input path contains no such data or observational prior. This does not establish how equations, bounds, starting points or branch choices were motivated earlier.
- **UNKNOWN:** available records do not settle the question. A text search with no matches cannot establish non-use.

Model-development exposure and statistical independence are separate questions. A new catalogue name or reduction establishes neither.

- Do not relabel these automatically as measured local-H0 Gaussian-prior fits.

- The verified 5193/Stage48 implementation removes calibrators and has no measured-H0 likelihood.

An initial search found `NGC_4258` in archived notebook L23, code cells 1, 6 and 8 (zero-based). The inspected cells contain hard-coded galaxy radius/velocity/mass entries, **not an MCP distance/H0 likelihood**. Their distance provenance is unresolved. An object-name match alone proves neither cosmographic reuse nor independence.

**Conclusion:** direct MCP cosmography use was not demonstrated; lifetime non-use was not proved. Audit NGC 4258 separately from the other five galaxies, including peculiar-velocity and any galaxy-distance priors.

### Overlapping lens releases

[H0LiCOW XIII](https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.04869v2) includes lens-only and combined-probe analyses. Combined chains are not clean lens-only validation. Its [data page](https://shsuyu.github.io/H0LiCOW/site/h0licow_data.html) identifies products and papers.

### Lens-only cosmographic distances

For the flat background:
`chi(z)=c/H0 integral_0^z dz'/E(z')`;
`D_d=chi(z_d)/(1+z_d)`;
`D_s=chi(z_s)/(1+z_s)`;
`D_ds=[chi(z_s)-chi(z_d)]/(1+z_s)`;
`D_Delta_t=(1+z_d)D_d D_s/D_ds`.

This maps expansion to geometry conditional on compatible metric light propagation and lens inference. It does not replace mass profiles, stellar dynamics, mass-sheet degeneracy, external convergence or multi-plane modelling. Keep correlated distance likelihoods and their priors.

The current interface is validated for `0 <= z <= 3`; both lens and source must be in range or an independently validated extension is required first. A cosmology-conditioned published H0 posterior is not automatically reusable as a likelihood.

Still needed: lifetime exposure, exact sample/release, external calibrations and nuisance compatibility. These overlapping lens programmes cannot be multiplied together as independent tests.
