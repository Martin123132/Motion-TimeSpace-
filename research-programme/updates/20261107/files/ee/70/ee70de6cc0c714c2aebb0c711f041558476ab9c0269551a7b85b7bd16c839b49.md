## 1. What is held fixed

Use the isolated complex-field hypothesis, not a redefinition of the original
real MTS field or its bath. The global U(1), rotating state and positive quartic
remain the explicit additions recorded at the condensate bookmark. The charge
in this document is that global Noether charge, **not electric charge**.

In flat spacetime with signature (-,+,+,+), hbar=c=1:

    C=f(r) exp(i omega t)/sqrt(2)
    V(f)=3g f^(4/3)/4 + lambda f^4/4, f>=0, g>0, lambda>0
    f''+2f'/r=V'(f)-omega^2 f
    Q=4pi omega int r^2 f^2 dr
    E=4pi int r^2 [f'^2/2+omega^2 f^2/2+V(f)] dr.

The underlying potential is V(|sqrt(2)C|), so fractional powers of a signed
Cartesian perturbation must not be evaluated without the absolute value.
No phase-only approximation is used through the vacuum boundary.

The previously derived coexistence scales are

    v_*=(g/lambda)^(3/8), mu_*^2=2g v_*^(-2/3), n_*=mu_* v_*^2.

Set s=mu_* r, h=f/v_*, w=omega/mu_*. Then

    h''+2h'/s = W'(h)-w^2 h,
    W(h)=3h^(4/3)/8+h^4/8,
    q=4pi w int s^2 h^2 ds,
    e=4pi int s^2 [h'^2/2+w^2 h^2/2+W(h)] ds.

E=(v_*^2/mu_*)e, Q=(v_*^2/mu_*^2)q, R_physical=R/mu_*.
At the inherited, unfitted g=10^(2/3)/3, lambda=1/150 point these factors are
66.7796876397, 75, and 1.12309599896 in the original natural units. No metre,
kilogram, cosmological abundance, or particle-charge identification is supplied.
The dimensionless profile equation is independent of these scale conversions.

## 2. The fractional vacuum gives a compact cubic edge

At an outer endpoint r=R_phys write x=R_phys-r and f~c x^p. Matching f'' to
g f^(1/3) gives p-2=p/3, hence

    p=3, c=(g/6)^(3/2).

Thus f=f'=f''=0 at the edge, while f''' can jump. Extending f by zero outside
is C^2 and creates no delta-function source in the second-order field equation.
This is not a massive exponential tail: V''(0) is divergent. A finite vacuum
particle mass or a standard free-particle decay threshold cannot be read off.
This dominant balance establishes the nontrivial positive edge asymptotic,
not by itself a global existence or uniqueness theorem.

For dimensionless d=R-s, write h=12^(-3/2) u(d)^3. Exactly within h>0:

    3u u''+6u'^2-6u u'/(R-d)=6-w^2u^2+u^8/3456.

The regular branch has u(0)=0, u'(0)=1. In particular

    u=d+d^2/(5R)+(42-5w^2R^2)d^3/(270R^2)+O(d^4),
    h=12^(-3/2)d^3[1+3d/(5R)+O(d^2)].

The solver derives and retains u through d^6. The quartic force first enters
the transformed expansion at d^8; it is retained in the full interior ODE.
At h=10^-9 the omitted distance is approximately 0.003464 in dimensionless
units. The series segment is included in charge/energy quadratures. The outer
support radius R, rather than the truncated integration radius, is held fixed
when changing the endpoint floor.

## 3. Necessary frequency condition and exact balances

The parent positive factorization gives W(h)-h^2/2>=0. Therefore no nonzero
finite-energy stationary solution with w<=1 can obey the Derrick identity:

    T+3U_w=0,
    T=4pi int s^2 h'^2/2 ds,
    U_w=4pi int s^2[W(h)-w^2h^2/2] ds.

Every nontrivial body must have w>1. This is necessary, not a blanket existence
claim for every w>1. The solved branches also satisfy

    e-wq=2T/3>0,
    -2K+2T+(4/3)V_4/3+4V_4=0,
    K=wq/2.

These are independent radius and amplitude stationarity tests. At fixed charge,
an amplitude rescaling a and radius rescaling b give the exact family

    E_q(a,b)=K/(a^2 b^3)+T a^2 b+V_4/3 a^(4/3)b^3+V_4 a^4 b^3.

Both the pure-radius and pure-amplitude functions are strictly convex for
positive a,b. The numerical joint two-parameter Hessian is positive at all
seven tested stationary solutions. All 42 finite fixed-charge rescalings
also increase energy. These are specified perturbation tests, not an exhaustive
nonlinear minimization over arbitrary configurations.

## 6. Relation to prior literature

These are candidate compact Q-balls, not a newly discovered class of soliton.
[Correia and Schmidt, 2001](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0103189) supplies the
fixed-charge variational framework and stability context. We independently
differentiate the candidate energy and test its actual Hessian; their ordinary
finite-vacuum-mass hypotheses are not imported across our singular vacuum.
[Bazeia et al., 2016](https://arxiv.org/abs/1604.08871) constructs compact
Q-balls in different 1+1-dimensional potentials. It motivates careful compact
endpoint treatment, not a proof for this 3+1-dimensional fractional-quartic
potential. Source pages checked 3 October 2026.
