## Evolution equation, rather than differences of rounded samples

Let u = ln(a/a_initial), p be the 96 saved scaled photon coefficients,
r the scaled gas departure, e the original photon energy row, t the
temperature template, chi the saved constant heat scale, and W expansion work.
The retained ProbeEquation variable-step BDF2 discretizes

    M p_u = K(u) (p - t r),
    chi r_u = -e p_u - 1 - chi r,
    W_u = 1 + chi r.

Thus the differential energy identity is e p_u + chi r_u + W_u = 0.
Compute these right-hand sides directly in 80-digit arithmetic and audit in
100 digits. Do not infer r_u by subtracting rounded gas samples. For the
half/quarter histories, compare the RHS with the original variable-step BDF2
derivative using its saved predecessor pair. For the original control, keep
both the original bank RHS and the actual derivative of its piecewise-linear
stored interpolant; their difference is a discretization residual, not zero.
Also evaluate the same smooth operator on the original state to distinguish
history repair from operator replacement. No operator is overwritten.

At fixed comoving frequency x and fixed reconstruction functions, the
microscopic physical coefficients are B = chi (p, tail_heavy, tail_cold, r).
Their kinetic tangent is B_u = chi (p_u, 0, 0, r_u). This is a PARTIAL
history derivative: zero coefficient derivatives do not prove stationary
tail functions. The original tail files contain time-dependent slopes.
Differentiate their saved interpolation too and retain the resulting
additional reconstruction-function tangent explicitly.

If V(u) reconstructs the photon/gas functions from B(u), the complete rule is

    f_u = V B_u + V_u B,
    C2(theta,f) = H_theta[f,f]/2,
    (C2)_u = (partial_u H_theta)[f,f]/2 + H_theta[f,f_u].

Equivalently a matrix in a time-dependent coefficient basis absorbs V_u
inside its explicit derivative. Do not omit it or count it twice. At fixed
basis the known term is J_B B_u. Stored matrices at other temperatures may
test this contraction, but cannot be labelled the target physical source.
The direct target operator must be evaluated at its own temperature.

For t_log = ln(theta/theta_reference), d/du = -d/dt_log on the saved
theta proportional to a^-1 reference trajectory. Conformal-time derivatives
are d/deta = H_conformal d/du. This conversion is algebraic; it does not
identify the old kinetic background with a different gravity history.

The explicit source derivative must also differentiate recoil, electron
distribution, phase space, event weights, moving domain endpoints and all
