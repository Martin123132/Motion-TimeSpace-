# Two-sided source-window matching: a derived handoff law

## Why the right edge matters

Stage221's causal first step fixes the left edge of an isolated source interval.
Its endpoint result at b is valid for that scoped increment. It must not simply
be appended to the old unforced BDF2 tail without checking the right edge.

For I'=F on0<t<N h, I'=0 afterwards, a causal first step gives I_n=n h F inside.
If the old BDF2 tail is used immediately after n=N, its first value is
I_(N+1)=(N+1/3)h F and its asymptotic value is
(3 I_N-I_(N-1))/2=(N+1/2)h F: a spurious extra half-step.
The original zero-past BDF2 has a left deficit that cancels this particular
constant-source tail excess. That cancellation does not justify either boundary
treatment for general time-varying source and response.

## Physical right-hand restart

Write the fixed-basis augmented photon/gas/ledger equations U'=A(u)U+F(u).
The original source contribution is defined on[a,b]. Outside it, its source is0;
this is a diagnostic partition, not a claim that the physical universe's source stops.

At the left edge I(a)=0:
(2/h Id-A_1) I_1=F(a+)+F_1.

At the first point after b:
(2/h Id-A_(N+1)) I_(N+1)=(2/h Id+A_N) I_N.

Then resume ordinary BDF2 with the two actual post-boundary states. These are
the trapezoidal right-hand limits of the same ODE, not a fitted cutoff or a
replacement of the physical history. With a nontrivial fixed mass matrix D,
replace Id by D and A by the weak operator B in D U'=B U+F.

## Preserve the incoming evolution independently

Let H be the homogeneous continuation of the saved physical incoming BDF2 pair.
Do not reset H or pretend the unknown incoming physical contribution is zero.
Let alpha_b=3/(2h), alpha_t=2/h and Q_t=alpha_t Id-A_1.
At the left edge, compute H_1 from the original BDF2 pair, then solve J independently:
Q_t J_1=(2H_0-.5H_old)/h+(alpha_t-alpha_b)H_1+F(a+)+F_1.
Subtracting the H_1 equation yields the causal increment equation exactly.

At the right edge, compute H_(N+1) from its preserved pair and independently solve:
Q_t J_(N+1)=Q_t H_(N+1)+(alpha_t Id+A_N)(J_N-H_N).
This is a linear two-state interface law, not an assignment J=H+I.
Test independent joint, homogeneous and increment solves. Preserve their actual
previous states for subsequent BDF2 propagation and arbitrary incoming maps.
