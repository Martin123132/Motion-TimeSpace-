## Derived equations

Let omega=a*m, H=a'/a, phi the retained background heavy field, v its canonical velocity, d=delta phi and u=d'. A prime denotes conformal time in the retained Mpc, c=1 convention. Define

```text
A = a [sqrt(omega) phi + i v/sqrt(omega)] exp(i theta)
B = a [sqrt(omega) d   + i u/sqrt(omega)] exp(i theta).
```

These complex amplitudes encode two real variables each. For example d=Re[B exp(-i theta)]/(a sqrt(omega)) and u=sqrt(omega)Im[B exp(-i theta)]/a. The background amplitude A is determined from the already validated background, not fitted to the perturbation.

With e_phi=phi'-v, e_v=v'+2Hv+omega^2 phi, and g=k^2/(2omega), direct differentiation yields

```text
A' = (3H/2) exp(2i theta) A*
   + a exp(i theta)[sqrt(omega)e_phi+i e_v/sqrt(omega)]
   + i(theta'-omega)A

B' = -i g B - (k z/2)A
   + exp(2i theta)[(3H/2-i g)B* + (k z/2)A*]
   + i(theta'-omega)B.
```

Here z is computed from the CURRENT coupled metric/source state. It is not a prescribed future forcing function. This formulation avoids requiring H' or differentiating the background twice.

The carrier theta is obtained by integrating a background-only interpolation of omega/H over N=ln a. Its small interpolation mismatch is explicitly retained through i(theta'-omega)B. No exact phase integral is assumed. The measured relative carrier-rate mismatch on the saved output grid is7.18e-16 for the heavy cases; the term remains even where small.

Canonical scaled stresses and the preceding virial endpoint W follow exactly:

```text
D_s = omega/a^2 Re(A B*)
P_s = -omega/a^2 Re(A B exp(-2i theta))
Q_s = k/(2a^2)[Im(A B exp(-2i theta))+Im(A B*)]
W   = 1/a^2 Im(A B exp(-2i theta)).
```

The prior gauge conversion is then applied before averaging. Eight symbolic checks cover evolution, inverse maps and these four stress/endpoint products; the grouped metric-coupling form is also checked numerically.
