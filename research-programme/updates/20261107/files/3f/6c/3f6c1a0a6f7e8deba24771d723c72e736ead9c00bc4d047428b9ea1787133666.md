## Gas-capacity term and equal baseline/MTS treatment

For fixed photon profile, rate, emission and background heat capacity, let
G=-N/C. Then

    G_tt = -(N_tt + G*C_tt + 2*G_t*C_t)/C.

The first two numerator terms give the linear B_tt projection. The last term
comes from differentiating the response denominator. In inherited normalized
gas-response units, with derivatives taken with respect to t:

| Saved background | Linear B_tt term | Capacity term | Full G_tt |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| heavy | +0.147853433258 | -0.295848704977 | -0.147995271720 |
| cold | +0.147418030898 | -0.294977340210 | -0.147559309312 |

The signed capacity term is about twice the full answer and cancels a positive
linear term. It is not a probability or an energy fraction. Implicit response
identities and 80/100-digit response arithmetic checks pass.

Multiplication by the same saved source kernels gives worst remainder errors
of 0.333801439% for control, MTS remainder and total (rounding differences only).
Their equality follows from the common scalar gas-response factor; it is not
three independent confirmations. The earlier energy-cancellation and component
sum checks remain in the initial analysis. This experiment isolates the
collision-only slaved response, not a newly evolved full gas/photon history.
