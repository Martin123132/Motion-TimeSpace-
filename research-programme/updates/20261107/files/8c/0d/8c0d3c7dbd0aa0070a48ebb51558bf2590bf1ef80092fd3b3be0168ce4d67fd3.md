# Common kernel, not an identical projection mesh

The cubic inner and weak operators share exactly the same derived shift
kernel. The finite inner matrix and its restricted discrete weighted
adjoint are checked independently. Ghost shifts produce explicit inflow.
Multiplication by a matching window does not commute with this kernel;
the shifted-window commutator is tested, rather than set to zero.

The radiation source calculation nevertheless uses a 96-mode basis,
its saved quadrature and a compact-window spline reconstruction of the
5265-node defect. It is therefore a different representation from the
finite inner grid. The exact common-kernel identity does not prove that
these two numerical projections are bitwise adjoints or eliminate all
quadrature/reconstruction errors. The saved physical-event source gives
the matched independent comparator, and Stage131's continuum correction
gives an additional discretization comparison.

The energy eigenvalue and null modes hold in the unrestricted bulk.
Finite-domain tests include boundary work; no zero-flux claim is made.
Weighted BDF stability is not a physical radiation-energy conservation
proof. The source/response energy diagnostics remain separately recorded.
