The first validation attempt applied a regular-value Green identity tolerance
at the parent pole itself, where both Green functions and the susceptibility
are singular. Roundoff in separate near-zero denominators gave relative
identity residuals up to 1.81e-7 there; the nonsingular evaluations passed.
This is not an accepted value of the susceptibility at a pole. Version 2 tests
the identity away from that one center, tests the singular response by contour
and derivative residues, and explicitly records the single exclusion. The
original validator and results/validation-console.txt are preserved.
