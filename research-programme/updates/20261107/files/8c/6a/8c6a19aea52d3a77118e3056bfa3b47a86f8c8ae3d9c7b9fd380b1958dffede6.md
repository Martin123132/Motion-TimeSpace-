## 1. Derive the constrained energy, not just a slope criterion

For a general instantaneous scalar configuration, minimizing its positive
kinetic energy at fixed global charge gives uniform internal angular velocity
omega=Q/I, with I=int f^2 d^3x. Spatial phase gradients and amplitude velocities
cost additional nonnegative energy. Thus the profile energy to vary is

    E_Q[f]=Q^2/(2I[f])+int [|grad f|^2/2+V(f)] d^3x.

Its second variation about a rotating solution is

    delta^2 E_Q[eta]
      = int eta[-Delta+V''(f)-omega^2]eta d^3x
        +(4omega^2/I)(int f eta d^3x)^2.

The positive rank-one term is indispensable: holding omega fixed is not
holding Q fixed. The unconstrained operator has one negative eigenvalue in
each of the seven computed radial spectra; retaining that negative value as
an instability at fixed charge would be the wrong ensemble. Conversely,
dQ/domega<0 alone is not used as the stability certificate here.

After angular decomposition and u=s eta, in dimensionless units:

    L_+,l=-d^2/ds^2+l(l+1)/s^2+W''(h)-w^2,
    L_-,l=-d^2/ds^2+l(l+1)/s^2+W'(h)/h-w^2.

For the radial amplitude channel the rank-one addition is

    (4w^2/I_r) |s h><s h|, I_r=int s^2 h^2 ds.

The radial inner product here is int ds, with angular normalization factored
out consistently. W''=h^(-2/3)/6+3h^2/2 and W'/h=h^(-2/3)/2+h^2/2.

## 2. Endpoint domains and the other angular channels

The cubic edge gives L_+~ -d^2/dd^2+2/d^2 and
L_-~ -d^2/dd^2+6/d^2. Their square-integrable regular branches are respectively
u~d^2 and u~d^3; the d^-1 and d^-2 branches are excluded. These limit-point
singular endpoints select the regular energy domain. A displacement of the
compact boundary has delta f proportional to f'~d^2 and is not omitted by
the regular amplitude domain.

For a positive monotone solution, two exact identities follow from its ODE:

    L_-,0 (s h)=0,
    L_+,1 (s h')=0.

The first function is positive inside the support and the second has fixed
sign. With their regular endpoint behavior, the ground-state transformation

    int [u'^2+(u0''/u0)u^2] ds
       = int u0^2 [(u/u0)']^2 ds

has vanishing boundary term for the regular domain (extend from compact test
functions in the quadratic-form closure). This proves conditional
nonnegativity of the phase l=0 and amplitude l=1 channels. All higher channels
add a positive centrifugal term. Thus only the constrained radial amplitude
channel remains to check for these nodeless monotone profiles. The solution
and monotonicity themselves are numerically resolved, not interval-certified.

## 4. Internal stress cancels at the body scale

The full model, without phase-only truncation, gives

    rho=f'^2/2+omega^2 f^2/2+V,
    p_r=f'^2/2+omega^2 f^2/2-V,
    p_t=-f'^2/2+omega^2 f^2/2-V,
    p_r'+(2/r)(p_r-p_t)=0.

The radial ODE proves the last equation. Compact support and zero boundary
traction give the integrated static stress relation

    int (p_r+2p_t) d^3x=-(T+3U_omega)=0,
    int T_ij d^3x=0 by spherical symmetry.

There is positive interior pressure and negative interfacial stress. We do
not set either to zero locally. Their cancellation explains why a localized
stationary body has a pressureless **rest-frame monopole** when coarse-grained
on distances much larger than its size. An ensemble can still acquire pressure
from velocities, collisions and interactions; formation is a separate issue.

Within the retained Einstein-Hilbert, minimally metric-coupled parent sector,
the weak static active source is rho+p_r+2p_t. Its integrated leading mass is

    M_active=int (rho+p_r+2p_t) d^3x=E=M_inertial  (c=1).

Hence the asymptotic Newtonian monopole is Phi=-G_R E/r, at leading weak-field
order. The numerical active/inertial integral ratios agree with 1 to better
than 1e-12 at the accepted resolution. This follows from the already checked
virial balance; it is not an independent observational test. G_R is the
retained parent coupling, not a new fitted force and not a derived value of G.
This statement is conditional on the minimal Einstein regime, negligible
extra parent fields/higher-curvature terms, and negligible self-gravity in the
flat profile used as its source. Gravitational binding changes the ADM mass
at higher order and cannot be discarded for compact relativistic objects.

The concrete next test is continuation from these checked flat profiles into
the parent Einstein sector. For the minimal, asymptotically flat case set

    ds^2=-N(r)^2dt^2+A(r)^2dr^2+r^2dOmega^2,
    A^-2=1-2G_R m(r)/r,
    rho=f'^2/(2A^2)+omega^2 f^2/(2N^2)+V,
    p_r=f'^2/(2A^2)+omega^2 f^2/(2N^2)-V,
    m'=4pi r^2 rho,
    N'/N=G_R[m+4pi r^3 p_r]/[r^2(1-2G_R m/r)],
    f''+(2/r+N'/N-A'/A)f'=A^2[V'(f)-omega^2 f/N^2],
    Q=4pi omega int (A/N)r^2 f^2 dr.

Boundary data: regular center, vanishing f and normal derivative at the
compact edge, exterior matching, N(infinity)=1. Re-derive and validate the
curved endpoint and charge measure in the implementing daughter. Continue in

the previously unfitted condensate scale. Recover the flat family as G_R->0,
then test ADM/charge and stress identities, small-compactness Newtonian
behavior, and turning points. Cosmological constant, extra parent matter and

must be justified before applying it to a physical system.
