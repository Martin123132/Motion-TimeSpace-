# The condensate proposal meets expansion measurements

**The earlier MTS parent-scalar cosmology still gives its competitive fit.**
The new homogeneous rotating condensate does not improve it.

We reused the same 1,624 Pantheon+ light-curve rows and 13 DESI BAO measurements,
with full covariances, matched nuisance parameters and refitted comparators.

| Model | chi2 — lower fits better |
|---|---:|
| LCDM | 1470.56 |
| wCDM | 1465.42 |
| CPL | 1465.09 |
| Earlier parent scalar without Lambda | 1465.13 |
| Homogeneous condensate with Lambda | 1470.56, at zero condensate |
| Homogeneous condensate without Lambda | 3575.60 |

This is an observational comparison, not agreement between our own simulations.

A 1% homogeneous share costs +0.87 in chi2; 3% costs +2.91; 10% costs +12.63.
Small shares are not sharply excluded by this screening. No confidence
interval or universal rejection of condensate states is claimed.

The new state gains pressure into the past; it does not behave as dust
throughout the measured interval and cannot supply acceleration by itself.
The dilute finite-body limit IS dust-like and is indistinguishable from
other pressureless matter in this background-only test.

**Decision:** retain the older cosmological route. Do not replace it with this
homogeneous condensate. Next derive the genuine motion/bath/matter connection,
with no double-counted source, before further isolated-body refinements.

The full homogeneous action check and distance-resolution checks support the
approximation at the tested nonzero profile. This does not test formation,
growth, CMB or every possible state of the full action.
