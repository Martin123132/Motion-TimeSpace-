# Stage54 — the two fields now enter the native early-universe solver

## What moved forward

The existing Stage49 slow/heavy motion–bath modes now have their own compiled
canonical perturbation equations inside CAMB1.6.6. Their separate density and
momentum perturbations source the same metric as photons, baryons, massive and
massless neutrinos, and remaining cold matter. This is an extension of the
retained parent route, not a fresh condensate theory or a fitted suppression curve.

- The separately rebuilt LCDM control reproduces Stage53 sigma8 to the reported
  precision, and f-sigma8 within 2.23e-16 fractionally.
- The exact slow-field F=0 branch reproduces the earlier slow-fluid sigma8 and
  f-sigma8 controls within 1.34e-8 and 1.06e-8. No fluid floor or PPF is needed
  for this canonical-field calculation. Transfer-shape differences stay below
  6.58e-6 of the respective column's maximum amplitude.
- Both fields now evolve through early heavy oscillations with the full native
  standard-species hierarchy. At a=.00025 (z=3999), the remaining cold-matter
  amplitude relative to the matched all-cold control is:

| k [Mpc^-1] | Native early amplitude ratio |

| 0.03 | 1.000088521 |
| 0.1 | 0.999650150 |

These are four conditional transfer calculations at the inherited heavy mass
and F=.5, with shared primordial curvature—not observational points, a late-time
power spectrum, or a refitted heavy fraction. The scale-dependent early growth
effect persists after adding realistic radiation, baryons and neutrinos.

## Numerical issues actually fixed

Ordinary solver accuracy blurred the rapid heavy background oscillations.
The native time-to-scale mapping had errors around 1.6e-6, which contaminated
the heavy phase. Tightening the ODE tolerance alone did not fix this.
Resolving the background grid reduced the mapping error to 8.27e-11 in the
refined earlier-start run. Changing background resolution, integration tolerance
and starting time now changes the cold amplitude ratio by at most 6.10e-8;
heavy density and velocity measures change by at most 4.66e-6 and 1.43e-6,
using the stored mode-normalized denominators.

A low-order phase projection also leaked oscillatory pressure into its mean.
The original 2e-6 gate failed; its threshold was NOT relaxed. Higher projection
orders, tested across 4pi/6pi/8pi half-windows, reduce the worst discrepancy to
about 1.07e-6 of the density/velocity mode scale. This is not 1.07e-6 relative
accuracy in pressure itself: very small pressures retain appreciable fractional
corrections. The exact field stresses are saved separately from this approximate
averaged pressure law.

51 final checks and five symbolic identities pass. Independent reconstruction
of the density entering the metric agrees within 9.45e-8; direct scalar-stress
reconstruction agrees at floating-point precision. Time differences independently
reproduce the stored heavy-field velocity within 1.77e-11 of its window amplitude.
The final three numerical runs have no native warnings. Earlier failed builds,
underresolved calculations and the failed initial pressure gate are retained.
