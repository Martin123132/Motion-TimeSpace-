## The important phase step

A rotating charged field has on-shell Lagrangian **omega Q minus E**, not
minus E. Simply translating that expression would misidentify a largely
surface-energy contribution as the body's inertia.

We keep the conserved charge fixed and eliminate its cyclic phase correctly.
The phase contribution cancels, leaving **minus the full energy times proper
time**. Exact boosts of three numerical flat profiles confirm the resulting
energy, momentum, charge and mass-shell relations.

The incorrect unreduced-action mass would be only5.30%,1.27%, and0.245%
of the full energy for the flat R=8,20,80 profiles. Those are rejected
bookkeeping alternatives, not physical predictions or errors in the
previous stationary calculations.

With gravity included, the canonical constraint/boundary calculation uses
ADM energy, including binding. For our weak R_flat20 body, proper matter
energy alone is1.14% greater than ADM energy; it is not the inertial mass.

## How the force was obtained

- Derived the body's inertial mass from its boosted field/stress, then
  verified the gravitational mass through boosted ADM surface integrals.
- Matched its fixed-charge response to a slowly varying external metric.
- Expanded the retained Einstein action, derived the Poisson constraint,
  and eliminated the potential field. Keeping the field-action contribution
  removes the otherwise erroneous factor of two in the pair interaction.
- Integrated the leading interaction and force over extended spherical
  source profiles, retaining local pressure. The field equations make
  integrated spatial stress vanish in the flat reference problem.

Thus the Newtonian expression is obtained from the field model in the
declared limit, rather than added as an unrelated acceleration prescription.
