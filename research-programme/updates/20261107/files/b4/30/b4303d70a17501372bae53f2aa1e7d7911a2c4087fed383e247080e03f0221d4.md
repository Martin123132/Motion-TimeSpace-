# Algebraic current evaluation without primitive subtraction

Let v=P2(v_i), g be the field gradient, T=v-Wg, A the material momentum
rate, Phi=alpha*W*T+beta*g, and I=integral_source^R h. Define

    Delta_i = integral_Ri^R h,
    S0 = sum_i b_i v_i Delta_i,
    S1 = sum_i (d_R b_i) v_i Delta_i.

For V=P2(v_i I(R_i)), exact interpolation identities give

    V=v*I-S0,   V_R=v_R*I-S1.

Writing the previous complete current in these variables gives

    J=A*(S0-W*g*I)+Phi*S1+Q*I+E*W*h,
    Q=-alpha*W*T*v_R
      -(W*alpha_R+alpha*W_R)*T^2/2
      +(W*beta_R-beta*W_R)*g^2/2.

This follows by using E_dot+(W_R+alpha_t/alpha)*E
=T*A+beta*g*g_t-(W*alpha_R+alpha*W_R)*T^2/2
+(W*beta_R+beta*W_R)*g^2/2, then substituting
g_t=v_R-W_R*g and Phi. It changes evaluation, not the action.

For h=(R-6)^p, p=0,1,2, compute Delta_i by the divided-difference
polynomial (R-R_i)*sum_{k=0}^p (R-6)^(p-k)*(R_i-6)^k/(p+1).
Compute R-R_i from the left-node separation and the cell-node fraction
without first rounding an absolute midpoint near R=6. The same identity
evaluates I without subtracting two nearly equal primitive values.

The Eulerian local coordinate family can also be written exactly as
local(epsilon)=local-epsilon*U/(width+epsilon*Delta_U).
This avoids an unnecessary local*width/width roundtrip and is not a
linearized replacement for the finite family. The material coordinate
remains fixed. Inherited input values, widths and labels are unchanged.
