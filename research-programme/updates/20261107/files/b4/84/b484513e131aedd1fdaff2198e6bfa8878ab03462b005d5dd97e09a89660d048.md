# Source ownership and discrete boundary translation

number and injected-energy coordinates. At fixed A the replacement is linear:

    U_new(T)-U_old(T) = T(T,a) delta U(a)
                        + integral_a^T T(T,s)[G_new(s)-G_old(s)] ds.

## Put the derived interface on the original BDF2 operator

Write alpha_b=3/(2h), alpha_t=2/h and I for the isolated interval correction.
At the first step I_0=I_-1=0 and the causal startup obeys

    (alpha_t Id-A_1)I_1 = G_0+G_1.

The exactly equivalent forcing on the ordinary BDF2 left-hand operator is

    G_eff,1 = G_0+G_1+(alpha_b-alpha_t)I_1.

At source-off b the derived rule is

    (alpha_t Id-A_next)I_next=(alpha_t Id+A_b)I_b.

Therefore its equivalent ordinary BDF2 forcing is

    G_eff,next=(alpha_b-alpha_t)I_next+(alpha_t Id+A_b)I_b
               -(2 I_b-I_prev/2)/h.

These are derived discrete quadrature terms, not physical delta-function
sources, new couplings or fitted boundary impulses. Evaluating D_BDF I-A I
from the saved packets independently checks each expression. This translation
allows a correction to be added without changing the homogeneous evolution
of the reference or erasing its previous state. All augmented coordinates are
included. The protected energy ledger annihilates A; its forcing equals the
measured injection row rather than an assumed zero exchange.

## Preserve small changes under large references

For residual vector R, correction d and saved positive weights W,

    ||R+d||_W-||R||_W = [2<R,d>_W+||d||_W^2]
                        /[sqrt(||R||_W^2+2<R,d>_W+||d||_W^2)+||R||_W].

Evaluate the cross term at80digits from the saved numbers. This retains the
sign and avoids subtracting two nearly equal norms. The reverse triangle bound
is |delta norm|<=||d||. Temperature, mu and y projections remain linear;
residual norms do not. Exact-rational high/low addition retains the small
spectral contribution separately from binary64 rounding of the large total.
These are identities of the saved numerical representation, not a continuum
error theorem or an observational uncertainty model.
