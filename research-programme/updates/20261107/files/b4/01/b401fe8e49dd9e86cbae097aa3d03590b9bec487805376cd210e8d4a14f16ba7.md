# Stage90: derive a dynamical infrared tail and its error control

This is a continuation of the retained MTS radiation/gas/metric calculation,
not a replacement theory. Stage89 supplied a nonlocal leading DC source and
identified an inadmissible constant continuation of its finite-band basis.
Here the tail is solved, rather than chosen as a boundary axiom. Established
QED collision physics supplies calibration; it is not relabelled a new MTS
interaction. The parent finite-capacity gas and radiation histories drive
this reconstruction. Feedback into those parent histories is not applied.

## 1. Variables and retained order

Let N=ln(a/a_start), x=h nu/(k_B T_reference), theta=k_B T_reference/(m_e c^2),
R=d tau/dN=n_e sigma_T c/H, and tau the electron Thomson optical time.
The saved linear perturbations use delta n=chi n_0(1+n_0) Phi and
delta T_e/T_reference=chi b_e, with chi=5.75547290237e-10. Set f=Phi/x.
The hard spectral functional from Stage89 is

    beta = integral x^4 n_0(1+n_0) Phi'(x) dx / I4,
    I4=4 pi^4/15.

Use the leading soft DC rate Gamma_DC=kappa_DC/x^2,
kappa_DC=(4 alpha/(3 pi)) I4 theta^2, and the inherited sourced H/He
free-free rate Gamma_BR. Write Gamma=Gamma_DC+Gamma_BR and

    B(x,N)=(Gamma_DC beta + Gamma_BR b_e)/Gamma.

BR keeps its frequency-dependent tabulated Gaunt factors. A source-only
equilibrium would be f=B, but we do not impose it. The finite-soft Gould
target and rate are separately checked at selected frequencies. Neither
that check nor the soft tail completes the finite-recoil/virtual remainder.

## 2. Exact-velocity soft transport becomes a positive jump operator

For a gas velocity v and outgoing rest-frame photon direction, r is the
exact Doppler frequency ratio. Define the positive event measure

    dc = dP_Juttner(v) (1-v.n_in) [3/(16 pi)] (1+c_rest^2) dOmega_rest.

Stage89 derived L Phi = integral (dc/r)[Phi(rx)-Phi(x)]. Thus

    T f = integral dc [f(rx)-f(x)] + lambda_1 f,
    lambda_p = integral (dc/r)(r^p-1),
    T(x^q)=lambda_(q+1) x^q.

The slope equation is therefore

    partial_N f = R [M f -(Gamma-lambda_1)f + Gamma B-lambda_1 b_e],
    M f = integral dc [f(rx)-f(x)].

Electron velocities and shifts are retained in the quadrature; a diffusion
operator is not substituted. The numerical M uses positive interpolation
on a uniform log-frequency mesh. Its interpolation error is tested by grid
refinement. Constants are annihilated before boundary restriction. The
source-off test retains lambda_1 growth of a constant slope, rather than
manufacturing decay. Equal photon/gas temperature is an exact equilibrium.

## 3. A comparison theorem, not an instantaneous closure

On a finite interval take exterior values as data. The interior generator is

    A=R[M_in+lambda_1-Gamma].

Off-diagonal entries are nonnegative. Including the exterior legs, its row
sum is -R(Gamma-lambda_1). If d(N)=inf_x R(Gamma-lambda_1)>0,
the maximum principle gives the continuous estimate for an approximate g
with residual r=partial_N g-A g-b:

    ||f-g||(N) <= exp(-integral_0^N d) ||f-g||(0)
       + integral_0^N exp(-integral_s^N d) ||r(s)|| ds,

with exterior errors included in b or controlled by comparison barriers.
This is a conditional theorem for the specified kinetic operator, not an
assertion about arbitrary unknown collective or higher-order interactions.

The executed backward-Euler system is

    (I-h A_n) f_n=f_(n-1)+h b_n.

I-hA_n is a strictly diagonally dominant M-matrix, with positive inverse.
For g_n=B_n compute its ACTUAL discrete residual r_n and solve

    (I-h A_n) z_n=z_(n-1)+h |r_n|,  z_0=|f_0-B_0|.

Induction proves |f_n-B_n|<=z_n componentwise. This comparison is executed
alongside each history, not inferred merely from a small final residual.
It certifies the discrete driven problem; frequency, time, coefficient and
velocity refinement provide separate numerical evidence about its limit.

## 4. A regularity barrier and source cancellation

For the exact continuum soft operator, T(x^2)=lambda_3 x^2. For the
positive discrete kernel replace lambda_3 by its computed discrete moment.
Let r_B be the residual of B with B also on its exterior. If

    |r_B| <= C R (Gamma-lambda_3) x^2,  Gamma>lambda_3,

then C x^2 is a supersolution for the source-generated error. A higher-end
boundary mismatch bounded by E is controlled by E(x/x_high)^p if
Gamma>lambda_(p+1) uniformly. A lower-end mismatch bounded by E_low is
controlled by E_low(x_low/x)^q if Gamma>lambda_(1-q). Nonnegative exterior
barriers and initial error must be included. These inequalities continue
to hold for time-dependent coefficients; there is no time-independent
equilibrium assumption in the comparison argument.

The executed full-step checks admit p=8, q=12 on the primary mesh. For
heavy/cold the source-generated constants are 11017.2486/11056.0443. Hence

    |f-B| <= C x^2 + E(x/x_high)^8

in the specified driven, discrete problem with the chosen lower exterior.
Separately any unit lower-exterior error has at most (x_low/x)^12 influence.
These powers are admissible barriers, not fitted spectral exponents.
The stronger p=10 fails the positivity condition and is NOT selected.

This explains why the combined photon-number source is regular within the
leading kinetic domain. Its soft integrand is proportional to

    -kappa_total(x) [f(x)-B(x)]/x.

An O(x^2) mismatch gives an integrable O(x kappa_total) expression for
bounded or logarithmic kappa_total. The individual DC and BR terms can
have opposite logarithmic pieces; they cancel in their derived weighted
combination, not because either process conserves photon number. The
combined source in the executed histories is NONZERO.

This is a conditional regularity argument. No vacuum photon kernel is
numerically continued through the physical plasma region or claimed valid
there. The data establish the comparison on x>=1e-6. The continuum theorem
requires its hypotheses on any enlarged domain; it is not a proof of a
zero-frequency collective-plasma completion.

## 5. First dynamical correction

Putting f=B+epsilon in the evolution gives the leading forced correction

    epsilon_1 = [T B-lambda_1 b_e-(partial_N B)/R]/Gamma.

Both the logarithmic frequency dependence of BR and the temporal variation

The independent residual comparison and refinement test whether this
