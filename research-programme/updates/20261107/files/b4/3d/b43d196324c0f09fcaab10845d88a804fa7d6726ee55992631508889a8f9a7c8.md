# Energy-coordinate repair:an equivalent moment representation

The first raw-coordinate time integration is preserved in
`results/history-response/status.json`. It fails the relative energy/work
test by about0.0066 while the original photon equations and time-step
comparisons pass. The derived identity has not been relaxed.

The saved source includes independent photon energy S_E and gas S_g.
Analytically t^T S_gamma=S_E and S_g=-S_E at every event. Their floating
independent sums differ slightly. Here the large collision rate multiplies
that tiny mismatch,while the physical energy correction is itself far
smaller than the spectral shape. Directly subtracting large spectral
moments therefore requires a conservation-aware representation.

The original basis has t=(t0,t1,0,...) and chemical direction
b=(b0,0,...). Retain the number source S0 and all higher source entries.
The one redundant spectral entry is fixed by the ALREADY DERIVED identity:
  S1=(S_E-t0*S0)/t1; S_g=-S_E.
This is not a new physical term,gas equilibrium constraint,fitted
coefficient or deletion of a mode. Keep the independently computed raw
values,quantify every change,and require it below1e-10 of the source
mass-dual norm. Number production is unchanged,not set to zero.

For the evolution variables use x=(E,y0,y2,...,y95),where E=e^T y and
e=M t. Define the invertible map y=T x:
  y1=[E-sum_(j!=1)e_j*y_j]/e1.
Take the first photon test to be the energy test t^T and retain all
remaining original tests except test1. If P has those rows,then
P M T has first row (1,0,...);this row is evaluated analytically rather
than as a cancellation of floating entries. No spectral dimension is lost.

The transformed original equations are
  (P M T) x' = r(P L T)x-r(P L t)g+r P S,
  E'+chi*g'+chi*g=0.
With BDF history v,h and coefficient alpha,write
  g_free=(v_E+chi*h)/[chi*(alpha+1)],
  beta=alpha/[chi*(alpha+1)],
  g=g_free-beta*E.
Let b=-r P L t. The photon solve is then
  [alpha P M T-r P L T+b beta e0^T]x
       =P M T v+r P S+b g_free.
The exact energy coordinate carries the small physical moment directly.
Expansion work uses the identical time-discretization coefficients.

Checks retain the original raw photon/gas equations,source-change size,
all96 modes,number evolution,step refinement,and reconstructed spectral
energy versus a stated floating-roundoff allowance. The raw failed runs
remain evidence;they are not overwritten or relabeled as passing.

This fixes representation of known moment identities only. It does not
certify the quarter quadrature or the two-anchor source interpolation,
and it does not promote a new full parent history.
