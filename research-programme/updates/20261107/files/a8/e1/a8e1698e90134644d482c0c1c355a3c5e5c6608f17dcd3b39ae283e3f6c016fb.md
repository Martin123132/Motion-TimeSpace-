## What is derived and checked

We constructed the mixed position/time loading needed for the position
column, including wave transport, the source clock's underlying source
distribution, and the physical native kernel's shape response.

The moving source/native endpoints contribute signed terms that are
absent from an interior-only derivative. For a label pushforward with
load L, weight w, label Jacobian J, position motion U and background
velocity W, their coefficients are

    lower endpoint: +w'(lower) L U W/J^2,
    upper endpoint: -w'(upper) L U W/J^2.

For the retained weight both endpoints have the factor +6. These are
derivatives of an existing moving distribution, not extra matter or a
new force added to repair a test.

The regular Eulerian terms plus these endpoint terms reproduce the
independently evaluated material weak moments. All four reference/MTS
and fine/third cases pass the mixed-loading tests (66 computational
checks). A separate original-kernel four-corner calculation and source
number test pass (22 checks). The mixed source number moment vanishes
in that calculation. First-derivative interior probes also pass in all
four cases (22 checks).

The Decimal identity errors describe arithmetic agreement for the
represented loading, not physical accuracy to dozens of decimal places.
Native label-expansion mixed errors on the tested off-grid labels are
at most 2.440e-19; wave material48/64 differences are at most1.966e-19.
These are sampled numerical checks, not rigorous total error bounds.
