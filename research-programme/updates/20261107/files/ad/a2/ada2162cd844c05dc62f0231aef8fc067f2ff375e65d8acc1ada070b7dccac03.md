# Stage56 — from native transfer to a growth observable

This extends the retained Stage49/54/55 canonical motion–bath route. Stage55's
native source and library are reused read-only. The new work supplies individually
evolved k modes and computes actual density/velocity integrals; it does not add
a new action, dark fluid, free transfer multiplier, or galaxy-sector fit.

## The observable map

Let R(k) be the primordial curvature and write
delta_m(k,a)=T_m(k,a) R(k), with dimensionless primordial power
Delta_R^2=A_s(k/k_star)^(n_s-1). All k are in Mpc^-1, k_star=.05 Mpc^-1,
and h=.7 for the inherited benchmark. The physical matter density includes
remaining CDM, baryons, massive neutrinos and the heavy field:

\[
 T_m={R_c T_c+R_b T_b+R_\nu T_\nu+D_h/{\cal R}
             \over R_c+R_b+R_\nu+R_h},\qquad R_I=8\pi G a^2\rho_I.
\]

The native calculations use unit curvature, so the saved D_h already has the
corresponding transfer normalization. The exact slow field remains a separate
dark-energy contribution, not part of the nonrelativistic matter denominator.
Then

\[
 P_m={2\pi^2\over k^3}\Delta_{\cal R}^2 T_m^2,\qquad
 \sigma_{8,m}^2=\int d\ln k\,\Delta_{\cal R}^2 T_m^2 W^2(kR_8),
 \quad W(x)={3(\sin x-x\cos x)\over x^3},\quad R_8=8/h\;{\rm Mpc}.
\]

No extra h^3 factor is applied to P_m in Mpc^3. The small-x window is evaluated
by its series to avoid subtractive cancellation. Both nested-grid resolution
and interpolation/integration choices are checked, with an independently
sampled native CAMB sigma8 integral for each of the two no-heavy controls.
The heavy model never uses stock dark-slot delta_tot or sigma8 as total matter.

## Velocity is not automatically density growth

The native Newtonian velocity output is T_v=-theta/H, where H is conformal.
Define a cold+baryon tracer velocity by density-weighting the two native velocity
transfers. This assumes galaxies trace that velocity with no additional velocity
bias; it is an explicit observational mapping, not a derived galaxy-formation law.
Retain the separate total-matter and cold+baryon density transfers. Calculate

\[
 C_{v m}=\int d\ln k\,\Delta_{\cal R}^2 T_v T_m W^2,
 \quad F_8(v,m)=C_{v m}/\sigma_{8,m},
 \quad \sigma_{8,v}=\left[\int d\ln k\,\Delta_{\cal R}^2 T_v^2 W^2\right]^{1/2}.
\]

The CDM-velocity/total-density version reproduces the convention implemented by
CAMB's get_fsigma8 for general models. The cb–m and cb–cb versions and the velocity
rms are saved too. Independently compute d sigma8_m/d ln a with two time stencils.
These quantities coincide in the ideal single growing pressureless mode; in a
mixed, scale-dependent model they need not coincide. None is silently renamed
as the other merely to match a data column.

At leading linear redshift-space order, with a simple cb density bias b,
P_g^s(k,mu) is proportional to [b T_cb+mu^2 T_v,cb]^2 P_R(k). Therefore an
observational f-sigma8 compression can depend on template shape, scale cuts,
survey window, nuisance/bias treatment and which material galaxies follow.
A well-computed matter sigma8 alone does not validate that compression.

## Numerical scope

Every grid mode has its own exact early native trajectory and heavy phase
projection, rather than interpolation of Stage55's four rows. Nested logarithmic
grids are supplemented by denser acoustic sampling. Matching uses the same
continuous-background-density roots near a=.0002/.000225/.00025. Mode matching
occurs in the native scheduler and preserves all other live state coordinates.

The early calculation spans1e-5..10 Mpc^-1. A few modes beyond6.85 miss the inherited
2e-6 pressure-closure tolerance in at least one epoch. Increasing projection
degree does not remove the residual. The main curve/integral is consequently
qualified only through k=5, with the identical cut on both controls. The5..10
tail is retained as an exploratory sensitivity test, not marked as passing that
pressure gate. Finite high/low cuts and the change between them are reported;
this is not a rigorous theorem about an arbitrary unknown ultraviolet spectrum.

The calculation remains linear, including the small-scale/low-redshift portion
where comparison to measured nonlinear clustering would require more modeling.

## Real-data screen

Use the existing source-backed SDSS archive, rechecking its saved SHA256 hashes.
The chosen full-shape-only growth marginals are the two retained DR12 LRG bins
and separate DR16 LRG and QSO bins. Retain the DR12 cross-bin covariance.
The superseded high-redshift DR12 bin, duplicate LRG alias, non-Gaussian ELG grid,
and unrelated data are not mixed in. This is growth-only, not an independent
second score on top of the same surveys' full joint likelihood.

For a Gaussian full vector x=(distance variables, growth variables), the
marginal growth covariance is C_gg, the corresponding covariance submatrix.
It is not (C^-1)_gg^-1, which instead conditions the other components. The
conditional screening statistic is (prediction-data)^T C_gg^-1(prediction-data).
Use all explicit mappings and identical data/cuts and inherited parameters for
heavy, slow-field cold and LCDM controls. No parameter fit, p-value, significance
or formal evidence claim follows from this screen.

Also quantify velocity-transfer shape differences over .007.. .14 Mpc^-1
(.01.. .2 h/Mpc) after removing a single unweighted log-k normalization.
This is a diagnostic band, not the actual SDSS window or a survey reanalysis.
If the altered shape is appreciable, the next step is forward-modeling the
measured multipoles/window and nuisance parameters, not declaring a compressed
four-point score a definitive win or exclusion.
