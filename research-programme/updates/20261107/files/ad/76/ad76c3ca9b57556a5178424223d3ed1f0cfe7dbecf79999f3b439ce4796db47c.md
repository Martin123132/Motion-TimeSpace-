# Exact rational Dirac intensity: no sampled coefficient fit

Start from the Stage236 on-shell reduced six-permutation amplitude, unchanged.
The incoming electron is at rest and its mass is the unit of energy. For a
fixed incident energy E, incoming direction n0, hard outgoing direction n1
and soft direction n2, put

    f=q/E, a=1-n0.n1, b=1-n1.n2, c=1-n0.n2,
    d=1+E a-E b f, H=1-(1+E c)f, h=E H/d,
    P=d p_final=d[(1+E-E f, E(n0-f n2))] - E H(1,n1).

P is a polynomial of degree at most2. On shell P^2=d^2, so the energy
component P0/d equals the positive square-root final energy on the retained
physical emission/absorption domains. The three final electron projections
are

    p.n0=V/d, V=1+E(a-b-c)f+E^2 b c f^2,
    p.n1=d,
    p.n2=K/d, K=(1+E c)(1+E a)-E b.

The dependence on square roots in the direction/polarization frames is
constant along a ray. The final spinor square root cancels in the final-spin
sum, using sum u(p) ubar(p)=slash(p)+1. This is an algebraic replacement of
the same spin sum, not an assumption about a different matter theory.

For each of the eight physical polarization triples, the Stage236 matrices
M_j,N_j already contain the initial spinors. Set D=f V d H. The matrix before
the final spinor, T, obeys

    D T = (f d^2 H/2) M0 - (f d H P.eps0/E) N0
          +(f V H/2) M1 + (f V P.eps1/E) N1
          +(f V d^2 H/(2K)) M2 + (V d H P.eps2/(E K)) N2.

Every coefficient on the right is formed by polynomial multiplication from
the amplitude and exact kinematics. Its degree is at most6. No amplitude or
density evaluations are used to choose these coefficients.

With S=gamma0[slash(P)+d I], the spin/polarization-averaged intensity is

    I_full(f)=N(f)/(d D^2),
    N(f)=(1/4) sum_polarizations Tr[(D T)^dagger S (D T)].

N has degree at most14. Construct its coefficients by matrix products and
polynomial convolution once per ray, then evaluate with Horner's rule. The
factor1/4 is precisely the retained spin/incoming-polarization average.
S depends polynomially on f; no final-spinor square root remains in I_full.

The leading/Born control retains Stage236's separately derived four-vector
Gram form. The full-minus-leading subtraction and both soft-pole subtractions
stay at arbitrary precision. The Bose stimulation and phase-space prefactor
remain their existing exact expressions: the entire thermal density is not
claimed to be a rational function.

As an independent normalization check, D/f at zero equals1+E a. Therefore
the full density's coefficient of1/abs(q) must be

    pole_full = alpha_factor E^2 N(0) / [tree (1+E a)^3].

Compare this unfitted residue with the retained eikonal soft pole. Test both
signs, small frequencies, an independent nonspecial frequency, all three
saved temperatures, and full/control/difference components before integrating.
The retained weak response factors, independent gas recoil and grids remain
unchanged. A successful scalar density test alone does not qualify every
integrated, cancelled source component.
