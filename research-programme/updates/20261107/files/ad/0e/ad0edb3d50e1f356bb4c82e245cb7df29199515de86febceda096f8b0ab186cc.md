## What was derived

The leading signed-frequency angular amplitude follows analytically from the
existing six-diagram scattering amplitude. Its four rational tensors give an
exact fast representation, without coefficient fitting. It reproduces outgoing
photon exchange, the soft matching pole and the independent angular Gould
normalization. The tensor converges to the exact amplitude as energy decreases.
The scope is a fixed-fraction transverse low-energy limit, not a uniform
endpoint theorem or a new all-polarization gauge proof.

The exact-kinematics control keeps the original recoil, physical profiles,
frequency jumps, gas energy and domain. Its pole and logarithmic addback were
derived and split consistently. Full = control + amplitude difference to
rounding accuracy, including matching terms. No mode or gas degree was removed.
