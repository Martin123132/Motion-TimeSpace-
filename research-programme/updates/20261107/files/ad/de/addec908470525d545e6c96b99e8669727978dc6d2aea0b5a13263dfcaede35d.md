equations or numerical theory results and remain read-only.

Every numerical archive actually consumed is individually hashed in the new
reports,including the background profile and static spline guesses. The inherited
primary-reference inventory is preserved in the seal;no fresh full-paper review
or new redistribution permission is implied.

Run order in a fresh daughter: `solve_susceptibility.py`,
`predict_response.py`, `derive_susceptibility.py`, `validate_susceptibility.py`,
then `seal_susceptibility.py` after documents are final. `susceptibility.py`
contains the operator and symbolic boundary derivatives. Writers use exclusive
result directories and must not overwrite sealed evidence.
