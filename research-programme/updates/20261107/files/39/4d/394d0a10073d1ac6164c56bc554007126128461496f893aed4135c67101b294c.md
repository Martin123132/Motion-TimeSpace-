The equations remain those of Stage182:

    M p_u = S(u)(p-T g), E=e p,
    chi (g_u+g) = -1-E_u, W_u=1+chi*g.

For preceding step H and new step h, r=h/H, the quadratic derivative is

    D y = [(1+2r)/(1+r) y_new -(1+r)y_prev +r^2/(1+r)y_older]/h.

The first quarter step from the original saved pair has r=1/4 and coefficients
(6/5,-5/4,1/20)/h. It is not backward Euler and does not interpolate a missing
seed. Thereafter r=1. Polynomial tests through degree2 check this rule, and the
same coupled gas Schur solve and work accumulator as Stage182 are used.

The restart inherits its original full-step state error. An earlier restart
quantifies startup sensitivity locally; neither calculation removes every
earlier-history error. No global accuracy claim follows from this local test.

For any one of the fixed gas curvature operators C, write Q for quarter-step
evolution and H for saved half-step evolution. Let Q_2 retain every second
quarter node before interpolation to the unchanged temperature probes. Then

    C[Q]-C[H] = (C[Q_2]-C[H]) + (C[Q]-C[Q_2]).

This separates changes at common evolution nodes from changes due to a finer
interpolation grid without changing any evolved state. Keeping every fourth
quarter node gives the corresponding original-native-grid comparison.

At the fixed microscopic anchor, the already evaluated unit-gas coefficients
give U_component=K_component*C[g], component=baseline, matched increment, total.
Their relative gas-only change must coincide for nonzero K by linearity.
Reconstruct the cached coefficients from the36 events and compare the actual
source vectors, rather than treating identical scalar percentages as three
independent empirical tests. The full photon-plus-gas law is not retested here.

from three grids without smoothness and roundoff controls. The scattering-bank
