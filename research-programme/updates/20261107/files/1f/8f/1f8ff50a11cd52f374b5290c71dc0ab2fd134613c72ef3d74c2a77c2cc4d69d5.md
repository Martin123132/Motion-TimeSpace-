## 1. Fix signs from the action, not the desired residual

On a reference P2 cell, write physical gradient g, mesh speed W, temporal
field T=v-Wg, alpha=r^2/(N sqrt(F)), beta=r^2 N sqrt(F), and reference
momentum p=J alpha T. The field-gradient covector and cell Euler residual are

    Fq=-alpha*T*W-beta*g,    E=-p_dot-partial_x Fq.

For a continuous admissible field test phi, integration by parts gives

    integral E phi dx + sum_faces E_f phi_f + B[phi] - G[phi]
       = phi_coefficients^T R_field,

where E_f=(beta-alpha W^2)(g_right-g_left),
B[phi]=[Fq phi]_outer endpoints, G[phi]=(Du)^T diag(S gamma) D phi,
gamma=r^2 N sqrt(F)/J, and R=F-p_dot=f-Ka is the ACTUAL discrete Euler
residual. The sign of the Gram contribution is minus in the variation.
All material weights are included. A tensor-product field test must belong
to BOTH the spatial P2 and the material cardinal space.

## 2. The observed transport generator is not an admissible field test

The measured transport/interface functional is

    T_transport = integral E psi dx + sum_faces E_f psi_average,
    psi=W*g*I,   I(r)=integral_source^r xi/(N^2 F) dr.

Because g can jump, psi is discontinuous. It must not be substituted into
the discrete Euler equation as though it were continuous P2.

Define three distinct tests, without changing the action:

- j: cellwise P2 interpolation of the OWN left, midpoint and right traces
  of psi in each cell. This is generally discontinuous at interfaces.
- s: continuous spatial P2 test with averaged interface traces, unchanged
  midpoint/outer traces and zero at the pinned source.
- phi: interpolate the nodal values of s at the15 material cardinals, so
  phi belongs to the actual finite field/material variation space.

At fixed state the following exact split holds:

    T_transport = integral E(psi-j) dx
                + integral E(j-s) dx
                + integral E(s-phi) dx
                + sum_faces E_f(psi_average-phi_f)
                + G[phi]-B[phi]+phi^T R_field.

The first term is a smooth cell interpolation commutator; the second is a
TRACE LIFT, with explicit local form

    j-s=(psi_left-average_left)*shape_left
       +(psi_right-average_right)*shape_right.

The next two terms measure material-space projection, including its face
trace. The Gram, endpoint and actual Euler-work terms remain explicit.
This identifies how the large cell/face contributions cancel without
postulating that every remainder is zero.
