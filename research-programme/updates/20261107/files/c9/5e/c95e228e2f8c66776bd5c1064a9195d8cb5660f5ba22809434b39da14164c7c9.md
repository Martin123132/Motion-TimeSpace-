# Feedback closure without assuming DeltaD

N=ln a. Use the EXACT retained carrier/native coordinates and fixed positive
normalization U, X=U^-1 x. Their equations are linear in perturbations.
All background dependence, fast scalar phase, hierarchy streaming,
collision terms and retained background numerical defects remain in A(N).

For the GR control and a prospective Newtonian branch:

    Xg'=Ag Xg,  Xn'=An Xn,  e=Xn-Xg
    e'=An e+r,  r=(An-Ag)Xg,  e(N0)=0.

The residual depends on the GR reference, not the unknown e. The old
DeltaD formula becomes an exact closed linear inhomogeneous problem. Its
feedback is entirely in An, rather than substituted by a guessed source.

For a terminal observable ell(T)X(T), define

    lambda'=-An^T lambda, lambda(T)=ell(T).

Differentiate lambda^T e to obtain lambda^T r. Therefore

    ell(T)e(T)=lambda(N0)^T e(N0)+integral_N0^T lambda^T r dN
    |ell(T)e(T)| <= |lambda(N0)^T e(N0)|
                   +integral_N0^T |lambda^T r| dN.

The exact matching gives zero initial term. This is a reference-conditioned
finite-time bound which includes arbitrarily strong density feedback
through the propagator/adjoint. It is not a claim that the bound is small.
It can be computed without Xn. A uniform time/parameter limit still requires
control of that propagator and source in the intended asymptotic regime.
