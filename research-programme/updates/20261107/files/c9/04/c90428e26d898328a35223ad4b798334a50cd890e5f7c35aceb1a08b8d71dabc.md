# Stage58 — what can be derived about the next velocity response

## Number conservation first

In a local plane-parallel patch, let u=v/(aH) be the comoving peculiar
displacement and let n be the line of sight. The redshift position is
s=x+u_parallel*n. Conservation of the tracer count gives

\[
 \delta_g^s(\mathbf k)=\int d^3x\,e^{-i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf x}
 \{[1+\delta_g(\mathbf x)]e^{-ik_\parallel u_\parallel(\mathbf x)}-1\}.
\]

Its power involves the pair-weighted characteristic function of the velocity
difference. Density-velocity correlations and separation-dependent pair
moments sit inside that expectation. In general they cannot be replaced by a
constant dispersion multiplying a linear spectrum. The retained parent supplies
linear coherent density/velocity correlations, not a complete galaxy-pair PDF.

## An explicit, restricted construction

Keep Stage57's coherent density and velocity response, and give each distinct
tracer an additional independent residual LOS shift epsilon. Assume this
residual is independent of the coherent fields and has the same symmetric
distribution at each tracer. The distinct-pair power is then multiplied by

\[
 D(k_\parallel)=|\phi_\epsilon(k_\parallel)|^2,\qquad
 \phi_\epsilon(t)=\langle e^{-it\epsilon}\rangle.
\]

This follows from independence of the two residual shifts. It is a legitimate
stochastic construction, not a derivation that real galaxies satisfy those
assumptions. Same-particle Poisson contacts are unchanged because the two
phases cancel; the residual white-noise nuisance remains undamped in our test.

For a centered distribution with variance s2, the cumulant expansion gives

\[
 \ln D(t)=-s2\,t^2+{\kappa_4\over12}t^4+O(t^6),\qquad
 D(t)=1-s2\,t^2+O(t^4).
\]

The sign, factor and angular dependence of the first correction are derived.
The numerical variance and higher cumulants are not fixed by this kinematic
argument. Gaussian residuals give D_G=exp(-s2*t^2). Independent Laplace residuals
with the SAME one-particle variance give D_L=(1+s2*t^2/2)^(-2). Their leading
terms agree but their t^4 terms differ. The pairwise residual variance is2*s2,
not s2. Testing both distributions checks dependence on a closure choice.

The noise-free spectrum before AP/window mapping is consequently

\[
 P^s(k,\mu)=D(k\mu)\,[bT_d(k)+\mu^2T_v(k)]^2 P_{\mathcal R}(k).
\]

Here T_v=-theta_cb/H_conformal and u(k)=i*k*T_v*R/k^2. s2 in the code has
units(Mpc/h)^2, so t=k_physical*mu/h. The field and primordial parameters stay
fixed. The additional fitted variance is a tracer nuisance, not a new derived
parent coefficient and not a second copy of the coherent velocity variance.

## Leading multipoles, including the term often dropped

Let A=b^2 P_dd, B=2b P_dv and C=P_vv. Multiplying the retained Kaiser
polynomial A+B*mu^2+C*mu^4 by -s2*k^2*mu^2 gives

\[
 \Delta P_0=-s2 k^2(A/3+B/5+C/7),
\]
\[
 \Delta P_2=-s2 k^2(2A/3+4B/7+10C/21),\quad
 \Delta P_4=-s2 k^2(8B/35+24C/77),\quad
 \Delta P_6=-s2 k^2(16C/231).
\]

This k uses the units inverse to the chosen displacement. The ell6 term is
nonzero even before AP remapping. The released Stage57 window only accepts
ell0/2/4, so we retain explicit higher-multipole diagnostics rather than claiming
the response is complete at this order. The full positive characteristic
functions avoid negative high-k spectra from truncating 1-s2*k_parallel^2;
their high-order resummations remain stated distribution assumptions.

## A velocity quantity the existing fields really do fix

From the linear cb velocity spectrum P_vv, isotropy gives the unweighted
one-point displacement variance and pairwise displacement variance:

\[
 \sigma_{u,\parallel}^2={1\over6\pi^2}\int dq\,P_{vv}(q),
\]
\[
 S_{\Delta u}^2(r,\nu)={1\over3\pi^2}\int dq\,P_{vv}(q)
 [1-j_0(qr)+2j_2(qr)\mathcal L_2(\nu)],\quad
 \nu=\widehat{\mathbf r}\cdot\mathbf n.
\]

Multiply by h^2 for numerical(Mpc/h)^2, and use r_physical=r_h/h. We evaluate
these using the inherited qualified finite range1e-5..5 Mpc^-1, with resolution,
interpolation and upper-cut tests. These are actual derived linear field
statistics: S_delta_u vanishes at r=0 and depends on separation and orientation.
They are not density-weighted galaxy-pair cumulants, and substituting the
one-point number into a constant residual damping factor would double-count or
misidentify coherent motions. No simulation-derived virial coefficient is claimed.

## Scope of the experiment

Every physical branch gets the same b, white-noise and s2 parameters per cap,
the same variance bounds, two distribution hypotheses, and predeclared cuts.
Geometry/window/covariance and primordial normalization remain as in Stage57.
Numerical calibration uses independent characteristic-function draws and
nontruth-start response injections, clearly separated from real BOSS fits.

The density response still uses constant linear bias. Symmetry permits
higher-derivative density bias and nonlinear tidal/density operators, but it
does not set their coefficients. Those corrections, field-loop dynamics,
pair-weighting and complete higher-multipole propagation are not supplied by
adding one residual variance. A better fit here is a robustness diagnostic,
not proof of a fully derived nonlinear galaxy model.
