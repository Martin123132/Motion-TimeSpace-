## Main results

- Photons and electrons exchange equal and opposite four-momentum after
  finite real/virtual matching, including the electron pole-energy shift.
- The state-measure normalization passes an independent control: a scalar
  mass insertion reproduces the known Klein-Nishina mass derivative.
- Three matching profiles, three angular/radial resolutions, two high-energy
  tail cutoffs and shrinking lower cutoffs give consistent source moments.
- The real kinetic weak form has been derived and checked against direct
  nonlinear reverse-reaction weights, including moving electrons.
- The directional Planck-bath rule needed for the moving-electron loop is
  derived. It preserves the scalar thermal mass moment without discarding
  the directional interaction terms.
