# Fourth-order preservation gate at a native-null initial slice

## Straighten the null section before testing time derivatives

Let x denote the actual physical cell/source geometry and n(x) the nonzero piecewise-quadratic field constructed in DERIVATION-20261001-nonzero-kernel-family-and-preservation.md, with fixed nonzero amplitude. For every admissible geometry with the same ordered cells and source incidence,

    D_x n(x)=0.

For each endpoint/source-pinned piecewise-affine shape generator eta_i, the transported nodal field is the derivative of sampling this same physical quadratic on the moved mesh. Hence its COMPLETE native derivative also vanishes:

    V_i n = [delta_i D_x + D_x W_i] n = 0.

This uses physical midpoint interpolation and the compensated source hinge. It is not true for an arbitrary geometry-frozen operator. The beta coefficient may vary; because the kernel image is zero it does not change either identity.

Therefore the symmetric shape quadratic form has n in its radical, not merely on its zero cone:

    Q_i n=0,
    B_i(q,x,g)=q^T Q_i(x,g) q
              =(q-n(x))^T Q_i(x,g) (q-n(x)).

This is stronger than finding a single zero by cancellation. The computational test verifies both Dn and V_i n with the actual native kernel, then verifies the quadratic cross term and a finite-curve oracle.

## The first informative time gate can be fourth order

Write v(t)=q(t)-n(x(t)). At the constructed time-symmetric data, all configuration rates vanish, so v(0)=0 and v_dot(0)=0. For a sufficiently differentiable admissible curve,

    v(t)=t^2*A_normal/2 + o(t^2),
    A_normal=q_ddot(0)-Dn_section(x0)[x_ddot(0)],
    B_i(t)=t^4*A_normal^T Q_i(x0,g0) A_normal/4 + o(t^4).

If the fourth time derivative exists,

    B_i^(4)(0)=6*A_normal^T Q_i(x0,g0) A_normal.

Thus B_i, B_i_dot and even lower Taylor derivatives vanish automatically on this slice; they are not evidence of dynamical preservation. Metric dependence of Q is retained but changes only higher terms under these time-symmetric assumptions. Moving source/boundary/mesh accelerations enter the section derivative and MUST be included in A_normal. An amplitude acceleration along the same kernel section does not affect the quadratic gate.

The next physical calculation is to obtain A_normal from the coupled parent Euler/source/geometry equations, then evaluate all native quadratic gates and their withheld tests. Do not replace it by an arbitrarily chosen acceleration or invert the previously diagnosed grid-induced near-null mass.

## Validation and scope

The finite-curve oracle holds geometry fixed and sets q(t)=n+t^2*w/2, with w the archived nonzero wave. Its exact force law is B_i(t)=t^4*B_i(w)/4; halving t gives a factor16. The independent scalar native adjoint evaluates the perturbed field directly, rather than replaying the fitted quadratic formula.

This oracle validates the algebra and shows how a first-derivative check can give a false positive. Its w is NOT the coupled acceleration. The flat-wave non-invariance of the simple null family also does not prove that every state satisfying B_i=0 is impossible: a state may leave Dq=0 while still satisfying the larger quadratic constraint set.
