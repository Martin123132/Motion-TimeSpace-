# Fixed-profile refinement before propagation

This stage starts from the completed live transmission candidate, not from a new fitted profile. Every old P2 cell is bisected and its existing polynomial is evaluated at the new nodes. The source coordinate, zero initial rates, 1094 native interaction points, native coefficients, material interpolation and carrier amplitude stay unchanged. There is no curvature iteration or carrier retuning here.

Let P be this field prolongation with the identity on the source component. For nested trial spaces the same represented initial field satisfies q_f=P q_c. The native interaction is restricted to the original native support, not all newly inserted field nodes. Its potential therefore remains the same functional. Both branches recompute their own radial geometry, so discrepancies caused by radial quadrature are measured rather than hidden by freezing the metric.

For the kinetic matrix and force, compare P^T H_f P with H_c and P^T F_f with F_c. The finer acceleration a_f solves H_f a_f=F_f; it is NOT defined as P a_c. The mass-weighted norm of a_f-P a_c, common-node differences and maxima distinguish a convergent response from a mesh-local acceleration. A small Euler residual alone does not demonstrate refinement stability.

The ordinary native laws remain [beta*q_R]=A and A*(q_R^++q_R^-)/2+C=0, with C including beta_R. The source corner remains c^2*k+(d+a_source)*p=0. The declared initial-data gates stay source 1e-9, relative point 1e-8, relative jump 1e-7. The empty-native reference receives exactly the same P2 initial data and its own radial solve; its nonzero field jumps cannot be scored against a nonexistent native load. It is a numerical/action comparator, not an observational GR test.

If refinement is stable, the next propagation test is the generic off-null B_i'' calculation including the live metric's position and velocity response. No time trajectory or uniform material interval proof is inferred from the initial-data refinement.
