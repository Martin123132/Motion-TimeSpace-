# Stage56 — resolved matter spectrum meets real growth data

## What moved forward

The retained Stage49/54/55 field equations now produce a resolved linear matter
spectrum, rather than four isolated samples. Each of 412 wavenumbers has its own
exact early trajectory and phase projection. The same native handoff evolves
those modes to the present. No external power-suppression curve was fitted or
inserted. This is an extension of the existing route, not a replacement theory.

The main spectrum and integrals use the 399 modes through k=5 Mpc^-1. The
remaining short-wavelength tail is explicitly exploratory: some modes miss the
inherited pressure-closure tolerance. Both control models receive the same cut.

## Concrete physical predictions

All numbers below use the inherited illustrative heavy fraction F=.5, inherited
mass, H0=70 km/s/Mpc, and common primordial normalization and standard species.
Neither the fraction nor the primordial amplitude was fitted to these data.

| Branch | Total-matter sigma8 today | CDM-velocity / total-density F8 today |
|---|---:|---:|
| Retained slow field + heavy benchmark | .804031 | .414059 |
| Same slow field + all-cold control | .828858 | .426933 |
| Matched LCDM control | .842207 | .435110 |

The heavy field lowers sigma8 by about 3.00% relative to the same slow-field cold
control. Its linear power reaches half that control at k=.55725 Mpc^-1 today.
Thus the strong smaller-scale suppression from Stage55 persists on a resolved
grid, while its effect on the 8/h-Mpc rms amplitude is much smaller. These are
linear predictions, not nonlinear galaxy or halo predictions.

The physical matter definition includes the heavy field explicitly. The stock
CAMB dark-slot total-matter output is not substituted for it. Density rms,
velocity-density cross statistics, velocity rms, and the time derivative of
density rms are calculated separately. For example, the heavy model's
d(sigma8_m)/d(ln a)=.412483 today, rather than .414059. This distinction also
occurs in the controls and must not be hidden by calling both quantities f-sigma8.

## The first source-backed observational screen

We reused and hash-verified the saved SDSS full-shape-only growth marginals:
the two retained DR12 LRG bins plus DR16 LRG and QSO. Their redshifts are
.38, .51, .698 and 1.48. The DR12 inter-bin covariance is retained; superseded
and duplicate bins are excluded. These are actual archived observations,
not synthetic values. The archived source README permits an RSD-only marginal.

The heavy example scores .67826 higher than LCDM in this screen, not lower.
Its largest marginal residual is the QSO bin, about -1.91 marginal errors;
LCDM is also low there, by about -1.52. The heavy example is closer to the
middle two measurements but lower at the other two. This is useful direction
from data, rather than a numerical pass being mistaken for an empirical win.

The cb-velocity mappings give heavy chi2 about4.76489 and LCDM about4.08290.
Substituting d(sigma8_m)/d(ln a) would instead give4.92315 and4.07738. All
definitions are retained; none was selected after seeing which scored best.

These are conditional, fixed-parameter screening scores, not a model-selection
likelihood or significance. The survey numbers were extracted with template,
geometry and tracer assumptions. No parameters were fitted and no AIC/BIC or
exclusion probability is reported. Do not combine this H0=70 calculation with
Stage53's separately H0-profiled BAO score as if they shared fitted parameters.

## What the checks establish

- 201 validation checks pass within the declared numerical scope. Every heavy
  mode undergoes exactly one native switch; other live state coordinates remain
  unchanged. The four previous power ratios are recovered within the saved gate.
- Nested-grid and interpolation tests change the main density/velocity
  integrals by less than1.40e-6 fractionally. Cutoff sensitivity is below4.82e-6
  across both controls and the heavy branch.
- Independently sampled native CAMB controls reproduce sigma8 within2.98e-5
  fractionally and the velocity-density statistic within2.82e-5. These check
  normalization, units and integration rather than simply repeating one formula.
- Changing the matching epoch moves heavy sigma8/growth by at most1.11e-6
  fractionally; changing the projection window/order has a still smaller effect.
  These stability spreads are not complete error bounds. Stage55's exact-field
  overlap discrepancy remains part of the inherited approximation budget.
- The failed high-k pressure tests remain failed. The largest residual is
  4.50e-6 against the unchanged2e-6 mode-normalized gate. The 5..10 Mpc^-1
  exploratory tail changes heavy sigma8 by under1.8e-9 fractionally, but that is
  a sensitivity calculation, not a proof about arbitrary uncomputed UV power.
- Both covariance submatrix selection and positive definiteness are checked.
  Numerical checks do not certify the survey-compression assumptions.

An initial postprocessor stopped before producing spectra because it used
abbreviated velocity column names. It was corrected to the native
v_newtonian_cdm/v_newtonian_baryon schema; no evolution was rerun or changed.

## The next physical question

After removing one normalization, the heavy model's cb-velocity transfer has
approximately +/-1.36% shape differences from LCDM over the declared
.007.. .14 Mpc^-1 diagnostic band (about .96% log-k rms). The cold slow-field
control is much closer to a pure rescaling. This is not the actual survey window.

Next, map these computed density and velocity transfers into the measured RSD
multipoles, with sourced scale cuts, window and covariance, and the same bias
and geometry treatment for all branches. First recover the baseline/template
response and check the effect of the changed shape; then undertake any declared
abundance or cosmological parameter fit. This tests whether the convenient
compressed four-point comparison is faithful instead of assuming it is.

Keep the native fields, mass and primordial normalization unchanged for that
mapping test. A later abundance scan must be labelled as a scan, not a derivation
of F. The original galaxy programme and the prior BAO results remain intact.
