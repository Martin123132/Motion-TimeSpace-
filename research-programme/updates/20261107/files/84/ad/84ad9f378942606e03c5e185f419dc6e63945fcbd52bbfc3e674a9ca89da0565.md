# Rejected phase-normalization candidate

The original source copies here match the script hashes recorded in
../../results/coarse/status.json and ../../results/fine/status.json.
Those runs completed computationally but FAILED the optical identity.
They are retained for provenance, not accepted as validated output.

Reason: the normalized surface slope inherited a spurious complex phase
from binary64 division of a number by itself. Its imaginary part could be
larger than the physical outgoing current. The fixed implementation derives
both signed surface currents from asymptotic flux normalization and rebuilds
the small phase with a real Dirichlet fundamental solution.

Accepted reruns are under ../../results/current/. Physics coefficients,
ODE settings and test thresholds were not tuned to conceal the failure.
No files in the earlier sealed checkpoints were edited.
