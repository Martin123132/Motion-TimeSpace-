# A constructive repair of the native transmission denominator

The unchanged quadratic-core samples admit the pointwise law g_mean=-C/A at the central material label, but the first material survey finds six sampled sign-change brackets for A and large nonzero C near four localized zeros on each grid. The central cubic reconstruction costs about19 times the original wave-gradient energy. This is not a satisfactory all-material initial state. The brackets are numerical, not interval-certified roots. The analytic consequence is conditional: at a simple interior material zero of A with C nonzero, g_mean has a pole. A fixed-width Hermite reconstruction then has a nonintegrable squared-gradient contribution under a positive material measure. A finite set of finite quadrature values would miss this obstruction.

Try to repair the field, not the denominator in the code. At fixed geometry the native potential is quadratic, so its field covector is exactly linear:

    v_epsilon = v_0 + epsilon*r,
    A_epsilon = A_0 + epsilon*A_r.

Here r is an explicitly stored alternating native-value carrier, zero outside the existing transition bands. It is initial data, not a new action term, force floor, added inertia, or observational fit. The source core and outer buffers are unchanged; the small nonzero native perturbations and their energy must be retained. Support halos follow the actual D, B and sampling stencils, not a threshold on small computed forces.

If A_r is nonzero at every active point and a bound holds on the entire material interval,

    epsilon > sup_(label,j) |A_0(label,j)/A_r(label,j)|,

then A_epsilon cannot vanish there. On the quiet complement, retain the old mean and account for the residual rather than discarding it. The script first chooses epsilon=2 times the maximum ratio on17 predeclared training labels of the fine grid, then tests that SAME epsilon at65 survey labels plus the previously localized roots, on both grids. Passing these finite tests is not the uniform bound in the theorem. Continuous-material certification remains a separate requirement.

For the new values compute C_epsilon from the full native action, including beta_R, and use

    g_plus/minus = -C_epsilon/A_epsilon +/- A_epsilon/(2*beta).

Build the cubic field on fixed physical native intervals and measure its wave energy, positive norm, native energy, source jets and endpoint flux. This directly tests whether a resolved nonzero carrier can avoid the singular mean without the large energy cost of unchanged samples. The amplitude is fixed before withheld-label/grid tests; it is not selected by the resulting energy ratio. The carrier remains tied to the inherited finite native support: changing that physical support is a different test from refining the field grid.

Even successful frozen-metric transmission data must be transferred to an adequate field representation and solved with their own radial geometry, material interpolation and source acceleration before they are initial data for a coupled evolution. No old acceleration or source-compatibility flag transfers automatically. Subsequent preservation must include the general second-order metric response, not the old native-null formula.
