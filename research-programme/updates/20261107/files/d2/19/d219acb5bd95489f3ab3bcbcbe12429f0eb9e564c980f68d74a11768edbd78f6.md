# Old-field reaction completing the position column

This is the continuation formula for the B0 calculation, not a record
of a numerical k0 or C0 solve.

At fixed material label and reference coordinate, let

    delta R=U=eta+D e_source, delta J=U_xi,
    delta q=e, delta q_t=edot, delta q_tt=0,
    delta R_t=D edot_source, delta R_tt=0.

The last two acceleration variations are zero only while constructing
the forcing r0. They are solved for afterwards, not assumed physical.
With g=q_xi/J, W=R_t, T=q_t-Wg:

    delta g = e_xi/J - g U_xi/J,
    g_t = (q_t,xi-W_xi g)/J,
    delta g_t = (edot_xi-D_xi edot_source g-W_xi delta g)/J
                - g_t U_xi/J,
    delta T = edot_field-D edot_source g-W delta g,
    delta T_t = -R_tt delta g-D edot_source g_t-W delta g_t.

Evaluate the metric and its Eulerian derivatives at the moved material
point. Let P=X+A_old(edot), Y_t be the saved background-time response,
and B0 the newly constructed fixed-acceleration mixed metric response:

    delta Y_at_material_point = P + Y_R U,
    delta Y_R_at_material_point = P_R + Y_RR U,
    delta (Y_t)_at_material_point = B0 + (Y_t)_R U.

Y_RR is obtained by differentiating the existing cell polynomial, using
the appropriate one-sided trace at a face. B0's endpoint jumps are
retained. A material query must not average unrelated coincident owners.

## Differentiate the actual covectors

The old field momentum density is J alpha T, and the old source-wave
momentum is -D g times that density. Consequently its time derivative
contains both -D g times the field momentum rate and -D g_t times the
field momentum. Do not freeze this source basis while varying position.

The old field force is the weak integral of
-(alpha T W+beta g) times the physical test gradient. The source-wave
force density is

    (D alpha_R+alpha D_R) T^2/2 + alpha T W g D_R
        -(D beta_R-beta D_R) g^2/2.

Its physical measure varies by delta J and D_R varies by
-D_R U_xi/J. The source-clock force and momentum rate use the existing
exact proper-clock expressions, with delta source=e_source,
delta source_velocity=edot_source and fixed source acceleration.

The native force must vary its physical edges and field together with
beta and beta_R. Include the source contraction of both the native edge
adjoint and its radial-coefficient load; a coefficient-only variation
is insufficient here.

These operations construct

    r0 = D0(p_hat,q v + p_hat,v a - F_hat).

Using the already owned live operator, solve

    L_live k0 = -r0,
    corrected metric-time variation = B0 + A_old(k0).

The instantaneous metric variation remains P. Re-evaluate the old Euler
residual independently at delta acceleration=k0, then propagate
material quadrature and B0 interpolation sensitivities through the same
inverse. Only after that insert the response into the support force and
all three current rows to obtain C0. C1 and C2 remain unchanged.
