## 1. Rotational average fixes the angular coefficient

For isotropic ensemble weights, the addition theorem and orthogonality give

    <P_l(n_i.q) P_l(n_j.q)>_q=P_l(n_i.n_j)/(2l+1).

Primary mathematical source: NIST DLMF14.30.8–9,
https://dlmf.nist.gov/14.30.E8 and https://dlmf.nist.gov/14.30.E9,
read2026-10-26. This is an exact angular identity, not a fitted closure.

For photon radial tests phi_a(x) and signed reaction legs s_i, the projected
weak event is therefore

    B_l,ab=sum_ij s_i s_j phi_a(x_i) phi_b(x_j)
                         P_l(n_i.n_j)/(2l+1).

For l=0 this is exactly the existing isotropic jump outer product. For l=1
use vector features n. For l=2 use sqrt(3/2)(n n-I/3), whose Frobenius inner
product is P_2(n_i.n_j). This gives a direct finite-dimensional implementation
and an independent axis-integration check. It applies to unpolarized
intensity; it does not silently supply the I/E polarization coupling.

All physical directions are transformed to the bath/lab frame before taking
their Gram products. A rest-frame angle inserted beside lab-frame frequencies
would be inconsistent on a moving electron. Global rotational averaging is
legitimate under isotropic ensemble weights; it is not integration over
electron speeds, scattering angles or incoming frequencies.

## 2. Gas momentum is an actual event transfer

For the monopole append the independently computed electron energy transfer
Delta E_e/T. For the dipole append Delta p_e/T as a vector, with the SAME
rotation-average factor1/3. The photon radial test x is physical momentum
for the retained massless photon kinematics. Exact event four-momentum gives

    D_gamma[x]+Delta E_e/T=0                  (l=0)
    sum s_i x_i n_i+Delta p_e/T=0             (l=1).

Thus common-temperature and common-velocity nulls follow from event kinematics
before integration, not a final matrix projection. They apply to the paired
gas/photon block, not to radiation alone. Photon dipole damping separately is
nonzero and transfers momentum to gas. Stage231's derived enthalpy converts

The l=2 block here contains photon intensity tests only. Its padded gas row
is explicitly zero, not an inferred electron shear closure. A complete gas
shear/relaxation calculation and polarized photon lift remain separate.

## 4. Differentiate directions and gas momentum as well as energy

For a photon leg k=(E,p), n=p/|p|,

    delta[phi(E/T) Y_l(n)] = phi'(E/T) delta E/T Y_l(n)
                           +phi(E/T) D Y_l(n).delta n,
    delta n=(delta p-n(n.delta p))/|p|.

Apply this to the already derived dressed on-shell four-vector displacement.
Append its independently derived electron energy/momentum displacement. The
derivative of B_l is (delta G G^T+G delta G^T)/(2l+1).
The fixed-canonical-p correction differentiates the COMPLETE Born angular
weak measure, not just its rate. Its velocity tangent and normalized mass
