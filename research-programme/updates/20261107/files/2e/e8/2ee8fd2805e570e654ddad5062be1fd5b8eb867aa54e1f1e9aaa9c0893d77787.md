## What was derived

The troublesome time-response term has an exact primitive supplied by the
retained GR constraint. It does not need a fitted Fourier envelope or an
assumption that the adjoint varies slowly.

For chi=1 (Poisson-spatial), chi=0 (dust-force),

    L=1+3chi Wq/(2k^2)
    P=-Dg/(2k^2), M=3H Qg/(2k^3)
    J=Phi_g-chi P, T=3H J_N/(k L).

In particular Phi_g=P-M implies J=-M in the spatial branch. Its primitive
is the small current correction, not the full metric potential.

Define d=3H b_z/(k L), C=lambda^T d. The temporal response is exactly

    integral G_z T dN = [C J]_initial^final - integral C_N J dN
    C_N=lambda^T(d_N-An d).

The full candidate operator remains in the derivative. We derived d_N with
the heavy carrier's mu, scale and velocity terms and the actual quadrature
enthalpy derivative. No phase term or other species was dropped.

Equivalently w=e-dJ satisfies a transformed source equation with nonzero
initial value w(initial)=-d(initial)J(initial). Retaining that boundary term
is essential. This is an error-analysis transformation, not a replacement
physical action or a newly assumed universal gauge generator.

## A useful physical dictionary

The retained pressureless-matter equations already give

    delta_n'=-theta+3Phi', theta'+Htheta=k^2Psi.

Defining coordinate-volume particle-number contrast
delta_count=delta_n-3Phi gives delta_count'=-theta exactly, without assuming
Phi'=0. The observable map back to physical density must be preserved.

Keeping OTHER matter/radiation/field sources live, the GR constraint becomes

    (2k^2+3Rc)Phi=-Rc delta_count-Dother-3H Q/k
    Psi=Phi-Pi/k^2.

The Poisson-spatial candidate has the same volume-source denominator but
omits the explicit current/shear corrections. This denominator is derived
from source normalization and volume conversion, not fitted.

In the formal single-pressureless-source subcase, the Newtonian source
coefficient Rc/2 is recovered with fractional finite-mode coefficient
correction epsilon/(1+epsilon), epsilon=3Rc/(2k^2). The current MTS/radiation
mixture is NOT assumed to be that single-dust subcase. The dictionary is
checked against four saved GR/spatial histories with all Dother/Qother
terms retained. See `VOLUME-DICTIONARY.md` and `volume-result.json`.

This identifies a way to formulate Newtonian continuity while retaining
changing geometry in the source/readout map. It does not show that the
dust-force discrepancy was merely a gauge artifact: that candidate actually
changed the evolution. Matching physical initial conditions remains required.
