# Stage82 primary sources

- H. Arthur Weldon,1993/1994, [Cancellation of Infrared Divergences in Thermal
  QED](https://arxiv.org/html/hep-ph/9308249),
  [publication](https://doi.org/10.1016/0375-9474(94)90698-X).
  Conserved soft current, thermal emission/absorption/virtual combination,
  and separation from finite hard amplitudes. Local `sources/hep-ph-9308249.html`.
- H. Arthur Weldon,1991, [Bloch-Nordsieck cancellation of infrared divergences
  at finite temperature](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.44.3955).
  Publication/abstract checked; full derivation here uses the accessible1993
  primary text rather than pretending access to the paywalled1991 full paper.
- Gupta, Indumathi, Mathews and Ravindran,1995,
  [Bloch-Nordsieck Thermometers](https://arxiv.org/html/hep-ph/9507341).
  Covariant soft four-transfer context. Its Q>>T condition is not a general
  license to apply its hard-process approximation to our thermal photon bath.
  Local `sources/hep-ph-9507341.html`.
- Dubrovich and Zalialiutdinov,2022,
  [Finite time effects in single and double Compton scattering](https://arxiv.org/html/2203.05252v1).
  Use only the zero-width six-diagram topology. The finite-width prescription
  is NOT adopted as an MTS closure or infrared regulator.
  Local `sources/2203.05252v1.html`.
- Ravenni and Chluba,2020,
  [The double Compton process in astrophysical plasmas](https://arxiv.org/html/2005.06941v1),
  [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1088/1475-7516/2020/10/025).
  Independent squared amplitude, Gould marginal and explicit caution about
  real/virtual plasma matching. Read-only archive at
  `../scaled-spectral-evolution-20261007/sources/2005.06941v1.html`.
