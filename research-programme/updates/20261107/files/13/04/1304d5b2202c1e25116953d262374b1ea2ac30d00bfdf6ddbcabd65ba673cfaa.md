# Necessary-depth extension (after the isolated-profile test)

The first test neglected the disk potential in the wave equation. Before calculating the depth results, fix the following relaxation: allow *any* monotonically attractive spherical potential tending to zero at infinity (including spherical baryons). Test a necessary width inequality of a nodeless, localized standing wave, not a second numerical fit. Use the unchanged Stage50 mass, 52 galaxy list, and one interior radius per galaxy nearest 0.70 of the outer radius within [0.50,0.85]; ties choose larger radius. No choosing witnesses by discrepancy. Use the original signed velocity templates; positive observed radius, speed and quoted error. For a conservative box vary both measured speeds by ±2 catalogue errors (truncate at zero), disk M/L in [.3,.8], bulge in [.5,1]. Distance, inclination and gas calibration stay nominal. Separating the inner and outer extremes enlarges the nuisance box; finding compatibility is inconclusive, incompatibility is conditional on these assumptions. No probability or survey-wide significance from this box.

For both angular sectors report the minimum central escape-speed *lower bound*, how many exceed 2 or 4 catalogue flat speeds (benchmarks, not measured escape speeds), how many require more than the previously used generous depth 1e-5 c², and how many fail even the general positive enclosed-mass interval. Apply the same two-radius ratio and errors to all cases. Do not infer a best-fitting mass or count conditional failures as exclusions of the parent action.
