# Stage197 — seven-frequency interval correction propagated

## Result

The completed seven-node 8–20 incoming-frequency panel now has a saved radiation
and gas response through the final epoch, with baseline, direct MTS increment
and total treated identically. This uses the existing original scattering and
emission operators, not a new physical bank or new fits.

The source repair has a negligible final residual-shape contribution **on the
tested local interval**. Its MTS contribution is about one millionth of the
saved Stage167 partial numerical allowance. It is therefore not useful to spend
another stage merely refining this interval's response mesh.

## What was propagated

- Reused all 1260 repaired event packets and their authenticated original
  moving-history controls at five physical temperature sites. No new scattering
  integrals. Existing weights were not applied twice.
- Summed primitive source terms with compensated twofold arithmetic; propagated
  candidate-minus-original directly rather than a curvature discrepancy ratio.
- Carried all four history groups, original and candidate absolute controls,
  baseline/MTS/total and zero controls. Baseline was not reset to zero.
- Used the original 96-coefficient frequency basis, 700-point readout and the
  original 2048/4096 terminal response meshes, with both infrared joins.
- Kept the global temperature curve unchanged. Composite-quadratic local source
  reconstruction was accompanied by a frozen piecewise-linear sensitivity check.

The attributed interval is fractional epoch
`[0.8707275390625, 0.8792724609375]`. It covers 98.37704865% of the very short
five-site tested span around epoch 0.875, **not** 98% of the full history.
The small excluded edge slivers and all history outside this interval are not
assigned a physical zero correction. See `DERIVATION.md` for the Duhamel split.

## Interpretation and retained limitations

The repair is numerically stable and locally immaterial to the saved final
spectrum. This is useful forward progress: the seven-frequency source test now
has a quantified final radiation/gas consequence, rather than only a source-level
curvature percentage.

The response operator and background are held fixed. The output response is not
iterated back through the microscopic source. `saved + interval addition` is a
conditional sensitivity spectrum, not an adopted globally corrected history.
Stage167's allowance is partial and belongs to the MTS source-refinement budget;
it is not a total theory uncertainty and is not assigned to the baseline.

Continuation outside the local domain and the response matching state still
need to be derived. In particular, this tiny local contribution cannot by itself
settle the earlier, differently supported held-out-temperature discrepancy.
