# Stage102: temperature,history and the first-order transfer law

## Conditional event functional,not a new fitted scaling

Let u=ln(a/a_start),theta=k_B T_gamma/(m_e c^2). The saved parent background
uses theta(u)=theta_start exp(-u),so theta'=-theta. Its gas perturbation g
is the inherited scaled temperature departure,NOT this background theta.
Let z(u) denote the photon profiles,joined infrared tails,and g.

In the existing linearized collision model every source term is linear in z.
For an event,its right factor is
  phi(x_h)-phi(x_in)+signed*phi(x_s)+e*g.
State terms also use derivatives of phi,which remain linear. The left tests,
phase space,Bose weights,amplitudes,virtual coefficients and normalized gas
measure depend on theta and event coordinates,not on the linear departure.
Thus the complete retained finite remainder is
  R(theta;z)=K(theta;z)+D(theta;z)+V(theta;z)+S(theta;z),
  K=control-sampled_leading.
This is conditional on the inherited linearized/isotropic collision model,
not a derivation of its equilibrium or a nonlinear collision theorem.

For a differentiable physical history,
  dR/du = -theta partial_theta R(theta;z) + R(theta;z'),
  d[rR]/du = r' R + r[-theta partial_theta R + R(theta;z')],
where r=opacity/H is the SAME rate coefficient/thermal_parameter used in
the original equations. partial_theta includes ALL temperature dependences:
electron measure,velocities/kinematics,amplitudes,loops,Bose factors,state
shifts and variable internal integration boundaries. Fixed-cutoff
dimensionless-frequency conventions remain the existing ones.

For the continuous normalized dilute-gas measure at fixed dimensionless
momentum p, f_theta proportional to exp[-gamma(p)/theta],
  partial_theta log f_theta = (gamma-<gamma>)/theta^2.
Consequently a moment derivative includes both the derivative of its
integrand and this normalization score. With theta'=-theta the score is
-(gamma-<gamma>)/theta. A temperature-dependent quadrature parametrization
must also differentiate its transformed nodes/Jacobian. Merely rescaling
an old matrix by theta^2 misses these terms. Only the leading sampled
control has that exact scaling at FIXED z,as its source code shows.

The saved histories are interpolated. At interpolation knots use one-sided
derivatives;the continuous differential statement holds within smooth
pieces. The following finite identity does not require differentiability.

## Measurable finite split at the quarter history point

Let theta_q,z_q be fraction.25 and theta_m,z_m be fraction.5.
Compute both R(theta_q;z_q) and R(theta_q;z_m) with the SAME new physical
events,then reuse the matched-grid R(theta_m;z_m):
  Delta_T = R(theta_q;z_m)-R(theta_m;z_m),
  Delta_H = R(theta_q;z_q)-R(theta_q;z_m)
          = R(theta_q;z_q-z_m),
  Delta_total = Delta_T+Delta_H.
This is an exact temperature-first path split. It is not a symmetric
four-corner mixed derivative;the fourth corner is not computed here.
No exponent or free coefficient is inferred by fitting these two epochs.

## First-order dynamics with independent gas

Introduce a bookkeeping epsilon multiplying the existing remainder.
At fixed saved geometry/rates/background capacity,write
z_epsilon=z_0+epsilon delta_z+O(epsilon^2). Since
epsilon R[z_epsilon]=epsilon R[z_0]+O(epsilon^2),the first-order forcing is
evaluated on the saved history. Derivatives of the remainder acting on
delta_z enter at next order,not at this first-order insertion.

Let M be the fixed spectral mass matrix,t the temperature direction,
e=M t the photon-energy row,chi the saved gas capacity,and L(u) the original
Compton-minus-leading-emission stiffness. For delta_z=(y,g):
  M y' = r L(u)(y-t g)+r R_gamma(u),
  chi(g'+g) = -r t^T L(u)(y-t g)+r R_g(u).
The unchanged external parent driving cancels in this difference equation.
This does not include a new geometry response or a self-consistent nonlinear
tail update. Those are separate extensions of the parent evolution.

Event energy conservation yields R_energy+R_g=0 and
t^T R_gamma=R_energy,for every profile independently. Therefore
  [e^T y+chi g]' +chi g = r[t^T R_gamma+R_g]=0.
The chi*g term is expansion work;omitting it falsely suggests conservation
of photon-plus-gas perturbation energy without cosmological work.

Equivalently x'=A(u)x+f(u),with block mass diag(M,chi) and generator
  [[rL,-rLt],[-r t^T L,r t^T L t-chi]].
For zero initial correction the exact conditional solution is
  x(u)=integral U(u,v) f(v) dv,
where U is the nonautonomous propagator. This is a transfer formula,not a
license to use the midpoint source at all times.

A same-step implicit discretization (or the parent's BDF2 history rule)
must update the accumulated work with the SAME derivative coefficients.
Its discrete energy-plus-work identity then follows by contracting the
photon equation and adding the gas equation. A two-anchor secant forcing
is an explicit pilot approximation;time-step convergence alone cannot
bound the unsampled physical source between the anchors.
