## What sets the frequency scale?

The frozen, nonrelativistic, constant-kappa homogeneous equation gives

    h = C exp(-x_c/x),     x_c = sqrt(kappa/theta).

This follows by setting s=1/x in the derived diffusion-limit equation;
x_c is fixed by rates. It is established thermalization physics, not a
new adjustable MTS parameter. With varying Gaunt factors and evolving
drivers the actual prediction solves the finite-shift driven equation,
not this exponential ansatz. Primary reference: Chluba & Sunyaev,
sections2.2.1 and3.2, equations4 and31:
