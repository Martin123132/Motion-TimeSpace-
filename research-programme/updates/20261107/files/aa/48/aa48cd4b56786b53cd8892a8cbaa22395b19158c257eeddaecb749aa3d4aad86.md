## 2. Seed curvature owns the coefficients

Choose cold-comoving synchronous time, signature(-+++), and a conformal
seed spatial metric q_ij=exp(2 zeta(x)) delta_ij. Write a=beta tau,
H=1/(beta tau^2) in the leading radiation solution. Define its intrinsic
curvature and mixed tracefree Ricci tensor:

    Rhat = exp(-2zeta)[-4 laplacian(zeta)-2(grad zeta)^2],
    Rhat^i_j,TF = exp(-2zeta)[-partial^i partial_j zeta
                 + partial^i zeta partial_j zeta
                 + delta^i_j(laplacian(zeta)-(grad zeta)^2)/3].

These have units of inverse comoving length squared. Their connection is
that of q, not the flat derivative. Direct differentiation in three
dimensions verifies

    D_j Rhat^j_i,TF = partial_i Rhat/6.

Let S=ln[sqrt(gamma)/(a^3 sqrt(q))]. Conserved cold matter and radiation
give, at the retained gradient order,

    delta_c = delta_b = -S,   delta_gamma = delta_nu = -4S/3.

The exact cold determinant law is 1+delta_c=exp(-S); its S^2 contribution
is fourth gradient order and is not silently counted as part of the
two-gradient seed. Coordinate density includes the initial sqrt(q)
factor; it must not be substituted for proper density.

The Hamiltonian constraint is

    R^(3) + K^2 - K^i_j K^j_i = 16 pi G E,
    K^i_j = -H delta^i_j - dot(S) delta^i_j/3 - sigma^i_j.

At two-gradient order it becomes
Rhat/a^2 + 4H dot(S)=8H^2 delta_c. The regular solution is

    S = -tau^2 Rhat/16,  delta_c=tau^2 Rhat/16.

The coefficient1/16 follows from the constraint and radiation continuity.
An independent divergent solution is excluded by regularity.

## 3. Neutrino stress and the momentum constraint

Let Rnu be the background fraction of relativistic collisionless neutrinos
in radiation, including the relativistic limit of the massive species.
The isotropic angular fourth moment gives the shear response

    d_t(pi_nu^i_j/rho_nu) = -(8/15) sigma^i_j.

This coefficient is checked from angular integrals. Photon shear is
tight-coupling suppressed at this early surface. At this order the
tracefree Einstein equation is

    dot(sigma^i_j)+3H sigma^i_j =
       -Rhat^i_j,TF/a^2 + 8 pi G pi_nu^i_j.

Put Dnu=1+4Rnu/15. Solving these equations together gives

    gamma_ij = a^2 q_ik[delta^k_j + 2S delta^k_j/3 + B^k_j] + O(grad^4),
    B^i_j = -tau^2 Rhat^i_j,TF/(3Dnu),
    sigma^i_j = -Rhat^i_j,TF/(3 beta Dnu),
    pi_nu^i_j/rho_nu = 4 tau^2 Rhat^i_j,TF/(45Dnu).

Here sigma is expansion shear; the ADM tracefree extrinsic curvature has
the opposite sign. The synchronous q-covariant conformal velocities are

    v_gamma,i = v_b,i = -tau^3 partial_i Rhat/144,
    v_nu,i = v_gamma,i [1+8/(15Dnu)],  v_c,i=0.

Baryon inertia is a higher correction in the early radiation limit.
The momentum density at this order is
j_i=a (4/3)[rho_gamma v_gamma,i+rho_nu v_nu,i].
Using the covariant spatial Bianchi identity,

    D_j K^j_i-D_i K = (2/3) partial_i dot(S)-D_j sigma^j_i = 8 pi G j_i.

The coefficients satisfy this constraint exactly at the retained order.
Using a flat divergence instead of D_j fails even for a nonlinear
long-wavelength seed: this is a coordinate/source error, not new physics.

The construction includes the tracefree spatial geometry, rather than
promoting a scalar density ansatz alone into a gravitational solution.
Higher-gradient matter terms, quadrupole transport and vector/tensor
evolution beyond this surface still require their equations.

## 4. The existing slow and heavy scalar seeds

For either canonical mass m_I in the frozen radiation regime,

    phi_I,bg = phi_I,0[1 - m_I^2 beta^2 tau^4/20 + ...],
    phi_I,bg' = -m_I^2 beta^2 phi_I,0 tau^3/5 + ... .

The spatially varying Klein-Gordon equation contains S' phi_bg'. At
two-gradient order the regular curvature-driven response is

    delta_phi_I = -phi_I,0 m_I^2 beta^2 tau^6 Rhat/1680
                = -phi_I,0 (a m_I tau)^2 delta_c/105,
    delta_phi_I' = 6 delta_phi_I/tau.

Thus the old factor105 is recovered and the nonlinear intrinsic curvature
supplies the quadratic seed. No initial field amplitude is selected by
fitting a desired late response.

For higher frozen-mass orders write
phi_bg/phi0=sum a_n(m^2 beta^2)^n tau^(4n) and
delta_phi/(phi0 Rhat)=sum b_n(m^2 beta^2)^n tau^(4n+2). Then

    a_0=1, b_0=0,
    a_n=-a_(n-1)/[4n(4n+1)],
    b_n=[(n/2)a_n-b_(n-1)]/[(4n+2)(4n+3)].

The initial scalar is not obtained by treating an initially frozen field
as dust or by dividing by a vanishing field velocity. Field and conjugate
momentum are both supplied. The finite-mass and subdominance conditions
are independently checked at the actual early native comparison surface.

## 5. Quadratic curvature and the spatial-chart correction

Write zeta=epsilon zeta1+epsilon^2 zeta2. Then

    Rhat1 = -4 laplacian(zeta1),
    Rhat2 = -4 laplacian(zeta2)
            +8 zeta1 laplacian(zeta1)-2(grad zeta1)^2.

For zeta1=cos(kx), zeta2=0:

    delta_c,1 = (k tau)^2 cos(kx)/4,
    delta_c,2,conformal = -(k tau)^2[5+3cos(2kx)]/16.

The zero mode here is real intrinsic-curvature/background bookkeeping, not
the zero mode of a Newtonian force convolution. It cannot be deleted just
because the Stage61 force kernel vanishes for a folded pair. In particular
the proper-volume-weighted curvature average differs from the coordinate
average. Background renormalization, if chosen later, must be explicit.

The conformal seed chart is not the native synchronous scalar chart.
At first order the spatial pullback x=y+epsilon grad(L)(y), with
laplacian(L)=-3 zeta1, gives native h_initial=0 and eta_initial=-zeta1.
Any scalar perturbation transforms as

    delta_f,2,native-extension =
       delta_f,2,conformal + grad(L).grad(delta_f,1).

This fixes the scalar quadratic correction without needing a second-order
spatial generator, because the homogeneous scalar has no spatial
derivative. Our explicit coordinate extension sets that generator to
zero. Tensor/metric data must use the full pullback Jacobian, not this
scalar-only rule. There was no second-order CAMB chart implementation
already available to assume.

For the single cosine,

    delta_c,2,native-extension = (k tau)^2[1-9cos(2kx)]/16.

For k=p+q the conformal scalar seed kernel per tau^2 is
-(p^2+q^2)/4+(p.q)/8, plus the zeta2 contribution k^2 zeta2/4.
The pullback adds
-(3/8)(p.q)(p^2/q^2+q^2/p^2) to the quadratic zeta1 convolution.
This is a coordinate conversion, not an additional primordial interaction.

At linear order the same construction gives the native/standard coefficients

    h = -zeta (k tau)^2/2,
    eta = -zeta + zeta(5+4Rnu)(k tau)^2/[12(15+4Rnu)],
    theta_gamma = zeta k^4 tau^3/36,
    theta_nu/theta_gamma = (23+4Rnu)/(15+4Rnu),
    sigma_nu = -2zeta(k tau)^2/[3(15+4Rnu)].

These recover the conventional growing-mode initial data with C=-zeta/2.
[Ma and Bertschinger, section7](https://arxiv.org/html/astro-ph/9506072v1)
is the primary convention reference. Gradient-expansion/separate-universe
methods are established, not claimed as original MTS mathematics:
[anisotropic separate-universe treatment](https://arxiv.org/html/2101.05707v1).
The displayed MTS scalar response and coefficient constraints are evaluated
locally rather than importing an external theorem as a proof of this model.

## 7. Why not copy the cold growing seed to the heavy mode?

In the exact planar pressureless single-flow limit, the growing solution
has e=d^2 and w=d t for any background obeying the same linear dust
equations. This follows directly from the Stage61 equations.

In unequal multi-component fields, substituting that ansatz leaves

    residual_j = sum_i g_i d_i(d_j-d_i) + 12 lambda_j d_j^2.

Thus it is not generally a valid heavy/cold initial prescription. Even
the ordinary controls have small c/b transfer differences; the same
criterion is applied to them. A nonzero residual is an ansatz error,
not an observational rejection of MTS or GR.

In the analytic EdS cold limit, starting quadratic fields at zero at a_i
instead of selecting the growing solution leaves a fractional final
deficit a_i/a. From z=1 to z=.38 that is69%; from z=10 it is12.55%.
These are controlled-limit diagnostics, not corrections to add directly
to a BOSS score. Stage61 deliberately measured generated response only.
