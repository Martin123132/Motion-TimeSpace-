## What the completed calculation says

The completed feedback history leaves the tested MTS source-window
contribution predominantly mu/y-shaped. The two shapes jointly account for
about **99.45% of its squared nonthermal norm**. The remaining norm is
about **7.40%** of the nonthermal norm (about **0.55% when squared**).
These are different quantities, not contradictory percentages.

For the baseline, the corresponding remainder is about **13.86% in norm**
or **1.92% in squared norm**. Both receive the same projection, frequency
range, two thermal backgrounds, two matching widths and independent
readout refinements. This baseline is the saved photon/gas calculation,
not a newly fitted LCDM observational spectrum.

About **97.31% of the MTS remainder's squared norm lies below x=0.1**,
versus about **55.4% for baseline**. The amplitudes are fixed by the
full-band projection before partitioning into frequency bins; the
low-frequency region was not separately fitted.
