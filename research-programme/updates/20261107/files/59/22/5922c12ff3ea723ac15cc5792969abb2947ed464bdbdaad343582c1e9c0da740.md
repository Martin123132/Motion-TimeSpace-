# Native kernel geometry now has an explicit angular-metric response

## What moved forward

This stage rebuilt the compensated Gram kernel on physical radii and
derived its complete field, interface, native-knot and source covectors.
It then constructed a specified areal-metric extension and derived the
angular stress from that same potential. The extension has a checked
spatial radial-relabelling law. It is a concrete coupling construction,
not a pressure chosen to cancel the measured residual.

There is also an important discrimination: on the saved states, the
source-motion kernel power is already represented by the old Jacobian
term. Adding it again would double-count existing physics. The general
shape response, however, differs from that old local Jacobian stress.
The areal-metric calculation identifies where this difference belongs.

All work is private and additive. Old live equations, archived numerical
states and executed scripts are unchanged. No new nonlinear solve,
subagent, GitHub operation or background worker is left from this turn.

## 1. Actual kernel and source power

With physical field-cell edges R_e, native knots X_j, and beta=R^2 N sqrtF,

    U_G=(1/2) sum_a (S beta)_a f_a^2, f=B k.

The four-native-knot divided-difference kernel k is assembled from all
P2 interface slope/curvature atoms. Only the inherited source slope jump
is compensated; the source curvature and ordinary interfaces remain.
The derivative includes knot denominators, stencil width, moving faces,
cell slopes/curvatures, and the source hinge. No dense inverse is used.

Writing g_e=partial U_G/partial R_e at fixed beta,q, its weak kernel
stress is T(R)=-sum_(R_e<R) g_e. For fixed endpoints,

    integral T eta'=sum_e g_e eta_e.

The independent checks also verify rigid-translation invariance,
sum R_e g_e=-U_G, and q dot U_q=2U_G. A direct nodal divided-difference
implementation independently checks the atom implementation and its
geometry tangent; signed finite variations check the reverse covectors.

For the actual source-fitted velocity W=d V, let dot f_shape denote the
factor rate at fixed field coefficients, and load=S^T(f^2/2). Then

    a_j=beta_j [S^T(f dot f_shape)]_j+beta_r,j W_j load_j,
    F_b,Gram V=-sum_j a_j,
    J_shape(R)=sum_j a_j [H(R-b)-H(R-X_j)].

The weak current and its interval primitive agree independently. On all
12 retained raw probes the kernel part of a_j agrees with
-beta_j (J_t/J)_j load_j to the declared high-precision arithmetic gate.
The full material-averaged source force is approximately
-9.35098375604e-12 on both MTS grids, differing from the old source force
by at most 6.47e-27. These are code-normalized quantities, not SI claims.

For a stencil and its sampling entirely on one affine branch the equality
follows from D_R=J^(-1/2)D_x. Cross-branch rows require the complete
kernel; the saved-probe result is not promoted to a universal equality
for every possible field. The original hypothesis of a newly missing
source force is therefore not supported on these fixtures.

## 2. A derived angular response, not an extra source force

Restore the areal scalar A(t,r) in

    ds^2=-N^2 dt^2+dr^2/F+A^2 dOmega^2.

For the specified monotone-patch extension, use R_e=A(r_e) and

    C_A=A^2 N sqrt(F) A_r,
    U_G=(1/2) sum_a (S C_A)_a (D_R q)_a^2.

The coframe factor A_r makes C_A a scalar under radial relabelling:
F transforms with the square of coordinate stretch, and A_r with its
inverse. Symbolic finite and infinitesimal checks pass; omitting A_r
leaves a nonzero coordinate-stretch response. This specifies an extension
of the Gram sector, rather than proving unique parent ownership.

At A=r, holding coordinate markers, N,F and nodal q fixed, set
h_j=beta_j load_j. Direct variation gives

    delta_A U_G=sum_e g_e delta A_e
              +sum_j h_j (2 delta A_j/r_j+delta A_r,j).

The old area tension is retained, and the kernel and coframe terms supply
the additional angular response. In the inherited reduced normalization,

    2N r p_perp,G/sqrtF
       =-sum_e g_e delta_e-sum_j (2h_j/r_j)delta_j
         +sum_j h_j delta'_j.

Delta-prime is interpreted weakly, with the specified two-cell mean
derivative trace at a discrete face. The N and F variations retain the
same seed Gram density and radial pressure; the angular equation changes.

Most importantly, the spatial Ward moment becomes

    W_new[eta]=sum_e g_e eta_e+sum_j load_j beta_r,j eta_j,
    W_new-W_old=sum_e g_e eta_e+sum_j h_j eta'_j.

This is exactly the physical marker-shape derivative of this potential.
The coframe term cancels the old local -h eta' part algebraically. It
does not cancel a measured whole-system residual by definition. Field
Euler work and the temporal mixed-current derivative remain separate.

## 4. Validation, including the failed premise

- Native main: 6,066 implementation/provenance checks, 431.916 seconds.
- Independent validator attempt02: 1,913 checks, 58.468 seconds; 84
  derivative cases / 252 signed pairs, 36 weak-current replays, plus
  direct nodal/atom agreement and all 448 material recombinations.
  Largest signed-action derivative discrepancy: 1.682e-23.
- Areal lift: 2,436 checks, 69.411 seconds; 108 metric derivative cases
  / 324 signed pairs across A,N,F, 36 spatial Ward rows, 12 source-power
  comparisons and 12 finite global areal-scaling checks. Largest metric
  derivative discrepancy: 2.412e-26. Reference Gram response is zero.
- The check totals include inherited hash checks; they are not thousands
  of independent physics tests. High-precision arithmetic discrepancies
  are not observational precision or continuum-convergence estimates.

Validator attempt01 is preserved as FAILED. Its derivative checks passed,
but its final requirement that removing source-hinge compensation must
visibly change these particular seeds' energies was unjustified. It did
not. Attempt02 keeps that observation and all derivative tolerances,
then adds explicit nonzero-source-jump controls on both actual meshes.
Those independently detect the omitted hinge. Removing curvature atoms
does affect the actual seeds. The correction is recorded separately;
the failed executable and output are not rewritten.

## 5. Next dynamical step toward the GR connection

Carry THIS metric extension through time and the nonzero-shift action,
then derive its mixed current and compare it with the old current before
any coupled rerun. Include the new angular response, kernel time rate,
coframe derivatives, all moving atoms and field/source Euler terms from
one consistent functional. Do not append the already-counted source work.

A useful exact starting coordinate identity, with shift denoted v_shift
to distinguish it from beta above, is

    F_A=F A_r^2, v_A=v_shift A_r-A_t,
    ds^2=-N^2 dt^2+(dA+v_A dt)^2/F_A+A^2 dOmega^2.

The inherited horizontal-leaf construction must be expressed in these
variables and varied, not obtained merely by replacing one coefficient
in the zero-shift code. At A_t=0 and zero shift its spatial coefficient
is precisely C_A. This supplies a concrete next calculation of the time
coupling. The combined finite wave/source discretization must be treated
consistently with the Gram basis, rather than borrowing its old current.
