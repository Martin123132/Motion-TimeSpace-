# Stage93: derive a pointwise leading angular amplitude

Continue the same MTS parent and standard-matter scattering calibration.
Stage92 showed that accurate full source is not accurate small remainder.
The new object is the leading ANGULAR amplitude, before any source moment
or integration, so a compatible control can be subtracted at each event.

## 1. Explicit low-energy limit of the inherited reduced amplitude

In the initial electron rest frame write incident energy epsilon, signed
soft fraction eta=omega_s/epsilon, and fixed photon directions n0,n1,n2.
For nonzero eta<1,

    omega_h/epsilon -> 1-eta,
    r/epsilon -> r0=n0-(1-eta)n1-eta n2.

Let l_j be the signed incoming-to-electron photon transfers divided by
epsilon: l0=(1,n0), l1=-(1-eta)(1,n1), l2=-eta(1,n2).
For physical transverse rest-frame polarizations e_j^0=0, the inherited
reduced right and left external-leg factors have limits

    R_j0 = slash(l_j) slash(e_j) u0 / (2 l_j^0),
    L_j0 = ubar0 [ -2(r0.e_j) I - slash(e_j) slash(l_j) ]
           / (-2 l_j^0).

The dot r0.e_j here is the Euclidean three-vector dot product. It follows
from p'.e_j=-epsilon r0.e_j+O(epsilon^2). The final spinor tends to u0
and p'.l_j=l_j^0+O(epsilon). The complete leading amplitude is the sum
over all six photon orderings of L_last,0 slash(e_middle) R_first,0.
No coefficient is inferred by fitting finite-energy data.

The squared spin/polarization average is I0(eta,n1,n2). The controlled
statement is a fixed signed-fraction, fixed-angle limit. Uniformity as
eta->0, at ordering boundaries or after integrating an unbounded domain
does not follow from that pointwise expansion alone.

## 2. Independent checks required before subtraction

Compare the tensor itself and its intensity with the existing exact
six-diagram calculation as epsilon decreases. Check outgoing photon
exchange and the soft matching pole:

    eta^2 (1-eta) I0/[2(1+cos(theta_h)^2)]
       -> |(n0-n1) cross n2|^2.

The integrated leading cross section must reproduce the independent

The transverse derivation does not license replacing a polarization by

## 3. Four exact rational angular tensors

At fixed directions the photon fraction cancels between numerator and
denominator of each right external factor. Write a=n0-n1 and b=n1-n2, so
r0=a+eta b. The left factor is a fraction-independent term plus the scalar
(r0.e_j)/l_j^0. For photon legs0,1,2 respectively this scalar decomposes as

    a.e + eta b.e,
    b.e - (a+b).e/(1-eta),
    -b.e - a.e/eta.

Consequently the complete leading tensor has the exact representation

    T0 = Tconstant + eta Tlinear + Tsoft/eta + Thard/(1-eta).
    I0 = v^T G v,  v=(1,eta,1/eta,1/(1-eta)),
    Gij = Re <Ti,Tj>/4.

These coefficients are constructed algebraically from the external factors,
not fitted to samples. G is a real positive-semidefinite Gram matrix. The
factorization agrees with the uncompressed tensor and intensity to rounding
across positive emission and negative absorption fractions. Expensive angular
tensor construction is reused at many energy fractions without interpolation.

## 4. Exact-kinematics split, including its own matching pole

For each actual population event retain its exact hard energy, Doppler factors,
hard Bose factor, recoil/gas energy, four physical departure profiles, all96
weak tests, and the lab ordering boundary. Replace only its intensity by I0
to define a numerical control C0; the remainder intensity is Iexact-I0.
This is an identity and neither coefficient nor observable is adjusted.

The real-event density before its observable product is

    D(I)=alpha/(4 pi^2) * h |s| I /
          [E d (hBorn/E)^2 Itree] * hardBose/hardBoseBorn,
    d=1+E(1-c)-s(1-n1.n2),
    Itree=2[1+c^2+E^2(1-c)^2/(1+E(1-c))].

As s->0, h=hBorn=E/[1+E(1-c)] and the Bose ratio tends1.
Thus D(I0) ~ p0/|s| with

    p0 = alpha/(4 pi^2) E^2 * 2(1+c^2)/Itree
         * |(n0-n1) cross n2|^2.

This is not the full exact pole pF, which uses the exact Born recoil.
Subtract p0 in the control and pF-p0 in the difference, and split the finite
logarithmic addback by the same coefficients. For the spontaneous contribution
the inherited prescription is 1/2 times the angular integral of

    integral_floor^emissionUpper [D(I) J - p JBorn/s] ds
       + p log(2 emissionUpper) JBorn.

The thermal contribution uses emission plus absorption minus2p JBorn/s,
with the same bath occupation, exact endpoints and inherited tail panels.
Linearity then gives Cfull=Ccontrol+Cdifference, including every matching
piece, not merely its numerically integrated non-singular density. Four
actual population nodes verify this to4.71e-15 relative, and the full replay
agrees with the old implementation to1.34e-16 relative.

The gas partner and photon-energy test cancel at each exact event in both
pieces. A control with a different recoil or a changed frequency jump would
not have this property automatically. This split is a numerical identity,
not an assertion that the leading amplitude alone is new complete physics.

## 5. Physical remainder and accountable addback

Let Q denote the actual finite population/soft quadrature and G the existing
leading Gould operator on the same window, physical profiles and frequency
floor. The corrected source estimator is

    Cest = Gaccurate + R,
    R = Q(Cdifference) + Q(Cvirtual+Cstate)
        + [Q(Ccontrol)-Gsampled].

The bracket is ESSENTIAL: exact recoil and a moving thermal population mean
the control is not already the stationary leading Gould kernel. Omitting it
would discard physical motion and recoil effects rather than derive them.
Here Gsampled and Gaccurate retain the Stage92 forward/reverse window
selector 1/2 window(seed)+1/2 window(hard). Their distinct measures are
recorded; subtract/addback is a control estimator, not equality of unmatched
finite quadratures. Gaccurate uses the independent verified high-order rule.

Therefore amplitude-difference convergence alone cannot establish convergence
of R. Measure each component and R itself in the original mass-dual norm;
then solve the existing coupled photon/gas response and refine that response.
The fixed-angular radial ladder changes only radial resolution; later angular
and outer refinements are separate requirements. Stable energy or a small
absolute source cannot waive the declared1% relative remainder criterion.
