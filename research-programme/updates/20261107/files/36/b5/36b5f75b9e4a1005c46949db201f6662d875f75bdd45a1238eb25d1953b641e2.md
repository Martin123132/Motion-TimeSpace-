# Actual wave-force flux integration

This implements the previously derived integration-by-parts identity on the
stored reference background, rather than assuming that it improves accuracy.
The native force and its completed precision repair are not recomputed.

At each material label, partition the physical interval at every mapped
material face and every polynomial cut of both the background metric and the
position response. On each open segment evaluate the same represented flux
Phi and its material variation H. The field component is

    F = -sum integral Phi N_R dR
      = sum integral Phi_R N dR + sum [Phi] N + inner - outer.
    delta F_regular = -sum integral H N_R dR
      = sum integral H_R N dR + sum [H] N + inner - outer.
    delta F_cut = sum_radial_cuts [Phi] (-U/J) N_xi.

The cut velocities are those of fixed physical polynomial cuts. Material faces
are fixed in the reference coordinate. Outer terms remain present; the pinned
source test is zero. No force component for the source coordinate is inferred
from this field-only calculation.

Spatial differentiation must differentiate the represented function, not a
rounded surrogate. For metric value polynomial Y and separately stored RHS D,
the inherited material variation is P + D U. Consequently its actual spatial
derivative is P' + D' U + D U_R. The volume implementation differentiates the
stored primitives for Y' and P', and the stored RHS for D'. This retains the
old variation; it does not silently identify a rounded primitive derivative
with a separately rounded RHS. Their discrepancy is measured.

Likewise, H_R is obtained with a mixed forward jet with coordinates R and the
material variation. This directly differentiates H. On a cell,

    g = q_xi/J,             g_R = q_xixi/J^2,
    delta g = e_xi/J-g U_R,
    (delta g)_R = e_xixi/J^2-g_R U_R,

because U_R is constant inside the piecewise affine material cell. This is
equivalent to delta(Phi_R)+U_R Phi_R when derivatives commute exactly.

Traces use the SAME endpoint coordinate with explicitly chosen left/right
polynomial cells, never a nextafter displacement. All background and response
cuts are included. Coincident material/radial cuts are reported and excluded
from a two-sided moving-cut claim until resolved, rather than silently choosing
an ambiguous derivative. Label-rule convergence remains a separate test.

Validation first compares weak and flux forms at identical labels and rules,
checks the analytic H against an independent complex-step flux derivative,
and records the effects of omitting jumps, endpoints, and cut terms separately.
Then compare successive label/radial rules under the existing inverse-norm
2e-11 gate and the baseline-force 2e-15 gate. A completed diagnostic is not
acceptance of a reaction replacement. No physical coefficients or tolerances
change. All results remain private and separate from the original workbench.
