# Stage108 — the saved correction changes shape, not just temperature

## Physical result

The Stage107 window correction is **overwhelmingly spectral distortion**,
not an ordinary temperature shift. In the explicitly energy-matched
convention, the temperature piece has only about **4.28e-10 of the
original spectral norm**. Removing it leaves essentially the whole
nonthermal shape at the saved numerical precision.

This is the already calculated incremental window contribution at
a=1e-5, T_star=272,550 K, not the entire MTS photon spectrum or today's
observed sky. No new source integral, history integration or fit was run.

The inherited emitting system can change photon number.

## What was derived

From the original occupation definition, delta n=chi n_P(1+n_P)Phi,
the exact linear temperature tangent is x n_P(1+n_P). In coefficient form,

    eta = E/(t^T M t)
    y_temperature = t eta
    y_distortion = y-y_temperature
    delta T/T = chi eta.

E is the independent saved energy. The finite-band capacity is
3.9999999999999787, consistent with the full-Planck value4. The projection
reconstructs all96 modes; it does not remove kinetic degrees of freedom.
The split is invariant to adding a pure-temperature perturbation.
An independent number-matched convention gives the same nonthermal
equivalence class; it is not misidentified as the same temperature.

The physical occupation and specific-intensity conversions are now saved.
No scaled coefficient is mislabeled as a directly measured intensity.
See `DERIVATION.md` and the authoritative stable readout packet
`results/stable-readout/status.json`.
