# Stage95: exact nine-tensor source factorization

## Exact rearrangement, not a low-energy series

In the initial electron rest frame use the same physical transverse photon
polarizations as the inherited reduced amplitude, with e_j^0=0. Set signed
transfers k_j=f_j n_j where n_j=(1,direction_j) and f=(E,-h,-s). Let p'=(E',r),
r=sum_j f_j direction_j, and d_j=E'-r.direction_j. All energies are in the
existing electron-mass units. The original factors reduce EXACTLY to

    R_j = (1/2) slash(n_j) slash(e_j) u(p),
    L_j = ubar(p') [ (r.e_j)/f_j I + (1/2) slash(e_j) slash(n_j) ] / d_j.

The spatial dot is Euclidean. The signs follow from p'.e_j=-r.e_j and the
original left denominator -2p'.k_j. Initial p.e_j=0 cancels the right transfer
energy. No expansion in E, velocity, s/E or recoil was made.

Transversality gives direction_j.e_j=0, hence

    (r.e_j)/f_j = sum_(m!=j) (f_m/f_j) direction_m.e_j.

For each last photon j, sum the two orderings of the other photons into one
matrix-valued angular tensor A_j0 and two A_jm, m!=j. There are nine such
tensors, independent of all photon energies. With photon-polarization and
initial-spin indices retained, the full amplitude is

    T = ubar(p') sum_j [ A_j0/d_j + sum_(m!=j) f_m A_jm/(f_j d_j) ].

This exactly reproduces the six orderings. The remaining spin sum uses
sum_s u_s(p') ubar_s(p') = slash(p')+I for the inherited unit-mass spinors.
For real coefficients c_q define five angular Gram arrays

    G^I_qr = Re sum A_q^dagger gamma0 A_r /4,
    G^mu_qr = Re sum A_q^dagger gamma0 gamma^mu A_r /4.

Then the exact unpolarized intensity is

    I = c^T [ G^I + E' G^0 - sum_a r_a G^a ] c.

The combined spin-completeness form is nonnegative for physical events;
individual G^mu need not be positive. Exact hard/recoil kinematics and both
emission and crossed absorption are retained. This factorization is for the
same transverse scalar source, not a new proof for arbitrary gauge-replaced
polarizations or a completed polarized collision operator.

## Why the incoming grid needs a different treatment

The original 96-function basis is generated in z=(x-p)/(x+p), p=0.1.
Multiplying any basis function by (1-z) produces a polynomial of degree at
most 95: this follows inductively from its compact-coordinate recurrence and
the two initial constant/linear-in-x functions. The inherited 14, 21 and 28
incident-frequency nodes cannot even reproduce a full-rank 96-function
incident Gram matrix: B^T W B has rank at most the number of nodes. This is
not a rank theorem for the whole collision operator, whose outgoing integral
also supplies information, but it identifies a concrete undersampling risk.

For a frequency integral, the exact substitution is

    x = p(1+z)/(1-z),   dx = 2p dz/(1-z)^2.

Compact-coordinate Gauss rules therefore resolve the known polynomial part
more directly than raising a very low order on seven broad x intervals.
The Bose weight, recoil maps and composed source functions are not polynomials;
96 nodes alone do NOT guarantee exact integration. Orders 96/128/160 are
tested in pilot_compact_control on one actual antipodal electron pair, keeping
all hard angles, 96 tests, four profiles, recoil, gas and the frequency window.
This pilot diagnoses a numerical method, not a replacement thermal population.

## Separate resolutions without changing the physical source

Let J = D+V+S and K = C0-Gsampled, with the same physical integration domains.
Linearity gives the exact continuum identity R=J+K. It does not require the
two integrals to use the same numerical quadrature. A mixed-resolution
estimator can therefore use

    R_mixed = K_fine + D_seed4 + V_seed4 + S_seed4.

Every expensive piece still uses its actual source-owned nodes. No loop is
interpolated, no term is removed, and Gaccurate is added only once. The cheap
control and its leading subtraction must use matching incoming windows/rules.

For computed coarse/fine differences, the norm triangle inequality is

    ||Delta R|| <= ||Delta K|| + ||Delta D|| + ||Delta V|| + ||Delta S||.

Dividing by the final mixed-source norm gives an observed refinement budget.
The existing seed3-to4 expensive-piece differences are evaluated separately
in assess_multirate. They are not a bound on the unknown exact integration
error, and their current denominator must be replaced after K_fine is known.
This gives a targeted next calculation instead of repeating all costly loops
when the dominant unresolved term is the inexpensive recoil/motion control.
