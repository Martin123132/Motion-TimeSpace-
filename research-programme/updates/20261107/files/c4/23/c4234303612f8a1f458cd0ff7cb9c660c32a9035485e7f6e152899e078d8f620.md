## 1. Gravitational kinetic term, contacts and boundary

The background Einstein equation gives 2H'+H²=-Pbar_total. Each canonical scalar's own metric contact is +a²Pbar_I s2, so those pieces cancel their share. The remaining combination

must be kept with the original rest-sector action, NOT silently set to zero. Its metric variations provide the rest-species stress source in the convention used below.

For the displayed gravitational block,
