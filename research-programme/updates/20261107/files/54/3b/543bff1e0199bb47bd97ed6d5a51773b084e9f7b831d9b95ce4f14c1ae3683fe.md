## Derived response weights keep the cold anchor fixed

The actual saved source curve already passes through the measured cold
source H6. Simply applying the cubic predictor weights through time would
silently move that cold anchor. We instead differentiated the saved curve:

    R6(s) = P(s) + bH(s) [H6 - P(.75)]
    J_W(s) = L_W(s) - bH(s)L_W(.75)
    J_C(s) = L_C(s) - bH(s)L_C(.75)

The diagnostic source change is J_W deltaW+J_C deltaC.
These bases equal1 at their own anchor and0 at the other anchors,including
the cold point. Independent full five-node interpolation reproduces the
cubic identities;piecewise three-node interpolation checks the local control.

This preserves H6,older endpoints and the declared source-curve family.
It is a sensitivity experiment,not adoption of the refreshed curve.
