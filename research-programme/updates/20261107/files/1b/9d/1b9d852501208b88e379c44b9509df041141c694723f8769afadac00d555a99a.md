## Reduced force and exact state Jacobian

At a material label let E_j=D f_j, g_j be the physical nodal gradient with the inherited average trace, and V_ij=D(eta_i*g_j).     B_i(a)=a^T Q_i a,

    partial_a B_i=2 Q_i a.

An independent complex variation of that potential checks the resulting contraction. The source remains pinned and its rate unchanged during this screen.
