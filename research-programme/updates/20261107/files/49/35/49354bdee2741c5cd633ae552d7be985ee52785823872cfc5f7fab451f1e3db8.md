local time. Source hashes and parsed table checks are recorded by
`results/sourced-emission/status.json`. Original notices in the downloaded
data are retained; no source rows are edited or inferred from a plot.

The original tables store linear Gaunt factors by rows of log10(u) and
columns of log10(gamma^2). gamma^2=Z^2 alpha^2/(2 theta_e). Both axes have
0.2dex spacing. The implementation uses tensor-product cubic Lagrange
interpolation of the linear values and rejects extrapolation. It reproduces
every original table node and preserves the stated orientation. These are
theory rate tables, not data points with observational confidence intervals.
Merged BHE tables do not imply the full Elwert-Haug integral is implemented.

The finite-frequency source functional used here is wider than Stage78's
soft rank-one model; its derivative must also be widened in the next stage.

The fine-structure input is inherited from Stage78's NIST-sourced value,
not inferred from this thermal calculation. The exact Planck constant uses
https://www.nist.gov/pml/special-publication-330/sp-330-section-2
Local `sources/nist-SI.html`.
No fitted emission amplitude, ion abundance or Gaunt factor is introduced.
