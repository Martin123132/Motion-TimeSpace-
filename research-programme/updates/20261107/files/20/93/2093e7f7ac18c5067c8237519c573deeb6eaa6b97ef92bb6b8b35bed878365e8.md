# Stage186: separate representation error from physical boundary curvature

The first analysis found one failed identity at h=0.0005: the relative residual
in the live/fixed/boundary centered-curvature split was 3.016061413592018e-10,
against the unchanged 1e-10 algebraic tolerance. Its original result remains in
results/status.json. This was not a failed 1% physical prediction gate.

## Why simply raising working precision is insufficient

Stage185 saved three binary matrices at each target: live L, boundary E, and
fixed-mask F=round(L-E). In general the saved binary operands do not satisfy
F+E=L exactly. Subtracting nearby matrices and dividing by h squared amplifies
that representation residual. Converting F to high precision cannot recover
the low bits already lost when it was formed.

The additive precision pass forms, using the same saved operands,

    F_star = mp(L) - mp(E)
    R = F_star - mp(F).

It retains both F and R. For paired offsets it checks

    D2 L = D2 F_star + (E_plus+E_minus)/h^2,
    D2 L = D2 F + (E_plus+E_minus)/h^2 + (R_plus+R_minus)/h^2.

The anchor belongs to its own fixed mask. R is calculated from saved operands;
it is not a fitted correction or a physical boundary contribution. In particular,
the physical E term is never discarded to make the curvature look smooth.

## Scope and retained record

precision_stage186.py repeats matrix, gas-capacity and baseline/MTS kernel
comparisons with this representation, at the same 1% prediction and 1e-10
identity tolerances. The centered matrix arithmetic is checked at 80 and 100
decimal digits. The physical quadrature matrices remain the original binary
calculations: this is NOT an 80-digit physical integration or a new integration
uncertainty bound.

No parent packets or original Stage186 comparisons are rewritten. The final
additive ledger is results/precision/status.json. It keeps the initial failure
under legacy_failed_checks, separates active_failed_checks, and records the
initial ledger's hash. Counts include repeated diagnostics of the same saved
inputs; they are not counts of independent physical experiments.
