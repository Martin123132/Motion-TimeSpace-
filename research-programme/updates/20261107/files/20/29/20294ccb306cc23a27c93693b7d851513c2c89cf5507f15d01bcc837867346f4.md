# Boundary correction before the finite-pullback rerun

The first real-node pullback attempt exposed a mistaken assumption in
the compact-support interpretation of the preceding diagnostic. Preserve
`annular-geometric-pullback-attempt01` and its executed script as FAILED.
It stopped at the first order64 finite map, before a successful finite
passive transformation was claimed.

## What failed and why

The archived spatial interval is approximately [5.2,6.8], shifted by the
material label. The original radial xi profiles vanish only outside
[5.1,6.9]. Subtracting d*xi(b) fixes the source but does NOT fix the
endpoints, because d is zero there. At the first fine reference material
label, eta at the endpoints for test0 is approximately 0.0013035914 and
0.0027737983. Either signed pullback moves an endpoint out of the domain.
The domain check correctly rejected this; it is not a reason to remove
that guard or extrapolate an exterior field without a model.

The exact rank-one kinetic Hessian and wave reparameterization identity
remain correct. The previously computed projected norms and shape work
also retain their numerical meaning for the stated profiles. However,
those nonvanishing-endpoint profiles cannot themselves establish a
fixed-boundary compatibility failure. For general endpoint-moving tests,
the wave boundary term [eta*(alpha*T^2-beta*g^2)/2] must be retained.
Any inference that those specific old numbers alone proved a violation
of the compact/fixed-boundary condition is withdrawn pending the test
below. Do not silently replace their values in historical reports.

## Geometry-defined admissible profiles

On each material layer with endpoints r_in, r_out and pinned source b,
subtract the piecewise affine interpolant through the three fixed
points. With the original source-motion shape d,

    h_in=1-d on r<b, zero on r>b,
    h_out=zero on r<b, 1-d on r>b,
    eta_pinned=xi-d*xi(b)-h_in*xi(r_in)-h_out*xi(r_out).

This vanishes at r_in, b and r_out. Derivatives use the same one-sided
map Jacobians. No taper width, force measurement or fitted coefficient
enters the construction. It is a fixed-endpoint admissible variation;
it need not vanish throughout a neighborhood of the boundary.

The finite construction now tests this profile. Compare its new forces
with the old ones as DIFFERENT tests, never require them to match. Keep
the old values in their own columns. Verify the three pinned values
explicitly before every finite map. Retain both one-sided interior
face derivatives and all steps from the original protocol.

The remaining action, saved states, quadrature, precision, tolerances,
physical Gram support, passive-current interpretation and resource/time
limits are unchanged. New executable version and attempt02 only; no
mutation of the failed attempt or executed source.
