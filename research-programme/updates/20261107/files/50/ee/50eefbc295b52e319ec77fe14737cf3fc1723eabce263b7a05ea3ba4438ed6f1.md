# Stage77 — from a stored spectrum to a physical local readout

## 1. Fix physical questions before projecting functions

The state retains the same metric, matter, two fields and collision couplings
as Stage76. We ask for the photon spectrum at a specified event, expressed in
the baryon rest tetrad, on a surface of fixed proper baryon number density.
This is a relational local readout, not yet the observer's past light cone.

Photon energy is E=-p.u_b. Occupation is a scalar on the mass shell, while
linear polarization is screen-tensor valued. A passive change of coordinates
or of an intermediate tetrad must not change this specified readout. An active
change of the physical observer can change the Doppler signal; we do not
declare different observers' measurements identical.

The phase-space/tetrad distinction is supported by Naruko et al.(2013),
sectionsIV.5–IV.7, https://arxiv.org/html/1304.6929 . Their reference background
is stationary in conformal energy. We retain the additional aging term of the
saved evolving, distorted background explicitly below.

## 2. Time and energy dilation are distinct tangents

Let s=ln(a/a_start), x=E/T_star(s), T_star proportional to a^-1, and

    n_bar(s,x)=n_P(x)+chi w(x)B(s,x),
    w=n_P(1+n_P), delta n=w X,
    q=-(x/w) partial_x n_bar
      =x+chi x[(1+2n_P)B-partial_x B].

At fixed *physical* energy E, the age tangent is

    A=(partial_s n_bar at fixed E)/w = chi B_s-q.

This is why using only the Planck tangent x can mistake background dilation
or aging for a new distortion. In a finite frequency interval, the strong
dilation is not quite the integration-by-parts form used in the parent solver:

    M q_change,strong = R_weak B - [x^3 w B psi/I3]_(x_min)^(x_max).

The finite-band term is retained in the readout and tested, not assumed zero.
The parent evolution is left unchanged; its boundary approximation is recorded.

## 3. Relational monopole and matter-rest dipole

For a first-order time relabeling with u=H T, the physical-energy occupation
and proper baryon density contrast d transform as

    X_0 -> X_0-A u,       d -> d+3u.

Hence the fixed-baryon-clock spectrum is

    R_0=X_0+A d/3.

For a passive intermediate tetrad boost beta, choose the convention

    X_1 -> X_1-q beta,    v_b -> v_b-beta.

The baryon-rest dipole is R_1=X_1-q v_b. These identities are independent of
the chosen coordinate description. Higher multipoles and polarization have
no background monopole/dipole to shift at this linear order. Screen axes
remain part of the specified polarization measurement.

The baryon clock is useful here precisely because baryon number is conserved
in the retained model even when photon production is added later. Its use is
a choice of which physical events to compare, not an imposed field equation
or an assertion that a temperature anisotropy on this slice is the whole CMB.

In the saved variables d=state[8]-state[0]-state[1]/2. The existing baryon
velocity is state[9]. The tests transform this sufficient set of readout
inputs; they do not pretend to evolve the full metric in a second gauge.
Separate finite time-shift and Lorentz calculations test the physical tangents.

## 4. Remove only the temperature/dilation degree of freedom

Define N as the photon-number-weighted spectral moment functional.
Only its intensity monopole is total photon number; in other multipoles or
Stokes components the same weighting defines a template convention, not
an additional conserved particle number.
For each spectral readout R, define

    Theta_N = (N R)/(N q),       S=R-q Theta_N.

Then N S=0, but its energy moment need not vanish. Theta_N is the number-defined
spectral-dilation amplitude of the *actual* background. In polarization it is
a thermodynamic spectral-template coefficient, not a separate gas temperature.
The full information is retained:

    X_0=S_0+q Theta_0-A d/3,
    X_1=S_1+q Theta_1+q v_b.

All other spectral modes reconstruct as S+q Theta. The temperature, clock and
velocity pieces are saved rather than discarded. Metric/clock and Doppler
contributions must subsequently enter the light-cone mapping.

The old complement of *both* number and energy was useful for diagnosing an
almost energy-free correction. It is not a general observable-distortion
projector. As a concrete counterexample, at a Planck background the chemical
potential template is M/w=x/beta_mu-1, with beta_mu fixed by N M=0. This is
entirely in the span of1 and x: removing that whole span erases a physical
distortion with nonzero energy. The one-temperature projection keeps it.

The related danger of reference-temperature artifacts is discussed by Chluba
et al.(2026), sections3.1 and4, https://arxiv.org/html/2602.14963v1 . We use the
retained spectral basis, not their finite template hierarchy or a fitted shape.

## 5. Measurement and coherent response

Specific intensity is proportional to E^3 n in a fixed observer tetrad.
An ideal band is therefore a linear functional of E^3 w(S+q Theta); the same
construction applies to Stokes components. Under a change of reference-energy
units, the physical-energy bounds, Jacobian and E^3 prefactor transform too.
Leaving out that prefactor produces a false calibration dependence.

The saved Stage76 wave is y_75+chi z. Because the readout is linear on their
common background, the *total* shape is

    S_total=S[y_75]+chi S[z].

The norm of this coherent sum is not the sum of its norms. This distinction
matters here: the mixed response is strongly opposite to the older spectral
response. Accuracy must therefore be checked again on the smaller remainder,
not inherited automatically from checks on either larger term.

## 6. Derived photon-production gate

Emission/absorption satisfying detailed balance has local monopole form

    C_em=-a_x (n-n_eq(T_e)),        a_x>=0.

For a Planck reference, its linearization is -a_x w(phi-x t). Deposit exactly
the opposite energy moment in the gas. With D_ab=int dmu a_x psi_a psi_b, the
joint weak matrix is

    W=[-D, D q_T; q_T^T D, -q_T^T D q_T].

It has joint temperature null(q_T,1), conserves total photon-plus-gas energy,
and gives dQ/ds=-int dmu a_x(phi-x t)^2 for the quadratic departure-from-
equilibrium functional (entropy deficit, not the physical entropy itself).
The chemical null is removed: photons can now be created or destroyed.
These are derived structural constraints, not an adjustable damping ansatz.

Chluba et al.(2026), Eq.(84), gives the finite-frequency detailed-balance
factor; Eq.(43) supplies the leading soft double-Compton scale. The gate uses
that soft estimate only where x<<1, with the retained opacity and temperature
histories. Alpha is the sourced CODATA2022 value, not derived by MTS:
https://www.physics.nist.gov/cuu/pdf/wallet_2022.pdf .

Optical-depth estimates are an omission diagnostic, not a complete physical
thermalization solution or an observational bound. Bremsstrahlung, emissivity
corrections, the distorted-background derivative, matter-rest boosts, and
finite heat feedback must be included consistently before evolving that source.
In particular do not keep the old photon-number-conservation projection once
photon production is present; retain temperature/number evolution explicitly.

The non-equilibrium linearization follows without inventing a closure. For
the same local physical-energy coordinate, write r_bar=n_bar-n_eq(T_bar).
Then

    delta C_em=-a_bar delta n
               +a_bar (partial_T n_eq)_bar delta T_e
               -(delta a) r_bar.

Here delta a must contain the actual emissivity, matter density, temperature
and conversion from proper scattering time to evolution time. The last term
vanishes at equilibrium but not on the retained distorted background. Thus
adding only a diagonal absorption matrix to Stage76 is not this derivative.
The background must evolve with C_bar=-a_bar r_bar, and its photon-energy
moment must enter the gas equation with the opposite sign. Applying the same
moment to delta C_em fixes the corresponding perturbed energy exchange.
Momentum exchange is obtained in the matter-rest frame and boosted with the
same physical occupation, not added as an independent adjustable drag.

For positive occupations the detailed-balance sign is also physical. If
f(n)=ln[(1+n)/n], then the photon-plus-thermal-reservoir entropy rate per
mode is C_em[f(n)-E/T_e]. Since f decreases and f(n_eq)=E/T_e, this product
is nonnegative. Linearizing gives the quadratic dissipative form above.
This proves a local collision sign and conservation contract, not the
cosmological entropy evolution or validity of a soft emissivity at high x.

## 7. Route to the sky, rather than identifying this mode with a measurement

The current k=0.1/Mpc Fourier mode and its angular monopole are not a measured
homogeneous distortion. After specifying the emission/visibility history,
the linear phase-space system defines a frequency- and polarization-dependent
propagator U. The observed band/multipole transfer has the form

    Delta_l^band(k)=Band[Observer_map U(k;eta_obs,eta_initial) state_initial],
    C_l^(ab)=4pi integral dln(k) P_initial(k) Delta_l^a(k) Delta_l^b(k)^*.

Endpoint redshift, clock and velocity terms must be kept in Observer_map;
the scattering sources, thermalization and visibility belong in U. The saved
wave supplies an initial/early transfer state, not those absent integrations.
The next concrete coupling is the sourced photon-production operator with
its background and matter exchange, followed by the visibility continuation.

## 8. Refinement without changing the physical basis or tolerance

The smaller coherent remainder exposes resolution errors hidden by looking
only at the larger separate contributions. The16-to24-function comparison
fails for the intensity monopole and dipole;24-to28 still fails the monopole.
Both failed packets remain saved. Their pass/fail decisions are not changed.

An obstacle to further refinement was traced to the evaluation algorithm.
Construction subtracts two successive orthogonal projections; evaluation had
combined the two rounded coefficient vectors before one subtraction. Those
operations are mathematically equivalent but not numerically identical.
At32 functions the old absolute re-evaluation error is2.23517418e-7, above
the existing1e-7 gate.

The successor stores and applies both passes in their construction order.
Native quadrature values, summed recurrence coefficients, mass matrix,
number/energy vectors and physical basis are unchanged. Re-evaluation now
reproduces the construction exactly at tested24/28/32/36 orders. Independent
70-digit point evaluations agree to1.362e-13 in max error/max(1,|value|);
analytic derivatives also pass independent finite differences. This repairs
the numerical representation rather than loosening the accuracy criterion.
The isolated probes are in `results/consistent-basis/status.json`.

These wave refinements deliberately hold the saved24-function background
fixed. They do not assert convergence of the full physical problem under
all background approximations, photon production or emission-rate choices.

## 9. Exact small-shape evolution coordinates, derived rather than a filter

The6400-step trial closes all global-shape time comparisons but not the
cold-parent ideal x=1 band.70-digit projection of the same states changes
only0.52% of that time discrepancy; nearly all of it lies in the first
energy-carrying shape component of the saved evolution. This localizes the
numerical issue without proving whether its cause is roundoff, stiffness or
another integration error. The gate remains open, not rounded down to pass.

Use the fixed Planck template q_T and the fixed-reference heat scale chi.
Let N be the same moment row and choose any full-rank complement V with
N V=0. One explicit choice is V_j=e_j-(N_j/N_0)e_0 for j=1,...,p-1.
For N q_T nonzero and chi nonzero, define

    X=q_T theta+chi V y,       theta=(N X)/(N q_T),
    t_gas=theta_0+chi eta.

All photon coefficients and the gas temperature are retained. These are
invertible coordinates, not a closure setting the spectral shape or the gas
slip to zero. They do not require every channel to scale as chi in a physical
limit; the chi=0 control must be treated separately. The same algebra can use
a better-conditioned fixed numerical scale if necessary without changing
the equations.

For two illustrative spectral coordinates, N=(1,n), q_T=(a,b), V=(-n,1),
the photon-plus-gas transformation is

    P=[[a,-chi*n,0],[b,chi,0],[1,0,chi]],
    det(P)=chi^2(a+n*b).

For the full state z, the exact equation transformation is

    M P z'=(L P-M P')z.

P'=0 when q_T and the reference scale chi are fixed. The retained scale is
based on conserved baryon density relative to T_star^3, not on a newly
assumed conserved photon number. If a time-dependent scale/template is used
instead, its derivative must be kept. A conservation row or stress functional
transforms with P; none may be silently discarded.

The cancellation must be implemented before floating-point assembly. For
example the Planck-linearized local monopole collision has the paired form

    C0=K0(X0-q_T t_gas)
      =chi K0(V y0-q_T eta).

Its gas row is the negative photon-energy moment, with the already derived
heat-capacity normalization. Assemble these shared terms directly. Merely
forming the large old matrix, multiplying by P^{-1}, and dividing an already
rounded cancellation by chi does not guarantee improved accuracy. Retain
the mixed-bath, metric and emission terms from their own derived formulas.

The observer readout can also be evaluated without subtracting large nearly
thermal spectra. Write q=q_T+chi q1 and R=q_T T0+chi W. With
P_T=1-q_T N/(N q_T), one obtains exactly

    Theta_dist=[T0+chi(N W)/(N q_T)]/[1+chi(N q1)/(N q_T)],
    S=chi[P_T W-Theta_dist P_T q1].

For the baryon-clock monopole, T0=theta0-d_b/3 and
W=V y0+(B_s-q1)d_b/3. For the matter-rest dipole,
T0=theta1-v_b and W=V y1-q1 v_b. Other modes use their corresponding
template amplitude and complement. These formulas preserve photon-production
changes of N B_s rather than assuming that moment vanishes.

`results/scaled-variable-identities/status.json` verifies eight symbolic
identities, including the direct readout, number complement and invertibility.
This is an implementation-ready coordinate derivation, not a completed new
evolution or a repaired band result. The next stage must assemble and evolve
it, compare to the same physical states/controls, and retest the open gate.
