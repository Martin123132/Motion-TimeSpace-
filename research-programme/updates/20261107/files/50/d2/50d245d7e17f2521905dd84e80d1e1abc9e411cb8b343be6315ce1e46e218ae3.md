# Stage91: from a conditional scattering matrix to an owned population source

This continues the retained MTS radiation/gas/metric parent. It uses the
Stage88 finite matched scalar QED kernel, Stage89 leading nonlocal histories
and Stage90 driven infrared tail. No gravitational action, fitted charge,
electron mass or Newton constant is changed. No new observational fit is
part of this calculation.

## 1. The physical departure, not a convenient diagnostic polynomial

Use the saved scaled photon departures Phi_H(x), Phi_C(x) and gas departures
b_H, b_C. Physical delta n=chi n_0(1+n_0)Phi and delta T_e/T=chi b_e.
For x<=1e-4 the wide-join profile uses x times the solved Stage90 slope.
For x>=2e-4 it uses the saved Stage89 rational representation on its actual
domain. A quintic partition connects them. A second join over5e-5..1e-4
measures this representation sensitivity. Neither join is a new field
equation or proof that the joined shape solves the full parent evolution.

The profile evaluator refuses x<1e-6 and x>60 rather than silently extend
the saved rational function. A dense smooth interpolant makes repeated
amplitude contractions practical; its agreement with both original domains
is checked separately. The derivative used in displaced observables is
the derivative of that same joined interpolant.

The weak test columns are

    F=(x, Phi_H_wide, Phi_H_narrow, Phi_C_wide, Phi_C_narrow, E_e/T).

For the heavy wide case c=(0,1,0,0,0,b_H); analogous vectors select the
other cases. Thus the scalar departure jump is exactly d_Phi=d_F.c,
including the electron energy change. It is not replaced by a fitted
temperature shift or a norm of a convenient trial basis.

## 2. The missing outer measure

For each seed x, initial electron velocity v and harder outgoing LAB angle
mu, Stage88 returns the Born-normalized matched weak matrix M_NLO. The
population matrix is

    L_NLO = integral dP_Juttner(v)
       integral dx [x^2 n_0(x)/I3]
       integral 2 pi dmu [d sigma_B/(sigma_T dOmega) (1+n_0(x'))]
       M_NLO(x,v,mu).

The bracket includes the actual relative flux and rest/lab angular
Jacobian, both in the inherited context function. I3=pi^4/15. The electron
measure is normalized at fixed electron density; electron density itself
belongs to d tau=n_e sigma_T c dt, not a second factor inside dP.

Because the seed distribution is isotropic its direction can be fixed to
the z-axis. The electron azimuth is integrated relative to the tagged hard
scattering plane; integrating only an electron meridian would be wrong for
the general moving event. Hard azimuth gives the displayed2pi. The
normalized Maxwell-Juttner radial, polar and azimuthal rules are retained.

The collision current in these readouts is

    C_NLO=-L_NLO.c.

The minus sign converts the positive-bilinear convention to collision
evolution. The elastic1/2 and ordered real/inverse factors are already in
the Stage88 matrix and are not added a second time to this outer measure.
The ordinary Born matrix is integrated using precisely the same measure,
and compared with the retained finite-shift collision bank. This independent
baseline test checks normalization, statistical weights and energy readout.

## 3. No rescaled virtual amplitude

At every node, evaluate the finite thermal scalar loop for that node's
temperature, incoming energy, electron velocity and hard angle. The
cache identity contains all those quantities, angular order and source
fingerprints. A cache hit reuses exactly the same amplitude, not a thermal
or kinematic rescaling. The finite vacuum term is likewise evaluated at
the actual node. Real emission, inverse absorption, finite reference
conversion, displaced phase space, shifted observables, canonical momentum

The rate remains reference-bath evaluated at this linear perturbation

not inferred to vanish merely because their unpolarized scalar projection

## 4. Source exchange and connection to the retained metric equations

For every real or displaced elastic event, independent photon and electron
energy calculations give d_x+d_(E_e/T)=0. Consequently with
u=(1,0,0,0,0,1), L_NLO.u=0 and

    C_gamma,E + C_gas,E = 0.

This identity is tested before the outer integral and after contraction
with the actual profiles. No energy projection is applied after the fact.

In a homogeneous isotropic frame the corresponding proper energy transfer is

    Q_gamma = chi rho_gamma n_e sigma_T c C_gamma,E,
    Q_gas=-Q_gamma.

The spatial average vanishes by rotational symmetry, so the local
four-force is parallel to the common bath four-velocity. This is the
homogeneous energy part of the covariant kinetic source, not a calculation
of its full finite-k or polarized momentum response. Paired source insertion
is compatible with the total stress conservation required by the parent
metric equations. An energy-only ad hoc photon source with no gas partner
would not be.

The Stage89 recoil-free leading DC term has zero photon energy readout, but
nonzero other spectral/number readouts. The full matched real contribution
must REPLACE its approximate overlap, not be added unchanged to every
spectral equation. A small energy correction is not a license to ignore
double counting in the other moments.

## 5. Finite-capacity gas susceptibility

Hold the photon profile fixed only for this response calculation. The Born
energy response to b_e has positive conductance H=L_Born,EE, following the
independent energy null. A supplied energy correction delta C produces

    chi (delta b_e' + delta b_e) + R H delta b_e = -R delta C,
    R=d tau/dN.

The homogeneous response decays. For known forcing and conductance over a
time interval, the comparison bound is

    |delta b_e| <= max(initial error,
                       sup |delta C|/(H+chi/R)).

This is derived from the finite-capacity equation, not an instantaneous
equilibrium closure. Individual population snapshots can evaluate the local
gain1/(H+chi/R); they do NOT prove a uniform bound between snapshots or
when the photon spectrum is simultaneously allowed to change. A completed
feedback run must retain that spectrum response as well.

## 6. Numerical and physical remainder ledger

The integration explicitly has a seed band and a soft lower floor. Numerical
refinement within it is not a proof about the omitted seed tail or collective
plasma propagation. Stage90 controls tail representation on its stated
kinetic interval; it does not complete unknown sub-plasma physics. Results
must distinguish loop/real quadrature refinement, outer population
refinement, joining sensitivity and missing-domain uncertainty.

The acceptance scale was set before runs:1% of the NLO energy correction
or1e-6 of the retained nonzero Born energy source, whichever is larger.
Passing that numerical scale licenses a finite-domain energy projection,
not a full all-frequency operator or a new likelihood result.

Each node is saved atomically, with resumable partial sums. Completed runs
are immutable. The progress.json file is an incremental snapshot; completed
status.json plus COMPLETE is authoritative even if the last incremental
record still says running. Cache files are sharded for publication safety.

    c_a = -integral W d_(B_a) d_Phi.
