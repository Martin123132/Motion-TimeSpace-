# Stage96: the whole source with independently resolved components

We retain the same continuum collision source and parent physical profiles:

    C = Gaccurate + R,   R = D + V + S + K,   K = C0 - Gsampled.

The angular tensor factorization and exact-kinematics control are inherited
without any change to their coefficients or energy dependence. This stage
changes the numerical frequency measure, not the field theory.

For the existing spectral coordinate z=(x-p)/(x+p), p=0.1,

    x=p(1+z)/(1-z),   dx=2p dz/(1-z)^2.

This is an exact coordinate substitution on the original finite incoming
window [1e-5,24]. The forward/inverse leading selector is unchanged; do not
replace its inverse hard-frequency window by a different seed cutoff.

## Separate grids follow linearity

Every component is an integral over its specified physical measure. Linearity
does not require their quadrature nodes to coincide. Thus

    R_mixed = K_compact160 + D_seed4 + V_seed4 + S_seed4

is a consistent mixed-resolution estimator of the SAME continuum expression.
It is not identical to the exact integral merely by being well-defined. The
expensive terms retain their actual owned photon/electron nodes and saved
coefficients; none is interpolated onto compact nodes. The control and its
leading subtraction use matching compact incoming quadrature.

## Independent component budgets

Use DeltaK = K160-K128 and DeltaD/DeltaV/DeltaS from seed4 minus seed3.
In the full96 source dual norm, the triangle inequality gives

    ||sum_j Delta_j|| <= sum_j ||Delta_j||.

Normalize by the newly assembled remainder norm, not the earlier unresolved
norm. Each difference is observed quadrature evidence, not a proven bound on
the omitted continuum error. Do not count cancellation between errors as
precision, and do not use the large leading source to hide a correction error.

At fixed parent background, the original finite-heat photon/gas response is
linear in the photon source and its independent gas source. Apply that SAME
response separately to every Delta_j, then sum their photon and gas norm
ratios. Both must meet the1% observed threshold. Check the original equations
and energy/work identities in every solve; there is no equilibrium-gas closure.

## Scope

All96 test functions, four physical profiles and the original16-electron
measure are retained. Incoming-frequency convergence does not certify the
unchanged hard/electron rules, virtual internal quadrature, physical tails
or time-history feedback. New extreme nodes receive a separate inner-rule
spot check, whose coverage must not be called a global error theorem.
