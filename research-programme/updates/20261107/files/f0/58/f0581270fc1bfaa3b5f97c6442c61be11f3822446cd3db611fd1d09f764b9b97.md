# The remaining reconstruction derivative has an equation owner

Stage242's first calculation finds nonzero saved-tail motion for both
backgrounds. A scalar amplitude alone does not exactly describe that motion:
the nonproportional component of the interpolant tangent has relative
unweighted slope norm 0.00094043 (heavy) and 0.00092522 (cold). These are
diagnostics of the saved interpolation, not bounds on a final observable.

The actual owner is `../dynamical-infrared-tail-20261008/evolve_tail.py`,
with its retained soft-transport derivation in that directory's DERIVATION.md.
The original bank and controls remain intact. In u=ln(a/a_initial), let
s=Phi/x, r be the gas departure and beta be the saved hard spectral functional.
With optical rate R, positive jump matrix M, soft eigenvalue lambda1,
DC/free-free rates Gamma_DC/Gamma_BR and Gamma their sum:

    s_u = R [M_in s + (lambda1-Gamma)s
              + Gamma_DC beta + Gamma_BR r - lambda1 r
              + M_low s_low + M_high s_high].

The retained lower exterior is its explicitly specified sourced value
s_low=(Gamma_DC beta+Gamma_BR r)/Gamma; the upper exterior is the actual
finite-band photon reconstruction divided by x. These remain boundary inputs,
not a proof that the unknown all-frequency dynamics has that closure.

Use the saved coefficient bank to evaluate this RHS, not a guessed stationary
slope. Distinguish three quantities: the stored interpolant's derivative,
the RHS at the original stored state and drive, and the RHS with the repaired
photon/gas drive at the same tail state. The latter is a local vector-field
evaluation, not evidence that the old tail has already evolved under the new
drive. It supplies a concrete starting point for coupled continuation.

For either fixed blend w(x), the missing photon shape is

    Phi_u,tail(x) = (1-w(x)) x s_u(x).

needed because the joins and comoving frequency coordinates stay fixed.

representation must nevertheless be checked through the saved precision
policy. Tail tangents require new wave columns or a direct directional
contraction; they are not silently expressible in the two old tail columns.
