Let s=(u-u0)/H,where u0=.25U,u2=.5U,H=.25U,U=ln(10).
Three calculated finite-remainder sources are R0,Rm,R2 at s=0,.5,1.
Delta=Rm-(R0+R2)/2. The quadratic interpolant is

    Q(s)=(1-s)R0+sR2+4s(1-s)Delta.

Its numerical coefficients are fixed by those sources. They are NOT new
physical constants,or a derived exact time dependence of the collision law.
At s=.25 and.75,the weights are (3/8,3/4,-1/8) and(-1/8,3/4,3/8).
Their sum is one;they reproduce every scalar polynomial through degree2.
Negative interpolation weights are not negative event-measure weights.
All physical integration weights remain positive.

Freeze Q at both positions before independent source calculation with
theta(u)=theta_start exp(-u),actual saved photon/gas/tail history,and the
same physical event quadrature. Store the piecewise-linear three-anchor
predictions too,so both approximations face the same new calculation.

Let dL=R(.3125)-Q(.25),dR=R(.4375)-Q(.75).
Define local hats hL(s)=max(0,1-4|s-.25|) and
hR(s)=max(0,1-4|s-.75|). Their supports are [0,.5] and [.5,1].
The defect-corrected interpolant

    Qc(s)=Q(s)+hL(s)dL+hR(s)dR

passes through all five computed source nodes,retains the old three
exactly,and introduces no extra physical parameters. It is continuous,
piecewise quadratic,with known slope jumps at the added knots.
This is not asserted to be the exact unknown source between the nodes.

For the unchanged linear first-order,fixed-geometry coupled photon/gas
equations and the same zero initial correction at.25,solution linearity gives

    state[Qc]-state[Q]=state[hL dL]+state[hR dR].

Evolve the left and right defect terms separately to test this identity
for all96 modes,independent gas,photon energy/number and expansion work.
Use common timestep operators for the baseline and every forcing column.
The original three-anchor piecewise-linear response is replayed unchanged.

Step error,forecast interpolation error,and new-epoch event quadrature
error are distinct. Two successful predictions do not prove a supremum
bound on all times,nor do they certify unrefined temperature-dependent
quadrature. The small interval correction relative to the saved parent
departure is useful context,not a new cosmological data fit.

The differential explanation from Stage103 is retained:
R''=theta^2 R_theta_theta[z]+theta R_theta[z]
    -2theta R_theta[z']+R[z''] on suitably differentiable segments.
A quadratic time interpolant uses observed curvature without asserting
