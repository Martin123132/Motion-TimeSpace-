## 2. Exact synchronous operator and its expansion

Use conformal time tau, Hcal=a'/a, and

    ds^2 = a^2[-d tau^2 + C_ij dx^i dx^j],
    C = I + epsilon h + epsilon^2 l,
    phi = phi0 + epsilon u + epsilon^2 v,
    Theta = ln sqrt(det C).

There is no factorial in this perturbation convention. Theta1=tr(h)/2 and Theta2=tr(l)/2-tr(h^2)/4. A time-independent primordial reference volume may be subtracted when defining the dynamic S2; it cannot change Theta2'. In particular, the independent second-order volume is not just tr(l)/2.

The exact canonical equation, multiplied by a^2, is

    phi'' + 2Hcal phi' + Theta' phi'
    - Cinv_ij partial_i partial_j phi
    - [partial_i Cinv_ij + Cinv_ij partial_i Theta] partial_j phi
    + a^2 m^2 phi = 0.

At first order L_k u=-Theta1' phi0', with L_k=d_tau^2+2Hcal d_tau+k^2+a^2m^2. At second order, for K=p+q,

    L_K v_K + Theta2_K' phi0' = convolution V_KG(p,q),

    V_KG(p,q) = -(Theta_p' u_q' + Theta_q' u_p')/2
      + {[K_i h_ij(p) q_j - (p.q)Theta_p]u_q
         + [K_i h_ij(q) p_j - (p.q)Theta_q]u_p}/2.

This is a symmetric ordered Fourier kernel. The inverse metric and volume-gradient terms have both been included. Discarding Theta2' would instead calculate a prescribed-metric response, not the self-consistent solution.

## 3. The complete canonical source split

The terms linear in the second-order field are

    E_L = phi0' v'/a^2 + m^2 phi0 v,
    P_L = phi0' v'/a^2 - m^2 phi0 v,
    j_i,L = -phi0' partial_i v/a.

The pure quadratic kernels, to be added to these rather than substituted for them, are

    E_Q(p,q) = [u_p' u_q' + (a^2m^2-p.q)u_p u_q]/(2a^2),
    P_Q(p,q) = [u_p' u_q' - (a^2m^2-(p.q)/3)u_p u_q]/(2a^2),
    j_i,Q(p,q) = -i[q_i u_p' u_q + p_i u_q' u_p]/(2a),
    pi^i_j,Q(p,q) = -[(p_i q_j+q_i p_j)/2-delta_ij(p.q)/3]u_p u_q/a^2.

j_i is covariant spatial momentum; pi^i_j is mixed spatial anisotropic stress. The homogeneous background has no spatial gradient, so metric corrections to this last quadratic gradient product start at third order. A convolution coefficient can have either sign without violating positivity of the full real-space canonical energy.

The action identity div(T)_nu=(Box phi-m^2phi) partial_nu phi fixes conservation. `derive_ward.py` checks the energy and covariant momentum expansions off shell for an arbitrary one-dimensional field and a diagonal first-order metric. This is supplemented by the genuinely nonparallel three-dimensional exact-operator and Fourier tests, not presented as an exhaustive numerical proof for all possible configurations.

## 4. Coupled Einstein volume equation, not a metric closure

Synchronous geodesic Raychaudhuri gives

    Theta'' + Hcal Theta' + Theta'^2/3 + tr(sigma_conf^2)
      = -a^2 delta(E+3P)/2,

where the background equation has been subtracted and sigma_conf is the tracefree part of Cinv C'/2. The second-order equation is therefore

    Theta2_K'' + Hcal Theta2_K'
    + sum_I[2 phi0_I' v_I,K' - a^2 m_I^2 phi0_I v_I,K]
    + (a^2/2)(E2_ordinary,K+3P2_ordinary,K) = convolution G_Q(p,q),

    G_Q = -Theta_p' Theta_q'/3 - (hTF_p' : hTF_q')/4
          - sum_I[u_I,p' u_I,q' - (a^2m_I^2/2)u_I,p u_I,q].

The scalar gradient terms cancel from E_Q+3P_Q, but not from energy, momentum or anisotropic stress. Removing them from the other Einstein equations would be wrong. `derive_volume.py` independently computes R00 from the connection for a general homogeneous diagonal spatial metric and checks the volume/shear decomposition and the canonical split. In the general synchronous unit-lapse slicing R_nn has these same local time-extrinsic-curvature terms.

This is a coupled field/volume block. The Hamiltonian and momentum constraints, the second-order tracefree metric and ordinary-species hierarchy still supply other parts of the finite-wave initial-value problem. They are explicit source/unknown channels, not zeros. In the CDM-comoving chart, conserved number density gives delta_c2=-S2+S1^2/2 only when S is measured relative to its inherited primordial volume. The nonlinear seed and its spatial pullback remain those of `../adiabatic-curvature-seed-20261007/62-regular-curvature-seed-and-quadratic-source.md`.

## 6. Constructive local weak-gravity response

As an independent limit check, take a locally Minkowski background with phi0=0 and on-shell first-order scalar waves. Their stress begins at epsilon^2, so the metric sourced at that order obeys the linearized Einstein equations without missing first-order gravitational self-products. With covectors p_mu=(-omega_p,p), p^2=q^2=-m^2 and K=p+q, the ordered kernel is

    T_mu_nu = -(p_mu q_nu+q_mu p_nu)/2 + eta_mu_nu(p.q-m^2)/2,
    K^mu T_mu_nu=0,
    bar_h_mu_nu = 2 T_mu_nu/K^2,
    h_mu_nu = bar_h_mu_nu - eta_mu_nu tr(bar_h)/2,    K^2 != 0.

`local_gravity.py` substitutes this metric into the full linearized Ricci and Einstein tensors and checks the Lorenz constraint for massive sum modes, nonparallel modes, a static beat, and a massless comparison. This is a particular solution; homogeneous gravitational waves and boundary conditions are not fixed by algebraic inversion. K=0 and K^2=0 cannot be divided out. They are kept as background/resonant channels. Weak-field conventions are cross-checked against Carroll's original lecture notes, section6: https://ned.ipac.caltech.edu/level5/March01/Carroll3/Carroll6.html and https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9712019. The explicit kernels and checks here are calculated locally.

For phi=A cos(kx)cos(omega t), omega^2=m^2+k^2, carrier averaging gives

    rho = A^2[(m^2+k^2)+m^2 cos(2kx)]/4,
    Pxx = A^2 k^2/4,
    Pyy = Pzz = A^2 k^2 cos(2kx)/4.

For its nonzero static K=2k mode let rho_K=A^2m^2/4 and pT_K=A^2k^2/4. In Newtonian gauge,

    Phi_K=-(rho_K+2pT_K)/(2K^2),
    Psi_K=-rho_K/(2K^2),
    Phi_K/Psi_K=1+2(k/m)^2.

The stress determines the slip; it is not an adjustable modification of G. As k/m tends to zero, the inherited physical normalization becomes laplacian Phi=4piG rho_physical. The homogeneous standing-wave energy requires a background response and is not erased by this nonzero-mode calculation. This benchmark has zero background field, flat-space carrier time and small gravity; do not copy its slip formula into the finite-density FLRW universe without the additional terms.

## 7. Exact null comparison: useful but distinctly scoped

    ds^2=-2du dv+dX^2+dY^2+H du^2,
    phi=F(u),
    H=-[F'(u)]^2(X^2+Y^2)/2,
    R_uu=-laplacian_perp H/2=[F'(u)]^2=T_uu.

The full inverse metric, connection, Ricci and scalar equation are checked, not just a linearized null Fourier denominator. A normalized Maxwell field with F_uX=F'(u) has the same stress tensor and satisfies both source-free Maxwell equations. Here the shared symbol F for a scalar profile must not be confused with the antisymmetric Maxwell tensor F_mu_nu. This is a local infinite-plane comparison, not a finite-energy object or a derived charge/U(1) sector. Approaching it at high frequency requires controlling mass, curvature and background-field corrections. No new black-hole or electromagnetic prediction is inferred from stress degeneracy.
