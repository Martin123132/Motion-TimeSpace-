# Propagate the precision repair, with the positive force sign

This calculation may run only after native orders48/64/96 pass the
unchanged inverse-norm comparisons on both MTS grids. It reuses the
saved reactions and changes the native force evaluation only.

With r0=Pdot-F_wave-F_native and L=M+P_metric A_old,

    L k0_old = -r0_old,
    Delta F = F_native_repaired - F_native_old,
    L Delta k0 = +Delta F,
    Delta metric-time = A_old(Delta k0).

The sign is positive. The native potential has no old-field acceleration
dependence, so its precision repair does not change L. The background,
position direction, momentum-rate forcing and wave force are unchanged.

The two final native integration orders also give a forcing difference;
propagate that through L rather than assume its fixed-metric inverse norm
equals the coupled response sensitivity. Check the metric-momentum coupling
against its independent complex-step expression. Additionally replay the
new precision-preserving native derivative against fresh70-digit central
differences at two off-grid labels and two steps on each MTS grid.

Store the candidate updated reaction and metric-time response separately.
The updated equation residual is obtained by adding the verified linear
correction residual to the previously independently re-integrated residual.
It is expressly NOT a new full material re-integration. Existing wave-force,
momentum-baseline and mixed-time flags remain. No acceptance for C0 follows
from this native-only update alone.
