# Propagating the radial representation sensitivity through k0

Reconstruct the degree-22 and degree-20 regular radial operators from
the OWNED Gauss mixed-load arrays. Both retain the identical physical
endpoint jumps and outer normalization. Replaying both saved states
must agree within 2e-18 absolute before any use of their difference.

For high/lower equations B_H'=L_H B_H+f_H and B_L'=L_L B_L+f_L,
the difference satisfies

    deltaB' = L_L deltaB + (L_L-L_H) B_H + f_L-f_H.

Compute the coefficient differences BEFORE converting to node values.
The jumps and the mixed outer-boundary inhomogeneity cancel; deltaB
has zero jumps and homogeneous coupled outer normalization. This
avoids subtracting two nearly equal complete metric responses. Verify
that B_L-B_H-deltaB is within 2e-18 absolute, and report its size.

Only the momentum-rate part of r0 depends on B0. Hence, at fixed P:

    delta r0 = P_metric(deltaB),
    delta k0 = -L_live^{-1} P_metric(deltaB),
    delta correctedY_t = deltaB+A_old(delta k0).

The validator solves this fresh live system for all four matched
cases, using the established residual/correction gates and requiring
max abs(delta k0)<=2e-11. This is a measured degree-truncation
sensitivity, not an enclosure of all B0 error, inherited position
errors, or total theoretical uncertainty. No earlier precision flags
are erased. No new dynamics, couplings, baseline subtraction or fits
are introduced.
