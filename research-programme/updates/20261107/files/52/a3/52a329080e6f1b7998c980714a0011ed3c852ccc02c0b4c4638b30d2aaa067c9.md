# Stage236: finite real remainder with correlated soft subtraction

The target is the complete finite-domain mixed event source: real emission,
absorption, spontaneous logarithm and profile conversion, plus the saved virtual
and displaced-state responses. Use the original leading real control alongside
the full-minus-leading remainder. No fit, parent action or bank is replaced.

## Exact reduced Dirac amplitude

For the resting incoming electron, the on-shell reduced first-leg factor is
R_j=slash(n_j) slash(eps_j) u_i/2, independent of the signed photon energy.
Group the six permutations by their last leg. Precompute

    N_j=sum_(first,middle; last=j) slash(eps_middle) R_first,
    M_j=slash(eps_j) slash(n_j) N_j.

Then the exact amplitude is

    ubar_f sum_j [M_j/(2 p_f.n_j)
                 -(p_f.eps_j) N_j/(r_j p_f.n_j)],

where r_j are the signed transfer energies (E,-h,-q). This is algebraically
the existing reduced amplitude, not a fitted soft expansion. At high precision
it allows density-minus-pole subtraction before conversion to binary64.
The leading control is the E->0 coefficient at fixed f=q/E, with rational
basis(1,f,1/f,1/(1-f)); its four amplitude coefficients give an exact Gram
matrix. This preserves the original leading-control definition.

## Correlated paired event algebra

Let L_sigma,R_sigma be the angular weak factors of the true signed real event,
and L_B,R_B the elastic mixed factors. Define

    X=h_B,s R_eq+D_B wave_s,
    c=2 n_s(1+n_s)/(1+2n_s), R_ref=R_B+c X.

On the interval where emission and absorption are both allowed, form

    L_even=(L_em+L_abs)/2-L_B, L_odd=(L_em-L_abs)/2,
    R_even=(R_em+R_abs)/2-R_ref, R_odd=(R_em-R_abs)/2,
    rho_sum=(1+n)rho_em+n rho_abs,
    rho_diff=(1+n)rho_em-n rho_abs.

The integrand after the common soft subtraction is

    [rho_sum-a(1+2n)/q] L_B R_ref
    +rho_sum[L_B R_even+L_even R_ref+L_even R_even+L_odd R_odd]
    +rho_diff[L_B R_odd+L_even R_odd+L_odd R_ref+L_odd R_even],

with the angular contraction1/(2l+1) and physical event weight/2. Restore the
known spontaneous-window/profile differences when the separator/profile is
changed. Outside the paired ordering interval keep the surviving process and
the matching reference explicitly; do not invent an absent event.

Use exact hard-frequency shifts and correlated polynomial differences before
constructing these factors. The number-counting soft test need not vanish at
zero frequency; the original finite floor is retained, not extrapolated away.
Gas four-force is calculated from independent on-shell recoil, not assigned
minus the photon result after integration. Exterior spectral terms remain open.

The general virtual angular uncertainty found in Stage235 is a separate
qualification requirement; scalar mass angular identities do not certify it.
