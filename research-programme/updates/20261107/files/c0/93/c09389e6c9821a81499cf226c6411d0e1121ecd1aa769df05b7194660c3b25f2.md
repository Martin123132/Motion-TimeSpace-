# Stage67 source ownership

Retrieved/consulted7October2026. Scientific equations in this stage are
derived in the local note; these references identify inherited physics and
the numerical calibration, not evidence of a new experimental fit.

1. NIST, CODATA2022 constants table:
   https://physics.nist.gov/cuu/Constants/Table/allascii.txt
   Local copy: `sources/nist-codata-2022.txt`.
   SHA256:77fb90e66c40db3e6eb16630bc9c88e4c7c8beddbe5e71be406f2f26e3f67e67.
   Supplies classical electron radius, Thomson cross section, electron rest
   energy, Boltzmann constant and their stated units/uncertainties. See
   `results/calibration/status.json` for parsed source lines and retrieval time.
   Release description: https://www.physics.nist.gov/cuu/Reference/versioncon.shtml

2. Cyril Pitrou, The radiative transfer at second order: a full treatment of
   the Boltzmann equation with polarization, arXiv:0809.3036v2 (2009 revision):
   https://arxiv.org/abs/0809.3036
   https://arxiv.org/html/0809.3036v2
   Sections1.2,1.7 and6 cover screen polarization, frame changes and collision
   terms; sections6.2 and6.4 separate recoil/thermal approximations. This is
   prior theory, not a claim of original discovery of polarized scattering.

3. Geant4 official Physics Reference Manual11.4, Compton scattering,
   Sampling the Final State:
   https://geant4.web.cern.ch/documentation/dev/prm_html/PhysicsReferenceManual/electromagnetic/gamma_incident/compton/compton.html
   Gives the Klein-Nishina differential law and scattered photon energy.
   We use its free-electron law with Z=1, not its separate empirical atomic
   cross-section fit, energy cutoffs or bound-electron model. The original
   reference identified there is O.Klein and Y.Nishina, Z.Physik52,853(1929).
