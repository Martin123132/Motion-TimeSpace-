## 2. Include the non-radiating component of the tide

The ell=2 polar source on an equatorial circular orbit has m=0 and m=+/-2.
Only the last two radiate, but all three contribute to the conservative energy.
Let f=1-2M/r, u^t=(1-3M/D)^(-1/2), sigma=m Omega, and use unit-normalized harmonics:

    Y20(pi/2,0)=-sqrt(5/(16pi)), Y22(pi/2,0)=sqrt(15/(32pi)).
    C_m=8pi mu u^t Ybar_2m.

At the equator the even tensor harmonic is Y_phiphi=(3-m^2)Y.
Consequently Qsharp=C_m Omega^2 r^2(3-m^2)/6 times delta(r-D).
The other even scalar projections remain as in doc19. Canonicalizing the delta
derivative before evaluation gives the jumps in x=r_star:

    [Z]=a_m=C_m(D-2M)/[3(2D+3M)],
    [Z_x]=b_m=-C_m(12D^3-16D^2M-24DM^2-33M^3)/[6D^2(2D+3M)^2]
                 +C_m M(m^2-4)/(6D^2).

The last term is essential for m=0. It is derived from the tensor source, not
assigned by copying the radiative harmonic. The angular conservation identity
holds for general m. `derive_orbit.py` checks these distributional and source
identities exactly.

## 3. A smooth metric difference and a reciprocal action

The body's and the BH's source jumps are identical. Their difference Delta Z
therefore has no delta or delta-prime source at the particle. In the selected
polar sector it is a smooth homogeneous exterior field there; singular local
source terms in metric reconstruction cancel. No subtraction of two nearly equal
total waves or separately divergent particle fields is required.

For any source-free radial master vector (Z,Ystar)=(Z,Z_x), reconstruct in RW gauge:

    A(r)=(6r^2+6Mr+6M^2)/[r^2(2r+3M)], K=A Z+Ystar,
    K_r=A_r Z+A Ystar/f+(V_Z-sigma^2)Z/f,
    H0=H2=(2+3M/r)/f*(3Z/r-K)+r K_r.

The helical tangent is k=partial_t+Omega partial_phi. At the source,
h_kk=(f H0+Omega^2 D^2 K)Ybar_2m. Direct symbolic substitution gives the useful
reciprocity identity

    h_kk[Z]= -3/(4pi mu u^t) * (b_m Z-a_m Z_x).

This checks both derivative-source terms and metric normalization against the
same worldline coupling; it is not a fitted matching condition. The reconstruction
formula is independently sourced to Martel's Appendix A8, with its source terms
cancelled only after taking the physical difference.

Take the time-symmetric, conservative part: one half retarded plus one half
advanced. For this real, nonrotating system at real frequency this is Re of the
on-source retarded response. Outgoing dissipative flux remains a separate object.
Integrating out a linear field with quadratic action gives ONE HALF its linear
source interaction. Since the point-worldline interaction is mu u^t h_kk/2,
the induced circular action per coordinate time is

    F(Omega)=Delta L_circ = mu u^t/4 * Delta h_kk^cons.

Using mu u^t h_kk/2 here would double-count the field-deformation energy.
The associated redshift change obeys

    Delta z=-u^t Delta h_kk^cons/2, F=-mu Delta z/2,
    partial_mu F=-Delta z at fixed Omega and body parameters.

This is an independent normalization check through the mass first law. Within
regular helically symmetric gauges with fixed asymptotic time and fixed M,
the contraction on the background circular geodesic has the appropriate
invariance. The present partial multipole result is not a full all-mode self-force.

Define overlap O_m(V)=b_m V-a_m V_x and W(U,V)=U V_x-U_x V. Use the same U,H,V
as doc19: center-regular body, horizon-ingoing BH, infinity-outgoing wave.

    Delta C_m=-W(U,H) O_m(V)/[W(U,V) W(H,V)],
    F_m=-3 g_m/(16pi) Re[Delta C_m O_m(V)], g_0=1, g_2=2.

All Wronskians may be evaluated at R and source overlaps at D. The formula is
normalization independent and does not introduce the convention-dependent
dynamic Love coefficient of an arbitrary buffer. For m=0, H=P and V=Q are the
static master bases and U is proportional to P+3lambda Q; therefore

    Delta C_0=3lambda O_0(Q)/W(P,Q).

Numerically, the source-overlap and explicit metric routes agree to better than
1e-12 relative throughout the grid. The stable wave-excess route agrees with a
fresh Wronskian reconstruction of every new saved interior to 1.34e-15 relative.

## 4. Derive energy and angular momentum at fixed frequency

The eliminated field is frequency-dependent. Its frequency derivative must be
retained; replacing it by a potential evaluated at fixed frequency misses stored
dynamical energy. Varying the conservative circular action gives

    Delta J=partial_Omega F,
    Delta E=Omega partial_Omega F-F,
    d(Delta E)/dOmega=Omega d(Delta J)/dOmega.

These are interaction/binding contributions from the conservative reduced
action. They are not the infinite standing-wave energy of an eternal symmetric
radiative solution, and they exclude dissipative energy escaping to infinity.
At this order the shift of the circular radius does not alter the on-shell point
action at first order, since that action is stationary on the background orbit.
Variations hold the central charge and reference mass fixed.

At large D the static master bases are P=r^3/6 and Q=r^-2, W=-5/6. Source evaluation
gives Delta h_kk=6lambda mu/D^6 and hence

    F0=3lambda mu^2/(2D^6),
    Delta E0=9lambda mu^2/(2D^6),
    Delta J0=6lambda mu^2/(Omega D^6).

The m=0 term supplies one quarter of F0; m=+/-2 supply three quarters. Thus a
radiation-only calculation would miss 25% of the leading interaction action.
The fixed-separation potential is negative, U_tide=-3lambda mu^2/(2D^6), while
Delta E at FIXED FREQUENCY is positive: the radius also shifts. These are not
contradictory signs.

## 5. First relativistic conservative coefficient, without fitting

At fixed body, use the near-zone basis from doc19 and differentiate before setting

Including u^t in C_m is necessary. Squaring the overlaps and summing the harmonic
weights gives, with u=M/D and L0=3lambda mu^2/(2D^6),

    F=L0[1+4u+...],
    Delta E=3L0[1+(44/9)u+...],
    Delta J=4L0/Omega[1+(14/3)u+...].

The body's additional dynamic-response terms start at Omega^2, hence D^-3 at

## 7. A compatible leading inspiral, rather than mixing approximation orders

For a circular weak-field binary with only the central body deformable, eliminate
its static quadrupole from the matched action. With separation d and mu_r=M mu/M_T,

    L=mu_r (d_dot^2+d^2 Omega^2)/2+M mu/d+3lambda mu^2/(2d^6).

Let d0=(M_T/Omega^2)^(1/3). To linear tidal order:

    delta d/d0=3lambda mu/(M d0^5),
    E=-mu_r(M_T Omega)^(2/3)/2+9lambda mu^2/(2d0^6),
    Iij=[mu_r d^2+3lambda mu/d^3] n_<ij>.

The moving induced quadrupole and the shifted orbital quadrupole both radiate.
Computing the STF tensor's third derivative gives P=(32/5)Omega^6 Iamp^2 and
angular-momentum flux P/Omega. At fixed frequency the leading corrections are

    delta P/P0 = 6lambda/d0^5 * (1+3mu/M),
    delta E'/E0' = -54lambda mu/(M d0^5),
    delta Omega_dot/Omega_dot0 = 6lambda/d0^5 * (1+12mu/M),
    Omega_dot0=(96/5)mu_r M_T^(2/3) Omega^(11/3).

This recovers doc19's 6lambda/D^5 flux excess in the test-mass, wide-orbit limit.
It also shows exactly why a wave-strength ratio alone does not give the chirp
once finite mass-ratio tidal orbital feedback is retained. These coefficients
follow from the same matched action and the total quadrupole; the independent
published energy/flux results are validation, not new free parameters.

With phases aligned at the same initial frequency, the leading time-domain GW
phase change between Omega_i and Omega_f is

    Delta Phi=-3lambda(1+12mu/M)/(8mu_r M_T^(7/3))
                 *[Omega_f^(5/3)-Omega_i^(5/3)].

The corresponding Fourier-phase term, modulo arbitrary coalescence time/phase,
is Delta Psi= -117 tildeLambda (M_T Omega)^(5/3)/(256 eta), where
eta=mu_r/M_T and tildeLambda=(16/13)(M+12mu)lambda/(M M_T^5).
Its second frequency derivative is checked directly against balance. Explicit
STF angular momentum and energy fluxes, the circular first law, and independent
