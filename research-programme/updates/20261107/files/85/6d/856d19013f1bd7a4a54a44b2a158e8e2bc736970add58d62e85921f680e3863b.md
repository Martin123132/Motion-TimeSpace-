# Stage84 — the thermal coupling is now a calculated amplitude

The same electromagnetic matter sector now has an unexpanded photon-bath
correction, with electron dressing derived alongside it. This advances the
source calculation used by the existing MTS radiation/gas/metric programme;
it does not replace that programme or restart its parent theory.

## What was obtained

- Derived the eight internal insertions and the external pole/spinor changes
  from one thermal self-energy. Their Ward identities close together.
- Derived the external pole residue. Its soft term combines with the internal
  loop to cancel stimulated-emission plus absorption poles direction by
  direction, using the same bath occupation.
- Reduced the radial loop exactly to two finite-energy poles, a constant and
  the soft pole. An analytic digamma expression agrees with two independent
  principal-value integrations. No fitted resonant width is used.
- Computed a finite, transverse, polarization-resolved thermal amplitude at
  the inherited temperature and E/T=1. Angular orders4,6,8 agree within the
  specified1e-9 relative gate.
- Crossed the real amplitude into absorption, including finite recoil,
  equilibrium reverse-reaction weights and photon/electron momentum balance.
- Read the actual two MTS histories. The electrons are extremely dilute in
  phase space, but not absent; their plasma scale and the resolved nonthermal
  mass moment are recorded rather than silently discarded.
