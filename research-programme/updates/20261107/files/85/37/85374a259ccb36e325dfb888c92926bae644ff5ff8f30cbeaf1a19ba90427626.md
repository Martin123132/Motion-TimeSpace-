perturbation. At the retained Taylor order write the weak collision source

    C(B)=L0 B + (1/2) H[B,B] + higher background order,
    JB W=H[B,W].

Consequently its homogeneous quadratic contribution is C2(B)=JB B/2 at
l=0. This is Euler's identity for a degree-two Taylor term, not a fit to the

## EOS response and the gravity source

Use Stage231's C=heat capacity and B_g=gas enthalpy. At fixed background,
the direct mixed-source rates are gas_r'=kappa JB_0[gas,:]W0/(chi C)
and gas_v'=kappa JB_1[gas,:]W1/B_g. These give total collision energy and
momentum rates rho_star kappa(Jenergy+Jgas), independently measurable.
Energy exchange can vanish while pressure and shear still respond:

    P_total'|collision = rho_star(CE/3 + 2 CgasE/(3C)),
    Pi_gamma'|collision = rho_star kappa JB_2[energy,:]W2/3.

Insert these into S_T=P_total-Pi_gamma, S_L=P_total+2Pi_gamma and the
retained metric equations. This is a physical route for collisions to alter
gravity without an invented net-energy source.

If the background photon transfer changes by dQ, Stage231 further fixes

    d(r') = -dCE/(chi C) + dQ b/(chi C) + C_R dQ r/(chi C^2),
    d(v') = -dCF/B_g + (1+2/(3C)) dQ v/B_g.

The C_R and enthalpy terms arise from C' and B_g', not an adjustable closure.
Keeping the old background slope then produces the known off-shell forcing
f_R=dQ/(chi C), which must enter the forced Einstein-constraint law instead
of being projected away. These conversions are in conformal time; divide
by H only when entering the retained logarithmic-scale-factor integrator.
