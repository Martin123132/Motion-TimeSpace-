# Accepted microscopic correction to calibrated gravitational source

## 1. Reuse the stress map, with the correct powers of chi

In Stage70/75's variables, rho_star scales as a^-4 and
chi=(3 n_total k_B T_star/2)/rho_star is constant on the retained background.
The photon correction is chi times its stored spectral potential. For the
saved interval increment (E,g,W,N,J), at fixed scale factor:

    q = delta rho/rho_star = chi(E+chi g),
    p = delta p/rho_star   = chi(E/3+2chi g/3),
    delta(T_e/T_star)      = chi g.

The gas energy correction is chi^2 g, not chi g. With s=ln a, the retained
coupled equation gives E_s+chi g_s=-chi g+J_s. Therefore

    delta rho_s+3(delta rho+delta p)=chi rho_star J_s.

The measured injection J_s is retained. Omitting gas pressure leaves an extra
-2 chi^2 rho_star g; this is an identifiable term even when too small to fail
a whole-density comparison. The full calibrated four-force is not inferred
from a residual-shape percentage.

## 2. Exact linear response within the retained parent branch

Use conformal Hubble rate Hc=a H, parent fields phi_i with momenta pi_i=dphi_i/deta,
positive constant masses m_i, and rho0=3 Hc^2/a^2 in c=8piG=1 units. All are
the existing calibrated parent inputs, not new fits. Let the state variation
at fixed a be v=(delta phi_i,delta pi_i), and let xi=delta H/H=delta Hc/Hc.

    xi = [rho_star q + sum_i(m_i^2 phi_i delta phi_i
                            +pi_i delta pi_i/a^2)]/(2rho0),
    delta phi_s = delta pi/Hc - (pi/Hc) xi,
    delta pi_s = -2 delta pi-a^2 m^2 delta phi/Hc
                 +(a^2 m^2 phi/Hc) xi.

This includes the fields' induced response. The source density alone is not
the self-consistent Hubble response. The thermal source pressure is separately
required for continuity; for a conserved test source rho_star q(s), its
normalized pressure is (q-q_s)/3.

Write xi=Cv+dq and v_s=A0 v+b xi, so A=A0+bC and B=bd. Then

    xi(S)=C(S)Phi(S,a)v(a)+d(S)q(S)
           +integral_a^S C(S)Phi(S,u)B(u)q(u)du.

The adjoint lambda_s=-A^T lambda, lambda(S)=C(S)^T evaluates the kernel
k(S,u)=lambda(u)^T B(u). Neither the homogeneous incoming term nor uncovered
q is silently set to zero. Zero incoming states are used only in the named
operator-probe and attributed-tail calculations.

## 3. A finite analytic bound including scalar feedback

Set z=(m_i delta phi_i,delta pi_i/a), w=(m_i phi_i,pi_i/a),
omega_i=a m_i/Hc. Then

    z_s = R z+b_z xi,
    R = [[0,diag(omega)],[-diag(omega),-3 Id]],
    b_z=(-omega_i w_pi,i, omega_i w_phi,i),
    xi=c_z z+dq, c_z=w/(2rho0), d=rho_star/(2rho0).

The symmetric part of R is nonpositive. No oscillation-averaged fluid closure
or positive damping gap is needed. Thus

    d||z||/ds <= ||b_z||||c_z|| ||z||+||b_z|| d |q|.

On[a0,a1], let rho_rad,0 be the retained positive radiation coefficient and
rho_phi,i(a0)=(m_i^2 phi_i^2+pi_i^2/a0^2)/2. Canonical background continuity
gives rho_phi,i(s)<=rho_phi,i(a0). Positive parent species imply

    rho_min = rho_rad,0/a1^4,
    omega_i,max = sqrt(3) m_i a1^2/sqrt(rho_rad,0),
    Bstar = sqrt(sum_i 2 omega_i,max^2 rho_phi,i(a0)),
    Cstar = sqrt(sum_i 2 rho_phi,i(a0))/(2rho_min),
    Dstar = rho_gamma,0/(2rho_rad,0), beta=Bstar Cstar.

For Q >= sup_[s0,S]|q(s)| and Z0=||z(s0)||:

    |xi(S)| <= exp(beta(S-s0)) [Cstar Z0 + Dstar Q].

The beta=0 limit is continuous. This is a conservative analytic majorant of
the coupled response, not merely an empirical maximum over probes. Numerical
coefficients inherit uncertainty in the parent calibration and initial state;
their floating evaluation is not an interval certificate of those inputs.

## 4. Gap-free source-free kinetic tail

For the retained weak photon/gas law, define

    M p_s=r K(p-t g)+r s_gamma,
    chi g_s=-chi g-r t^T K(p-t g)+r s_g,
    V^2=p^T M p+chi g^2.

Here M is positive and the physical collision/emission assembly has symmetric
K<=0. Compton blocks are negative positive-weight Gram sums; convex temperature
interpolation preserves that property. The retained emission blocks are
positive Gram sums subtracted from K. Thus, when this attributed source is off,

    (V^2/2)_s=r(p-tg)^T K(p-tg)-chi g^2 <=0.

This does not postulate a nonzero relaxation gap or erase equilibrium modes.
Cauchy-Schwarz with the physical energy row Erow gives the uniform tail bound

    |q(s)| <= chi sqrt(Erow M^-1 Erow^T+chi) V(s0) = Q_tail.

Combining with section3 gives a bound for the gravitational response driven
after the chosen saved restart, plus its explicit incoming-field term. The
real correction before that restart is not set to zero. Other source intervals
and nonlinear feedback remain separate. Tests of stored symmetric blocks and
saved-state comparisons diagnose floating representation; they are not a proof
of uncomputed continuum quadrature or global initial-state accuracy.
