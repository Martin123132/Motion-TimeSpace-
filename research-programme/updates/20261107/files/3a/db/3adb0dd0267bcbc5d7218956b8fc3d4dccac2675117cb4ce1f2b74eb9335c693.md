## Result

from separate uncertainty estimates. At a common incoming-frequency coordinate,
combine the three temperature contributions with their own physical weights:

`F_joint(x) = F_H(x) + F_W(x) - 1.5 F_C(x)`.

Across incoming orders 4, 5 and 6, the sum of these joint-frequency norms is
only **14.53–14.61%** of the sum of the separate weighted temperature norms.
This is about **85.4% cancellation** by this norm diagnostic. It is an aggregate
over common frequency nodes, not a claim that every frequency has exactly the
same cancellation. The actual vectors and norms at every node are saved.

The latest coherent virtual refinement difference is **44.74–44.76%** of the
preceding difference. Its size has fallen by about **55.2%**. The most useful
next integration targets are now localized: incoming panels **1–3** and **3–8**
account for **85.73–85.74%** of the latest sum of joint-panel refinement norms.
The first panel is the larger and more slowly shrinking contribution.

The temperature curve, frozen predictions, physical operators and baseline
are unchanged. This step reconstructs and checks saved results; it does not
adopt a different curve or create an observational fit.
