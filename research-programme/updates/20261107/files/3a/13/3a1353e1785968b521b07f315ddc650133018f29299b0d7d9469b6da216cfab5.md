# Stage112 — conservative low-frequency matching and feedback

## Actual advance

Derived and implemented the source that can be added WITHOUT counting
stored photons twice:

    D = C_ext[q] - K M^-1 m,   m_i = <V_i,q>.

Here q is the prescribed Stage111 low-frequency replacement, K is the
existing parent collision operator, and M is its96-mode mass matrix.
The derivative of the replacement's stored moments cancels after the
resolved variables are changed consistently. If the replacement merely
re-expresses the old finite-mode equations, D is zero.

This is a real constraint on the calculation, not just a small fitted
coefficient. Applying K to the projected new curve and adding that alone
would have produced artificial feedback. See `DERIVATION.md`.

We then computed the compact collision difference with all DC photon
legs, BR/gas exchange and a conservative leading-soft Compton extension.
The fixed parent response propagator evolves the paired difference and
retains expansion work and photon-number bookkeeping. Both correction
joins and baseline are treated alike in both saved backgrounds.

  0.126% baseline.

The last change is still small compared with the OLD full spectrum:

old spectrum is not precise compared with the new effect. The finest
net shift is therefore not a settled physical amplitude or sign.

The matching window is a numerical partition, never a fitted physical
cutoff. Its non-negligible sensitivity is retained as a limitation.
