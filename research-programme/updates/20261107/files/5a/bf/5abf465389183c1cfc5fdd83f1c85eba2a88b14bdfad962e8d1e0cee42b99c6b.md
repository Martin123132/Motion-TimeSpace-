# Independent field-force derivative and compensated accumulation

For the SAME represented field action, write g=q_xi/J, T=v-Wg and
Phi=alpha*T*W+beta*g, so the old field force is -integral Phi*N_xi dxi.
At a fixed material label and reference coordinate, the position direction is

    delta R=U, delta J/J=U_R,
    delta g=e_xi/J-g*U_R,
    delta W=D*edot_source,
    delta T=edot-delta W*g-W*delta g.

With F=1-2mu/R and delta mu=P_mu+mu_R*U,

    delta F=-2delta mu/R+2mu*U/R^2,
    delta nu=P_nu+nu_R*U,
    delta alpha=alpha*(2U/R-delta nu-delta F/(2F)),
    delta beta=beta*(2U/R+delta nu+delta F/(2F)),
    delta Phi=delta alpha*T*W+alpha*delta T*W
              +alpha*T*delta W+delta beta*g+beta*delta g.

The physical test-gradient and measure variations cancel in the weak
field force: J*(N_xi/J)=N_xi. No metric-time or acceleration response enters
this particular old FORCE term. This allows its precision to be isolated
without recomputing the expensive momentum-rate jets.

The new diagnostic evaluates the closed derivative above and a separate
complex directional derivative of the unexpanded flux on identical nodes.
It records both ordinary accumulation and block-compensated accumulation,
using the source-centered coordinates and split-label partition already
owned by the current calculation. This is NOT an integration-by-parts or
flux-form repair: no face term is removed, and no moving-cut claim is made.
