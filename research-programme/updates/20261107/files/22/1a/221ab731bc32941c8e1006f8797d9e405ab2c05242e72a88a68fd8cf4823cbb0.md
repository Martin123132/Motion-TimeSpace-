# Constructing the old-field position reaction

Private continuation of the completed four-case B0 calculation. This
document fixes the calculation before its results are known. No new
physical coefficient is fitted; the inherited action and four matched
reference/MTS, fine/third inputs are unchanged.

## Equation to solve

With e the owned compensated position mode, edot its owned transport,
and the independent support displacement eta, take the material family

    q(s,t)=q+s e+t v+s t edot,
    v(s,t)=v+s edot+t a+s t k0,
    R(s,t)=R+s U+t W+s t V,
    U=eta+D e_source, W=D v_source, V=D edot_source.

Here v is the velocity coordinate: its time derivative has the unknown
position response k0. Terms quadratic in t, although not displayed in
R, supply R_tt=D a_source and delta R_tt=D k0_source. The first forcing
calculation sets k0=0, not the final equation.

Let P=X+A_old(edot) and let B0 be the newly solved fixed-acceleration
variation of Y_t. The old Euler derivative is

    r0 + (M+P_metric A_old) k0 = 0,
    k0 = -L_live^{-1} r0,
    delta Y_t = B0+A_old(k0).

P remains the instantaneous position response. The live solve is the
same owned operator used in the velocity calculation, not a new
adjustable inertia.

## Two momentum derivative routes

Route A differentiates the explicit moving-measure momentum-rate and
force formulas using a complex directional step. It changes the
radius, Jacobian, field gradient, velocity gradient, physical test
gradient, source clock, and source momentum basis. The metric inputs
are Y+s(P+Y_R U), Y_R+s(P_R+Y_RR U), and
Y_t+s(B0+A_old(k0)+(Y_t)_R U).

Route B independently differentiates the ORIGINAL material momentum
as a four-component (value,s,t,st) jet, rather than differentiating its
already expanded rate formula. For either metric component:

    Y_s_material = P+Y_R U,
    Y_t_material = Y_t+Y_R W,
    Y_st_material = B0+A_old(k0)+P_R W+(Y_t)_R U
                    +Y_RR U W+Y_R V.

Then read the st coefficient of

    p_field = J alpha (v-R_t q_xi/J),
    p_source_wave = -D (q_xi/J) p_field,
    p_clock = m_source v_source/(F sqrt(exp(2 nu)-v_source^2/F)).

This also tests the changing source momentum basis. Y_RR is obtained
by analytic differentiation of the stored cell polynomial, never by
subtracting nearby grid values. At an exact interface the inherited
right-cell trace is used, explicitly and consistently with the owned
solver; no average of coincident endpoint owners is introduced.

## Native force

For the physical native energy E(edges,field,beta), the old force is

    F_field = -E_field,
    F_source = -E_edges . D - E_beta . (beta_R D_native).

Differentiate ALL of edges, field, beta and beta_R. D is fixed in
reference coordinates. Use signed Decimal70 evaluations at 1e-12
and 5e-13 in the position parameter; retain their discrepancy. The
metric coefficient direction comes from P and the moved-point metric
chain, including Y_RR for beta_R. This is not coefficient-only loading.
