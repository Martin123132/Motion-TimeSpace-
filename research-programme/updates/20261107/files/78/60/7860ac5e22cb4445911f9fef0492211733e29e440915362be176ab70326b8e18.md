# Retained failure and paired time-difference repair

The first budget correctly retained the direct MTS refinement channels, but
compared their time-step difference with a subtraction of already-rounded
baseline-plus-MTS totals. Eight checks failed at2.40e-4 to4.58e-4 relative
to that tiny time-difference residual, against the unchanged1e-8 threshold.
The large baseline is about1e5 times the MTS residual. This is a precision
failure of the total-spectrum subtraction, not a physical temperature-curve
failure or permission to relax the test.

Original build_budget.py, results/budget and NUMERICAL-SUMMARY.json remain
untouched. The authoritative corrected analysis uses build_budget_checked.py,
results/budget-checked and NUMERICAL-SUMMARY-CHECKED.json. The checked validator
must verify all eight retained original failures and their hashes.

For fine/coarse total arrays t=fl(b+f), define rounding defects e=t-b-f in
60-digit arithmetic. Then exactly

    (t_fine-t_coarse) - [(b_fine-b_coarse)+(f_fine-f_coarse)]
      = e_fine-e_coarse.

precision_pair.py reconstructs both sides from the saved binary64 inputs,
checks this identity and the rounding scale, and retains the rounded-total
difference as a diagnostic. The correct combined time response is formed from
the separate components before rounding. A separate60-digit template readout
checks cancellation of the unchanged nonzero baseline. No stored baseline,
MTS response, temperature curve, quadrature band or tolerance is modified.

The correction affects the combined-time diagnostic, not the selected budget:
that budget already used the direct MTS time difference, not rounded totals.
Validation compares the original and corrected selected budgets bitwise.
