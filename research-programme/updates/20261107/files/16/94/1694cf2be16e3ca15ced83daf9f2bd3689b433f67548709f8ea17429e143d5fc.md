# Stage185: differentiate the scattering integral, not the interpolation rule

## Coordinates and retained physical approximation

t=ln(theta/theta0), theta=k_B T_star/(m_e c^2), x=E/(k_B T_star).
The tangent below holds dimensionless incoming x and the numerical basis fixed.
It differentiates the existing thermal finite-frequency-shift Compton operator,
with its existing Maxwell-Juttner quadrature, recoil and stimulated factors.
It does not differentiate a fixed physical photon energy instead of x, introduce
a new electron distribution, or derive the full varying-temperature MTS source.
Along the retained expansion, t=-(u-u0), u=ln(a/a_start).

## Thermal event tangent

Let y be a fixed generalized-Laguerre radial node, s=theta*y,
gamma=1+s, beta=sqrt(s(2+s))/(1+s). Then

    gamma_t=s, beta_t=beta/[(2+s)(1+s)].

The normalized radial weights are w_i proportional to
w_i,GL gamma_i sqrt(2+s_i). Thus

    l_i=s_i/gamma_i+s_i/[2(2+s_i)],
    (w_i)_t=w_i(l_i-sum_j w_j l_j).

At fixed electron cosine mu and rest-frame scattering cosine c, put

    d=1-beta*mu, I=gamma*d,
    b_parallel=(beta*mu-beta^2)/d,
    b_transverse=beta*sqrt(1-mu^2)/I,
    O=gamma[1+c*b_parallel+sqrt(1-c^2)*cos(phi)*b_transverse].

I and O are the incoming and outgoing Doppler factors of the original boost
implementation. The event weight is w times the fixed angular weights times d.
Differentiate these expressions by the product/quotient rules, including weight
normalization. Their values and tangents are tested independently against the
original boost code and centered finite differences.

## Frequency redistribution tangent

For fixed incoming x, recoil R, outgoing frequency z and recoil addition E are

    R=[1+theta*x*I*(1-c)]^-1,
    R_t=-R^2*theta*x*(I+I_t)*(1-c),
    z=x*I*R*O,
    z_t=z(I_t/I+R_t/R+O_t/O),
    E=(1-R)^2/R, E_t=R_t*(1-R^-2).

Let n(z)=1/(exp(z)-1), and the original positive pair conductance be

    a=(measure_x/2)*event_weight*(3/2)*R^2
      *[1+n(z)]/[1+n(x)]*(1+c^2+E).

Its logarithmic tangent is

    a_t/a = weight_t/weight + 2R_t/R - n(z)*z_t
            + E_t/(1+c^2+E).

For v=psi(x)-psi(z), v_t=-psi_z(z)*z_t. With the original membership
m=1{minimum<=z<=maximum}, the stiffness and its interior tangent are

    B=-1/2 sum m*a*v*v^T,
    B_t=-1/2 sum m*[a_t*v*v^T+a*(v_t*v^T+v*v_t^T)].

No finite difference of the old bank matrices appears in this derivative.
Symmetry follows term by term. The constant mode has v=v_t=0, preserving
photon-number nullity. The derivative itself need not be negative semidefinite;
only each original finite-temperature collision matrix must be nonpositive.

## Domain-boundary qualification

A finite quadrature with a hard membership mask is only piecewise smooth in t.
The expression above differentiates its fixed-mask branch. For each independently
evaluated temperature, record the exact signed boundary term

    B_live(t)-B_frozen(t)=-1/2 sum [m(t)-m(0)] a(t) v(t)v(t)^T.

This permits separate live and fixed-mask derivative tests. In a continuum
description the derivative of the mask is

    m_t=z_t[delta(z-minimum)-delta(z-maximum)],

which contributes a boundary integral. Fixed discrete event derivatives alone
do not prove that this continuum contribution vanishes. Keep the numerical
domain, dropped weight and crossing contribution visible; do not smooth or
replace the bank during this test.

## Frozen predictions and the gas-energy projection

Use B_pred(t)=B0+t*B_t0. Its small-change residual is normalized to
||B_live(t)-B0|| (and separately to the corresponding frozen-mask difference).
Centered and one-sided quotients test the derivative, at the same1% gate.
Near-zero differences are not silently assigned a successful relative error.

For the saved anchor state and emission matrix, retain

    A=M^-1*r(B-D), C=chi-e*A*T,
    G=-(1+e*A*p)/C, q=p-T*G.

The contribution of scattering-temperature evolution to the slaved gas u-slope
is the linear functional

    L(B_t)=r*e*M^-1*B_t*q/C,

because B_u=-B_t. Rate, emission and photon-history derivatives are separate
terms; they must not be relabelled as this collision-only contribution. The
two old bank secants correspond to B_t=theta0*B_theta,warm/cool. Applying L to
their difference recovers Stage184's approximate gas slope jump.

For each baseline/control, MTS increment/remainder and total cached gas kernel,
multiply the same scalar L by K_g. This checks the contribution on equal terms
and preserves different absolute source amplitudes. Identical fractional errors
are expected by linearity, not independent empirical confirmations. This does
not update the previous full-source curvature gate or any observed spectrum.
