# Compensated acceleration: derivation, repair and cross-method closure

Private local checkpoint, 1 October 2026. This stage addresses the small C2 acceleration column on the existing third spatial grid. All numerical values use the inherited code-normalized units. No new physical coefficient, fitted amplitude, field or phenomenological term was introduced.

## Main result

The large historical discrepancy in the tested acceleration currents is primarily a floating-point cancellation problem. Forming the small compensated acceleration as a polynomial BEFORE evaluation repairs it. Two independent integration organizations now agree on the repaired column: the resolved radial/label quadrature and the historical material quadrature. The result occurs in both MTS and the reference.

This is stronger than noting that an uncertainty was missing. The stage derives the replacement expression, checks it against high-precision evaluation, reproduces the older calculation, swaps one problematic term while holding the old integration points and other formula pieces fixed, and then compares both integration organizations. The remaining source-representation difference is recorded separately; it is much larger than the new integration-order differences and must still be propagated.

## Exact construction

Let x be the local material coordinate, hJ the physical cell width, q=q0+q1*x+q2*x^2 the existing field polynomial, d=d0+d1*x+d2*x^2 the existing acceleration direction, and w=w0+w1*x the map acceleration. Instead of evaluating d-w*q_R as a difference of larger rounded numbers, construct

    e = (N0+N1*x+N2*x^2)/(hJ),
    N0 = hJ*d0-w0*q1,
    N1 = hJ*d1-2*w0*q2-w1*q1,
    N2 = hJ*d2-2*w1*q2.

The layer dependence is also polynomial: the parent layer degree is14, and multiplying existing Chebyshev polynomials gives numerator degree28. Form these coefficients at80decimal digits before casting the already-compensated remainder to binary64. The retained small remainder is not set to zero. This introduces no independent high-degree physical mode.

Two variants are kept distinct. rounded_coefficients uses the existing rounded polynomial coefficients as exact supplied inputs; parent_nodal constructs the polynomial from the actual common-state parent nodal values and forms the separately archived direction-minus-projection subtraction before rounding it. The latter cannot reconstruct precision absent from the archived parent inputs.

Both corrected probes pass14checks on96actual points per branch, over2188cells. Exact polynomial-versus-direct identities agree at about1e-73 relative. New binary64 arithmetic differences are about7.12e-16 for rounded coefficients and1.7–1.8e-16 for parent-nodal reconstruction. Manufactured nonzero and incompatible controls prevent silently projecting the remainder to zero.

## Retained failures and source precision

The first two polynomial probes stopped because the loader assumed an attribute of CoordinateAction while the actual parent uses CommonCoordinateAction. Version2 binds to its actual common nodal coordinates; no parent data were invented.

The first two material replays failed their unchanged gate because the control adapter regrouped map_acceleration*reference_gradient/J as map_acceleration*(reference_gradient/J). Algebraically equal expressions have different rounding in this near-null subtraction. Version2 restores the actual historical multiplication/division order and force contraction in the CONTROL only; modified variants and tolerances are unchanged. These failed attempts are preserved, not overwritten or retrospectively passed. See DERIVATION-20261001-material-replay-arithmetic-binding.md.

The two polynomial source interpretations differ, for reference currents, by about[-2.15e-23,3.37e-24,-3.41e-25]. This spread cannot be discarded because the integration-order differences became much smaller. The canonical parent representation and its propagated coefficient uncertainty remain the next precision issue. The earlier warning history is retained, but the specific cancellation mechanism tested here now has a derived and validated repair.
