Notation: Q_mic denotes the coordinate primitive below. It is NOT scalar momentum density Q_s, electric charge, or the local source q_loc.

## Explicit coupled inverse

Let U=(Re B,Im B), let y contain spectator states, and write the first map as

The code constructs L_B from the two scalar columns of the actual complex coefficient row. It retains the scalar dependence of z,z0. The exact real2x2 inverse is used; no fixed-point iteration or spectator-free geometry is assumed.
