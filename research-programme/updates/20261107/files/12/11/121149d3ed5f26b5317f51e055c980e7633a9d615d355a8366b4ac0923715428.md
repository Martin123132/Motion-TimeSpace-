## Remaining discrepancy identified algebraically

With independently accumulated photon energy E and number N, the first two spectral moments are `S0=N/n0` and `S1=(E-mean*N)/n1`. The frozen stored temperature vector has `t1=n1` and `t0=float(mean*n0)`. Consequently,

`t0*S0+t1*S1-E = (t1/n1-1)*E + (t0/n0-t1*mean/n1)*N`.

Here the coefficient of E is zero, while the coefficient of N is **-9.411384129177006e-17**. The stored multiplication omitted a low part **6.698660524092655e-17** in t0. Near a cubic energy cancellation, the number-to-energy ratio reaches about **1.379 million**, amplifying that representation difference above the unchanged relative threshold. Independently reconstructed primitive moments reproduce the full unrounded discrepancy, frequency integration being held fixed.

`diagnose_basis_representation.py` verifies the factorization, primitive reconstruction and reproduced dense-scan maxima.
