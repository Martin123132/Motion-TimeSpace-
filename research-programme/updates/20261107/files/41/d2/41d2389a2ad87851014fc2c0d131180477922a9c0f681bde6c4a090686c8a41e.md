# Stage108 — frozen projection acceptance

Scope: separate the Stage107 final saved window correction into a
pure-temperature shift and a remaining spectral distortion. No new
collision/source integration, fit, model choice, time evolution or tail extrapolation.

1. Verify the Stage107 seal and all 48 payload hashes before using data.
   Every input must be covered by its saved seal chain; record consumed hashes.
2. Use all 96 retained modes, two backgrounds, both join variants and
   all 16 central/43/54 sensitivity columns. Read independent stored energy.
3. Define the energy-matched temperature convention explicitly.
   Derive the Planck tangent and the physical chi normalization from
   original definitions, not by interpreting scaled coefficients as occupations.
4. Test projector complementarity, idempotence, M-self-adjointness,
   rank, pure-temperature and nonthermal synthetic controls (<1e-11).
   Test reconstruction, norm splitting and reference-temperature invariance
   (<1e-11 relative to each tested vector's norm).
5. Energy cancellation is conditioned: compare discrepancies with
   256 machine eps times sum(abs(moment-row * coefficients)),
   using the original or decomposed vector magnitude as appropriate.
   Do NOT normalize cancellation error solely to a tiny true energy.
6. Verify temperature response against central finite differences of the
   exact Planck law at synthetic steps 1e-4 and 5e-5:
   finer relative derivative error <1e-4 and smaller than coarser.
   This tests the tangent, not a physically tiny temperature by subtraction.
7. Verify finite-domain moment normalizations C_E versus 4, C_N versus
   3 I2/I3 to 1e-9; report missing tails explicitly. These template checks
   do not bound the true spectrum below x=1e-5.
8. Reconstruct delta occupation and specific intensity (W m^-2 Hz^-1 sr^-1)
   and verify their pointwise normalization. Keep the frequency domain
   x in [1e-5,60] and final a=1e-5 explicit, not today's spectrum.
9. Project component differences BEFORE summing their norms/absolute
   temperature responses. No cancellation credit. Retain both 43 and 54.
   Record downstream step, inherited terminal and split-restart sensitivity.
   Contraction and nonzero distortion are outcomes, not imposed pass gates.
10. Retain gas, work and number offsets. Energy plus gas/work must match
    Stage107; number is not constrained to be conserved.
11. All results finite; scripts compile without bytecode. Documents, source
    paths and consumed input hashes validated. All old payloads unchanged.
    Any failed diagnostic stays recorded; no threshold changes after seeing results.
12. Local/private, no GitHub, no subagents, at most two modest workers.
    This stage uses one below-normal single-core worker.
