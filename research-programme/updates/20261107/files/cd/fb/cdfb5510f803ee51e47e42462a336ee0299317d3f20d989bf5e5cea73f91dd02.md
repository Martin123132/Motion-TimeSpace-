# Coupled phase corrector from the retained equations

## 1. Scope

We keep the same background, action parameters, native matter/radiation
equations, G192 hierarchy and first balanced wavelength. The preceding
heavy mean forward failure is retained. The task is to recover its missing
metric micromotion, not to refit an observable or replace the parent theory.

The native rest variables include collision/streaming terms; they are not
assumed to be new canonical variables. The construction is a linear-system
change of variables and a stated approximation to its generator.

## 2. Assemble the actual coupled coefficients

Let X=(w,y), where physical canonical coordinates satisfy Z=S w and y is the
native rest vector. The retained order2 action supplies

    M=M0+V
    M0 X=(J(Kmean w+Bmean l(y)), D y+R rmean w/H).

Here l(y) is the current pressure/shear source, D is the native rest operator
with its constraint-defined metric source subtracted, and R consists of the
actual native expansion/shear source vectors. No future y history enters.

At fixed instantaneous background variables, the nonzero Laurent coefficients
of core, source and feedback give V=sum_(n!=0) M_n exp(i n theta).
The native D term is left as its actual background-dependent value; this is
not a phase average of every microphysical term in the universe.

Set U_n=M_n/(i n mu), theta_N=mu, for the derived oscillating blocks.
Conversions from balanced canonical matrices back to PHYSICAL coordinates
are made before the time derivative. In particular:

    core physical = D_bal (J K_n) D_bal^-1
    source physical = D_bal (J B_n) D_bal
    feedback physical = r_n D_bal^-1 / H.

The last source factor acts on l(y); its coefficient derivative therefore
includes the derivative of that physical linear functional, not just B_n.

The original stress routine gives l4=-a²(P_rest/2-Pi_rest/3)/H and
l6=2a² Pi_rest/H. In the retained regime its support is exactly the baryon
density, photon and massless-neutrino monopoles/quadrupoles and the five
massive-neutrino monopoles/quadrupoles:15 native slots. Native source calls
are used to evaluate these coefficients, with support and general-state
linearity checked against the original routine. Higher multipoles still
evolve through the unchanged native/G192 operator.

## 3. Low-rank representation and inverse

U acts as

    U(w,y)=(A w+B E y, R C w),

where E selects the15 supported rest slots. A is8x8, B is8x15, C is2x8;
R is the original constant rest metric-source matrix. C includes1/H.
No full1066x1066 dense operator is needed.

For x=(I+U)v:

    v_w=(I+A-B E R C)^-1(x_w-B E x_y)
    v_y=x_y-R C v_w.

This is an exact8x8 Schur reduction, not a small-U inverse approximation.
Use fixed initial/native state units, never units chosen from the future
solution. If the induced infinity norm is u<1, the dimensionless inverse
norm is bounded by1/(1-u). The numerical bound here is sampled; not an
interval-certified uniform theorem. Direct inverse roundtrips are separate
algebra checks. No forward integration is authorized if the assembly
representation or inverse gate fails.

## 4. Full derivative, not only fast phase

    U_N=sum_(n!=0) exp(i n theta)
         [M_n+(M_n,N-(mu_N/mu)M_n)/(i n mu)].

M_n,N includes all physical scale, source, H, background amplitude and
coefficient dependence along the retained background. Merely writing
U_N=V loses precisely the terms at issue.

For this numerical test U is evaluated directly from those equations at the
ORIGINAL saved background nodes. A cubic representation of the complete
physical factors is differentiated, including native l(y) coefficients and
1/H. Independent direct finite differences at non-midpoints and two step
sizes, plus every-second-node derivative comparisons, check its accuracy.
This numerical representation is openly distinguished from a closed-form
analytic expression for every native thermal derivative.

## 5. A genuine approximation with a visible remainder

Write U_N=V+W as a definition, with W fully evaluated, not set to zero.
The exact conjugate operator is

    Rexact=(I+U)^-1[M(I+U)-U_N].

Using Rexact alone is just a coordinate-equivalent control and supplies no
reduction claim. Choose instead the stated first corrected generator

    R1=M0+M U-U M0-W
      =M+M U-U M0-U_N.

Its discarded term is explicit, without a commutativity assumption:

    (I+U)R1+U_N-M(I+U)=U(R1-M0).

The corresponding exact-generator difference is

    R1-Rexact=(I+U)^-1 U(R1-M0).

Thus ||R1-Rexact|| <= ||(I+U)^-1|| ||U|| ||R1-M0|| in one consistent
induced norm. This controls the pointwise generator error, not automatically
the integrated trajectory; a finite-time bound would also require evolution
operator/stability bounds. Direct matched forward tests remain required.

R1 retains residual phase dependence. No speedup or phase-free effective
fluid is claimed. The cold branch has U=V=0, hence R1=M0 exactly.
