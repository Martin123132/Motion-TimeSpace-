# Stage81 sources and provenance

Primary derivation source: Andrea Ravenni and Jens Chluba,2020,
[The double Compton process in astrophysical plasmas](https://arxiv.org/html/2005.06941v1),
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1088/1475-7516/2020/10/025).
Read equations4,34–35,54–55 and section4.4. Archived unchanged at
`../scaled-spectral-evolution-20261007/sources/2005.06941v1.html`.
The web source was opened again during this stage. In particular its discussion
of stimulated infrared matching prevents interpreting our resolved rate as
an inclusive transport coefficient.

Independent historical matrix form:
[Chluba, Sazonov and Sunyaev, soft double Compton emission](https://arxiv.org/html/astro-ph/0611172),
archived at `../emitting-wave-response-20261008/sources/astro-ph-0611172.html`.
This source supplies a cross-check of amplitude conventions, not a second
observational data set.

Inherited isotropic-source approximation:
[Chluba and Sunyaev, spectral distortion evolution](https://arxiv.org/html/1109.6552),
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2966.2011.19786.x), archived at
`../emitting-thermal-background-20261008/sources/1109.6552.html`.
The denominator1+14.16theta is retained as a reference scalar correction;
the pure-Gould runs measure its effect instead of presenting it as exact.

Unchanged hydrogen/helium BR inputs:
[Cloudy Gaunt-factor tables](https://data.nublado.org/gauntff/), local files
`../emitting-thermal-background-20261008/sources/gauntff_merged_Z01.dat` and
`../emitting-thermal-background-20261008/sources/gauntff_merged_Z02.dat`.
The prior source audits remain authoritative for these reused inputs.

adds is the angular projection, event-consistent assembly, mixed derivative,
