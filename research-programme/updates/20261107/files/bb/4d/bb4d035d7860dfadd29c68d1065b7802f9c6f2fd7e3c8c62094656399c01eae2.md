## Controlled universe and conventions

Use N=ln(a), tau=M t, and a synchronous coordinate system comoving with the remaining pressureless component. Its density contrast fixes the trace metric perturbation, h_L=-2 delta_c. No scalar anisotropic stress is included. All wavenumbers are comoving Mpc^-1, not h/Mpc. A bar on k denotes c k/H0. The three adiabatic modes have k=0.1,1,5; the independent field mode is evaluated at k=5.

This subsystem includes perfect radiation, cold matter and the canonical heavy scalar. It does not include a photon/baryon acoustic hierarchy, neutrino free streaming, recombination, nonlinear halo evolution, or an observed initial power spectrum. After N=-5, the archived slow-scalar background is used, with its perturbations neglected as in Stage50's subhorizon calculation. Before N=-5 its density is held at its value at that boundary, avoiding backwards extrapolation of a late scalar solution. That density is0.8171321245 in critical-density units and is negligible throughout the oscillation matching interval. H is continuous at the bridge; this is not an exact all-sector solution through late dark-energy domination.

Unlike Stage50's strictly identical-background comparator, the present candidate and cold control have physically different early histories: the candidate heavy field is initially frozen, whereas cold matter redshifts immediately. They share late matter/radiation budgets, the slow background prescription and the initial curvature normalization. This early difference is part of the physical hypothesis, not a baseline mismatch to conceal. The previous SN/BAO parameter values are reused, but their likelihood score is not assigned to a new full finite-mass cosmology without recalculating relevant observables, especially sound-horizon-dependent ones.

## Exact scalar and Einstein-fluid equations before matching

Set u=phi/(sqrt(6) M_Pl), w=du/dtau, d=delta_phi/(sqrt(6) M_Pl), s=dd/dtau. Densities below are divided by3 M_Pl^2 H0^2. Write omega=nu/E, K^2=kbar^2/(a^2 E^2), g=d(delta_c)/dN, and u_r=theta_r/(aH).

```text
R_h = nu^2 (u^2+w^2),       P_h = nu^2 (w^2-u^2)
delta R_h = 2 nu^2 (u d+w s)
delta P_h = 2 nu^2 (w s-u d)
J_h = (R_h+P_h) theta_h/(aH) = 2 nu^2 K^2 w d/omega

R_c = (1-F) Omega_m/a^3,    R_r = Omega_r/a^4
E^2 = R_c+R_r+R_h+R_dark,frozen
h_E = dlnE/dN = (-3 R_c/2-2 R_r-3 nu^2 w^2)/E^2

u_N = omega w
w_N = -3w-omega u
d_N = omega s
s_N = -3s-(omega+K^2/omega)d+w g

delta_c,N = g
g_N = -(2+h_E)g + 3[R_c delta_c+2R_r delta_r+delta R_h+3delta P_h]/(2E^2)
delta_r,N = 4(g-u_r)/3
u_r,N = -(1+h_E)u_r+K^2 delta_r/4
eta_N = 3[4R_r u_r/3+J_h]/(2E^2 K^2)
```

Both field density and momentum are retained; no division by the oscillating background field velocity is needed. The independently monitored00 constraint is

```text
K^2 eta+g+3(R_c delta_c+R_r delta_r+delta R_h)/(2E^2)=0.
```

Differentiating the scalar stress using the field equations gives, exactly in this subsystem,

```text
R_h,N+3(R_h+P_h)=0
delta R_h,N+3(delta R_h+delta P_h)+J_h-(R_h+P_h)g=0
J_h,N+(5+h_E)J_h-K^2 delta P_h=0.
```

These imply C_N=-(2+2h_E)C for the00-constraint residual C when all included stresses are conserved. This supplies a physical consistency check independent of matching a desired transfer ratio. It is not a claim about conservation of every unfinished sector of the full parent action.

## Regular early states, rather than guessed late densities

In radiation domination E approximately sqrt(Omega_r)/a^2. A regular frozen field has w=-omega u/5 at leading order. Choose a_i=1e-9 and the common adiabatic mode

```text
delta_c = A K^2,     g=2 delta_c
delta_r = 4 delta_c/3,    u_r=A K^4/9
d = -u omega^2 delta_c/105,    s=6d/omega.
```

The factor105 is not fitted. The regular field perturbation scales as a^6. In the radiation-era Klein-Gordon equation its leading derivative term is42d; the metric forcing is -2u omega^2 delta_c/5. Thus42d=-2u omega^2 delta_c/5. The exact finite-start00 constraint defines eta, and all perturbations are rescaled to eta_i=-4A in both candidate and control. The finite-start expansion is leading-order, not an exact finite-a initial solution; starting twice as early is an explicit test.

A=1e-5 is only a linear basis normalization. Final basis responses of order unity do not represent simulated nonlinear objects or a measured primordial amplitude; the basis can be rescaled arbitrarily small.

For the independent field-isocurvature basis choose d_i=A u_i and the regular leading derivative s_i=(-K^2/3-omega^2/5)d_i/omega. With D=delta R_h/E^2, its compensating leading cold/radiation response is delta_c=-D/4, g=4delta_c, delta_r=4delta_c/3, u_r=K^2 delta_r/20. This removes the leading curvature contribution; residual finite-start terms are tested by earlier starts. The amplitude and correlation of this independent basis with the adiabatic one remain unspecified. Its ratio to an adiabatic cold control is a response normalization, not a predicted isocurvature fraction.

## Abundance and matching without resetting the perturbations

The early homogeneous displacement is calibrated to the chosen late heavy abundance. At a=0.0006, use the adiabatic oscillator invariant estimator

```text
I = nu^2 a^3 [u^2+(w+3E u/(2nu))^2].
```

This removes the leading expansion-induced phase term but is not an exact conserved quantity. The displacement is iterated until I=F Omega_m. Independent phase-averaged densities at the switch test the approximation and integration error. Calibrating an occupation is not deriving it.

The exact field is followed to a=0.0006, and switches at a=0.0002,0.0003,0.00045 are compared. At a switch, sample801 points over tau_* plus/minus8pi. Fit a quartic slowly varying part, together with quadratic envelopes multiplying cos(2tau),sin(2tau), and a fourth harmonic. The constant slow coefficient defines the averaged quantity at the switch. Windows of plus/minus4pi and12pi test sensitivity. This is our numerical harmonic projection, not an implementation of a cited authors' optimized algorithm or accuracy guarantee.

Project a^3 R_h, a^3 delta R_h, a^3 delta P_h, a^3 J_h and the metric/fluid variables. Normalize the latter scalar stresses by the averaged a^3 R_h, obtaining delta_h, p_h=delta P_h/R_h and q_h=J_h/R_h. Thus density, momentum and pressure all come from the field; none are set equal to cold matter at redshift99. Pressure closure is independently checked against the directly projected stress, using a perturbation norm that does not blow up at density zero crossings.

## Averaged continuation and return to the existing late calculation

In a locally slowly varying background, the scalar perturbation's relative phase frequency is sqrt(M^2+k^2/a^2)-M. Averaging the background/perturbation cross terms gives the ratio of slow pressure to slow density (r-1)/(r+1), with r=sqrt(1+kappa^2), kappa=kbar/(a nu). Consequently

```text
c_s^2 = kappa^2/[sqrt(1+kappa^2)+1]^2
p_h = c_s^2 [delta_h+3q_h/K^2]
delta_h,N = g-q_h-3p_h
q_h,N = -(2+h_E)q_h+K^2 p_h.
```

The3q_h/K^2 term converts to the rest-frame density for a background treated as dust. Curvature corrections of order(H/M)^2 and departures of the averaged background from dust are not retained in this fluid approximation; their acceptability is checked numerically across the switches. The radiation and metric trace equations retain the heavy contribution R_h(delta_h+3p_h). The momentum constraint uses R_h q_h. The cold comparator sets all matter pressure to zero and uses the identical initial-curvature convention and numerical methods.

At redshift99 the state supplied to the already sealed Stage50 propagation matrix is exactly

```text
(delta_c, g, delta_h, g-q_h-3p_h).
```

Its final density is compared with the radiation-retaining continuation. This is a direct reuse of the existing mathematical ladder. In the subhorizon, nonrelativistic limit c_s^2 approaches kappa^2/4; neglecting the smaller relativistic terms returns Stage50's Q=kbar^4/(4nu^2 a^4 E^2).

Reported density ratios also include the gauge-invariant total matter combination Delta_m=(1-F)delta_c+F delta_h+3Fq_h/K^2. At the final tested scales its difference from the synchronous total density is negligible. The derivative of synchronous delta_c is not misidentified as a directly observed peculiar-velocity divergence.

In particular, the saved late eta is not a full-sector lensing prediction: the slow scalar's momentum perturbation is absent from its evolution. The exact00-constraint tests apply to the early frozen-dark subsystem. Completing late metric observables requires restoring that sector as well as the radiation hierarchy; numerical precision on the current matter ratios does not remove these physical approximations.
