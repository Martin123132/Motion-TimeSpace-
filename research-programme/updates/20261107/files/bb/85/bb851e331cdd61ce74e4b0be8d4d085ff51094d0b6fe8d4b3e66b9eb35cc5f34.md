## What is established, and what the estimate assumes

The explicit channel identities and norm inequalities give a
conditional uniform response bound on a fixed finite N interval.
Sufficient conditions include bounded density fractions, scalar
amplitudes/H, background logarithmic derivatives, native pressure-row
norm, and kappa_Omega, with positive nondegenerate norm weights.
See DERIVATION.md section6 for the full regime.

This does NOT demand kappa_Omega->0 or that Newtonian gravitational
growth disappear. Nor is a large upper bound outside that regime
evidence of physical instability. No critical Jeans threshold is
claimed.

These conditions still have to be combined with the physical current,
shear, preparation and final dual-readout bounds to obtain a uniform
small observable error. That is the next calculation, now with explicit
coupling functions rather than an unspecified gain.
