## What this test found

The fine inner integration and the65-epoch source interpolation are different
numerical controls. The inner-step result remains valid at its fixed source
sampling;this test shows that the source sampling itself is too coarse to
regard the saved tiny gas correction as settled.

Fresh quarter states show large source differences in BOTH baseline and MTS.
Their largest component-normalized differences occur at the first quarter
of interval0:about74.9%baseline and88.4%MTS,not merely roundoff.
Radiation corrections move modestly,but the much smaller MTS gas correction
moves substantially and reverses sign in the narrow matching window.

This is a numerical diagnosis,not a new empirical prediction or a verdict on
the field theory. The percentages describe changes in the conditional
correction,not changes of that size in the whole gas temperature or Universe.
