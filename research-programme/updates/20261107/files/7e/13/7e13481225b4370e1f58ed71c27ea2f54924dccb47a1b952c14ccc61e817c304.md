## 1. Stated physical extension, not passive conjugation

physical radii, retaining the original native support, index template B,
and positive index-sampling S. For physical field-cell edges R_e, physical
native knots X_j, and nodal field q, use

    U_G = (1/2) sum_a (S beta)_a f_a^2,
    f=B*k, beta(R)=R^2*N(R)*sqrt(F(R)).

Each physical cell is affine from the archived reference cell; its midpoint
moves as the mean of its two edges. This preserves the stated P2 cell
shape, rather than silently using midpoint formulas on skewed triples.
All native knots are shared field edges on both retained refined grids.
Fields at the source remain pinned; the source slope jump is compensated
but its curvature jump and ALL ordinary interface atoms are retained.

This is an explicit geometrically rebuilt candidate using the inherited
kernel, not a proof of unique parent ownership. Under a global affine
map R=J*x+a, the factor scales D_R=J^(-1/2)D_x, so beta*D_R^2 agrees
with the old (beta/J)*D_x^2 action. Check agreement at the saved nearly
translated seed before comparing force derivatives. Away from that seed
the source and geometry derivatives may genuinely differ. No coefficient
is chosen to match the measured radial residual.

## 2. Differentiate the complete native kernel

For cell width H, the endpoint slopes and curvature are

    l=(-3q_L+4q_M-q_R)/H, r=(q_L-4q_M+3q_R)/H,
    c=4(q_L-2q_M+q_R)/H^2.

At each ordinary face z, A=l_right-r_left and C=c_right-c_left.
Set A=0 at the source by the inherited hinge compensation, but retain C.
For four physical native knots X_j, h=(X_3-X_0)/3 and

    a_j=6h^3/product_(m!=j)(X_j-X_m), p_j=(X_j-z)_+,
    Psi_1=sum_j a_j*p_j, Psi_2=(1/2)sum_j a_j*p_j^2,
    k=h^(-1/2)sum_faces(A*Psi_1+C*Psi_2).

The full derivative is

    delta k=-(delta h/(2h))*k
      +h^(-1/2)sum_faces(delta A*Psi_1+A*delta Psi_1
                        +delta C*Psi_2+C*delta Psi_2),
    delta a_j/a_j=3delta h/h-sum_(m!=j)(delta X_j-delta X_m)/(X_j-X_m),
    delta p_j=H(X_j-z)*(delta X_j-delta z).

Coincident native knots and faces are the SAME geometric variable: they
move together. Do not create fictitious crossings or discard the physical
source trace term. At fixed q, delta l=-l*delta H/H,
delta r=-r*delta H/H, delta c=-2c*delta H/H. Reverse this expression
to obtain the complete field covector and edge/native-knot shape
covectors without constructing a dense stiffness matrix.

The kernel-only shape covector g_e includes scattered native-knot terms.
It obeys sum_e g_e=0 under rigid translation. It defines a genuine weak
shape stress T_kernel(R)=-sum_(R_e<R)g_e, since

    integral T_kernel*eta' = sum_e g_e*eta(R_e)

for fixed endpoints. This stress comes from U_G, not from minus an
observed Ward residual. The coefficient contribution is separately
load_j*beta_r(X_j)*delta X_j, load=S^T(f^2/2).

## 3. Actual source force and current term

On the retained source-fitted map, W_e=d_e*V and physical midpoint speeds
are interpolated from edges. Contract the same shape covector with d to
derive the Gram contribution to the source force. No independent support
inertia is introduced. Let dot f_shape be the factor derivative at fixed
q from these actual geometry velocities. Then the native atom rates due
to geometry are

    a_j=beta_j*[S^T(f*dot f_shape)]_j + beta_r,j*W_j*load_j,
    F_b,Gram*V=-sum_j a_j.

The field rate is g_j=beta_j*[S^T(f*D_R*qdot)]_j. Apart from explicit
Eulerian metric-time exchange, dot h_j=g_j+a_j, h_j=beta_j*load_j.
Localizing the same action's source power gives the shape exchange
current

    J_shape(R)=sum_j a_j*[H(R-b)-H(R-X_j)].

It is zero outside the source/native support, and its weak test is
integral xi'(R)J_shape(R)dR=sum_j a_j*(xi(X_j)-xi(b)). Together with
the field-force cut term, g_j and moving-atom advection this is the
action's localized energy-current construction. In the inherited
horizontal prescription, S^T(f*dot f_shape) is the newly retained term
in the time derivative of the Gram coefficient covector. Derive/check
the source power identity; do not simply assert that it cancels the old
radial residual. New action forces require a consistent coupled solve
before an updated whole-system radial verdict.
