## 5. Constructive next stage: no-subtraction background metric-time derivative

The radial equations are Y_R=f(R,Y,E,G,H,rho,V), Y=(mu,nu). Differentiating at fixed physical R gives

    (Y_t)_R = f_Y Y_t + B,
    B = f_E E_t + f_G G_t + f_H H_t + f_rho rho_t + f_V V_t.

ALL FIVE local source columns are now derived and implemented, with no division by vanishing V or rho. They pass symbolic checks and independent complex differentiation of the actual original radial_rhs; the full simultaneous geometry/source derivative discrepancy is2.169e-19 on the verification sample. The detailed forcing and boundary formulas are in `DERIVATION-20260928-exact-background-radial-time-forcing.md`. Its D_t denotes the explicit source part at held-fixed metric; metric-time terms are supplied separately by f_Y Y_t.

The remaining construction is concrete:

1. Assemble E_t,G_t using the moving-cell weak transport identity, including every interface term, and H_t from the native pushforward and the existing exact Dq tangent.
2. Include source transport rho_t=-partial_R(rho V), source acceleration and the inherited boundary conditions. Solve the coupled radial/old-Euler tangent, rather than subtracting two near-identical radial solutions.
3. Reevaluate the SAME reference/MTS currents and support force. Then use a resolved constraint operator for the position/velocity support-history correction.

For a continuous Y across a moving radial interface, differentiation gives the exact jump condition [Y_t]=-Rdot_face[f]. It must be represented through weak forcing or primitive jumps whenever [f] is nonzero; blindly feeding only smooth nodal derivatives to the existing tangent solver would omit it. Where the actual averaged source is continuous, this jump is zero, not an extra term to invent.

For a native Gram pushforward H=sum_i integral a_i delta(R-R_i) dlambda,

    <H_t,h> = sum_i integral [a_dot_i h(R_i)+a_i V_i h_R(R_i)] dlambda,
    a_i=w U_i/J_i,
    (Dq)_t=D qdot + Ddot q.

This is the route to an actual background derivative with transport included. The local forcing is implemented; its full weak projection/solve is the next numerical stage. The support-history position block and compensated projection time derivatives are not silently filled in by the pure-acceleration calculation.
