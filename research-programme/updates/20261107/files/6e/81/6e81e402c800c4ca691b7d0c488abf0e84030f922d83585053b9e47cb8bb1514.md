## The useful result

The earlier MTS-heavy score advantage is **not robust to giving every model
the same residual galaxy-velocity freedom**. On the primary BOSS data cut the
retained heavy branch and LCDM now score almost equally. This identifies a
specific degeneracy to resolve; it is not grounds to retune the parent field
until it wins, nor does it show the field equations are invalid.

| Primary P0/P2, .02<k<.10 h/Mpc | Stage57: no extra residual | Stage58: Gaussian residual | Stage58: Laplace residual |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| MTS-heavy benchmark | 38.4834 | 34.4878 | 34.4740 |
| Slow/cold control | 41.2819 | 34.8477 | 34.8226 |
| LCDM control | 45.0120 | 34.2319 | 34.1858 |

These are Hartlap-corrected chi-squared values for the same 32 data entries.
Stage57 used four tracer parameters; Stage58 uses six: bias, white noise and
one-particle residual displacement variance in each of the two survey caps.
All branches get identical bounds and fitting rules. Field, primordial and
cosmological inputs remain fixed, including the inherited F=.5 and H0=70.
These are conditional benchmark scores, not refitted cosmologies or a formal
model-evidence calculation. The Gaussian heavy-minus-LCDM difference changes
from -6.5286 to +0.2559; the Laplace version gives +0.2881. Neither number is
a decisive distinction between the physical branches.

The added freedom improves LCDM by 10.7802, the cold control by 6.4342 and the
heavy branch by 3.9956. That is why it matters to apply the same correction to
the controls rather than treating the original heavy advantage as secure.

## What was derived, and what was fitted

`58-residual-displacement-derivation.md` starts from number conservation under
the redshift-space coordinate map. Independent symmetric residual displacements
multiply the distinct-tracer power by the square of their characteristic
function. The leading correction is **-s2*k_parallel^2**, with s2 the
one-particle displacement variance and pair variance 2*s2. The angular
coefficients through ell6 are derived exactly, not fitted. Same-particle
Poisson contacts remain undamped.

Gaussian and Laplace displacement laws share that leading coefficient but
differ at fourth order. Their finite-amplitude forms are explicit stochastic
assumptions. The fitted numerical s2 is a tracer nuisance: this stage does
not claim the parent field determines the residual galaxy distribution.

The existing parent density/velocity transfers do independently fix linear
**coherent** velocity-displacement moments, with no new fitted coefficient:

| Branch | One-point LOS variance [(Mpc/h)^2] | RMS [Mpc/h] | Pair variance at r=100 Mpc/h, transverse | Pair variance at r=100 Mpc/h, parallel |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| MTS-heavy | 10.75107 | 3.27888 | 16.46205 | 21.67894 |
| Slow/cold | 11.51526 | 3.39341 | 17.96056 | 23.25280 |
| LCDM | 12.36471 | 3.51635 | 19.28559 | 24.96831 |

Pair columns also have units (Mpc/h)^2. These are unweighted, linear cb
statistics at z=.38, integrated over the qualified finite physical band
1e-5..5 Mpc^-1. Pair variance is zero at coincident points and changes with
separation and line-of-sight orientation. Upper-cut checks are retained in
`results/derived-moments/status.json`; high-q convergence of this linear
integral is not a nonlinear-galaxy calibration.

Do not insert the coherent one-point number as the fitted residual variance.
Doing so discards separation correlations and can double-count the coherent
motion. The measured distinction between those quantities is the next useful
derivation target.

## Verification and remaining approximation

`results/validation/status.json` records 628 passing checks. They include 12
exact rational angular coefficients, independently drawn residual pairs,
36 off-grid/direct-response parameter injections from nontruth starting points,
independent fixed-variance profile scans, finite-difference Jacobians, the
Stage57 zero-variance limit, covariance dimensions and Bessel angular identities.
These are controlled numerical/stochastic tests, **not N-body or galaxy-catalog
mock validation**. No new physical accuracy follows merely from their count.

- Largest table/direct distance: 5.92e-10 covariance-weighted sigma across all fits.
- Largest 32/64-angle difference on primary fits: 2.50e-12 sigma.
- Largest cubic/PCHIP transfer difference on primary fits: .004996 sigma, below the predeclared .01 numerical gate.
- Coherent-moment resolution change: below 1.31e-9 of the one-point variance; interpolation change below 2.37e-5.
- A +100% change to the retained input response above k=.2 h/Mpc shifts each fit by less than .006963 sigma. This probes that particular tail, not all nonlinear errors.

The released window accepts only ell0/2/4. The residual response generates
ell6 already at leading order, and AP also generates higher multipoles.
At fitted optima ell6/8 reach at most .0701% of the monopole over the relevant
input fitting bands. That small input ratio is **not** a bound on omitted
survey-window leakage: the missing higher-ell coupling columns have not been
supplied. Full input modes through .3995 h/Mpc remain in the retained columns.

Constant density bias, omitted field loops and an independent residual PDF
are still approximations. The data test is meaningful as a like-for-like
robustness comparison within that model family, not a complete nonlinear
description. No cosmological parameter or field abundance was retuned.

## Next bounded derivation

Use the retained density and velocity transfers to derive the separation-
dependent **density-weighted pair streaming moments**, including the
density-velocity cross-correlation and mean infall. First derive them under
an explicit jointly Gaussian linear-field construction and recover the
Kaiser/Stage57 linear limit. Do not label that construction a derived
nonlinear galaxy PDF, or force positivity where a linear tracer model fails.

This puts the computed coherent pair variance to work rather than replacing
one missing constant with another fitted constant. Apply the same construction
to heavy, cold and LCDM, and identify any genuinely irreducible residual
coefficient. Before a sharper BOSS ranking, source or construct the matching
higher-multipole survey response; if it cannot be justified, keep the missing
coupling explicit rather than treating the small input ratio as a proof.

Do not enlarge the cosmology/abundance fit yet. The useful physics question is
which part of the apparent suppression the parent fields predict and which
part the galaxy-velocity nuisance can mimic.
