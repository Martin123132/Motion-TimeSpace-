# Exact flux form for the remaining force-integration gap

This is an algebraic alternative for evaluating the SAME force, not a
new coupling or a completed numerical repair.

At fixed material label let g=q_xi/J, T=v-Wg, and define

    Phi = alpha*T*W + beta*g.

The old field force on a reference basis function N_i is

    F_i = -integral Phi * N_i,xi dxi.

Write the material position derivative of the flux as

    H = delta alpha*T*W + alpha*delta T*W
        +alpha*T*delta W +delta beta*g+beta*delta g.

Then, with continuous N_i and the partition containing ALL material
faces and ALL radial-polynomial cuts,

    delta F_i = sum_cells integral H_xi*N_i dxi
                +sum_internal_faces [H]*N_i
                +H(inner)*N_i(inner)-H(outer)*N_i(outer).

The jump is right minus left. This follows by integrating each cell
by parts and collecting the two traces. It keeps the source-anchor
test zero where the retained finite-element space pins that node.
Do not drop outer terms when an outer nodal test is nonzero.

In the cell interiors this can instead be evaluated as

    integral dR (delta Phi_R + (delta J/J)*Phi_R)*N_i,
    Phi_R = alpha_R*T*W + alpha*T_R*W + alpha*T*W_R
            +beta_R*g+beta*g_R,
    T_R = v_R-W_R*g-W*g_R.

For the piecewise affine position map,

    g_R = q_xixi/J^2,
    delta g_R = e_xixi/J^2 - 2*g_R*delta J/J.

The variation delta Phi_R is the material one, including evaluation
at R+delta R; it therefore uses the already available Y_RR and P_R.
The momentum-rate part and the solved live operator are unchanged.

Why this is worth the next numerical test: the weak expression
multiplies the flux by a large, sign-changing derivative of a basis
function in very small cells. The equivalent expression multiplies
the differentiated flux by the basis VALUE and carries explicit face
loads. It can reduce cancellation, but its benefit is not presumed.
Artificial radial cuts must be included if their stored polynomial
traces give a nonzero H jump; retaining only material faces would
silently change the represented weak integral.

Acceptance remains comparison with the original weak force, both
quadrature resolutions, and the re-evaluated old Euler residual. This
identity does not cure the outstanding baseline-momentum discrepancy
by assertion. The successful B0 response, independent native-energy
test, and diagnostic k0 solve should be reused rather than rerun.
