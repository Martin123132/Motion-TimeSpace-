# Tensor Ward identity and the soft response

3 October 2026. Private daughter calculation in the isolated condensate candidate.

The growing low-momentum inverse-propagator response now has a derived explanation: the two-phase-wave logarithm. Its radial and mixed coefficients reproduce the previous numerical first rung without fitting. The leading nonanalytic matrix has a factorization that cancels its apparent divergence in the associated propagated shear contribution. A spatially covariant temporary regulator, varied together with its stress source, leaves the tested weak static collective eigenvalues small.

These are connected results, not an assumption that a phonon is a graviton. They concern the chosen complex-scalar candidate, a one-loop Ward calculation and a bare cubic-exchange static ladder. The next derivation can use the identified soft structure instead of interpreting a growing unweighted component as a new collective mode.

## Hard cutoff Ward identity

At one loop the cubic bubble is
\[
 \Sigma_{ab}(p)=-\tfrac12\int_k
 C_{acd}C_{bef}G_{ce}(k)G_{df}(p-k).
\]
The quartic tadpole has no spatial l=2 derivative on a homogeneous isotropic background. For a constant inverse-spatial-metric variation with
\[
 h_{ij}=\operatorname{diag}(-1/2,-1/2,1),\qquad
 p\parallel\hat z,\qquad u=\hat p\cdot\hat k,
\]
define
\[
 k_h^2=k^2P_2(u),\quad
 q_h^2=p^2-2pk u+k^2P_2(u),\quad q=p-k.
\]
Since delta G=-G(delta G^{-1})G, the two bubble insertions are
\[
 V_1=\tfrac12\int_{|k|<L} C[k_h^2Z_kG_k^2]C G_q,\qquad
 V_2=\tfrac12\int_{|k|<L} C G_k C[q_h^2Z_qG_q^2].
\]
Here contracted indices are exactly those in the bubble; Z=1 for the bare propagator. A cutoff defined by the deformed spatial metric must itself be varied:
\[
 \delta\Theta(L-\sqrt{k_\gamma^2})
 =-\frac{k_h^2}{2|k|}\delta(L-|k|).
\]
This gives the positive surface term
\[
 B=\frac{L^3}{4}\int\frac{d\zeta\,d\Omega}{(2\pi)^4}
 P_2(u)\,C G(\zeta,L) C G(\xi-\zeta,|p-L\hat k|).
\]
Consequently the constant-shear Ward identity is
\[
 \boxed{p^2\partial_{p^2}\Sigma=V_1+V_2+B.}
\]
The numerical slice calculation leaves d zeta unintegrated; after azimuthal integration its measure is 1/(8 pi^3). The direct derivative inserts Z_q p(p-ku) in the second line.

The previous collapsed first rung is 2V_1. With a one-line hard cutoff its correction is therefore
\[
 p^2\partial_{p^2}\Sigma-2V_1=(V_2-V_1)+B.
\]
There are TWO effects: routing and the cutoff surface. It would be incorrect to repair only the surface or demand V_1=V_2 at each fixed internal frequency. On an infinite translation-invariant domain, exchanging k and p-k also exchanges zeta with xi-zeta; the equality holds after pairing/integrating those frequencies.

Nine hard-boundary fixtures, including bare and regulated propagators, agree with the completed identity to at most 2.52e-18 absolute error. Omitting the surface instead leaves errors between 4.17e-9 and 2.63e-6 in these fixtures. Four infinite-spatial paired-frequency fixtures check the routing collapse separately. These are numerical checks in addition to the analytic variation, not a numerical proof at every momentum.

## Temporary regulator and matching source

Use a field-independent, internal-U(1)-scalar Euclidean regulator
\[
 r_\Lambda(s)=s^3/\Lambda^4,\quad t=s+r_\Lambda(s),\quad
 G_\Lambda=\frac{1}{t(t+m^2)+4\mu^2\xi^2}
 \begin{pmatrix}t&2\mu\xi\\-2\mu\xi&t+m^2\end{pmatrix}.
\]
It is defined on the co-rotating fluctuation fields and extended under the spatial deformation by s=xi^2+gamma^{ij}k_i k_j. It vanishes at zero frequency and momentum. Thus it preserves the homogeneous saddle and the internal symmetry used for the tadpole identity.

This is a calculation regulator, not a new physical MTS term. Its finite-Lambda higher-derivative poles are not physical predictions. No regulator-removal or renormalized parameter matching is asserted.

The consistent metric vertex is
\[
 Z_\Lambda(s)=1+3s^2/\Lambda^4,\qquad
 J_\Lambda(k)=k_h^2Z_\Lambda(s)I,\qquad
 \delta_hG_\Lambda=-k_h^2Z_\Lambda G_\Lambda^2.
\]
Four finite metric differences verify this source. Deliberately omitting Z gives an error of at least 0.00171 in the chosen fixtures.

Normalize the response by
\[
 F(p)=p^2 Z_\Lambda(p)h(p)Y_{2m}(\hat p),\qquad h_{\rm bare}=I.
\]
The preceding ladder is then regulated by replacing each G with G_Lambda and multiplying its h-space integrand by Z_Lambda(k)/Z_Lambda(p). There is no new adjustable tensor coupling. The analytic self-cell logarithm is retained. Near angular coincidence, subtract the bare singular part using the exact identity
\[
 G_\Lambda-G_0=-r_\Lambda G_\Lambda G_0
\]
and integrate only the smooth difference numerically. At self coincidence the Z ratio is one. Away from coincidence ordinary angular quadrature suffices.

This completes the regulator and source for the bare ladder. It does not turn that ladder into a fully self-consistent conserving approximation: dressing the propagator, kernel and background together remains a separate calculation.

## The background correction protects the phase mode

Let
\[
 C_\sigma=\operatorname{diag}(C_{111},\gamma),\quad
 C_\phi=\begin{pmatrix}0&\gamma\\\gamma&0\end{pmatrix},\quad
 D_{\phi\phi}=\operatorname{diag}((C_{111}-2\gamma)/v,3\gamma/v).
\]
For arbitrary scalar t in the regulated propagator, direct matrix algebra gives the pointwise identity
\[
 \tfrac12\operatorname{tr}
 [D_{\phi\phi}G-GC_\phi GC_\phi]
 =\frac{1}{2v}\operatorname{tr}(C_\sigma G).
\]
Therefore, including both the quartic tadpole and cubic bubble,
\[
 \Sigma_{\phi\phi}(0)=T_\sigma/v,\qquad
 \delta v=-T_\sigma/m^2,\qquad
 \boxed{\Sigma_{\phi\phi}(0)+\gamma\,\delta v=0}
\]
to one-loop order at fixed mu. No independent phase mass needs tuning.

The full regulated four-dimensional integrals use R in [0,infinity) through R=Lambda x/(1-x), angular measure R^3 sin^2(theta)/(2 pi^3) on theta in [0,pi/2], and orders 32,64,96.

## Derivation of the low momentum logarithm

Put a=2mu/m^2 and
\[
 P_s^2=p^2+\xi^2/c_s^2,\quad
 F(k)=\frac{1}{k^2+\zeta^2/c_s^2},\quad
 G_s(k)=\begin{pmatrix}
 1/m^2-a^2\zeta^2F&a\zeta F\\
 -a\zeta F&F
 \end{pmatrix}.
\]
Keep the leading nonanalytic contribution from two phase denominators. The cubic contraction fixes its matrix numerator, not merely its radial entry:
\[
 \Sigma_{\rm IR}(p)
 =-A_{\rm IR}\log\frac{\kappa^2}{P_s^2}\,M(\xi),
 \qquad M(\xi)=
 \begin{pmatrix}1&-a\xi\\a\xi&-a^2\xi^2\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad
 \boxed{A_{\rm IR}=\frac{\gamma^2 c_s}{32\pi^2}}.
\]
The scale kappa parametrizes matching/local terms, not a fitted multiplier. The temporal rescaling supplies c_s; the Bose factor supplies 1/2. The four-dimensional massless bubble satisfies
\[
 \partial_{P_s^2}B(P_s)=-\frac{1}{16\pi^2P_s^2}.
\]
For example Feynman parametrization uses
\[
 \int\frac{d^4l}{(2\pi)^4}(l^2+\Delta)^{-3}
 =\frac{1}{32\pi^2\Delta},\quad
 \Delta=x(1-x)P_s^2.
\]
Local tadpoles and subleading nonanalytic pieces have not been equated to this leading term. In particular the phase-phase derivative is of order one on scaling rays, so competing analytic terms cannot be neglected when estimating that full component.

At fixed xi the leading tensor-source correction is
\[
 \partial_{p^2}\Sigma_{\rm IR}=\frac{A_{\rm IR}}{P_s^2}M(\xi).
\]
At the chosen point A_IR=1.8225789144112618e-5 in mass-squared units. The radial coefficient grows as 1/P_s^2 and the mixed coefficient as 1/P_s; this explains the raw h-space growth from the preceding stage.

On the finest saved parent grid, the first radial rung divided by this prediction is 1.00494897; the antisymmetric component divided by -sqrt(2)a xi A_IR/P_s^2 is 1.00549220. No coefficient was fitted to either component. These are finite-grid comparisons, not precision claims about the complete self-energy.

## The leading propagated contribution has a cancellation

The same matrix factorizes:
\[
 M=u\,w,\quad u=(1,a\xi)^T,\quad w=(1,-a\xi).
\]
The soft propagator obeys exactly
\[
 G_su=(1/m^2,0)^T,\qquad wG_s=(1/m^2,0),
 \qquad
 \boxed{G_sMG_s=\operatorname{diag}((m^2)^{-2},0).}
\]
Consequently the particular leading nonanalytic contribution to the change of propagator is
\[
 \delta G_{\rm IR}
 =-\frac{p_h^2 A_{\rm IR}}{P_s^2}
   \operatorname{diag}((m^2)^{-2},0).
\]
For a unit traceless shear, |p_h^2|<=p^2<=P_s^2. Hence its radial magnitude is bounded by
\[
 |\delta G_{{\rm IR},11}|\le \frac{A_{\rm IR}}{(m^2)^2}
 =1.4762889206731219\,10^{-5}
\]
in inverse-mass-squared units at the chosen point.

This is an exact cancellation within the stated leading soft approximation. It is NOT a bound on the whole stress correlator, the free phase response, all subleading loop pieces or arbitrary pair fluctuations. A normal gapless free propagator can itself have a divergent response. It is the identified nonanalytic insertion whose apparent enhancement has been explained.

There is a useful exact algebraic completion for any invertible full G. With e_sigma=(1,0)^T and A=G^{-1},
\[
 u_{\rm full}=Ae_\sigma/m^2,\quad
 w_{\rm full}=e_\sigma^TA/m^2,\quad
 G u_{\rm full}w_{\rm full}G=e_\sigma e_\sigma^T/(m^2)^2.
\]
The identity is verified, but the actual full vertex has NOT been proved to equal u_full w_full. It may be used as a decomposition basis, not silently substituted for dynamics.

## Regulated static operator results

The physical soft powers motivate weights chosen before the matrix replay:
\[
 w_{11}=1+\mu^2/P_s^2,\quad w_{22}=1,\quad
 w_3=w_4=1+\mu/\sqrt{P_s^2}.
\]
For each finite matrix L,
\[
 \rho(L)\le\max_i\frac{\sum_j|L_{ij}|w_j}{w_i}.
\]
This is an elementary finite-dimensional induced-norm bound, with rounding not enclosed by interval arithmetic. It is not yet a continuum norm theorem. The parent five-matrix replay gives bounds between 7.89e-5 and 9.59e-5.
