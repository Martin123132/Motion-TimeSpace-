# Connecting the bridge back to the evolving physical source

## Preserve the equation, not just an endpoint curve

For the same stored mass matrix M, energy row e^T, temperature mode T,
positive heat capacity chi and dimensionless expansion coordinate u, use

    M p' = K(u)(p-T g),
    e^T p' + chi g' = -1-chi g,
    W' = 1+chi g,
    K(u) = r(u)[H(t(u))-D(u)],   t(u)=-(u-u0).

H is Stage200's separately tested operator bridge. D and r remain the saved
emission/background functions. The linear energy ledger e^T p+chi g+W stays
constant. This is distinct from the quadratic stability functional.

At the warm join H and H_t equal the earlier candidate's value and slope. Thus
there is no stiffness jump caused by this substitution and no new impulsive
source. If the retained r and D are C1 there, K is C1 and the linear evolution
has C2 histories through the join. H_tt may jump; matching C1 does not establish
arbitrary higher smoothness. In particular, do not apply Stage199's artificial
source-cut endpoint correction at this smooth operator join: that correction
belongs to an attributed source discontinuity, not to every matching surface.

Continue each Stage199 half/quarter pair without reinitializing p, g or W. The
last Stage199 step is partial: its previous coordinate is reconstructed as
source_start+(done-1)*nominal_step. Resume the same regular lattice after that
partial endpoint, rather than translating the whole grid to the new start.
The final fraction 0.90625 is sampled exactly, with a partial step if necessary.

For new step h and old step k, the variable-step BDF2 coefficients are

    rho=h/k, a=(1+2 rho)/(1+rho), b=rho^2/(1+rho),
    a delta_y - b delta_y_previous = h f(y_new,u_new).

The existing high-precision Schur update is reused, including its positive
denominator check. All increments and state arithmetic use 80 digits; the final
step is recomputed at 100 digits. This tests arithmetic at the endpoint, not a
global rigorous error bound. The original half/quarter comparison tests accumulated
time-step error, including both absolute histories and the smaller repair relative
to the saved original-bank endpoint. A large isolated step ratio is recorded;
the continuous stability lemma is not a proof of variable-step BDF2 zero stability.

Eight-step blocks carry complete states, equation residuals and chained hashes.
A restart records both states and both coordinates. No step or matrix is replaced
by a fitted gas curve. The original bank histories remain the reference, not
overwritten or relabelled as independently exact solutions.

## Next microscopic derivative: what this bridge supplies

For a weak microscopic source functional S(theta,p,g,eta), eta collects every
additional history-dependent input, such as the supplied infrared tail and any
explicit background coefficients. The total derivative is

    dS/du = -theta S_theta + D_p S [M^-1 K(p-Tg)]
            + S_g[-1-chi g-e^T M^-1 K(p-Tg)]/chi
            + D_eta S eta'.

Thus the cold operator and continued histories supply the p' and g' arguments,
not a licence to omit the explicit thermal derivative or the other inputs.
Differentiating once more includes K'=r'(H-D)+r(-H_t-D'), alongside p'', g'' and
the source's mixed derivatives. H_t is analytic from the endpoint-constrained
cubic; its numerical approximation error remains separate from the exact chain
rule. Neither the old bank corner nor a mixed warm/cold polynomial is needed to
define these derivatives inside the candidate bridge.

The fixed saved gas kernels test only the S_g part. A full source continuation
must reevaluate the existing event equations with the repaired p and g (and
explicitly handled remaining inputs) at the farther temperature, freeze its
prediction first, and keep the same baseline/MTS diagnostics. Stage200 does not
silently promote its gas-only check to a full photon-plus-gas source result.
