# Stage55 acceptance gates, set before late runs

- Every used matching mode is sourced from the Stage54 native trajectory; no k interpolation or empirical transfer multiplier.
- Background density is continuous at the chosen near-nominal crossing to 1e-9 fractionally; the retained slow field is re-integrated for that background.
- The transition happens exactly once per mode, outside derivative callbacks. Every other stored state is unchanged exactly. CAMB's existing hierarchy approximations remain active; no extra radiation averaging is introduced.
- The heavy chart is exact before and averaged after the event. Both chart equations must satisfy their own density/momentum conservation identities symbolically.
- Standard LCDM and slow-field cold controls remain finite and reproduce saved results where directly comparable.
- Numerical-resolution and projection-window changes must keep late physical matter amplitudes within 5e-4 fractionally; near zeros use a mode's trajectory-amplitude normalization instead.
- The three matching epochs must agree in late physical matter amplitude within 0.5%. This is a numerical working gate, not an observational precision requirement or proof of the exact scalar dynamics.
- Quantify rather than hide the instantaneous heavy stress changes at projection. Density-continuous background matching does not make perturbation projection an exact junction without a remainder.
- Reconstruct combined matter with the heavy contribution included. Stock delta_tot and stock sigma8 exclude that contribution and are not accepted as MTS total-matter observables.
- Four k values support four transfer/power samples only, not an interpolated full spectrum, sigma8 integral, data fit, or selected F.
- Preserve all prior sealed files and the original installed CAMB. No GitHub actions.
