# Stage188 — equation-sampled response at the original probe times

## Outcome

The five original probe times are now evaluated from the coupled photon/gas/work
equations, rather than by linear interpolation between stored states.
Both the unchanged scattering bank and the separate Stage187 smooth candidate
were tested, using the same saved histories and frozen tolerances.

For the candidate, equation-based sampling removes 98.9178% (heavy background)
and 97.0646% (cold background) of the magnitude of the quarter-grid
coarse-versus-fine probe-width discrepancy seen with linear sampling.
The sign also changes. This is a sampling correction in the local gas-response
diagnostic, not that percentage improvement in the full microscopic source,
an observed spectrum, or a cosmological fit.

The candidate's small remaining width discrepancy still fails its own 1%
history-step convergence gate. Its last-step split control passes on the
quarter grid. The next calculation should therefore test precision retained
through the saved evolution, not repeatedly subdivide the probe step.
