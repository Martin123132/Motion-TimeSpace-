## 3. Time-dependent A completes, rather than just repeats, angular stress

The static A variation in the previous checkpoint is only part of the
Euler derivative if the full extension also depends on A_t. At the base
A=r, A_t=v_shift=0, a variation of A_t changes both

    delta W_e=delta A_t(r_e),  delta v_A(R)=-delta A_t(R).

Let Q_shift=delta S/dv_A=K_total/(N^2 F), interpreted as the weak
functional covector AFTER the horizontal time integration by parts, with
its explicit temporal endpoint retained separately. Let P_mesh denote
the mesh-velocity momentum distribution from the zero-shift action.
Then the coefficient paired with delta A_t is

    Pi_A=P_mesh-Q_shift.

For the potential-only Gram sector P_mesh,G=0, hence Pi_A,G=-K_G/(N^2 F).
For physical P2 waves,

    <P_mesh,w,kappa>=-sum_cells integral alpha T q_R kappa_h dR,
    alpha=R^2/(N sqrtF), T=qdot-W q_R,

where kappa_h is the piecewise-affine edge-velocity perturbation. For the
source, P_mesh,d=p_d delta_b and Q_shift,d=p_d delta_b; they cancel. In
fact (V_A+v_A)^2/F_A=(V+v_shift)^2/F exactly, independently of A_t.

Therefore the full areal Euler derivative contains

    E_A=E_A,static-part - partial_t Pi_A,

in addition to any spatial/leaf terms obtained in the complete functional
chain. Its time term cannot be inferred by simply appending the previous
static angular stress to an unchanged mixed equation. In the radial weak
balance the two explicit time terms combine as

    partial_t Q_shift+partial_t Pi_A=partial_t P_mesh.

This is an off-shell accounting identity, not a prescription for setting
the matter or gravitational residual to zero. Test the remaining mesh/

The complete inherited leaf is nonlocal; the functional chain and its
