# Native-support transmission conditions, not another smooth trial family

The native potential depends on field values at a finite physical support. Its field variation therefore supplies point loads to each material layer. A smooth source core repairs the distinguished source corner, but a globally smooth trial family need not satisfy the interface conditions at the ordinary native knots.

This derivation concerns the inherited conditional annular action, at zero initial field and knot velocities, with a continuous positive metric coefficient beta=R^2*N*sqrt(F). It does not replace the parent by a new point-interaction theory. The point support is already present in its native potential.

Let v_j=q(X_j), A_j=partial U_native/partial v_j, and C_j=partial U_native/partial X_j at fixed v and Eulerian metric, including the explicit sampled beta derivative. Hold the distinguished source and endpoints fixed. In a quadratic source core the compensated-hinge covector vanishes by disjoint support; numerical deviations from that premise must be recorded.

## Field equation and force balance

The wave equation has distributional form

    alpha*q_tt = partial_R(beta*q_R) - sum_j A_j*delta(R-X_j)

away from separately retained source terms. Requiring no delta acceleration at an ordinary native knot gives

    [beta*q_R]_j=A_j.

The same result follows from varying the common field trace in the two adjacent wave-action integrals. Varying the knot position while keeping its common trace fixed gives the wave boundary contribution [beta*q_R^2]/2; stationarity of the massless geometric support direction gives

    [beta*q_R^2]_j/2+C_j=0.

The sign is fixed by the left interval contributing -beta*g_minus^2/2 and the right interval +beta*g_plus^2/2 to the potential's endpoint derivative. These are necessary transmission equations for a regular piecewise field at this zero-rate slice, not a static bulk solution or a proof of subsequent evolution.

For continuous beta and A_j!=0, solve both equations explicitly:

    mean_gradient_j = -C_j/A_j,
    g_plus_j = -C_j/A_j + A_j/(2*beta_j),
    g_minus_j = -C_j/A_j - A_j/(2*beta_j).

The second condition is exactly A_j*mean_gradient_j+C_j=0, which is the local form of the native shape covector under the inherited average-trace prescription. If A_j=0 and C_j!=0 there is no solution of this zero-rate interface pair. If both vanish, the mean is free and the jump is zero. Near-zero A_j can require large gradients; it must not be silently floored.

## Concrete test

On the source-compatible quadratic-core fixture, extract the complete native covectors at the central material label on both grids. Verify their contraction against the full33 shape variations, including the residual covectors outside ordinary native nodes. Compute the required one-sided slopes on a predeclared transition band, retaining the quadratic source and zero outer buffers. Record all denominators, any singular rows, and the residual outside the band. No coefficient is tuned to an observed astronomical residual.

Construct a continuous piecewise cubic Hermite field on the fixed native intervals, using v_j and these two one-sided slopes. This is not a shrinking inter-knot bubble or an invisible zero-native-energy field: it keeps the actual nonzero native samples and potential, and its physical intervals do not shrink when the field discretization is refined. Evaluate its positive norm and wave-gradient energy with two split quadrature rules; check the source trace/core and endpoint behavior. An excessive energy cost or singular slope law is a real obstruction, not a pass to hide.

This first probe freezes the existing metric and uses one material label. Even if the point equations close algebraically, all-label interpolation, live metric response, the coupled source condition and time preservation must be solved before the reconstructed field can replace the initial data. The earlier finite polynomial-space certificates remain valid in their scope; this construction need not belong to those spaces.
