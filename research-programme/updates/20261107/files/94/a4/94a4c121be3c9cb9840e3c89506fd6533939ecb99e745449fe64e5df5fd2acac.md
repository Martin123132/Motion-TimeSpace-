# Stage231: local thermodynamic source and off-shell gravitational constraints

This extends Stage75's local source rows using Stage228's particle-owned EOS
and Stage230's coupled backgrounds. The original MTS parent action, constants,
calibration and photon banks remain unchanged. Units are c=8piG=1, conformal
time eta, and the inherited k=0.1/Mpc. The functions return stresses divided
by rho_star, except where an explicit dimensional factor is stated.

## 1. Density, temperature and velocity are different perturbations

Let b=delta n/n, r=delta(T/T_star), v=longitudinal velocity, J=ikv+S.
Number conservation gives b'=-J. At fixed species fractions:

    delta rho_g/rho_star=(rho_rest/rho_star+chi A)b+chi C r
    delta P_g/rho_star=(2chi/3)(R b+r)
    j_g/rho_star=B v
    B=rho_rest/rho_star+chi(A+2R/3).

A_R=C, A_s|R=A-RC and C_s|R=-R C_R follow from the same normalized electron
measure. The ions remain the inherited ideal NR ions. The electron moment
identity is C_R=(2 f_e/3R)[central_moment_3(y)-2 Var(y)].
No new fitted pressure or momentum coefficient appears.

Pressure is NOT 2 delta u/3 once electron heat is relativistically corrected.
Using that old shortcut would add an erroneous chi delta_A density pressure
and chi delta_C temperature pressure. This matters for the spatial source
even though pressure at fixed actual T remains the ideal n k_B T.

The common-velocity current follows by boosting the isotropic kinetic stress:
T^0i=(rho_rest+u+P)v to first order. Heat capacity does not replace enthalpy.

## 2. Primitive evolution from the same gas first law

Write Q=Q_gamma/rho_star, CE=delta Q_gamma/rho_star and CF=delta F_gamma/rho_star.
Positive photon transfer is negative gas transfer. Define the physical slopes:

    R_p'=H R-(2H R+Q/chi)/C
    A_p'=H(A-RC)+C R_p'
    C_p'=C_R(R_p'-H R)
    B_p'=H rho_rest/rho_star+chi(A_p'+2R_p'/3).

Then the local primitive equations are

    r'=[H-2H/C-C_p'/C]r-2R J/(3C)-(CE-Q b)/(chi C)
    B v'=-CF-B_p'v-ik delta P_g/rho_star.

CE and CF must be moments of the SAME photon collision RHS. Their cancellation
in total stress is independent of whether a particular collision model is an
accurate approximation: conservation alone does not certify that accuracy.
The source_rows API accepts those rows, never fits them or silently supplies
a separate force. With A=R,C=1,C_R=0 the Stage75 NR equations are recovered.

In the source-free local rest frame, entropy conservation gives

    r=(2R/(3C))b
    c_s^2=(P_g/(rho_g+P_g))[1+2/(3C)].

This is a derived gas characteristic speed, not the coupled photon acoustic
speed. The latter still requires radiation transport. Positive enthalpy and
heat capacity make this mode stable. Subluminality is checked on the actual
saved states rather than asserted for arbitrary invented EOS parameters.

## 3. Do not hide finite background defects

An interpolated or discretized background is not exactly on shell. Set
f_R=R_observed'-R_p'. Differentiate the actual stress using R_observed', but
keep the physical primitive perturbation RHS above. The residuals are

    E_g/rho_star=chi f_R(C b+C_R r)
    M_g/rho_star=chi(C+2/3) f_R v.

Here E_g is the gas perturbation energy-continuity residual including CE,
and M_g is its momentum residual including CF. Substituting the observed
slope into the perturbation coefficients merely to make these zero would
change the physical linearized equations. This stage does not do that.

For each retained canonical scalar, define f_phi=phi_observed'-pi and
f_pi=pi_observed'+2H pi+a^2 m^2 phi. Its stress residuals are

    E_phi=f_pi delta pi/a^2+m^2 f_phi delta phi
    M_phi=-ik f_pi delta phi/a^2.

Photon finite-band/moment/kinetic defects add their own residuals; they must
not be declared zero because gas exchange moments cancel. The numerical
probe below measures the represented-bank versus saved-step discrepancy.

## 4. Forced Einstein-constraint propagation

Let E and M be TOTAL matter continuity residuals in dimensional units, and
R_H=H_observed'-H^2+a^2(rho+p)/2. With Stage75's metric evolution, define

    C_H=2k^2 h_T+4H(2sigma_T+sigma_L)-2a^2 delta rho
    C_M=2ik sigma_T-a^2 j.

Direct differentiation, without setting any defect to zero, gives

    C_H'=-4H C_H-2ik C_M+4S R_H-2a^2 E
    C_M'=-2H C_M-(ik/2)C_H-a^2 M.

Thus a nonzero wave constraint can be predicted from finite background and
matter residuals rather than removed by projection. The on-shell identities
are recovered exactly when R_H=E=M=0. The coefficient 4S is fixed by the
metric equations, not an adjustable compensation. No residual is corrected
away in the saved backgrounds.

## 5. Representation and implementation scope

Stage230 evolved the temperature-linear heat U=A(s,1)+C(s,1)(R-1), including
the time dependence of its coefficients. The local exact EOS here is evaluated
at the saved R, with the omitted quadratic heat estimated independently at
high precision. This does not turn the earlier approximation into an exact
nonlinear EOS evolution. Both NR and EOS cases are tested.

The 96-function isotropic bank and its established upper-triangle solver
convention are reused. The raw thermal column is retained; no matrix is
clipped, symmetrized in the archive or re-fitted. This stage tests the local
collision/gas/stress interface, exact identities, characteristics and forced
constraint equations. It does not manufacture an angular hierarchy from
isotropic data. A propagated spatial wave must also connect the retained
angular representation and its higher mixed collision derivatives explicitly.
