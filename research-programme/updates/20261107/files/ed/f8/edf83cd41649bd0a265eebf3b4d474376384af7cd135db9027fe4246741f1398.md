# Stable event-moment probe, not an adopted source replacement

The saved real-emission code forms the photon jump by subtracting nearly equal
bath-frame photon frequencies. The electron moment is independently computed
from recoil momentum and its mass-shell energy. Summing saved doubles at60digits
cannot restore digits already lost in those event inputs.

Let e be the incident rest-frame photon energy, s the signed soft energy,
A=1-cos(hard), B=1-n_h.n_s, C=1-n_s,z and d=1+eA-sB.
The existing kinematics give h=(e-s-esC)/d.
Algebra, without a small-energy expansion, gives
h+s-e=[-esC-(e-s)(eA-sB)]/d.
For a Born event (s=0), h-e=-e^2 A/(1+eA).
These rational forms avoid subtracting h+s and e after evaluating them separately.

With recoil p=e*n_in-h*n_h-s*n_s, the photon lab-frame energy change is
[T00*(h+s-e)-T0i*p_i]/theta.
The electron calculation remains independently
[T00*p^2/(sqrt(1+p^2)+1)+T0i*p_i]/theta.
The probe does NOT set electron=-photon.
The constant and linear spectral test functions imply exactly
jump_const=sign(s)/norm0 and
jump_linear=(photon_jump-mean*sign(s))/norm1.
For Born events sign(s) is0. These are analytic test-function identities,
not a posteriori changes to make a finished source vector conserve energy.

Test the two largest ranked saved real D-event pair defects at each endpoint.
Use the same histories, angles, radial rule, intensity and Bose factors.
Require unchanged higher spectral rows and independently unchanged electron rows.
This small probe tests whether the derived stable arithmetic targets the problem;
it does not replace all old events or certify a whole endpoint source.
