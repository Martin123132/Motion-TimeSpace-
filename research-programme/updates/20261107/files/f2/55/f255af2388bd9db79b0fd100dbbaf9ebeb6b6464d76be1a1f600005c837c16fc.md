# Stage158 — targeted 1–3, then 3–8 frequency refinement

## Result

Both requested bands were refined at all three temperatures, in the requested
sequence. The incoming Gauss rule rises from order6 to order7 only in those
bands. Other panels and quadrature axes stay fixed. All three D/V/S source
components were evaluated, rather than changing the virtual component alone.

The virtual refinement difference shrinks again:

| Incoming band | New / preceding virtual step norm | Reduction |
|---|---:|---:|
| 1–3 | 0.52865–0.52868 | 47.13–47.14% |
| 3–8 | 0.21029–0.21031 | 78.97–78.98% |

Cross-temperature cancellation survives. The latest combined virtual step
retains 14.666–14.708% of the separate-temperature step budget in1–3,
and14.478–14.546% in3–8. This continues the approximately85% cancellation
seen in the saved rules.

The paired baseline/MTS response checks pass. The largest response-time-step
difference is **0.060069%** of the relevant new response's own history scale,
below the unchanged1% gate. Both heavy/cold cases, local/cubic routes,
two joins,2048/4096steps and both saved matching readouts are retained.

The source-prediction discrepancy remains informative rather than disappearing:
the matched cubic diagnostic moves from **1.80226–1.80502%** to
**1.81081–1.81352%** after both numerical panel replacements. Thus this targeted
refinement has not removed the approximately1.8% cubic source mismatch.
No curve was refitted to absorb it.

## Source comparison

H is the colder-half test point, W the warm-quarter anchor and C the midpoint.
Each temperature retains its own physical weights. Identical incoming x nodes
are used across temperatures within each rule; no cross-temperature event
values are copied.

For each band the cubic error-sensitive combination is
`delta S_H + delta S_W -1.5 delta S_C`.
The full combined D/V/S band-change norms, on the fixed cold order4 source
scale, are:

| Band | Full combined source change | Virtual part alone |
|---|---:|---:|
| 1–3 | 0.154760–0.154851% | 0.0907445–0.0908402% |
| 3–8 | 0.0583650–0.0584275% | 0.0268824–0.0269421% |

After combining both vector changes, the full matched cubic change is
0.148937–0.149028% on that same scale. Norms do not add as signed scalars.
The diagnostic cubic discrepancy is1.82199–1.82465% after1–3 alone, then
1.81081–1.81352% after3–8. The latter band partly offsets the former.

The two-panel total changes at individual sites, relative to each site's
saved full order6 source norm, are:

- H:0.204289–0.204438%.
- W:0.128557–0.128654%.
- C:0.160998–0.161203%.

These are numerical source changes, not changes in observed cosmological
parameters or new data-fit scores. Contraction ratios compare finite rules;
they are not certified continuum tail bounds.
