## Weighted combination and cancellation

The original incoming seed weights are **2.5077551020408158** at x=14 and
**2.2909803030307123** at x=16.435.... The inherited event weight already includes
these factors together with the electron, angular, Bose and Born factors.
The saved weighted event arrays are added directly, with compensated high/low
sums: the frequency weights are not applied twice and the partial sum is not
renormalized. Independent 100-digit Decimal sample checks verify the sums.

The largest inter-node cancellation factor in the history correction is
**1.26461**, in the cold photon virtual term. Thus a pair check was necessary;
passing the individual node is not assumed to imply passing the sum. The pair
does pass with the same gates, despite this modest cancellation.

This is **two of the original seven incoming nodes in the 8-20 band**, not the
whole frequency integral. Neither the two-node seed-weight sum nor its error
should be interpreted as the fraction or uncertainty of the missing spectrum.
No final-spectrum propagation has been performed with this partial panel.
