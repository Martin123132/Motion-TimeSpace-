# Matter–gravity baseline: transport remainder and matched integration

## Parent conventions and inputs

This is a daughter of the completed integrated-baseline stage, not a replacement of its sources. The reduced polar chart uses F=1-2m/r, N=exp(nu), positive coupling kappa, and the source proper-clock rate ell=sqrt(N^2-v^2/F)>0. The monotone material source map is r=S(z), with J=S_z>0 and material weight w(z)=6(z+1/2)(1/2-z). All numerical quantities retain the parent's normalized units.

The inputs are the actual dust current in `scripts/annular_position_support_column_20260930.py`, the mass loading in `scripts/annular_candidate_radial_constraint_20260920.py`, and the independently computed background-plus-velocity metric time responses. A momentum equation must not be used to redefine those responses so that a desired cancellation holds.

## 1. Exact off-shell common-measure identity

Write rho_z=w(z)/J and let D=kappa*mass*rho_z*sqrt(F)*N/ell denote ONLY the dust part of the radial mass constraint. Its contribution to the baseline current density is

    j_d = mass*rho_z*N^2*v/ell.
    C = N/(kappa*sqrt(F)).
    j_d = C*v*D.

Consequently, for each moment h=(r-6)^p,

    B_dust[h] + B_gravity[h] = integral C*(m_t+v*D)*h dr.

Outside the source collar D=0. Inside the collar the equivalent label integrand is C*J*(m_t+v*D)*h. The term m_t is the fixed-radius derivative, not the derivative following a source layer. Replacing it by a material derivative without its advection term would double-count transport.

Define R=m_t+v*D. This identity is off shell: R is NOT set to zero. It removes an independently integrated advective pair without discarding its remainder. It does not remove scalar, native, boundary, or metric-source contributions.

## 2. When a zero is justified

In a pressureless, scalar-free spherical solution with material enclosed-mass conservation, (partial_t+v*partial_r)m=0 and m_r=D. Then R=0 and the two currents cancel. In the actual scalar/Gram calculation m_r also has non-dust loading. Even material mass conservation alone would not imply R=0 there. The parent already derives the pressureless control in `scripts/derive_annular_source_gravity_20260914.py`; that conditional identity cannot be promoted to the coupled branch.

## 3. A constructive equation for the remainder

Differentiate the actual radial mass equation. If m_t'=A_mm*m_t+A_mnu*nu_t+f_m, with A the radial-constraint Jacobian and f_m its explicit load-time forcing, then on each smooth cell

    R' = A_mm*R + A_mnu*nu_t + f_m + (v*D)' - A_mm*v*D.

At an interface, [R]=[m_t]+[v*D]; retain any stored trace jumps and the inner boundary value. This gives a route to evolving the small remainder directly, rather than subtracting independently accumulated large numbers. It is an algebraic consequence of the differentiated constraint, not a claim that its forcing vanishes. All forcing and interface data must be supplied before using this as a replacement solver.

## 4. Numerical test for this checkpoint

First test the identity with exact symbolic algebra and manufactured positive/negative controls. Then retain the already-solved metric and its two time responses, but evaluate their stored polynomial coefficients at 70 decimal digits. Preserve the parent's registered polynomial centres, lengths, and left constants. Do not silently renormalize source cardinal functions.

Split the radial integral at every stored metric/response edge and both source endpoints. On those panels integrate the actual R, and separately the gravity and advective pieces; compare against an independent source-label integration with matching interface cuts. Orders 12 and 20 test these piecewise-smooth integrations. Their agreement diagnoses quadrature/arithmetic for the stored inputs, not the physical accuracy of the parent fields.

Two source interpretations are retained: high-precision contraction of the parent's binary nodal values with its binary cardinal matrix, and its rounded binary coefficient contraction. Their difference is a representation sensitivity, not a tunable parameter. Existing historical dust and gravity integrals remain available as controls. No wave, native, C0/C1/C2, source action, or metric-time input is refitted in this stage.

Only if the matched integrations succeed should their measured corrections be propagated into a daughter coupled-equation assembly, with source-interpretation, replay and quadrature differences retained. The result cannot be judged by widening an error envelope alone; the fixed-budget comparison remains necessary.
