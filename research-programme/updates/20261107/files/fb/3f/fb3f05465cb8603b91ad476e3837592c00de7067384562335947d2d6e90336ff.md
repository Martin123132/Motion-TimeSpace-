# The preservation equation away from the native-null radical

The proposed nonflat core generally has positive native energy. Even if its initial shape constraints vanish, the old fourth-order radical test cannot be imported unchanged.

For a symmetric shape matrix Q_i depending on the actual source/map coordinates and Eulerian geometry, write B_i=q^T Q_i q. At a time-symmetric initial slice with q_dot=0 and Q_i,dot=0, exact differentiation gives

    B_i,ddot = 2*q_ddot^T*Q_i*q + q^T*Q_i,ddot*q.

Equivalently, insert q(t)=q_0+t^2*a_q/2+o(t^2) and Q_i(t)=Q_i,0+t^2*Q_i,2/2+o(t^2). The coefficient of t^2 is a_q^T Q_i,0 q_0 + q_0^T Q_i,2 q_0/2. This is a necessary second-order preservation equation at a generic constrained state. Both the coupled physical acceleration and the geometric matrix response belong in it.

It is not legitimate to set Q_i,2=0 merely because the inner root search froze the metric. Nor is it enough to differentiate a zero-rate radial solve only with respect to positions. If the solved geometry is g=G(z,v), where z denotes all configuration variables and v their velocities, and G is even in v, then at v=0

    g_dot=0,
    g_ddot=G_z[a_z]+G_vv[a_z,a_z].

The second term is the curvature in velocity space: kinetic energy grows quadratically when v(t)=t*a_z+o(t). More generally a nonzero G_v also contributes G_v[j_z] and must not be omitted. The even-velocity premise and radial-solve regularity must be checked for the actual parent implementation.

In implicit radial equations R(g,z,v)=0 with invertible radial Jacobian and the same zero-rate/even-velocity assumptions, derive the metric acceleration by solving

    R_g*g_ddot = -R_z[a_z]-R_vv[a_z,a_z].

This supplies the geometric part of Q_i,2 without fitting a stress cancellation. Source/map accelerations supply the other part. The equation is a derived calculation target, not a report that those terms have been evaluated for the new core.

Only when the stronger moving-null-section identities hold does the lower-order test vanish and the previous normal-acceleration quartic law become the relevant first obstruction. The new joint construction must first satisfy B_i(0)=0, the source-corner law and the generic second-order equation, with held-out generators.
