# Source-split septic construction for the next live update

The physical source is already an element edge in the P6 mesh. We therefore need not raise an entire native interval to degree eight or ten to preserve its interior source value while changing native third derivatives. Split that interval at the actual source and use one degree-seven Hermite polynomial on each half. This is an alternative to the global-interval bubble route in `DERIVATION-20261003-explicit-third-jet-preservation-update.md`, not a change to the completed P6 state.

For one physical cell [L,U], h=U-L and x=(R-L)/h, prescribe

    (q,g,k,ell) at L and U.

Let H be the eight-by-eight matrix taking monomial coefficients to derivatives of orders zero through three at x=0 and x=1. Then

    coefficients = H^{-1} * (q_L, h*g_L, h^2*k_L, h^3*ell_L,
                             q_U, h*g_U, h^2*k_U, h^3*ell_U).

This produces the unique degree-at-most-seven polynomial. H has exact rational entries and nonzero determinant; the supplied checker constructs its inverse and verifies all eight endpoint identities. Since evaluation derivatives are with respect to x, the powers of h are essential. Native endpoint values, slopes and curvatures remain those of the qualified state; third derivatives become the derived targets.

At the distinguished source, choose a common source slope p and a common third derivative ell_b as retained initial-data choices, not new physical couplings. The source value is zero on both halves. The common source curvature is fixed, rather than fitted independently:

    k_b = -(d_b+a_b)*p/c_b^2.

Both halves then have the same q=0, g=p, k=k_b, ell=ell_b at b. Thus the compensated source slope jump is zero and the source corner c_b^2*k_b+(d_b+a_b)*p vanishes algebraically. With fixed metric and computed a_b, all these source conditions coexist with independently prescribed native endpoint third jets. No denominator involving the source's fractional position is needed. One must keep b strictly between its neighbours and c_b^2 positive.

For the future live iteration, take p from the qualified initial state's common source slope and ell_b from its previous source third jets (or another explicitly recorded initial-data choice), construct the P7 cells, recompute gravity and the complete acceleration, and update k_b from the new a_b. The native third-jet targets likewise use updated covector and metric second derivatives. The polynomial interpolation proof is not convergence of that coupled iteration. The new state and its energy are still to be calculated.
