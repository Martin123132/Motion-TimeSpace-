## 2. Continuous bounds, not just sampled maxima

The reference interpolation is piecewise linear in s. Split at every reference
history knot, Compton bank corner and bremsstrahlung spline corner. On each
cell d_ref is a convex combination of its endpoint vectors.

The unchanged operator decomposes as

    K(s)=K_Compton(theta)-C_DC theta^2 N_DC-BR(log theta).

its matrix controls with the two endpoint vectors. Triangle inequality joins
