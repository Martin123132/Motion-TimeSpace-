# Stage187 derivation and calculation scope

## Candidate and domain

Use t=ln(theta/theta0)=-(u-u0) and
B_c(t)=B0+t*B_t0+(t^2/2)*B_tt0.
The coefficient matrices are the frozen Stage186 physical fixed-membership
derivatives. No interpolator derivative or fit is substituted. The minimal
extension to the exact saved restart/end temperatures is validated at two new
edge temperatures, with predictions frozen before calculating them.

For t=a+(b-a)s, the quadratic has Bernstein form

    B_c = (1-s)^2 C0 + 2s(1-s) C1 + s^2 C2
    C0 = B_c(a)
    C1 = B_c(a) + (b-a)/2 * B_c'(a)
    C2 = B_c(b).

If the three controls are negative semidefinite, so is the candidate throughout
the interval. Symmetry and the common photon-number null direction propagate
in the same way. Test the mass-whitened controls after removing the null mode.
The convex-hull implication is algebraic; numerical eigenvalue checks at the
inherited tolerance are not a certified interval-arithmetic spectral proof.
No eigenvalue clipping is permitted.

## Actual coupled evolution, not slaved equilibrium

The inherited reduced photon/gas system is

    M p' = S(u) (p - T g)
    e^T p' + chi (g' + g) + 1 = 0
    W' = 1 + chi g

with constant basis mass M, energy row e, temperature template T and heat
capacity chi. Thus (e^T p + chi*g + W)'=0.
S=r(B-D) uses the SAME evolving background rate r and emission D in both runs.
The candidate evolves p and g together; it does not prescribe the old photon
profile while claiming a fully evolved new gas solution. The cosmological
background itself and the fixed-anchor source kernels are not re-evolved.

At a BDF step, alpha is the derivative coefficient and v,s,w are the historical
photon, gas and work combinations. Solve

    A = alpha M - S
    f = A^-1 M v
    h = A^-1 (-S T)
    g_new = [chi*s - 1 - e^T(alpha*f-v)]
            / [chi*(alpha+1)+alpha*e^T h]
    p_new = f + h*g_new
    W_new = (w+1+chi*g_new)/alpha.

The denominator retains the gas-capacity feedback; replacing it by a constant
would not be this system. Both the sampled slaved capacity and every actual
discrete denominator are checked positive.

Use the inherited variable-start BDF2 rule when the first refined step follows
native-step history. For first-step ratio r_step=1/divisor,

    alpha=(1+2r_step)/[(1+r_step)*step]
    history=[(1+r_step)*current-r_step^2/(1+r_step)*older]/step.

After startup, alpha=3/(2step) and history=(2current-older/2)/step.
Native, half and quarter schedules and both previous states are reused exactly.
The old native history remains the starting data, not a newly fitted initial
condition.

## Difference reconstruction and conservation control

Let delta p=p_bank-p_candidate and delta g=g_bank-g_candidate.
Then, exactly for this linear reduced system,

    M delta p' = S_candidate (delta p-T delta g)
                 +(S_bank-S_candidate)(p_bank-T g_bank)
    e^T delta p' + chi(delta g'+delta g) = 0
    delta W' = chi*delta g.

The same candidate factorization solves this forced difference using a third
right-hand side and zero initial differences. Candidate plus forced difference
must reconstruct the saved bank history. Check original coupled equations,
forced equations, expansion work, integrated energy, and reconstruction.
This avoids interpreting subtraction noise as a small physical correction.
It is an identity control, not an independent empirical confirmation.

## Curvature and source projection

Keep the original probes at offsets0,+/-0.01,+/-0.005, the same linear temporal
interpolation and 80-digit arithmetic of those saved binary probe values.
Compare the centered curvatures at widths0.01 and0.005 and their difference.
Keep the 1% refinement gate for both the original bank and candidate.

Split quarter-minus-half into (quarter sampled on half nodes minus half)
plus (full quarter sampling minus thinned quarter sampling). This separates
sample interpolation from common-node evolution without another evolution.

For each cached gas kernel K_component and background-specific curvature C[g],

    U_component = K_component * C[g].

Control/baseline, MTS remainder and total get identical treatment. The absolute
responses differ; equal fractional changes arise from this scalar multiplication
and are not independent physical tests. Heavy/cold label two background
histories, not baseline versus MTS.

## Accuracy scope

The two endpoint matrices and eight older temperatures test the candidate
against the SAME finite physical quadrature. Their explicit domain-boundary
terms are retained separately, including Stage186's saved-rounding ledger.
This does not certify a uniform physical quadrature or continuum boundary
error, nor propagate a rigorous bound on it into a full cosmological spectrum.
A smooth candidate that reduces the old sampling artifact must still preserve
the genuine thermal curvature; no part of this check authorizes replacing the
live bank or the original full-source decisions.
