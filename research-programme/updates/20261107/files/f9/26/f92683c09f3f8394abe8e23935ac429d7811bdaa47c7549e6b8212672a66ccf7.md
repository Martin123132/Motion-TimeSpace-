# Stage132: a common corrected inner/source operator

Let u=1/r. The inherited physical event acts on the slope f as

H_u f(x) = [f(x/u)+u^3 f(ux)-(u+u^2)f(x)]/2.

Its adjoint under integral x v f dx is

F_u v(x) = [u v(x/u)+u^2 v(ux)-(u+u^2)v(x)]/2.

For each event choose the four log-grid shifts surrounding it, and let
P(t) be the cubic Lagrange interpolant of the entire event operator in
inverse ratio t, not a separate interpolation of its gain/loss pieces.
The positive two-node deposition has mean u, variance V and third central
moment mu3. Its support a,b is the middle pair of these four nodes.
Taylor's formula for a cubic is exact:

E[P(U)] - P(u) = V P''(u)/2 + mu3 P'''(u)/6.

Thus cubic deposition implements Stage131's derived correction with
discrete derivative operators. It is not identical numerically to the
continuum Taylor correction; that difference must be measured. Cubics
reproduce 1,u,u^2,u^3, so the event energy eigenvalue
lambda=(1-u-u^2+u^3)/2 is preserved without fitting or rescaling.

If raw_j is the signed deposited event rate at r_j=exp(j h), then
k_j=(raw_j+r_j^3 raw_-j)/2 and k_-j=k_j/r_j^3 for j>0.
Set k_0=-sum_{j!=0}k_j and G=sum_j k_j shift_j + lambda I.
The weak source coefficients are k_j/r_j for j!=0 and k_0+lambda on
the diagonal. On the infinite grid these are exact weighted adjoints
with quadrature weight x^2 h. At finite endpoints keep the omitted-shift
terms as boundary work/inflow; do not erase them by a zero-flux assertion.

## Finite-domain stability

With D_i=x_i^(3/2), D G D^-1 is symmetric Toeplitz with
t_j=k_j exp(-3 j h/2) and diagonal d_0=k_0+lambda.
Its symbol is a(theta)=d_0+2 sum_{j>0}t_j cos(j theta).
Every finite principal truncation has maximum eigenvalue <= sup a.
For uniform theta spacing delta on [0,pi], linear interpolation gives

sup a <= max(sampled a) + (2 sum j^2 |t_j|) delta^2 / 8.

The code adds a conservative floating evaluation allowance and explicitly
labels the result numerical, not a formal outward-rounded certificate.
Convex interpolation between coefficient knots preserves the bound;
positive DC/BR damping and positive clock factors preserve dissipation.

For BDF2, with a,b,c consecutive weighted states,

<a,1.5a-2b+0.5c> = [||a||^2+||2a-b||^2
 -||b||^2-||2b-c||^2+||a-2b+c||^2]/4.

The recorded budget includes damping, source work and boundary inflow.
This controls a weighted squared norm, not total thermodynamic energy.
No positivity claim follows from dissipativity of a signed stencil.

## Scope

The old solver used a positive stencil on the defect and a different
signed stencil on the outer driver. The candidate applies the same
corrected paired stencil to both, including boundary rows. Physical
ghost values, outer histories, clock and DC/BR inputs are unchanged.
The comparison therefore tests this combined derived implementation;
it is not an isolated retest of source interpolation on unchanged states.
