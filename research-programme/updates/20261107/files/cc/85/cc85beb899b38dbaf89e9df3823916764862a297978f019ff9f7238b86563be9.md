## Coordinate conversion matters

The conformal seed chart and native synchronous chart agree physically,
but their quadratic scalar coefficients differ by a derived spatial
pullback. For one curvature cosine, the double-frequency cold coefficient
changes from -3(k tau)^2/16 to -9(k tau)^2/16 under that conversion.
This is a gauge/coordinate difference, not a factor-three physical effect.

The correction is tested against an explicit coordinate transformation.
Ignoring it leaves a finite error as amplitude shrinks. Likewise, using
a flat divergence in the curved momentum constraint fails its negative
control. These are exactly the kinds of source-mapping errors that
could otherwise masquerade as a theory problem.
