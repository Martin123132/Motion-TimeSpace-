# From a prescribed tide to an orbiting source

Private result, 4 October 2026. Full derivation:
`19-conserved-companion-source-and-quadrupole-wave-prediction.md`.

We have connected the condensate's response to the gravitational source of a
light orbiting companion. Its stress obeys conservation, and the comparison
uses the identical orbit and source around a same-mass Schwarzschild black hole.

- The baseline reproduces two published quadrupole wave-power benchmarks to
  about .005%, without fitting the source normalization.
- For this candidate, the dominant quadrupole pair radiates .4262% more power
  at an orbital radius of twice the body's radius, falling to 1.27 parts per
  million more at ten radii. These are conditional model predictions for a
  light circular companion, not observations or a complete inspiral model.
- Derived the wide-orbit amplitude correction 3 lambda_static/D^5 and its first
  relativistic multiplier, 1+(85/21)M/D. The numerical calculation approaches
  this prediction without fitting either coefficient.
- The previously found resonance cannot be reached by an external circular
  quadrupolar orbit for this body: its formal resonance radius lies inside it.
- 191 validation checks pass, including conservation/source identities, published
  reference benchmarks, precision comparisons and saved-solution replays.

This is a finite-size prediction within the retained Einstein coupling: changing
the central object, rather than declaring a failure of GR. No physical scale,
Newton constant or extra coupling was fitted. Everything remains local/private.
