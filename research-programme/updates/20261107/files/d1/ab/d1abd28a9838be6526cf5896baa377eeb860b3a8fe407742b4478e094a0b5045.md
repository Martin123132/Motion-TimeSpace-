    beta = beta_row p,

Here A=R[M_in+diag(lambda1-Gamma)] and Gamma=Gamma_DC+Gamma_BR.
Therefore, for baseline and repaired drives, the exact retained-equation
difference is

    delta_s_u = A delta_s + delta_b,
    delta_b = b(u;delta_p,delta_r).

The retained A is Metzler. With the given exterior truncation its row sums
are at most -d(u), where d>0 is bounded by the saved absorptive margin.
Its evolution operator U is positive and

    ||U(u,v)||_infinity <= exp(-integral_v^u d(w) dw).

Consequently the exact inhomogeneous solution is

    delta_s(u) = U(u,u0) delta_s(u0)
                 + integral_u0^u U(u,v) delta_b(v) dv,

with -residual_bar included when using an interpolated reference. A bound
on the forcing over the WHOLE interval is needed for an interval error bound;
the minimum damping at one target point is not such a bound.

delta_s_stationary=-A^{-1}delta_b. This is a useful transient diagnostic,
