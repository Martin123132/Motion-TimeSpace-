# Stage56 predeclared scope and gates

The target is a sourced dense linear matter spectrum, converged rms amplitude
and velocity/growth diagnostics, and a transparent first comparison with actual
saved SDSS growth data. This is not an observational parameter fit or an automatic
license to treat compressed RSD constraints as model-independent measurements.

- Every admitted heavy k has its own early exact native evolution and phase projection.
- Use nested grids on 1e-5..10 Mpc^-1 plus a denser .02.. .4 acoustic region.
- Compare sigma8 on both k grids; require relative change below .2%. Check lower/upper-tail sensitivity and comparison of integration methods explicitly.
- Recover independently computed native CAMB sigma8 for both cold controls within .2%; do not use its dark-slot sigma8 for heavy matter.
- Require once-only handoff and unchanged other states for every heavy mode; no interpolation of Stage55's four rows.
- Test matching epochs and projection choices; keep changes in sigma8 below .2%. Retain errors at high k even if their contribution to sigma8 is negligible.
- Separate total-matter density, cold+baryon density, and tracer velocity statistics. Do not equate them by naming convention.
- SDSS covariance must be sourced, symmetric and positive definite. Marginal growth covariance is a submatrix of covariance, not a submatrix of precision.
- Avoid double-counting the replaced DR12 high-redshift bin and non-Gaussian ELG approximations. Use the two retained DR12 bins plus separate DR16 LRG/QSO growth rows.
- Use identical primordial/cosmological benchmark settings and data rows for all branches. Any compressed-data score is a conditional screening diagnostic, not an evidence or preference claim.
- Quantify scale dependence of growth and transfer shape over a declared RSD-like band before interpreting a single f-sigma8 number.
- New work local only; preserve Stage55, original framework and galaxy work. Single-threaded libraries, one active numerical worker, checkpoint every batch.

## Numerical scope refinement before late runs

The source projection fails the inherited2e-6 pressure gate at a few k>6.85
tail modes in at least one matching epoch; raising projection degree does not
remove it. Preserve the gate and the failures. Use k<=5 Mpc^-1 for the primary
integral/qualified curve, with the same cut for both controls. Retain5..10 as an
explicitly exploratory tail-sensitivity calculation, not an equally validated
extension. This numerical scope change is made before the data comparison.
