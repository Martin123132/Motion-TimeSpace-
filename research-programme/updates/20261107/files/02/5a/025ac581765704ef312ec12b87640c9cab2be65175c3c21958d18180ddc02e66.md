## 2. Derive the interfaces the interaction requires

The native potential depends on q(X_j). Define A_j=partial U_native/partial q(X_j) and C_j=partial U_native/partial X_j at fixed native values and Eulerian metric, retaining the sampled beta_R term. For beta=R^2*N*sqrt(F), regular piecewise wave acceleration and massless-support force balance require

    [beta*q_R]_j=A_j,
    [beta*q_R^2]_j/2+C_j=0.

At a continuous positive beta and A_j nonzero, these determine

    mean_j=-C_j/A_j,
    g_plus/minus,j=mean_j +/- A_j/(2*beta_j).

This supplies actual one-sided slopes rather than another unsuccessful smooth ansatz. The original native covectors, including their full atom implementation and all33 shape directions, verify the reduction.
