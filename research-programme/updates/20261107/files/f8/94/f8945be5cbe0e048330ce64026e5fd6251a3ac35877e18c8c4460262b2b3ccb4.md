## Constructive derivation

    q' = A_c q + (A_c-A_b)y_b - r_b,
    r_b = y_b' - A_b y_b - f,
    Delta S(u) = L(u)U_c(u,u0)q(u0)
      + integral L(u)U_c(u,v)[(A_c-A_b)y_b-r_b](v) dv.

The stored reference's interpolation defect is explicit, not set to zero.
Eliminating history in favour of a linear source-only equation requires

The criterion is proved but not claimed numerically established for the actual
MTS operator. An exact conserving counterexample shows why energy balance
alone does not guarantee a correct source-temperature law.

physically evolved target history, rather than interpolating sources that
carry different histories. Test that construction at a different temperature,
