but its unconstrained unit-basis transfer composition differed by1.02415323e-6
(required1e-10). Its code and outputs remain in `attempt1-matching198.py` and
`runs/heavy-2048/`. The peak is the gas response to the energy-coordinate unit
direction. Gas was computed as g_free-c_E*E: both terms contain1/chi, so the
small heat capacity loses digits for these unit directions.

Retain the exact coupled photon/gas equations, but eliminate photons first:

    A_p = alpha M_c - rate P
    F = A_p^-1 (M_c history + rate force)
    R = A_p^-1 coupling
    D = chi(alpha+1)+alpha R_E
    g = (history_E-alpha F_E+chi history_g+rate source_pair)/D
    state = F+R*g.

original energy-first Schur complement. The denominator includes radiation's
thermal response instead of dividing by the small gas capacity alone. Require
positive finite D. Use fused multiply-add for history_E-alpha*F_E. The matrix
bank, source, coordinates, time steps, startup rules and acceptance tolerances
are untouched. Recheck physical replay as well as the unit-basis transfer; do
not infer agreement merely from algebraic equivalence.

This changes arithmetic conditioning, not the physical source or model.
