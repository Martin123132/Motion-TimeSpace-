## 1. Derived equation and exact test definition

Protocol: `DERIVATION-20260928-directional-coupled-Euler-test.md`.

The direction is the same test1 on both branches: subtract the piecewise-affine interpolant through the physical endpoints and source, then use cell-affine edge motion. Midpoints move by the mean of their edges. The endpoints and source are fixed. Both edge motion and continuous averaged-gradient field transport are interpolated in the original15-cardinal material space. No reference residual is subtracted from MTS as a correction.

With mechanical Euler sign E=Pdot-L_coordinate, physical gradient g, mesh velocity W and T=qdot-Wg, define

    d=z-eta*g
    A_dot=(alpha_t+W*alpha_R+W_R*alpha)*T+alpha*Tdot
    Tdot=qddot-Wdot*g-W*(qdot_R-W_R*g)
    C=alpha*W*T+beta*g.

The finite-space wave equation obeys the independently checked identity

    E_wave[z,eta] = integral (A_dot*d+C*d_R) dR
                   - sum_faces eta*(Ldensity_left-Ldensity_right).

The native kernel contributes its already derived action variation

    B_G=U_q dot z + U_edges dot eta
        +sum_native load*beta_R*eta_native,
    E_total=E_wave+B_G.

Actual archived coupled accelerations and metric time derivatives enter A_dot. They are not replaced by zero or fitted to cancel the result. The material-interpolation defect, spatial trace defect, face work and kernel variation are separate channels. Dust has no direct contribution in this source-pinned direction.

    delta(dot l)=S^T[f*(D_R*chi + D_R'[zeta]*q)],
