# Stage171: temperature variation of the complete finite electron sum

Use xi=ln(theta), with theta=k_B T/(m_e c^2). At fixed dimensionless incoming
frequency x=14 and scattering cosine0, each inherited event contributes

    s_a(xi) = -W_a(xi) F(P_a(xi), h(xi));  S(xi) = sum_a s_a(xi).

W already contains normalized electron probability, reflection multiplicity,
incoming photon quadrature/Planck factor, hard-angle weight and Born differential.
The saved event vectors are s_a, not F. Therefore their sum requires no new weights.
For the finite rule, differentiation and summation commute exactly. With a smooth
incident field, D=d/dxi, L_a=D ln W_a,

    DS = -sum_a W_a [L_a F + DF],
    D2S = -sum_a W_a [(L_a^2 + DL_a)F + 2 L_a DF + D2F].

DF includes parameter, incident-field and moving integration-boundary derivatives;
Stage170 derives these terms. Here native histories are only piecewise linear in
time, so we test finite-step variations, not classical differentiability at a knot.
Fixed incident histories separately isolate kernel and thermal-population changes.

For normalized radial weights w_a=r_a/Z, L_a=D ln r_a-<D ln r>. Consequently
sum_a Dw_a=0. Quadrature azimuths0,pi/2,pi,3pi/2 use reflection symmetry in the
scattering plane: retain0,1,2, double1, omit3. Reflection representatives alone
do NOT have zero y-momentum: expand the mirrored velocities when checking odd
moments. The reconstructed16-node rule has sum w=1, sum w beta=0 and
sum w beta_i beta_j=delta_ij <beta^2>/3 at each temperature. These statements
use bare electron probabilities, not the velocity-dependent Born weights.
