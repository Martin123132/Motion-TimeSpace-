# Sources and reproduction scope

## External primary source

D. I. Nagirner and J. Poutanen, *Relativistic kinetic equation for induced
Compton scattering of polarized radiation*, Astronomy & Astrophysics379,
664–682 (2001). DOI https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361:20011198 .
Accessible source: https://arxiv.org/html/astro-ph/0108357 .
Local archive: `sources/nagirner-poutanen-0108357.html`.
Archive hash and metadata: `results/matrix-pair/status.json`.
The rendering date is not the paper's publication date.

Used for the density-matrix collision structure, induced terms, loss
anticommutator and reciprocal channel. The nondegenerate electron assumption,
isotropic scalar background, and shared-temperature gas approximation are
explicit. The derivatives, stable evaluation, mixed bank, coupled response and
background-relaxation diagnostics in this stage are our own implementation and
checks of that structure. Established Compton physics is not an MTS discovery.
