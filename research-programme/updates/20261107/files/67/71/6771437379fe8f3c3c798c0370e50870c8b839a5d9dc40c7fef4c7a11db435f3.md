## Derived and measured: the interface amplification

At fixed initial geometry, let L be the field-linear part of the second moving-jump residual. At a native interface,

    L(delta a) = beta * [partial_R delta a] - (partial_q A) delta a.

At an inserted force-free midpoint the second term is absent. The native derivative is computed from the same quadratic native action, not fitted from the observed discrepancy. The full source/field inertia matrix H is positive definite in the tested solves. Solving H*z=L^T gives the exact finite-dimensional conditional bound

    |L(delta a)| <= sqrt(L*H^(-1)*L^T) * sqrt(delta a^T*H*delta a).

This follows directly by Cauchy--Schwarz in the H inner product. It bounds the field-linear contribution at the fixed geometry, not every nonlinear geometric/source term. The adjoint uses the full coupled Hessian, even though L has zero direct source component.

The saved coarse acceleration is prolonged with the exact rational P7 map, and delta a is the fine acceleration minus that prolongation. The measured kinetic defect norm is 5.96915065e-8. Three independent witness calculations give:

| Old native maximum (-0.5, column 609) | 5.62253e9 | +2.48564941025 | +2.48564941025 |
| New midpoint maximum (+0.5, column 601) | 4.96246e11 | -2.17174518368 | -2.17174518368 |

At the new native witness the coarse residual was -0.6879008710, not the coarse *maximum* 3.2165; the fine residual is +15.2782891332. Comparing at the same location matters. At the old maximum the residual improves from -3.2165035509 to -0.7308541407, but a different interface becomes worse.

The full observed changes and the independent fixed-geometry field-linear predictions differ by at most 1.78e-15 in these floating comparisons. This is measured agreement, not an interval-certified error enclosure. The adjoint relative residuals are 3.0e-15--5.3e-15. All 25 construction, adjoint, Decimal-functional and bound checks pass.

The rigorous conditional bounds are deliberately reported even though they are loose: approximately 19737, 336 and 29622 for the three cases. They do **not** certify small interface errors. They explain why the small overall kinetic defect cannot be used as that certificate: these derivative/native-load observables are extremely sensitive on this mesh.

This identifies the measured mesh-to-mesh increase as an amplified change in the field acceleration at the tested witnesses. It does not yet distinguish spatial truncation, material resolution and floating arithmetic within that acceleration change. No missing coupling is inferred or inserted from this diagnostic.
