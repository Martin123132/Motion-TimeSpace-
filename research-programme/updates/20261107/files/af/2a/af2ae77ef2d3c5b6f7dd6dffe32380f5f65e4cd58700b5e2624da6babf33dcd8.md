## Calibrated stress, not a shape norm

With s=ln a, rho_star proportional to a^-4, and the inherited heat ratio chi:

    q = delta rho/rho_star = chi (E + chi g)
    p = delta p/rho_star   = chi (E/3 + 2 chi g/3)
    delta rho_s + 3(delta rho + delta p) = chi rho_star J_s

Gas density is chi^2 g. The actual source injection J_s is not zeroed.
Removing gas pressure leaves the derived residual -2 chi^2 rho_star g.
That omission is tested at its own scale; it is not called observationally
detectable merely because an algebraic negative control finds it.

2048/4096 response resolutions, both joins, quadratic/secant source profiles,
quarter/eighth histories, baseline/MTS/total and a zero control.
Conversion to SI uses the existing photon energy normalization.

## Coupled field response and the sharper bound

DERIVATION.md derives the canonical two-field/Friedmann tangent operator,
its adjoint and a conservative norm majorant. Field response is included;
a frozen-field Friedmann estimate is not substituted for it.

ENERGY-WORK-BOUND.md exploits opposite photon/gas exchange:

    q_s = -chi^2 g
    V^2 = photon^T M photon + chi g^2
    (V^2/2)_s <= -chi g^2

For source-free duration L and saved initial norm V0:

    Q_work = |q0| + chi^(3/2) V0 min(L,sqrt(L/2))
    Q_sharp = min(Q_work,Q_dual)
    |delta H/H| <= G_source Q_sharp + G_incoming ||z_initial||

Here z=(m_i delta phi_i,delta pi_i/a). The incoming term is never erased.
The bounds do not require a fitted relaxation rate or a positive spectral gap.
The physical photon energy moment is used for q0 rather than discarding its
small discrepancy with the separately carried energy variable.
