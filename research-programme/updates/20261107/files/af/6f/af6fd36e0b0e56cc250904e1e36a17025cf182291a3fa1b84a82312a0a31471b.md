## 1. Inputs, conventions, and scope

The retained theory is the complex-scalar condensate with Einstein-Hilbert
gravity and fixed G_R. This is a matter-response calculation inside that
theory, not a derivation of the Einstein-Hilbert term or of G_R.
The potential and background are unchanged. The numerical body coefficients
come from the actual isolated-body perturbation problems:

- `../axial-tides-20261004/results/fine-v2/status.json`: magnetic response.
- `../orbital-backreaction-20261004/results/fine/status.json`: electric response.
- `../pn-balance-20261004/pn_algebra.py`: retained first-PN electric calculation.
- `../axial-tides-20261004/22-derived-axial-response-and-magnetic-tidal-matching.md`:
  stress-derived axial equation and static matching.

Use G_R=c=1 in the inherited geometric units. Let M_T=m_body+m_comp,
chi=m_body/M_T, a=1-chi, eta=chi*a, and mu=eta*M_T. Define

    x=(M_T*Omega)^(2/3), d0=M_T/x,
    t=lambda/d0^5, b=sigma_bar/d0^5.

Both lambda and sigma_bar have dimensions length^5. The barred coefficient
means the geometric coefficient G_R*sigma_bar_phys with c=1. Its convention is

    S_ij=sigma_bar*B_ij=2*sigma_BV*B_ij.

For the inherited reference body:

    M=0.296546804477414, R=19.73166220910539,
    lambda=62823.92235391785,
    sigma_bar=-270.0740921927915,
    sigma_BV=-135.03704609639576.

The previous electric paper labels the deformable body2. Banihashemi–Vines
label it body1. Here chi ALWAYS belongs to the deformable body, hence X1=chi,
X2=a in BV. The relative vector runs from companion to deformable body.
No physical orientation is changed without changing its associated labels.

The retained order is instantaneous point-mass1PN, leading and1PN electric
tides, and leading magnetic tides, all linear in body response. In normalized
energy/flux/chirp the basis is (1,x,t,x*t,x*b). Terms x^2, t^2, t*b, x^2*b,
spin, higher multipoles of the body response, dissipation, and propagation
tails are outside this basis. The previously tested shared1.5PN tail is not
selectively added to one sector here. Circular motion is treated adiabatically;
there is one deformable body and a point companion, not a newly solved
nonlinear two-condensate spacetime.

## 2. Source-to-multipole derivation

The external-field/current coupling and harmonic-GR multipole map are retained
matching inputs, specifically BV Eqs22,23,45,48. They are not inferred from
the desired final circular flux. The executable calculation in
`derive_magnetic_moments.py` integrates the distributional source for a generic
time-dependent STF tensor S, position z and velocity v, before imposing an
orbit or constitutive law.

The intrinsic contributions in geometric units are

    rho_S=(4/3) S_ij v_l epsilon_kli partial_jk delta(x-z),
    j_S^i=(1/3) epsilon_ijk S_jl partial_kl delta(x-z).

The second-derivative distribution has positive integration-by-parts sign.
The mass quadrupole correction uses BOTH terms

    delta I_ij = int x_<ij> rho_S d^3x
                 -(20/21) d/dt int x_<ijk> j_S^k d^3x.

The time derivative differentiates z and S, rather than freezing the induced
current as the orbit rotates. The ddot(T00) source piece involving S starts
one order higher; its point-mass counterpart is already in the parent.
The current multipole comes from the epsilon-weighted quadratic moment of
j_S. Direct STF polynomial differentiation gives

    delta I_ij=(8/9) epsilon_kl<i (2 S_j>l v_k - dot(S)_j>l z_k),
    delta J_ij=S_ij.

The intrinsic mass dipole vanishes, so Newtonian COM translation suffices at
this order. Setting z=a*r*n, v=a*r*Omega*tangent gives BV Eq46. Every tensor
component is checked symbolically. This is a reduction of the sourced GR
matching equations, not an independent observational confirmation of them.

For the companion's magnetic tide, direct epsilon contraction gives

    B_ij=(6*m_comp*Omega/r^2) n_<i e_z,j>,
    B_ij B_ij=18*m_comp^2*Omega^2/r^4.

With S=sigma_bar*B, the circular system moments are

    I_ij=mu*r^2*n_<ij>
         +(8*M_T*a^2*Omega^2*sigma_bar/(3*r))
          (5*n_<ij>+4*tangent_<ij>) + existing electric/point1PN terms,

    J_ij=[(1-2*chi)*mu*r^3*Omega
          +6*M_T*a*Omega*sigma_bar/r^2] n_<i e_z,j>
          + existing transported electric-current terms.

The intrinsic axial S is not the transported electric quadrupole current.
Their product is a quadratic-response term and is excluded. Magnetic effects
in the system mass octupole need its additional radiation1PN factor, so enter
at x^2*b. The current octupole likewise contributes beyond x*b. This is an
order argument, not an assertion that those moments vanish.

## 3. Orbit and energy at fixed frequency

The inherited matched conservative term is L_B=sigma_bar*B^2/3. For a
circular orbit it is 6*sigma_bar*m_comp^2*Omega^2/r^4. Solving the radial
equation before eliminating r, then computing E=Omega*dL/dOmega-L, yields

    delta r/d0=(8*a/chi)*x*b,
    E_B/(mu*x)=(22*a/chi)*x*b.

Do not keep the original orbit when computing the emitted mass quadrupole.
The velocity-dependent L_B also cannot be inserted as an ordinary static
potential with its sign reversed; that gives the wrong energy.

## 4. Radiation: three indispensable pieces

Contract third time derivatives of the explicit rotating STF tensors in

    P=(1/5)<I'''_ij I'''_ij>+(16/45)<J'''_ij J'''_ij>+mass-octupole piece,
    P0=(32/5)*eta^2*x^5.

The magnetic coefficient in P/P0 is the sum, multiplying x*b:

| Origin | Coefficient |
|---|---:|
| Orbital-radius change in the mass quadrupole | 32*a/chi |
| Intrinsic current contribution to the mass quadrupole | 16*a/(3*chi) |
| Interference in the current quadrupole | (1-2*chi)/(3*chi) |
| Total f_B | (114*a-1)/(3*chi) |

These terms follow from the integrals and contractions above. BV Eqs41,51,52
are subsequently checked after sigma_bar=2*sigma_BV; they are not used as
inputs to solve for the coefficients. Energy-only, current-only, missing-orbit,
and wrong-factor-two controls all fail the coefficient checks as intended.

## 5. Combined balance and phase

The inherited electric terms are reproduced, unchanged:

    E/(mu*x)=-1/2+(9+eta)*x/24
       +9*a*t/(2*chi)
       +11*a*(3+2*chi+3*chi^2)*x*t/(4*chi)
       +22*a*x*b/chi,

    P/P0=1+f1*x+ft*t+fxt*x*t+fB*x*b,
    f1=-1247/336-35*eta/12,
    ft=6*(3-2*chi)/chi,
    fxt=(-704-1803*chi+4501*chi^2-2170*chi^3)/(28*chi),
    fB=(114*a-1)/(3*chi).

Differentiate the energy holding lambda and sigma_bar fixed, not t and b:
both scale as x^5. Thus the magnetic term in the positive slope
D=-2*dE/dx/mu is -308*a*x*b/chi. Balance gives

    dx/dtime=(64*eta/(5*M_T))*x^5*C,
    C=1+c1*x+ct*t+cxt*x*t+cB*x*b,
    c1=-743/336-11*eta/4,
    ct=6*(12-11*chi)/chi,
    cxt=(4421-12263*chi+26502*chi^2-18508*chi^3)/(56*chi),
    cB=(1038*a-1)/(3*chi).

The inverse has mixed electric coefficient 2*c1*ct-cxt and magnetic
coefficient -cB. The Fourier phase, apart from the usual time/phase constants,
is

    Psi=3*x^(-5/2)/(128*eta)
         [1+psi1*x+psi_t*t+psi_xt*x*t+psi_B*x*b],
    psi1=3715/756+55*eta/9,
    psi_t=-24*(12-11*chi)/chi,
    psi_xt=-5*(3179-919*chi-2286*chi^2+260*chi^3)/(28*chi),
    psi_B=-10*(1038*a-1)/(21*chi).

Two differentiations with respect to orbital Omega reproduce
d²Psi/dOmega²=2/(dOmega/dtime). This also matches BV Eq54 after the convention
conversion. The magnetic contribution to accumulated time-domain GW phase
between fixed frequencies is the endpoint difference of

    delta Phi_B(x)=-(5*cB*sigma_bar/(112*eta*M_T^5))*x^(7/2).

Numerical quadrature independently checks that primitive. It must not be
confused with Fourier phase at one frequency, or with a detector-calibrated
dephasing. Large accumulated electric phase differences at very small mass
ratio can coexist with tiny instantaneous tidal changes: many cycles are
being integrated in this idealized model.

## 6. Numerical size and sign

At companion/body mass ratio epsilon=0.01 and d0=10R:

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---:|
| x | 0.0015179272245192548 |
| Electric chirp increment / Newtonian chirp | 1.4131834443034423e-6 |
| Magnetic chirp increment / Newtonian chirp | -4.280887856319144e-12 |
| Magnetic / electric chirp increment | -3.029251349904677e-6 |
| Magnetic flux increment / Newtonian flux | -5.939332137903041e-14 |
| Electric chirp increment / point1PN chirp, order-expanded | 1.4179789999127409e-6 |

So the derived axial correction is about3 parts per million of the electric
chirp correction for this illustrative body/orbit, not3 parts per million
of the total chirp. Its addition reduces the electric correction slightly.
The pure magnetic accumulated GW phase between20R and10R is
+0.0001979753285686493 rad for this mass ratio. These are conditional weak-field
adiabatic examples, not observations or calibrated astrophysical predictions.

A negative sigma_bar does NOT alone determine the inspiral sign. In terms
of epsilon=m_comp/m_body the new coefficients simplify to

    fB=(113*epsilon-1)/3,
    cB=(1037*epsilon-1)/3.

The flux changes sign at epsilon=1/113; the chirp changes sign at1/1037.
There is an interval where magnetic flux and chirp corrections have opposite
signs, because orbital-energy changes matter too. Both effects and their
zeros are tested, rather than imposing a sign as a prior.

The small magnetic term is NOT an error estimate for the whole waveform.
Uncomputed higher electric orders can be comparable or larger depending on
their coefficients and separation. We have not assigned their coefficients
the value one or used a dimensional proxy as an uncertainty bound. Finite
frequency response and any resonances also need their own calculation.
