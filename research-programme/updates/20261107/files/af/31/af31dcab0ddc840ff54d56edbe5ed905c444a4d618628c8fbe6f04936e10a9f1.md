Private extension of the retained constant-mass linear MTS cosmological
branch, 2026-11-07. Parent: ../mts-metric-channel-factorization-20261107/.
This continues the saved GR-reference versus spatial-Poisson comparison.
It does not alter the matter action, its gravitational normalization,

## 1. Remove derivative terms algebraically, not by freezing time

Use the parent's physical-weighted variables and explicit metric channels:

    B = F+U_Psi p+U_Phi t,
    p = -d/(2k^2),
    t = -(ell+3DeltaW p)/(2k^2 L),
    L=1+3W_q/(2k^2),     DeltaW=W_q-W,
    d U_Phi=3W_q,        d U_Psi=3DeltaW,
    ell=d_N+dF.

N=ln a; H is conformal Hubble rate; omega=am; Omega=sqrt(k^2+omega^2).
The saved physical sources are

    M=3H Q_GR/(2k^3),    S=Pi_GR/k^2.

For the exact metric-time primitive, with E=S_energy e and
V=U_Phi/L, Y=E-V M, the error equation is

    Y_N=B Y+H_M M+G S,
    G=U_Psi-[3DeltaW/(2k^2 L)]U_Phi,
    H_M=G+B V-V_N.

The initial and terminal primitive terms do not vanish even when e(0)=0.

Define the FREE commutator vector

    C=F U_Phi-(U_Phi)_N.

Differentiating dU_Phi=3W_q gives ell U_Phi=3(W_q)_N+dC.
Substitution into H_M cancels every explicit (W_q)_N term and yields

    H_M=(U_Psi+C)/L-U_Phi(dC+3DeltaW)/(2k^2 L^2).

This is exact product-rule and constraint algebra. It is not a
slow-current assumption, a slow-adjoint approximation, or a dropped
metric derivative.

## 2. Compute C from the retained species equations

For a scalar h in coordinates (Omega phi_n,pi_n),

    C_h=(3Omega v/H, 3omega^2 phi/H),
    d_h C_h=6omega^2 phi v/H.

Thus, for all retained scalar species,

    dC=6 sum_h omega_h^2 phi_h v_h/H.

All other contributions to this contraction vanish because their C
components are velocity/dipole/quadrupole, whereas the density row
uses monopoles and alpha. The alpha component of C is zero.

The remaining nonzero entries, with the parent's physical weights:

- Baryon velocity: the native baryon-pressure row applied to U_Phi.
  It is evaluated from the native pressure observable. No barotropic
  pressure law is imposed if the implementation uses more than that.
- Photon dipole: C=U_Psi in that component.
- Full massless dipole: C=U_Psi.
- High-k-tau massless dipole: C=3U_Psi.
- High-k-tau quadrupole: C=12 weight_F2/(H tau), for this retained flat
  branch whose outgoing coefficient is1/tau.
- Massive-node dipole: C=v_i^2 U_Psi in that component.
- Scalar entries are those above; all other entries are zero.

In particular, the scalar part of the first term in H_M is

    (U_Psi,h+C_h)/L=(4Omega v/H,2omega^2 phi/H)/L.

The oscillatory product phi v remains in dC. Its sign is not averaged
away. A phase-independent inequality is

    |dC| <= sum_h 3omega_h E_h^2/H,
    E_h=sqrt(v_h^2+omega_h^2 phi_h^2).

The new H_M and G require instantaneous background fields and pressure,
but no explicit background or current time derivative. The prior code's
derivative-based expression remains an independent comparison.

For validation, V_N is also evaluated directly:

    V_N=(U_Phi)_N/L-U_Phi L_N/L^2,
    L_N=3(W_q)_N/(2k^2).

The U_Phi derivatives use:

the analytic reason no M_N or noisy background derivative is needed
in the final forcing formula.

## 4. Combine sources and original physical readouts

Balance only the heavy error norm by r=k/Omega, as in the parent.
Let u_h=r||Y_h|| and u_r=||Y_r||. The parent's explicit comparison
bounds give a nonnegative two-block system, with row-sum rate Gamma.
Both coupling directions, pressure, quadrature defect and G192 remain.

Define explicit coefficient bounds on the chosen interval:

    K_M = sup max(r||H_M,h||,||H_M,r||)/k,
    K_S = sup max(r||G_h||,||G_r||)/k,
    K_V = sup max(r||V_h||,||V_r||)/H.

These are functions of the derived coefficients, not unknown response
gains or a fit to the observed error. For a normalized original physical
readout ell at the endpoint, define

    Lambda_ell=||ell_h||/r_final+||ell_r||.

Then, with e_initial=0,

    |ell e_final| <= Lambda_ell exp(integral Gamma) [
        K_V H_initial |M_initial|
        + integral k(K_M|M|+K_S|S|) dN]
        + |ell V_final M_final|.

A sharper two-block propagator replaces the scalar exponential in the
numerical budget. The displayed inequality shows all ingredients and
retains both nonzero primitive boundaries.

## 5. Sufficient conditions for a small observable error

Retain all background/norm/pressure conditions stated in the parent.
Also require a bounded positive Hubble ratio over the fixed N interval
and bounded1/(H tau) where the outgoing massless closure is active.
Let kappa_Omega=H Omega/k^2 be bounded. It need not tend to zero.

The explicit species entries give

    ||balanced H_M||_block <= C k,
    ||balanced G||_block <= C k,
    ||balanced V||_block <= C H.

For example, the scalar numerator after multiplication by r is O(k).
The phase-dependent contraction is bounded by O(k^2 kappa_Omega).
The pressure contribution is bounded by the native pressure-row norm
times ||U_Phi||. None introduces an unknown solution difference.

For CDM define A_c=max_I|delta_c,GR|. For total density define
D_star=max_I|D_GR| and A_D=D_star/H_final^2. These are the SAME
normalizations already used for the physical observables, not new scores.
For either corresponding A_ell, define

    epsilon_star=sup_I H/k,
    q_star=sup_I |Q_GR|/(H^2 A_ell),
    pi_star=sup_I |Pi_GR|/(H^2 A_ell),
    R_H=sup_I H/H_final.

The physical dual-row coefficient

    K_ell=(H_final^2 A_ell/k) Lambda_ell

is bounded under the parent's stated norm assumptions:
for CDM it is H_final/sqrt(R_c,final);
for D it is (||d_h||/r_final+||d_r||)/k=O(1+kappa_Omega).
Thus the inverse heavy factor cannot simply be dropped, but it does
not defeat this bounded-kappa estimate.

Substitution into the previous inequality gives the sufficient bound

    normalized_error_ell <=
      R_H^2 K_ell exp(integral Gamma) [
        (3/2)K_V epsilon_star^2 q_star
        + DeltaN((3/2)K_M epsilon_star q_star+K_S pi_star)]
      + terminal_ell.

The endpoint term is also explicit:

    terminal_CDM <= (9/2)epsilon_star^3 q_star/L_final,
    terminal_D <= (9/2)(W_q,final/H_final^2)
                         epsilon_star^3 q_star/L_final.

No numerical derivative of Q appears. Fast variation is allowed;
the amplitude, stress and prepared initial data are what enter.

CONDITIONAL CONSEQUENCE: on a fixed finite N interval with the stated
uniform background/coefficient conditions, bounded q_star,
pi_star->0 and epsilon_star->0 imply vanishing original D/CDM
GR-to-spatial-Poisson error. More generally it suffices that
epsilon_star q_star->0 and pi_star->0, with the displayed boundary
products also tending to zero. Nonzero initial mismatch adds its
propagated contribution and must be bounded separately.

This is a sufficient statement for the RETAINED LINEAR BRANCH.
It is not a theorem for every nonlinear MTS solution or a local-vacuum
parent-action reduction. The reference current/shear conditions must
be checked, not assumed to follow from H/k alone. Numerical point
maxima are diagnostics, not certified continuum suprema of K.

    delta_count''+H delta_count'=-k^2 Psi.

    3|Phi_GR|/A_c <= (3/2)epsilon_star^2 d_star

sufficient weak-stress regime. It is restored at the endpoints rather
than used to change the scored observable. This connects the error
estimate to the existing Newtonian continuity/Euler route; it does
not introduce a different density calibration.

All original signed errors and the earlier tighter output-specific
