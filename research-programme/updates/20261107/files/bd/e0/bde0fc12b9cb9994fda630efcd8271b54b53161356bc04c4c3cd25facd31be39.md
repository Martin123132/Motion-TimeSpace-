## What this establishes, and what it does not measure

The hard-angle comparison is successful at the declared component-specific
incoming grids. These are observed quadrature differences, not rigorous bounds
on exact continuum error. The inherited incoming differences were measured
at hard3; this does not also calculate every mixed angle/frequency difference.
`DERIVATION.md` makes that distinction explicit rather than silently treating
the combined ledger as a uniform two-dimensional certificate.

The next uncertainty is electron averaging. The efficient first step is the
nested azimuth4-to8 rule at fixed radial2/polar2: it retains the16 old directions
with halved azimuth weights and adds16 new ones. New directions require their
actual coefficients, while verified matching nodes can be reused. This tests
the full source, not just its angular control or a fitted surrogate.

Independent polar/radial, necessary mixed-resolution and consistent domain/tail
tests follow before full physical-history feedback and a new data comparison.
