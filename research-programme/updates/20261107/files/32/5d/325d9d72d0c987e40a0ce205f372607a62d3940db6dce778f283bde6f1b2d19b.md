# Stage165: source-basis representation diagnosis

This diagnostic changes no accepted source, basis, temperature interpolation, band order, or tolerance. It explains the remaining dense-phase failures after independent state/Born and soft-recoil precision repair.

The saved basis definitions in `../expanding-thermal-gravity-20261007/stable_spectral_basis.py` and `../observer-frequency-projection-20261007/consistent_spectral_basis.py` use `b0=1/n0`, `b1=(x-mean)/n1`, and `temperature=(mean*n0,n1,0,...)`. The first temperature coefficient is stored after binary64 multiplication. The moment reconstruction instead promotes `mean` and `n0` separately to high precision.

For independently accumulated photon energy E and photon number N, the reconstructed source is S0=N/n0 and S1=(E-mean*N)/n1. With the frozen stored temperature coefficients t0,t1, its exact arithmetic residual is

`t0*S0+t1*S1-E = (t1/n1-1)*E + (t0/n0-t1*mean/n1)*N`.

In these banks t1=n1, but `t0` is the rounded product `mean*n0`. Its tiny multiplication remainder therefore produces an N-proportional discrepancy, magnified when cubic interpolation nearly cancels E. This is distinguishable from a collision-energy defect: the independently computed electron/gas term is not used in this identity.

The script independently reassembles E,N from saved primitive moments and checks this factorization against all 4,097 response phases, both windows, both interpolation routes and all physical order increments. It separately reports the discrepancy already present before binary64 source casting and the extra discrepancy produced by casting. Previously failed gates remain failed; explaining a failure does not waive it.

A non-adopted probe retains both high and low parts of the same analytic basis product and reconstructed source moments. For an 80-digit value v, store high=float(v), low=float(v-high), and evaluate high+low. No coefficients are fitted, no gas value is assigned from photon energy, and no energy-pivot projection is applied. The probe tests whether consistent higher-precision representation can resolve the discrepancy without altering the physical basis definition. It does not authorize response propagation: downstream forcing, numerical-reference accounting, baseline and MTS must first be checked with the same representation.
