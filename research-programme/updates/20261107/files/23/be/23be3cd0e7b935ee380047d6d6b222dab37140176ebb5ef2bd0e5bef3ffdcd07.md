# C1: velocity response of the repaired current

## Scope and the direction being differentiated

This checkpoint differentiates the actual zero-position velocity direction in `annular_reacted_support_columns_20260929.py`. Instantaneous field and source positions, cell widths, integration measures, and interface locations stay fixed. Field rates change by d, map speeds change by w, accelerations by the sourced old-field reaction, and the metric and its time derivative by their separately owned responses. In particular w contains the edge-generator contribution as well as the source-velocity contribution. It is not merely displacement times a source-speed change.

The source's position does not vary in C1. Thus the primitive I and moment h, and all spatial face locations, are fixed under this derivative. Velocity-dependent face densities must still vary wherever a face formulation is used. This is not the moving-position C0 derivative.

## Exact bulk formula

Use g=field_r, v=field material rate, W=map speed, a=field material acceleration, and Z=map acceleration. Define

    T = v-W*g, b = v_r-W_r*g, s = a-Z*g-W*b,
    L = alpha_t+W*alpha_r+W_r*alpha,
    A = L*T+alpha*s,
    F_wave = alpha*W*T+beta*g,
    E = (alpha*T^2+beta*g^2)/2,
    H = W*(beta_r*g^2-alpha_r*T^2)/2+beta*g*v_r-W_r*E.

For V=P2[v*I] and D=T*I-V, the already-derived current is J=A*D-F_wave*V_r+H*I+E*W*h. Holding positions fixed gives

    e = delta(T) = d-w*g,
    delta(b) = d_r-w_r*g,
    delta(s) = delta(a)-delta(Z)*g-w*b-W*delta(b),
    delta(L) = delta(alpha_t)+w*alpha_r+W*delta(alpha_r)+w_r*alpha+W_r*delta(alpha),
    delta(A) = delta(L)*T+L*e+delta(alpha)*s+alpha*delta(s),
    delta(F_wave) = delta(alpha)*W*T+alpha*w*T+alpha*W*e+delta(beta)*g,
    delta(E) = (delta(alpha)*T^2+delta(beta)*g^2)/2+alpha*T*e,
    delta(H) = w*(beta_r*g^2-alpha_r*T^2)/2
             +W*(delta(beta_r)*g^2-delta(alpha_r)*T^2-2*alpha_r*T*e)/2
             +delta(beta)*g*v_r+beta*g*d_r-w_r*E-W_r*delta(E).

Consequently

    delta(J) = delta(A)*D+A*(e*I-P2[d*I])
             -delta(F_wave)*V_r-F_wave*(P2[d*I])_r
             +delta(H)*I+(delta(E)*W+E*w)*h.

Both weighted products use the existing coefficient-based interpolation-remainder formula, including its registered-coordinate defect. This identity does not justify replacing a small e by zero. Any compensated representation of e must be compared against the same parent source interpretation, not a different rounded packet.

## Metric response

Let F=1-2m/r, alpha=r^2/(N*sqrt(F)), beta=r^2*N*sqrt(F), N=exp(nu), at fixed radius. With c=delta(nu)-delta(m)/(r*F),

    delta(alpha)=-alpha*c, delta(beta)=beta*c,
    delta(alpha_r)=-alpha_r*c-alpha*c_r,
    delta(beta_r)=beta_r*c+beta*c_r,
    c_r=delta(nu_r)-delta(m_r)/(r*F)+delta(m)*(F+r*F_r)/(r*F)^2.

For t=nu_t-m_t/(r*F), alpha_t=-alpha*t and

    delta(t)=delta(nu_t)-delta(m_t)/(r*F)-2*m_t*delta(m)/(r^2*F^2),
    delta(alpha_t)=-delta(alpha)*t-alpha*delta(t).

The induced metric-time term is not optional. It is taken from the completed old-field reaction, not the position-response candidate used in C0.

## Dust–gravity response

For C=N/(kappa*sqrt(F)), R=m_t+v_source*D_dust and D_dust=kappa*M*rho*sqrt(F)*N/ell, the fixed-position C1 direction has delta(rho)=0 and

    delta(C*R)=C*[delta(m_t)+delta(v_source)*D_dust+v_source*delta(D_dust)
                          +(delta(nu)+delta(m)/(r*F))*R],
    delta(D_dust)/D_dust=-delta(m)/(r*F)+delta(nu)
        -(N^2*delta(nu)-v_source*delta(v_source)/F-v_source^2*delta(m)/(r*F^2))/ell^2.

Use this without dividing by D_dust at vanishing support weight. Native terms remain separately sourced. A successful bulk identity is not by itself a complete integrated C1 result.
