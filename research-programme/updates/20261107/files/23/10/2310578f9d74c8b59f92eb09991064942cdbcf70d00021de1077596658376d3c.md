## Coordinates and normalization

For each event, let s_i be the retained incoming/outgoing sign (-1/+1),
n_i = 1/expm1(x_i), a_i = n_i + 1(s_i<0), b_i = n_i + 1(s_i>0).
Then a_i-b_i=-s_i. The photon potential is
phi_i = delta n_i/[n_i(1+n_i)]; g=delta T/T; velocity is v/c and density is
a fractional perturbation. Electron energies and momenta are in the retained
m_e c^2 and m_e c units; theta=k_B T/(m_e c^2). All coefficients below are
dimensionless. The common equilibrium event measure is outside this flux.

At zero velocity/density the normalized exact collision imbalance is

    F(phi,g) = r_initial(g) product_i(1+a_i phi_i)
             - r_final(g) product_i(1+b_i phi_i).

Here r_E is the retained relativistic electron distribution ratio, normalized
at g=0. Define S_E=(E-mean(E))/theta and DeltaS=-DeltaE/theta, so
S_final=S_initial-DeltaS. Differentiation of the same distribution gives

    r_E(0)=1, r'_E(0)=S_E,
    r''_E(0)=S_E^2-2 S_E-C_V.

C_V=d mean(E)/d theta is common to initial and final electrons at this bath
temperature, so it cancels in their second-derivative difference. It is not
set to zero or replaced by a fitted heat capacity.

## Derived coupling matrix

Write H for the photon/gas Hessian of F at equilibrium. Direct differentiation
gives

    H_ii = 0,
    H_ij = a_i a_j - b_i b_j                            (i != j),
    H_ig = H_gi = a_i S_initial - b_i S_final,
    H_gg = DeltaS (S_initial+S_final-2).

The vanishing diagonal follows from each photon factor being affine in its
own occupation perturbation. It does not say that the whole collision law
is linear. The retained mixed source is H times the background perturbation;
it is the second-order expansion about equilibrium, not the exact finite-
amplitude nonlinear flux.

For stable evaluation, the same entries are written without subtraction of
nearly equal products:

    H_ij = -[s_i(a_j+b_j)+s_j(a_i+b_i)]/2,
    H_ig = a_i DeltaS - s_i S_final.

The retained velocity and density derivatives add rectangular rows, with
p_final=p_initial+Delta p:

    H_vj,i = [-p_initial,j s_i - Delta p_j b_i]/theta,
    H_vj,g = [p_initial,j DeltaS - Delta p_j(S_final-1)]/theta,
    H_density,i = -s_i,    H_density,g = DeltaS.

These follow by differentiating the velocity score
(r_initial p_initial-r_final p_final)/[theta(1+g)] and the density prefactor
(1+density) in raw_flux. Only photon/gas background columns are used by the
retained calculation; no untested background velocity sector is introduced.
The rectangular matrix is not claimed symmetric; its photon/gas square
block is symmetric. Equilibrium wave scores are
(-s_i, DeltaS, -Delta p/theta, 0).
