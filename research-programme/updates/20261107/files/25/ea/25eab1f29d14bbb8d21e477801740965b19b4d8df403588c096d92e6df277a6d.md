# Stage117 calculation

## The frequency refinement changes no physical event

For a saved Doppler ratio r, let s=ln(r), h the new log spacing,
j=floor(s/h). With y=1/x, linear interpolation has weights

    w_plus = [1-exp(-(s-j h))]/[1-exp(-h)],
    w_minus =1-w_plus.

Both are nonnegative. They sum to1 and satisfy

    w_minus exp(-j h)+w_plus exp(-(j+1)h)=exp(-s).

Depositing each saved positive rate on these two offsets preserves both
constant slope and the chemical-potential1/x mode. The physical moment
lambda1=sum rates*(1-1/r) is reused, not fitted. The same four-point
background interpolation and positive small-defect evolution from Stage116
then apply on2633 nodes. DC=kappa/x^2 and BR use the existing functions.
The source target at the low exterior is unchanged.

The sparse interior can be assembled directly from its diagonals: for
row i, column i+j, the entry is the rate at offset j. This is the same
matrix as the old extended-grid slice, verified numerically before use.

## First-response feedback

The inherited formulas are unchanged:

    q=x W e; m=<V,q>; p=M^-1 m;
    D=C_Compton[q]+C_DC[q]+C_BR[q]-K p.
    M z'=R[K(z-t b)+D],
    chi(b'+b)=-R t^T[K(z-t b)+D].

The physical readout is V z+q-V p, with expansion-work derivative chi b.
Keep the whole compact weak Compton operator, all DC legs and both windows.

## A higher-precision coupled step, not an energy-ledger substitute

Let alpha=1/dt for the first step and3/(2dt) subsequently. Let h_z,h_b be
the BE/BDF history terms assembled from the preceding stored states.
The original equations give a97-by97 block system:

    [ alpha M-R K          R K t                   ] [z]
    [ R t^T K              chi(alpha+1)-R t^T K t   ] [b]
      = [ M h_z+R D ; chi h_b-R t^T D ].

Solving this block system in arbitrary precision tests the gas equation
directly. It does not infer that equation from a separately enforced total
energy row. Both photon and gas rows are checked against the solved
state, at50 and70 digits. Matrices M,K, drivers, dt and prior states are
the saved double-precision inputs promoted exactly; the assembly/history
arithmetic and linear solve are genuinely higher precision.

The test distinguishes local solve/assembly errors from residual-evaluation
rounding. Conversion back to double is a separate experiment. It neither
reconstructs missing digits of the physical kernels nor substitutes a few
accurate steps for an entire high-precision evolution. Canonical energy
is computed as t^T M z here, with no manually rewritten mass row.
