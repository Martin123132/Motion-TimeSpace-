## What made the update conservative

The evolved heat variable is U=A0+chi C0 g. Its initial value is A0(0), not1.
The same BE/BDF2 derivative is used for photon energy, gas heat and expansion
work. The resulting identities are

    E_NR+chi g_NR+W_NR=0,
    dE+dU+dW=A0(0)-1.

This includes the temperature dependence of heat capacity without applying an
invalid discrete product rule. The directly evolved difference has its own
gas Schur solve, so its source is not recovered through cancellation of the
two much larger completed runs. Dropping initial excess heat fails the frozen
prediction negative control. Matrix files were never edited or clipped.

The initial preflight independently checked the97-state coupled step in
80-digit arithmetic at three epochs per parent and derived the capacity
derivative from the population's third central moment. All33 checks passed.
The positive-definite upper-triangle convention of the inherited float solver
is explicit; raw physical-equation residuals are measured separately.
