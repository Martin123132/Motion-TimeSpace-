# Coarse-source diagnostic retained

During the first polar spectrum calculation, the e=0.6 coarse source grid
(16 composite panels per half-period, order24 on each) stopped resolving
the far sidebands adequately. At n=96 the metric/source reciprocal integral
disagreed by about6.96e-8 on the absolute-integrand scale, despite a modal
optical identity accurate to floating-point rounding. The latter is an
algebraic identity of the supplied overlaps and cannot establish that those
overlaps were integrated correctly.

The original coarse-e03, coarse-e06, coarse-tail03 and coarse-tail06 runs are
retained as numerical diagnostics, not the accepted radial-refinement dataset.
In particular, an apparent far-tail or resonant signal in an underresolved run
is not a physical result. Final numerical sizes are recorded by the validator.

run_resolved_coarse.py repeats the coarse interior and exterior calculation
while increasing the source grid to32 panels per half-period and order40.
It imports the unchanged original runner and overrides only the source-grid
settings; both scripts are hashed in the run's input provenance. The separate
quadrature-* runs use that same dense grid with the fine interior/exterior
settings. The accepted fine spectra retain24 panels of order32 and are checked
against those independent denser-source runs.

This is a deliberately retained failed numerical control and a documented
resolution correction, not a physical failure of either the body or BH model.

Final outcome: at e=.6,m=2,n=159 the original coarse-grid metric/source mismatch
is0.15725, and its spurious outgoing power is1.96e-6 per mu^2, vastly larger than
the entire resolved orbit's power. This is a quadrature artefact, not a resonance.
The accepted resolved coarse/fine aggregate change is at most9.40e-8 on absolute
component-sum scales; the independent fine/denser-source change is1.00e-13.
The validator retains maxima and rejected-mode counts for all four diagnostic
groups. Their698 wave evaluations and2 static solves are not included in the
1648 accepted wave evaluations and8 static solves.
