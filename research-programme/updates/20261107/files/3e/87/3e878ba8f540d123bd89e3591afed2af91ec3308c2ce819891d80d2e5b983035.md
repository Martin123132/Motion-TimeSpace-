## 1. Extend the calculation, not the theory
Use f=Phi/x, u=ln(a/a_start), and the inherited soft equation

    f'=R[M f+lambda1(f-b)+Gamma_DC(beta_H-f)+Gamma_BR(b-f)].

The original low exterior is B=(Gamma_DC beta_H+Gamma_BR b)/Gamma.
The high exterior is the saved outer spectrum. These are unchanged.
Inside the original domain, choose the known reference g=Phi_outer/x,
and evolve e=f-g from e_initial=0. On the exterior choose the reference
equal to the prescribed physical data, so exterior e=0. This is
bookkeeping, not a replacement boundary law.

The well-balanced interior equation is

    e'=A_low e+S_ref-g',
    A_low=R[M_low,in+diag(lambda1-Gamma)],
    S_ref=R[M_ref g+lambda1(g-b)
            +Gamma_DC(beta_H-g)+Gamma_BR(b-g)].

Away from the lower exterior, M_ref uses the Stage115 four/six-point
interpolation of the known spectrum in1/x at saved physical Doppler ratios.
The positive inherited two-point operator acts on the unknown e.
At rows whose higher-order stencil reaches below the original frequency
floor, M_ref uses the original positive transport with B on its lower
collar. No rational basis is extrapolated below1e-5, and no outer value
is substituted for B. Both grids use this same geometrical rule.

The source can equivalently be read as the old discrete equation plus
(M_ref-M_low)g: an explicitly tested consistency correction to scattering
of the known background. It is not a new force or adjustable physical rate.
In the common continuum limit both operators approach the same M.
Constant-temperature equilibrium holds when beta_H=b and all exterior
slopes equal b. The positivity proof belongs to A_low; higher-order
weights act on the known forcing. No BDF2 comparison envelope is asserted.

BDF2 after one BE start uses

    (a0 I-h A)e_n=-a1 e_prev-a2 e_older+h S_ref
                  -(a0 g_n+a1 g_prev+a2 g_older).

This retains memory and the actual saved-driver time interpolation.

## 3. Lossless linear compression of the existing DC quadrature
The old three-leg quadrature is linear in q, and a not-a-knot cubic
spline is linear in its nodal e values. Let B_node c=e and let B_point
evaluate that same spline at quadrature points. Then

    q_point=diag(x W) B_point B_node^-1 e.
    C_DC/ kappa_DC
      =D_soft q_soft+D_hard q_hard-D_seed q_seed
      =H_e e.

Build H_e once per frequency grid and fixed window using sparse spline
design matrices and the unchanged sourced quadrature. Verify it against
direct CubicSpline evaluation and the uncompressed DC sum before use.
This saves repeated large quadrature evaluations; it is not a new
low-frequency approximation, fitted spectral projection or discarded leg.
