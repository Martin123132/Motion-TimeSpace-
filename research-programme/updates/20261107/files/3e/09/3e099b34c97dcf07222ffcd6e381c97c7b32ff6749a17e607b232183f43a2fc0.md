Set reduced Planck mass M_Pl²=(8 pi G)^(-1), c=hbar=1, varphi=phi_physical/M_Pl. Divide the scalar action by M_Pl² throughout:
S_phi/M_Pl²=-1/2 integral sqrt(-g)[g^{ab} partial_a varphi partial_b varphi+m²varphi²].
Signature (-,+,+,+). This is the scalar part only; retain the parent Einstein, other-field and kinetic-species sectors.

B and c have units Mpc^-1/2. h below is the spatial metric TRACE, not H; Q_mic is a coordinate primitive, not momentum Q_s or electric charge.

## 2. Vary the metric before fixing its lapse/shift

Use ADM lapse L=a(1+alpha), shift N^i=partial_i beta, gamma_ij=a²(delta_ij+h_ij), h=tr(h_ij), h2=h_ij h_ij. Quadratic coefficients refer to the expansion in a bookkeeping perturbation parameter, without a separate 1/2 convention.

The scalar integrand is
L sqrt(gamma)[(varphi'-N^i partial_i varphi)²/(2L²)-gamma^{ij}partial_i varphi partial_j varphi/2-m²varphi²/2].
Expanding gives
L2=a²/2[u²-(grad d)²-omega²d²]
 +a²[(h/2-alpha)v u-(h/2+alpha)omega²phi d-v grad beta.grad d]
 +L2_metric,
L2_metric=a²/2[v²alpha²-alpha h(v²+omega²phi²)/2
 +(h²/8-h2/4)(v²-omega²phi²)].

The mixed coefficients at zero metric perturbation give
delta L2/delta alpha=-a²D,
delta L2/delta h=a²P_s/2,
D=v u+omega²phi d, P_s=v u-omega²phi d.
For real Fourier modes normalized so integral(grad beta.grad d)=k² beta d, the shift coefficient is -a²kQ_s, Q_s=k v d.
Linear scalar anisotropic stress vanishes. These statements follow from the retained minimally coupled action, not a dust ansatz.

Metric-only terms cannot be removed before varying the metric: they supply background-stress/contact terms. At general lapse, the local normal-observer density and pressure also contain -alpha v². The native packets use synchronous alpha=beta=0.

## 3. The boundary term carries metric momentum

Let E_bg=v'+2Hv+omega²phi. Then, with d'=u,
a²h(vu-omega²phi d)/2
 = (a²hvd/2)' -a²vd h'/2 -a²h d E_bg/2.

The boundary B_boundary=a²hvd/2 must be retained in finite-window matching. After this rearrangement the scalar momentum is pi=a²u; before it, p=a²[u+(h/2-alpha)v]. The gravitational trace momentum receives
Delta p_h=-a²v d/2=-a²Q_s/(2k).
Thus the coupling to the metric is not just an externally prescribed force.

On the background shell E_bg=0 the synchronous scalar equation is
u'+2Hu+(k²+omega²)d+v h'/2=0.
With native h'/2=kz this is exactly the retained equation. Off the background shell there is an additional h E_bg/2. Saved dense-background errors are retained separately, not interpreted as new physics.

The synchronous scalar first-order action in N is, up to that explicit boundary,
integral dN [pi d_N
 -(pi²/(2a²)+a²(k²+omega²)d²/2)/H
 -a²vd h_N/2 -a²h d E_bg/(2H)].
Add L2_metric and the other parent sectors. Do not fix h when varying the total action.

## 4. Time-dependent rotating canonical variables

Let U=(Re B,Im B), theta_N=mu, s=a sqrt(omega),
X=(d,pi)=T U,
T=[[cos(theta)/s,sin(theta)/s],[-s sin(theta),s cos(theta)]].
J=[[0,1],[-1,0]], T^T J T=J, s_N/s=3/2.
T_N=diag(-3/2,3/2)T+mu T J.

Using pi d_N=-X^T J X_N/2+(d pi/2)_N, the rotating action is
-U^T J U_N/2-H_rot(U,N)-a²v(TU)_1 h_N/2
-a²h(TU)_1 E_bg/(2H),
plus the retained boundary and other sectors, where
H_rot=1/2 U^T [T^T diag(a²(k²+omega²),a^-2)T/H+T^T J T_N] U.
The matrix T^T J T_N is symmetric. Omitting it loses both the carrier subtraction and expansion squeezing; it is not an optional small correction.

Variation at fixed metric history gives
U_N=J[partial_U H_rot+a²v T_row1^T h_N/2]
on the background shell. Substituting h_N=2kz/H recovers the original rotating equation. Metric variation must still be performed on the complete action.

## 5. Exact second-coordinate action and sources

The verified second map is C=M U-f(y), C=(Re c,Im c), M=I-K-L_B; write L=M^-1 and f(y)=P_y y.
Both M and P_y depend on the background. The spectator vector y includes the remaining field/metric/species variables; it is NOT a future source history. Define W=C+P_y y, U=L W.

The exact scalar pullback of the rotating kinetic term is
-1/2 W^T L^T J L (C_N+P_y y_N+(P_y)_N y)
-1/2 W^T L^T J L_N W.
The Hamiltonian is H_rot(LW,N), with unchanged metric one-form
-a²v[T L W]_1 h_N/2,
background residual term and boundary. This explicitly retains time dependence, spectator velocities and metric momentum. It is not the action obtained by simply replacing B by c and keeping its old canonical bracket.

At a fixed background, the scalar two-form blocks are
Omega_CC=det(L)J,
Omega_Cy=det(L)J P_y,
Omega_yy=det(L)P_y^T J P_y.
These are the scalar contribution to the FULL form; they do not replace the other sectors' own forms. Spectator variables in the native Einstein/Boltzmann representation are not all canonical pairs.

Let t_d=T_row1, t_u=T_row2/a². The derived phase-space sources are

This is an explicit source law in the new coordinates, with the same calibrated normalization and no fitted sound speed.

## 6. What the truncated flow does to the action

The saved candidate omits E2 in c_N; at the same current state
Delta C_N=-E2, Delta U_N=L Delta C_N.
Define delta_d=Delta d_N and delta_u=Delta u_N. Parent-versus-candidate scalar Euler one-form difference in X=(d,pi) is
Delta E_X=(-a² delta_u, delta_d).
Its pullback is therefore
Delta E_C=(T L)^T Delta E_X=-det(L)J Delta C_N.
This identifies the finite variational defect precisely; the earlier numerical tolerance is not a proof that this covector vanishes.

A related distinction matters for stress. On the exact canonical equations u=d'; on the truncated trajectory d'=u+H delta_d. With represented background phi'=v+e_phi, the scalar covariant stress computed using the actual field velocity differs from the native canonical source by
D_cov-D=P_cov-P_s=e_phi u+H(v+e_phi)delta_d,
Q_cov-Q_s=k e_phi d.
The first term is represented-background error, the second is the truncation's kinematic correction. Merely adding this to density/pressure does not establish conservation of a new truncated action. A variational truncation must derive its corrections to equations and all metric sources together.

The exact off-shell source identities are
D'+H(D+3P_s)+kQ_s+kz v²
 =e_v u+omega²e_phi d+H(v delta_u+omega²phi delta_d),
Q_s'+2H Q_s-kP_s=k e_v d+Hk v delta_d,
e_v=v'+2Hv+omega²phi.
The test compares these with saved source derivatives, not only a coordinate round trip.
