# Rejected first reconstruction — retained for audit

Stage47, 6 October 2026. This note supersedes the unversioned local-map route
in this daughter only. No stage46 or earlier files were changed.

## What went wrong

The first derivation omitted the angular density contribution -b h^2 K from
-b h^2(H+2K)/2. Consequently its angular constraint coefficient lacked
+4 alpha b h^2, and its reconstruction denominator was wrong:

- Rejected: Omega = beta + y + (1+2F/3)x.
- Correct: Omega = beta + y + x.
- Here x=alpha r^2 b h^2 and y=alpha r^2 F h_prime^2.

An energy-only replay did not expose this: a substitution can preserve an
energy expression without satisfying the original constraint. The independent
curvature-versus-density check did expose it before this stage was sealed.

The corrected derivation restores that term and reruns the local map, energy,
phase elimination and principal-form proof. Only version2 results are accepted.

## Reproducible rejection control

`check_reconstruction_controls.py` deliberately reads both versions. Its
39-point off-shell checks against the original stage45 constraint give:

|---|---:|---:|
| Gravity | 0.50688315 | 2.17e-14 |

| Phase | 0.00945477 | 7.54e-16 |

These are reconstruction defects, not physical instabilities of the candidate.
The test explicitly requires the rejected map to fail and the corrected map to
pass. Results: `results/controls-v2/status.json`.

## Retained but excluded

Unversioned scripts derive_local_map.py, derive_local_energy.py,
local_system.py, inspect_local_form.py, derive_reduced_form.py,
replay_local_form.py and prove_principal_form.py belong to the rejected route.
The unversioned principal-proof script was not executed.

Their existing results/map, results/energy, results/inspect and results/reduced
folders are historical artifacts only. Their state=complete means that those
individual calculations finished, NOT that the reconstruction was accepted.
They are preserved and hashed, but excluded from accepted proof totals.

The tentative identity d_phase=beta/Omega was tested and rejected in both
routes. The positive-square expression in the version2 principal proof
replaces that guess.

During the later potential probe, an incorrect background dictionary key
caused an immediate exception before any output was saved. It was corrected
by evaluating the lapse-gradient background identity directly; that was an
implementation typo, not another change to the physical equations.
