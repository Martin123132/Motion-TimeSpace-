linear in the photon and gas departures, including its real, virtual and state

## Failure found and precision repair

The initial full attempt passed its 1% correction/mesh tests but failed **276**
stricter reconstruction identities. Its worst aggregate correction discrepancy
was **0.125928%**. Those outputs, frozen predictions and failed checks remain
intact at the root of this child.

The repair carries saved coefficients as high/low pairs and forms the fixed
spline/event feature differences before contracting them with the histories.
The same ordering is used for soft-minus-Born and derivative-plus-gas terms.
This avoids losing a small history correction when separately rounded absolute
spectra and near-equilibrium state terms are subtracted. It changes the
arithmetic representation, not the physical histories or interpolation rule.

Against the retained attempt, the largest change in an absolute source is
**4.12230e-9 relative**. The largest change in its much smaller directly
subtracted correction is **0.125770%**, while the separately calculated increment
prediction changes by only **1.39146e-11 relative**. This supports the diagnosis
of cancellation error in the original absolute subtraction, rather than a
changed physical correction. Full diagnostics are in
`results/representation-comparison.json`; no new acceptance gate was added
to that comparison.

The repair followed an observed failure and the two routes share the event
evaluator. This is a numerical superposition/precision test, not an independent
observational prediction or evidence that the retained approximation has no
missing nonlinear terms. The half/quarter comparison is an empirical numerical
check, not a rigorous bound on the complete physical error.
