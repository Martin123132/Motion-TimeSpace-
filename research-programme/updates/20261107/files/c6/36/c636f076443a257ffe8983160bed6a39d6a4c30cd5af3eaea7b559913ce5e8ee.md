The Stage119 defect equation is unchanged. At each inner step,
a e_n-h L_n e_n = -b e_(n-1)-c e_(n-2)+h f_n
                  -(a u_n+b u_(n-1)+c u_(n-2)),
with BE(1,-1,0) startup then BDF2(3/2,-2,1/2).
Only h is halved. Frequency nodes, physical source coefficients, ghost
boundaries,65 output epochs and parent drivers remain the same.
Checkpoint snapshots preserve both history states, outer histories and all
saved output, so restoring at step n resumes at n+1 without a new BE startup.

Matched forcing remains the physical collision source of q=x W e minus
the represented overlap Kp, where Mp=V^T measure q. The source operators
are reused, but their action on the new e is recalculated. The coupled
photon/gas response stays at2048 steps with identical physical readout.

For a chosen observable/norm, let d_old be its absolute10240-minus5120
difference and d_new its absolute20480-minus10240 difference. Report
r=d_new/d_old and p_eff=-log2(r). The contraction target is r<=1 and the
separate relative-sensitivity target is d_new/new amplitude<=0.01.
These are different tests. A resolved-denominator check compares d_old
against100 machine eps times the corresponding history scale.
An effective order near two is consistent with, not proof of, BDF2's
asymptotic regime. No Richardson-extrapolated result is promoted.

Peak,late-quarter,endpoint,inner-defect and final-radiation differences
are assessed separately for baseline and both MTS columns, both backgrounds
and both windows. For gas,all2049 response states are directly comparable.
Joining-window and65-knot source-interpolation dependence remain separate.
