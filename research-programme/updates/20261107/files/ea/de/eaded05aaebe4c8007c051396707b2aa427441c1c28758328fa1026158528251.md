# Same-history panel operator, no source-value closure

For each of the seven original incoming nodes, let L_ij be the finite
linearized97-input/98-output operator at anchor temperature i and join j.
The retained repair has zero changes in its two infrared tail coefficients.

All original event quadrature weights are already in L_ij. Therefore

    L_panel,i = sum_frequency_nodes L_i,node,
    S_prediction(theta,q) = [sum_i w_i(theta) L_panel,i] q.

This is linearity of the existing event functional. It does not fit a
temperature law to sources with DIFFERENT photon/gas histories. Use the
actual repaired history q(theta) supplied by the retained evolution; retain
its native-reference residual and distinguish q from the additional response.

The source-to-response map B tested in Stage217 then supplies B L_panel q.
The finite-panel lift adds no physical coefficient. A partial sum over
indices0,1,6 is just those weighted node contributions, not the complete
8–20 panel and not a renormalized approximation to it.
