# Stage128 — product interpolation and energy-moment factoring

Let s=(t-t0)/(t1-t0),L the endpoint linear interpolant,and Delta the
right-minus-left difference. A maps the sampled unresolved field d to
the Compton source covector. Matrix order is retained throughout.

Expand the product of the two linear interpolants:

    (L A)(L d) = L(A d) - s(1-s) Delta A Delta d.

Consequently the exact difference between a directly sampled product and
the endpoint chord is

    A d-L(A d) = O + H + C,
    O=(A-L A)d,
    H=(L A)(d-L d),
    C=-s(1-s)Delta A Delta d.

The operator term O also equals the direct-minus-assembled raw source on
the same saved d. Identical represented overlap cancels;DC/BR are not part
of this diagnostic decomposition. The decomposition of raw Compton source
is not automatically the decomposition of the entire net physical source.

The positive energy moment already supplied by the same kernel is

    m(theta)=sum_e w_e (r_e-1)^2(r_e+1)/r_e^3,
    temperature_basis^T A(theta)=quadrature_weight*x*m(theta)/(2 I3).

The r_e and w_e are the saved positive Doppler ratios/rates. No new
empirical coefficient is introduced. Set B=A/m and interpolate B rather
than A. The candidate A_m=m(theta)L B has the same exact analytic energy
row because every B has the same energy row. It also retains the number
invariant and the two endpoint operators. This statement concerns the
discrete source quadrature and existing kernel,not a new fundamental law.

Use the product identity again,now for scalar m times matrix B:

    A-L A = (m-L m)L B + m(B-L B) - s(1-s)Delta m Delta B.

After applying d,the three signed terms are M,Q,J. They satisfy
O=M+Q+J. The direct-minus-candidate source is exactly Q=m(B-L B)d.
Thus propagation of Q measures the residual left by energy-moment factoring;
propagation of M+J measures what factoring removes. No cancellation term
is discarded and no assumed low-temperature power law is fitted.

The fixed response is linear in its forcing. Propagating O,H,C,M,Q,J
separately therefore predicts corresponding signed final gas/radiation
changes. Verify superposition numerically rather than equating it to exact
floating-point equality. Keep a direct-source reproduction control and
full candidate trajectories with every other source knot unchanged.

All calculations are conditional on the saved inner trajectory and
initial state. Factoring the source operator alone does not refine the
inner coefficient history. That consistent refinement is the next test
if these decompositions identify the relevant mechanism.
