## 2. Executed equation, equivalent to the derived repair

Use N=ln(a), conformal velocity v, mu=am and R_other=kappa a^2 rho_other. The expanding-branch evolution is

    phi_j,N = v_j/H,
    v_j,N   = -2v_j - mu_j^2 phi_j/H,
    R_j     = (v_j^2+mu_j^2 phi_j^2)/2,
    Pbar_j  = (v_j^2-mu_j^2 phi_j^2)/2,
    H       = sqrt[(R_other+sum_j R_j)/3] > 0,
    tau_N   = 1/H.

Substituting phi_new=phi_tab+u and v_new=v_tab+w yields exactly the defect-correction equations proved in the preceding stage. Solving this direct form avoids repeatedly subtracting the old table defects and does not change the initial-value problem.

To preserve tiny slow-field motion, the evolved coordinate is phi_j-phi_j(initial), while the velocity is retained separately. The physical field is reconstructed only when required by the force or energy. The saved displacement must not be discarded merely because adding it to the constant initial field rounds away in binary64. Normalizations use initial field/velocity/mass and the declared interval, not a fit to later solutions.

The old a(tau) relation is not imposed. tau is integrated with the corrected H and the same initial clock anchor. Other density functions and their thermal-neutrino approximation are retained; this is a local physical-window repair, not a recalibrated global expansion-history fit.
