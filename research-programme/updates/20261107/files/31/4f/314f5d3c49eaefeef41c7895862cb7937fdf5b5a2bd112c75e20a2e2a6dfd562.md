# Stage115: a common-band solution driven by the outer collision defect

## 1. Same physics, independently predicted band
Use x=h nu/(k_B T_ref), u=ln(a/a_start), f=Phi/x, R=d tau/du.
The inherited leading soft equation is

    f' = R [M f +(lambda1-Gamma) f
            +Gamma_DC beta_H +Gamma_BR b-lambda1 b].

M f=sum_e dc_e [f(r_e x)-f(x)], Gamma=Gamma_DC+Gamma_BR,
Gamma_DC=kappa_DC/x^2. The temperature/hard-photon drivers b,beta_H
are saved, not fitted. BR retains sourced frequency-dependent Gaunt factors.
Use the invariant-preserving saved discretization; it is not declared
free of spatial interpolation error. Physical-shift source integrals
from Stages113/114 are not replaced by that discretization.

On a shared band B, with saved OUTER values on both exterior collars:

    f_B' = A_B f_B + F_B,
    A_B=R[M_B+diag(lambda1-Gamma)].

The exterior collar and initial spectrum are fixed from the outer history.
The inner spectrum at subsequent times is not an input. This is an
independent conditional prediction on B under a common SOFT approximation,
not proof that the full-frequency outer equation equals this approximation.
It tests whether common collision dynamics can explain their overlap.

## 2. Derived defect correction, including memory
Let g=Phi_outer/x restricted to B, and

    rho = g' - A_B g - F_B.
    e = f_B-g,    e' = A_B e-rho,    e(u0)=0.
    e(u) = -integral_(u0)^u T_B(u,v) rho(v) dv.

Thus the overlap is Phi_overlap=x(g+e). No free amplitude, spectral fit,
window parameter, or instantaneous source equilibrium is introduced.
The discrete form uses the ACTUAL saved-driver interpolation:

    rho_n=(g_n-g_(n-1))/h-A_n g_n-F_n,
    (I-h A_n)e_n=e_(n-1)-h rho_n.

Independently solve (I-h A_n)f_n=f_(n-1)+h F_n.
Their difference tests the small correction scale. Initial e is exactly
zero. Driver corners are handled by finite differences, not a guessed
time derivative of sparse snapshots.

## 3. A computable comparison envelope
A_n is Metzler. If the diagonal margin of I-h A_n is positive, it is an
M-matrix and its inverse is nonnegative. Solve

    (I-h A_n)E_n=E_(n-1)+h abs(rho_n), E_0=0.

Induction gives abs(e_n)<=E_n componentwise. The executed margin includes
loss to both exterior collars. This certifies the discrete driven solve;
it does not certify omitted full-frequency or external-driver physics.
Time/grid controls remain separate from the envelope.

## 4. Matching cannot throw away the bridge
With h_old=Phi_inner-Phi_outer, define

    h_res=Phi_inner-Phi_overlap,
    h_bridge=Phi_overlap-Phi_outer,
    h_old=h_res+h_bridge.

For the inherited fixed windows W40,W20 and canonical orthogonal
projection P_perp=I-V M^-1 <V,.>, the window difference obeys

    Delta U_res=P_perp[(W40-W20) h_res],
    ||Delta U_res|| <= ||(W40-W20)h_res|| <= ||h_res||_[.01,.04].

The same split holds for Delta U_old=Delta U_res+Delta U_bridge.
Reducing the residual alone is NOT reducing the full window sensitivity.
It identifies an equation-derived outer repair that would have to be
carried consistently in a later matched evolution. No old J term or
paired gas exchange is dropped, and no candidate replaces old responses.
The projection test uses the SAME 700-node quadrature and mass matrix.

## 5. Explicit finite-Planck remainder at physical Doppler shifts
For recoil-free y=r x, the forward weak conductance divided by its
Rayleigh-Jeans value has the exact Planck multiplier

    F_P(x,r) = r x^2 n_0(x) [1+n_0(r x)]
             = [x/expm1(x)] [r x/(-expm1(-r x))].

As x tends to zero,

    F_P=1+(r-1)x/2+[(1+r^2)/12-r/4]x^2+O(x^3).

This includes the x^2 n_0(1+n_0) weak measure as well as the outgoing
Bose ratio. Checking ONLY the Bose ratio would be inconsistent.
For any event set/domain on which |F_P-1|<=epsilon,

    |C_P[A,Phi]-C_S[A,Phi]|
       <=epsilon sqrt(D_S[A] D_S[Phi]),

where D_S is the positive soft Dirichlet form on those events.
This is Cauchy-Schwarz, not a relative bound on a cancelling net force.
Our numerical extrema cover the saved event quadrature and sampled band,
not an analytic bound for an unbounded Juttner velocity population.
We do not invent recoil data from ratios alone: recoil also needs the
incoming boost and rest-frame angle. Likewise finite-emission corrections
are not set to zero. These are separate common-space remainders.
