# Stage79 — photon number changes, energy exchange does not go missing

This extends the same two retained parent histories and finite-shift Compton
operator. It does not replace the MTS action, refit the parents, or change the
electron mass, alpha, Newton constant or species abundances. The emitting
background replaces the no-emission background only for the new route.
The earlier wave results remain the no-emission control.

## Variables and normalization

Use s=ln(a/a_start), a_start=10^-6, a_end=10^-5, x=E/(k_B T_star),
T_star=T0/a, theta_star=k_B T_star/(m_e c^2), d tau=n_e sigma_T c dt.
The ratio r=d tau/ds=opacity/H uses the same conformal Hubble convention
as the retained parent. Photons are described by

    n(x,s)=n_P(x)+chi w(x) B(x,s),  w=n_P(1+n_P),
    T_e/T_star=1+chi b(s),
    chi=(3/2)(n_e+n_H+n_He) k_B T_star / rho_gamma,reference.

The conserved species abundances make chi constant on this fully ionized
interval. The numerical basis represents B, not n itself. Define the inner
product with measure x^2 w dx/I3, I3=pi^4/15. Its mass matrix is M;
the constant and temperature functions are represented exactly by q_C and
q_T. The moment rows are N=q_C^T M and E=q_T^T M. Thus

    delta rho_gamma/rho_gamma,reference = chi E B,
    delta N_gamma/N_gamma,Planck = chi N B I3/(2 zeta(3)).

The saved photon-number *moment* is chi N B. It must not be called the
fractional photon-number change without the last conversion.

## Matched emitting equations

The inherited Compton stiffness K is a physical finite-frequency-shift
integral, not a replacement Kompaneets diffusion operator. It is symmetric
nonpositive and K q_C=0. Isotropic production/absorption has positive
absorption rate a_em(x). Its positive matrix is

    D_ij = integral x^2 w psi_i psi_j a_em(x) dx/I3.

Linearizing an emission source satisfying detailed balance about the Planck
reference gives C_em/(chi w)=-a_em(B-x b). Therefore

    M B' = r (K-D)(B-q_T b),
    E B' + chi b' = -1-chi b,
    W'=1+chi b,
    J'=-r q_C^T D(B-q_T b).

The gas gains exactly the energy lost by photons, including their emission
term: its collision contribution is minus the same photon energy moment.
There is no fitted exchange sign or amplitude. With zero initial distortion,

    E B + chi b + W = 0,    N B = J.

The second equation is now photon-number production/absorption, not number
conservation. A common temperature B=q_T b is an emission equilibrium;
a chemical-potential distortion alone is not. The symmetric nonpositive
operator K-D gives nonpositive photon entropy-quadratic dissipation at
fixed gas temperature. Gas thermal inertia is retained in the implicit
Schur-complement solve, not set to zero by a tight-coupling prescription.

Adiabatic expansion cools the gas. The resulting sign here is net photon
absorption and a negative radiation-energy change, consistent with the
known baryon-cooling mechanism. This is a required ordinary-matter sector
inside the parent models, not a new observational discovery of MTS.

## Finite-frequency derivative needed by the next wave calculation

Write A=(4 alpha/3 pi)theta_star^2/(1+14.16 theta_star), and

    R[n](x) = integral_(2x)^infinity t^4 n(t)[1+n(t-x)] G(x/t) dt,
    G(v) = [1-3y+(3/2)y^2-y^3]/(1-v),  y=v(1-v),
    H[n,xi](x)=1-n(x)[exp(x/xi)-1],
    C_DC = A R[n](x) H[n,xi](x)/x^3.

This is the sourced resting-electron Gould approximation, not an assertion
about the full relativistic DC collision integral. Its actual derivative is

    delta R[h](x)=integral_(2x)^infinity t^4 G(x/t)
       { [1+nbar(t-x)] h(t) + nbar(t) h(t-x) } dt,

    delta H = -[exp(x/xibar)-1]h(x)
               + nbar(x) exp(x/xibar) x delta xi/xibar^2,

    delta C_DC = A [Hbar delta R + Rbar delta H]/x^3.

This finite-frequency ambient derivative is generally not rank one.
The Stage78 rank-one statement remains correct for its stated soft-factor
approximation, not for this more resolved source. On our emitting background
Hbar=O(chi); Hbar delta R is O(chi epsilon) and belongs in the next mixed
wave system. The density/clock prefactor variation adds delta r times the
background collision source. The gas must receive the negative energy
moment of all these terms. Neither contribution can be dropped merely
because the background distortion is small.

As x tends to zero, G tends to1 and R[n](x) tends to the earlier
I[n]=integral t^4 n(1+n)dt. Its variation tends to the earlier delta_I.
The new integral therefore contains the old soft source as a limit rather
than discarding it or introducing a separate fitted radiation field.

There is also an exact frame check within this sourced approximation:

    R[n_P(t/xi)](x)=xi^5 R[n_P](x/xi),
    delta R[x w](x)=5 R_P(x)-x R_P'(x).

It follows by changing the integration variable t=xi u, including the
lower limit2x. The derivative is not just5R once finite frequency is
retained. This identity fixes the temperature/dilation contribution that
the next wave implementation must reproduce. The numerical check is in
`verify_emission_temperature_identity.py`.

This proves the identity for the ambient Gould functional, not for an
arbitrary relativistic rate. The background factor1/(1+14.16 theta_star)
is a reference-Planck approximation. A mixed perturbation that also claims
the corresponding relativistic order must source its spectrum-dependent
variation, or bound and explicitly omit that higher-order rate correction.
Holding it fixed defines the tested approximate functional; it is not a
proof that the true physical correction is invariant under temperature or
reference-frequency changes. This distinction matters for the next wave
and covariance checks, although the leading emitting background remains
evaluated at its actual reference Planck temperature.

## Emitting gravity and continuity

### Matched BR temperature and ion-density derivative

At fixed theta_star and x, rewrite the sourced BR coefficient using
theta_e=theta_star xi and phi=1/xi. Omitting only a common constant,

    kappa_BR = theta_star^(-7/2) xi^(-1/2)
        sum_i Z_i^2 N_i g_i(gamma_i^2/xi,x/xi),
    C_BR = kappa_BR [exp(-x/xi)(1+n)-n]/x^3.

For each species the xi derivative contains

    d[xi^(-1/2)g]/d xi = xi^(-3/2)
       [-g/2 - partial_g/partial_ln(gamma^2) - partial_g/partial_ln(u)].

Density variations enter linearly in N_i. The source derivative is the
product rule: kappa_BR delta_H + Hbar delta_kappa_BR, divided by x^3.
The second term again contributes at mixed background-distortion/wave
order. Electron density in d tau/ds is a separate prefactor variation,
not a reason to count the ion density twice. `verify_br_jacobian.py`
differentiates the actual source-table interpolator and checks joint
occupation, temperature and ion-density perturbations. At stencil changes,
the table derivative is the selected cell's one-sided derivative; this
does not establish differentiability of an exact physical Gaunt function.

### Conserved background stress

With rho_star proportional to a^-4, define

    rho_gamma=rho_star(1+chi e),  p_gamma=rho_gamma/3,
    u_gas=chi rho_star(1+chi b), p_gas=2u_gas/3,
    e=E B.

The thermal continuity remainder divided by chi rho_star is exactly

    e' + 1 + chi b + chi b' = 0.

Rest-mass species obey rho'=-3rho, neutrinos rho'=-4rho, and the retained
scalar fields obey the same scalar equations with the emitting Friedmann
density. Their stress contribution obeys rho_scalar'=-3(rho_scalar+p_scalar).
Adding these equations yields rho_total'+3(rho_total+p_total)=0.

Dense stress uses e=-s-chi integral_0^s b du-chi b, the integrated exchange
identity, and is checked against the independently evolved spectral moment.
This is not permission to project an inaccurate spectrum onto a conservation
law: the raw spectral spline and its derivative are separately tested.
The arbitrarily short initial electron relaxation layer is not claimed
pointwise resolved by the uniform expansion-time mesh.

The cold parent has an almost constant field whose derivative is around
10^-20 to10^-17 in representative expansion-time samples. Subtracting
nearby total field values loses this derivative to floating-point rounding,
even though the integrated momentum and dense increment contain it. The
initial finite-difference ODE diagnostic fails and is retained unchanged.
Differentiating the stored dense polynomial alone also fails: the solver
constructs that polynomial using a subtraction of total field endpoints,
which has already lost the tiny increment. Both failed cold packets remain.
The repair is to integrate dphi=phi-phi_initial as the state variable and
reconstruct phi only for the physical stress and force. The derivative is
then taken from the representable increment polynomial. This is an affine
coordinate change of the same field equations, not an added degree of
freedom or a relaxed residual threshold. The independent polynomial and
cancellation tests are in `verify_dense_derivative.py`; unchanged physical
histories are checked by `compare_geometry_coordinates.py`.

The emission kinetics uses the reference parent H at leading sourced-heat
order. The corrected scalar/Friedmann geometry is constructed separately;
its small feedback must be quantified. This is a controlled leading-heat
background, not an exact nonlinear kinetic/Friedmann fixed point.

## Numerical versus physical accuracy

The unchanged0.002 comparison threshold applies to numerical refinements of
the stated equations. It is not an aggregate confidence bound on all missing
QED corrections or on observational predictions. The constant/temperature
basis functions and the photon-number source remain untouched when the
low-frequency basis is refined. Failed trials remain stored. Only accepted
successors can supply the next perturbation background.
