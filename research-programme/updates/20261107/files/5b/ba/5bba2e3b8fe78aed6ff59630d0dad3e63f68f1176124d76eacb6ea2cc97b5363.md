# Constructing the position-dependent support column C0

This continues the corrected old-field reaction without selecting a new
force law. It differentiates the existing support force and horizontal
currents at h=1,(R-6),(R-6)^2. C1 and C2 are inherited unchanged.
The column is a derivative at zero amplitude, not a finite-amplitude fit.

## The family and its ownership

At fixed material coordinate xi and layer label, use

    R_epsilon = R + epsilon U,       U = eta + D e_source,
    q_epsilon = q + epsilon e,
    v_epsilon = v + epsilon edot,
    a_epsilon = a + epsilon k0,
    W_epsilon = D (v_source + epsilon edot_source),
    Wdot_epsilon = D (a_source + epsilon k0_source).

Here e is the saved compensated position direction, edot the owned mode
rate, and k0 the corrected reaction from the wave-flux live-response
archives. D is fixed as a material function. Raw support test vertices
z and eta are fixed in this material chart: their physical gradients
vary, but the test selection is not silently recomputed. The source and
outer support-test pins must be checked when restoring each case.

For Y=(mu,nu), P=X+A_old(edot) is the instantaneous Eulerian response and
H=B0+A_old(k0) the corrected Eulerian metric-time response. Therefore

    delta Y(material)   = P + Y_R U,
    delta Y_R(material) = P_R + Y_RR U,
    delta Y_t(material) = H + (Y_t)_R U.

All radial derivatives and traces belong to the registered polynomial
cell. One-sided cut ownership is retained; no average across owners.
Within an affine material cell set d=U_R. The first variation of the
physical measure is d dR, and each physical material gradient has the
factor (1+epsilon d)^(-1). In particular

    delta g   = e_R - d g,
    delta v_R = edot_R - d v_R,
    delta W_R = D_R edot_source - d W_R,
    delta z_R = -d z_R,       delta eta_R = -d eta_R.

The implementation has two routes: a directly perturbed packet and an
explicit product-rule column. Their agreement tests a derivative, not
whether the parent action is empirically correct.

## Force and current differentiated together

Write T=v-Wg, gdot=v_R-W_R g, Tdot=a-Wdot g-W gdot,
A=(alpha_dot+W_R alpha)T+alpha Tdot and Phi=alpha W T+beta g,
where alpha_dot=alpha_t+W alpha_R. With E=(alpha T^2+beta g^2)/2,
the inherited force density is

    f = A(z-g eta) + Phi z_R - alpha T gdot eta
        - (alpha_R T^2-beta_R g^2)eta/2
        - (E+alpha W T g)eta_R.

For I_h(R,s)=integral_s^R h(r)dr and V_h the P2 interpolant of
v_i I_h(R_i,s), the inherited current density is

    j_h = -A V_h - Phi (V_h)_R
          + [Edot+(W_R+alpha_t/alpha)E] I_h + E W h(R).

Two easily missed variations are now explicit:

    delta I_h = h(R) U - h(s) e_source,
    delta V_h = P2[edot_i I_h(R_i,s)
                    + v_i(h(R_i)U_i-h(s)e_source)].

The physical gradient of delta V_h additionally subtracts d(V_h)_R.
The regular material-volume column is delta(f,j_h)+d(f,j_h), including
the corrected k0 in Tdot and corrected H in alpha_t.

## Cut terms: the integral is more than its regular volume

Let a fixed radial polynomial cut R_c have right-minus-left density
jump [L]=L_right-L_left. Its material preimage moves by -U/J.
Differentiating both neighboring integrals gives the additional atom

    + U(R_c) [L(R_c)]

for L=f or j_h, at each layer where the cut lies inside the material
domain. This sign follows from (L_left-L_right) times the moving cut
speed in material coordinates, including J. It agrees with the earlier
wave-force moving-cut term when L=-Phi*N_R. Material faces are fixed
in this chart and have no separate moving-location atom. Outer motion
is already included by varying the material map and its measure.

An equivalent Eulerian implementation integrates delta_material L-U L_R
over fixed radial cells, adds -U[L] at moving material faces, and adds
U_outer L_outer-U_inner L_inner. Fixed radial cuts then have no moving
location atom. Do not combine both representations' atoms: that would
double-count. Coincident moving and fixed cuts require one-sided or
directional handling; a two-sided derivative is not assumed to exist.

The new packet helper constructs regular volume only. Integral promotion
requires either complete cut terms or the equivalent Eulerian route.
Independent piecewise-family tests must catch an omitted cut term.

## Native, source-clock, and gravitational pieces

The native first variation lifts physical edges, fields, beta, beta_R,
field velocities, source position, and source velocity together into a
Decimal directional derivative. The native force is the full edge and
field contraction, including load*beta_R*eta. The current is

    -sum(F_q v I_node) + sum(atom_rate I_native)
        + sum(beta load W_native h_native),
    atom_rate = beta load_dot + beta_R W_native load.

Thus moving primitive origins, native evaluation points, operator
weights, and kernel geometry all vary. No coefficient-only shortcut.
The inherited minimal Decimal tangent repair is retained. A simultaneous
signed Decimal family is an independent derivative check. If a native
evaluation point crosses a fixed radial coefficient cut as the layer
label varies, that label-integral boundary must also be accounted for;
pointwise agreement is not permission to omit those events.

The source proper-clock current is

    J_dust,h = m N(s)^2 v_s / sqrt(N(s)^2-v_s^2/F(s)) * h(s).

Its derivative includes delta s=e_source, delta v_s=edot_source,
delta Y(s)=P(s)+Y_R(s)e_source, and delta h(s).
The raw support test is source-pinned, so no new dust support force is
inserted. The fixed-Eulerian-domain gravitational current derivative is

    integral N/(kappa sqrt(F))
       * [H_mu + mu_t*(P_nu+P_mu/(R F))] h(R) dR.

checks precede numerical C0 promotion. Pointwise identities, native dual
checks and manufactured moving-cut tests precede paired integration.
Then propagate quadrature and k0/H sensitivity into the four-row system

    residual_base + C0*s + C1*sdot + C2*sddot = 0.
