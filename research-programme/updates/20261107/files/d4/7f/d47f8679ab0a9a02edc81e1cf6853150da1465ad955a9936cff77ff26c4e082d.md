# Stage233: mixed gain/loss derivative on the saved radiation/gas histories

This continues Stage232, not a replacement action or a three-test-function model.
All96 spectral inputs, both explicit infrared-tail inputs and the gas input remain.
Use the same target histories while varying the three operator temperatures, as
the production temperature comparison does. The stored potentials and gas shifts
are heat-scaled: physical h=chi phi and r=chi g. Do not insert unit-scaled
coefficients directly into occupation numbers. Here c=m_e=k_B=1 for event kinematics.

## A. Exact statistical vertex and its mixed derivative

Let s_j be -1 for incoming photons and +1 for outgoing photons, n_j=1/(exp(x_j)-1),
N_j=n_j+n_j(1+n_j)h_j, and R=T_e/T_star=1+r. The classical, fixed-number electron
distribution has normalization Z(T)=4pi T K_2(1/T). Its initial/final ratios to
the reference distribution are a and b. At common equilibrium, detailed balance
equates the forward and reverse event weights W0. Write the normalized net rate:

    D=F-B,
    F=a product_j[1+A_j h_j], B=b product_j[1+B_j h_j],
    A_j=n_j+1_(s_j<0), B_j=n_j+1_(s_j>0).

This covers Compton, emission and inverse absorption with their actual signs.
It retains all stimulated photon factors. No symmetric outer-product assumption
is made away from equilibrium. A physical weak source is W0 times the left
observable jump times D, not minus a positive matrix imposed at a displaced bath.

The derivative coefficients are

    d_j=a A_j product_(k!=j)(1+A_k h_k)-b B_j product_(k!=j)(1+B_k h_k),
    d_r=F (E_i-meanE)/(T R^2)-B (E_f-meanE)/(T R^2),
    d_v=(F p_i-B p_f)/(T R), d_density=D.

The last coefficient follows from multiplying BOTH electron populations by the
same density, not differentiating a Compton rate alone. BR's two-body density
power remains a separate Stage231 source; it is not covered by this vertex.

At equilibrium, d_j=-s_j, d_r=-DeltaE_e/T and d_v=-Deltap_e/T.
Thus the existing linear jump form is recovered, including its collision sign.
Let g_i=(E_i-meanE(T))/T and g_f=(E_f-meanE(T))/T. For a small background,

    f1=g_i r+sum A_j h_j, b1=g_f r+sum B_j h_j,
    delta d_j=A_j(f1-A_j h_j)-B_j(b1-B_j h_j),
    delta d_r=[g_i^2-g_f^2-2(g_i-g_f)]r
              +g_i sum A_j h_j-g_f sum B_j h_j,
    delta d_v=[p_i(f1-r)-p_f(b1-r)]/T,
    delta d_density=f1-b1.

The common heat-capacity derivative cancels between the initial/final scores
in delta d_r; it is present in each score separately. This gives the mixed
background/wave derivative directly. An80-digit exact finite-background
evaluation separates its higher-order remainder instead of subtracting nearly
equal float64 matrices. Coefficients, not just total matrices, are tested.

## B. Angular projection and the independent gas row

For scalar intensity, Y_0=1, Y_1=n, Y_2=sqrt(3/2)(nn-I/3). The rotational average
is Y_l(n):Y_l(m)/(2l+1)=P_l(n.m)/(2l+1). For any retained radial tests phi_a,

    L_a=sum_j s_j phi_a(x_j)Y_l(n_j),
    R_b=sum_j d_j psi_b(x_j)Y_l(n_j),
    J_l,ab=L_a:R_b/(2l+1).

Append independently computed DeltaE_e/T to L_0 and Deltap_e/T to L_1; append
d_r and d_v to their respective right sides. Full momentum conservation uses
the independently on-shell electron, not a gas row defined as minus photons.
The l2 padded gas row supplies no gas shear model. The complete angular gas
distribution and polarized I/E transfer are not replaced by this scalar row.

For every background, four-momentum conservation implies a LEFT null of the
source/Jacobian. A common velocity is a RIGHT null only at equilibrium. A boost
of a distorted, collisionally evolving bath generally has a nonzero source.
The moving-background response must satisfy the differentiated collision law,
not an artificial zero constraint. Gas accelerations subsequently use Stage231's
enthalpy and heat susceptibility, not a dust mass or2delta-u/3 pressure closure.

## C. Matching and scope of the derivative

This module implements the event statistical derivative, full finite spectral
domain and directional four-force. It must be inserted consistently into real,
soft-subtraction, virtual and displaced-state terms. Applying it to just a final
integrated monopole would lose the individual angular correlations. In particular
the existing cached loop scalar is not a license to drop the derivative of a
medium-dependent loop amplitude or phase shift. The full mixed derivative is

    delta(C'[background])=delta(statistical vertex)*kernel
                          + statistical vertex*delta(kernel/phase/measure).

Production scalar replay and statistical-vertex tests are recorded separately.
Successful scalar replay does not by itself validate the full mixed inclusive
operator. Full integration retains the old physical soft-tail representation,
matching terms, precision repairs and original tolerances; this stage does not
extend constant/chemical modes to zero frequency or select a fitted cutoff.
