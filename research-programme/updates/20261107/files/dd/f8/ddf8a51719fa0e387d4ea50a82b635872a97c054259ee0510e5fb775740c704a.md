## The background failure led to an actual correction

Inserting the nonlinear collision residual *alone* fails the negligibility test
at the extremely small bolometric correction scale. The failed packet and both
diagnostic waves remain intact in the accuracy review. They are not promoted
as self-consistent background solutions.

Solving the compensating photon spectrum p and gas temperature t gives

    E p + chi(t + integral t ds)=0,
    E p' = -chi(t+t').

The proper collision correction is chi^2 H p', including relaxation, not just
the raw forcing. Its energy moment is suppressed by another chi. The conserved
charge is checked directly, including expansion work, with residual below
2.6e-15. The forced spectrum correction is about7.14e-9 of the saved background
distortion. The relaxed collision, redshift, boost and temperature-pressure
source response is then evolved separately; see the two relaxed-source-probe
packets and `results/background-closure-review/status.json`.
Its largest feature change is2.742e-8 of the mixed response on either parent,
well below the unchanged0.002 gate. This replaces the failed raw insertion
with an explicitly derived and tested source, not a loosened tolerance.

This source test is not a complete order-chi^2 Jacobian/new Friedmann solution.
The smallest stress digits remain conditional on that explicit truncation;
the robust leading spectral/temperature conclusion does not require promoting
them into a full nonlinear prediction.
