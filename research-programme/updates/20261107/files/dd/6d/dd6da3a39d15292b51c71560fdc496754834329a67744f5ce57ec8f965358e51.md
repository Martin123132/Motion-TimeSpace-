# Extend the retained source rather than replace it

The selected new rows change only the outgoing hard-photon scattering angle
from event0 to events1,2 in the original x14 quadrature at the first electron
velocity. Incoming frequency, target physical histories, photon basis,
temperature anchors and electron/reflection bookkeeping retain their owners.
The six new rows and three reused rows form one complete three-hard-angle
quadrature at this one electron node, not the full microscopic event bank.

## Real part

The Stage239 rational Dirac intensity is algebraically identical to the
retained amplitude. The original Stage236 weak-factor integrator keeps the
signed emission/absorption pairing, common soft subtraction and spontaneous
logarithm. Both equilibrium and all six mixed-history matrices are retained,
with leading and full-minus-leading contributions separate. No event weight
is applied twice when adding the three hard angles.

## Virtual part

For each new hard angle, recompute the finite dressed tensor decomposition
in soft direction, with its own pole positions and coefficients. Contract
with that angle's Born tensor, not the event0 tensor. The common radial basis
moments remain unchanged; every angle-dependent pole integral is recomputed.
The finite-domain/exterior-pole distinction follows the retained prescription.

Writing R for the full vacuum+soft-constant+thermal scalar at the new angle,
L for the mixed statistical block, and J0 for its equilibrium derivative,

    virtual = (weight/2) [ R L + delta_R_background J0
                 + linear_statistical_source left * delta_R_wave^T/(2l+1) ].

This directly evaluates the formula previously assembled by updating a
saved event0 block. Its generalization must first reproduce those saved
blocks for event0 at both angular orders and joins.

## Displaced state

The mass-moment coefficients may be reused at each identical temperature
and join because the retained on-shell scalar mass functional has no
hard-angle dependence. It is not a scalar approximation to the complete
spinor response. Recompute the angle-dependent state derivative U and keep
all three product-rule terms:

    state = weight [ m_P^2 U_mixed
                    + delta_m_background^2 U_equilibrium
                    + (U_equilibrium B) (delta_m_wave^2)^T ].

The last term is monopole-only for this scalar mass functional. The phase,
observable, canonical-velocity and normalized-population derivatives stay
inside U. Both canonical steps are checked; independent gas recoil remains
a measured row. This reproduces Stage235 on the reused event before adding
the new kinematics.

## Combined source and gravity

For every angle and for the three-angle sum, retain leading, real correction,
virtual, state, MTS correction and combined matrices separately. The existing
Stage238 map supplies the EOS heat-capacity/enthalpy and stress/metric ports.
No new closure, H(z), fitted coupling or substituted background is introduced.
Finite-frequency support, low-frequency input tails and full electron-node
coverage retain their explicit scope. Numerical angular/radial gates concern
the calculated support; they do not certify missing quadrature nodes.
