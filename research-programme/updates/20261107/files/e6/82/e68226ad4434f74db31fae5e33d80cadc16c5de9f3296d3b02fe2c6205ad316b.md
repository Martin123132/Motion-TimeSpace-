# Stage54 — canonical motion/bath modes inside the radiation hierarchy

Local daughter of Stage53, not a replacement framework. The Stage49 rotation,
quadratic masses, slow branch, gravity action, and standard species are retained.
The new work is a native perturbation implementation and its control tests.

## Physical equations

Write the already diagonalized canonical modes as
\(\varphi_I=\phi_I/M_{\rm Pl}\), with conformal-time primes and masses in
inverse Mpc. For each of the slow and heavy modes,

\[
 \varphi_I''+2\mathcal H\varphi_I'+a^2 m_I^2\varphi_I=0,
\qquad
 \delta\varphi_I''+2\mathcal H\delta\varphi_I'
 +(k^2+a^2m_I^2)\delta\varphi_I=-kz\varphi_I'.
\]

Here CAMB's synchronous-CDM variable obeys \(\delta_c'=-kz\).
Its metric convention is not silently identified with another package's eta.
The individual stresses, in native units, are

\[
 D_I=8\pi G a^2\delta\rho_I
 =\varphi_I'\delta\varphi_I'+a^2m_I^2\varphi_I\delta\varphi_I,
\quad
 P_I=8\pi G a^2\delta p_I
 =\varphi_I'\delta\varphi_I'-a^2m_I^2\varphi_I\delta\varphi_I,
\quad Q_I=k\varphi_I'\delta\varphi_I.
\]

The sums of D and Q enter the SAME metric constraint and momentum evolution
as photons, baryons, collisionless neutrinos, and remaining cold matter. Neither
field is imposed as an external transfer multiplier. Both have zero linear
anisotropic stress, while the standard radiation hierarchies retain theirs.
The exact per-field continuity identities are

\[
 D_I'+\mathcal H(D_I+3P_I)+kQ_I+kz\varphi_I'^2=0,
\qquad Q_I'+2\mathcal H Q_I-kP_I=0.
\]

For the regular radiation-era adiabatic branch, with \(a=\beta\tau\),
\(\delta_c=C\tau^2\), and \(m/H\ll1\),

\[
 \varphi'=-m^2\beta^2\varphi_0\tau^3/5+\cdots,
\quad \delta\varphi=-\varphi_0(m/H)^2\delta_c/105+\cdots,
\quad \delta\varphi'=6\delta\varphi/\tau+\cdots.
\]

This reproduces the Stage51 seed using the actual native cold seed, including
its radiation/neutrino normalization. Corrections from finite matter fraction,
finite wavelength, and departure from the frozen solution remain higher order;
the earlier-start test checks their numerical effect rather than declaring the
truncated series exact. Independent primordial isocurvature is not fitted here.

## Bounded implementation

The source is pinned to CAMB1.6.6 commit
3ef0272d6f7ba1231128872e56e6d4c12af8267b and its own forutils submodule commit
841f06d5356877e90437f94b2d976dd98f7f923c. The separately compiled library lives
in this daughter only. The working installed library is never overwritten.

The F=0 branch evolves the canonical slow field directly all the way to today:
no w+1 floor and no PPF replacement. It is compared to the saved Stage53
slow-fluid controls and a separately rebuilt standard LCDM control.

The F=.5 branch evolves BOTH canonical perturbations through the early heavy
oscillations, with all standard species. Exact perturbations are guarded at
a=.00028. Beyond that epoch the background alone uses the explicitly numerical
coarse-graining to dust, needed to initialize the package's full thermal history.
The exact part ends before that averaging affects the evaluated perturbations.
The two-field calculation deliberately errors if asked for later perturbations.
This prevents a background-only approximation being sold as a matter spectrum.

The native initialization buffer previously had room for only two dark-sector
states. Initialization is now directed into the actual allocated state slice;
otherwise four-state use could silently write past that small buffer. Ten new
time-evolution diagnostics expose the two fields and their separate D/P/Q.
The stock transfer names and their normalization are unchanged. In particular,
the stock delta_tot denominator EXCLUDES a heavy field stored in the dark slot;
it must not be relabeled as the physical total matter perturbation for F>0.

## Next physical interface

Use the saved native early-time states to perform the Stage51 phase-window
stress projection, now with the full radiation/neutrino state. Continue the
ENTIRE native state across an explicit, tested matching event into the averaged
heavy variables while retaining exact slow-field perturbations. Test matching
scale and solver convergence before computing a late heavy matter spectrum.
Only then compare with structure data using the same primordial amplitude and
fairly refitted cosmological controls. BAO alone did not select F in Stage53.
