# Stage127 — paired Compton adjoint without cancellation

The consumed direct source is
`../direct-doppler-compton-feedback-20261012/direct_common.py`.
Its symmetric soft-Doppler test-function increment for ratio r>0 is

    P_n(x,r)=v_n(r*x)-v_n(x)+[v_n(x/r)-v_n(x)]/r.

It is weighted by rate/r. Directly subtracting evaluated high-degree basis
vectors can lose accuracy near r=1, including an odd-order cancellation.
This risk is to be measured, not assumed to explain the whole Stage126 shift.

The saved ConsistentBasis uses z(x)=(x-p)/(x+p), p>0, and the exact recurrence

    norm_n*v_n(x)=z(x)*v_(n-1)(x)-sum_(repeat,k<n) projection_(n,repeat,k)*v_k(x).

Both stored reorthogonalization passes are kept. Basis values and coefficients
are unchanged. Set dplus_n=v_n(r*x)-v_n(x), dminus_n=v_n(x/r)-v_n(x), d=r-1.
The compact-coordinate increments can be factored exactly:

    aplus=2*p*x*d/[(r*x+p)*(x+p)]
    aminus=-2*p*x*d/[(x+p*r)*(x+p)]
    apair=aplus+aminus/r
         =2*p^2*x*d^2*(r+1)/[(x+p)*r*(r*x+p)*(x+p*r)].

Then

    norm_n*dplus_n=(z+aplus)*dplus_(n-1)+aplus*v_(n-1)-sum projection*dplus_k
    norm_n*dminus_n=(z+aminus)*dminus_(n-1)+aminus*v_(n-1)-sum projection*dminus_k
    norm_n*P_n=z*P_(n-1)+aplus*dplus_(n-1)
              +(aminus/r)*dminus_(n-1)+apair*v_(n-1)-sum projection*P_k.

For the constant mode all increments vanish. For v1=(x-mean)/norm1,

    dplus1=x*d/norm1, dminus1=-x*d/(r*norm1),
    P1=x*d^2*(r+1)/(r^2*norm1).

Every term in the paired recurrence is manifestly second order in d before
basis recursion, avoiding subtraction of two order-one basis evaluations.
This is algebraically the same retained soft kernel, not an alternative
collision law. At r=1 the increment vanishes exactly.

Weighted temperature increment is x*sum rate*(r-1)^2*(r+1)/r^3.
Test this before applying the same analytic constant/temperature rows used
by the legacy implementation. Exact identities imposed by replacement rows
are not by themselves independent accuracy evidence.

## What high precision and refinement do and do not establish

Use80-digit evaluation of the original rational recurrence with exactly
converted stored float coefficients; compare its direct shifted difference
with the new paired computation. This tests evaluation error, not uncertainty
in the original basis construction, photon physics or parent coefficients.

After that, the six fixed variants separate arithmetic changes, event
quadrature, frequency quadrature and a first mixed effect. Propagate source
differences with the unchanged response and both baseline/MTS columns.
A two-level difference or observed contraction is not a rigorous error bound.

The basis evaluate method analytically extrapolates its rational recurrence
beyond its orthogonality interval; it does not clip at the interval boundary.
Record any shifted-node crossings and their positive integration-weight
fraction. That fraction is not an observable error bound, and we do not
change the domain policy here.

All other source nodes, represented overlap, DC/BR, inner coefficient bank
and initial matching remain fixed. Thus this isolates source accuracy,
not full inner/source consistency or physical initial-memory ownership.
