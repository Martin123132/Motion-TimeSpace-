# Stage109 — frozen decomposition gates

Reuse Stage108/107 states and the saved parent baseline. Work locally.
No new scattering/source integrals, time evolution, physical fits or subagents.

- Freeze the same templates, Planck-potential metric, frequency bounds
  and numerical method for correction and baseline. Coefficients may be signed.
- Derive standard mu and y potentials, then energy-match them in each band.
  Keep the residual; a two-template description is an outcome, not a gate.
- Primary band: x=1e-5..60, inherited 700-node quadrature and basis values.
  Refine only cheap readout quadrature to 1400 and 2100 nodes.
  Compare lower bounds 5e-5,1e-4,1e-3 at 1400/2100 nodes for BOTH inputs.
  Do not interpret removing frequencies as a proof of infrared convergence.
- Full-band correction uses independent saved energy. Baseline uses its
  saved energy; verify baseline coefficients are exactly those already used.
  Restricted bands use their own energy moments, never full-band energy.
- Check standard full-band Planck template moments within 1e-8.
  Check energy-free templates and residual moment consistency with
  explicit 256eps cancellation allowances; original input discrepancies
  remain recorded rather than silently changed.
- Weighted template design must have rank2 and finite singular values.
  Record the condition number and correlation. A condition above1e6 blocks
  interpreting separate amplitudes; do not force a useful answer.
- Reconstruction, template orthogonality, QR versus SVD, synthetic
  temperature-only, mu-only, y-only, mixed and residual controls:
  relative gate1e-10. Normalize residual orthogonality to the INPUT norm,
  not a vanishing residual. Save normalization choices.
- Preserve the split into temperature plus joint mu/y plus residual;
  verify squared-norm partition. Mu and y are correlated, so their
  individual squared norms must not be presented as additive fractions.
- Apply the same linear readout to all16 correction columns and the saved
  timestep/terminal/restart variants. Component uncertainty is summed
  in absolute amplitude or residual norm, without cancellation credit.
  No pass condition requires shrinkage or a small residual.
- Physical mu/y amplitudes are chi times scaled coefficients.
  State the accompanying temperature-convention transformation explicitly.
  Export the leftover shape, not just two amplitude numbers.
- Verify Stage108 and107 payloads and every consumed sealed input.
  Reuse the Stage108 archive-wide verification; this stage does NOT
  claim a fresh hash sweep of every historical cache file.
- Compile without bytecode; all outputs finite; citations and input hashes
  valid; old files read-only; no GitHub. One modest single-core worker.
