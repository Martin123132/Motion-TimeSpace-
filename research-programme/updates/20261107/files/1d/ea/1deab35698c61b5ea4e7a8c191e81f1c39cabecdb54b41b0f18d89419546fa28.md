# Stage99: polar moments and an actual-source reflection reduction

## Remaining angular bias

Write mu=cos(theta_e). The normalized Gauss polar rule integrates dmu/2.
Its two-node values mu=+-1/sqrt(3), weights1/2, give <mu^4>=1/9 instead
of the isotropic1/5. The three-node values mu=0,+-sqrt(3/5), weights
4/9,5/18,5/18, integrate all polynomials through degree5 exactly.

Combining polar3 with the already-tested azimuth8 gives, at fixed speed,
<n_i^2>=1/3, <n_i^4>=1/5 and <n_i^2 n_j^2>=1/15 for i!=j.
Polar2/azimuth8 instead has <nx^4>=1/6, <nz^4>=1/9,
<nx^2 ny^2>=1/18 and <nx^2 nz^2>=1/9. Thus the proposed polar change
removes a distinct three-dimensional fourth-moment anisotropy. This is an
identity of the quadrature, not proof that all nonlinear source terms converge.

## Reflection, including the complex virtual amplitude

Both external lab photon rays lie in the xz plane. Let R=diag(1,-1,1)
reflect spatial y, and J=diag(1,1,-1,1) act on four-vectors. The actual
boost obeys B(Rv)=J B(v) J. The context's first/third rest axes transform
with R, while its cross-product second axis transforms with -R. Consequently

    T(Rv) = J T(v) J,    bath(Rv) = J bath(v).

Rest incoming energy, scattering cosine, hard Doppler factor, relative flux,
angular Jacobian and Born scalar weight are unchanged. Reflecting the soft
direction similarly preserves every Doppler/Bose factor, recoil energy,
physical integration boundary and the spectral/gas jumps.

In the actual Dirac representation, take U=gamma0 gamma1 gamma3.
It is unitary and obeys

    U gamma^mu U^-1 = J^mu_mu gamma^mu,
    U^dagger gamma0 U = gamma0.

Every vector-QED slash, propagator and contracted polarization sum therefore
transforms covariantly. After summing final and averaging initial spins and
polarizations, the REAL SCALAR Born/real/intensity and interference are even
under y reflection. This is a unitary conjugation argument, NOT complex
conjugation of the virtual amplitude: its absorptive imaginary coefficients
are retained. The thermal dressed parent consists of slash/propagator terms,
scalar products, radial pole coefficients and external spinor/kinematic shifts.
Pole locations, temperature and slope signs are reflection-invariant; hence the
same argument applies to its complex principal-value/absorptive radial kernel.
The external-state correction uses in-plane phase shifts and the covariant
velocity tangent -v/(2 gamma^2), so its finite-difference stencil also maps.

The actual soft and virtual angular rule has
phi_j=pi*(j+1/2)/order. Reflection permutes j to2*order-1-j with the same
weight. Thus the finite angular sums, not only their continuum integrals,
respect the reduction in exact arithmetic. Radial cutoffs and panels map too.

This applies only to the currently used unpolarized scalar energy/spectral
source and scalar virtual interference. Do not copy a full polarized Mueller
matrix, a signed spatial-current component or a parity-violating operator
using this rule. Those would need their own transformation law.
