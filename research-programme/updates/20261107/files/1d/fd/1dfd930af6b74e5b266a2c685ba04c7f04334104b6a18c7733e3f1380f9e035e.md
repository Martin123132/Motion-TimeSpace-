# Electric and magnetic tidal balance: combined

4 October 2026. Private, with the previous checkpoints preserved.

We have now carried the body's derived frame-dragging response through
the radiating multipoles, emitted power, orbital energy, inspiral rate and
wave phase. The coefficients are derived from the source integrals and
matched action; no body response parameter was fitted.

The important correction is that magnetic response changes both radiation
and orbital energy. In terms of companion/body mass ratio epsilon, its
new chirp coefficient is (1037*epsilon-1)/3, multiplying
x*sigma_bar/d0^5. Its sign cannot be decided from sigma_bar alone.

For our illustrative epsilon0.01 orbit at10R, the magnetic term changes
the chirp by -4.28089e-12 of its Newtonian value: about3 parts per million
of the existing electric correction. The electric result is recovered
exactly when sigma_bar is switched off. Both responses switched off
recover the retained point-mass1PN baseline.

Validation includes generic STF source identities, published-convention
checks, independent circular roots and rotating-tensor flux evaluations,
phase quadratures, and old-electric-table regression. A mismatched
normalization in the first regression attempt was caught and retained;
the underlying physics coefficients did not change.

These small isolated terms are not a total waveform-accuracy claim. The
next useful derivation is the **time-dependent axial response**, to test
the static-response approximation at the actual forcing frequencies.
