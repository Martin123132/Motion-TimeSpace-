# Stage113 — physical shifts without mesh deposition

## 1. Replace only the compact Compton quadrature
Let r_e>0 be each exact boost-derived outgoing/incoming frequency ratio
and w_e>=0 its inherited normalized thermal/Thomson angular weight.
The leading-soft conductance is k_e=w_e/r_e. For a test A and the
compact prescribed replacement q,retain Stage112's conservative form:

    C[A;q]=-1/(2 I3) sum_e k_e integral_0^infty dx
                   (A(r_e x)-A(x))(q(r_e x)-q(x)).

Changing variable y=r_e x in the q(r_e x) terms gives exactly

    C[A;q]=1/(2 I3) integral_support(q) dx q(x) sum_e k_e
           [A(r_e x)-A(x)+(A(x/r_e)-A(x))/r_e].

This is an algebraic change of variables,no diffusion expansion and no
interpolation of r_e onto a log-frequency mesh. The same inherited
basis is evaluated at the actual shifted frequencies. Streaming bounded
event batches limits memory without changing the quadrature.

For A=1 the bracket vanishes event-by-event. For A=x it is

    x [r-1+(r^-1-1)/r] = x (r-1)^2(r+1)/r^2.

Use the latter factored expression to avoid cancellation. Thus the
analytic energy functional is

    C[x;q]=[sum_e w_e(r_e-1)^2(r_e+1)/r_e^3]/(2 I3)
           integral dx x q(x).

Its gas contribution is equal and opposite in the saved response.
For A=q the original weak form is nonpositive. These are conservation/
dissipation identities of the selected soft functional,not a derivation
of finite-recoil or finite-Planck corrections.

The inherited gas quadrature is Maxwell-Juttner in kinetic energy;
the incoming flux factor and rest-frame Thomson angular law are retained.
Finite event-order detailed balance is checked through the inherited
conductance moment L(p)=sum_e (w_e/r_e)(r_e^p-1),with L(p)=L(-1-p).
No discrete rate is fitted.
