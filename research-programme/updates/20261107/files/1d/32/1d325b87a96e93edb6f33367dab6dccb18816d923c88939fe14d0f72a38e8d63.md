# Stage55 — the heavy field now reaches today's matter growth

## What changed

The previously exact early heavy oscillations now hand off, inside the native
solver, to their averaged density and momentum evolution. The slow field stays
exact; the metric, baryon, photon and neutrino states survive the event without
being replaced or phase-averaged. The earlier Stage49 action and Stage54 route
are retained, not put aside for another theory.

This closes the numerical early-to-late handoff for the four sourced test modes.
It produces actual conditional linear matter-transfer samples, rather than
applying an external suppression formula to an unrelated baseline.

## Physical result

For the inherited illustrative heavy fraction F=.5 and mass, the ratios of
today's linear matter power are:

| k [Mpc^-1] | Relative to slow-field cold control | Relative to matched LCDM |
|---:|---:|---:|
| .03 | .973847 | .943208 |
| .1 | .948834 | .918994 |
| 1 | .070608 | .068387 |
| 5 | .001211 | .001172 |

Large-scale growth is comparatively close to the controls; the smaller-scale
linear power is strongly suppressed. That is a concrete prediction to test,
not automatically a success: such a large suppression may constrain or exclude
this F=.5 benchmark when confronted with suitable structure data. The fraction
has not been inferred here. The k=1 and5 values are linear calculations, not
nonlinear galaxy predictions. Four points do not constitute a full spectrum.

The matter definition explicitly includes the heavy field. Stock CAMB
delta_tot/sigma8 from the dark-sector slot cannot silently substitute for it.

## Why the handoff is credible within its tested scope

- Exactly one explicit switch per mode; 131–182 other live state coordinates
  remain unchanged. Reversing mode order reproduces the outputs exactly.
- Moving the switch among the three epochs changes today's amplitude by at
  most5.13parts per million; across391 late sample times, at most7.54ppm.
- Changing background resolution/integration tolerance changes the late
  amplitude by at most.658ppm. Projection-window/order changes stay below.024ppm
  across the late trajectory. These are robustness spreads, not all-order errors.
- Both the cold slow-field and LCDM controls reproduce the prior native library
  exactly in all compared output columns.
- An independent exact-field overlap test has a worst density discrepancy of
  .01893% of the density/velocity mode scale (.132% relative to the particular
  k=5 density value). This is larger than the late matching-time spread and is
  retained in the error discussion.
- A deliberately raw, unaveraged handoff changes one final amplitude by13.51%.
  Removing the phase projection is therefore a detectable mistake, not an
  equivalent implementation choice.

110 validation checks, five additional trajectory-stability tests, and seven
symbolic identities pass. Native and finite-difference constraint checks are
saved separately; finite-difference errors reflect single-precision transfer
output and must not be described as exact zeros. The pressure is a leading
averaged closure with an explicit matching remainder, not an exact microscopic
conversion at a single instant.
