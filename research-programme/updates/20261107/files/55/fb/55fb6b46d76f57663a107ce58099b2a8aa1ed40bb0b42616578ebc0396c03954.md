# Stage230: two-way homogeneous thermal/field/gravity evolution

## What is now coupled

Photons/gas, both retained canonical scalar fields and the Friedmann constraint
now use one dynamically solved conformal expansion rate at every step. Heat
changes H; that H changes both collision rates and scalar evolution. This is
not a post-processing insertion of heat into a fixed parent trajectory.

The existing MTS parent action, calibrated constants, electron population,
NR-ion approximation and original scattering/emission bank are unchanged.
No coefficient is fitted. No new observational dataset or likelihood is used.

Three branches share the SAME integration method: reference geometry without
added heat, NR thermal source, and electron-EOS thermal source. Small field,
metric, gas and spectrum differences are evolved directly. We do not subtract
two complete cosmologies to obtain a1e-13 response.

## Derived cancellation and regularity

Differentiating the actual gas first law at fixed step histories gives

    [chi(E+U)]_lnH=chi^2(C0-2)g_lnH/alpha.

The leading exchange term cancels because photons and gas use the SAME energy
row. The remainder is expansion work. This is a derived suppression, not a
zero-coupling assumption.

The coupled root's analytic derivative agrees with independent differentiation.
Representative full80-digit field/97-state thermal calculations verify the
NR and EOS difference Friedmann equations, resolving the small increment
itself. Across the computed roots, the minimum scaled slope divided by2 is
approximately0.999999967. That stored quantity differs from the physical J by
the explicit1+fraction factor; it is not silently treated as exactly identical.

BRANCH-BOUND.md gives a sufficient positive-H root bracket and Jacobian bound
for the retained passive physical weak form. Evaluated at every saved NR/EOS
step on both parents and resolutions, its lower J bound is0.9999532853.
Its largest direct thermal contribution to the step-Jacobian bound is
2.20e-17. That number is NOT a full-history amplification factor.

The theorem depends on the named passive physical weak form, sourced in the
previous work. Floating coefficient evaluation is not an outward-rounded
certificate of every stored binary matrix. The actual implemented solve has
its own independent numerical checks. The bank was not edited or projected
to make either check pass.

## Conservation and numerical error are distinguished

The shared-H root uses the actual evolved density in each branch. Thermal
energy plus expansion work obeys its exact discrete budget, including initial
gas heat. The tiny EOS difference has a separately normalized budget.

BDF2 does not preserve the scalar-field continuum product rule exactly.
Reconstructing TOTAL stress differences from the actual stored fields and
thermal moments gives these normalized continuity residuals:

| Difference |3200 steps |6400 steps |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| NR, either parent | 1.94e-6 | 4.84e-7 |
| EOS, either parent | 3.44e-7 | 8.58e-8 |

They shrink by about4 when the step is halved, as expected for the bulk
scheme. They are reported as discretization errors, not relabelled exact
conservation. No constraint projection, compensating heat or tolerance change
was used. Spatial perturbation constraints are a next test, not silently
inferred from this homogeneous calculation.
