## Derived next construction, without dividing by zero

At a time-symmetric ordinary knot with A0=C0=0, common initial slope g, zero curvature jump and common metric coefficients, differentiate the two interface equations:

```
beta*j_ddot = A_ddot
C_ddot + g*A_ddot = 0
j_ddot = c^2*[ell]
```

Therefore

```
[ell] = A_ddot/(beta*c^2)
```

The denominator is positive beta*c^2, not A. The mean third derivative remains free only if the separate point-compatibility equation holds. These identities are checked symbolically in the zero-load-law run. Membership in the numerical inactive mask is not by itself a proof of the exact zero-load assumptions.
