# A common physical normalization and transported support jet

## Why this precedes the fine/third current comparison

The archived fine and third states sample the same analytic seed and source history, with unchanged physical Gram support. They are not literally the same P2 field: the third seed is resampled rather than merely prolonged. Likewise each grid independently constructs and projects its support direction. A comparison of fitted jet coordinates alone can conflate source/field refinement, direction changes, and normalization.

Let eta be the pinned radial generator, d the compensated field rate, and v_s its source-rate component. The map-rate direction is w=eta+D v_s. The Eulerian wave-rate direction is

    e = d-w*g.

Measure this residual using the compensated polynomial representation, not subtraction of binary quantities that nearly cancel. Keep the two inherited source interpretations distinct.

## One normalization, fixed by geometry

Use the continuous pinned h1 profile already underlying the nodal generator:

    eta_star(R,l) = h1(R)-D(R,l) h1(S(l))
                         -(1-D(R,l)) h1(R_endpoint(l)),
    h1(R) = ((R-6)/0.9) * max(1-((R-6)/0.9)^2,0)^4.

For a common source map, define

    N^2 = [integral w_label(l) integral eta_star^2 dR dl]
          / [integral w_label(l) integral 1 dR dl].

N is a geometric RMS, not a fit, kinetic Schur complement, or residual-dependent weight. Compare the same number across branches and quadrature refinements. The normalized generator is eta/N and the physical amplitude is x_hat=N*x. If the support force test is normalized too, with R_N=diag(1/N,1,1,1), then

    B_hat = R_N B,
    C_hat = R_N C/N,
    B_hat + C_hat*x_hat = R_N*(B+C*x).

The same transformation applies to componentwise error arrays. Normalization cannot create compatibility or remove an obstruction. Derive/check this identity before using normalized columns. A kinetic-normalized direction would divide by a quantity tending to zero; do not substitute that convention.

## Exact nested-space transfer

Use the archived rational P2 prolongation P for fields and the P1 edge prolongation E obtained by copying endpoints and averaging edge midpoints. The source component is copied unchanged. The transported fine candidate on the third mesh is

    d_T = P*d_F, eta_T = E*eta_F, v_s,T = v_s,F.

This represents the same material perturbation, not an independently refitted third direction. Its Eulerian response at the resampled third background differs from the fine response by

    e_T-e_F = -(eta_F+D*v_s,F)*(g_T-g_F),

when evaluated in the common coordinates and with exact transferred polynomials. This separates the known background-gradient change from re-projecting the direction. Verify the identity at high precision; do not assume the newly transported candidate already satisfies the third-grid old-field equations.

Save the transferred raw generator and projection separately, along with their difference. The next live-metric projection can therefore act on a sourced, reproducible candidate rather than an invented mode.

## Actual comparison

Use the third spatial partition as a common integration partition and identical material quadrature points. Check source histories, nested restrictions and fixed interaction support. Compute the positive common-metric Gram matrix of the two actual e profiles, their norm ratio, signed cosine and best-scale remainder. The best scale is descriptive only: it is not used to tune the theory. Independently compare eta against the continuous pinned profile.

A small norm ratio alone is not convergence of a nonzero physical mode, and a large scalar amplitude alone is not divergence of a physical observable. Distinguish geometric generator convergence, vanishing projected kinetic residual, and collinearity of that residual. No B/C0/C1/C2 comparison is labelled matched until its direction and evaluation contracts both match.
