# Stage122 — fixed-grid inner-time contraction

This child reuses the Stage121 discretization, with only its inner time step
halved. Heavy background only; baseline and both MTS columns share each solve.

At the fixed inner frequency nodes the defect e relative to saved outer
history u satisfies the inherited linear equation. Its discrete update is

    (a I-h L_n)e_n = -b e_(n-1)-c e_(n-2)+h f_n
                    -(a u_n+b u_(n-1)+c u_(n-2)).

The startup uses backward Euler(1,-1,0), then BDF2(1.5,-2,0.5).
This is unchanged; restarting restores both time levels and never repeats
startup. Both positive inner stencil and four-point outer forcing stencil,
including the inherited near-boundary order reduction, are unchanged.
40960 steps halve h relative to20480, with identical saved65 source epochs.

For each existing matching window the repaired correction is q=x W e.
The canonical projection obeys M p=<V,q>, and the replacement source is the
physical collision/emission action on q minus the represented action on p.
Sources are recomputed from the new defect using the sealed operator cache.
The response is advanced on the unchanged2048-step grid and the unresolved
q-Vp term is retained in total radiation readout and energy bookkeeping.

For any saved response y, compare d_old=y20480-y10240 and
d_new=y40960-y20480. Report r=norm(d_new)/norm(d_old) and
p_eff=-log2(r) only when the old difference is numerically resolved.
Peak,late-quarter,endpoint and radiation norms remain distinct.
The endpoint difference is also saved signed; a relative difference alone
cannot hide cancellation or a sign flip. A ratio near1/4 would be consistent
with second-order contraction, not a proof or certified continuum estimate.

This tests inner-time sensitivity of the conditional response, not source-knot
interpolation or the separate matching-window law. Cold-background40960 and
