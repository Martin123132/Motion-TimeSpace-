# Common physical motion: the small inertia is a spatial jump mechanism

## What advanced

The common-grid test exposed a structural numerical mechanism, not another current-integration error. The imposed geometric motion is close on the two grids, but its projected kinetic remainder is not the same physical profile times a different normalization. A derived minimum-energy jump lifting reproduces essentially the entire measured wave inertia on both grids, in both reference and MTS cases.

This is a constructive reason to change the evolution formulation: compute the action's constraint along the continuum kinetic-null direction, rather than divide a force by the shrinking grid-generated inertia. No action coefficient was fitted or introduced.

## 2. Derived minimum jump lifting

At each material label the residual e=d-w*g is piecewise quadratic, with internal jump

    [e]=[d]-w_right*[g]-[w]*g_left.

For the parent-nodal interpretation d and w are continuous, giving [e]=-w*[g]. The actual high-precision checks have identity errors below 3.8e-82. Rounded-coefficient continuity defects are retained in the general identity.

With the inherited kinetic mass M and jump/boundary trace matrix B, the unique minimum-energy broken-P2 lifting is

    j=M^(-1)*B^T*(B*M^(-1)*B^T)^(-1)*B*e,
    e=j+r,   B*r=0,   <j,r>_M=0.

This is an explicit tridiagonal solve, derived in DERIVATION-20261001-spatial-jump-kinetic-lifting.md. It uses no fitted parameters and does not depend on assuming that the existing projected residual already minimizes the norm. The continuous component is measured separately.

## 3. Scope and decision

This identifies the dominant spatial origin of the small inertia in the selected direction. It does not justify changing any previously measured current, setting a nonzero force to zero, or treating the native potential as reparametrization invariant. The test holds source response fixed and allows a continuous spatial correction independently at each material label. It is not a replacement for the full parent live-metric/source projection.

The kinetic-null direction already derived in DERIVATION-20260927-support-map-Euler-and-kinetic-null.md now has a concrete finite-space explanation: the numerical positive inertia is the minimum residual needed to carry the discrete gradient jumps. This stage adds that quantitative mechanism, rather than merely repeating the old warning that the Schur complement is small.

For a reduced row mu_h*a_h+F_h=0, bounded acceleration and mu_h tending to zero require F_h tending to zero. The implication is conditional; two meshes do not prove the limit. In particular, a force balance obtained by increasingly large acceleration is not evidence that the corresponding continuum constraint has closed.
