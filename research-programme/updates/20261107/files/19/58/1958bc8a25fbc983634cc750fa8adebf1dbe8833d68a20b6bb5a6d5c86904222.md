# Native position force: exact directional propagation over owned inputs

The four matched reactions are saved. Their remaining MTS paired-rule
discrepancy is native-force dominated on both grids. Global label orders
96/128/256 did not converge. This stage changes numerical evaluation,
not the action, fields, coefficients, directions, or acceptance tolerance.

## Forward differentiation

Represent each scalar by a value and one directional derivative. Propagate
the sum, product, quotient, integer powers and positive square root by
their exact chain rules through the existing NativeKernel.evaluate.
This differentiates both its field gradient and geometric gradient, so
the native force variation is a directional Hessian action, not a fitted
replacement force. Strict edge ordering is retained. Coincident stencil
points sharing the same material edge also share its variation; an
independent crossing of kernel branches is outside this derivative.

For the represented kernel energy E(edge,q,beta), the source covector is

    F_source = - E_edge . displacement
               - E_beta . (beta_R * displacement_native).

The reference displacement is fixed in this variation. Forward propagation
therefore differentiates every term, including beta_R. It removes the
need to subtract two perturbed gradients. The old central-difference
calculation remains an independent check at off-grid labels.

## Metric coefficient chain rule

Let F=1-2m/R and beta=R^2 exp(nu) sqrt(F). Define the total moving-point
variations m_dot=delta m+m_R U and nu_dot=delta nu+nu_R U. Then

    F_dot = -2 m_dot/R + 2 m U/R^2
    (F_R)_dot = -2 (m_R)_dot/R + 2 m_R U/R^2
                +2 m_dot/R^2 - 4 m U/R^3
    beta_dot = beta * (2 U/R + nu_dot + F_dot/(2 F))
    beta_R = beta * (2/R + nu_R + F_R/(2 F))
    (beta_R)_dot = beta_dot * (2/R + nu_R + F_R/(2 F))
                  + beta * (-2 U/R^2 + (nu_R)_dot
                    +(F_R)_dot/(2 F) - F_R F_dot/(2 F^2)).

Here (m_R)_dot=delta m_R+m_RR U, and similarly for nu. An independent
high-precision scalar differentiation of manufactured polynomial fields
checks all four beta/beta_R values and variations.

## Isolated precision modes

- legacy: unchanged binary64 geometry/metric input formation, differentiated
  in Decimal arithmetic; must replay the old derivative.
- decimal_geometry: retain the same represented cardinal values, but form
  source, edge and field direction sums before rounding, in Decimal.
- decimal_metric: additionally evaluate the SAME stored metric polynomial
  around its original stored centers in Decimal, including the chain rule.
- decimal_all: additionally evaluate the represented cardinal polynomial
  in Decimal. The stored inverse/cardinal coefficients are not regenerated.

No background constraint solve is rerun and no coefficient is retuned.
The metric values, stored radial-derivative polynomial, and derived second
radial derivative retain their existing definitions. Extending arithmetic
does not imply that stored polynomial interpolation or interface traces
have become exact solutions. Initial probes establish derivative fidelity
and local sensitivity only. Actual integrated convergence must be tested
separately with the unchanged inverse-norm gate2e-11.

All probes and candidate rules remain private, versioned and non-claim.
Reference-native-disabled controls use the same evaluator and must stay
zero. Full current column C0 awaits an independently rechecked controlled
reaction, not just a successful local derivative test.
