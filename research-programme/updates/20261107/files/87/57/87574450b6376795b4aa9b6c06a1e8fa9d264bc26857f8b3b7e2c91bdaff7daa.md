# Stage75 — conserving thermal radiation inside the retained gravity system

## What is inherited, and what is extended

Stage70 already derived the leading homogeneous plasma-cooling response and
its effect on the two retained scalar fields and Friedmann background.
Stage74 supplied a physical finite-shift, polarized Compton operator and
opposite finite-capacity gas sources at a frozen local point. This stage
connects those two results to an expanding, spatially varying scalar wave.
Neither the parent action nor the earlier empirical calibration is replaced.

The independent cold-limit reference is Ma and Bertschinger (1995),
sections4–5. Their usual nonrelativistic baryon treatment is a limiting
check, not the source of the finite-heat extension derived here.
See `SOURCES.md` for primary provenance.

The working units and gravitational normalization are inherited:
c=8piG=1 in the evolution equations, conformal time eta, comoving wave
number k=0.1/Mpc. G, electron mass, Thomson cross section, abundances and
temperature normalization remain sourced inputs. Both retained parent
calibrations, named heavy and cold, receive the same tests. The label
**cold parent** is distinct from the **dust/elastic limit** of its matter
equations.

## 1. Compact representation without fitting the scattering response

Let x be photon energy divided by the reference temperature T_star,
T_star proportional to a^-1. Write n=1/(exp(x)-1), w=n(1+n) and

    delta f = w sum_A X_A psi_A(x).

Here psi_A denotes numerical frequency-basis functions, not an additional
MTS field; B below is a spectral potential, not the earlier memory parameter.

The measure is dmu=x^2 w dx/I3, I3=pi^4/15. The rational basis contains
the constant and x exactly. Its other functions follow a twice
reorthogonalized compact-variable recurrence, not a fit to observations
or to the reference scattering matrix. Define

    M_AB = integral dmu psi_A psi_B,
    E_A  = integral dmu x psi_A,
    N_A  = integral dmu psi_A,
    q_T(x)=x.

The same positive physical event measure and exact finite recoil shifts
as Stage74 are integrated against this basis. In particular, the
isotropic intensity block is assembled directly from pair differences.
The chemical null is retained by that difference, not by clipping an
eigenvalue. Each angular mass is M/(2l+1).

Sixteen- and24-function responses are compared with Stage74's independently
refined reciprocal frequency grid, including its small warm-minus-elastic
response. The attempted32-function recurrence fails its existing
pointwise evaluation check and is excluded rather than silently accepted.
Positive convex interpolation between physical electron-temperature banks
preserves dissipation and nulls. Its accuracy needs separate midpoint
and quadrature checks; positivity alone is not that accuracy test.

The new hot-bath event check exposes a degree8 quadrature error in the
original six-point event rule. It is retained as a failed accuracy trial.
The successor builds degree8 with order8 at every ladder temperature and
tests it against order10, including the hot midpoint. Lower-degree and
frequency-quadrature checks remain unchanged; the error tolerance is not
relaxed. The final paired waves explicitly rerun with the refined block.

## 2. Expanding background and gas heat

Use rho_star proportional to a^-4 and chi=u_star/rho_star, with
u_star=(3/2)n_total k_B T_star. The inherited fully ionized, shared-temperature,
leading nonrelativistic ideal-plasma equation of state gives

    T_e/T_star = R = 1+chi t,
    u = chi rho_star R,     p_g = 2u/3,
    rho_gamma = rho_star (1+chi e),     delta f_bar = chi w B.

Here e=E B; chi is derived from the parent abundance, temperature and
opacity inputs, not refitted. With s=ln(a/a_start), the finite-capacity
homogeneous system is

    M B_s = (kappa/H) K_0(B-q_T t),
    e_s + chi t_s = -R.

The second equation is radiation-plus-gas energy continuity, including
gas expansion work. It implies

    rho_gamma' +4H rho_gamma = chi rho_star H e_s,
    u' +5H u = -chi rho_star H e_s.

The scalar fields obey their retained canonical equations. Friedmann's
equation uses photons, gas rest mass AND heat, cold matter, neutrinos and
both fields. Their total background therefore obeys
rho_total'+3H(rho_total+p_total)=0 and the usual Raychaudhuri identity.

The physical spectrum is first evolved on the retained reference geometry,
consistent to leading order in chi. Its stress is then coupled back into
the field/Friedmann evolution. Feedback into the already tiny spectral
source would require the next order in chi; it is not secretly iterated
here. Very rapid initial electron relaxation is treated implicitly rather
than advertised as a time-resolved microscopic transient.

For continuous interpolation we integrate R analytically from its spline
and set e=-integral R ds-chi t. The interpolated spectrum's number and
energy moments receive the corresponding two-dimensional correction.
This is explicitly a conservative background interpolation, not a
correction to evolved metric perturbations. Both its size and the
remaining kinetic-equation residual are independently checked.

## 3. The temperature and enthalpy equations are derived, not selected

Let the scalar spatial metric be diag(h_L,h_T,h_T), rates sigma=h'/2,
S=2sigma_T+sigma_L, v the longitudinal physical gas velocity, and

    delta_b = N_b-h_T-h_L/2,       N_b'=-ik v,
    delta_r = delta(T_e/T_star),
    delta u = chi rho_star (R delta_b+delta_r),
    B_g = (rho_b+5u/3)/rho_star.

Let C_E and C_F denote the photon collision energy and momentum moments
in units of rho_star, per conformal time. The gas receives their
negatives. Expanding the gas first law and momentum equation gives

    delta_r' = -H delta_r -(2R/3)(ik v+S)
               -C_E/chi -(Q_bar/(chi rho_star)) delta_b,

    B_g v' = -C_F-B_g' v-ik (2chi/3)(R delta_b+delta_r),

    Q_bar = u'+5Hu,       B_g'=H[rho_b/rho_star+(5chi/3)R_s].

These follow by differentiating delta u and the actual gas current
(rho_b+5u/3)v. Omitting the background-heat dilution, the evolving
enthalpy or the pressure force leaves a definite nonzero remainder.
It is not consistent to retain heat exchange but keep pressureless
dust's temperature and momentum equations.

The scalar stress rows used by gravity are

    delta rho = rho_c delta_c+rho_b delta_b+delta u
                +rho_star E X_0+rho_nu Fnu_0+delta rho_fields,
    j = rho_star(B_g v+E X_1/3)+rho_nu Fnu_1/3+j_fields,
    S_T = rho_star(E X_0/3-E X_2/15)
          +rho_nu(Fnu_0/3-Fnu_2/15)+2delta u/3+delta p_fields,
    S_L = S_T+rho_star E X_2/5+rho_nu Fnu_2/5.

For each retained field, delta rho_phi=pi delta pi/a^2+m^2 phi delta phi,
delta p_phi=pi delta pi/a^2-m^2 phi delta phi and
j_phi=-ik pi delta phi/a^2. The inherited field equations make these
stress rows conserve separately.

## 4. Redshift and moving-bath terms

An isotropic non-Planck background redshifts with q_bar=-x d f_bar/dx/w,
not exactly x. Weak differentiation gives the retained coefficients

    q_bar = q_T+chi M^-1 R_weak B,
    (R_weak)_AB = integral dmu (3psi_A+x psi_A') psi_B.

Consequently E q_bar=4rho_gamma/rho_star and its number counterpart has
the factor3. These identities are tested independently of the wave runs.
Finite-band endpoint terms are recorded explicitly.

If C_bar is the evolving isotropic collision source, a small common
Lorentz boost adds the dipole -(C_bar+x dC_bar/dx) v in occupation units.
In potential coefficients this is M^-1 V_weak C_bar v, where

    (V_weak)_AB = integral dmu (2psi_A+x psi_A') psi_B.

Its momentum moment is E C_bar v because E M^-1 V_weak/3=E.
Changing the local scatterer density also adds C_bar delta_b to the
monopole. The implemented Planck-linearized collision generator therefore
contains these derived density and velocity sources, the temperature
column -A_0 q_T delta_r, and the dipole column -A_1 q_bar v.
All gas exchange moments are taken from that SAME photon operator before
free streaming is added.

This is a conservative kinetic approximation on a weakly distorted
background. It does not include the full occupation-dependent mixed
Jacobian of scattering about that distorted bath. Section7 derives its
next inputs rather than treating it as zero.

## 5. Constraint propagation follows from those source equations

Summing the derived components cancels C_E and C_F exactly:

    delta rho'+3H delta rho+H(2S_T+S_L)+ik j+(rho+p)S=0,
    j'+4Hj+ik S_L=0.

The retained metric equations are

    h_T'=2sigma_T,      h_L'=2sigma_L,
    sigma_T'=-4Hsigma_T-Hsigma_L-k^2 h_T/2+a^2(delta rho-S_L)/2,
    sigma_L'=-2Hsigma_T-3Hsigma_L-k^2 h_T+a^2(delta rho+S_L-2S_T)/2.

Define C_H=2k^2 h_T+4HS-2a^2 delta rho and
C_M=2ik sigma_T-a^2 j. Differentiating, using source continuity and
H'=H^2-a^2(rho+p)/2, gives the exact identities

    C_H'=-4H C_H-2ik C_M,
    C_M'=-2H C_M-(ik/2)C_H.

Thus initially satisfied constraints remain satisfied by this continuous
scalar system. The symbolic checker expands the actual equations to zero;
the numerical evolution measures discretization error without projecting
the constraints afterwards. This connects the finite-temperature source
extension to the existing gravitational dynamics, rather than supplying
an independent derivation of the gravitational action.

## 6. Resolving a small response without subtracting two large answers

The matched-elastic control keeps the same warm background, gas
thermodynamics and isotropic heat operator, but replaces angular l>=1
collisions by their exact Thomson limit. It is a diagnostic ablation,
not a claim that such a hybrid is a separate physical theory.

For common mass M, physical operator Lp and control Le, write
y_p=y_e+d. Then

    M d_s = Lp d+(Lp-Le)y_e.

The paired BDF solve evolves d directly. It is algebraically the
difference of the two discrete solves, without final catastrophic
cancellation. Time, spectral-basis and angular refinements must resolve
this small d, not merely the much larger common solution. Independently
evolved physical/control solutions check reconstruction.

The dust/elastic limit is also compared directly against Stage69's
independent full-angular right-hand side, sector by sector.
The propagated waves are the retained scalar seed in each parent over
a=10^-6 to10^-5. I/E are active; B/V operator sectors are retained but
this scalar seed is not a nonzero vector/tensor-polarization experiment.
Amplitudes are transfer coefficients for that inherited seed, not measured
sky fluctuations or a fitted new power spectrum. Constraints are also
checked on d itself, against the small response rather than the baseline.

## 7. Derived next kinetic input: the non-equilibrium pair Jacobian

For a scalar intensity pair i to j, divide forward and reverse rates by
their reference Planck detailed-balance conductance. Let N_i,N_j be the
actual isotropic occupations and a,b the initial/final electron
distribution ratios to the reference Juttner bath. The normalized flux is

    D = a (N_i/n_i)(1+N_j)/(1+n_j)
        -b (N_j/n_j)(1+N_i)/(1+n_i).

For perturbations delta f=w delta X, differentiating gives
delta D=L_i delta X_i-L_j delta X_j, with

    L_i=a(1+n_i)(1+N_j)/(1+n_j)-b n_i N_j/n_j,
    L_j=b(1+n_j)(1+N_i)/(1+n_i)-a n_j N_i/n_i.

At the reference bath L_i=L_j=1. With no electron-temperature shift,
N=n+chi wB yields the exact simpler defects

    L_i-1=chi B_j(n_j-n_i),    L_j-1=chi B_i(n_i-n_j).

The electron-temperature derivative is also fixed: for r=T_e/T_star-1,
d ln a/dr=(gamma_i-mean_gamma_e)/(theta_star(1+r)^2), with the same
formula for b and gamma_j. Here mean_gamma_e=K1(1/theta_e)/K2(1/theta_e)
+3theta_e. At equilibrium D_r=x_j-x_i, recovering the existing
temperature column. These are explicit coefficients for a successor,
not unspecified coupling placeholders.

`mixed_pair_jacobian.py` verifies the occupation derivatives exactly and
samples their actual correction on both physical backgrounds. A sampled
maximum is not a uniform bound or a propagated error bound. The full
polarized lift, temperature derivative assembly and evolving mixed
Jacobian are the next calculation; neither their smallness nor their
effect on the small transfer response is assumed from this sample alone.

## Scope toward observations

This stage is a controlled source/transport calculation using the retained
empirical parent inputs, not a new likelihood improvement. It does not
replace the saved cosmology fit or the separate galaxy work. Photon
production, the unresolved very-low-frequency thermalization regime,
relativistic corrections to the gas equation of state, later recombination
and a full observed CMB spectrum require their own extensions. The next
useful comparison is the mixed kinetic correction to this measured
transfer response, followed by propagation to an observable.
