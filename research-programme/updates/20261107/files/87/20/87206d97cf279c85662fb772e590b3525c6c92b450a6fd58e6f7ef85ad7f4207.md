# A physical cold-side operator bridge

The coordinate is t=ln(theta/theta0) with fixed dimensionless incoming frequency,
the original spectral basis, and the original order-6 electron/angular rule.
The retained collision matrix is B=-1/2 sum a v v^T, with a>=0 and
v=psi(x)-psi(z). Therefore B is negative semidefinite and annihilates the constant
chemical mode. Its temperature derivatives follow by differentiating recoil,
Doppler factors, normalized thermal weights, Bose factors and the same basis
recurrence. Stage186 supplies the analytic derivative triples; no data fit is
used to obtain a cold derivative.

For a cold endpoint b and warm join a=-0.015, let h=b-a<0 and s=(t-a)/h.
Write P0=Q(a), V0=Q'(a) for the established quadratic candidate and P1=B(b),
V1=B_t(b) for the directly integrated endpoint and analytic fixed-membership
slope. The unique cubic with those four boundary conditions is

    H(s)=(2s^3-3s^2+1)P0+(s^3-2s^2+s)h V0
         +(-2s^3+3s^2)P1+(s^3-s^2)h V1.

This is a separate numerical continuation of the physical operator, not a
replacement fundamental law or a free fitted response. It matches Q and Q'
at entry; it does not erase Q(a)-B(a). The endpoint slope treats quadrature
membership as fixed locally. Discrete nodes crossing the domain are explicitly
reported in live/fixed tests; a global smooth fourth-derivative error bound is
not assumed across those crossings.

Its Bernstein matrices are P0, P0+h V0/3, P1-h V1/3, P1. If all are symmetric
negative semidefinite with the same chemical null, nonnegative Bernstein weights
give those properties at every s in [0,1]. The derivative is analytic from this
cubic. Numerical spectra are evidence for the finite matrices, not an exact
interval certificate or a continuous-operator quadrature theorem.

For M positive definite, e=M T, positive heat capacity chi and
K=r(H-D), r>0, D positive semidefinite, the Stage199 Schur identity gives
D_g=chi(alpha+1)+alpha e^T(alpha M-K)^(-1)(-K T)>0 for alpha>0.
For two homogeneous solutions of the same operator,
Q_energy=(delta_p^T M delta_p+chi delta_g^2)/2 obeys
Q_energy'=(delta_p-T delta_g)^T K(delta_p-T delta_g)-chi delta_g^2<=0.
This is a stability functional, not the physical linear energy ledger.

For a fixed saved photon state p, fixed r,D,chi, define d=e^T M^-1,
C_B=chi-d r(B-D)T and G_B=-(1+d r(B-D)p)/C_B. For an approximation H,

    G_H-G_B = -r d(H-B)(p-T G_B)/C_H.

This exact resolvent identity isolates the operator-induced gas change and
includes capacity in the denominator. Multiplication by the same saved gas
kernel projects it to baseline, MTS and total with equal diagnostics. This does
not derive the remaining photon-history contribution or full microscopic source.
