# Held-out response, numerical reference and independent moment audit

At normalized interval nodes0,1/4,1/2,1,the cubic evaluated at3/4 has exact
Lagrange coefficients(1/4,-1,3/2,1/4). Therefore E=H-A/4+W-3C/2-B/4.
Refining integration at anchors updates E without changing the temperature
interpolation weights. The existing cold profile b has b(3/4)=1 and zeros at
the four predictor nodes. It remains a declared sensitivity profile,not a
derived full interpolation-error function. No polynomial degree is increased.

Linearity of the frozen response gives D=L[bE]. For the saved candidate M,
which already includes the H anchor, the counterfactual predictor is M-D.
Compare baseline B,B+M-D,B+M with a common linear readout. High-precision
small-increment cancellation avoids subtracting rounded large total spectra.

The older interiors have nonzero independent moment residuals. For their
raw source J=(J_E,J_s,J_g),the historical coordinate contract keeps J_E and all
nonpivot spectral components,sets the pivot to reproduce J_E,and uses -J_E
for gas. This is a computational reference,not a physical-source derivation.
For a separate raw audit,use the actual spectral moments and J_g,with the
discrete spectral energy forcing T.J_s. Report its difference from the
reference and the independent raw failures. Both use the same frozen operator.

The linear discrete photon and gas equations imply

    dE/dt + chi(dg/dt+g) = rate * (J_E_effective + J_g).

In the BDF solve this changes only the known part of gas:

    gas_free = (history_E + chi*history_g + rate*(J_E_effective+J_g))
               / [chi*(alpha+1)].

The photon/gas response matrix stays identical. Accumulate the supplied
imbalance with the same BDF rule; check E+chi*g+work-injected is constant.
For the closed reference, injected=0 and the old integrator is recovered
bitwise. The raw audit does not turn a nonconserved source into a conserved
one; it measures the consequence of the saved moment mismatch.

The source-energy entry used in the raw audit is the moment of its actual
spectral source,not a replacement value written into the saved bank. Retain
the original independently computed J_E in preflight records for comparison.

The endpoint and three interior-band latest steps enter E with the same
predictor weights. Propagate them under b as separate channels. Their signed
effects and final timestep difference supply a finite defect-specific
sensitivity allowance,distinct from Stage167's current-spectrum allowance.
Do not treat either as a proved bound on untested numerical axes.
