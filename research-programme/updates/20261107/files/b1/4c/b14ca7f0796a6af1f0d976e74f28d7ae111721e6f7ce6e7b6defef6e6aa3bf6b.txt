A.
System multipole moments

ﬁeld is determined in the PN approximation by the mul-
tipole moments of the entire binary system, which are
encoded in the near-zone PN gravitational ﬁeld [18]. We
will denote the system’s mass-multipoles by IL and its

ﬁeld equations, these moments agree with both the source
moments IL and JL and the canonical moments ML and
SL deﬁned in [18].
