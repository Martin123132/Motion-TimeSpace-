# Stage111 — a low-frequency prediction with memory,not a fitted cutoff

## Reuse the derived soft equation

Stage89/90 already supplies,with f=Phi/x and u=ln(a/a_start),

    f'=r[M f+(lambda1-Gamma)f
             +Gamma_DC beta+Gamma_BR b-lambda1 b],
    Gamma=Gamma_DC+Gamma_BR,
    Gamma_DC=kappa_DC/x^2.

M is the positive exact-velocity soft Thomson jump operator on f.
The cached Stage90 stencil is translation-invariant in log x:it depends
on log Doppler shifts and mesh spacing,not an absolute frequency.
Therefore it can be used on a larger frequency interval WITHOUT a new
velocity/angular event integration. This is still a soft-photon model;
it must be tested against the finite-frequency96-mode history.

BR retains its frequency-dependent H/He Gaunt factors. r,lambda1 and
kappa_DC are read from the cached physical coefficient bank.
We do not replace M by diffusion or impose a source-only equilibrium.

## Hard driver with the test band withheld

The full soft target is beta=integral x^4 w Phi' dx/I4.
For this conditional prediction use

    beta_H = integral_(x_H)^60 x^4 w Phi' dx
                  / integral_(x_H)^60 x^4 w dx, x_H=0.1.

Its exact constant-temperature value is b when Phi=bx,so equilibrium is
not broken by truncating the hard-driver integral. This is a declared
approximation to the full weighted mean,not a new fitted parameter.
Changing x_H to0.05 is a prefixed sensitivity check,both above the test
band. Measure beta_H-beta_full after solving;do not feed the test-band
values back into the prediction.

The omitted normalized Planck weight is known. If the derivative on the
omitted part differs from beta_H by at most D,then
|beta_full-beta_H| <= weight_omitted D,up to the negligible high tail.
This is a conditional bound;not an assertion that an unknown derivative
or the sub-plasma region is controlled. Saved finite-band diagnostics can
measure their actual contribution without certifying the unsaved tail.

## Driven response and turnover

On the chosen interior mesh,

    f'=A(u)f+F(u),
    A=r[M_in+diag(lambda1-Gamma)].
    F=r[Gamma_DC beta_H+Gamma_BR b-lambda1 b
                   +M_low f_low+M_high f_high].

Initial f comes from the saved starting epoch. For later times F sees
ONLY gas,hard slope and higher-frequency exterior values. f_low is the
derived source target (Gamma_DC beta_H+Gamma_BR b)/Gamma. Its numerical
boundary is varied;it is not a physical cutoff law.

Duhamel's solution is

    f(u)=T(u,u0)f(u0)+integral_(u0)^u T(u,v)F(v)dv.

This retains memory,changing rates,transport and the hard-photon drive.
The instantaneous source target and first dynamical correction are
diagnostics,not replacements for this equation. In particular,a tiny
remaining shape can regrow when the forcing changes even though the
operator's self terms are dissipative.

Backward Euler solves (I-hA_n)f_n=f_(n-1)+hF_n. Test timestep refinement
rather than calling this exact continuum evolution. Old Stage90
contraction/barrier constants are NOT copied to the enlarged domain:
Gamma-lambda1 may change sign at its upper edge. Check the actual
implicit diagonal dominance and positive transport instead.
