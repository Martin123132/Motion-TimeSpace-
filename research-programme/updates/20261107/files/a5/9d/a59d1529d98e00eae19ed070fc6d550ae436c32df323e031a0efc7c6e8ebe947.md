## Attribution and assumptions

Established QED, the electron mass, Newton's constant, the shared-temperature
nonrelativistic ideal-gas thermodynamics and the current cosmological
calibration are inherited inputs, not newly derived MTS constants.
The new results are a physical polarized grid implementation, its
finite-capacity heat/momentum evolution, and its source interface to the
existing metric equations. The kernel is linearized about equilibrium.
Finite temperature in the photon event law does not imply a nonlinear
nonequilibrium Boltzmann solver or an all-order plasma equation of state.
The auxiliary synthetic fixture is a numerical exchange/polarization
probe, not a new observation or a fit.
