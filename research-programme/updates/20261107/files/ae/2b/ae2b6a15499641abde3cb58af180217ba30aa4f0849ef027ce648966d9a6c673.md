## 2. Derived law

Write the compensated old momentum response as

    R(w) = g - M w + P[Y_d] = 0,
    Y_d = A_original - A_old w,
    L = M + P A_old.

Here M is the old field/source velocity Hessian, P is its metric-momentum coupling and A denotes the radial metric response. Differentiating along the newly solved direct coupled central jet gives

    L wdot = (dR/dt) with w held fixed
           = g_live_dot - Ldot w.

The right side includes geometric measure transport, motion of the source basis, the original geometric z_dot, the full source acceleration, and the mixed radial response. It does not replace L by a frozen-metric Hessian.

The mixed radial equation and its physical outer boundary are

    (Y_d_dot)_R = f_Y Y_d_dot + (f_Y)_dot Y_d + (B_d)_dot,
    nu_d_dot(outer) = -mu_d_dot/B - 2 mu_dot mu_d/B^2,
    B = R_outer - 2 mu_outer.

A manufactured nonzero-boundary example tests the extra boundary term; its value is extremely small in the actual four cases. Moving label cuts and one-sided source traces remain included. This differentiates the averaged physical equations on the central partition, not a topology-changing grid-generation algorithm.

Consequently the completed mode-history entries are now known:

    z_c_dot = z_dot - wdot_field,
    source_c_dot = -wdot_source,
    eta_c_dot = -D wdot_source

at fixed reference coordinates, where the original independent eta is held fixed.

## 6. Next constructive calculation: the full velocity column

Let e_c=(z-w_field,-w_source) be the old-coordinate part of the compensated mode, with independent support displacement eta. Let V=A_old e_c+A_eta eta be its instantaneous **velocity-to-metric** response, and X the metric response to its **position** change at fixed velocities. Along the corrected mode history,

    delta Y = s X + sdot V + s A_old(e_c_dot).

Therefore, at (s,sdot,sddot)=(0,1,0),

    delta Y = V,
    delta Y_t = X + A_old(e_c_dot) + V_dot,
    delta old acceleration = 2 e_c_dot.

V_dot is now available from the completed mixed-response arrays. The additional X term must not be omitted or confused with V_dot. Construct X from the compensated field/source position change **and the independent support geometry**, retaining the physical native-kernel shape derivative, density pushforward, moving faces and boundary conditions. Then form the full force and three weak-current velocity columns, and compare them jointly with the already computed acceleration column on the same controls.

A useful next bounded test is the zero-position linearized response in (sdot,sddot); do not infer that one mode automatically spans all constraints. The position column and finite-amplitude evolution are later calculations. Carry the recorded mixed-metric precision warning into any inference, rather than converting it to a pass.
