# Stage186: second log-temperature derivative of the physical collision integral

## Scope and coordinate

Use t=ln(theta/theta0), theta=k_B T_star/(m_e c^2). Incoming dimensionless frequency
x and the inherited spectral basis remain fixed. All derivatives are with respect
to t, not theta or fixed dimensional photon energy. B_theta=B_t/theta and
B_thetatheta=(B_tt-B_t)/theta^2. Along t=-(u-u0), B_uu=B_tt.

This differentiates the retained finite-order scalar Compton quadrature. It is not
a new electron distribution, a new bank, or the entire microscopic MTS-source
temperature derivative.

## Differentiation of thermal and event factors

Represent each scalar/vector f by its exact derivative triple (f,f_t,f_tt).
The implementation applies

    (fg)_tt = f_tt g + 2 f_t g_t + f g_tt
    (f^-1)_tt = 2 f_t^2/f^3 - f_tt/f^2
    h(f)_tt = h''(f) f_t^2 + h'(f) f_tt.

At fixed Laguerre node y, s=theta*y has s_t=s_tt=s. The original geometry is

    gamma=1+s, beta=sqrt(s(2+s))/(1+s),
    d=1-beta*mu, I=gamma*d,
    parallel=(beta*mu-beta^2)/d,
    transverse=beta*sqrt(1-mu^2)/I,
    O=gamma[1+c*parallel+sqrt(1-c^2)*cos(phi)*transverse].

Normalized radial weights w_i=f_i/sum f_j with f_i=w_GL gamma sqrt(2+s) must be
differentiated INCLUDING normalization. Equivalently, if l_i=(ln f_i)_t,

    (ln w_i)_t = l_i-<l>,
    (ln w_i)_tt = (l_i)_t-<(l)_t>-Var(l),
    (w_i)_tt = w_i[(l_i-<l>)^2+(l_i)_t-<(l)_t>-Var(l)],
    (l_i)_t = s/(1+s)^2 + s/(2+s)^2.

The independent normalization formulas and Stage185's analytic first derivatives
check the derivative-triple implementation. Event first-derivative finite
differences check the new second derivative without a full collision evaluation.

At fixed x, rest-frame scattering cosine c:

    R=[1+theta*x*I*(1-c)]^-1,
    z=x*I*R*O, extra=(1-R)^2/R,
    a=(measure_x/2)*event_weight*(3/2)*R^2
      *[1+n(z)]/[1+n(x)]*(1+c^2+extra),
    n(z)=1/expm1(z).

Differentiate all factors twice. No finite differences or target matrices enter
the production B_tt coefficient.

## Basis recurrence and collision curvature

The inherited basis uses compact coordinate h(z)=(z-p)/(z+p), with fixed
Gram-Schmidt coefficients. Its derivatives are h'=2p/(z+p)^2 and
h''=-4p/(z+p)^3. Differentiate the SAME recurrence twice, including both
orthogonalization passes. No refit or new basis is introduced.

For v=psi(x)-psi(z),

    v_t=-psi_z*z_t,
    v_tt=-psi_zz*z_t^2-psi_z*z_tt,
    B=-1/2 sum m*a*v*v^T,
    B_tt=-1/2 sum m [
        a_tt*v*v^T
        +2*a_t*(v_t*v^T+v*v_t^T)
        +a*(v_tt*v^T+v*v_tt^T+2*v_t*v_t^T)
    ].

All matrices are symmetric; the constant mode gives v=v_t=v_tt=0, so number
nullity survives. B_tt need not be negative semidefinite: dissipativity is a
condition on B, not on its derivatives.

## Finite-domain boundary and retrospective tests

m=1{minimum<=z<=maximum}. The formula above is the anchor-fixed membership
derivative. Stage185 saved, at every target,

    B_live(t)=B_fixed(t)+B_boundary(t).

For symmetric offsets h, the boundary contribution to curvature is exactly

    [B_boundary(h)+B_boundary(-h)]/h^2.

In a continuum treatment,

    m_t=z_t[delta(z-min)-delta(z-max)],
    m_tt=z_tt[delta(z-min)-delta(z-max)]
          +z_t^2[delta'(z-min)-delta'(z-max)].

With F=a*v*v^T, the full integrand curvature includes m*F_tt+2*m_t*F_t+m_tt*F.
No claim that those continuum boundary terms vanish follows from this discrete
test. Report live/fixed tests separately; small offsets can amplify boundary
crossings and roundoff.

Use frozen B_quad=B0+t*B_t0+(t^2/2)*B_tt0. Test against saved targets:
(1) full small change B(t)-B0; (2) second-order remainder B(t)-B0-t*B_t0;
(3) centered curvature [B(h)+B(-h)-2B0]/h^2.
The second test prevents a linear approximation getting credit for curvature.
Already-seen targets make this a no-fit retrospective check, not preregistration.

## Collision-only gas response, including capacity

At each saved heavy/cold anchor, hold p (photon profile), T (temperature mode),
rate r, emission D, heat capacity chi and e (energy row) fixed. Put

    dual=e*M^-1, S(t)=r[B(t)-D],
    C(t)=chi-dual*S(t)*T,
    G(t)=-(1+dual*S(t)*p)/C(t), q=p-T*G(0).

Then at the anchor,

    C_t=-r*dual*B_t*T, C_tt=-r*dual*B_tt*T,
    N_t=r*dual*B_t*p, N_tt=r*dual*B_tt*p,
    G_t=-(N_t+G*C_t)/C=-r*dual*B_t*q/C,
    G_tt=-(N_tt+G*C_tt+2*G_t*C_t)/C
        =-r*dual*B_tt*q/C+2*r*dual*B_t*T*G_t/C.

Thus just projecting B_tt misses the capacity term. The implicit identity
C*G+(1+dual*S*p)=0 and its first two derivatives provide exact algebraic checks.
Numerical target G values use each saved B(t) with the SAME fixed p,r,D,chi,T.
They do not evolve a new gas or radiation history. Along u, this isolated second
derivative has the same sign as in t.

Multiplying G_tt and finite target remainders by the same saved K_g maps the
collision-only effect to control, MTS increment/remainder and total. Preserve
source mass-dual norms, energy cancellation and both heavy/cold histories.
Equal relative errors for proportional kernels follow from linearity, not
independent empirical evidence. Other time/temperature derivatives are not zero
in the actual evolving system; they are deliberately outside this isolation.
