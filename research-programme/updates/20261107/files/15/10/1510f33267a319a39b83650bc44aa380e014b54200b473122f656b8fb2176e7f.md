# Construct B0 with smooth loading and explicit jumps

Use the local jet and endpoint law derived in
DERIVATION-20260929-position-time-loads-and-interface-jumps.md.
The numerical construction now differentiates the Eulerian inverse label
and inverse reference maps with a four-component nilpotent jet. The
moving label integration cuts and their measures are differentiated too.
The native image and its three derivatives use the validated physical
kernel expansion. This is not a finite displacement of the background.

All mixed density values are sampled strictly inside each inherited
radial cell, at Chebyshev-Gauss nodes. Reconstruct their polynomial on the
Lobatto grid only after differentiation. This supplies separate regular
one-sided traces at each interface, without choosing an arbitrary
inclusive endpoint mask or smearing endpoint atoms.

Let P=X+A_old(edot) and T=the saved coupled background metric-time
response. Write F for the five physical load fields and F_s,F_t,F_st
for the differentiated arrays. The regular B0 forcing is

    b = (D_s f_Y) T + D_s(f_F F_t),
    D_s f_F F_t includes f_F F_st,
    (B0)_R = f_Y B0 + b.

Both coefficient derivatives are evaluated with complex-step analytic
formulas, not differenced completed metric solves. The source/native
endpoint atoms are multiplied by the corresponding partial derivatives
f_rho or f_gram and retained as signed jumps. Coincident owners are
summed only after their separate velocities have been included.

The mass integration starts with B0_mu(Rin)=0. The outer lapse obeys

    B0_nu + B0_mu/(Rout-2mu)
        = -2 P_mu T_mu/(Rout-2mu)^2.

The first-order radial inverse is solved with explicit jump prefixes.
This does not yet solve k0; B0 holds the old acceleration fixed.
