## Local derivations

`kernel_common.py` implements the cancellation-free Pauli reduction of the
two tree-level Dirac diagrams; `verify_channels.py` recomputes those diagrams
independently as4x4 matrices. `verify_reciprocity.py` recomputes the actual
reverse event with a recoiling initial electron. `derive_reciprocal_form.py`
checks exact identities and independent Bessel moments.

`polarized_integral.py` integrates full spin channels and boosted screens.
`dipole_galerkin.py` projects the physical reciprocal weak form and evolves
the dipole/plasma block; `compare_galerkin.py` distinguishes smooth accuracy
from unresolved oscillatory stability probes. All amplitude, continuum,
grid and fit scopes are kept separate. Standard QED is inherited input,
not a new MTS discovery or a derivation of the electromagnetic coupling.
