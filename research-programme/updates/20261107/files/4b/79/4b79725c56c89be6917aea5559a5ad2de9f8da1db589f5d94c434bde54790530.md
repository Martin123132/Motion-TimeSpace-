Its inherited static electric deformability is lambda=62823.92235391782 (length^5).

The radiative scope is all equatorially sourced metric ell=2 channels:

## 2. Orbit expansion: hold p fixed

Set r0=M p, p>6, r0>R, tau=Omega_r t. Then

    Omega_phi = 1/(M p^(3/2)),
    Omega_r = Omega_phi sqrt(1-6/p),
    r = r0[1-e cos(tau)] + O(e^2),
    v = dr/dt = e r0 Omega_r sin(tau) + O(e^2),
    phi = Omega_phi t + e kappa sin(tau) + O(e^2),
    kappa = 2 sqrt[p/(p-6)] (p-3)/(p-2).

This follows by expanding the Darwin time Jacobian with coefficient
a_t=2/(p-2)-2+1/(p-6), expanding its azimuth Jacobian with coefficient 1/(p-6),
and inverting time before computing phi(t). E,h,Omega_r,Omega_phi have no linear
e changes at fixed p. The small-e finite computations explicitly reset E and h
after selecting p=5R/M.

Stage34's fixed-periapsis family instead has p=5R(1+e)/M. Its finite-e damping
numbers should not be compared as if that were the same derivative.

## 3. A source-sideband law applicable to both parities

Strip exp(-im phi) from the source and let x=r_* be the tortoise coordinate.
For any homogeneous master solution y, write the weak source functional as

    T(r,v;omega) = [a(r)+i v d(r)] y(r) - c(r) y_x(r) + O(v^2).
    f=1-2M/r,   y_r=y_x/f,   (y_x)_r=(V-omega^2)y/f.

All radial derivatives here hold E,h,omega fixed. Evaluate the homogeneous
solution and potential at the frequency of the particular sideband:
omega=m Omega_phi+n Omega_r. The carrier response cannot substitute for it.

The carrier is I_m0=a y-c y_x. For n=+/-1,

    lim[e->0] I_mn/e = A_mn y(r0)+B_mn y_x(r0),
    A_mn = {-r0[a'-c(V-omega^2)/f]-n r0 Omega_r d+n m kappa a}/2,
    B_mn = {-r0[a/f-c']-n m kappa c}/2.

Indeed the first-order Fourier integral is

    <-r0 T_r cos(tau)
      +[r0 Omega_r T_v-i m kappa T0]sin(tau)>_n
    = [-r0 T_r+i n r0 Omega_r T_v+n m kappa T0]/2.

There is no linear carrier correction, and |n|>=2 starts at O(e^2).
The first polar m=0,n=1 sideband must be retained: it transports radial action
and energy even though its angular-momentum flux is zero. Exactly static m=n=0
is not inserted in a radiative expression containing 1/omega.

### Polar coefficients

For ell=2 define L=2+3M/r, C=8 pi E Y/f, Omega=f h/(E r^2),
Bpoly=8+12M/r+84M^2/r^2, and

    a = C{-r Omega^2(3-m^2)/6
          +[-f f'-2f^2/r+r f Omega^2]/(3L)
          +f^2 L'/(3L^2)-f Bpoly/(12r L^2)},
    c = C f/(3L),
    d = -2m C Omega/(3L).

Y=Y20(pi/2)=-sqrt[5/(16pi)] for m=0; Y22(pi/2)=sqrt[15/(32pi)].
These are obtained from stage33's canonical delta and delta-prime source.
Differentiating a,c for the sideband varies C(r) and Omega(r) as well. This is
not the auxiliary source-radius derivative used during delta canonicalization.

### Odd coefficients

For m=1 define j=4pi h sqrt[15/(8pi)]/(3r). Then

    a=-j f/r,   c=j,   d=-j omega/f.

They follow from the inherited integrated-by-parts weak source
I_y=-<exp(i omega t) J[(f/r+i omega v/f)y+y_x]>,
J=j exp(-i phi). The earlier odd-only numerical e->0 extrapolation is now a
validation point rather than the definition of the law.

## 4. Interior matching, currents and work

The inherited current-preserving real basis is essential for the small
body-minus-BH difference. With Q(R)=0,Q_x(R)=1,

    O=O_R+i beta_O Q,
    H=O_R+(alpha_H-alpha_O+i beta_H)Q,
    beta_O=omega/|O0|^2,   beta_H=-omega/|H0|^2,
    W_B=alpha_O-b+i beta_O,
    W_H=alpha_O-alpha_H+i(beta_O-beta_H),
    DeltaC=-(alpha_H-b+i beta_H) I_O/(W_B W_H),
    A_BH=I_H/(W_H O0),   DeltaA=DeltaC/O0,
    A_H=I_O/(W_H H0).

b is calculated from the actual interior at this frequency. Pair power is
3 omega^2 |A|^2/(4pi). Carrier powers are per mu^2; analytic sideband powers
are their leading coefficients per mu^2 e^2. Difference power is formed by
interference, not subtraction of rounded nearly equal totals.

The inherited reciprocal-work identity reads

    F_ret=-3 DeltaC I_O/(8pi),
    2 omega Im(F_ret)=DeltaP_infinity-P_H.

The same identity with omega replaced by m or n supplies the azimuthal or
radial action rate. Numerical source, boundary, current and reciprocity
controls are applied to both the body and its BH reference.

## 5. Derive the near-circular secular law

Let z=e^2 and let j_r denote specific radial action. Its exact leading
geodesic susceptibility is

    j_r=C(p) z+O(z^2),
    C(p)=M p^(3/2) sqrt(p-6)/[2(p-2)sqrt(p-3)],
    C'/C=3/(2p)+1/[2(p-6)]-1/(p-2)-1/[2(p-3)].

For each channel (body, BH, or their difference), define

    P0 = lim[p_dot/mu] = -sum_carriers m Pbar/(omega h_p),
    R0 = lim[j_r_dot/(mu z)] = -sum_sidebands n Pbar^(2)/omega,
    Gamma_z = lim[z_dot/(mu z)] = [R0-C' P0]/C.

Here h_p is the circular derivative of specific angular momentum, Pbar is
power per mu^2, and Pbar^(2) is power per mu^2 z.
The C'P0 term is required because the orbit is shrinking while it circularises.
Dimensions: C has length, P0 and Gamma_z length^-2, R0 length^-1.
The exactly circular solution remains z=0.

For the leading point-mass Newtonian quadrupole,

    Gamma_N=-608/(15 M^2 p^4).

This law is local and adiabatic. A continuous trajectory requires integrating
the p-dependent rates and checking their separation from orbital and response
timescales; it is not generated by extrapolating these four radii by assertion.

## 6. Leading outgoing electric-tidal coefficient, derived rather than fitted

Use stage20's matched total STF quadrupole in the test-mass limit,

    Iij=mu[r^2+3lambda/r^3] n_<ij>,    k=3lambda/r0^5.

At first order in e, the m=2 carrier coefficient is 1+k; the n=+1 and n=-1
coefficients are 1+7k/2 and -3-k/2. For m=0,n=1 the coefficient is -1+3k/2.
The squared spherical-harmonic normalization ratio is |Y20/Y22|^2=2/3.

Relative to zero-tide circular power P00, the sideband powers per e^2 are

    P21/P00 = (729/64)(1+7k/2)^2,
    P2,-1/P00 = (9/64)(1+k/6)^2,
    P01/P00 = (1/96)(1-3k/2)^2.

The radial-action loss factor is 11/3+(53/2)k+O(k^2).
Subtracting the carrier shrinkage contribution (1+k)^2/2 and normalizing
by the zero-tide damping factor 19/6 gives

    DeltaGamma_infinity/Gamma_N = (459/19) lambda/r0^5 + subleading terms.

This is the leading outgoing electric-tidal contribution, not an exact
finite-radius formula for the full body-minus-BH-total rate. No coefficient
has been fitted to the four numerical values.

## 7. Why horizon absorption needs its own asymptotic term

Poisson's primary slow-motion absorption formula is

    P_H=(16M^6/45)<dot E_ab dot E_ab+dot B_ab dot B_ab>.

Source: Eric Poisson, Physical Review D70,084044(2004), equation8.38,
PDF page25: https://arxiv.org/pdf/gr-qc/0407050 .
The leading electric tide is Eij=-3mu r^-3 n_<ij>.
Its circular derivative norm gives P_H0/P00=(M/r0)^4.
The magnetic contribution is subleading in this slow-motion limit.

The electric first-sideband amplitudes are 7/2,-1/2,3/2 for (2,1),(2,-1),(0,1).
Horizon power weights frequency squared, rather than the infinity
quadrupole's frequency to the sixth power. Consequently the powers relative
to e^2 P_H0 are 441/16,1/16,3/8, the radial-action factor is 19/2, and

    Gamma_H/Gamma_N = [(19/2-1/2)/(19/6)](M/r0)^4 + ...
                    = (54/19)(M/r0)^4 + ... .
    DeltaGamma_total = DeltaGamma_infinity-Gamma_H.

Thus the removed BH absorption is a relative r0^-4 effect; the outgoing
electric tide is a relative r0^-5 effect. At arbitrarily large radius with
fixed M and lambda, the former can dominate the difference. The total is
not asserted to have a strictly leading r0^-5 asymptotic or a universal sign.

These are separate leading expansions, not a complete combined PN expansion
through r0^-5: subleading absorption terms also require accounting at the next
orders. At the four radii computed here, absorption is only 1.45e-6 to 1.15e-5
of the magnitude of the total difference, but it remains included.
