# The compact body's dynamical quadrupole now includes radiation

**New result:** derived the coupled time-dependent field, current and metric
equations, and computed an outgoing, slowly damped quadrupole resonance for our
existing weak-gravity body. Its moving surface follows from the conserved current.

- Frequency: **0.02045661** in model units; damping rate **9.468e-7**.
- It takes about **3,439 oscillations** for the amplitude to fall by a factor e.
- Four numerical formulations/settings agree closely. Independent nonlinear
  field/curvature checks resolve the equations at roughly a few parts in a million.
- The zero-frequency solution recovers our previous static tidal result; removing
  gravity recovers the earlier flat-space oscillation frequency independently.
- For two bodies separated by 20 radii, the leading quadrupole driving frequency
  is about **0.96%** of this internal frequency: a useful scale separation.

The unit scale remains uncalibrated. These are predictions of the retained
Einstein-condensate candidate, not measurements of an observed object.
Tighter near-edge attempts that failed are kept and excluded, not counted as passes.
