# Position-enabled force/current compatibility

Once complete matched C0 integrals exist, the tested instantaneous system has four equations and three unknowns:

    B + C0*s + C1*u + C2*a = 0,
    u = sdot, a = sddot.

These are local jet coordinates, not three empirical fit parameters. An instantaneous compatible jet need not extend to a finite trajectory.

## Eliminate position, not the small acceleration coefficient

For C0_F nonzero, the force equation gives exactly

    s = -(B_F + C1_F*u + C2_F*a)/C0_F.

Define r=C0_J/C0_F for the three current rows. The remaining system is

    d + G1*u + G2*a = 0,
    d = B_J-r*B_F,
    Gi = Ci_J-r*Ci_F.

This avoids the old division by the very small C2_F. It does not add an equation, suppress a residual, or change the model: substitution is exactly equivalent to the four-row system. If G1 and G2 are independent, a left-null vector w is their cross product and compatibility requires w.d=0. If they are dependent, rank and the augmented rank must instead be checked; a vanishing cross product is not evidence of compatibility.

Compute column-scaled singular values, the left-null obstruction, and force plus each pair of current equations. Report the omitted current in each three-equation solve. Also compare unweighted and row-balanced least squares; no one row-weight choice is an acceptance criterion. Retain all four residuals, sampled error propagation, and coefficient magnitudes. Large or grid-sensitive amplitudes require a finite-family/history test.

## Ownership and uncertainties

Use the completed C0 column at each grid, without mixing it with partial archives. C1/C2 and their augmented sampled uncertainties remain those already owned by the reacted-column sensitivity stage. Preserve both the historical baseline and any freshly integrated baseline as separate candidate inputs; explicitly report their difference before interpreting compatibility. Quadrature comparisons, independent-force disagreement, and known reaction sensitivity are diagnostic contributions, not universal error bars.

For a candidate x=(s,u,a), propagate sampled component differences as

    e_res = e_B + |s|e_C0 + |u|e_C1 + |a|e_C2,

plus a floating summation allowance. Unresolved inherited position/mixed-time and spatial errors remain outside this sampled budget. No definitive compatibility or incompatibility theorem follows from one fitted candidate or from these sampled differences alone. Fine-grid C0 reaction sensitivity must not silently stand in for a missing third-grid sensitivity computation.

If the fresh baseline disagrees beyond its inherited numerical budget, the discrepancy is a separate test result. Do not hide it by silently replacing the baseline or averaging it away. Both baseline alternatives can be explored, with the assembly labelled provisional until ownership is reconciled.
