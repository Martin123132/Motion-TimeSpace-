# Source and dependency map

## Primary external sources

- Chluba, Sazonov and Sunyaev (2007), [astro-ph/0611172v2](https://arxiv.org/html/astro-ph/0611172):
  distinguishes electron-temperature and incident-spectrum dependence and
  states limitations of approximate induced emission. Newly archived here as
  `sources/astro-ph-0611172.html`, with URL/hash in `sources/provenance.json`.
- Chluba and Sunyaev, [1109.6552](https://arxiv.org/html/1109.6552), DOI
  [10.1111/j.1365-2966.2011.19786.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2966.2011.19786.x):
  inherited isotropic DC/BR background expressions, not a general angular kernel.
  Local authority: `../emitting-thermal-background-20261008/sources/1109.6552.html`.
- Ravenni and Chluba (2020), [2005.06941v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2005.06941v1), DOI
  [10.1088/1475-7516/2020/10/025](https://doi.org/10.1088/1475-7516/2020/10/025):
  near-equilibrium DC factor, Gould integral, and the distinction between
  emission/absorption and redistribution. Archived at
  `../scaled-spectral-evolution-20261007/sources/2005.06941v1.html`.
- [Author Gaunt tables](https://data.nublado.org/gauntff/): inherited hydrogen
  and helium numerical tables; physical temperature/frequency derivatives use
  the same interpolation as the background, without fitting a new coefficient.
  Local files: Stage79 `sources/gauntff_merged_Z01.dat` and
  `sources/gauntff_merged_Z02.dat`.

All equations differentiating the retained sources and the numerical density
response are this stage's calculations. Established QED emission physics is
an input to the MTS parents, not claimed as an MTS discovery.
