position, field, velocity, acceleration, metric and metric-time response

moving source position, and moving lower limit of each current primitive.

field, radial coefficients, velocities, and current primitives. It uses
the previously validated minimal Decimal tangent repair, not a newly
rounded direction. The source-clock derivative is regular even at zero
source velocity. The gravitational current uses the same corrected
metric-time response, with radial integration split at all participating
response grids.
