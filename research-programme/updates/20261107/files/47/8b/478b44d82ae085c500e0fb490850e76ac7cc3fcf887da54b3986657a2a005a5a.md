## Source coupling result

Photon and electron energy–momentum sources were integrated separately.
Their sum vanishes to the working precision. Newly emitted photons carry
positive energy; removing their contribution breaks the balance.

At E=.00045962015785263325 and scattering cosine .1, the finite photon
energy correction per Born differential rate is -2.0951955134081748e-15
in electron rest-energy units. Created photons carry +1.0928716275919751e-13;
the harder photon contributes -1.113823582726057e-13. The electron receives
the remaining +2.0951955134081748e-15. These are conditional event-integrated
coefficients, not a cosmological heating rate.
