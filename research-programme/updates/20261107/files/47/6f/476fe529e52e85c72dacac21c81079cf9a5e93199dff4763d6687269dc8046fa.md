## 1. Physical problem and unchanged inputs

Keep the previous compact Einstein-scalar body, its coupled six-variable polar
interior, moving free-edge conditions, and Schwarzschild exterior. Its inherited
model-unit values are R=19.73166220910539 and M=0.296546804477414. A conserved
test particle follows the same exterior geodesic in the body and BH problems.
Its periapsis is 5R and e is 0, 0.3 or 0.6. Thus the dimensionless semilatus is
p=5R(1+e)/M. No response coefficient or coupling is refitted here.

The source, its distributional normalization and metric reconstruction come
from stage33. Let x=r_* and let S0 delta_x+S1 delta'_x be that source. For a
homogeneous radial solution y, the orbit-period Fourier functional is

    I_y = (1/T_r) integral_0^T_r exp(i omega t) (S0 y - S1 y_x) dt,
    omega = m Omega_phi + n Omega_r.

Pericentre at t=phi=0 fixes the canonical phase. Reflection around pericentre
makes I_y real when y is real. This is checked by a separate full-period complex
integral, not assumed for an arbitrary orbit-phase convention. In a different
phase convention the reciprocal action must use the appropriate dual source.

For even ell=2 the independent reality pairs are m=2 with every signed n,
and m=0 with n>0. Each represents two conjugate modes. The m=0,n=0 solution
is static and is not inserted into any expression containing 1/omega.
The odd ell=2,m=1 reality pairs already calculated in stage31 complete the
equatorially sourced radiative metric ell=2 channels when combined with these.
This is not a sum over all multipole orders or additional asymptotic species.

## 2. Current-preserving exterior basis

Let O0 and H0 be the values at R of the unit-amplitude outgoing-infinity and
ingoing-horizon solutions. For signed omega, negative-frequency solutions are
obtained by conjugation. Normalize both values at R to unity and write

    O = O_R + i beta_O Q,
    H = O_R + (alpha_H-alpha_O+i beta_H) Q,
    Q(R)=0, Q_x(R)=1, O_R(R)=1, O_R,x(R)=alpha_O,
    beta_O = omega/|O0|^2, beta_H = -omega/|H0|^2.

Here O_R is the real part of the outgoing solution, propagated with the polar
Zerilli potential; Q is an independently integrated real homogeneous solution.
The Wronskian W(O_R,Q)=1 and the reconstruction of H are tested along the orbit.
This preserves the tiny radiative current instead of extracting it by subtracting
nearly real complex numbers. Outgoing and horizon Frobenius/asymptotic series
have separate truncation controls. This is the polar potential throughout, not
the axial potential reused by accident.

The actual coupled interior gives U(R)=1, U_x(R)=b. Its imaginary part is checked
before using real b: this daughter is the inherited conservative, nonabsorbing
body, not a phenomenological lossy material. A material dissipative current
would require an extra term in the work identity and cannot be silently removed.

Let I_R=I[O_R] and I_Q=I[Q]. Define

    I_O = I_R+i beta_O I_Q,
    I_H = I_R+(alpha_H-alpha_O+i beta_H) I_Q,
    W_B = alpha_O-b+i beta_O,
    W_H = alpha_O-alpha_H+i(beta_O-beta_H),
    W_D = alpha_H-b+i beta_H.

The exact same-source amplitudes are

    C_BH = I_H/W_H,
    Delta C = -W_D I_O/(W_B W_H),
    A_BH,infinity = C_BH/O0,
    Delta A_infinity = Delta C/O0,
    A_BH,horizon = I_O/(W_H H0).

Independently propagating U and integrating I_U gives A_body=I_U/(W_B O0).
The difference formula is checked against this direct construction. Importantly,
small body/BH differences are evaluated using the factored Delta C, not by
subtracting two separately rounded almost-equal powers.

## 3. Radiation, retarded work and two actions

For the ell=2 reality pairs, the established Moncrief-normalized flux law is

    P = 3 omega^2 |A|^2/(4 pi),
    Ldot_out = m P/omega,  Jrdot_out = n P/omega.

The separate single-mode factor is 3/(8 pi). Signed omega matters: a pair with
m>0 and omega<0 can carry negative angular momentum while carrying positive
energy. The axisymmetric m=0 sidebands carry energy and radial action, but no
angular momentum. All radiative powers here are per squared geometric test mass.

For a pair, define the smooth body-minus-BH reciprocal kernel

    F_ret = -3 Delta C I_O/(8 pi).

The exact modal identity is

    2 omega Im F_ret = Delta P_infinity - P_BH,horizon,
    2 m Im F_ret = Delta Ldot_out,
    2 n Im F_ret = Delta Jrdot_out.

This includes the BH horizon sink. Replacing the BH by the body removes that
sink as well as altering the outgoing wave. The equal-boundary control gives
Delta C=0 exactly. Five algebraic identities in derive_work.py check this
matching, work and action structure for arbitrary real source overlaps and
surface data. Those are algebraic tests, not independent observational evidence.

Independent controls of the source integral use the previously derived metric
reciprocity functional over a complete radial cycle. Its endpoint term cancels
over that cycle. No analogous cancellation is assumed on a partial interval.

Add the same-source odd fluxes without rerunning or altering their sealed files.
Each row keeps its own m; the old odd-only mapper had m=1 and must not be reused
unchanged for m=0 or m=2. For each body or BH channel, to leading test-mass order,

    E_dot/mu = -sum Pbar,
    h_dot/mu = -sum m Pbar/omega,
    j_dot/mu = -sum n Pbar/omega,
    E_dot = Omega_phi h_dot + Omega_r j_dot.

Use z=e^2, the regular stage32 Jacobian, and

    j_p=(E_p-Omega_phi h_p)/Omega_r,
    j_z=(E_z-Omega_phi h_z)/Omega_r,
    det(h,j)=-det(E,h)/Omega_r,
    p_dot=(h_dot j_z-h_z j_dot)/det(h,j),
    z_dot=(h_p j_dot-h_dot j_p)/det(h,j).

At e=0, j_p=0 exactly and only n=0 carriers remain, so z_dot=0. These are local
secular rates on three specified background orbits, not an integrated inspiral.
Conservative orbital shifts are not inferred from dissipative balance alone.

## 4. Static contribution and units

At omega=0 use the previously validated static basis P,Q, with its reference
radius set to one model length. The interior fixes U proportional to P+3lambda Q.
The regular BH static solution is P. Thus

    Delta C_static = 3 lambda I_Q/W(P,Q),
    F_static = -3 Delta C_static I_Q/(16 pi).

This single m=0 mode has degeneracy one and zero energy/angular radiation.
Its circular action is checked against the independently coded stage20 result.
It is one conservative kernel contribution, not the entire eccentric binding
energy and not a new static fit to eccentric data.

All length/time units are inherited inverse-mu_star units, with geometric
G=c=1 for the exterior calculation. Restoring a physical body scale or deriving
the retained Einstein-Hilbert coefficient is outside this wave calculation.
Pbar has dimension length^-2; angular and radial action fluxes per mu^2 have
dimension length^-1; p_dot/mu and z_dot/mu have dimension length^-2. F_ret/mu^2
has dimension length^-1. The source normalization mu=1 is bookkeeping for the
linear response, not a statement that the companion has comparable mass to M.

## 5. Controls, finite spectra and resonance

Two accepted settings change the compact-edge matching distance, interior
collocation tolerance and exterior boundaries/series order, with adequate
source quadrature in each. Separate quadrature-only controls keep the fine
radial settings unchanged. The original coarsest source grid fails in the far
sidebands and is retained as a diagnostic, as detailed in NUMERICAL-DIAGNOSTIC.md.
Each solved mode saves its full interior spline, source sampling and exterior
basis arrays before the next begins. Numerical failures must be retained and
reported, never converted into zero-flux points.

The circular flux is regressed against stage19. A separate Newtonian calculation
averages Q_ij''' Q_ij'''/5 and (2/5) epsilon_zjk Q_jl'' Q_kl''' along a Kepler orbit.
Its analytic Peters-Mathews energy and angular-momentum factors are checked by
this tensor calculation. Finite-Schwarzschild results need not equal leading
Newtonian values exactly; this is a normalization/size check, not a demand to
erase relativistic corrections or a substitute for a weak-field limit sequence.

The harmonic scan passes the previously calculated polar pole near
0.020456606655586105-i 9.468001385059438e-7. Nearest signed orbital harmonics are
identified and evaluated rather than inheriting a circular nonresonance claim.
High-harmonic source integrals can reach a cancellation floor. Individual tiny
tail amplitudes therefore cannot be assigned relative precision just because
the algebraic optical identity is exact. Use component-sum-normalized errors,
quadrature changes and explicit finite-band remainders. Neither a small last
band nor absence of a peak in the checked band is a certified infinite-tail
bound or an exclusion of every higher internal resonance.

The numerical summary states which finite sums are accepted and their measured
refinement sensitivity. The next calculational step is to use the combined
channels in a controlled orbit sequence, including the near-circular polar
sideband law, before constructing a continuous secular trajectory.
