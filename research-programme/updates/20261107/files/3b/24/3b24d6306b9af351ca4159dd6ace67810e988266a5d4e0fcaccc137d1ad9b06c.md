## Faced the existing observational pipeline

Replayed the actual full-covariance Pantheon+/DESI likelihood from Stage48. Replacing part of the **existing** pressureless budget by the leading cold heavy mode preserves chi2=1465.10191 with Lambda or1465.13071 without Lambda. The largest numerical change is2.3e-13. This is background degeneracy, not new evidence for the heavy mode or a new fit improvement. No finite-mass two-field cosmology fit, CMB, galaxy fit or growth likelihood is claimed.

The empirical slow masses are of order H0, so simply renaming those same modes “cold galactic condensates” does not work. An illustrative10kpc/200km/s localization scale needs a frequency around6.42e8 H0. In a positive gapped quadratic bath, making even1% of the original motion variable overlap with a bath at that scale requires a relative bare-to-light mass-squared matching shift of roughly1e15–5e15. That is a quantified structural challenge, not a statistical exclusion or a computed radiative-instability result.

A very weakly mixed fast bath component avoids a large matching cancellation, but could then be an ordinary scalar-dark-matter completion rather than an MTS-specific explanation. We must not disguise that distinction. Neither a specific spectrum nor the populated state has been derived here.
