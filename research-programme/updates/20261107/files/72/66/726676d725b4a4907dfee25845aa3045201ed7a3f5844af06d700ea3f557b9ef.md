# Stage110 — equation for the non-mu/non-y remainder

## Exact fixed projection

Use u=ln(a/a_start),x=h nu/(k_B T_star). Comoving x,Planck weight and
the Stage109 finite-band projection are constant on the saved interval.
Let V map96 coefficients p to Phi,W be the positive quadrature measure,
M=V^T W V,t represent temperature g=x=Vt,S=(s_mu,s_y),C=<g,g>,
G=S^T W S. The orthogonal projection is

    P=g C^-1 g^T W+S G^-1 S^T W, Q=I-P.
    eta=<g,Vp>/C, a=G^-1 S^T W Vp, R=Q Vp.

The identical downstream photon equation for baseline and correction is

    M p'=r (K-D_DC-D_BR)(p-t b).

Gas equations differ by the baseline's adiabatic expansion source:

    chi(b'+b)=-1-r t^T(K-D_DC-D_BR)(p-t b)  [baseline]
    chi(b'+b)=   -r t^T(K-D_DC-D_BR)(p-t b)  [window increment]

Keep actual gas histories. The extra window source is off downstream;
this is not a claim that the complete outside-window source is zero.
For L_j in {K,-D_DC,-D_BR},the residual obeys exactly

    R'=sum_j r Q V M^-1 L_j(p-t b).

## Self-damping and resolved-template driving

Analytic y need not belong to the96-mode space. Define

    U=M^-1 V^T W S, z=M^-1 V^T W R.
    p-t b=z+t(eta-b)+U_mu a_mu+U_y a_y.

This identity is obtained by mass-projecting spectral reconstruction.
Retain the full R,not just Vz. Apply each L_j to these four pieces.
For full-band H=||R||^2/2,the exact balance is

    H'=sum_j r z^T L_j z
       +sum_j r z^T L_j[t(eta-b)+U_mu a_mu+U_y a_y].

Each self term is nonpositive for the saved symmetric dissipative
operators. The other terms may drive or erase R. A dissipative collision
operator can feed this particular remainder from the resolved templates;
a low-frequency leftover does not by itself imply kinetic instability.
The derived instantaneous self rate is

    gamma_R=-sum_j r z^T L_j z / ||R||^2.

It is not an autonomous scalar equation,eigenvalue or fitted constant.
For a frequency bin D,H_D'=<R,R'>_D uses the SAME full-band projection.
Individual bin contributions need not be negative because transport and
template subtraction mix frequency bands.

## Test against saved histories

65 shared epochs cover u/U=0.5..1. They are saved grid points of baseline
6400 and correction1024/512/256. Test64 post-seam epochs using each
history's actual native BDF2 step:

    D_h p_n=(3p_n/2-2p_(n-1)+p_(n-2)/2)/h.

At the window's initial seam no derivative is claimed. The independent
gas test includes the -1 source only for baseline.

For any fixed readout R and positive band norm,the exact BDF2 identity is

    <R_n,D_h R_n>=[E_G(n)-E_G(n-1)]/h
                        +||R_n-2R_(n-1)+R_(n-2)||^2/(4h),
    E_G(n)=[||R_n||^2+||2R_n-R_(n-1)||^2]/4.

The last term is numerical curvature,not physical dissipation.
Use this to check rate/history attribution without calling finite-step
differences exact continuum derivatives.

A fixed spectral-energy matrix is used for differentiation. Independently
saved energies are retained and compared within explicit roundoff bounds;
Stage109's endpoint residual must be reproduced. No history is altered.

## Connection to the already derived infrared limit

Stage89 derived a hard-photon-controlled soft target,not a free cutoff:

    b_DC[Phi]=integral z^4 n_P(1+n_P) Phi'(z) dz/I4,
    b_IR=(kappa_DC b_DC+kappa_BR b)/(kappa_DC+kappa_BR),
    partial_tau Phi=L_soft(Phi-b x)
        -(kappa_DC+kappa_BR)/x^2 (Phi-x b_IR)+remainders.

Here test saved finite-band weak matrices first. Do not replace them by
a diffusion/soft approximation or infer a physical infrared continuation
from analytic template tails.
