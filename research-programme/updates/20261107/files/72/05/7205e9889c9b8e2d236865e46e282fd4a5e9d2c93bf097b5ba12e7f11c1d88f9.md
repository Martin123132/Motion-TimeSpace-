## 1. Independent pressure

For either retained quadratic canonical field, write phi for its background value, v for its tabulated conformal velocity, d for its synchronous perturbation, p=d', and mu=am. The field stress gives

    D_phi = v p + mu^2 phi d,
    P_phi = v p - mu^2 phi d,
    Q_phi = k v d,
    R_phi = v^2,       Pi_phi = 0.

The minus sign in P is fixed by the canonical action; it is not selected to make a continuity test pass. Both scalar pressures agree exactly with the original native Diagnostics readout.

For the massive-neutrino moment representation, with positive native weights W_j already including the a^-2 density factor and speed u_j=q_j/sqrt(q_j^2+a^2 m_nu^2),

    D_nu = sum_j W_j f0_j/u_j,
    P_nu = (1/3) sum_j W_j u_j f0_j,
    Q_nu = sum_j W_j f1_j,
    Pi_nu = sum_j W_j u_j f2_j.

The pressure agrees with a separate call to the original Nu_Integrate_L012. Photon and massless-neutrino pressures equal one third of their density perturbations. Baryon pressure uses the original delta_p_b thermal force; CDM has zero pressure. The total is the sum of these seven sectors, with no galaxy-scale substitution or phenomenological pressure term.

## 2. Conservation test and exact defect decomposition

The imported linear conservation laws in these conventions are

    C = D' + H(D+3P) + k Q + k z R = 0,
    E = Q' + 2H Q - k P + (2/3) k Pi = 0.

Conventions and kinetic stress definitions are checked against [Ma & Bertschinger, sections4–5](https://arxiv.org/html/astro-ph/9506072v1). The new work is their explicit evaluation on the retained MTS/native sources and the decomposition below, not a new discovery of these conservation laws.

The readout differentiates the stress definitions using the unchanged native perturbation RHS and the actual scalar-spline derivatives. It does not define P from C or E. Background time derivatives here use H d/d ln(a); the native thermal a(tau) interpolation is checked independently by directional differences.

Define the scalar-table defects

    Delta_phi = H d phi_tab/d ln(a) - v_tab,
    Delta_v   = H d v_tab/d ln(a) + 2H v_tab + mu^2 phi_tab.

Using the retained perturbation equation

    p' = -2H p - k z v - (k^2+mu^2)d,

direct differentiation gives exactly

    C_phi = Delta_v p + mu^2 Delta_phi d,
    E_phi = k Delta_v d.

These vanish for an on-shell background; no fitted closure is required. They also give immediately usable bounds

    |C_phi| <= |Delta_v| |p| + mu^2 |Delta_phi| |d|,
    |E_phi| <= k |Delta_v| |d|.

Two inherited approximations have different signatures:

    C_baryon = 3H P_baryon,
    C_nu = k z [R_nu,thermal - (4/3)sum_j W_j/u_j].

The first arises because native baryon continuity evolves rest-mass density while its Euler equation retains a thermal-pressure force. The second compares tabulated thermal enthalpy with the optimized five-node perturbation quadrature. Photon-baryon momentum transfer cancels in their sum. The neutrino monopole/dipole equations and scalar stress identities are checked algebraically, not guessed from residual shapes.

## 4. Exact constructive repair, not a pressure adjustment

The source of the heavy defect is the background representation. Changing P to hide it would alter the action. Instead use N=ln(a) and correct both field and velocity, with the Friedmann rate recalculated from their corrected energy.

For each retained scalar define old tables phi,v and corrections u,w:

    phi_new=phi+u,       v_new=v+w,
    r_phi = phi_N - v/H0,
    r_v   = v_N + 2v + mu^2 phi/H0,

    u_N = (v+w)/H1 - v/H0 - r_phi,
    w_N = -2w - mu^2[(phi+u)/H1 - phi/H0] - r_v.

The exact rescaled density change is

    Delta R_phi = v w + w^2/2 + mu^2(phi u+u^2/2).

On the retained expanding, flat branch,

    H1 = sqrt(H0^2 + sum_fields Delta R_phi/3) > 0.

Keep other background densities as the retained functions of a. Set u=w=0 at the same initial physical scale factor; there are no fitted correction parameters. Direct substitution proves that phi_new,N=v_new/H1 and v_new,N=-2v_new-mu^2 phi_new/H1. Each corrected canonical background therefore has R_phi,N+R_phi+3Pbar_phi=0.

Reconstruct the physical conformal clock with d tau_new/dN=1/H1 and the same initial clock anchor. Do not silently reuse the old a(tau) relation after changing the background. A local repair of only v, or merely overwriting analytic derivatives while retaining inconsistent values, is insufficient.
