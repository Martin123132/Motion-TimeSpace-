## Main result

thermal kinetic limit:

    g(theta)=1-(1+4pi^2/3)*theta+O(theta^2),
    1+4pi^2/3=14.159472534785811.

Recoil and hard-photon stimulation supply a suppression2pi^2; thermal motion
with stimulation supplies the enhancement2pi^2/3-1. Their difference gives
the coefficient. This reproduces standard source physics in our conventions;
it is not a new MTS interaction or a derivation of electron charge/mass.
