# Inner symbol check before implementing the correction

The source improvement does not by itself prove that an inner time step
using its adjoint is stable. Test this now on the same33 saved event sets.
For chemical-weighted log waves f=x^(-1+i k),the positive grid generator
has nonpositive real symbol. Compute the exact analytic symbols of
H_grid/c-D2_inner and H_grid/c-D2_inner-D3_inner from the derivative
formula,without introducing a spatial derivative stencil.

Use the same smooth band kh<=0.25 and full Nyquist band kh<=pi as130.
Gate the maximum positive real part at1e-9 relative to the physical
eigenvalue,for BOTH bands;retain any failed high-frequency gate rather
than introducing a fitted cutoff. Also record error against the physical
finite-event symbol. Passing sampled symbols is necessary evidence,not
a proof for all frequencies or for the bounded-domain implementation.
This is a numerical stability test of a derived correction,not a new
physical assumption or an observational preference.
