The all-native jump lift changes the largest actual moving-jump second residual only from 32.5743863 to 32.3884462, while the strong/weak slope-response gap remains about 0.839. This does not establish a new physical obstruction. First test whether cancellation in the finite-element force evaluation contributes.

On each physical cell, the scalar gradient action gives the field force

```
-integral beta*q_R*phi_R dR
= integral (beta*q_RR+beta_R*q_R)*phi dR - [beta*q_R*phi]_cell_ends
```

Summing cells produces the internal traction beta*[q_R] at each unpinned interface. The native force is the negative full native field covector. Combine these two terms in 75-digit Decimal arithmetic BEFORE converting to binary floating point. The separate source-chain contribution in the full covector is retained, not erased. The source has a pinned value and therefore no independent field-value test function. Outer flux signs are retained explicitly.

In the inherited source-affine reference coordinate xi, the bulk term is

```
(beta/J)*q_xixi + beta_R*q_xi
```

where J is constant within each physical mapping side. Evaluate q_xixi from the high-precision polynomial coefficients and beta_R from the actual radial metric derivatives. Assemble these bulk loads plus the already-balanced nodal tractions. Retain the SAME initial profile, radial solution, complete mass/Hessian, source inertia, native action and source force. No smoothing, altered coupling, or mode deletion is introduced.

Both primary and identical-data empty-native reference are tested at 12/48 and 16/64. Compare the resulting force vectors with the direct weak quadrature, solve the complete Euler system, and then compare the time response. Agreement of the large force norms alone does not establish accuracy of their small differences or of spatially differentiated acceleration. Conversely, this alternative is not assumed more accurate merely because a residual decreases: a discrepancy needs an independent check.
