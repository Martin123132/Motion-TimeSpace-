# Preserved failed normalization check

The first numerical run completed its independent circular roots, explicit
STF tensor flux controls, and phase quadratures, but failed the final
electric-parent regression before writing a successful status file.

The attempted comparison was between the new increment normalized by the
Newtonian chirp and the parent's increment normalized by the point-mass
chirp through first PN order. Those are different quantities.

At companion/body mass ratio0.01 and d0=10R, the former is
1.4131834443034423e-6, while the latter is1.417978999912741e-6.
The consistent latter coefficient is
[ct+(cxt-c1*ct)*x]*lambda/d0^5. An unexpanded numerical division also
retains higher PN terms, so it is not the correct strict-order regression.

The original script and three provisional CSV tables are preserved here.
The corrected runner adds clearly labelled columns and compares every
overlapping row against the source table. It changes no energy, flux,
chirp, or phase coefficients and does not relax the tolerance.
