## 2. Deriving the temperature tangent

At fixed physical frequency nu, d n_P/d ln T = -x d n_P/dx = x w.
Hence a small relative temperature change epsilon gives
delta n_T=epsilon x w and, in scaled variables, y_T=t eta with
epsilon=chi eta. There is no fitted temperature template.

Choose the reference temperature by matching photon ENERGY. Set

    C_E = e t = t^T M t
    eta = E/C_E
    y_T = t eta
    d = y-y_T.

Then E_T=E and e d=0, to the recorded floating-point/input consistency.
On exact consistent coefficient vectors:

    P_T = t e/C_E
    P_D = I-P_T
    P_T^2=P_T, P_D^2=P_D, P_T P_D=0
    P_T^T M=M P_T
    ||y||_M^2 = C_E eta^2 + ||d||_M^2.

P_D has rank95 but the PAIR (eta,d) retains every original degree of
freedom: y=t eta+d. No kinetic mode has been deleted or re-evolved.
This is also the least-squares temperature tangent in this inherited M
metric, not a fit with an instrument's noise weighting.

On the complete Planck domain C_E=4 by integration by parts.
The finite saved domain has its own C_E; do not replace it by 4 before
checking the small template-only boundary difference. Physical fractional
temperature shift is chi E/C_E; in kelvin multiply by T_star.

The tests use independent stored E, while retaining e y-E as a conditioning
diagnostic. Do not silently overwrite y or pretend their floating-point
difference is an exact mathematical zero.

## 3. What 'genuine distortion' means

A spectrum perturbation is pure temperature at this order iff it lies in
span(t), equivalently P_D y=0. Adding any t beta and shifting E by C_E beta
leaves d unchanged. Thus d represents a nonthermal SHAPE modulo temperature.

The split needs a convention. An alternative number-matched choice is

    eta_N = (nrow y)/(nrow t), d_N=y-t eta_N.

It assigns zero NUMBER to its residual rather than zero energy. It differs
from d only by a temperature mode; P_D d_N=d. The two temperatures need
not agree when emission changes number. We test this instead of calling
energy matching a unique instrument-measured temperature.

In the energy-matched convention the residual number is
nrow d=nrow y-(nrow t)eta. Signed zero-energy distortion can have nonzero
number and substantial norm: zero net energy does not mean zero spectrum,
nor does it mean each frequency has zero energy.

The temperature tangent is standard blackbody calculus, cross-checked
against Chluba et al., MNRAS466 (2017), section2 spectral templates:
https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stw3230 .
The separation/observability convention is also discussed by Chluba,
Cyr and Johnson, MNRAS535 (2024), section3:
https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stae2464 .
These references do not establish any new MTS result.

## 4. Occupation and radiance

On the represented frequency band:

    delta n_T = chi w x eta
    delta n_D = chi w sum_i b_i d_i
    delta n = delta n_T+delta n_D
    nu = x k_B T_star/h
    delta I_nu = (2 h nu^3/c^2) delta n.

I_nu is specific intensity in W m^-2 Hz^-1 sr^-1, not energy density,
bolometric flux, or today's observed sky. For isotropic photons,
rho=(4pi/c) integral I_nu dnu, consistent with chi E.
h, k_B and c are read from the retained native constants file, and T0
and chi from the same saved background lineage. None is newly fitted
or claimed derived. These are first-order incremental spectra; do not
add tiny delta n to n_P in float64 and then subtract to 'measure' it.

## 5. Sensitivity propagation, not a new error theorem

For each saved component delta y_j, delta E_j use the SAME map:

    delta eta_j=delta E_j/C_E
    delta d_j=delta y_j-t delta eta_j.

The conservative finite-rule comparisons are sum_j ||delta d_j||_M
and sum_j |delta eta_j|, with the unchanged central projected denominator.
Also report component-coherent sums as diagnostics; never credit cancellation
as a smaller uncertainty. Map the saved timestep, incoming-terminal and
restart alternatives the same way. These remain measured sensitivities,
not full quadrature, infrared, continuum or physical-model error bounds.

All results describe ONLY the already tested source window at the final
saved epoch. Outside-window source, later-time evolution, the driven
infrared tail of this new increment and actual instrument response are
