# Spatial jump mechanism of the projected kinetic residual

This is a finite-space derivation using the inherited annular action, not a new physical term. Inputs are the completed common-physical-jet probes, the live support projection, and the immutable C1 stage authority. The geometric normalization is fixed at the base state; it is not differentiated along an evolution.

## 1. The exact obstruction

At a fixed material label, write the physical rate residual as e=d-w*g, where d is the reacted P2 field variation, w=eta+D*v_source is the P1 map variation, and g is the physical P1 field gradient on each cell. Thus e is broken P2. At an internal face, with [f]=f_right-f_left,

    [e] = [d] - w_right*[g] - [w]*g_left.

For continuous d and w this reduces to [e]=-w*[g]. A continuous P2 field correction cannot remove this jump. The rounded-coefficient interpretation can carry small [d] defects; the implementation retains these instead of silently imposing continuity. The parent-nodal interpretation is checked independently at high precision.

## 2. Minimum kinetic lifting, not an arbitrary smoothing

For each cell let a=(e_left,e_middle,e_right) and let phi denote its P2 nodal basis. With the inherited positive kinetic weight alpha, define

    M_cell = integral_cell alpha * phi * phi^T dr.

Let M be the block diagonal matrix of these 3x3 masses. B extracts the two outer boundary values and all internal jumps. The boundary rows preserve actual inherited boundary defects; they are not discarded. Set b=B*a. Among all broken P2 functions with the same jumps and boundary traces, minimize one half j^T*M*j. The Lagrange equations give

    S = B*M^(-1)*B^T,
    S*lambda = b,
    j = M^(-1)*B^T*lambda,
    r = a-j.

Every local mass is positive definite, and B has full row rank; therefore S is positive definite. S is tridiagonal because adjacent jump rows share only one cell. The exact identities are

    B*r=0,
    j^T*M*r=0,
    a^T*M*a = b^T*S^(-1)*b + r^T*M*r.

The first term is the minimum kinetic norm forced by the actual spatial jumps and boundary traces. This is an exact variational statement for the discrete positive mass. Numerical quadrature and linear-solve errors remain separately checked. It is not a continuum lower bound unless the mass integrals themselves are certified.

## 3. What this calculation holds fixed

The fine and third fields use their respective existing reacted directions, not independently refitted amplitudes. Both are measured with the same third-grid metric and physical source map used in the common-jet comparison. The source-clock term is retained separately. The projection here allows an independent continuous spatial field correction at each label, holding the source response fixed. Consequently the continuous remainder can include finite material representation, source coupling, live-metric response, and (for the fine case) the difference from its own metric. A large jump fraction identifies a spatial discretization mechanism; it does not by itself prove that every other contribution vanishes or that the native potential is a gauge symmetry.
