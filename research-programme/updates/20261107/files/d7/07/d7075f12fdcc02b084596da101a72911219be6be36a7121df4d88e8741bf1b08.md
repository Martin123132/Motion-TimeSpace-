# Ward adapted remainder and a continuum interaction bound

3 October 2026. Private daughter of the tensor Ward stage. The original framework and its rollback are unchanged.

The leading cancellation is now connected to an exact transformed kernel for arbitrary pair responses. That calculation exposes a limitation of subtracting only the first logarithm: the bare Cartesian ladder can regenerate a phase-channel logarithm. Rather than close the remainder by assumption, we derive the full leading-derivative phase interaction from the same candidate action and prove a continuum bound on its static tree kernel. At the selected weak point this bound is small throughout a stated low-energy domain.

This is a result about a specified effective interaction, not a finite-grid extrapolation or an exclusion of every microscopic/dynamical collective state.

## Inherited model and notation

Keep the candidate and unfitted point from the three preceding stages:
\[
 {\cal C}=\varrho e^{i\theta}/\sqrt2,\quad
 {\cal L}=-\tfrac12(\partial\varrho)^2+\varrho^2X-V(\varrho),
 \quad X=-\tfrac12(\partial\theta)^2,
\]
\[
 V=\tfrac34g\varrho^{4/3}+\tfrac14\lambda\varrho^4,\quad
 \theta=\mu t,\quad \varrho=v,\quad
 \mu=1,\ v=10,\ y=2,\ \epsilon=\mu^2/v^2=.01.
\]
Write M=m^2=10/9 for the radial stiffness, D=M+4mu^2=46/9, c_s^2=M/D=5/23, gamma=C122=M/v=1/9. M has mass dimension2; thus M^2 below means (m^2)^2, not a new mass. The explicit complex field/U(1), rotating state and quartic stabilization remain hypotheses of this isolated extension.

External relative Euclidean momentum is P=(xi,p). The pair's total momentum is zero. A constant traceless spatial source defines p_h^2=h_ij p_i p_j. Define the acoustic norm P_s^2=p^2+xi^2/c_s^2.

## Exact basis change without throwing away pair components

For t=s+r(s), s=xi^2+p^2, and b=2mu xi, set
\[
 a=t+M,\quad S=t+b^2/a,\quad
 L=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\b/a&1\end{pmatrix},\quad
 U=\begin{pmatrix}1&-b/a\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\quad
 {\cal D}=\operatorname{diag}(a,S).
\]
Then, exactly,
\[
 G^{-1}=L{\cal D}U,\qquad G=U^{-1}{\cal D}^{-1}L^{-1}.
\]
For an arbitrary two-by-two inverse-propagator response F, introduce
\[
 H=L^{-1}FU^{-1},\qquad
 {\cal X}={\cal D}^{-1}H{\cal D}^{-1}=UGFGL.
\]
Consequently delta G=-U^{-1}Xcal L^{-1}. This is an invertible change of variables at nonzero Euclidean momentum. It keeps all four matrix components, including the antisymmetric mixed component. It is not a projection onto the previous sourced solution.

Let (C^d)_ac=C_acd. The original collapsed cubic-ladder map is
\[
 ({\cal K}F)(P)=\int_K\sum_{d,f}C^dG(K)F(K)G(K)(C^f)^T G_{df}(P-K).
\]
It becomes the explicit H-kernel
\[
 (\widetilde{\cal K}H)(P)=\int_K\sum_{d,f}
 [L_P^{-1}C^dU_K^{-1}{\cal D}_K^{-1}]
 H(K)
 [{\cal D}_K^{-1}L_K^{-1}(C^f)^TU_P^{-1}]G_{df}(P-K).
\]
For the propagated variable, instead use
\[
 ({\cal K}_{\cal X}{\cal X})(P)
 ={\cal D}_P^{-1}\int_K\sum_{d,f}
 [L_P^{-1}C^dU_K^{-1}]\,{\cal X}(K)\,
 [L_K^{-1}(C^f)^TU_P^{-1}]G_{df}(P-K){\cal D}_P^{-1}.
\]
The source transforms by the same rule. Angular/frequency integration and any regulator-domain terms remain those of the preceding stage; this formula does not silently drop them. At finite discretization an invertible version of this change is a similarity transformation, so it cannot manufacture or remove eigenvalues.

The kernel equality was checked for arbitrary random matrix responses at48 momentum fixtures, with and without the temporary regulator, including both mixed components. These tests are independent of solving for one special Ward source.

## Strengthening the leading cancellation using the full propagator

The previous leading nonanalytic vertex is
\[
 F_{\rm IR}(P)=\frac{A_{\rm IR}p_h^2}{P_s^2}M_0(\xi),\quad
 A_{\rm IR}=\frac{\gamma^2c_s}{32\pi^2},\quad
 M_0=\begin{pmatrix}1&-b/M\\b/M&-b^2/M^2\end{pmatrix}.
\]
This M0 is a matrix, whereas M is the scalar stiffness. In the exact Schur basis,
\[
 L^{-1}M_0U^{-1}=
 \begin{pmatrix}1&-\delta\\\delta&-\delta^2\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad \delta=\frac{bt}{M(t+M)}.
\]
At simultaneous low momentum delta is of order |P_s|^3. Dividing by the Schur phase denominators S therefore leaves finite radial, vanishing mixed and vanishing phase contributions from this particular insertion.

One can also prove boundedness directly, without replacing the external propagators by their soft expansion. With det=t(t+M)+b^2,
\[
 G(1,b/M)^T=(f,g)^T,\quad
 f=\frac{1-t^2/{\rm det}}M,\qquad
 g=\frac{bt}{M\,{\rm det}}.
\]
For t>=0, M>0,
\[
 0\le f\le1/M,\qquad |g|\le1/(2M),
\]
because det-t^2=Mt+b^2>=0 and
\[
 {\rm det}-2|bt|=(t-|b|)^2+Mt\ge0.
\]
The row factor gives (f,-g), so every entry of GM0G is bounded in magnitude by1/M^2. Together with |p_h^2|<=P_s^2, this bounds every entry of the inserted contribution by A_IR/M^2. The algebra is global for this specified insertion, but the approximation identifying it as the leading physical vertex is still a low-energy statement.

## Why this is not yet a closed remainder

Act once more with the bare Cartesian kernel on F_IR. To leading soft order,
\[
 GF_{\rm IR}G=\frac{A_{\rm IR}}{M^2}\frac{k_h^2}{K_s^2}E_{11}.
\]
Its output in the phase-phase channel contains
\[
 ({\cal K}F_{\rm IR})_{22}
 =\frac{\gamma^2A_{\rm IR}}{M^2}
 \int\frac{d\zeta\,d^3k}{(2\pi)^4}
 \frac{k_h^2}{K_s^2(P-K)_s^2}+\cdots .
\]
Rescale the temporal variable by c_s. Feynman parametrization and the tracelessness of h give
\[
 \left.\int_K\frac{k_h^2}{K_s^2(P-K)_s^2}\right|_{\rm nonanalytic}
 =\frac{c_s}{48\pi^2}p_h^2\log\frac{\kappa^2}{P_s^2}.
\]
The factor1/3 is the exact integral of x^2 over[0,1]; rotational loop-tensor terms vanish against traceless h. Hence
\[
 ({\cal K}F_{\rm IR})_{22}
 \supset \boxed{\frac{2A_{\rm IR}^2}{3M^2}
 p_h^2\log\frac{\kappa^2}{P_s^2}}.
\]
The coefficient is1.7937687055985276e-10 at the chosen point. The leading radial cancellation is therefore not, by itself, an invariant one-component subspace of the ladder.

This is a definite term in an isolated bare-ladder iteration, not a complete two-loop prediction. Analytic phase stiffness terms, other nonanalytic pieces, source vertices, background shifts and matching can compete. We do not infer a physical instability or tensor pole from this logarithm. It explains why a claim of uniform control based only on subtracting M0 would have been premature.

## Deriving the full leading phase interaction

A symmetry-consistent way to organize the low-energy remainder is to eliminate the radial amplitude at tree level before splitting diagrams. On the stable branch,
\[
 V'(v(X))=2Xv(X),\qquad P(X)=v(X)^2X-V(v(X)),\qquad
 \frac{dv}{dX}=\frac{2v}{M}.
\]
The earlier stage established P_X and P_XX. Here the next derivatives are derived, not fitted:
\[
 P_1=v^2,\quad P_2=\frac{4v^2}{M},
\]
\[
 P_3=\frac{24v^2}{M^2}-\frac{8v^3C_{111}}{M^3},
\]
\[
 P_4=\frac{192v^2}{M^3}-\frac{192v^3C_{111}}{M^4}
      +\frac{48v^4C_{111}^2}{M^5}-\frac{16v^4D_{1111}}{M^4}.
\]
At the chosen point:
\[
 (P_1,P_2,P_3,P_4)=(100,360,-1728/5,487296/125).
\]
Their mass dimensions are2,0,-2,-4 respectively.

Put theta=mu t+pi and chi=sqrt(Z_t) pi, where Z_t=P_1+mu^2P_2=460. Expanding the same P(X) gives
\[
 {\cal L}_2=\tfrac12\dot\chi^2-\tfrac12c_s^2(\nabla\chi)^2,
\]
\[
 {\cal L}_3=a_3\dot\chi^3+b_3\dot\chi(\nabla\chi)^2,\qquad
 {\cal L}_4=a_4\dot\chi^4+b_4\dot\chi^2(\nabla\chi)^2+c_4[(\nabla\chi)^2]^2,
\]
where
\[
 a_3=\frac{\mu P_2/2+\mu^3P_3/6}{Z_t^{3/2}},\quad
 b_3=-\frac{\mu P_2}{2Z_t^{3/2}},
\]
\[
 a_4=\frac{P_2/8+\mu^2P_3/4+\mu^4P_4/24}{Z_t^2},\quad
 b_4=-\frac{P_2+\mu^2P_3}{4Z_t^2},\quad
 c_4=\frac{P_2}{8Z_t^2}.
\]
Numerically,
\[
 (a_3,b_3)=(0.01240636832,-0.01824465929),
\]
\[
 (a_4,b_4,c_4)=(0.0005719848771,-0.00001701323251,0.0002126654064).
\]
The cubic coefficients have dimension-2 and the quartics dimension-4. Every leg of every vertex carries a derivative. Radial exchange already integrated into these coefficients must not be added a second time.

This is the leading-derivative, tree-matched phase theory. It is not a claim that loop-renormalized P(X) is already known. Using pressure to organize a superfluid Goldstone EFT follows the established framework of [Son, 2002](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0204199); the displayed coefficients are derived for this candidate, not imported from that paper.

## Continuum static tree kernel bound

Here is an actual operator bound, with its space and truncation specified. For Euclidean momentum Q define
\[
 |Q|_s^2=\omega^2/c_s^2+\mathbf q^2,\qquad
 G_\chi(Q)=\frac{1}{c_s^2|Q|_s^2}.
\]
The symmetrized cubic and quartic Euclidean vertices obey triangle-inequality bounds
\[
 |V_3(Q_1,Q_2,Q_3)|\le C_3\prod_i|Q_i|_s,\quad
 C_3=6(|a_3|c_s^3+|b_3|c_s),
\]
\[
 |V_4(Q_1,\ldots,Q_4)|\le C_4\prod_i|Q_i|_s,\quad
 C_4=24(|a_4|c_s^4+|b_4|c_s^2+|c_4|).
\]
The factors6 and24 are independently checked by differentiating the cubic and quartic polynomials with respect to labeled fields. Each exchange diagram has two internal derivative factors, which cancel its massless propagator in this absolute bound:
\[
 |V_3(\ldots,Q)G_\chi(Q)V_3(-Q,\ldots)|
 \le(C_3^2/c_s^2)\prod_{\rm external}|Q_i|_s.
\]
Keeping up to all three channels gives the conservative coefficient
\[
 C_{\rm tree}=C_4+3C_3^2/c_s^2.
\]
At an exactly zero exchanged momentum the phase zero mode needs a prescription. The bound covers any bounded directional continuation of the derivative exchange; no physical value is inferred from0/0. In the static tensor channel no stronger claim about that isolated zero mode is needed.

For the zero-total-momentum pair kernel on the acoustic ball |K|_s<=kappa,
\[
 ({\cal B}F)(P)=-\tfrac12\int_{|K|_s\le\kappa}
 \frac{d\omega\,d^3k}{(2\pi)^4}
 V_{\rm tree}(P,-P,K,-K)G_\chi(K)^2F(K).
\]
The factor1/2 is the identical-pair Bose factor; the absolute bound is independent of vertex sign. Use the norm
\[
 \|F\|_2=\sup_{0<|P|_s\le\kappa}\frac{|F(P)|}{|P|_s^2}.
\]
Then
\[
 \frac{|{\cal B}F(P)|}{|P|_s^2}
 \le\frac{C_{\rm tree}}{2c_s^4}\|F\|_2
 \int_{|K|_s\le\kappa}\frac{d\omega\,d^3k}{(2\pi)^4},
\]
and the exact acoustic-ball measure is c_s kappa^4/(32 pi^2). Therefore
\[
 \boxed{\|{\cal B}\|_2\le
 \frac{C_{\rm tree}\kappa^4}{64\pi^2c_s^3}.}
\]
There is no infrared cutoff in this inequality. It bounds the full angular kernel, and hence its rotationally invariant l=2 sector, without relying on a quadrature grid or cancellation between angular lobes.

For completeness, this does not evade possible eigenfunctions merely by choosing a smooth source. If an eigenfunction has nonzero eigenvalue and satisfies the minimal absolute-integrability condition
\[
 \int_{|K|_s\le\kappa} d^4K\,|F(K)|/|K|_s^2<\infty,
\]
the vertex bound itself implies B F is O(P_s^2); the eigenfunction is therefore in the weighted space. Responses requiring divergent, subtraction-dependent integrals are outside this operator definition and would require a separate renormalized prescription.

 C_{\rm tree}=0.05320538822,
\]
\[

\]

## Stress sources derived with the interactions

For a single traceless shear insertion, the same P(X) gives
\[
 T_h=P_X(X)\,h_{ij}\partial_i\pi\partial_j\pi.
\]
In canonical variables the quadratic, cubic and quartic pieces are
\[
 T_h^{(2)}=c_s^2 h_{ij}\partial_i\chi\partial_j\chi,
\]
\[
 T_h^{(3)}=\frac{\mu P_2}{Z_t^{3/2}}\dot\chi\,
 h_{ij}\partial_i\chi\partial_j\chi,
\]
\[
 T_h^{(4)}=
 \left[\frac{P_2+\mu^2P_3}{2Z_t^2}\dot\chi^2
       -\frac{P_2}{2Z_t^2}(\nabla\chi)^2\right]
 h_{ij}\partial_i\chi\partial_j\chi.
\]
A second source derivative also has contact terms. Along the explicitly specified inverse-spatial-metric path gamma^{ij}=delta^{ij}+eta h^{ij},
\[
 \left.\frac{d^2}{d\eta^2}\left[\sqrt\gamma\,P(X_\eta)\right]\right|_0
 =\frac{P_{XX}}4(h_{ij}\partial_i\pi\partial_j\pi)^2
 +\frac P2\operatorname{tr}(h^2).
\]
The second term is the measure variation. Leaving it out would spoil a two-source response even though the first volume variation vanishes for traceless h. These are coefficients of an external metric probe; an independent dynamical spacetime metric is not introduced here.

## The first dispersive correction is also fixed

Retain the amplitude kinetic term in its derivative expansion. Evaluating it on the leading stationary amplitude gives
\[
 {\cal L}_{\rm NLO}
 =-\tfrac12[v_X(X)]^2(\partial X)^2
 =-\frac{2v(X)^2}{M(X)^2}(\partial X)^2.
\]
The correction to the stationary amplitude contributes at higher derivative order because the leading algebraic action is already stationary. Around the homogeneous background,
\[
 {\cal L}_{2,\rm NLO}=\beta[
 (\ddot\chi)^2-(\nabla\dot\chi)^2],\qquad
 \beta=\frac{2\mu^2}{MD}.
\]
Solving the dispersion relation perturbatively, not treating the truncated higher-time-derivative equation as a new fundamental theory, gives
\[
 \omega^2=c_s^2k^2+2\beta c_s^2(1-c_s^2)k^4+\cdots
 =c_s^2k^2+\frac{16\mu^4}{D^3}k^4+\cdots.
\]
This exactly matches the expansion of the original two-mode spectrum. The apparent extra root of the truncated fourth-time-derivative equation is not a new physical mode. Here beta=0.3521739130 and the k^4 coefficient is0.1198323334 in the chosen units.

The continuum bound above does not include this NLO correction or its source variations. We have instead derived its coefficient and an independent matching check, giving the next extension a controlled starting point.

## Decision and next target

The simplest weak, deep-infrared phonon interaction has now been tested by an analytic continuum bound, not only small matrices. Continuing to hunt an eigenvalue-one tensor mode by refining the same weak low-energy grids is not justified.

Use the derived phase action, stress vertices and NLO term to derive the covariant stress/current identities through the same derivative order, with the background metric treated explicitly as an external probe. Then match that result to the retained parent geometry/coupling sector rather than quietly demanding that two scalar constituents already supply independent gravitational dynamics. The useful question becomes which coupling and stress contributions this condensate candidate actually derives and how they can enter the existing framework.

An immediate bounded calculation is the variation of P(X)-2v(X)^2/M(X)^2 (partial X)^2 with respect to the phase and metric, plus a check of its on-shell conservation identity and the correct order-reduced degrees of freedom. Do not assume universal matter coupling, Newton's constant or an independent tensor kinetic term from the acoustic metric.
