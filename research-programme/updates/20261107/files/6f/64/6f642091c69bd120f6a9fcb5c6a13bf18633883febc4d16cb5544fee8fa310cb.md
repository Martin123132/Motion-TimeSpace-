# Condensate tensor-response step: results

3 October 2026. Private; original framework preserved.

## What moved forward

We now have an explicit calculation that retains both the amplitude and
phase waves, rather than only the low-energy sound approximation. It
reproduces that earlier approximation and an independent free-field baseline.

The candidate action also gives a nonzero quadrupolar interaction. Its
formula and strength are derived, not fitted to manufacture gravity.
For freely propagating soft waves it falls as momentum to the fourth
power; the exchanged radial disturbance remains short-range.

Crucially, internal virtual states retain an additional phase-exchange
contribution. We derived the full matrix angular kernel, including its
logarithmic static limit, instead of assuming that the cancellation for
free external waves also removes it inside a collective calculation.
