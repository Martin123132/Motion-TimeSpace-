# One-sided pushforward traces in the Eulerian tangent

The independent material-space weak test exposed a second, different discretization issue before coupled-current use. In reference fine, the untraced Eulerian collocation density derivative integrates to 5.6865e-7 instead of zero total source-number rate; its first radial moment differs from the exact 0.03 transport rate by 3.3892e-6. Wave moments agree at about 1e-17 or better. This is not a new source term or a physical loss of particles.

Although rho=w(lambda)/b_lambda is continuous and zero at either support endpoint, rho_t has distinct interior/exterior traces because w'(+-1/2) is nonzero. The same applies to each native Gram pushforward. A Lobatto endpoint shared by two radial cells must use the trace from THAT cell's interior. A single inclusive support mask gives the interior derivative to the adjacent exterior cell and inserts spurious quadrature weight. Smaller time differences do not fix this representation error.

For fixed physical R, lambda_t=-D delta b/L. On an interior trace,

    H_t=(w U_t+w' lambda_t U)/(J L)-H(J_t/J+L_t/L),
    rho_t=w' lambda_t/b_lambda-rho*(delta b_lambda+b_lambdalambda lambda_t)/b_lambda.

Exterior traces are zero for that atom/source. Select lower-end interior only for the cell to its right, upper-end interior only for the cell to its left. Use the archived edge-merging tolerance solely to identify which analytic support endpoint the represented boundary denotes; do not shift the physical support or smooth the jump. Interior radial nodes retain the previously complex-verified analytic derivative. Fixed nodal source velocity must also take the appropriate trace when it enters the coefficient multiplying rho_t.

The correction reuses the immutable full-grid derivative arrays from attempt02, replaces only source/Gram endpoint traces (plus the corresponding source velocity values/rates), and resolves the linear radial equations. Require independent material-space weak moments of E,G,H,rho, powers 0,1,2, to agree before solving the coupled old-Euler system. Preserve and report the uncorrected weak discrepancy as a negative control. No original equation, physical parameter, baseline subtraction, or acceptance tolerance is altered.
