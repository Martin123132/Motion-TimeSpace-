# Frozen driven-tail numerical method

No new physical temperature ansatz is fitted. The saved discrete80-digit
photon/gas solution is reconstructed piecewise linearly. Projection onto gas,
the original hard-photon soft-slope functional and upper-boundary rows is
linear, so projecting before temporal interpolation is exactly equivalent.
Original baseline states remain the saved piecewise-linear history. At the
two clipped endpoints its original binary64 interpolator is evaluated once
and lifted; this preserves the authenticated common seed. Rounding of those
endpoint values is not a new physical effect.

The tail step grid contains the union of both repaired histories, original
history/tail times and coefficient knots. Every interval is subdivided1,2,4.
No kink is crossed without a step boundary. Duplicate representable floating
times are removed, not distinct history knots. Interior dense output is an
explicit numerical approximation, tested by the inherited half/quarter
comparison; it is not called an exact solution of the photon/gas ODE.

At80 digits form the small projected drive difference first. Form all three
affine forcings and retain high/low files. The sparse tail solve uses
binary64; independent80/100-digit forcing checks, discrete residuals and
full-state versus direct-difference solves test that arithmetic. This is
not an80-digit tail integration. The original binary64 coefficient bank
and its linear interpolator are unchanged, including the original
binary64 dc/gamma assembly. All bands, boundary rules and joins are retained.

Both arms start from the same original tail at the authenticated start.
The full repaired solution is checked against baseline plus separately
evolved difference. A positive driven comparison solution bounds the
magnitude of that difference for this executed discretization.

Since rate R and each gamma-eigenvalue-sum(coefficients) are piecewise
linear on coefficient knots and positive, min(R)*min(gamma-eigenvalue-
sum(coefficients)) bounds damping from below throughout the interval.
Nonnegative off-diagonal coefficients at the endpoints imply positivity
throughout. Truncating the positive exterior couplings only increases
the internal row damping. This proves the comparison structure for this
retained coefficient interpolant, not uncertainty in microscopic rates.

For a coarse trajectory interpolated onto the fine times, define
d_n = c_n-c_(n-1)-h_n(A_n c_n+b_n).
Then the fine-minus-coarse discrepancy obeys
(I-h_n A_n)e_n=e_(n-1)-d_n.
The same positive inverse propagates abs(d_n) into a componentwise
discrete refinement envelope. It includes the actual variation of drive
and operator on the compared grids; it is not a continuum error bound.
Source approximation, exterior prescription and frequency discretization
remain inherited uncertainties, not removed by refining time.

Preserve the original absolute core-slope refinement gate1e-4 on
1e-5<=x<=1e-4. Preserve moment gates
max(.01*max_abs(moment),1e-5*amplitude_envelope), with the original
number/energy integration weights. Also check direct differences under
the declared1% history criterion. Report failure rather than widening
gates. Physical sources and final observables require the full retained
tail shape; none is obtained by projecting it into the old two amplitudes.
