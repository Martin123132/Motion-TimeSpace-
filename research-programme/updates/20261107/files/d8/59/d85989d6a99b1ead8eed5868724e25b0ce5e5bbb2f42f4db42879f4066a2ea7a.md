# Exact source of the gas-interpolation discrepancy

The frozen linear-tail experiment and all16 failed refinement gates remain
unchanged. This note records a diagnostic derivation, not a replacement fit.

## Exact drive partition

Let H and Q denote the saved half/quarter histories, and I_H the piecewise
linear interpolator on the half-history knots. For the gas drive,

    I_H(r_H) - I_Q(r_Q)
      = I_H(r_H - I_Q(r_Q)|H) + [I_H(I_Q(r_Q)|H) - I_Q(r_Q)].

The first term is the discrepancy between saved solutions at common nodes.
The second is the curvature lost by linear interpolation. This equality
holds without assuming that either discrete history is the continuum answer.

The retained tail equation is affine in gas, the hard-photon soft functional,
and the upper-boundary profile. Its same-grid difference equation therefore
propagates each contribution separately, with zero contribution initially.
attribute243.py checks the three-driver partition; corner243.py checks the
two gas partitions after propagation. No cancellation term is discarded.

## Relaxation law from the retained photon/gas equations

Write D(u)=M^-1 K(u), heat parameter chi>0, energy row e, and temperature
template t. The existing equations are

    p_u = D(u)(p - t r),
    chi r_u = -e p_u - 1 - chi r.

Substituting the first equation, without modifying either equation, gives

    r_u + Gamma(u) r = F(u,p),
    Gamma(u) = 1 - e D(u)t / chi,
    F(u,p) = -(e D(u)p + 1)/chi.

For Gamma>0 define r_star=F/Gamma. Then, exactly,

    r_u = -Gamma(r-r_star),
    (r-r_star)_u = -Gamma(r-r_star) - (r_star)_u.

For a specified photon history the integrating-factor formula is

    r(u) = E(u,u0) r(u0) + integral[u0,u] E(u,v) F(v,p(v)) dv,
    E(u,v) = exp(-integral[v,u] Gamma(w) dw).

This exposes the inherited response time1/Gamma in logarithmic scale factor.
No cutoff, fitted relaxation time or new material coupling is inserted.
The photon history is still coupled to r; the formula does not authorize
holding photons fixed when their change matters.

## Local diagnostic, not an adopted reconstruction

relaxation243.py evaluates Gamma and r_star at the first half-step endpoints
and midpoint using the actual saved operator and80-digit states;100-digit
calculations check the midpoint. It compares the saved quarter-step gas with
a coarse-photon driven equilibrium plus a frozen-start memory term and the
first slow-lag correction -(r_star)_u/Gamma. The slope of r_star is estimated
from the two endpoints for this diagnostic only.

The exact integrating-factor law is distinct from those approximations.
The frozen-start memory term is not an interval-certified bound; replacing
the old history requires checking varying coefficients, photon interpolation,
initial memory and coupled photon/gas energy residuals. This is a known-data
numerical audit motivated by the observed failure, not a fresh held-out test.

A naive higher polynomial can spread a genuine stiff transient across the
whole coarse interval. Evaluate the physical response time before selecting
polynomial dense output or taking another arbitrarily small time step.
