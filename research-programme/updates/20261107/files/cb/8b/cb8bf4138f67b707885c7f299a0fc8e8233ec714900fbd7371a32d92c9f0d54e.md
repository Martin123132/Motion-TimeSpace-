# Separate a resolved collision change from a shape introduced at the join

For each window the stored-moment construction gives
    response_W = V z_W + U_W, U_W=W h-V M^-1 <V,W h>.
Since the projection uses exactly the parent mass inner product,
    <V_i,U_W>=0 for every retained basis function.

Therefore two windows satisfy the stronger,exact vector statement
    Delta response=V Delta z+Delta U,
    ||Delta response||^2=||V Delta z||^2+||Delta U||^2.

This distinguishes two possible sources of window sensitivity without
assigning huge cancelling bulk/join terms a misleading causal percentage.
The fractions of the SQUARED norm attributable to the resolved and
unresolved subspaces are nonnegative and sum to1. They are not energy
fractions,observational preferences,or fractions of a signed amplitude.

The identity uses the unfiltered full-spectrum norm. Removing temperature,
mu and y shapes is another linear map,and need not preserve this exact
orthogonality. The main comparison records that residual-shape attribution
separately;do not transfer the Pythagorean percentages to it without a check.

If Delta U dominates,the next repair target is a common inner/outer
overlap that determines the missing shape,not removal of the necessary
joining collision term. This is a measured distinction,not assumed here.
