sequence and tests an explicitly stated cosmological state against the
existing supernova/BAO likelihood. The previous framework is a comparator,

## Answer first

The tested homogeneous charge-tracking condensate does not improve this
late-time data fit. When Lambda is retained and a share of pressureless
density is replaced by the homogeneous condensate, the best fit drives that
share to zero. Small shares are not sharply excluded by this screening;
larger shares progressively worsen the fit.

Without Lambda, this particular state fits very poorly. The earlier parent
scalar, including its no-Lambda branch, still gives its previously competitive
fit. Thus the new state should NOT displace the older cosmological route.

The dilute finite-body/coexistence limit is different: its leading density
scales as a^-3. Background distances alone cannot identify that component
separately from other pressureless matter. This degeneracy is neither a
detection nor an exclusion of condensate bodies.

## 2. From the action to a specific observable history

Keep the candidate action, with no new potential terms,
```text
L_C = -(partial varrho)^2/2 - varrho^2(partial theta)^2/2 - V(varrho)
V = 3g varrho^(4/3)/4 + lambda varrho^4/4, g,lambda>0.
```
In homogeneous FLRW the exact equations are
```text
J = a^3 varrho^2 theta_dot = constant
varrho_ddot + 3H varrho_dot - J^2/(a^6 varrho^3) + V_varrho = 0
rho_C = varrho_dot^2/2 + J^2/(2a^6 varrho^2) + V
p_C   = varrho_dot^2/2 + J^2/(2a^6 varrho^2) - V.
```

For the slowly tracking rotating state, amplitude acceleration and amplitude
kinetic energy are small. Radial force balance gives
theta_dot^2=V_varrho/varrho. Define y=lambda varrho^(8/3)/g. Then
```text
rho_C = A sqrt(y)(5+3y)
p_C   = A sqrt(y)(y-1)
A = g^(3/2)/(4 sqrt(lambda))
w_C = (y-1)/(5+3y)
c_s^2 = (3y-1)/(5+9y)

a^6 y^(5/4)(1+y) = y0^(5/4)(1+y0)
R_C(z) = rho_C(z)/rho_C(0)
       = sqrt(y(z)/y0) [5+3y(z)]/[5+3y0].
```
The existing preferred homogeneous branch has y0>=1. Looking backward across
the data's redshift range increases y, so it stays on that branch. An additional
no-Lambda check admits the locally stable but metastable range 1/3<y0<1.

The charge law implies the continuity equation exactly at this retained
adiabatic order. The numerical algebraic root is independently checked against
integration of dy/dln(1+z)=24y(1+y)/(5+9y).

There is already a useful physical constraint:
```text
rho_C+3p_C = 2A sqrt(y)(1+3y) > 0.
```
Thus this circular/adiabatic state, with nonnegative dust and radiation and no
Lambda, cannot cause acceleration. This does not prohibit potential-dominated,
nontracking histories of the full two-field action; they are different states.

## 3. Density bookkeeping: what replaces what?

For the primary Lambda-retaining test:
```text
Omega_C,0 = f_C Omega_mix,0
Omega_pressureless,0 = (1-f_C) Omega_mix,0
Omega_Lambda = 1-Omega_mix,0-Omega_r

E(z)^2 = Omega_pressureless,0 (1+z)^3
       + Omega_C,0 R_C(z)
       + Omega_r (1+z)^4 + Omega_Lambda.
```
The code calls the present combined budget Omega_m for compatibility with the
historical interface. It is not all cold matter once f_C is nonzero.
Fraction f_C replaces a share of that budget; it is not added on top of it.

This background-only test does not assign the replaced share to baryons or
claim that the finite objects are observed particles. The old parent-scalar
models are scored as alternatives, not added to this density budget.

For no Lambda, Omega_mix,0=1-Omega_r. For the dilute-body dust limit,
R_C=(1+z)^3 and f_C cancels exactly: the background is the same as LCDM for
the same combined density. Formation, abundance and perturbations are not
determined by this cancellation.

These are likelihood profile values, not confidence limits. A few-percent

majority contribution has much greater tension in this state family.

The physical reason is visible in the derived equation of state: even if
w_C=0 today at y0=1, y increases into the past and w_C tends toward 1/3.
The density then grows faster than dust. It cannot be treated as pressureless
at all measured redshifts merely because its present pressure vanishes.

## 7. Is the adiabatic approximation manufacturing this conclusion?

We also integrate the full homogeneous amplitude/charge/Friedmann equations
for the nonzero 10% profile. No extra shape coefficient is fitted. Initial
amplitude and its drift are set on the tracking branch at the largest observed
redshift; the physical frequency scale is normalized to E(0)=1.

| alpha=4piG v_star^2 | Max fractional E difference | Full minus adiabatic chi2 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Prior isolated-body example: 0.000196620 | 1.35e-4 | +0.07290 |
| Fixed control: 0.00001 | 6.57e-6 | +0.00355 |
| Fixed control: 0.000001 | 6.43e-7 | +0.00036 |

Tightening the full solver changes chi2 by about 8e-12. This demonstrates
controlled approach to the stated reduction for one nonzero profile/history;
it is not a fit of every possible state of the full action.

The scale alpha is not measured by the leading adiabatic likelihood.
The smaller values are approximation checks, not values chosen to rescue a
bad fit. A general full-action fit with free alpha/initial data would need to
count that additional freedom and is not performed here.

Eightfold distance-grid refinement shifts the fixed-fraction relative scores
by at most 0.00156 in chi2. It does not change the conclusion. The no-Lambda
absolute score changes by about0.09, negligible beside its large mismatch.

## 8. Decision and next physics question

Do not install the homogeneous rotating condensate as a replacement for the
older cosmological acceleration mechanism. Preserve the earlier parent-scalar
route and its empirical comparisons as the active baseline.

Keep the condensate as an optional physical-state/matter-sector hypothesis.
Its finite bodies and dilute dust limit are not excluded by this test, but
the background-data degeneracy does not support them specifically either.

The next useful derivation is the integration question, not another
isolated-body energy refinement: can the original motion/bath theory produce
or couple to a finite-charge matter population while retaining its already
tested cosmological scalar, without introducing unaccounted degrees of
freedom or double-counting stress? Start from the actual parent source/action
and derive the exchange or matching terms. Do not invent a fluid history to
restore a preferred score. Any resulting non-dust correction should return to
this same data comparison before a long new stability sequence.

This is a scoped negative result for one cosmological state and a preserved
positive result for the older framework's ability to compete on these data.
