# Stage50 — where the matter route becomes distinguishable

We now have a calculated structure-growth response from Stage49's motion/bath completion. It can preserve the old expansion history while changing how matter clusters. The existing framework and galaxy route remain intact.

**Example, not a fitted detection:** half of the pressureless budget in a heavy wave of mass9.586e-25eV, evolved from an explicitly equal seed at redshift99 on the zero-Lambda parent background:

| Comoving k (Mpc^-1) | Growth-amplitude reduction relative to its identical cold control |
|---:|---:|
|.1|.00013%|
|1|1.30%|
|2|18.43%|
|5|78.01%|

These are finite-time linear responses, not observed halo predictions. Actual structure at the smaller scales can be nonlinear. Lower masses move the effect to larger scales; smaller populated fractions reduce it. The same qualitative response appears on LCDM, so this is not yet an MTS-specific observational advantage.

**The most important finding:** changing the initial preparation and starting epoch moves the k=5 ratio from.22 to.76 of cold growth. We therefore have a testable mechanism and its response map, but must carry the early history through before assigning a growth-data score. At large scales the example remains near cold for all tested preparations.
