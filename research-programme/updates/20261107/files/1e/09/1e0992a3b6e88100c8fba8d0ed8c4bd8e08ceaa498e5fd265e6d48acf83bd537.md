## The initial transport exposed a numerical issue

The original BDF2 schedule starts the attributed new interval contribution
with both current and previous increment zero. For A=0 and constant source F,
its exact discrete solution is

    I_n = (n - 1/2 + 1/(2*3^n)) h F.

Thus the startup loses a half-step of the continuous right-hand integral.
For32 versus64 steps its leading difference is1/127, about0.7874%.
This is derived, not inferred by fitting the measured result.

The original frozen runs retain38 failed checks:32 total-channel response/
history comparisons and six very tight joint-versus-split identities.
Baseline and the MTS increment individually pass the1% comparisons, but
their cancelling total cannot be declared accurate from that fact.
