# Stage162 — the endpoint contribution is derived, not fitted
Let A,W,C,H,B denote the fixed temperature samples at interval positions0,1/4,1/2,3/4,1.
The cubic through A,W,C,B predicts P(H)=A/4-W+3C/2+B/4.
Holding H,W,C fixed, changing endpoint quadrature therefore gives
deltaE=-(deltaA+deltaB)/4 for E=H-P.
Save separate endpoint/component/panel vectors before their sum.
Their independent norm allowance is one quarter the sum of their norms; the matched allowance first sums endpoint vectors at each panel.
Neither is automatically an upper bound on the unobserved integration remainder.

For general interval position s, with the inherited warm bubble bW,
L_A=(1-s)(1-2s)-3*bW/8; L_B=s*(2s-1)+bW/8.
L_W=bW and L_C=4s(1-s)-3*bW/4.
For the cubic both endpoint weights atH are1/4; for the local route they are-1/8,3/8.
The saved H-enriched trajectory R=P+bH*(H-P(H)) has endpoint sensitivity
J_A=L_A-bH*L_A(H), J_B=L_B-bH*L_B(H).
These vanish at W,C,H and equal the appropriate endpoint cardinal value.
If propagated, use J_A deltaA + J_B deltaB, not the pointwise weights everywhere.
Keep earlier history fixed: this is a terminal-window sensitivity calculation, not a globally recomputed earlier window sharing A.

Sources retain control minus sampled leading plus D,V,S.
Only incoming integration changes; no control, electron population, hard-angle, radial, virtual or history refinement is silently included.
The sampled equilibrium control is not a new empirical baseline: paired final baseline/MTS readouts must retain their saved actual baseline, not relabel heavy/cold as baseline/MTS.
