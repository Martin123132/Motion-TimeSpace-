# Safe continuation of the independent position check

The main position calculation has an8400-second safety budget. If that
budget interrupts its last case, do not repeat already completed cases or
discard the saved last-case analytic response. The continuation accepts
only that explicit safe-boundary failure, rehashes all saved evidence,
reuses completed cases, and reconstructs the remaining independent check.
It does not accept an accuracy failure as a timeout.

The continuation uses two rather than six Newton iterations in the complex
moving-map oracle. This is a numerical-work reduction, not a new physical
approximation: inside a cell the map is affine in the reference position,
so one Newton step solves that spatial inverse exactly in exact arithmetic.
For an inverse label initially at a simple unperturbed root, one Newton
step gives its exact first variation; the second corrects floating-point
base-root error. Monotonicity and the original fixed cell incidence apply.

Keep the independent nonlinear construction, all radial nodes, material
cuts, kernel data and the original end-to-end position sensitivity gate.
Compare two/six iterations at the original off-grid probes and verify the
resolved manufactured direction at the original tolerance. Record their
compensated-density differences without pretending that the previously
failed relative density gates now pass. The full-grid propagation and
independent metric-jet validator still determine numerical acceptance.

Continuation and parent are separate immutable runs. The timeout, completed
case reuse, actual oracle iteration counts and original precision warnings
remain explicit in the final report. A continuation is not launched if the
main run completes normally or fails a scientific accuracy check.
