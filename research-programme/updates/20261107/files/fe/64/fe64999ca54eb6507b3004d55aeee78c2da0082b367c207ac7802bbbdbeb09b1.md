## Interpretation and next target

This check directly tests the new temperatures instead of borrowing a
midpoint accuracy estimate. The measured frequency effect on the
evolved correction is small. Stage104's distinct time-interpolation
differences and its36 source-forecast misses remain unchanged.

Electron/hard-angle geometry, inner rules, control accuracy, domain/tails
and other temperatures are separate error sources. Two quadrature rules
alone do not prove a continuum error bound or establish convergence order.

At the more frequency-sensitive late temperature (.4375), compute only the seed5 D/V/S corner at the SAME pilot geometry, reusing the frozen seed3/seed4 sources and control. Compare the 4-to-5 component differences with the saved 3-to-4 differences to test whether refinement actually reduces the sensitivity; propagate that single new difference through the same local hat. No global precision claim from one additional rule.

All work remains local. Earlier derivations, histories and observational
results remain the same authority.
