# Finite condensate bodies: results, 3 October 2026

**Outcome:** constructed seven compact, finite-charge bodies in the full
fractional-quartic complex-field candidate. They pass the tested fixed-charge
stability checks and approach the previously derived bulk/surface limit.

## New physical results

- Derived a cubic vacuum edge, f~(g/6)^(3/2)(R-r)^3. It joins vacuum without
  a delta-function surface source; no assumed exponential tail or wall term.
- Solved support radii 6,8,12,20,40,80,160 in coexistence-scale units. The
  same action supplies the interior density, interface and finite-size energy.
- The largest body has E/(mu_*Q)=1.0035052. Its leading surface-energy estimate
  agrees within 1.0914%; its chemical-potential shift agrees within 0.5475%.
- Derived the fixed-charge Hessian and checked it on three radial grids per
  body. All seven lowest constrained radial eigenvalues are positive. The
  phase and translation zero modes converge to zero; conditional ground-state
  identities control the other angular sectors.
- Derived exact integrated stress cancellation. It gives a pressureless
  rest-frame monopole and M_active=E at leading weak gravity in the retained
  minimally coupled Einstein sector, without assigning a separate force.

## Validation and scope

21 exact identity checks; seven refined profiles plus seven coarse controls;
21 spectral resolutions; 42 finite fixed-charge energy-increase tests.
Maximum coarse/fine observable change <4.78e-9, fine virial residual <7.32e-11.
Maximum last-grid change of the lowest constrained eigenvalue: 0.03705%.
The charge is global U(1), not electric charge. The original real field/bath
has not been replaced. These are classical candidate-model results with
unfitted physical scales, not a measurement or a formation calculation.
Numerical interior stability is not a nonlinear boundary/exterior theorem.

The discarded attempts are retained, including two mesh-limit failures and
two spline-reconstruction bugs. Final evidence is only
`results/regular-center-bodies/status.json` and `results/identities/status.json`;
earlier folders are exploration or incomplete attempts, not additional passes.
