## A derived alternative, not an assumed repair

For Phi=alpha*T*W+beta*g and H=delta Phi, the same field-force variation has
the exact flux form

    delta F_i = sum_cells integral H_xi N_i dxi
                +sum_internal_faces [H] N_i
                +H(inner)N_i(inner)-H(outer)N_i(outer).

The jump is right minus left. ALL material faces and artificial radial
polynomial cuts belong in the partition. This replaces a sign-changing
basis gradient in very small cells with basis values plus explicit face
loads; its numerical benefit still has to be tested.

`DERIVATION-20260929-position-force-flux-form.md` gives the moving-map chain
rule. The exact manufactured checker passes eight checks, including negative
controls showing that omitting the internal jump or endpoint terms gives

`source-intake/navier-stokes/20260914/annular-position-force-flux-identity-attempt01/status.json`.
