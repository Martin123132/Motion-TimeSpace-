## 6. Additional derived precision tool, not yet substituted

The validator also proves a conservative identity for either dust radial coefficient A in f_dust=A(R,Y,V)rho. With rho_t=-(rho V)_R and V_t=a-V V_R,

    B_dust = -partial_R(A rho V)
             +rho V*(A_R at fixed Y,V + A_Y Y_R)+rho A_V a.

For this background-coordinate partial a=0. Defining Z=Y_t+A rho V turns the flux derivative into an algebraic flux subtraction, leaving an ordinary radial equation for Z with forcing B_nondust+smooth-f_Y(A rho V). Since rho vanishes at physical endpoints, the boundary conditions are unchanged. This offers a derived way to reduce the remaining cancellation in source transport if higher accuracy is needed; it has not been implemented in the reported numbers. No normalization or guessed conservation correction is implied.
