# Stage129 — matching the inner and source energy moments

For saved positive Doppler rates w and ratios r,define

    lambda=sum w(1-r^-1),
    m=sum w(1-r^-1-r^-2+r^-3),
    H(p)=sum (w/r)(r^p-1).

The paired kernel symmetry is H(p)=H(-p-1). At p=0 this implies
sum w r^-2=sum w r^-1. At p=1 it implies sum w r^-3=sum w.
Thus m=2 lambda,provided these detailed-balance identities hold. They
must be checked on the actual saved quadrature;no value is fitted.

The inner generator on a pure log-frequency power x^p has eigenvalue

    sigma(p)=sum w(r^p-1)+lambda.

In particular sigma(-1)=sigma(-2)=0 and sigma(-3)=lambda. The first is
the right chemical mode. Under unrestricted frequency integration,the
second and third correspond to left number/energy moments of the slope
field. Finite domain/boundary fluxes remain separate terms and are not
eliminated by these bulk identities.

The inherited positive two-point deposition interpolates in u=exp(-h j).
For a physical shift with target mu=1/r between grid values a and b,
its nonnegative weights reproduce E[U]=mu and E[1]=1,not all powers.
Let v=(mu-a)(b-mu)>=0 (equivalently w0*w1*(b-a)^2,with endpoints ordered).
Then exactly

    E[U^2]-mu^2=v,
    E[U^3]-mu^3=v(a+b+mu).

The deposited bulk eigenvalue biases are consequently

    delta_sigma(-2)=sum rates*v,
    delta_sigma(-3)=sum rates*v(a+b+mu).

These are nonnegative and vanish only for grid-aligned shifts (or zero
rates). Positivity,unit total weight and exact first and second moments
of a deterministic off-grid shift cannot all hold:its variance would
have to be zero. This is a restriction on this point-deposition rule,
not a no-go theorem for conservative finite-volume schemes.

The inherited four-point Lagrange rule in u reproduces powers0,1,2,3,
so it can preserve these bulk moments to roundoff,but its weights may
be negative. It is used for the inherited smooth outer forcing,not
silently substituted into the positive inner evolution.

Rebuilding the temperature samples can reduce interpolation error but
cannot remove this spatial deposition bias at a fixed frequency grid.
Measure both effects separately. The current experiment keeps the
spatial discretization fixed and tracks its moment defects explicitly.
