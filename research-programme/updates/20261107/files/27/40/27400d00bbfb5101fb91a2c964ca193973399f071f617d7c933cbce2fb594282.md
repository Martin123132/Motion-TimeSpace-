# Stage98: electron-direction refinement preserves the complete correction

## What was derived and tested

A uniform four-direction electron azimuth rule introduces a specific
fourth-moment anisotropy. At fixed transverse speed it gives
<vx^4>=vperp^4/2 and <vx^2 vy^2>=0, instead of the continuum
3vperp^4/8 and vperp^4/8. The eight-direction rule gives both exact
angular moments. The Fourier-selection derivation and its limits are in
`DERIVATION.md`; the direct checks are in `results/preflight/status.json`.

The same source is recalculated with32 rather than16 electron directions,
at unchanged radial2/polar2/hard5 and the same physical temperature/profiles.
The expensive D/V/S block has4480 nodes:2240 exact reuses with half weights
and2240 new actual-node loop/state calculations. The control has20480 nodes:
10240 exact reuses with half weights and10240 new calculations. All24960
identities, weights and node aggregates are checked; reconstruction is exact.
No source mode, gas variable, coupling or correction component is removed/fitted.
