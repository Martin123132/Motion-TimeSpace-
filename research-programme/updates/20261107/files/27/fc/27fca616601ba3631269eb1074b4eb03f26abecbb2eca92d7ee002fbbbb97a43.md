# Stage118 — grid target reached; short precision histories remain stable

13 October 2026. Private/local continuation of Stage117.

## Result in plain language

The next frequency grid meets the frozen **1% refinement target** for final
radiation, peak gas response and the reconstructed inner defect, for baseline
and both MTS correction joins in both backgrounds and matching windows.

The short higher-precision evolutions find **small but measurable error
accumulation**, not a runaway. At worst the accumulated gas difference is
7.08e-10 of the saved peak response. It can be about3.11 times the independently
reset one-step difference, so the result is not that accumulation is zero.

This lets the next numerical effort move toward the remaining time-step and
matching-window questions, rather than simply demanding more decimal digits.

## Consecutive high-precision histories

Each interval has32 BDF2 steps. Its first two states come from the saved
Stage118 double history. Every subsequent photon AND gas state is carried
at50 or70 decimal digits, without casting to double or restarting it.
At each step the same matrix factorization also solves an independent
reset-to-saved-history control. All three columns are included in both.

The centres were selected from Stage117's largest **total-column** raw gas
residuals before inspecting these new trajectories; they were not chosen
after observing a favourable Stage118 result.
