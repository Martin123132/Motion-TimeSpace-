## What changed mathematically

The previous scalar energy estimate treated the metric/matter response as
an input. This stage constructs a positive comparison norm for the full
retained state and closes that response with a two-block differential
inequality. Both heavy-to-rest and rest-to-heavy couplings stay in the
comparison. Initial and terminal metric-time primitive terms stay in it.
No unknown solution difference or unknown adjoint gain is supplied as an
external bound.

This is an error-analysis result on the existing equations, not a new
matter action or a fitted coupling. The dust-density completion is a
mathematical norm choice, not a claim of positive gravitational energy.

The original numerical scaling produced useless large logarithmic bounds
even for the cold control. Using full scalar kinetic/gradient weights,
Newton-frame matter variables and weights derived from the multipole
recurrences removes that coordinate-induced overestimate. The G192 tail
and its boundary coupling are retained as a skew streaming operator plus
a nonpositive diagonal rate. Thomson drag is nonpositive in these weights.
