# Compute the compensated acceleration before cancellation

This is an algebraic re-evaluation of existing source coefficients, not a new coupling or a fit. Use exactly the saved third-grid integration nodes, measures, metric response, current weights and gravitational term. Change only how the fixed-metric acceleration remainder is evaluated.

For a material cell with local coordinate x, width h*J(label), field polynomial q=q0+q1*x+q2*x^2, acceleration-direction polynomial d=d0+d1*x+d2*x^2, and map acceleration w=w0+w1*x, the physical remainder is

    e = d-w*q_R = N(x,label)/(h*J(label)),
    N0 = h*J*d0-w0*q1,
    N1 = h*J*d1-2*w0*q2-w1*q1,
    N2 = h*J*d2-2*w1*q2.

Here w0=eta_left+D_left*source_change and w1=eta_right-eta_left+(D_right-D_left)*source_change. All cell-coefficient fields are existing Chebyshev polynomials in twice the layer label. J=1+side*(source_offset-width*label), with side=1/(-inner_offset) on the inner half and -1/outer_offset on the outer half. Products can therefore be formed using T_m*T_n=(T_(m+n)+T_|m-n|)/2 BEFORE binary64 evaluation. The numerator has local degree2 and layer degree at most twice the parent degree (for degree>=1). No high-degree physical mode is added: these degrees arise from products already present in the original expression.

Construct N at80decimal digits, then round its already-compensated coefficients once. Evaluate the small polynomial and divide by the positive material width. This isolates a candidate loss of significant digits in d-w*q_R and in the layer contractions. The original geometric/current weights and metric-induced acceleration stay fixed in the ablation.

Two source interpretations must remain separate:

1. rounded_coefficients: treat the actual saved/evaluator binary64 polynomial and geometry inputs as exact numbers. Carry out only their algebra at higher precision. This tests evaluation/contraction roundoff.
2. parent_nodal: reconstruct the field polynomial from the existing parent rationally embedded field values, and form direction-minus-projection from the individually archived binary64 nodal arrays BEFORE rounding that subtraction or the polynomial coefficients. This tests an earlier representation-rounding stage. It cannot recover precision missing in those parent arrays and must not be called a newly derived physical coefficient.

Keep the local coordinates supplied by the old packet evaluation for this controlled comparison. Reconstruct the same width/map formulas algebraically; record the effect of their arithmetic changes rather than pretending every intermediate binary64 value is identical. The integration positions and weights do not change. A later coordinate-precision check can isolate the mapping inversion itself if necessary.

Validation: exact numerator-versus-direct evaluation at90digits; binary64 error compared with a conservative degree-dependent arithmetic envelope; a manufactured compatible polynomial with a very small nonzero remainder; an incompatible control; and actual matched-point comparisons for both branches.
