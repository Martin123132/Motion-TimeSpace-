# Initial normalization-roundtrip audit

The first comparison stopped after the heavy-half family. It required bitwise equality between the intended physical seed and a stored physical state reconstructed from normalized coordinates. The accepted solver stores (seed / units) * units; floating-point division and multiplication need not reproduce the seed bit for bit.

The independent saved-packet diagnostic checks all 48 sealed trajectories. Every stored initial state equals its normalization roundtrip bit for bit, and every unseeded coordinate remains exactly zero. Only heavy-balanced nonzero cases differ from their intended physical seed: at most one ULP, with maximum whole-seed-relative difference 1.3995879128291427e-18.

The replacement checker explicitly enforces exact roundtrip equality, exact unseeded zeros, and the unchanged 1e-10 algebra tolerance relative to the intended physical seed. Numerical convergence tolerance remains 2e-5. Initial capture and the source-preserving momentum promotion are checked separately against the native seed.

No numerical trajectory, parameter, wave number, source bank, or solver tolerance is changed. The failed checker, partial comparison folder, and comparison-01 receipts remain preserved. The corrected analysis writes comparison-roundtrip/ and comparison-roundtrip-result.json; it does not overwrite the partial original analysis. This is a checker-contract repair, not a failed evolution retry.
