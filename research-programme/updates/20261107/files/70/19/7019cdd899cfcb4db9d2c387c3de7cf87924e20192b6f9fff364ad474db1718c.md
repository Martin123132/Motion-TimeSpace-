# A cold-adapted metric gain and a self-bounded GR reference

Private continuation of the retained MTS constant-mass, two-canonical-field linear branch. This changes the comparison norm, NOT the action, physical fields, source coupling, native bank, approximations, reference normalization or equations. Parent sources are listed at the end. N=ln a, conformal H>0, fixed comoving k>0; epsilon=H/k, eta=H_N/H. Native conformal densities have units Mpc^-2, H,k have units Mpc^-1, potentials are dimensionless, and weighted state coordinates have units Mpc^-1.

## 1. Why not use the first cold-only weight?

For massive momentum node i write its positive density coefficient d_i and speed 0<v_i<=1. The inherited normal-frame hierarchy has

    F0n_N = -(k v_i/H) F1n + 4 Phi_N
    F1n_N = (k v_i/3H)(F0n-2F2) + (4k/3Hv_i) Psi.

We retain every G192 mode and its actual last upper-link-zero closure. The background identities are

    (ln d_i)_N=-(1+v_i^2),  (ln v_i)_N=-(1-v_i^2).

A monopole weight sqrt(d_i)H/k and higher weights v_i sqrt[d_i(2l+1)/4] remove inverse-speed gravity, but their monopole/dipole symmetric estimate grows with (kv_i/H)^2. That is unnecessarily restrictive for the relativistic-to-cold transition.

Instead ADD the two positive monopole energy contributions:

    s_i = sqrt[v_i^2+4epsilon^2]
    x0_i = (sqrt(d_i)/2) s_i F0n_i
    xl_i = v_i sqrt[d_i(2l+1)/4] Fln_i, l>=1.

The coefficient4 simply gives the cold term the proposed sqrt(d_i)epsilon weight; it is a norm convention, not a parameter of MTS. For v_i>>epsilon this approaches the velocity-weighted kinetic norm; for v_i<<epsilon it resolves the density even when velocity tends to zero. Other matter coordinates remain the retained physical energy coordinates.

## 2. Exact uniform streaming bound

Let

    sigma_i = (s_i)_N/s_i
            = [-v_i^2(1-v_i^2)+4epsilon^2 eta]/s_i^2
    a_i = -(1+v_i^2)/2 + sigma_i
    b_i = -(3-v_i^2)/2.

The free monopole/dipole block is

    [[a_i, -s_i/(sqrt(3)epsilon)],
     [v_i^2/(sqrt(3)epsilon s_i), b_i]].

Its symmetric off-diagonal entry is EXACTLY

    c_i = -2epsilon/(sqrt(3)s_i),  |c_i|<=1/sqrt(3).

All higher streaming pairs, including the core/tail link, remain skew. Their diagonal weight rate is b_i<=-1. Therefore the whole node's logarithmic norm is bounded by

    mu_i = (a_i+b_i+sqrt[(a_i-b_i)^2+4c_i^2])/2
         <= max(eta-1/2,-1)+1/sqrt(3).

The last inequality follows because sigma_i is a convex combination of -(1-v_i^2) and eta. This is uniform in 0<v_i<=1 and epsilon>0, with neither a lower speed floor nor an upper kv_i/H cutoff. At fixed epsilon>0 the coefficient limit v_i->0 has a_i=eta-1/2, b_i=-3/2, upper streaming=-2/sqrt(3), lower streaming=0. No inverse speed remains.

    D_defect=3H DeltaW alpha, Q_defect=-k DeltaW alpha

## 4. Derive the physical current equation as well as density

The preceding work established

    D_N=ell_D x+3DeltaW Psi+3W_q Phi_N.

The new current equation is

    Q_N=ell_Q x+(W_thermal k/H) Psi,
    q U_Phi=0, q U_Psi=W_thermal k/H.

It follows directly from the native species equations, including collision cancellation, with

    ell_Q=-2q+(k/H)(P-2pi/3)
            -[(DeltaW_N+DeltaW)/sqrt(R_c)] e_alpha^T.

P is the pressure dictionary for the ACTUAL native equations: baryon native synchronous pressure, normal-frame radiation pressure, massive quadrature pressure and canonical scalar normal pressure. In particular the massive monopole coefficient is 2sqrt(d_i)v_i^2/(3s_i), and a scalar contributes [-omega^2 phi/Omega, field_velocity]. This expression is separately checked against q_N+qF. The retained thermal/quadrature discrepancy affects the equation, rather than being hidden by imposing a conservation formula.

## 5. Close BOTH metric systems algebraically

Write the complete equations as x_N=F x+U_Psi Psi+U_Phi Phi_N. The spatial-Poisson channel remains

    p_P=-d/(2k^2)
    t_P=-(ell_D+3DeltaW p_P)/(2k^2+3W_q)
    B_P=F+U_Psi p_P+U_Phi t_P.

For the GR reference itself, the physical constraints are

    Phi=-D/(2k^2)-3H Q/(2k^3)
    Psi=Phi-Pi/k^2.

Thus

    p_GR=-d/(2k^2)-3Hq/(2k^3)-pi/k^2
    t_GR=-[ell_D+3(H_N q+H ell_Q)/k+3W_q p_GR]
                     /(2k^2+3W_q)
    B_GR=F+U_Psi p_GR+U_Phi t_GR.

To derive t_GR, differentiate the Phi constraint and substitute BOTH D_N and Q_N. The Psi coefficient combines 3DeltaW+3W_thermal=3W_q. The denominator is the original positive implicit metric denominator. There is no Phi_N ansatz, slow-metric approximation or unexplained reference gain. These GR rows and the entire extended generator are compared against the independent native GR evaluator, not merely against the Poisson branch.

## 6. Keep heavy micromotion skew and bound both responses

The symbol r used as a subscript means the remaining block, whereas the scalar r=k/Omega_h is the rescaling. In the cold control there is no heavy block and only D is needed. These entries bound the two block energy derivatives, including the massive tail. An explicit Euclidean gain rate is

## 7. A sufficient uniform family without a speed floor

On a fixed finite N interval retain these explicit regime conditions:

- Expanding H>0; epsilon<=epsilon_0<1; bounded eta and positive R_c/H^2 bounded away from zero.
- Bounded conformal density fractions (including sum d_i/H^2), scalar background amplitudes E_j/H, DeltaW/H^2 and DeltaW_N/H^2.
- Bounded slow-field omega_s/k and bounded native baryon pressure-row norm in these coordinates; nonnegative opacity and the retained closures.
- Bounded kappa_Omega=H Omega_h/k^2, with the constant-mass heavy branch used throughout.
- Finite weighted initial data and consistent initial physical data for the GR/Poisson comparison.

No v_min>0 or kv/H bound is needed for the constants. Positive velocities are used to invert the coordinate transform at each finite member of the family. The limit controls weighted density/current/stress, not uniformly every raw multipole F_l at v=0. The exact zero-speed coefficient limits are a statement about these equations, not a claimed independently constructed cold cosmological background.

The new massive estimates give the same rest scaling d,ell_D,U_Psi=O(k), U_Phi,q,pi=O(H) as before, now uniform in v_i. Also ell_Q,r=O(k). For the heavy block ell_Q,h=O(k E_h/H+k E_h/Omega_h); after balancing it is O(Omega_h), but its GR channel coefficient is H/k, giving O(H Omega_h/k)=O(k kappa_Omega). Therefore p_GR and t_GR in balanced coordinates are O(1/k) under the listed conditions. Both explicit gain rates stay bounded on the fixed interval. No scalar phase is averaged out.

This is a sufficient regime. For example an unbounded native pressure row or kappa_Omega would invalidate this uniform estimate; it would not prove an instability of the underlying solution.

Let A>0 be the SAME dimensionless observable normalization as in the previous stage (A_c for CDM, or A_D=D_GR,envelope/H_final^2 for density). Require a prepared family with
