# Stage77 — a physical spectral readout and its thermalization gate

## What moved forward

We derived a local spectrum defined by a physical observer and a physical
clock, instead of treating an arbitrary subtraction of spectral moments as
the observable. The existing metric, matter, two-field and collision model
is retained. No new physical coefficient was fitted.

On the baryon rest tetrad and a fixed proper-baryon-density slice, the
first-order readouts are R0=X0+A d_b/3 and R1=X1-q v_b. Here q is the actual
distorted-background energy-dilation tangent, and A includes background
aging. Only the temperature/dilation component is separated; the shape,
temperature, clock and velocity information remain reconstructible.

The older number-and-energy-free diagnostic is useful for its limited
purpose, but it is not the full observable distortion: it removes the
physical chemical-potential template too. The new split retains that mode
and its energy. Finite frequency-boundary terms are explicit.

`DERIVATION.md` gives the equations, transformation laws, collision energy
exchange and next observable-transfer construction. `SOURCES.md` separates
our derivations from imported established-physics inputs.
