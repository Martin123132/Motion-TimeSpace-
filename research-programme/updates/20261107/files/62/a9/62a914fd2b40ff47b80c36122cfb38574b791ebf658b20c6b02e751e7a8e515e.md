## What failed, and what fixed it

Simply reusing the energy-oriented quadrature was inadequate. The raw
96-vector changed by132.3% in the pilot/outer refinement and123.1% in the
linear real-integration refinement, although energy and photon-number
totals looked stable. These failed tests remain on disk. They reject those
raw spectra as insertion data; they are not evidence of a failing MTS action.

Logarithmic soft-energy integration respects the actual infrared source
structure. It reduced the raw outer change to2.09%, but the raw real
refinement still changed125.5% of the source norm. Logarithmic panels alone
were therefore not declared a solution.

The successful numerical estimator uses a known leading term:

    C_est = C_matched,sampled - C_Gould,sampled + C_Gould,accurate.

The coefficient of the subtraction is exactly1, from the identity, not a
fit. All96 modes, physical profiles, finite recoil, virtual terms and
displaced-state terms remain. The coarse leading term uses the same
emission/inverse decomposition and soft-panel sampling convention; the
accurate term is integrated independently at higher order. This removes
large quadrature errors in a part already known analytically instead of
interpreting them as a new physical correction.
