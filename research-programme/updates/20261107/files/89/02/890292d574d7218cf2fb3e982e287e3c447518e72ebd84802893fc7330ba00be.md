## 1. Variables that keep all metric components

Use the same conformal synchronous chart and no-factorial expansion:

    ds^2=a^2[-d tau^2+C_ij dx^i dx^j],
    C=I+epsilon h+epsilon^2 l,
    A=Cinv C'/2=epsilon A1+epsilon^2 A2,
    theta=tr A, sigma=A-I theta/3, Hcal=a'/a.

Then A1=h'/2 and A2=(l'-h h')/2. A2 is a mixed tensor, not an independently Euclidean-symmetric matrix: C A must be symmetric. Its antisymmetric part satisfies A2-A2^T=-[h,A1]. Use

    l'=2A2+2h A1

as the second-order metric kinematic equation. Treating A2 as only a trace, or dropping vector/tensor parts generated by nonparallel scalar modes, loses physical equations.

Let R^i_j[C] be the three-dimensional mixed Ricci tensor of C, R its trace, and D its covariant derivative. All E,P,j_i,pi^i_j include 8piG, as in Stage65. j_i is covariant ADM momentum and S^i_j=P delta^i_j+pi^i_j.

## 2. Exact constraints and spatial evolution

Subtracting the homogeneous Hamiltonian equation gives

    R + 4Hcal theta + theta^2 - tr(A^2) = 2a^2 delta E,
    partial_i theta - D_j A^j_i = a j_i.

The full spatial Ricci equation is

    A' +(2Hcal+theta)A + R^i_j[C]
    +(Hcal'+2Hcal^2+Hcal theta)I
       = a^2[pi + (E-P)I/2].

Its background-subtracted trace and tracefree part are

    theta'+5Hcal theta+theta^2+R = (3a^2/2)delta(E-P),
    sigma'+(2Hcal+theta)sigma+[R^i_j[C]]TF=a^2 pi.

On the Hamiltonian constraint surface the trace reduces to the Stage65 Raychaudhuri equation. Off that surface the two trace equations differ by the Hamiltonian residual. This matters when choosing numerical evolution equations; it is not a new physical assumption.

## 3. Explicit quadratic geometric source

For h define

    Gamma1^i_jk=(partial_j h_ik+partial_k h_ij-partial_i h_jk)/2,
    Gamma2Q^i_jk=-h_il Gamma1^l_jk,
    R1_ij=partial_k Gamma1^k_ij-partial_j Gamma1^k_ik,
    R2Q_cov,ij=partial_k Gamma2Q^k_ij-partial_j Gamma2Q^k_ik
              +Gamma1^k_kl Gamma1^l_ij-Gamma1^k_jl Gamma1^l_ik,
    R2Q_mixed=R2Q_cov-h R1,  R2Q=tr(R2Q_mixed).

All contractions on these coefficients use the zeroth-order Euclidean metric. Write E2=E_L[v]+E_Q, and analogously for momentum and stress, including each ordinary species. The second-order constraints are

    R1[l]+4Hcal theta2-2a^2 E_L
      =2a^2 E_Q-R2Q-theta1^2+tr(A1^2),

    partial_i theta2-partial_j A2^j_i-a j_L,i
      =a j_Q,i+Gamma1^j_jk A1^k_i-Gamma1^k_ji A1^j_k.

The spatial evolution block is

    theta2'+5Hcal theta2+R1[l]-(3a^2/2)(E_L-P_L)
      =(3a^2/2)(E_Q-P_Q)-theta1^2-R2Q,

    sigma2'+2Hcal sigma2+[R1[l]]TF-a^2 pi_L
      =a^2 pi_Q-theta1 sigma1-[R2Q_mixed]TF.

Together with the Stage65 KG equations and l'=2A2+2hA1 these give the full metric-side second-order operators. Products become symmetric ordered convolutions; for example hA1 becomes (h_p A1_q+h_q A1_p)/2. No division by K^2 is required to express these equations, including their generated K=0 channel.

## 4. Constraint propagation rather than enforcement by fitting

Define the conformal residuals Hc and Mc_i as the left minus right sides of the exact Hamiltonian and momentum constraints. If the background and first-order equations hold, the second-order spatial-Ricci evolution and matter Ward identities imply

    Hc2'+4Hcal Hc2+2 partial_i Mc2_i=0,
    Mc2_i'+2Hcal Mc2_i+partial_i Hc2/2=0.

`derive_constraints.py` derives these from the contracted Bianchi identity using the residual Einstein tensor, not by substituting the constraints as equations. For these spatial-Ricci evolution equations Z00=Hc/2, Z0i=-Mc_i, Zij=Hc delta_ij/2 at leading nonzero error order. The positive error norm obeys

    partial_tau(Hc^2+4Mc.Mc)+4 partial_i(Hc Mc_i)
      =-8Hcal Hc^2-16Hcal Mc.Mc.

For an expanding background and periodic/vanishing error flux this controls constraint errors. Exact zero constrained initial data propagate as zero for the stated linear error system. Actual first-order numerical residuals add source terms and must be measured in a coupled run. This does not certify every formulation of the unrestricted nonlinear ADM system as strongly hyperbolic. Relevant primary context: Yoneda and Shinkai, https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0103032. The displayed residual equations are derived here for the explicitly chosen evolution system.

## 5. Ordinary collisionless matter from the mass shell

Use canonical covariant comoving momentum Q_i, Q=|Q|, n=Q/Q. To avoid confusion with the density E, denote the particle conformal Hamiltonian by omega:

    omega=sqrt(a^2 m_particle^2+Cinv^ij Q_i Q_j),
    partial_tau f+omega_,Qi partial_i f-omega_,i partial_Qi f=C_tau[f].

This is geodesic phase-space transport, not an assumed equation of state. C_tau=0 for collisionless particles. Define omega0=sqrt(Q^2+a^2m_particle^2). Its expansion is

    omega1=-Q.h.Q/(2omega0),
    omega2=Q.(h^2-l).Q/(2omega0)-(Q.h.Q)^2/(8omega0^3).

With f=f0(Q)+epsilon f1+epsilon^2 f2, the Fourier equations have the same free streaming operator

    L_K=partial_tau+i K.Q/omega0.

The quadratic equation is

    L_K f2 = i K.n f0_Q omega2 + C2
      + convolution Sym[i p_i omega1_p partial_Qi f1_q
                        -i q_i (partial_Qi omega1_p)f1_q].

Here Sym means average under p↔q. The independent l-dependent omega2 term remains present. Its exact collisionless Green function is

    G_K(tau,s;Q)=exp[-i K.Q integral_s^tau dt/omega0(t,Q)].

Consequently f2 is the sum of a propagated primordial boundary distribution, a retarded linear functional of l, and the retarded quadratic first-order drive. This is a derived causal memory response of ordinary particles, with no fitted relaxation time. It is not a claim that the earlier MTS memory sector has thereby been derived or replaced. The full angular/momentum distribution is retained instead of closing the hierarchy with an assumed pressure/shear.

Variational kinetic context: Andersson and Korzynski, https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.12152. The canonical momentum expansion and implementation here follow directly from the displayed mass shell.

## 6. Metric factors in the kinetic stress cannot be omitted

Absorb the usual species degeneracy, phase-space normalization and8piG into the measure. Then

    E=(a^4 sqrt(det C))^-1 integral omega f d^3Q,
    j_i=(a^3 sqrt(det C))^-1 integral Q_i f d^3Q,
    S^i_j=(a^4 sqrt(det C))^-1 integral (Cinv Q)^i Q_j f/omega d^3Q.

Let Theta1=tr(h)/2, Theta2=tr(l)/2-tr(h^2)/4 and d2=Theta1^2/2-Theta2. The energy and momentum quadratic integrands are

    a^4 E2 integrand = omega0 f2+omega1 f1+omega2 f0
      -Theta1(omega0 f1+omega1 f0)+d2 omega0 f0,
    a^3 j_i2 integrand = Q_i[f2-Theta1 f1+d2 f0].

For B=(Cinv Q)^i Q_j/omega, expand B0=Q_i Q_j/omega0,

    B1=-(hQ)_i Q_j/omega0-Q_i Q_j omega1/omega0^2,
    B2=((h^2-l)Q)_i Q_j/omega0+(hQ)_i Q_j omega1/omega0^2
       +Q_i Q_j(omega1^2/omega0^3-omega2/omega0^2).

Then a^4 S2 integrand=B0 f2+B1 f1+B2 f0-Theta1(B0 f1+B1 f0)+d2 B0 f0. `check_kinetic.py` tests the Hamiltonian, full phase-space transport and every stress/current component against exact finite-amplitude expressions for massless and massive particles on a nonparallel metric.

Integrating the transport equation by parts, with vanishing momentum-boundary terms, gives

    E'+(3Hcal+theta)(E+P)+sigma^i_j pi^j_i+a D_i j^i
      =(a^4 sqrt(det C))^-1 integral omega C_tau d^3Q,
    j_i'+(3Hcal+theta)j_i+a D_j S^j_i
      =(a^3 sqrt(det C))^-1 integral Q_i C_tau d^3Q.

These supply the conservation input needed by section4. For colliding photon/baryon species, individual collision moments need not vanish: their total energy-momentum transfer must cancel, including electron/recoil/thermal effects at whatever order is retained. This stage does NOT replace colliding photons with free neutrinos, set C2 to zero for photons, or supply a numerical second-order Thomson/recombination operator. A comoving, pressureless CDM component instead obeys its exact conserved-number relation relative to the primordial reference volume. Finite baryon temperature and photon momentum exchange require their own equations.

## 7. Massless directional response and a calculated quadratic drive

For massless collisionless particles omega=Q e(C,n), with e=sqrt(n.Cinv.n). Define the canonical bolometric angular occupation J=integral Q^3 f dQ / integral Q^3 f0 dQ and the tangential projector Pn=I-nn. Radial integration of the exact phase-space equation gives

    J'+[e n+Pn grad_n e].grad_x J
       +4(n.grad_x e)J-(Pn grad_x e).grad_n J=0.

At first order the physical tetrad brightness F1=J1-2n.h.n satisfies F1'+i k.mu F1=-2n.h'.n. For a scalar native mode this is

    F1'+i k mu F1=-4theta1/3-4 shear_scalar (mu^2-1/3),

where theta1=Theta1' and shear_scalar=(h'+6eta')/2. Differentiating this equation with respect to mu evolves the angular gradient needed at second order, without finite differences of the directional distribution.

For p+q=K write

    e1_p=-n.h_p.n/2,
    e2Q= n.(h_p h_q+h_q h_p).n/4
          -(n.h_p.n)(n.h_q.n)/8,
    v1_p=-h_p n+(n.h_p.n)n/2,
    J1_p=F1_p+2n.h_p.n.

Then the quadratic drive is

    QJ2=-4i(n.K)e2Q
      -Sym[i(q.v1_p)J1_q+4i(n.p)e1_p J1_q
           -i e1_p(Pn p).grad_n J1_q].

The independent metric contribution is +2i(n.K)n.l_K.n. The full solution is G J2_initial plus the retarded l contribution plus integral G QJ2. No component is secretly fitted.
