## Retained action and units

This starts from the same Einstein action with the two retained canonical
scalar fields and the existing matter/radiation sector. It does not introduce
a replacement action or derive the numerical value of Newton's constant.

The parent divides by M_Pl²=(8pi G)^-1 and uses conformal time tau, N=ln a,
H=calH and comoving k in inverse Mpc. Native D,P,Q,Pi have units Mpc^-2.
In particular D=a² delta rho/M_Pl² in its declared gauge. The source
normalization is inherited, not fitted again.

For the static weak-field, nonrelativistic reduction, eliminating the
nondynamical gravitational constraint leaves, mode by mode,

    L_P/M_Pl²=-a²[k² Psi²+D Psi].

Varying Psi at fixed leading matter variables gives
2k² Psi+D=0, equivalently -k² Psi=4pi G a² delta rho.
The coefficient1/2 is thus the parent's calibrated8pi G normalization, not
an independently invented coupling. This static reduction requires a bound
on the neglected momentum, shear, pressure and time-dependent terms.

The exact retained constraints instead give

    Phi=-(D+3 H Q/k)/(2k²)
    Psi=Phi-Pi/k².

The native Pi convention is essential; do not import another shear convention.

    delta_n=delta_s-3 H alpha

    delta_n'=-theta_c+3 Phi_driver'
