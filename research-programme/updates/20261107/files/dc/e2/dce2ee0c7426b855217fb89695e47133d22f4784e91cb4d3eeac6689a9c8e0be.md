# Stage172 — complete finite scattering-angle temperature source

At x=14, define xi=ln(theta), theta=k_B T/(m_e c^2). The inherited hard-angle rule
is the three-point Gauss-Legendre integral over cosine c. Nodes are
c=(-sqrt(3/5),0,+sqrt(3/5)), weights=(5/9,8/9,5/9). Their moments integrate
polynomials through degree5 exactly; this does not establish accuracy for the
non-polynomial microscopic scattering kernel.

With electron index a and hard-angle index j, the saved source is already

    s_aj(xi) = -W_aj(xi) F(P_aj(xi),h(xi)),  S_x14(xi)=sum_j sum_a s_aj(xi).

W includes the electron probability and reflection multiplicity, incoming seed
quadrature and x^2/expm1(x) factor, 2pi, hard-angle weight and Born differential.
Thus no extra factors are attached when adding saved vectors. Three-angle sums
retain the common incoming frequency weight; they are a quadrature contribution
to a later frequency integral, not the entire source or a separately normalized
angular average. Electron and hard-angle sums commute because both are finite.

For smooth input histories D=d/dxi and L_aj=D ln W_aj,

    DS = -sum_aj W_aj [L_aj F + DF],
    D2S = -sum_aj W_aj [(L_aj^2+DL_aj)F + 2L_aj DF + D2F].

The hard Gauss nodes/weights are temperature independent; no spurious derivative
of those weights is introduced. Temperature still changes electron probabilities,
velocities, boosts/recoil, thermal factors, moving integration boundaries and the
incident state. Stage170's source-chain-rule terms and precision representation
are reused, not replaced by a fitted temperature curve.
