# Quadrupole response: results

4 October 2026. **Derived the flat-reference bodies' shape stiffness,
oscillation gap and response to an applied quadrupole tide.**

- Translation supplies an exact zero mode. Its ground-state identity
  gives positive angular stiffness: L_plus,2 >= 4/R^2, L_minus,2 >= 6/R^2.
- The rotating amplitude and phase equations give actual shape frequencies,
  not just energy-Hessian eigenvalues. For support radii 8,20,80:
  Omega_2 = .13091267, .018811725, .0018175917 in coexistence units.
- Varying the full weak metric derives the driving force and moving-edge
  response. No free tidal coefficient was inserted.
- Independent static solvers agree within 5.45e-7 relative; refined
  translation errors tend to zero. Thirteen algebraic checks, twelve
  primary spectra, nine boundary-value solutions, three generator controls
  and twenty-four finite-energy tests support this stage.
- The same potential's surface tension also predicts a large-body liquid
  limit. For the largest body it predicts the shape frequency within .765%
  and the static response within 3.54%, without fitting either result.

The finite-energy discrepancy falls approximately fourfold when the
distortion is halved; its smallest-step maximum is .416%. All figures are
dimensionless candidate-model results, not physical measurements.
The static coefficient here is the field-relaxation response in a prescribed
weak metric, not the full self-gravitating relativistic tidal observable.

**Numerical lessons retained:** the coarse linear mesh over-stiffened the
largest body, so the final result uses interface-refined quadratic elements.
A tighter collocation attempt hit its node limit for R80; that failed
attempt is preserved and excluded, rather than counted as extra precision.
