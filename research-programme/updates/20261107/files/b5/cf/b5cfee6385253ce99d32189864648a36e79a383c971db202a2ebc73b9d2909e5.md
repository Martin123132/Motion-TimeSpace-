# Preserved normalization-check failure

The first validation stopped at published_asymptotic_normalization_1.
The computed metric response and the Cartesian current susceptibility were
correct, but the initial output labelled sigma_bar/M^5 as k2B. Cardoso2017
uses k2B=2*G*sigma_bar/M^5: its asymptotic metric has S_l/l in EqB10.
The omitted l=2 conversion caused an exact factor-two mismatch at every
nonzero coupling. This is a convention-label bug, not numerical noise.

The original solver/results and validator remain preserved. Version2 labels
sigma_bar and Cardoso k2B separately; no radial equation, source coefficient,
fit parameter or acceptance tolerance is changed. The 14 numerical solves
are repeated into coarse-v2 and fine-v2 for clean, source-owned outputs.
Authoritative aggregate validation is validation-v2/status.json.
