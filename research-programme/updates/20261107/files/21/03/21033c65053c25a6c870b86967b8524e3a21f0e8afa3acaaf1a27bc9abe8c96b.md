# Final-epoch physical readout

Before final-epoch values are inspected, register the existing Stage167 physical
readout: heat-scaled temperature shift, mu, y, residual norm, distortion norm and
potential norm. Reuse its implementation and metric order exactly; do not invent
a conversion, fit an extra amplitude or download data.

Compare both original response grids and quarter/eighth history inputs at the
unchanged1% numerical-comparison gate, for both backgrounds, joins and operator
continuations. Check linear temperature/mu/y component sums at1e-10. Report
absolute differences too; a near-zero scalar's large relative error is not
silently dropped. No norms are assumed additive.

This is the tested interval's contribution, not a whole-sky/full-history MTS
prediction or a data fit. Incoming and other-source contributions remain explicit.
Retain every failed row rather than changing the numerical tolerance.
