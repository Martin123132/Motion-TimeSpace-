# Follow-up derivation: correlate the soft event with its Born limit

The complete stable-jump repair leaves a small warm-endpoint5-to6 pair defect. Eight worst-event probes show that compensated radial/angular sums alone barely change it. The independently evaluated real and Born jumps enter an infrared subtraction; a roundoff-sized mismatch before subtraction can be magnified by the soft pole and thermal occupation. The following algebra addresses that mismatch without forcing electron energy to equal minus photon energy.

Let d0=1+eA, d=d0-sB, h0=e/d0 and h=(e-s-esC)/d. The exact signed-soft differences are

    dh = h-h0 = -s[(1+eC)d0-eB]/(d0*d)
    dh+s = s[e(d0(A-C)+B)-s*B*d0]/(d0*d).

These expressions vanish continuously as s approaches zero without subtracting two nearly equal hard energies. Define p0=e*n_in-h0*n_h and dp=-dh*n_h-s*n_s. The independent electron rest-energy difference follows by rationalizing the square roots:

    dEe = [2*p0.dp + dp.dp]/[sqrt(1+|p0+dp|^2)+sqrt(1+|p0|^2)].

The lab photon and electron differences are respectively [T00*(dh+s)-T0i*dp_i]/theta and [T00*dEe+T0i*dp_i]/theta. Neither is assigned from the other. The number difference is sign(s). With left/right vectors L,R and Born vectors L0,R0,

    LR-L0R0 = (L-L0)R + L0(R-R0).

Consequently the pole-subtracted integrand is evaluated as

    density*[(L-L0)R+L0(R-R0)] + (density-pole/s)*L0R0.

For the thermal emission/absorption pair, combine the Born coefficients as sum(active densities)-2*pole/|s|, retaining the original cutoffs and omitted-channel subtraction exactly. All angular/radial nodes, amplitudes, occupation weights, source profiles and virtual coefficients remain fixed. This is a precision rearrangement, not a physical cutoff, refit, gas projection or new collision model.
