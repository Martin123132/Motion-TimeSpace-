I. INTRODUCTION
There
have
been
many
calculations
of
finite-
temperature
corrections
to various
processes because, as
experiments
and
observations
have
become
better
and
produced
more
accurate
results,
it has become
corre-
spondingly
necessary
to make more and more exact cal-
culations
for these processes.
In addition,
theoretical
in-
vestigations
of the early Universe
have also necessitated

of finite-temperature
corrections.
There-
fore, it is important
to calculate at what temperatures

become of sufficient

For example,

of QED [1—4], some have calculated
helium

in the early Universe
[5,6], and some have calculated
the
finite-temperature
corrections to the anomalous
magnetic
moment of the electron [2,7—8].
However,
there have not been many calculations
of the
corrections
to fundamental
scattering
processes.
Such
processes are interesting,
not only in themselves,
but also
as parts of other phenomenona.
In this paper
we shall
calculate the T-dependent
O(a
) corrections for one such
process,
namely,
Compton
scattering,
for
which
the
zero-temperature
corrections
were calculated
by Brown
and Feynman
[9]. The Compton
scattering
cross section,
apart
from its intrinsic
interest,
is useful
in the calcula-
tion of several processes such as calculating
stellar opaci-
ty [10],and calculating
relic radiation [11].
