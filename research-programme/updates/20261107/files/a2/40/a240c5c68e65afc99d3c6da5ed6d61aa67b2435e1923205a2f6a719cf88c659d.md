# Stage118 calculation

## Grid refinement
The Stage117 positive two-point interpolation in 1/x, four-point forcing on
the known background, original lower exterior and stored outer history are
unchanged. Halving log spacing changes resolution, not a physical parameter.
The sparse stencil construction and paired feedback operators are reused.
See `../finer-grid-and-gas-precision-20261013/DERIVATION.md`.

## Consecutive high-precision evolution
For U=(z,b), dt the saved response step, alpha=3/(2dt) and H=diag(M,chi),
each interior BDF2 step solves

    B_i = [ alpha M - R_i K_i       R_i K_i t ]
          [ R_i t^T K_i     chi(alpha+1)-R_i t^T K_i t ],
    F_i = (R_i D_i, -R_i t^T D_i),
    B_i U_i = H(2 U_{i-1} - U_{i-2}/2)/dt + F_i.

There are 96 photon coefficients and one gas variable. Intervals start after
step1 so both predecessor states exist. Only the first two seeds are taken
from the saved double history. Subsequent U states remain arbitrary precision;
there is no cast to double or reset on the accumulated branch.

The reset control replaces both predecessors by their saved double values at
each step. Both controls use the identical matrix, source and factorization.
All three columns (baseline, MTS join1, MTS join2) receive identical treatment.

Let U^d be the saved double trajectory and r_i its residual in these original
physical equations, evaluated at high precision. Then the accumulated change
delta_i=U_i-U_i^d obeys the exact discrete identity

    B_i delta_i - H(2 delta_{i-1}-delta_{i-2}/2)/dt = -r_i.

This identity tests the propagation of the local equation defects. It is not
a continuum stability theorem or a global bound outside the selected intervals.
The energy identity uses t^T M z, not a manually rewritten mass row.

## Precision scope
50/70-digit arithmetic is used for matrix assembly, histories, linear solves,
and the carried states. M,K,t,chi,R,D and the two initial seeds originate in
the saved binary64 data. Differences from the old calculation include its
energy-coordinate row rewrite and assembly/solve arithmetic. Rate-table
uncertainty, time-step bias, grid error and matching-window dependence are
separate from this precision comparison and are not cleared by it.
