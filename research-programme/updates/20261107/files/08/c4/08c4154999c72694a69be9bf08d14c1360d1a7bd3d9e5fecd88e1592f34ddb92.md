# A charge preserving bulk continuation

3 October 2026. Follow-on to06-conserved-stress-and-parent-source.md.

The homogeneous expansion calculation reaches y=1, but fixed charge forbids simply replacing a charged region by empty vacuum. The same potential supplies a constructive alternative in bulk equilibrium. The derivation below uses the full two-field candidate, not the phase-only derivative expansion at its vacuum boundary.

## A positive energy certificate

For
\[
 V(\varrho)=\tfrac34g\varrho^{4/3}+\tfrac14\lambda\varrho^4,
 \qquad g,\lambda>0,
\]
minimize 2V(varrho)/varrho^2. The critical amplitude and chemical potential are fixed:
\[
 \boxed{v_*=(g/\lambda)^{3/8},\qquad
 \mu_*^2=2g v_*^{-2/3},\qquad n_*=\mu_*v_*^2.}
\]
Set z=(varrho/v_*)^(2/3). The entire effective potential at mu_* factorizes:
\[
 U_*(\varrho):=V-\tfrac12\mu_*^2\varrho^2
 =\boxed{\frac{g v_*^{4/3}}4\,
 z^2(z-1)^2(z^2+2z+3)}\ge0.
\]
Equality occurs at vacuum and the critical amplitude. Thus the degeneracy is derived from the original candidate coefficients; it is not an inserted plateau.

In flat spacetime the full classical energy density is
\[
 {\cal E}=\tfrac12\dot\varrho^2+\tfrac12|\nabla\varrho|^2
 +\tfrac12\varrho^2\dot\theta^2+\tfrac12\varrho^2|\nabla\theta|^2+V,
 \qquad n=\varrho^2\dot\theta.
\]
Completing the square gives
\[
 {\cal E}-\mu_*n
 =\tfrac12\dot\varrho^2+\tfrac12|\nabla\varrho|^2
 +\tfrac12\varrho^2|\nabla\theta|^2
 +\tfrac12\varrho^2(\dot\theta-\mu_*)^2+U_*\ge0.
\]
Using either charge sign yields
\[
 \boxed{E\ge\mu_*|Q|.}
\]
This is a classical nongravitating energy bound. It is not an ADM-mass inequality in a strongly gravitating spacetime.

## Bulk coexistence at fixed mean charge

For 0<=nbar<=n_*, fill a fraction f=nbar/n_* with the critical charged phase, and the rest with vacuum. Neglect interface and kinetic contributions in the thermodynamic limit:
\[
 \bar\rho=f\,\mu_*n_*=\mu_*\bar n,\qquad
 \bar p=\bar n\,d\bar\rho/d\bar n-\bar\rho=0.
\]
The energy bound proves that this is the bulk infimum, rather than merely a configuration with lower energy than one selected homogeneous trial state. Finite interfaces cost positive energy, but their area/volume contribution vanishes for macroscopic phase domains in the nongravitating thermodynamic limit.

If local equilibrium and scale separation justify this bulk closure in an expanding state, conservation gives nbar proportional to a^-3 and hence rhobar proportional to a^-3. This is a derived pressureless bulk equation of state, conditional on equilibrated phase coexistence. It is not a derivation of cosmological formation, a galaxy distribution, an abundance or a new massless tensor particle.

At the inherited example, in mu_initial=1 units:
\[
 v_*=7.7110541270,\quad
 \mu_*=0.8903958352,\quad
 n_*=52.9432531192.
\]
Starting from n_initial=100 at a=1 reaches n_* at a=1.2361270940, independently confirming the y=1 crossing found from the homogeneous charge equation.

## Interface cost is fixed as well

For a stationary planar wall with theta=mu_*t, the full amplitude equation has first integral
\[
 \tfrac12(\partial_z\varrho)^2=U_*(\varrho).
\]
The wall's grand-potential tension is
\[
 \sigma=\int_0^{v_*}d\varrho\,\sqrt{2U_*(\varrho)}
 =\frac32\sqrt{\frac g2}\,v_*^{5/3}
 \int_0^1 dz\,z^{3/2}(1-z)\sqrt{z^2+2z+3}.
\]
It is positive and finite. In the chosen units,
\[
 \boxed{\sigma=9.5627254914.}
\]
This is excess E-mu_*Q per area, not an assumption that the wall carries zero charge. Two variable choices and50/80-digit quadrature agree. The tension gives a concrete coefficient for finite-size corrections, rather than an arbitrary surface penalty.

A finite spherical charged body is not a planar wall: curvature changes the pressure balance, its chemical potential and its profile. Those equations have not been solved by this integral. The phase-only expansion also cannot be extrapolated through varrho=0, where the original fractional potential does not have a finite ordinary mass Hessian.

## Best next test

Establish whether a controlled large-body limit actually approaches the bulk pressureless result. Only then use the derived stress source to assess weak gravitational backreaction.

This avoids two shortcuts: declaring failure at the homogeneous branch boundary, or declaring a dust component while ignoring interfaces and formation.
