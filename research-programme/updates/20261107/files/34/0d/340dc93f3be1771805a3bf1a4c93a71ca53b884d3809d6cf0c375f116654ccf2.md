## Next calculation:derive the interpolation contribution

Stage126's assembled branch linearly interpolates the original65-epoch
adjoints;the direct branch evaluates them at additional temperatures.
These are different treatments even if each direct integral is accurate.
The present test makes that distinction actionable rather than identifying
the old difference as quadrature noise.

For an operator A and unresolved state d on a coarse interval,let s be
the fractional coordinate,L denote endpoint linear interpolation,and
Delta denote the right-minus-left endpoint value. Exactly,

    L(A d) - (L A)(L d) = s(1-s) Delta A Delta d,

    A d - L(A d)
      = (A-L A)d + (L A)(d-L d) - s(1-s)Delta A Delta d.

No commutation of matrices is assumed:A acts on d on the left. The terms
separate operator curvature,state evolution beyond the endpoint chord,
and interpolation of their product. This is an algebraic identity,not
yet a measured physical attribution. For the specific direct-minus-
assembled source on the same saved state,the operator term is(A-L A)d.
The identical represented overlap cancels in that comparison.

Use the saved operators and states to evaluate these signed terms and
propagate them through the same response for baseline/MTS. Then refine
the initial-interval inner collision coefficients consistently with the
source,starting from the saved restart. Response integration,remaining
intervals and initial matching remain distinct questions.
