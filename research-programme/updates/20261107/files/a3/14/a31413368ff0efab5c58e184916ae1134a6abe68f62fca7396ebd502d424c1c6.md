# Pilot: source quadrature resolution diagnostic

The global 128-point full-orbit quadrature loses resolution at e=.6,n=16:
the full-source versus symmetric-half discrepancy is5.7362e-6 on the
integrated absolute source scale. The circular normalization and modal
optical identity pass, but those alone cannot validate orbital quadrature.
The pilot is retained, NOT an accepted converged spectrum. Its original
scripts are archived alongside the unchanged raw status and primitive data.

Before accepted runs, use composite Gauss-Legendre anomaly panels:
coarse16x24 per half orbit; fine24x32; quadrature control32x40.
Use twice as many panels for the full interval, so local spacing is equal.
No source physics or response coefficient changes. Predeclared gates:
full/half/raw source agreement below1e-10 on the absolute integrand scale;
total energy/AM fluxes and differences must refine below1e-6 relative to
their own component sums; signed-difference tails use absolute modal sums.
A finite tail scan is numerical evidence, not a certified infinite sum.
