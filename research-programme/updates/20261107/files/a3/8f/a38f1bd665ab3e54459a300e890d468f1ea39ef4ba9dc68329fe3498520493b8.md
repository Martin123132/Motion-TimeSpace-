# Stage112 — matched low-frequency feedback, not duplicated photons

## 1. Stored photons matter as well as collision rates

Let V a be the saved96-mode photon potential, M its mass matrix, and
K the saved Compton-minus-DC-minus-BR weak operator per Thomson time.
The parent equations are

    M a'=R K(a-t b),
    chi(b'+b)=-R t^T K(a-t b) + inherited cooling source.

Here t represents x exactly; e^T=t^T M is the photon-energy row.
For this response the inherited background/cooling source cancels.

Define a prescribed compact low-frequency replacement q(x,u) from
Stage111 prediction minus saved potential. Its matching window is1
on the tested core and smoothly zero outside the tested soft domain.
It is a numerical partition, not a fitted physical cutoff.
Let m_i=integral measure V_i q and p=M^-1 m.
In the execution both M and m use the SAME saved700-node inner product;
this makes the moment projection consistent with the retained parent.
Separate finer panel-quadrature moments are recorded as a sensitivity,
not silently mixed with the old mass matrix.

If the physical photon field is V(a+eta)+q, its weak equation includes
the derivative m'. Ignoring it counts stored photons twice:

    M eta'=R K(eta-t delta_b)+R C_ext[q]-m'.

Define z=eta+p, the correction to the TOTAL resolved photon moments.
Then the m' terms cancel analytically and the correct equation is

    M z'=R K(z-t delta_b)+R D,
    D=C_ext[q]-K p,
    chi(delta_b'+delta_b)=-R t^T[K(z-t delta_b)+D].

Thus if q is represented entirely using the existing operator,
C_ext[q]=K p, it produces ZERO physical response. Merely projecting
the new curve and adding K p is not a new physical correction.

The final physical correction is V z + q - V p. Its unresolved part
q-Vp is M-orthogonal to every retained test, including number and
energy. Report both the resolved response and the remaining shape.

## 2. Extend the collision functional on the compact replacement

BR uses its unchanged positive sourced rate:

    C_BR[A;q]=-integral dx x^2 n_P(1+n_P) A Gamma_BR q / I3.

DC uses the existing leading recoil-free Gould weak form with ALL legs:

    C_DC[A;q]=-kappa_DC integral ds dz W(s,z) Delta A Delta q,
    Delta A=A(s)+A(z-s)-A(z),
    Delta q=q(s)+q(z-s)-q(z).

W is the same normalized kernel used in Stage89's matrix construction.
The domain remains s>=the inherited finite floor, z>=2s, z<=60.
Because q has compact support below x_match, all events with s>x_match
vanish. Hard-photon energy transfer is retained, not put into the gas.
Delta x=0 by kinematics, so t^T C_DC[q]=0 without a fitted repair.

The compact Compton correction uses the leading Rayleigh-Jeans,
exact-velocity soft kernel in a symmetric weak form:

    C_C[A;q]=-(1/(2 I3)) integral dx sum_j k_j
                          [A(r_j x)-A(x)] [q(r_j x)-q(x)],
    r_j=exp(j Delta), k_j=stencil_j/r_j (j not0).

These positive shifts reuse Stage111's cached invariant stencil.
The continuum weak symmetry follows from detailed balance. Symmetrizing
its discrete representation preserves the constant test exactly and
is recorded as a leading-soft extension, not the full finite-frequency
Compton operator. Its equivalent compact-support adjoint is

    C_C[A;q]=(1/(2 I3)) integral dx q(x) sum_j k_j
              [A(r_j x)-A(x)+(A(x/r_j)-A(x))/r_j].

The number test is identically zero. Photon energy exchanges with gas
through its actual energy moment, not an assumed zero. Finite-frequency
Compton/recoil and the finite Planck-weight difference remain approximations
on this compact soft extension; the resolved propagator remains the
unchanged parent operator. Do not describe the extension as exact full QED.

Subtract the parent term separately for EACH process: D_a=C_a[q]-K_a p.
Its energy identities are then inherited: DC has zero energy, BR and
Compton are balanced by equal and opposite gas transfers.

to zero analytically rather than evolving interpolation roundoff.

evaluation of the compact source is new lightweight work; no finite-recoil
amplitude calculation or full parent history is rerun. The linear forced

No closure feedback is iterated into the original q. The result is a
first conditional response, not a fully recoupled parent cosmology.

sensitivities before interpreting a small net change.
