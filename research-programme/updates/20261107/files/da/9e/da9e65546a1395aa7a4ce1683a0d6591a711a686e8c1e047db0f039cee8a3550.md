# Stage55 — native heavy-field matching and late matter transfer

This is a daughter of Stage54, using the already retained Stage49 slow/heavy
canonical modes and the same gravity/matter coupling. No new sector, transfer
multiplier, fitted suppression curve, or combined dark-energy fluid is introduced.
The slow mode remains an exact canonical perturbation. The standard CAMB species
retain their own equations and existing native approximation schedules.

## 1. Variables and averaged evolution

Primes denote conformal time, H denotes the conformal Hubble rate, and k and
the canonical heavy mass M use inverse Mpc. In the heavy averaged chart define

\[
 R=8\pi G a^2\bar\rho_h=C/a,\quad
 D=R\delta_h,\quad Q=R u_h,\quad P=R\Pi_h.
\]

Here u_h=theta_h/k, NOT theta_h/H; it is also NOT the background's equation
of state. C is the inherited comoving heavy abundance, not a new fitted input.
The retained leading-order averaged background has w_h=0 and R'=-H R.
Substituting into the stress conservation equations gives

\[
 \delta_h'=-3H\Pi_h-k u_h-k z,\qquad
 u_h'=-H u_h+k\Pi_h.
\]

The synchronous-CDM convention remains delta_c'=-k z. The rest-frame pressure
closure, with zero leading background adiabatic sound speed, is

\[
 \Pi_h=c_h^2\left(\delta_h+{3H\over k}u_h\right),\qquad
 c_h^2={\kappa^2\over(\sqrt{1+\kappa^2}+1)^2},\qquad
 \kappa={k\over aM}.
\]

Locally holding a fixed, the perturbation/background beat frequency is
omega_b=a M(sqrt(1+kappa^2)-1). Hence omega_b^2=k^2 c_h^2, and
c_h^2 -> k^2/(4a^2 M^2) in the nonrelativistic limit. This fixes the leading
gradient response from the canonical dispersion, not a data fit. Expansion
corrections of order (H/(aM))^2 and higher-order averaging remain omitted; the
small pressure itself can have much larger fractional error than its error
normalized to the density/velocity mode amplitude.

For these specified averaged equations, direct substitution gives exactly

\[
 D'+H(D+3P)+kQ+kzR=0,\qquad Q'+2HQ-kP=0.
\]

The seven identities in `results/identities/status.json` check this algebra,
the beat dispersion, the cold limit, and constant comoving abundance. They do
not turn a finite-order averaging approximation into an exact scalar solution.

## 2. A background-consistent explicit event

At each nominal a=.0002, .000225, .00025, select the nearest root of

\[
 a R_{\rm exact}(a)=C.
\]

Use exact heavy background evolution before that root and C/a afterwards.
The roots are .00019994869096312038, .00022501566262210325 and
.0002499397245504851. Background density and the Friedmann H are continuous
there. The exact pressure is not continuous: the first two roots are near
w_h=-1, the third near +1, whereas the effective background has w_h=0.
This is a controlled change of description, not a new physical instantaneous
conversion. No exact distributional junction law is claimed for the projection.
The slow background is re-integrated and flatness re-solved for each choice.

At a matching root, project ONLY the heavy stresses from the source-complete
Stage54 exact trajectory. The default projection uses a 6pi phase half-window,
degree-eight secular polynomial, degree-six second-harmonic envelopes, and
degree-two fourth-harmonic envelopes. Set

\[
 \delta_h(a_*)={\langle aD_{\rm exact}\rangle_*\over C},\qquad
 u_h(a_*)={\langle aQ_{\rm exact}\rangle_*\over C}.
\]

Window and degree variations are retained. The exact source trajectory includes
look-ahead samples around the event; this is an offline matching calculation,
not an assertion of an acausal physical process or a locally closed extraction
algorithm. Only four explicitly sourced k modes are supported. Unknown k values
fail rather than silently interpolating a sparse matching table.

The event is handled by the native evolution scheduler, not inside a derivative
callback. It changes exactly three entries in the existing dark state block:
the two heavy coordinates and a per-trajectory chart flag. The integrator is
restarted at that event. Every other live state, including the exact slow field,
metric, baryons, photons, polarization, and available neutrino hierarchy, is
unchanged. Existing CAMB hierarchy switches copy the chart flag along with the
dark state; there is no shared mutable per-k switch in the field class.

The native audit counts 131/147/182/182 preserved evolving coordinates for
k=.03/.1/1/5, and exactly zero change outside the three heavy entries. Reversing
the mode order reproduces the outputs exactly. This is preservation of CAMB's
current live hierarchy, not a claim that no native hierarchy truncations exist.

## 3. Matching remainder and independent checks

The heavy perturbation stress has an explicit projection remainder. For the
central event the jumps (Delta D/R, Delta Q/R) are approximately
(-4.723e-6,4.200e-6), (-7.638e-5,2.564e-4),
(-.003397,.08227), (-.0005201,.03453) across the four modes.
In particular, the momentum coordinate can have a large instantaneous phase
component. Keeping the raw instantaneous stresses instead produces a 13.5073%
change in today's k=1 matter amplitude. That negative control demonstrates why
the field cannot simply be relabeled as dust at one arbitrary phase.

The averaged evolution overlaps the independently retained exact scalar
trajectory at a=.00025. The worst density difference is 1.893e-4 of the saved
density/velocity mode scale; the corresponding momentum differences are below
3.071e-7 of that scale. Those are NOT relative errors in tiny momentum or
oscillating density components. The worst density comparison at k=5 is about
0.132% relative to that particular density value. These overlap errors must
not be replaced by the much smaller late matching-epoch spread as an all-order
accuracy claim.

Late checks use 2001 and4001 time samples. Independent density assembly agrees
within9.82e-8, the Hamiltonian constraint within2.10e-5, and heavy continuity
within4.01e-5 under the documented sum-of-absolute-terms normalization. Momentum
residuals are below3.76e-8. The cold transfer output is float32: numerical
derivative residuals are not monotonically reduced by halving the stencil step.
Exact algebra and these finite-difference diagnostics are recorded separately.

## 4. What is actually compared to matter

CAMB's stock delta_tot excludes the heavy field stored in the dark-sector slot.
It is not relabeled as total matter. Instead reconstruct

\[
 \delta_m={R_c\delta_c+R_b\delta_b+R_\nu\delta_\nu+D_h
                  \over R_c+R_b+R_\nu+R_h},
 \quad
 P_m(k,z)={2\pi^2\over k^3}A_s(k/.05)^{n_s-1}|\delta_m|^2.
\]

The transfer is normalized to the same primordial curvature in every branch.
The inherited H0=70, Omega_m, slow mass, heavy mass, A_s=2.1e-9, n_s=.9653,
standard species, and illustrative F=.5 are fixed here, not newly fitted.
Both a slow-field all-cold control and LCDM with matching cosmological/primordial
parameters are run; these are not separately best-fit observational models.

Only four linear-theory power samples are calculated. They do not determine
sigma8, a continuous transfer curve, a CMB spectrum or an observational likelihood.
In particular, small-scale low-redshift structure requires appropriate nonlinear
modeling rather than treating these linear values as galaxy measurements.
