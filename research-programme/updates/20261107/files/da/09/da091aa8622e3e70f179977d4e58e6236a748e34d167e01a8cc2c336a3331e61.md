## What makes this a useful result

- Switching from the resolved field to its averaged description at three different epochs changes the adiabatic transfer ratios by less than0.0005% relative.
- Earlier starts, different averaging windows, tighter numerical settings and an independent onset integrator preserve the result. Removing the heavy occupation recovers the cold control.

The example stays near cold matter on the largest tested scale but loses substantial smaller-scale clustering. That is not automatically a success: it makes this mass/occupation combination sharply testable against observed structure. It is generic canonical wave-matter physics within this conditional completion, not by itself an MTS-specific advantage.
