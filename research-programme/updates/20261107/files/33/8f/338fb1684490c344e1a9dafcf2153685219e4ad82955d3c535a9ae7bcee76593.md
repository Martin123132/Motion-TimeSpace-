# Constructive field theory lead received during angular testing

Checked 27 October 2026. This is a research lead, not an input to Stage237;
the frozen bank, angular test, parameters and tolerances have not changed.

The [26 October OpenAI announcement](https://openai.com/index/building-scientific-understanding-with-academic-communities/)
reports four-dimensional gauge and fermion existence results and a Lean
formalization of Yang–Mills existence. The physics collaborators are preparing
an explanation before releasing the artifacts. No new proof was inspected here.

The [Clay problem statement](https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/yangmills.pdf)
requires both existence and a positive mass gap. A matter-theory construction
also does not, by itself, establish a dynamical gravitational sector.

Accessible background is [Dimock, The Renormalization Group According to
Balaban I](https://arxiv.org/abs/1108.1335), linked by the announcement through
DOI10.1142/S0129055X13300100. Its example is scalar phi4 in three dimensions.
Do not mistake this older exposition for the new four-dimensional proof.

After saving the angular test, a bounded relevance review should extract the
actual theorem assumptions and ask whether the MTS action, fields, couplings
and proposed limiting procedure satisfy them. Potentially reusable items are
scale-by-scale stability estimates and a controlled continuum construction.
These are questions for inspection, not a claimed transfer theorem. Preserve
the current framework rather than starting a replacement theory from the news.

No new proof downloads, correspondence, fits, public updates or model changes
were made for this lead. The publisher abstract endpoint returned403; the
author's arXiv abstract supplied the accessible bibliographic cross-check.
