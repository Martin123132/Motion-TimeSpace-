At each fixed physical epoch e,write the finite remainder on the existing
electron/hard-angle pilot as

    R4_e=K_e+D4_e+V4_e+S4_e,
    R3_e=K_e+D3_e+V3_e+S3_e.

Here the subscript3/4 changes ONLY the seven-panel incoming-frequency
rule from21 to28 points. K=control-sampled_leading is reused unchanged.
All event coefficients are evaluated at the actual temperature,velocity
and kinematics;there is no temperature rescaling or loop interpolation.

Define delta_ej=R4_ej-R3_ej for j=D,V,S.
Then R4_e-R3_e=sum_j delta_ej exactly.
The coherent norm may benefit from cancellation. The conservative
finite-difference diagnostic retains B_e=sum_j||delta_ej||/||R4_e||
using the full96-mode source mass-dual norm. It obeys
||R4_e-R3_e||/||R4_e|| <= B_e but B_e is NOT a demonstrated
upper bound on the omitted continuum quadrature error.

sources retained. Replacing ONLY the two new anchors by their seed3
companions gives

    Qc3=Qc-hL sum_j delta_early,j-hR sum_j delta_late,j.

The old three anchors and both compact-control terms do not change.
This variation is a test of two-epoch frequency sensitivity,not a new
central theory prediction or a source-accuracy claim at every time.

with the same zero extra initial state at.25,define X[F] as the response

    X[Qc]-X[Qc3]=sum_(e,j) X[h_e delta_ej].
