## 1. Differentiate the equation actually solved

Let the original support direction be (eta,z), with eta fixed on reference edges and z the previously derived nodal transport. Let w be the old field/source compensator. At the central instant the compensated direction is

    field = z - w_field, source = -w_source,
    eta_c = eta - D*w_source,
    d = field - eta_c*q_R.

The live projection residual is R = g - M*w + P[Y_d], where Y_d solves the radial metric response to the compensated velocity direction, and P maps this metric response into old canonical momentum. Equivalently L*w = g + P[Y_original], with L = M + P*A_old.

Differentiate R at fixed w along the **coupled** central time jet. Then

    L*wdot = (dR/dt)|w fixed.

This includes Mdot, Pdot and the derivative of the radial response, rather than declaring w constant. Invert the same live L using the established old Hessian as preconditioner. It is a local differentiated projection, not evolution of a finite support amplitude.

## 2. Mixed radial response

For Y_R = f(Y,E,G,H,rho,V), let J=f_Y and B_d=f_E*E_d+f_V*V_d. With w fixed:

    (Y_d)_R = J*Y_d + B_d,
    (Y_d_dot)_R = J*Y_d_dot + Jdot*Y_d + B_d_dot.

At fixed physical radius, obtain E_d_dot by differentiating both the moving label partition and the integrand 2*T*d. Include cut endpoint speeds, cardinal interpolation, reference inverse-map motion, q_R transport, source velocity acceleration, and z_dot. Obtain V_d_dot from the source inverse label map. Use one-sided source traces at radial-cell endpoints; do not identify incompatible velocities of coincident numerical interfaces.

The radial outer condition also differentiates. With B=R_outer-2*mu_outer:

    nu_d = -mu_d/B,
    nu_d_dot = -mu_d_dot/B - 2*mu_dot*mu_d/B^2.

The second term must not be dropped. The central physical outer radius and inner mass are fixed. Differentiate the continuum averaged-density equations on the fixed central partition, not a topology-changing grid algorithm.

## 3. Momentum residual derivative

At fixed material label and reference coordinate, set alpha=R^2/(N*sqrt(F)), W=D*V, r=mu_d/(R*F)-nu_d. The wave covector density is J_spatial*alpha*(d+T*r). Its derivative includes the geometric measure rate, d_dot, T_dot, r_dot and the changing source basis -D*q_R. The dust covector is I*(-w_source)+p_mu*mu_d+p_nu*nu_d; differentiate I, p_mu, p_nu and the response evaluated on the moving source, using the actual source acceleration.
