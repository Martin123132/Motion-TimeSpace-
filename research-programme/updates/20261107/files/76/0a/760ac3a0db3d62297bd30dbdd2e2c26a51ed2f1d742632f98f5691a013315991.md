# 25 - Conserved axial companion source and radiated power

5 October 2026. Private daughter of the bookmarked condensate branch.

## Result and scope

The actual circular companion source now drives the inherited axial body
response all the way to an outgoing gravitational-wave amplitude. Distributional
source conservation, the metric reconstruction, and the power normalization
are derived rather than adjusted to recover a desired answer. The wide-orbit
answer agrees with stage23's leading magnetic radiation in its test-mass limit.
The first relative post-Newtonian correction is also derived here.

We keep the same Einstein-Hilbert/G_R plus complex-scalar model, potential,
and isolated reference body. This step introduces no new body fit or new
background solution. These are conditional gravitational matter-response
predictions, in the inherited geometric length units, not an SI-calibrated
observational signal. This daughter does not replace the retained action.

Take G_R=c=1, body mass M, radius R, particle mass mu, and Schwarzschild
areal orbital radius D>R. Work to first order in mu in the perturbation, so
power is of order mu^2. The orbit is a circular test geodesic:

    F(r)=1-2M/r, Omega^2=M/D^3, u^t=(1-3M/D)^(-1/2), x=M/D.

In this document mu denotes the particle mass, NOT the finite-binary reduced
mass in stage23. We compare with that stage only as m_comp/M tends to zero.
Replacing M by total binary mass inside this fixed-background wave solver
would not supply the missing finite-mass terms.

## 1. Source convention and primary reference

[Martel and Poisson (2005)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/gr-qc/0502028),
[doi:10.1103/PhysRevD.71.104003](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.71.104003).

`sources/provenance.json` records its immutable hash and page extracts.

Their master satisfies psi=2U relative to stage24's U in a source-free
region. This factor is necessary for the radiation flux, not a selectable
normalization after computing a result. Let L=ell(ell+1), kappa=L-2.
The present orbit excites ell2,m=+/-1 in the axial quadrupole, at omega=Omega,
not 2Omega. For the positive-m mode,

    Y21=-sqrt(15/(8pi))*sin(theta)*cos(theta)*exp(i phi),
    X_phi(pi/2)=sqrt(15/(8pi))*exp(i phi),
    conjugate(X_phiphi)(pi/2)=-i*sqrt(15/(8pi))*exp(-i phi).

The ell2,m=0,+/-2 axial projections vanish on this circular equatorial source.

## 2. Conserved point source, including both distributions

Start with the invariant point-particle stress, in fully contravariant form:

    T^(alpha beta)=mu*u^alpha*u^beta/(u^t*r^2)
                   *delta(r-D)*delta(cos(theta))*delta(phi-Omega*t).

Martel-Poisson's projected Einstein sources are

    P^a=16pi*r^2/L * integral T^(aB)*conjugate(X_B) dOmega,
    P=16pi*r^4/(kappa*L) * integral T^(AB)*conjugate(X_AB) dOmega.

Direct harmonic integration gives, suppressing exp(-i omega t),

    A=(8pi/3)*mu*Omega*u^t*sqrt(15/(8pi)),
    P^t=A*delta(r-D), P^r=0,
    P=-i*omega*r^2*A*delta(r-D)/kappa, kappa=4.

Both geodesic normalization and radial acceleration vanish as required.
The projected Bianchi identity holds distributionally:

    partial_t P^t+partial_r P^r+2P^r/r-kappa*P/r^2=0.

With drstar/dr=1/F and V=F*(6/r^2-6M/r^3), the CPM equation is

    (partial_star^2+omega^2-V)*psi
        =(2A*r*F/kappa)*partial_r[F*delta(r-D)]
        =G_delta*delta(r-D)+F_delta_prime*delta_prime(r-D),
    F_delta_prime=2A*D*F_D^2/kappa,
    G_delta=-2A*F_D/kappa.

The coefficient of delta includes the derivative when multiplying delta-prime;
one cannot evaluate only its prefactor at D. Integrating the distributions
gives the jump pair, exterior minus interior:

    J=[psi]=2A*D/kappa,
    K=[partial_star psi]=-2A*F_D/kappa.

The intermediate relation is
G_delta=F_D*K-F_D*F'_D*J, not just F_D*K. Omitting the value jump changes
the flat leading amplitude by a factor1/4 and power by1/16, even though an
ordinary wave integration would still run successfully.

## 3. Reconstructed metric and the contact term

For P^r=0 the full reconstruction is

    h_t=F*(r*psi)'/2-r^2*F*P^t/kappa,
    h_r=r*partial_t(psi)/(2F).

The first term in h_t contains a delta because psi jumps. The explicit
P^t term cancels that delta exactly. Its removal is therefore not optional.
The regular part of h_t is continuous because (F_D*J+D*K)/2=0; h_r has
the jump required by P. Substitution into all three projected Einstein
equations and the CPM definition reproduces P^t,P^r,P for a general radial
source before specializing to the point distribution. The point-source
metric jumps provide a second direct distributional check.

`derive_source.py` performs these identities, harmonic normalizations, source
conservation, flux normalization, and the independent stage23 limit check.

## 4. Retarded matching and the fair reference

For the condensate, stage24 supplies the regular interior solution with
psi and partial_star psi continuous at R. The surface vector is normalized
to B_R=(1,Y_body/R). No fluid prescription or reflecting surface is added.

Independently integrate the same vacuum potential for an outgoing solution O
with unit exp(+i omega rstar) amplitude at infinity and an ingoing solution H
with unit exp(-i omega rstar) amplitude at the horizon of a same-mass black
hole. Both use the identical companion, geodesic, source jumps and power
normalization. Surface-normalized versions obey O_R=H_R=1. Denote by O0 the
unscaled outgoing value at R and W(V,O)=V*O_star-V_star*O. Then

    A_infinity(V)=[K*V(D)-J*V_star(D)]/[W(V,O)*O0].

For tiny body effects, do not subtract two almost equal computed amplitudes.
Use the exact algebraic difference

    e = A_body/A_BH-1
      =-W(B,H)*(K*O(D)-J*O_star(D))
        /[W(B,O)*(K*H(D)-J*H_star(D))].

`axial_binary.py` implements this identity, then independently propagates B
to D to cross-check the direct amplitude and both source jumps. Boundary
fluxes Im(conjugate(psi)*psi_star) are used to retain the very small imaginary
parts lost by naive log-derivative subtraction. Flat outgoing waves are
checked against exp(i omega r)*(1+3i/(omega r)-3/(omega r)^2).

The infinity power of the m=+/-1 pair is, from MP Eq6.16,

    P21=3*omega^2/(4pi)*abs(A_infinity)^2,
    Delta P21/P21_BH=2*Re(e)+abs(e)^2.

The baseline also has an independently computed horizon flux. The regular
conservative body's inner axial flux vanishes; it has no horizon absorption.
Thus Delta P_infinity and Delta(P_infinity+P_absorption) are DISTINCT.
Both are reported; only the former is the current-radiation comparison in
this document. Treating the black hole as nonabsorbing would silently change
that reference. Wave-current conservation is compatible with these choices:
at the source, [Im(conjugate(psi)*psi_star)] equals the work injected by J,K;
it feeds infinity plus the inward absorbed flux, or infinity alone for B.

## 5. Recovering the magnetic result without fitting it

The flat near-zone growing solution is g=r^3 and the unit-outgoing solution
has leading part -3/(omega^2*r^2). Their Wronskian is15/omega^2. Applying J,K
gives the ordinary point-current radiation

    P21_point=(8/45)*mu^2*M^4/D^6,
    P0=(32/5)*mu^2*M^3/D^5,
    P21_point/P0=x/36.

This agrees with an independent STF contraction of the orbital current
quadrupole J_ij=mu*D^3*Omega*n_<i*e_z,j>. Using U in the CPM flux formula
instead of psi would yield one quarter of this physical normalization.

Stage22's static metric has h_t=a*P+b*Q, R_B=b/a. Converting its basis to
the master gives g=r^3 and d=r^-2 with coefficient

    C_master=-4R_B=24*sigma_bar, sigma_bar=-R_B/6.

For the source numerators N_g=K*g-J*g_star and N_d=K*d-J*d_star,
the flat ratio is N_d/N_g=-1/(4D^5). Consequently, at leading order,

    e=-6*sigma_bar/D^5,
    Delta P21/P21=-12*sigma_bar/D^5,
    Delta P_infinity/P0=-x*sigma_bar/(3D^5).

Stage23 independently gave f_B=(114*(1-chi)-1)/(3chi). Its test-mass limit
chi->1 is precisely -1/3. The magnetic orbital-energy and induced mass-
quadrupole terms proportional to 1-chi disappear in this limit; that is why
the axial source alone can recover this particular limiting coefficient.
It cannot supply those terms for a finite mass ratio.

## 6. A further derived correction: first relative PN order

At fixed body, expand the vacuum equation to first order in M/r and omega^2*r^2:

    g=r^3*(1-omega^2*r^2/14)+higher orders,
    d=r^-2*(1+5M/(3r)+omega^2*r^2/6)+higher orders.

Substitution verifies both expansions. Their tortoise Wronskian remains -5
at this order. On the circular orbit, the source numerator ratio gives

    Re(e)/[-6*sigma_bar/D^5]=1+(275/84)*x+O(x^2),
    P21_BH/P21_point=1-(17/14)*x+O(x^(3/2)).

The long-range wave normalization first contributes a real propagation-tail
term beyond this instantaneous1PN order. Combining the two derived factors,

    Delta P_infinity/P0
      =-x*sigma_bar/(3D^5)*[1+(173/84)*x+O(x^(3/2))].

These are leading linear-response statements. Response-squared terms enter
the full wave power separately. Finite-frequency body terms are of relative
order (omega*R)^2, which scale as x^3 at fixed R/M in this wide-orbit limit;
they do not change the1PN coefficient. Horizon-response dissipation is not
assigned a static Love coefficient. No coefficient here was fitted to the
numerical ratios. `analyze_matching.py` derives and checks these fractions.
The remaining numerical residual scales consistently with x^(3/2), but we
do not extract or claim its coefficient from that observation.
