## 5. Derived fifth-power radiation law

For real frequency and a lossless interior, lambda_K is real. Algebra gives

    Im chi_ret = 3 Im(p/q) |chi_ret|².

There is no internal heating term here. The imaginary part represents outgoing
gravitational radiation. It can be tiny and must not be computed by subtracting
two almost equal floating-point fluxes.

In flat vacuum the exact outgoing l=2 solution is

    V = exp(i sigma r) [1+3i/(sigma r)-3/(sigma r)²].

Normalize its static decaying piece by multiplying by -sigma²/3:

    -sigma² V/3 = r^(-2) + sigma²/6 + sigma^4 r²/24

Since P_Z=r³/6, the leading mixing is Im(p/q)=(2/15)sigma^5. Hence

    Im(3p/q) = (2/5)sigma^5 + higher orders,
    Im chi_ret = (2/5)lambda_static² sigma^5 + higher orders.

For fixed Schwarzschild mass, the matched low-frequency region M<<r<<1/sigma
uses the same asymptotic normalization and static Wronskian. This supplies the
formal leading curved-space law, not a theorem excluding every subleading tail
or logarithm. Direct curved calculations approach the predicted coefficient .4:
at sigma=5e-5, 3 Im(p/q)/sigma^5 is .40003694, .40003654 and .40003598 in the three
conventions. At b=2R, Im chi/(lambda_static² sigma^5)=.40005020.
