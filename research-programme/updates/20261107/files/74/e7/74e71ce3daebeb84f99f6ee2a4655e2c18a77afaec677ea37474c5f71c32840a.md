## Moving cuts: the required extra term is derived

The earlier regular-flux integration-by-parts identity must be supplemented
when a represented baseline flux jumps at a moving artificial cut:

    delta F = regular flux volume/jump/outer terms
              +[Phi]*dot(xi_c)*N_xi(xi_c).

At a fixed physical cut, dot(xi_c)=-U/J. Even when baseline Phi is
continuous, its regular derivative H can jump; then
[H]+[Phi_xi]*dot(xi_c)=0. These are different terms and neither may be
dropped by conflating baseline continuity with derivative continuity.

Twenty exact manufactured checks pass for continuous and discontinuous
families, including negative controls that omit required terms. This does
not certify numerical continuity of the stored metric traces or constitute
an implementation of the actual force in flux form.
