# Stage74 — polarized radiation, finite plasma heat, and the gravity source

This extends the existing MTS parent and Stage73 physical scattering kernel.
It does not replace the action or refit its coefficients. Standard Compton
microphysics and the previously sourced plasma composition are inherited
inputs. The new calculation is a local linear collision evolution in a
frozen background, expressed per unit Thomson optical depth.

## 1. Exact acceleration of the spin-channel calculation

Let h = [1 + epsilon(1-mu_*)]^-1 be the electron-rest recoil ratio,
t = h + h^-1 - 2, and J the real overlap of the transported polarization
screens. For a Hermitian incident occupation matrix X, the spin-summed map
derived from Stage73's explicit Dirac channels is

    G(X) = J X J^T
           + t/4 [tr(X) 1 + mu_* tr(sigma_y X) sigma_y].

The second map is completely positive for |mu_*| <= 1: its Choi eigenvalues
are proportional to 1 +/- mu_*. Its coefficient t is nonnegative.
This is a computational factorization of the inherited tree-level
amplitude, not an expansion in temperature or a fitted rate.
Independent spin-channel comparisons and angular checks are retained in
`results/angular-blocks/status.json`.

The event includes the normalized Juttner electron distribution, incident
relative flux, exact recoil, Lorentz boosts of directions and screens, and
the equilibrium Bose ratio (1+n_j)/(1+n_i). The reciprocal weak form is
inherited from Stage73's physical forward/reverse and matrix-induced
scattering derivation. Pair symmetrization is an assembly of that form,
not an assertion that a sampled forward grid is exactly a reverse grid.

## 2. All polarization sectors without an angular closure

Let P = 1 - nn^T, e_ij = epsilon_ijk n_k, and mu = n.u for a unit mode
axis u. For each angular degree l use

    T_I = P_l(mu) P,
    T_E = 2 sqrt[(l-2)!/(l+2)!] P_l''(mu) TT[(P u)(P u)],
    T_B = (e T_E - T_E e)/2,
    T_V = -i P_l(mu) e.

E and B exist only for l >= 2. Their angular trace inner products obey

    <tr(T_a T_b)> = 2 delta_ab/(2l+1).

An isotropic electron bath makes the collision map rotation invariant.
It acts independently on each irreducible l sector and equally on each
magnetic index m. Integrating the direction of u extracts this block from
one representative, including its I/E/B/V multiplicity; it does not
average away linear or circular polarization. Tensor products are degree
2l polynomials in u, so the chosen axis quadrature is exact for them.
The implementation also compares a separately refined axis quadrature.

For one event define the rotationally averaged cross block C_ab and
self coefficient d:

    C_ab = <tr[T_a(i) G*(T_b(j))]>_u = C0_ab + t C1_ab,
    d = (1+mu_*^2+t)/(2l+1).

The reciprocal contribution to the quadratic form is

    -1/2 integral positive_weight
       [d (|f_i|^2+|f_j|^2) - 2 Re(f_i^dagger C f_j)].

Physical channel positivity bounds each singular value of C by d.
Consequently positive quadrature and congruent finite-element projection
preserve nonpositive dissipation. The constant I monopole is a photon
chemical-potential null vector. Tiny cross entries below 1e-14 are omitted
and explicitly recorded as a numerical threshold, not a physical rule.
Actual matrices and null residuals are tested without eigenvalue clipping.

## 3. Frequency projection and the quadrature repair

Use x = photon energy/(k_B T_*), n = 1/(exp(x)-1), w = n(1+n), and
delta f = w X. Each component of X is piecewise linear in x. With
I3 = pi^4/15 its mass matrix is

    (M_l)_ij = integral x^2 w phi_i phi_j dx / [I3(2l+1)].

The mass and energy moments use the same basis. Constants and x are
represented exactly. The initial implementation integrated each incoming
frequency cell but did not split cells at outgoing interpolation knots.
The large cold signal converged much more easily than its small thermal
correction. All unsuccessful small-response refinement trials are retained
in `results/response-refinements/status.json`, not reclassified as passes.

For a fixed physical event its outgoing energy is

    y(x) = A x/(1+B x),
    A = incident_boost * outgoing_boost > 0,
    B = theta * incident_boost * (1-mu_*) >= 0.

An outgoing knot y_k has preimage x_k = y_k/(A-B y_k) when its denominator
is positive. Split each frequency integral at BOTH incoming knots and
these preimages. On every resulting segment both endpoint interpolants
are smooth functions of x. Positive Gauss quadrature then integrates the
same physical pair form without stepping across an unaccounted kink.
`build_partitioned_bank.py` implements this change; the earlier banks and
scripts remain untouched. A wider band moves the artificial zero-flux
edge away from the response. This is a quadrature/domain repair, not a
change in scattering physics or a subtraction of a fitted counterterm.
Discarded-event weight is recorded, not called an operator-norm bound.

## 4. Dynamical finite heat capacity and common equilibrium

Let r = delta T_e/T_* and v the plasma velocity component along u. Define
q_l as the vector with x in its intensity component and zero elsewhere.
For l=0 use capacity c=chi; for l=1 use c=B_plasma:

    chi = (3/2) n_total k_B T_*/rho_gamma,
    B_plasma = rho_b/rho_gamma + 5 chi/3.

The symbol chi here denotes a sourced gas heat capacity, not an additional
MTS field. The gas is the inherited nondegenerate, nonrelativistic,
fully ionized shared-temperature plasma. The exact photon kernel does
not turn this leading ideal-gas thermodynamics into all-order plasma QED.

If K_l is the negative physical stiffness and M includes all polarization
components, then for l=0 or 1:

    M X' = K_l (X-q_l z),
    c z' = -q_l^T K_l (X-q_l z),   z=r or v.

Equivalently W Y' = K_full Y with

    W = diag(M,c),
    K_full = [1,-q_l]^T K_l [1,-q_l].

The combined moment q_l^T M X+c z is conserved. The photon chemical
moment is separately conserved for l=0. Quadratic entropy satisfies

    H' = Re[(X-q_l z)^dagger K_l (X-q_l z)] <= 0.

Whitening by W, separating the ANALYTIC null vectors, and exponentiating
the remaining symmetric matrix evolves the finite-capacity system. No
instantaneous-temperature closure or refitted equilibrium is used.
Raw null residuals are tested before eliminating analytic null coordinates.
This is exact semidiscrete frozen-operator propagation; it is not a test
of a full expanding-background integration scheme.

Saved parent scalar modes have a common-temperature monopole initially;
that monopole is therefore a null control, not evidence of nonzero heat
exchange. The separate nonthermal synthetic fixture deliberately excites
heat exchange and every I/E/B/V sector. Its energy balance is normalized
to the tiny EXCHANGED heat, not the much larger photon bath.

## 5. The source that must enter the retained gravity equations

For a normalized angular tensor expansion the bolometric tensor is

    B_gamma(n) = sum_la T_la(n) integral x^3 w X_la(x) dx/(2 I3).

Its angular trace, first moment, and second moment give radiation energy,
flux, and spatial stress in the parent's existing conventions.
The l=0 and l=1 moments equal q_l^T M_l X_l.
This equality is checked through an independent angular reconstruction.

The local gas tensor is the perfect-fluid tensor

    T_gas^(mu nu) = (rho_rest+u+p) U^mu U^nu + p g^(mu nu),
    u = (3/2) n_total k_B T,  p = 2u/3.

At fixed particle density and fixed reference background its linear
thermal source contribution is

    delta rho_gas/rho_gamma = chi r,
    delta j_gas^(orthonormal)/rho_gamma = B_plasma v,
    delta S_gas/rho_gamma = (2 chi r/3) 1.

For general perturbations one must also keep
delta u = u(delta n/n + delta T/T), not merely the temperature piece.
The fixed-density test here sets the extra density perturbation to zero.

The parent Hamiltonian and momentum constraints are evaluated read-only:

    C_H = tr(Ricci) + 4 H tr(metric_rate) - 2a^2 delta rho,
    C_M = i k tr(metric_rate) - i k.metric_rate - a delta j_cov.

During a collision-only step geometry is fixed. Opposite photon/plasma
energy and momentum sources give C_H'=0 and C_M'=0. The plasma pressure
also has to enter the spatial Einstein evolution; it cannot be omitted
just because it does not appear in these two constraint source moments.
`verify_stress_interface.py` tests both instantaneous sources and finite
exchanges through the actual inherited constraint methods, with deliberate
heat/enthalpy-omission controls.

## 6. Derived route to the next coupled propagation

Write the radiation source as -Q^nu and gas source as +Q^nu, with
Q^nu = Q U^nu + F^nu, U.F=0. Projecting the gas divergence onto U and
using number conservation gives

    dot(rho_rest)+rho_rest Theta = 0,
    dot(u)+(u+p)Theta = Q,
    (3/2)n_total k_B dot(T)+p Theta = Q.

For homogeneous FLRW:

    u' + 5 H u = a Q,
    rho_gamma' + 4 H rho_gamma = -a Q,
    rho_rest' + 3 H rho_rest = 0.

The source cancels in TOTAL stress conservation; gas heat redshifts with
its own pressure rather than behaving as pressureless rest mass.

and then repair the resulting constraint residual by projection.
