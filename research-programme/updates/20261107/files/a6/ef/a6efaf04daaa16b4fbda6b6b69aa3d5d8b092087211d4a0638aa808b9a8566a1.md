## What was derived

The full finite-energy scattering amplitude has an exact representation using
nine energy-independent angular tensors. The spin sum becomes five small Gram
matrices. This is an algebraic rearrangement, not a recoil/energy truncation.
Across 9,180 signed events, the largest tensor discrepancy is 9.22e-16 and the
largest intensity discrepancy is 3.67e-15. Actual-source replay and photon
exchange also pass. The cached intensity benchmark is 15.7 times faster;
angular preprocessing is excluded, so this is not a whole-run speed claim.

The derivation and its limits are in `DERIVATION.md`, with evidence in
`results/exact-factor/status.json` and `results/fast-source/status.json`.

## A targeted fix, tested rather than merely proposed

The recoil/motion control-minus-leading term dominates the observed change.
Its change has about 98.56% of the norm of the total remainder change; this is
a norm ratio, not an additive variance attribution.

The original basis is built in z=(x-0.1)/(x+0.1). Its incident Gram matrix
cannot be resolved in all 96 directions by only 14–28 incident samples.
That observation motivates integrating in the basis coordinate with its
exact Jacobian, without deleting any mode or changing a physical function.

A pilot retains one actual antipodal electron pair with equal weights,
all three hard angles and the full four-profile/96-test control. It uses
96, 128 and 160 compact-coordinate incoming nodes:

| 96 to 128 nodes | 0.09842% |
| 128 to 160 nodes | 0.00008225% |

All eight pilot checks pass, including event energy exchange. This is a
successful numerical-method pilot, not a completed full thermal-population
source. Its electron measure and restriction to control-minus-leading are
explicit in `runs/compact-control-pilot/status.json`. Its finest diagnostic
is `runs/compact-control-pilot/compact-160.npz`.

## How to finish this repair affordably

Linearity permits the exact source decomposition R=K+D+V+S to use separate
numerical resolutions for the cheap K and expensive D/V/S integrals. The
current seed3-to4 differences for those expensive pieces, divided by the
still-unresolved seed4 remainder norm, are:

| Piece | Observed difference / remainder norm |
|---|---:|
| Exact-amplitude difference D | 0.61019% |
| Virtual V | 0.13074% |
| State S | 0.003929% |
| Triangle sum | 0.74485% |

These are not continuum-error bounds and their denominator must be recomputed
after the fine control is known. They show why simply repeating every costly
loop on a much larger grid is not the best first move. The budget and exact
additive reconstruction are in `results/multirate-budget/status.json`.
