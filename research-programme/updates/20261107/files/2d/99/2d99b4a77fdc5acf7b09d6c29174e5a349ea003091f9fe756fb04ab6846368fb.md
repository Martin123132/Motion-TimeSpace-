## 2. Derive the integrated equations

Carry forward stage35's source-owned circular shrinkage P(p) and near-circular
damping Gamma(p):

    p_dot=mu P(p),
    z_dot=mu Gamma(p) z,
    phi_dot=Omega_phi(p)=1/[M p^(3/2)].

P and Gamma have dimensions length^-2; p and z are dimensionless. Thus

    dT/dp=1/[M^2 P(p)],
    dPhi/dp=Omega_phi/[M P(p)],
    d ln z/dp=Gamma(p)/P(p).

Since P<0, integrating from larger to smaller p gives positive elapsed time.
The initial-value solution is

    T(p)=integral[p_initial,p] dp'/[M^2 P(p')],
    Phi(p)=integral[p_initial,p] Omega_phi(p') dp'/[M P(p')],
    z(p)=z_initial exp{integral[p_initial,p] Gamma(p')/P(p') dp'}.

The model therefore supplies the evolution, not merely a sequence of
unconnected local damping numbers.

### Stable same-source differences

Let P_B=P_Hole+DeltaP and Gamma_B=Gamma_Hole+DeltaGamma. Here the subscript
Hole means total BH loss; horizon-only terms are explicitly called absorption.
Then

    Delta(1/P)=-DeltaP/[P_Hole(P_Hole+DeltaP)],
    Delta(Gamma/P)=[DeltaGamma P_Hole-Gamma_Hole DeltaP]
                  /[P_Hole(P_Hole+DeltaP)].

Compute these small quantities directly, not by subtracting large integrated
times or accumulated phases. The outgoing and absorbed contributions remain
separate until this projection:

    DeltaP=P_outgoing_difference-P_absorption,
    DeltaGamma=Gamma_outgoing_difference-Gamma_absorption.

The same data also permit a null control: identical effective responses give
exactly zero differential time, phase and eccentricity kernels.

### A genuine conditional history-error inequality

For an ordinary integrable retarded kernel after treating any instantaneous
local terms separately, put

    q(t)=integral[0,infinity] K(s) A(t-s) exp[-i phi(t-s)] ds,
    chi(omega)=integral[0,infinity] K(s) exp(i omega s) ds,
    M_j=integral[0,infinity] s^j |K(s)| ds.

If the relevant past derivative bounds L_A=sup|A_dot| and
L_omega=sup|omega_dot| hold and M1,M2 are finite, Taylor's integral remainder
and |exp(ix)-1|<=|x| give

    |q(t)-A(t)exp[-i phi(t)]chi(omega(t))|
        <= L_A M1 + (|A(t)|/2) L_omega M2.

For one exponentially damped kernel with magnitude |a|exp(-gamma s),
M_j=|a| j!/gamma^(j+1). These moments are explicitly checked.
An arbitrary surviving free oscillation adds another term and is not
suppressed by slow orbital drift.

The full coupled kernel's absolute moments are not numerically bounded here.
The known-pole indicators support the assumed adiabatically prepared,
nonresonant history; they are not a certified error bound for every continuum
tail or unclassified mode. No arbitrary initial ringing is included in this
trajectory. A sudden switch-on would be a different physical problem.
