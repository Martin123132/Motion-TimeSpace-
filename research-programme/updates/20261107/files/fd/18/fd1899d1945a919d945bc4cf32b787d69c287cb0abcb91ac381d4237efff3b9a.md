# The first gravitational response of the expanded microscopic source

## Retained source and its physical meaning

Stage240 supplies the mixed weak matrices J_l(B) and C2(B)=J_0(B)B/2.
This child imports, rather than rederives, that retained Taylor-order
source, Stage231's EOS, and Stage238's weak-to-physical normalization.
The physical time is conformal eta; c=8piG=1; the same calibrated opacity
kappa=a n_e sigma_T and source weights are used. No new coupling is fitted.

The rectangular photon/gas injection F_l is

    q_l'=(2l+1) kappa M^-1 J_l[radial,:] W_l,
    r'=kappa J_0[gas,:]W_0/(chi C),
    v'=kappa J_1[gas,:]W_1/B_g.

The independently integrated photon and gas rows must still cancel at l0
and l1. The photon energy readout is Stage238's same-mass high/low row;
the photon solution and gas recoil are not modified to enforce cancellation.

Let E_F,J_F,S_TF,S_LF be the *rates of change of stresses* produced by this
state injection, evaluated with the native retained stress rows. These are
not stresses assigned instantaneously to an unchanged state. In particular,
balanced collisions give E_F=J_F=0 but can have nonzero pressure and shear:

    P_F=rho_star (CE/3+2 CgasE/(3C)),
    Pi_F=rho_star kappa J_2[energy,:]W_2/3,
    S_TF=P_F-Pi_F,  S_LF=P_F+2Pi_F.

For balanced monopole exchange with CgasE=-CE this becomes

    P_F=rho_star CE (C-2)/(3C).

The pressure response is fixed by radiation pressure and the independently
derived gas heat capacity, not an adjustable gravitational source. In the
retained low-temperature limit C->1 it tends to -rho_star CE/3.

## Derived local metric response: distinguish a rate from a stress

Take the difference from the unchanged incoming solution, with delta y(0)=0,
and switch on the specified additive source F at eta0. The native metric
equations contain

    h_T'=2 sigma_T, h_L'=2 sigma_L,
    sigma_T'=a^2(delta rho-S_L)/2 + metric-rate terms,
    sigma_L'=a^2(delta rho+S_L-2S_T)/2 + metric-rate terms.

The source acts on photons/gas, not directly on metric or scalar fields.
Consequently delta h'=delta h''=delta sigma'=0 initially. Differentiation
of these actual equations gives the first nonzero source-owned derivatives:

    delta sigma_T''(0)=a0^2(E_F-S_LF)/2,
    delta sigma_L''(0)=a0^2(E_F+S_LF-2S_TF)/2,
    delta h_T'''(0)=a0^2(E_F-S_LF),
    delta h_L'''(0)=a0^2(E_F+S_LF-2S_TF).

Thus delta h_T=a0^2(E_F-S_LF) Delta_eta^3/6+O(Delta_eta^4),
and similarly for h_L. Time-dependent coefficients and source derivatives
first enter the displayed metric expansion at higher order because the
incoming response vanishes. This is a local Taylor statement assuming the
required smoothness, not a finite-time error estimate or a frozen-background
cosmological solution. No numerical cutoff or damping gap is inserted.

For the balanced monopole the two cubic coefficients equal

    -a0^2 rho_star CE (C-2)/(18C).

For a pure quadrupole they are -a0^2 Pi_F/3 and a0^2 Pi_F/3 times2,
so2 delta h_T+delta h_L has zero leading trace. This explains how an
energy-balanced microscopic exchange can source a gravitational response
through pressure/shear without an invented net-energy injection.

The instantaneous constraint-rate injection is exactly

    (C_H', C_M')|F=(-2a0^2 E_F, -a0^2 J_F).

It vanishes for a genuinely balanced direct source. Background/off-shell
residuals retain Stage231's separately sourced forcing; they are not
projected out. Later constraint propagation needs the actual transport and
background derivatives, not merely this instantaneous identity.

## Density and background-transfer columns

One incoming electron gives density injection kappa C2(B)b. Its direct gas
rate plus the per-particle background term Q2 b/(chi C) cancels, where
Q2=kappa C2[photon energy]. This is checked with the independent gas row.
The remaining background-transfer tangents are retained explicitly:

    d r'|Q=Q2 b/(chi C)+C_R Q2 r/(chi C^2),
    d v'|Q=(1+2/(3C))Q2 v/B_g.

They are not a second copy of the microscopic relative-velocity column.
If an unchanged background slope is used, f_R=Q2/(chi C) produces the
known energy/current defect. The new density and feedback columns must be
kept distinct from the balanced direct-source jets above.

## Scope of the next propagation

The six microscopic backgrounds used to form J_l are saved input vectors,
not automatically their on-shell evolution at each anchor temperature.
Interpolating the three temperature packets and calling that a physical
source history would silently add an assumption. Preserve the local jets
as exact coefficients of the named source. A subsequent finite-interval
calculation must supply the actual evolving background, explicit tails and
matched transport, and carry its residuals through the forced constraints.
