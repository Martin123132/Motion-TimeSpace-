# A radial instability located within one fixed theory

3 October 2026. Private condensate-origin daughter; earlier work preserved.

## Physical result

Keeping both the potential and gravitational coupling fixed, we have
continued the compact condensate bodies to a resolved radial instability.
The fundamental mode changes from an oscillation to a growing mode. This
happens while the metric is well away from a horizon, not because a horizon
or a numerical failure was declared to be the endpoint.

Two calculations meet at the transition:

- The equilibrium mass and charge reach a maximum near central amplitude
  **h(0) = 1.9843**.
- The actual radial pulsation frequency approaches zero at that location
  as the spatial mesh is refined. Its eigenfunction agrees with the
  independently differenced equilibrium-family deformation.

The action supplies the link: **de/ds = w dq/ds**. Differentiating its
stationarity condition shows why the family tangent becomes a
charge-preserving Hessian/pulsation zero at a charge turning point.
The compact moving edge is included in the domain argument.

This is a numerical linear-stability boundary for this retained
Einstein–complex-scalar candidate, not an observation of a real object or
a demonstration that an unstable body forms a black hole. The original
real-field/bath programme and its pre-condensate bookmark remain intact.

## What the checks establish—and their resolution limits

- Five symbolic identity checks support the written first-law/turning-point
  derivation. The zero-mode implication has explicit regularity assumptions.
- 49 converged, integral-gated equilibrium rows were saved, including repeats
  and numerical controls. They are not49 independent physical predictions.
- 16 rows from the final cleaned fine-mesh runs support the critical-window
  results. Their direct scalar and angular second-derivative residuals are
  below4.0e-6 and3.73e-5 respectively in the reported normalization.
- The33 earlier rows are exploratory. Some coarse spline angular
  second-derivative residuals reach1.34 despite accurate integral constraints.
  Those rows do not certify pointwise curvature accuracy. The clean-mesh
  critical calculation supersedes them where they overlap.
- 18 additional signed spectral calculations and four turning-tangent mesh
  calculations check resolution and formulation. Changing quadrature order
  or coarse/fine background changes squared frequencies by less than2.6e-13
  at the compared512-cell meshes. Spatial discretization dominates instead.
- Near zero, the largest recorded scaled eigen-equation residual reaches
  0.0071. Its denominator is floored at1e-7; it is not a uniform relative
  error bound at an exact zero. The narrow-bracket residual reaches0.00155.
- The family tangent has an excellent low-mode overlap and a vanishing
  Rayleigh value, but its strong pulsation-action norm is still about4.92e-4
  at the finest smaller-step test. We do not claim strong-norm convergence
  or a certified continuum kernel from overlap alone.

The coarse192-cell result at h(0)=2 was slightly positive. Refinement
reverses its sign. This is precisely why coarse positive modes were not
treated as final evidence.
