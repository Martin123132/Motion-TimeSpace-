# 46 — Phase energy bound and analytic polar factorization

6 October 2026. Private/local daughter of stage45. Inherited action unchanged.

## Result and scope

The phase-momentum block of the **already gravity-constrained** Hamiltonian now
has a uniform analytical lower bound, not just positive finite matrices:

\[
 E_{\pi\pi}\geq\frac{77}{200}\int a\,\pi_v^2dr,\qquad a=Nr^2/\sqrt F.
\]

Its Hessian retains at least 77% of the reference phase norm in an explicit
dimensionless background box containing the saved R20/C0.03 solution.
This proves the invertibility needed for the actual nonlocal phase Schur
completion, rather than completing a local square while freezing the metric.

Further derived ingredients are a positive-factorization bound on the bare
amplitude form, and an off-shell reduction of the vacuum metric energy to the
positive polar master form **with its inner-surface contact retained**.
The unfinished joint estimate is the interior gravity-amplitude Schur form,
including metric backreaction and that surface contact.

These results concern the retained Einstein-condensate candidate in its
finite-energy quadratic domain. They are not empirical calibrations or a new
derivation of the Einstein-Hilbert action/G_R. No new field, background or
outgoing-wave solves, and no fitted parameters, were used.

## 2. Local phase completion changes the constraint

Set t=H/2+K and sigma=pi_v-sqrt(b)(2u+ht). The charge/potential terms become

\[
 E_Q+E_W=\int a\left[\frac{\sigma^2}{2}+\frac{W_{hh}-b}{2}u^2
 +(W_h-bh)ut+(W-bh^2/2)(HK/2-H^2/8)\right]dr.
\]

K depends on pi_v. Since t=Q_geom/2+3K/2, the transformed constraint is

\[
 \widetilde{\mathcal D}_lK=-\frac F rQ_{\rm geom}'
 -[4/r^2-\alpha(2W+bh^2)]Q_{\rm geom}
 +2\alpha[(W_h+bh)u+Fh'u'+\sqrt b\,h\sigma],
\]
\[
 \widetilde{\mathcal D}_l=\mathcal D_l-3\alpha bh^2.
\]

Using the background identity D_0N=0, with w0=r^2/sqrt(F),

\[
 \int w_0f\widetilde{\mathcal D}_lf\,dr
 =\int w_0[FN^2((f/N)')^2+(6/r^2-3\alpha bh^2)f^2]dr.
\]

The bound below leaves a positive 27/(5r^2) angular remainder. This establishes
a well-defined change of variables on regular/decaying constraint solutions.
It does **not** make sigma the final minimizing phase variable: the remaining
metric energy still contains K(sigma). Freezing K would omit an operator term.

## 3. Background inequalities without a spatial scan

Assume a regular nontrivial static solution with F,N>0, alpha>0, h>0 on 0<r<R,
h(R)=h'(R)=0, no material surface shell, and the stated potential. Write

\[
 F=1-2m/r,\quad m'=\alpha r^2\rho,\quad
 P=\tfrac12(Fh'^2+bh^2)-W,\quad \rho=\tfrac12(Fh'^2+bh^2)+W,
 \quad n=\frac{m+\alpha r^3P}{r^2F}.
\]

The exact pressure balance is

\[
 P'=-2Fh'^2/r-n(Fh'^2+bh^2).
\]

To avoid circularly assuming n>=0, first write

\[
 P'+\frac{\alpha r}{F}(Fh'^2+bh^2)P
 =-\frac{2F}{r}h'^2-\frac{m}{r^2F}(Fh'^2+bh^2)\leq0.
\]

Integrating backward from P(R)=0 with the positive integrating factor proves
P>=0. Hence n>=0, P is nonincreasing outward, b is nonincreasing, and
P<=P(0), b<=b(0).

### Why the scalar profile decreases

This sign can also be derived. Let g(h)=W_h/h=(h^(-2/3)+h^2)/2.
At a positive stationary minimum, h'=0 and h''>=0 require b<=g(h).
P>=0 requires b>=2W/h^2. Compatibility gives

\[
 2W/h^2-g(h)=\tfrac14(h^{-2/3}-h^2)\leq0,\quad h\geq1.
\]

On h>=1, g'(h)>0. Once such a minimum starts increasing, g(h)-b(r) cannot
turn negative: h increases while b decreases. The flux equation
(Nr^2 sqrt(F) h')'=a h[g(h)-b(r)] prevents a subsequent maximum or arrival at
h(R)=0. Thus no stationary minimum is possible.

A solution increasing from the center has the same obstruction. In the
degenerate central case g(h_c)=b_c, the r^2 scalar coefficient vanishes, but the
r^4 coefficient is positive for alpha>0 because b decreases quadratically from
the center. That branch also starts increasing and cannot be compact.
The positive compact solution therefore starts decreasing and has no first
interior turning point: h'<0 throughout 0<r<R. This argument does not cover
sign-changing or noncompact branches.

### Explicit uniform box

\[
 0<\alpha\leq1/5000,\qquad R\leq20,\qquad b(0)\leq11/10.
\]

At a maximum of h, the field equation gives W_h/h<=b(0).
Since W_h/h>=h^2/2, h^2<=11/5. Monotonicity of W gives W<5/4.
A rational certificate is (11/5)^(2/3)<43/25, since
(11/5)^2<(43/25)^3, and 3(43/25)/8+(11/5)^2/8=5/4.
Consequently

\[
 P\leq b_ch_c^2/2\leq121/100,\quad
 Fh'^2\leq2P+2W\leq123/25,\quad \rho\leq123/25,
\]
\[
 F\geq1-\frac{2\alpha R^2}{3}\frac{123}{25}\geq461/625.
\]

Also n<=alpha r(rho_max/3+P_max)/F, so

\[
 3\alpha r^2bh^2\leq363/625<3/5,
\]
\[
 2\alpha r^2(Fh'^2+W)+r^2Fn^2
 \leq1218973/1152500<53/50.
\]

These are analytical majorants, not maxima inferred from samples.
The saved model has alpha=0.000196620, R=19.7316622, b_c=1.0690278563,
well inside the box. Membership uses its numerical background parameters.
This work does not provide a new interval existence enclosure; the theorem is
conditional on a regular solution satisfying the stated box, not on a mesh.

## 4. Bare scalar energy factorization

Set zeta=sqrt(F)h', which has no zero in the open body. Differentiating the
background equation gives

\[
 \mathcal L_+\zeta=\mathcal B_l\zeta,\quad
 \mathcal L_+=-a^{-1}\partial_r(aF\partial_r)+W_{hh}-b+L/r^2,
\]
\[
 \mathcal B_l=(L-2F)/r^2-2\alpha(Fh'^2+W)-Fn^2-2bn\,h/h'.
\]

The sqrt(F) cancels an otherwise troublesome F'h''/h' term at the edge.
The energy identity is

\[
 \int a[Fu'^2+(W_{hh}-b+L/r^2)u^2]dr
 =\int a[F\zeta^2((u/\zeta)')^2+\mathcal B_lu^2]dr.
\]

Since h'<0, n>=0 and F<=1, the box inequalities give

\[
 \mathcal B_2\geq147/(50r^2).
\]

The endpoint contact a(zeta'/zeta)u^2 vanishes: O(r^5) at the center and
O((R-r)^3) for moving-edge u=O((R-r)^2), h=O((R-r)^3).
The identity extends by closure to the associated finite-energy form domain.
This controls the **bare amplitude piece**, not the final scalar Schur block,
which still includes induced metric contributions.

## 5. Actual constrained phase block

Return to original canonical variables and set Q_geom=u=0. The metric responds:
H=K and D_lK=2alpha sqrt(b)h pi_v. Keeping every stage45 term, integrating its
metric cross derivative, and using the background equations gives

\[
 E_{\pi\pi}=\tfrac12\|\pi_v\|_a^2-\frac7{8\alpha}I
 +\frac18\int a(7bh^2+5W)K^2dr,
\]
\[
 I=\int aK\mathcal D_lK\,dr
 =\int a[FK'^2+(6/r^2+\alpha(bh^2-W))K^2]dr.
\]

D_l is naturally symmetric with weight w0, not a. The extra lapse derivative
is included in this I identity. The exact elliptic tail is included; finite
surface contacts are joined and the endpoint contacts vanish at 0 and infinity.
The box bounds imply

\[
 I\geq(59/10)\int aK^2/r^2dr.
\]

Multiply the constraint by aK and apply Cauchy-Schwarz:

\[
 I\leq\frac{4\alpha^2\sup_{r<R}(r^2bh^2)}{59/10}\|\pi_v\|_a^2.
\]

Discard only the manifestly nonnegative last term of E_pi_pi. As
alpha sup(r^2bh^2)<=121/625,

\[
 E_{\pi\pi}\geq
 \frac12\left(1-\frac{7(121/625)}{59/10}\right)\|\pi_v\|_a^2
 =\frac12\frac{5681}{7375}\|\pi_v\|_a^2
 >\frac{77}{200}\|\pi_v\|_a^2.
\]

This is a bound on the **actual phase Hessian C after solving gravity**, not
on a frozen-metric term. The elliptic estimates and this coercivity give a
bounded phase inverse on its energy space. This norm uses a; the finite
matrices below use the parent canonical Gram weight w0.

For z=(Q_geom,u), the valid nonlocal completion is therefore

\[
 E_{\rm even}=\tfrac12\langle\pi_v+C^{-1}B^\dagger z,
 C(\pi_v+C^{-1}B^\dagger z)\rangle
 +\tfrac12\langle z,(A-BC^{-1}B^\dagger)z\rangle.
\]

The domains are the finite-energy domains of the inherited constrained forms.

## 6. Off-shell vacuum energy with exact contact

In the exterior F=1-2M/r, N=sqrt(F), define

\[
 Z=\frac r3[K+F(H-rK')/(2+3M/r)].
\]

The spatial constraint, without a frequency or wave equation, gives

\[
 K=FZ'+cZ,\quad c=\frac{6M^2+6Mr+6r^2}{r^2(2r+3M)},\quad
 H=rK'+\frac{2+3M/r}{F}(3Z/r-K).
\]

The exact vacuum energy is

\[
 E_{\rm vac}=\frac3{4\alpha}\int[FZ'^2+(V_Z/F)Z^2]dr
 +[\mathcal C_Z/(4\alpha)]_{\rm endpoints},
\]
\[
 V_Z=F\,\frac{24r^3+24Mr^2+36M^2r+18M^3}{r^3(2r+3M)^2}.
\]

The bulk is positive for r>2M, M>=0; its normalization matches stage44's
3/(2alpha) kinetic normalization. The exact contact polynomial is recorded in
`results/vacuum/status.json` and derived by `derive_vacuum_energy.py`.
For M=0, C_Z=9Z^2/r-r(Z'+3Z/r)^2.

**Retain the finite inner contact.** An exterior beginning at the body surface
is not an isolated positive ADM subsystem. Independent arbitrary off-shell
decaying profiles give:

| Profile | Exterior ADM | Positive master bulk | Inner contact |
|---|---:|---:|---:|

| (R/r)^3+0.2(R/r)^4 | -251.1497217 | 591.2901600 | 842.4398817 |

ADM=bulk-inner_contact agrees to 2.3e-14 relative. Omitting the contact fails

## 7. Remaining joint estimate

After phase completion write

\[
 S=A-BC^{-1}B^\dagger=
 \begin{pmatrix}G&T\\T^\dagger&A_u\end{pmatrix}.
\]

If G and A_u are positive in their energy spaces, the useful continuum target is

\[
 \eta_{\rm mix}=\sup_{q,u\ne0}
 \frac{|\langle q,Tu\rangle|}
 {\langle q,Gq\rangle^{1/2}\langle u,A_uu\rangle^{1/2}}<1.
\]

Then S retains at least (1-eta_mix) of its diagonal forms. Minimizing over the
gravitational coordinate leaves at least (1-eta_mix^2) of the phase-reduced
scalar diagonal form. This energy-space statement does not assume an exterior
spectral gap. Eta_mix is a diagnostic norm, not a fitted coupling or Gamma field.
