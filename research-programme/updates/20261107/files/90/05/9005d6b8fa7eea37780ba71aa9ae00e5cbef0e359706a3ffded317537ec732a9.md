# Stage157 — frequency-resolved correlated virtual source

Let V_J,e,a(x) be the SAVED unweighted virtual event vector at site J=H,W,C,
electron representative e,hard angle a,incoming coordinate x.
Its physical integration weight is w_J,e,a(x)=s_n(x)*rho_J,e,a(x),where
s_n is the common incoming Gauss weight and rho contains all site-specific
electron,angular,cross-section and photon-population factors.

Define F_J,n(x)=-sum_(e,a) w_J,e,a(x) V_J,e,a(x).
Then V_J,n=sum_x F_J,n(x),and the unweighted common-coordinate integrand
is G_J,n(x)=F_J,n(x)/s_n(x).
The error-sensitive joint virtual term is
F_joint=F_H+F_W-1.5F_C,with the minus sign in eachF matching saved assembly.

Within each order the xnodes and incoming weights match exactly.
Electron velocities need not match across temperatures and are never copied.
Some cold order5xnodes equal order3nodes:only event VALUES can be reused;
the order5s_n must be used. This is checked directly against the saved rule.

For the same source mass-dual norm,triangle inequality gives
||sum_panels Vjoint,p|| <= sum_panels||Vjoint,p||
 <= sum_x||Fjoint(x)|| <= sum_x sum_J|a_J| ||F_J(x)||.
Thus cross-temperature cancellation at each x can be distinguished from
cancellation between frequencies and between panels. These are finite-rule
identities,not continuum-error theorems.

For each panel p and refinement n-1 to n,
dV_J,p=V_J,n,p-V_J,n-1,p;dVjoint,p=dV_H,p+dV_W,p-1.5dV_C,p.
Summing overpanels must reproduce the saved virtual part of dE.
The panel nodes differ acrossorders,so only integrated panel vectors are
compared. Norms and signed projections onto the total step identify
dominant panels without fitting or resampling their integrands.

For responses,the saved forcing functions differ:
cold uses bH*dVH;warm andcentre use J_W*dVW,J_C*dVC.
Their saved response slots ALREADY contain those bases.
The combined virtual trajectory increment is the SUM of the three slots,
not the source-point combination with another-1.5 factor.
Decompose matched trajectories M_n=M_(n-1)+dR_other+dR_virtual,where
dR_other is the sum of saved D/S slots. Compare baseline+M_n with
baseline+(M_(n-1)+dR_other),isolating the virtual update without altering
the actual saved history. This is an exact linear-response decomposition,
not a new adopted trajectory or a baseline inner-history rerun.
No per-frequency response follows from these aggregate slots alone.

Readout convention: the inherited comparison products retain their saved
older spectral contribution rather than replacing it with a projection of
the evolved older state. Therefore replay the accumulated spectral readout as
P_n=P_4+L(sum of matched response increments), and likewise for energy.
Use P_n-L(dR_virtual) for the virtual-update-off comparison. State-history
identities are checked separately against the saved combined trajectories.
The initial attempt incorrectly equated these two readout conventions; its
failed checks and outputs are retained in results/initial-readout-convention.
No source, response trajectory, tolerance or frozen temperature curve changed.
