# Stage229: a kinetic realization of the electron-derived thermal source

Use the Stage228 electron EOS, existing NR ions, shared temperature, calibrated
heavy/cold parents and original Compton/nonlocal-DC/BR bank. No new empirical
coefficient, particle content or gravitational action is introduced.

With R=1+chi g, the retained linear-temperature-departure heat is

    U=u/(chi rho_star)=A0(s)+chi C0(s) g,
    A0=A(s,1), C0=A_R(s,1), C0_s=-C_R(s).

The first law is D_s(E+U)+2R-U=0. Photon evolution is unchanged:

    M B_s=r K(B-t g), E=Erow B.

In continuous form this gives

    E_s+chi C0 g_s=(C0-2)+chi(C0-2+C_R)g.

Keeping C0 but dropping C_R is inconsistent. Numerically we instead carry U
and discretize the conserved first law with the same BE startup/BDF2 as the
original engine, thereby avoiding a discrete product-rule error.

At a step let D_s B=alpha B-vector and D_s U=alpha U-history_U.
The common photon solve gives

    T=alpha M-rK,
    free=T^-1 M vector, response=T^-1(-rKt), B=free+response*g,
    denominator=chi C0(alpha-1)+2chi+alpha Erow response,
    numerator=history_U-(alpha-1)A0-2-Erow(alpha free-vector).

Set g=numerator/denominator. The old A0=C0=1 update is recovered algebraically.
Initialize B=g=0, U=A0(0). The work equation D_s W=2R-U then gives
E+U+W=U(0), including the excess initial heat.

## Direct small-difference evolution

To avoid subtracting common spectra, evolve the NR solution and its EOS
difference together through the same factorization. Let dB=B_EOS-B_NR,
dg=g_EOS-g_NR, D=A0-1, dC=C0-1. The gas-energy difference is

    dU=D+chi dC*g_NR+chi C0*dg = F+chi C0*dg.

Compute the NR step first using the original numerator written in g (avoiding
subtraction of large constant heat histories). For the difference,

    free_d=T^-1 M vector_d,
    dg=[history_dU-(alpha-1)F-Erow(alpha free_d-vector_d)]/denominator,
    dB=free_d+response*dg,
    D_s dW=2chi dg-dU.

Initial dU=D(0), dB=dg=dW=0. This is an algebraic difference of two physical
linear-temperature EOS evolutions, not an independently tuned source. Its
energy budget is dE+dU+dW=D(0). Stress differences supplied to gravity are

    dq=chi(dE+dU), dp=chi(dE/3+2chi dg/3).

Compare these with the Stage228 frozen, leading-chi conserved prediction over
the whole interval. The comparison is of the small correction itself.

The original solver uses SciPy's positive-definite upper-triangle solve.
Retain that convention equally for NR and EOS. Bank matrices are never edited
or clipped. Physical raw-equation residuals are measured separately from an
independent high-precision solve of the matrix actually represented by that
solver; do not confuse floating symmetry conventions with a new physical law.

## Scope

This supplies a finite-capacity, leading-temperature-departure kinetic
realization on the original reference geometry. Exact electron thermodynamic
moments do not make the retained weak collision operator fully nonlinear in
occupation or temperature. No new spatial-wave evolution or second-order
gravity is claimed. Corrections to the source can subsequently be transported
through Stage228's retained gravitational response without changing the action.
