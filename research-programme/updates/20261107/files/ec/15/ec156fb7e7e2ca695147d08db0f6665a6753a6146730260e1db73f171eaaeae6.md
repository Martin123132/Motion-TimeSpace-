# Repaired acceleration in the coupled force/current system

Private daughter calculation; original executed sources and failed attempts remain immutable.

## Evaluation contract

For each of reference-reference and MTS-primary at the third spatial level, solve the same four-row system

`B + C0*s + C1*u + C2*a = 0`.

Rows are the force and currents h0, h1, h2, in the inherited code normalization. Amplitudes are position, velocity and acceleration jets of the existing support direction, not new physical couplings. C0 and its reaction sensitivity come from the completed higher-order position integral. C1 is the archived reacted velocity derivative. Both historical and independently integrated baselines are retained as separate cases; neither is selected after looking at fit quality.

C2 is replaced by the completed compensated polynomial integral, not by the historical noisy central column. Variant A uses the archived rounded coefficients; B reconstructs from archived parent nodes. The variants are two interpretations of available finite-precision input, not distinct theories or statistically calibrated errors.

## Error provenance and replacement

The historical generator explicitly constructs its sampled error as:

* all columns: absolute material48/64 difference plus gravitational24/32 difference;
* C1 only: the maximum absolute material-reaction sensitivity over both rules;
* C2 only: the maximum absolute raw-versus-analytic acceleration difference over both rules.

The later sensitivity validator adds the position-oracle sensitivity to C1 only. Thus the old C2 array has no separately recorded parent-coefficient uncertainty to silently drop. Its two wave-arithmetic diagnostics may be superseded now that their origin and repair have been tested. The daughter script must reproduce this decomposition numerically before replacing them. This is not evidence that the parent coefficients themselves are exact.

The new sampled C2 envelope sums: resolved24/32 variation, shared-kernel material48/64 variation, material-versus-resolved difference, A/B source-representation spread, inherited gravitational quadrature difference and a floating-arithmetic diagnostic. Each term is saved separately. Baseline and C0/C1 uncertainties remain; the alternate baseline also carries its own quadrature difference and full inter-method displacement. No envelope here is a proved continuum error bound or a confidence interval.

## Exact algebra and diagnostic feasibility

The force pivot gives `s=-(B_F+C1_F*u+C2_F*a)/C0_F`. Substituting into the three currents gives `d+G*(u,a)=0`, with `d=B_J-C0_J*B_F/C0_F` and `G=C[1,2]_J-C0_J*C[1,2]_F/C0_F`. Rank and the left-null obstruction are computed on column-normalized G. This elimination is an identity, not an extra equation.

Five fitted representatives cannot establish that no amplitude works. Therefore also test the entire componentwise sampled-envelope problem

`abs(B+C*x) <= E_B + E_C*abs(x)`.

On an amplitude orthant `x=diag(sign)*diag(scale)*y`, `y>=0`, these are eight linear inequalities:

`(+C*diag(sign)-E_C)*diag(scale)*y <= E_B-B`,

`(-C*diag(sign)-E_C)*diag(scale)*y <= E_B+B`.

All eight sign orthants are examined with the same row/column scaling for both branches. The solver minimizes the sum of scaled magnitudes to avoid intentionally choosing a huge-amplitude witness. An optimum is independently substituted into the original equations before it is accepted as a sampled-envelope witness. Solver infeasibility is recorded as a numerical result, not an exact proof. Envelope feasibility permits independent coefficient perturbations and therefore does not demonstrate a realizable correlated parent perturbation or a valid finite trajectory.
