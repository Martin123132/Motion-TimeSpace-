# Stage231 saved: EOS-corrected local stress and forced constraints

## Physics advanced

The same electron population now supplies a usable local gas source interface:
density/temperature pressure, enthalpy current, temperature evolution and
momentum transfer, rather than inserting a relativistic heat correction into
an otherwise NR stress. The retained parent action and constants are unchanged.

The important replacement is

    delta P_g/rho_star=(2chi/3)(R delta_b+delta r),

not 2 delta u/3. The latter was correct in the old NR approximation but is
incorrect when the electron heat and heat capacity change. The source uses
the same collision energy/momentum rows as the photons with opposite signs.
It recovers the earlier NR temperature equation and enthalpy exactly.

The derived gas sound-speed squared is

    c_s^2=(P_g/(rho_g+P_g))[1+2/(3C)].

Both parents give c_s^2 about7.06156e-7 initially and7.06307e-8 finally.
Relative to the NR gas, the squared speed changes by-0.0237903% initially
and-0.00238178% finally. These are gas-only characteristic coefficients,
not the coupled photon sound speed or new observational predictions.

energy/momentum residuals. The new implementation specifies their gas and
canonical-field contributions, including the difference between an observed
