# 4920 - Graviton-mediated curvature-Higgs packet and invariant-vacuum local-GR gate

**Status:** The internal-graviton exception left by checkpoint 4919 is not
closed by pretending that a particular off-shell `beta_xi` is observable.
It is closed at low energy by reducing the complete curvature-Higgs packet to
its on-shell Higgs residue, bounding that residue with current ATLAS and CMS
global signal strengths, and separately retaining the physical nonanalytic
graviton tail. This gives a private conditional promotion of the selected
metric-only invariant-vacuum branch to the existing weak-field local-GR
certificate. It is not a promotion of the full MTS programme, a prediction of
`xi_H`, a UV completion, or a deletion of the pure-metric `C^3` and nonlocal
ledgers.

Marker: `MTS_GRAVITON_HIGGS_OBSERVABLE_LOCAL_GR_GATE_4920`.

## 1. The calculation actually required

Checkpoint 4919 proved exact fixed-metric factorization,

```text
Gamma[g,chi,Phi_SM] = Gamma_X[g,chi] + Gamma_SM[g,Phi_SM],
```

but correctly excluded diagrams containing internal graviton lines. A hidden
loop and a visible Higgs leg can still communicate through metric
fluctuations. The correct question is therefore not whether those diagrams
exist. They do. The useful questions are:

1. which part is a basis-dependent local renormalization;
2. which part is an invariant long-range amplitude;
3. whether the total physical Higgs residue is currently bounded; and
4. whether the surviving low-energy amplitude can upset the local-GR branch.

Write the graviton-mediated one-particle-irreducible contribution as

```text
Gamma_grav = Gamma_local,analytic + Gamma_nonanalytic.
```

`Gamma_local,analytic` renormalizes a complete local operator basis. It
contains the curved-space term `R HdagH` but also its field-redefinition
partners. `Gamma_nonanalytic` contains structures such as
`q^2 log(-q^2)` and threshold cuts. Local field redefinitions can move the
first class but cannot remove the second class from an on-shell amplitude.

That split prevents two opposite errors:

- fixed-background factorization is not promoted into a no-graviton-loop
  theorem;
- an off-shell coefficient in one Jordan basis is not promoted into a
  directly measured force.

## 2. Why a naked gravitational `beta_xi` is not the gate

Use a declared Jordan-basis packet

```text
S_J = integral sqrt(-g) [
        (M_R^2/2 + xi_H HdagH) R
        - (D H)dag(D H) - V(H) + L_SM,rest
      ].
```

At first EFT order, a local conformal or metric redefinition moves the
`R HdagH` monomial into correlated scalar-kinetic, Higgs-potential and
matter-trace operators. Therefore the numerical running assigned to the
single symbol `xi_H(mu)` depends on the operator basis, subtraction scheme
and, for a noncovariant off-shell calculation, gauge and field coordinates.
The complete effective action and on-shell amplitudes do not have that
freedom.

The field-space-covariant Vilkovisky-DeWitt construction is the clean
reference for this distinction. It makes the full one-loop gravity-scalar
effective action independent of gauge fixing and field coordinates. It does
not prove that every individual local coefficient is zero. It says the
correlated packet, not an isolated coordinate coefficient, is the invariant
object.

Accordingly define, in any declared matching basis,

```text
xi_total(mu)
  = xi_SM(mu)
  + xi_grav(mu)
  + xi_hidden-grav(mu)
  + xi_finite-match(mu).
```

Checkpoint 4919 fixed only `xi_direct-MTS=0`. Checkpoint 4920 does **not** set
`xi_total=0` and does **not** invent a microscopic value for it. Instead it
bounds the on-shell residue generated by the full packet.

## 3. Exact on-shell Higgs reduction

In unitary gauge,

```text
HdagH = (v+h)^2/2,
x     = xi_total^2 v^2/Mbar_Pl^2.
```

After the measured Planck residue is fixed and the Higgs-metric kinetic
system is diagonalized, checkpoint 4919 gives the exact vacuum quadratic
normalization

```text
Z_h     = 1 + 6x,
h_c     = sqrt(Z_h) h,
kappa_h = 1/sqrt(Z_h).
```

Every ordinary single-Higgs coupling inherited from the Standard Model is
then multiplied by the same `kappa_h`. On the active factorized vacuum branch
there is no new direct Higgs decay channel. All Standard-Model partial widths
scale by `kappa_h^2`, the total visible width scales by the same factor, and
the visible branching fractions are unchanged. Hence the inclusive signal
strength obeys

```text
beta       = 6 xi_total^2 v^2/Mbar_Pl^2 >= 0,
mu_pred    = kappa_h^2 = 1/(1+beta) <= 1.
```

This is the observable image of the entire local curvature-Higgs packet at
the Higgs pole. It includes any graviton-mediated contribution that
renormalizes `R HdagH`; no separate `xi_grav` number is needed for this gate.

## 5. EFT validity rather than silent extrapolation

A conservative vacuum power-counting scale for a large nonminimal coupling
is

```text
Lambda_xi,vac = Mbar_Pl/abs(xi_total).
```

Using the looser ATLAS `q=3.84` envelope gives

```text
Lambda_xi,vac > 1.99976e3 GeV,
m_h/Lambda_xi,vac < 0.062573.
```

Thus the physical `125.13 GeV` Higgs pole used in the normalization recast is
below the conservative vacuum cutoff. The field-space-covariant small-field
comparator `Mbar_Pl/sqrt(abs(xi))` is much higher, approximately
`6.98e10 GeV`; the lower `Mbar_Pl/abs(xi)` scale is retained as the safer
local power-counting gate.

The proton-proton beam energy is not the momentum flowing through every
single-Higgs vertex. Nevertheless, high-energy tails above the conservative
cutoff cannot be claimed by this EFT. The inclusive recast is therefore a
low-energy on-shell residue bound, not a statement about arbitrary LHC
kinematics or a UV completion.

## 6. Internal-graviton residual after the residue is bounded

### 6.1 Analytic class

Analytic loop pieces renormalize local operators. The dimension-four
curvature-Higgs packet is bounded above. Higher-derivative local terms are
treated in the strict EFT sense used in checkpoints 4878 and 4879:

- their positive-gap two-body image is contact supported;
- source self terms renormalize measured body parameters;
- they are not resummed into extra poles or ghosts;
- an anomalously large UV Wilson coefficient exits the certificate and must
  be scored separately.

This is not a theorem that every local coefficient vanishes.

### 6.2 Nonanalytic class

The long-range nonanalytic class is physical. With the conservative ATLAS
two-sided envelope, define the explicit no-large-log, order-one-coefficient

```text

 = [abs(xi_total) E/Mbar_Pl]^2/(16 pi^2)
```

It is not substituted for an exact diagram coefficient. It is a conservative
arena-scale envelope identifying whether this class can possibly threaten

## 7. Maxwell, clocks and source coupling

Classical four-dimensional Maxwell theory still has `T_EM=0`, so the
tree-level Higgs-trace route is absent. Graviton or charged-particle loops can
produce nonzero trace-anomaly and form-factor terms, but at a one-eV optical
scale the conservative xi-enhanced loop envelope is `1.58e-27`. The standard
charged-particle loop physics is part of the adopted Standard-Model limit,
not a new MTS force.

For atomic clocks, R10, PPN and orbital systems, the analytic packet is local
or absorbed into calibrated source parameters and the nonanalytic packet is
bounded above. The single universal source normalization and measured
`G_N` ownership remain those of checkpoint 4915. No new composition-dependent
source charge is introduced by this calculation.

## 8. Conditional promotion theorem

Consider the selected active branch satisfying all of the following:

1. the integrated-`H` parent has one positive Einstein-Hilbert residue and
   universal Hilbert source coupling as in checkpoint 4915;
2. the state is the Lorentz-invariant zero-enthalpy vacuum branch of
   checkpoint 4918;
3. the hidden and Standard-Model fixed-metric action factorizes as in
   checkpoint 4919;
4. the total curvature-Higgs packet has no pole other than the measured
   Higgs and satisfies the current on-shell residue bound above;
5. energies obey `E << Lambda_xi,vac` and the higher-operator controls of
   checkpoint 4880;
6. local higher-curvature operators are used perturbatively rather than
   resummed into additional degrees of freedom; and
7. sources are weak, positively separated and minimally coupled in the
   checkpoint-4879 domain.

Then the curvature-Higgs/internal-graviton sector adds neither a resolvable
new pole nor a competitive long-range residual in R10, PPN, clock, orbital or
classical Maxwell arenas. Therefore the checkpoint-4879 private 1PN
local-GR certificate can be promoted from "Higgs/graviton class open" to
"Higgs/graviton class bounded" on this selected invariant-vacuum branch.

The promoted private certificate is

```text
Newton long-range source law = Einstein-Hilbert calibrated law,
gamma = beta = 1 through the inherited 1PN domain,
classical one- and two-PM light propagation = GR,
point-clock and quantum light residuals = inherited bounded values,
curvature-Higgs pole = 125.13 GeV only,
xi-enhanced nonanalytic local residual = arena-bounded above.
```

This is a domain theorem, not an unrestricted identity between MTS and GR.

## 9. What remains outside the certificate

The following are deliberately not promoted:

- a microscopic prediction of `G_N` or `xi_total`;
- a unique gravitational beta function independent of basis and scheme;
- energies above the conservative nonminimal-coupling cutoff;
- nonvacuum bath, cosmological, coherent or preferred-flow states;
- compact-matter interiors, charged electrovac response and perturbation
  spectra;
- resummed higher-derivative poles;
- the first nonredundant pure-metric `C^3` operator;
- finite nonlocal pure-metric form factors beyond the already bounded local
  arenas;
- a combined ATLAS-CMS experimental likelihood;
- a public claim that the whole MTS programme has derived GR.
