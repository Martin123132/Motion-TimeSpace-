### Exact Conditional Theorem

`delta S/delta A_Q_nu -> nabla_mu(g_*^-2 F_Q^{mu nu}) = J_e^nu + J_other^nu`

and the electron equation of motion contains `q_e A_Q`. For `q_e != 0` and non-empty electron support, this is a nonzero electron-photon action morphism.

What this theorem does **not** prove is just as important: it does not forbid pre-variation source weights, duplicate EM representatives, hidden `f(Xhat)F_Q^2` coefficients, non-Hilbert currents, or radiative/readout reentry.
