## Current Verdict

2115 sharpens the spin/coupling bottleneck. The good news is real: the spin sector has a clean exact route to zero. This is not a defeat; it is the right kind of narrowing. The theory is no longer failing on an amorphous local-GR worry. It is asking for a precise coupling contract.
