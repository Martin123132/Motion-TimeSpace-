# 2236 - Y5/R2FR R_AB Auxiliary Compatibility Parent Sort and No-Derivative Grammar

## Verdict
- 2236 imports the old `1563` auxiliary-compatibility grammar gate into the current R2FR chain after `2235` selected this route.
- The algebraic elimination route is still the best non-cheaty local-GR path, but only as an exact conditional.
- To upgrade it into a theorem, `R_AB` must be parent-typed as an auxiliary/vertical compatibility coordinate rather than an observable physical scalar/tensor.
- The parent grammar must forbid `D R_AB`, `D Lambda_R`, vertical metrics/connections, and boundary derivative terms; current sources do not parent-sign those bans.
- Therefore `Z_R=0`, `q_R=0`, and local GR/Newton recovery remain nonclaim.
- If the vertical-null proof fails, the finite `Z_R/q_R` residual branch becomes mandatory rather than optional.
