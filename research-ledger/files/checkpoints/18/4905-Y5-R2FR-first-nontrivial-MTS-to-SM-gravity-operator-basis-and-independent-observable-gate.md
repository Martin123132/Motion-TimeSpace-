## 2. Fixed-metric factorization theorem

Let `chi` denote all microscopic MTS variables and `Phi` the Standard-Model
fields. On the active parent slice,

\[
S_{\rm parent}[\chi,\Phi,g]
=S_{\rm MTS}[\chi,g]+S_{\rm SM}[\Phi,g].
\]

At fixed public metric,

\[
\begin{aligned}
e^{i\Gamma_{\rm eff}[g,\Phi]}
&=e^{iS_{\rm SM}[g,\Phi]}
\int D\chi\,e^{iS_{\rm MTS}[\chi,g]},\\
\Gamma_{\rm eff}[g,\Phi]
&=S_{\rm SM}[g,\Phi]+\Gamma_{\rm MTS}[g].
\end{aligned}
\]

The quadratic Hessian makes the obstruction explicit:

\[
\det\begin{pmatrix}K_{\rm MTS}&J_{\rm mix}\\
J_{\rm mix}&K_{\rm SM}\end{pmatrix}
=K_{\rm MTS}K_{\rm SM}-J_{\rm mix}^2,
\]

\[
\Delta\log\det
=-\frac{J_{\rm mix}^2}{K_{\rm MTS}K_{\rm SM}}
+O(J_{\rm mix}^4).
\]

Therefore `J_mix=0` gives exact determinant factorization and no direct mixed
threshold operator. This is stronger than saying that a coefficient has not
been found.

The theorem is deliberately scoped. It does not set ordinary Standard-Model
loop effects to zero, and it does not cover graviton loops or a future
parent-signed mixed vertex. The historical `b T M^2` branch would break the
factorization assumption, but that source was not retained after its
constraint failure and cannot be reused without re-entry.

## 4. Complete dimension-six gravity basis

The nonredundant four-dimensional GR plus Standard-Model basis at dimension
six contains ten operators:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal L_6={}&\zeta_+ C C C+\zeta_- C C\widetilde C\\
&+c_H H^\dagger H C C+\widetilde c_HH^\dagger H C\widetilde C\\
&+c_B BB C+\widetilde c_BBB\widetilde C\\
&+c_G GG C+\widetilde c_GGG\widetilde C\\
&+c_W WW C+\widetilde c_WWW\widetilde C.
\end{aligned}
\]

There are two pure-gravity and eight mixed bosonic directions. No new gravity
operator appears at dimension seven; fermionic gravity operators first enter
at dimension eight.

For the current parity-even neutral MTS threshold:

- `C C C` has a conditional massive-scalar matching owner;
- `C C tilde C` is zero on that parity-even threshold;
- the eight mixed coefficients are zero by the fixed-metric factorization
  theorem;
- ordinary charged Standard-Model thresholds remain part of known physics,
  not novel MTS coefficients.

\[

`10^-3` horizon shift at the ten-solar-mass EFT edge, before their backreaction

therefore retained as a UV matching prediction route, but rejected as the
first competitive macroscopic MTS signal.
