# MTS research status — November 26, 2026

Two questions organize this update: can the chosen state predict forces in real systems without adjusting it to the target, and does its stress drive gravity consistently enough for different calculations to predict the same photons? Neither question is answered by an attractive fit or a small algebraic residual alone.

| Test | Result | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Galactic radial force | Both fixed preparations miss the shape of a young-star Cepheid rotation comparison. With one published stellar disk and bulge, the reset preparation comes within 1.42 km/s in the inner bin but predicts a rise where the next bin falls. [Calculation and source conventions](../../research-programme/updates/20261126/stages/20261122/mts-cepheid-newton-gravity-590/RESULTS.md) | This tests specific states and ordinary matter inputs, not every MTS preparation or a new six-galaxy fit. The tracers share some systematics with earlier tests. |
| Stream discrimination | At the positions of 17 Palomar 5 tail RR Lyrae, opposite flows separate by 0.39 or 0.38 measurement-error units with preparation fixed, and one crossed-preparation comparison is only 0.0671. [Astrometric forecast](../../research-programme/updates/20261126/stages/20261122/mts-pal5-astrometric-separation/RESULTS.md) | This uses real sampling and an adopted error model. It is not a detection or a selection of the true flow direction. |
| First-order gravity | Against two native gravity reconstructions over all 782 retained modes, the two repaired background tables give worst discrepancies of 0.09950% and 0.10831%; the original wave gave 1.12641% and the cold control 0.01880%. [Mode and exchange tests](../../research-programme/updates/20261126/stages/20261126/mts-live-fullbank-90p4/RESULTS.md) | An internal consistency gain on the stated data, preparation and mode bank, not 782 independent observations or a general proof of the GR limit. |
| Second-order photons | From 89.65 to 90.4 conformal Mpc, evolving the resolved perturbations on their corrected background reduced the finer-grid joint discrepancy from 0.65514% to 0.60700%. Adding the metric work required by the matched description's background stress difference then **worsened** it to 0.63433%. [Resolved background](../../research-programme/updates/20261126/stages/20261126/mts-coupled-background-photons-90p4/RESULTS.md) · [Matched stress and both photon channels](../../research-programme/updates/20261126/stages/20261126/mts-matched-metric-work-90p4/RESULTS.md) | Both miss the unchanged 0.5% target on the original small-correction scale. On the same window an incident-source reduction predicts 0.63428% without the later matched state. Predicting the discrepancy is not removing it, and this is not a fit to the observed CMB. |

The resolved, matched and cold histories are different comparisons; do not relabel them as interchangeable baselines. The earlier [selected GR/Newton branch and claim limits](../../CLAIM_CEILING.md) remain separate from the success or failure of any particular galactic occupation or photon calculation. No new fundamental mass-gap or general quantum-gravity result is claimed.

**Next test:** separate the known incident drive into gravitational geometry, heavy and slow fields, ordinary matter and radiation. Compare their signed contributions after scattering, and test the conservation identity of the combination that survives. The [release catalogue](../../research-programme/updates/20261126/README.md) includes negative results and verification instructions.
