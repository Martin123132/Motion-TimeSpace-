lapse/metric annular chart, not fresh astronomical measurements. The metric
extension is an explicit inherited choice; current agreement does not prove
that the diagonal action uniquely determines every off-diagonal response.
The earlier selected two-derivative local branch and its assumptions remain
as documented in `CLAIM_CEILING.md`; this update does not change those premises.
