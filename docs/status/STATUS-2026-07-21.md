# MTS Status - 2026-07-21

## Executive Position

MTS is materially further advanced than the July 6 public snapshot, but it remains a research programme rather than a completed theory.

The strongest progress is no longer another conditional shell-zero ledger. The selected parent now has an explicit weak-local theorem chain: under the declared integrated metric-density `H`, exact-Diff/BRST parent and the standard positive massless-spin-2 premises, the leading matter coupling is universal, the infrared equation is Einstein's equation, the weak static limit is Newton's inverse-square law, and the selected reflection-even `psi=0` branch has leading `PPN gamma=beta=1`. Maxwell propagation, Lorentz force, electromagnetic stress, and Poynting momentum arise from the same local action.

That result is deliberately bounded. The integrated metric-density, gauge quotient, visible fields, and gauge representations are currently parent content rather than consequences derived from motion, time, and space alone. Strong-field compact bodies, complete radiation/back-reaction, higher-derivative matching, and nonvacuum motion-state effects remain open.

## What Advanced

### Parent and RG Structure

- A source-complete minimal gravity-photon-curvature fixed point was constructed within a declared truncation and scheme.
- Its one relevant direction has a numerically regular Gaussian/GR infrared separatrix; the opposite sign runs away in the executed domain.
- The actual fractional-potential motion action was varied. Its bare vacuum Hessian is singular, so the valid entry is a renormalized 1PI motion two-point operator rather than a guessed finite bare mass.
- The motion scale is a genuine additional relevant coordinate unless a coupled fixed point or parent identity fixes its ratio to the gravitational scale.

### Weak Local GR, Newton, and Electromagnetism

- The `H`-to-Hilbert source map is exact and invertible.
- Soft-spin-2 consistency leaves one connected species-coupling direction.
- Graviton field normalization cancels from conserved-source exchange.
- One massless-pole residue therefore controls Einstein dynamics, Newtonian gravity, orbits, lensing, and waves at leading weak order.
- The relation `G_N=1/(8 pi M_R^2)` is derived; the numerical value of `G_N` remains one experimental calibration.
- On the selected `psi=0` branch, the classical one-motion-scalar source charge is zero and the standard leading PPN values are recovered.
- Maxwell, Lorentz force, Hilbert stress, Poynting momentum, and total stress conservation come from the same action and leading electromagnetic normalization.

### Local-to-Galaxy Bridge

- A same-parent block-Hessian/Schur-complement route was constructed: `B=0` preserves the local GR branch, while an occupied motion state with `B!=0` can alter long-distance response.
- Within a declared minimal monomial class, the required inner suppression and outer flat-rotation behavior uniquely select a spectral response shape.
- Its real-space transform is ultraviolet suppressed and has the required long-distance plateau form.
- A read-only 175-galaxy interface smoke is encouraging, but it is not a galaxy fit or a derivation of the parent response.

### Current Numerical Discrimination

- A matched MTS/CDM formation comparison was added rather than testing MTS alone.
- The first apparent CDM advantage was traced to directionally under-resolved cube-corner modes and did not survive a shared spherical-Nyquist control.
- Restoring that physical band at higher isotropic resolution separated the branches again, with CDM closer on slope and RMSE in one shared high-mode realization.
- One stochastic realization cannot establish preference. The next valid test is a predeclared paired multi-seed ensemble with unchanged physics inputs and stopping rules.

## Claim Boundary

The current work supports a serious conditional weak-local field-theory result and a concrete large-scale derivation target. It does not support a completed unified theory, a unique fundamental ontology, ultraviolet completeness, strong-field completeness, or an empirical claim that MTS outperforms GR plus dark matter or Lambda-CDM.
